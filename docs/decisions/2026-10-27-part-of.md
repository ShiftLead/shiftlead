**Paul, 2026-10-27:** *it seems some finished items are laid out like they depend on unfinished items. Is that
true? How do we make the display more clear?* And, choosing between the shapes: *Should we make a distinction between
subtasks (e.g. build items for a feature) and blockers so that a new fix/feature can be listed with its subtasks but
also show blockers (when applicable)?* And: *what happens if a design needs some other item to land first (a real
blocker) plus has its design items?*

## 1. The problem

TD-1129 (shiftlead `docs/technical_debt.md`). A Queue card (design §4.4 *The Queue: cards, and the rank that orders
them*, *Rows*; `src/sessionorc/queue.py` `_card`) draws its rows as a tree built from `Blocked by:` edges taken **in
either direction**. So indentation means *waits on the parent* at one level and *the parent waits on it* at the next,
and finished builds of a design sit under a row that still waits, so they read as if they wait on unfinished work.

Underneath it, a `design-first` entry's `**Blocked by:**` does two jobs today.
- **Its parts**: once designed, it lists its builds (and live checks), which keeps it from being picked again until
  they land (cadence §2.4; `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md`; `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py` `design_builds`; §4.9c *A design
  entry archives with its last build*).
- **Real blockers**: what must land first. Live mixed cases on 2026-10-27: TD-857 is blocked by TD-786, which is a build
  of another design (TD-112), so TD-786 is the one entry both a part and a blocker. TD-1029 is blocked by
  `dev-cadence#TD-144`.

The ledger can't tell the two apart: titles do not say *Build TD-n* reliably (TD-1010–TD-1017 under TD-1009; TD-772, a
trial, under TD-748; live checks such as TD-292 under TD-290). An interim that keeps `Blocked by:` and reads `Kind: build`
plus the title is ruled out for the same reason [F12].

Research done in the session covered Jira, Linear, GitHub, Asana, ClickUp, MS Project, npm/cargo/pipdeptree, OmniFocus
and Kanban. No tool indents by dependency in mixed directions. Trackers indent only part-of (a parent field on the
child) and show blockers as named links.

## 2. The ledger: `**Part of:**`

- **The field.** A new optional cadence field, **`**Part of:** TD-n`**, on the part. It names **one** parent in the
  **same ledger** [F7]. The parent may be any entry: a design and its builds or live checks [F9], or a fix or feature
  and its subtasks. Free comment may follow the id, as on any field.
- **The parent's blockers.** A parent's `Blocked by:` keeps only real blockers, and a design stops listing its parts
  there.
- **Pickable** (derived, cadence §2.4). An entry is not pickable while any open entry names it in `Part of:`. This
  replaces what listing parts in `Blocked by:` did. A part's own pickability is unchanged and comes from its own
  `Blocked by:`.
- **Pick order** [F2, R6] (cadence §2.4, ledgerboard SPEC *The rank*, `_card_order`, and the Queue's own within-card
  loop, `queue.py:545–550`). Within a card, an entry comes after
  what it is blocked by **and after its parts**.
- **Parts do not inherit the parent's blockers.** A part that also waits on its parent's blocker says so in its own
  `Blocked by:`.
- **Cards.** ledgerboard's `components()` joins entries by `Part of:` as it joins them by `Blocked by:`.
- **A part is never blocked by its own parent or any ancestor** [S3]. That would deadlock: the parent waits on the
  part's archive, and the part waits on the parent's. A build is filed when its design's round has merged, so it has
  no need to wait on the design. TD-1118, blocked by TD-1117, is an ordinary entry blocked by a design. It does not
  become a part of TD-1117.
- **Checks** (`ledger.py --check`). Ids are normalised as `Blocked by:` ids are (`_norm`: `TD-7` reads as `TD-007`)
  [S7].
  - `Part of:` names exactly one entry, which resolves in the ledger or the archive of the same file pair.
  - A `Part of:` naming another repo's entry (`<repo>#TD-n`) is an error of the unreadable-item kind [T5].
  - A `Part of:` naming an archived parent is ℹ, not an error: it is the archived-parent case in section 3 [S7].
  - **Two rules** [R1]. The graph is drawn in waits-on edges: `X → Y` when X is `Blocked by:` Y, and `P → C` when C is
    `Part of:` P (a parent waits on its part).
    - **(a) No cycle in that graph.** This catches a part blocked by its parent or any ancestor (`P → C` and `C → P`),
      and any longer loop. The flag is raised on **every entry on the cycle**, and its kind joins `EDIT_ERRORS`. So an
      edit to any entry on a cycle fails, whichever end it touched; `check()` (`ledgerboard.py:329–334`) downgrades a
      flag only when its own entry is unchanged [T1]. A standing cycle is ℹ, and the live ledger has none (round 3).
    - **(b) No legacy edge**: an entry's `Blocked by:` naming an entry that is `Part of:` it, or of a descendant. That
      is the old style, with a parallel edge and no cycle. The flag is carried by the entry whose `Blocked by:` holds
      the edge, the parent [T1]. It is ℹ on a standing ledger and an error when that entry is **changed**. (b)'s code joins `EDIT_ERRORS` (`ledgerboard.py:53`), the mechanism `check()` already uses for "an error
      in a new edit, information on a standing ledger" [F4].
  - The check reads the entry as the edit leaves it, so an edit that removes the bad id passes [S7].
- **Not cross-repo** [F7]. A parent's pickable needs every entry that names it, and `pickable()` computes that over one
  ledger. The archive-with-last-part rule cannot cross repos either. Another repo's entry a design waits on stays a
  real blocker. TD-909 today lists only its local builds. Its dev-cadence waits sit on its builds (TD-917, TD-1012),
  so whether TD-909 itself names `dev-cadence#TD-121`/`#TD-122` is the backfill's judgement, read entry by entry
  [S6].
- **`CADENCE_FIELDS`** (ledgerboard.py:42) gains `part of`, so a repo with a `Fields:` line does not read it as
  undeclared [S2].
- **Version.** A ledgerboard minor bump. An older reader ignores the field, and a ledger with no `Part of:` reads as
  today.
- **Archive rule** [F3, R5] (§4.9c *A design entry archives with its last build*, the grinder stage brief). The PR of a
  part archives its parent when no other open entry on `origin/<default>` names the parent in `Part of:`, and the
  parent's own `Blocked by:` is empty or every id in it is a local id that is archived, with no decision. A parent
  whose `Blocked by:` names another repo's item is left to the fallback, as §4.9c does today: resolving `<repo>#TD-n` is
  the home's, not the build session's. The *builds TD-n* back-link reads
  the part's field.
  - A part that is itself a parent archives one level per PR. A grandparent left behind with no open parts becomes
    pickable, and the designer-lane fallback already written archives it. Nothing cascades.
- **ShiftLead's ledger reader** [S1] (`src/sessionorc/ledger.py`).
  - Today `"pickable": "no" if blocked_by else "yes"` (line 521) derives pickable from `blocked_by` alone. It takes
    ledgerboard's `rec["pickable"]` instead.
  - It carries `part_of` (one id or none) and `parts_open` (the open entries naming it) onto the entry. Open parts are
    **not** put in `blocked_by`, which keeps only real blockers.
  - `kind_of` (line 254) reads a parent with open parts as `blocked`, as it reads a design with open builds today. So
    the Repo page's design list, `ao repo` and the lanes keep their meaning.
  - `blocked_open` and Back to design (`agent_inbox.py:979–985`, and the Inbox's copy of `blocked_open`,
    `ui/inbox.py:2439` [T4]) count open parts as they count open blockers, as a
    design with open builds behaves today.
- **Designer.** A `design-first` part is picked as any design-first entry is, once it has no open parts and no open
  blockers (open point 4).

## 3. The Queue card: shape C

Mockup: the review pages named at the top, showing TD-748's and TD-1117's cards from the live reading of
2026-10-27. Section 3 governs where the mockup differs: the meter's colours [F6], and the top entry [F5].

- **A parent's state** [F1] (§4.4 *A row's state*, `_status.waits`, `_wait`). A row some open entry names in `Part of:`
  reads **waiting**, and its meter says on what. It comes after *settling* and before *after TD-n*. A card whose
  members are all parents waiting on parts, or parts waiting, is Waiting, as today, and never *ready*. Start session is
  not offered on it (`_start`).
- **Indentation means only *part of*.** A parent's parts hang one step under it on a **spine** (a 2 px rule down the
  left). Parts of parts nest further on the same rule.
- **The header** [F5] is the card's top entry. The rule becomes: the root parent (no `Part of:` within the card) with
  the best key. If the card has no parent, today's rule applies (the `design-first` entry that waits on no other
  design-first entry, else the entry with no open blocker; ties by key). The key is the measure, not the number of
  parts, because a count moves as parts archive. The header's own parts hang directly under it.
- **Other parents** are rows at the left edge, each with its parts on its spine. **Loose rows** (no parent on the card)
  sit at the left edge too.
- **Order.** The display order is deliberately not the pick order [T6]. A parent is picked after its parts, but it is
  drawn above them, as their heading. The header's group comes first. The other groups (a parent with its parts, or a loose row) follow in pick
  order of their first open member. Within a group, open parts go in pick order.
- **Finished parts** fold to one line on their parent's spine, *✓ n done*, which opens in place. A done part of a part
  folds into its own parent's line. A parent never folds while one of its parts is open. Finished loose rows fold to one
  line at the card's end.
- **An archived parent with an open part** [F6] is drawn as a done group head with its open parts under it. The
  reading's one-hop archived rows (`queue.py` `touch` / `names_open`, which read only `Blocked by:` today) learn the
  `Part of:` direction too.
- **The parts meter** goes on every parent row and on the header. It has one small segment per part, in `branch()`'s
  four colours, so a segment and its row's rail agree [F6]:
  - done is green;
  - *needs you* is amber, the person's alone (§4.5 *Amber is spent on what needs the person alone*);
  - waiting is teal;
  - anything else is grey, including *in review*, which keeps its word in the segment's title.

  *d/n* goes beside the meter.
- **Blockers are words on the row:** *after TD-772* [F8], a link that scrolls to and rings the row it names (hover or
  focus lights it). State words are as §4.4 *A row's state* has them, except that *after TD-n* moves into the relation
  words and the state reads *waiting*.
- **Done…** [F10]. The guard `builds_open` (`queue.py:400`, `ui/inbox.py:2440`) becomes "some open entry names this
  entry in `Part of:`". Today it tests the reverse ("some open entry is blocked by this design"), so it hides Done… on
  TD-112 and TD-1117 and shows it on TD-909.
- **The Repo page's builds line** (`design_builds`) reads `Part of:` in place of the design's `Blocked by:`. So do
  `builds_of` (`queue_row.html:57`) and the Done guard (`queue_row.html:22`, `:43`) [V3] and `inbox_row.html`, and `blocking` (`ui/inbox.py:2381`), the
  second copy of `builds_open` [S4].
- **The card's graph** [S7] (`_card`, `queue.py:496–560`). Membership still joins by both fields (`components()`). The
  drawn tree is `Part of:` alone: a row's parent is its `Part of:` parent when that is on the card, else none (a loose
  row). Blockers are words. The breadth-first walk over `adj` and its *also after* note go. The within-card pick loop
  (`queue.py:545–550`) takes a separate `parts` map beside `blockers`, so that a parent comes after its parts. That map
  is kept out of the tree and out of the *after TD-n* words [T7].
- **Archived rows are found from the part's side** [S7, R4]. After the backfill, a design names none of its parts, and
  each archived part names its design in its own `Part of:` line, in the archive file. So:
  - `queue.py:300–316` (`touch`, `names_open`) reads each archived entry's `Part of:`. An archived part of an open
    parent is that parent's done row (*✓ n done* counts it), and an archived parent of an open part is drawn as the
    done group head.
  - `ledger.py:825` (`archived`, the ids some `blocked_ids` names) adds the archived ids whose `Part of:` names an open
    entry, so `design_builds` and the *every build archived* line (`queue_row.html:62`) see them.
  - `queue.py:768` (`_completed`, `named_by_open`) excludes an archived component that an open part names, or that
    names an open parent, so an archived parent with an open part is not also drawn as a Completed card.
- **A parent whose only open parts are the person's own** [S7] reads *waiting*. Its card's status comes from the need
  order as today, so *needs you* wins (§4.4 *The card's status*).
- **§4.4 *Rows*** states the reading's `part_of` per row, replacing "edges taken in either direction".

## 4. The split, the order, and what *done* is [F11]

ledgerboard has no team of its own. Paul chose (2026-10-27) to have **dev-cadence's team work the release**, as the
first step of the dev-cadence entry. So the format and the cadence text that states it are written by one hand, and
shiftlead's builds wait on that one entry.

1. **A ledgerboard release** [S2] (ShiftLead/ledgerboard, the canonical copy: its `SPEC.md`, `ledgerboard.py`,
   tests and `release.sh`). Worked by dev-cadence's team as the dev-cadence entry's first step, and recorded in
   ledgerboard's own ledger as that team sees fit.
   - The SPEC field.
   - `parse` and `pickable`: a parent is not pickable while a part is open, and it is ordered after its parts.
   - `components()` joining through the field.
   - The `--check` rules in section 2.
   - `CADENCE_FIELDS`.
   - A minor version: v2.1. The canonical copy is at v2.0 ("Release v2.0 (#21)"), while shiftlead and the pinned tree
     vendor v1.3. So the vendor also brings v2.0's changes (its directory loader). SPEC says a v1 ledger reads exactly
     as before, but a builder should know of the jump [R3].
   - Done when ledgerboard's tests cover a parent with open and with archived parts, the order, and each check
     (including the combined-graph cycle, with a test that every entry on a cycle carries its own flag, since
     `flags.add` takes one entry id).
2. **The dev-cadence entry** (filed with `ao td add --repo dev-cadence`, pointing at this ADR). After the release it
   vendors it and writes
   cadence §2.4 and §2.12 (the field, the pick rule, the order, no part blocked by an ancestor). Shiftlead's pin bump
   carries it.
3. **shiftlead build 1, the reading.** It is blocked by the dev-cadence entry. Until shiftlead vendors v2.1, its
   ledger cannot carry `Part of:` (an undeclared field fails `--check --since`), so the builds TD-1129 names are
   written the old way: TD-1129's `Blocked by:` lists them, and the backfill converts them with the rest.
   - **Its first PR is the `dev-cadence bump` that runs `scripts/vendor_ledgerboard.sh v2.1` in the same PR** [S2, R3].
     `tests/test_ledgerboard_fixtures.py:40` requires the wheel's copy to be byte-equal to the pinned tree's, so the
     two cannot land apart. The PR changes no behaviour, since nothing reads the new field until `ledger.py` does.
   - `ledger.py`: pickable from `rec["pickable"]`, `part_of`, `parts_open`, and `kind_of` [S1].
   - `part_of` per row.
   - A parent's state and wait.
   - `builds_open`.
   - The archived-parent hop.
   - `design_builds` from the field.
   - The top-entry rule.
   - Back to design's guard.
   - Done when the tests on a TD-748-shaped card (a design, its parts, a trial, a part blocked from another group, a
     cross-repo blocker) pin each row's parent, state and order.
4. **shiftlead build 2, the page.** The spine, the fold, the meter, the links, the help text, and phone width.
5. **The wheel's texts** [S4], with build 1. They go live with the promote, so they are safe to land then [R2]. They say what a parent with open parts is, and that a new build carries
   `Part of:`:
   - §4.9c, including *A design entry archives with its last build*;
   - `src/shiftlead/flows/td/build.md` and `flows/td/design.md`;
   - `src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md` (its "nothing `**Blocked by:**` names is still open" gains "and no open entry
     is `Part of:` it");
   - `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md`, where the designer writes `Part of:` on each build it files and stops
     listing builds in the design's `Blocked by:`;
   - `src/shiftlead/briefs/entry.md`, the header lines `ao td add` writes;
   - the ledger's preamble (`docs/technical_debt.md`, the Owner/Kind paragraph `entry.md` sends a filer to). It
     names `Part of:` and the pick rule. The `Fields:` line does not change, since `Part of` is a cadence field [R7].
6. **Backfill**, last [S5].
   - **`docs/briefs/` moves first in this step** [R2, T3]. It is the first PR after the promote, and no designer team
     is started between the promote and its merge. Until it lands, a team's own brief wins over the wheel's texts.
     It covers `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` and any other team brief under `docs/briefs/`. A team's brief is read from the checkout at team start, not from the wheel (AGENTS.md). So it changes the moment it
     merges, whatever is promoted, and it must not tell a designer to stop listing builds before the live reader knows
     `Part of:` [V2].
   - "Live" means **promoted** (AGENTS.md *The live copy is promoted*): the host agent, `ao`, and every container
     node's wheel. An older reader reads a backfilled design as pickable and would put it into a lane.
   - **One PR per design.** The parts' `Part of:` lines and the design's `Blocked by:` edit land together, so a
     half-done backfill is safe design by design. Each PR is checked with `ledger.py --pickable` before and after: the
     design stays not pickable.
   - Once build 1's first PR has pinned v2.1, a PR that adds `Part of:` while the changed parent still lists the part in
     `Blocked by:` fails rule (b) when the design is also edited. Left untouched, the design's old edge is a standing ℹ, which is
     harmless, since the design stays not pickable under both rules. Changing both lines in one PR is the practice; the
     check enforces it only on the design's own edit [T2, V1].
   - **Scope** [S6]: 18 open designs have parts in `Blocked by:`. TD-1029 and TD-857 name only real blockers and keep
     them. Eleven designs name nothing; three of those are decisions. Each design's `Blocked by:` is read by hand.
   - TD-786 stays a part of TD-112 and a blocker of TD-857.
   - The *Done when … Blocked by: names them* sentences (TD-748, TD-909, TD-857, TD-1009) are fixed as they are
     touched.

## 5. Settled from round 1's open points

1. A cross-repo `Part of:`: no, same ledger only [F7].
2. The word for a blocker stays *after*, which is §4.4's word. *Waits on* collides with the Waiting group's phrases [F8].
3. The top entry: the root parent with the best key, else today's rule [F5].
4. A `design-first` part: picked as any design once its parts and blockers clear. Archives go one level per PR [F3].
