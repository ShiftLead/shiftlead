**How a design is reviewed here, before its PR merges** (Paul, 2026-10-15). A design is not landed on one fact-check: the build entries its PR files are in the grinders' lanes the moment it merges (a `build` settles 0 minutes, design §4.4 *A new entry settles before it is pickable*), so every round of review runs while the PR is open.
- **Fable reviews the written design first**: an agent on the Fable model, given this worktree's path and the design's sections and entries. It returns ranked findings, each with the problem, where it bites (file:line) and one recommendation. Adopt each finding by the template's three tiers. One that follows from what is written is yours to write in. A choice Paul might care about is a `steer` with your adoption as its `--default`. Taste or scope is an `ask`, with the entry's Status saying it waits on it.
- **Then Fable and Sonnet rounds alternate**, each a fresh agent told what earlier rounds settled and what changed since. Fable re-reviews the design as now written. Sonnet reviews it for gaps, contradictions with the rest of the design, and anything a builder would have to guess, ending `READY` or with findings. The loop ends when Sonnet says READY and Fable has no finding beyond a follow-on of the last adoption. Past three Fable rounds of follow-ons only, Fable may close its half of the loop; Sonnet still says READY. Record that override and why in the history line.
- **Each round is a line in `docs/design-history.md`** under the section: the model, the findings count, and what was adopted or overridden. The PR body lists the rounds. Only then the fact-check below, and the merge.
