# TD-1573: An open entry no lane takes is silent: a mechanical no-lane check

## What it does

- The home checks every open design or live-check entry against the lanes of the repo's teams, the same way a
  session's pick does. One that no lane would ever take is marked "no lane", with a few words naming the header
  field that keeps it out, such as "Owner techlead: the lanes that take design-first take owner:designer".
- A build tagged for a team that has no lane yet gets the same mark.
- The team card's kind bar, the Repo page, ao repo and the Start dialog all show such entries as "no lane" with
  those words, rather than counting them under design. The bar and Start's counts then differ only by what is
  meant to wait: entries settling or waiting, held by a decision, yours, needing a team, or taken by another team's,
  a teamless or an ended session's lane.
- When the fault is the entry's Owner, the session that reserved its id gets one quiet note once the mark has stood
  for 30 minutes, telling it how to fix the header.

## The key choices, and why

- A note after the entry lands, not a warning when it is filed. ShiftLead writes no ledger itself (agent-cadence's
  helpers do), and a handed entry's header is written later by a seat, so there is nothing to warn at. You took this
  default when asked.
- Builds are checked only for the team tag. A build whose owner no lane names is often on purpose (yours, or one
  needing an attended run), and the existing "the rest" count already shows it by owner.
- Any lane counts, even one held only by a session that has ended or belongs to no team.
- Entries you own, and entries held by a decision, an open entry or an open part, are never marked: what holds them
  is already said.
- The 30-minute wait lets a team start or a quick header fix happen before anyone is told.
- The note goes once per entry, and only for an Owner miss: a missing team or kind lane turns on which teams have
  sessions (one not started, one wound down over a day), not on the header. It is held back while someone has the
  entry claimed.

## What it rules out

- Nothing is refused at filing or on a header edit; the check only marks and tells.
- Teams that have never started are not read, since the home reads no team definition. Their work shows as no lane
  until their first start; Start's dialog names only what the planned lanes still leave.
- No note for an entry whose id was typed by hand, reserved by you, reserved long ago (past the last 200 ids), or
  reserved by a session since forgotten, nor for anything already marked when the check first runs.

## What you will see

- An amber "no lane" segment on the team card's kind bar and the Sessions rollup, after live check; on the card its
  hover lists each entry and why, and the lanes line ends "· 1 no lane: TD-058".
- On the Repo page, such entries in the "no lane takes these" fold rather than in the Design column.
- In ao repo, "1 no lane" on the first line and a line per entry with its words.
- In Start's confirm, "no lane: TD-058 (Owner techlead: …)" under the lane counts.
- On the Queue, a card made only of such entries reads blocked: no lane takes it.
