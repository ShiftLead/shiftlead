# TD-1804: A ledger-filing PR whose author's run ended has no owner: 19 stood open and unmerged

## What it does

A pull request that only files or changes ledger entries (or the board) is a filing PR. When one has been
open for a day and no live session holds it, it becomes a work order for the grinders, beside the orders
they already take for red, sync and lift PRs. The grinder who takes it closes it if what it files is already
on main or no longer true; otherwise it brings it up to date, has it fact-checked, prechecks and merges it.

## The key choices, and why

- A day, not an hour: a live author holds its PR by its claim or its branch, and a restarted run carries
  its claims on; the one author no test sees is one that has moved to other work, and a day lets it return.
- Every filing PR is fact-checked before the merge, even one with an old approval, since that approval
  read nothing main has gained since.
- A PR that also touches a held path stays the reader's, and is only marked, as today.
- A draft is how anyone, you included, says a filing PR is not ready; anything else is taken after a day.
- Filing orders are taken after red and lift orders and before a grinder's ledger entries, at Medium.
- A grinder never quietly drops one: it closes it with a comment and files what is left.

## What it rules out

- The home merging such PRs by itself: it cannot review the unreviewed ones or judge a stale one.
- Raising them to the techlead or to you: the lanes can do the work.
- Ordering every green PR nobody holds: a code PR left green may be unfinished.

## What you will see

- In a repo a team serves, stranded filing PRs disappear within a day or so of their author going away,
  merged or closed with a comment saying why.
- The Repo page marks such a PR with a quiet filing tag and whose order it is; nothing reaches your Inbox.
- The 19 PRs open today are triaged by hand on their own thread, not by this design.
