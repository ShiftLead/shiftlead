# TD-1829: Lift the hold could not be done, because no unattended session may lift your hold

## What it does

Pressing Lift the hold on an entry you are holding used to write a "go ahead" note for some session to act on
by removing your hold. No unattended session may remove a hold of yours, so nothing ever happened. Now the press
removes the hold itself, at once, as your own act: it is your button, so it counts as done by you. A question a
session sends you can also mark the answers that lift a hold, so choosing one of them lifts it on the spot.

## The key choices, and why

- Only two presses lift, and neither writes a note for a session: Lift the hold, and an answer a session's
  question marked as lifting. Any other answer you give on a held entry leaves the hold in place, so the entry
  stays out of the lanes while a session acts on your words. Where they mean the hold should go, that session
  asks you with a lifting answer.
- If an open question on the board still asks about that hold alone, the press is refused and points you to
  it, so you answer the question itself. A question shared with other held entries stays for them.
- Only the person the hold names can lift it: anyone else's press, or a lifting question about their hold, is refused.
- If a session has taken up your earlier answer about that hold, or holds the entry itself, the press is
  refused rather than pulling the work from under it, unless that session is the one that asked you to lift.
  Open that session instead: it is the way out.
- Changing the shared tool's rule so that sessions may lift on your written word was rejected: your press is
  already yours, and holds stay liftable only by you.

## What it rules out

- A question with no lifting answers never lifts, whatever its answers say; "may this go ahead?" needs no
  question at all, since Lift the hold asks it.
- An answer to a question whose asker has gone, a typed reply, or an answer on a closed question lifts nothing.
  A lifting answer whose hold was already gone is still delivered, saying so.
- While nobody has taken up an earlier answer of yours, Lift the hold still works, and the entry can be picked
  at once. That earlier answer's open order on the board is closed with the hold when it names the hold; one
  that does not name it, or is shared with other held entries, stays for a session to take.
- An entry that is yours to do has no Lift the hold, and every lift refuses it: your Done answers it.
- Until the shared tool's weekly check learns about these lifts (asked of it), it lists each one as merged
  outside the usual review.

## What you will see

Lift the hold and a lifting answer both say they lift the hold, and ask you to confirm; a refused press says why.
On the Queue, the Inbox and the Repo page, every Lift reads one mark this design sets on the entry, the answer bar
placing it (TD-1832); a session holding the entry does not hide it, and the rare refusal that brings (a hold line
someone else answered) is a refused press. On a stuck answer one press lifts and then tells the asker,
in an ordinary reply from you, that the hold was lifted or was already gone (TD-1879).
Where your answer on the board still waits for a session, the entry has no item, so its row on the Queue card
and the card's header offer Lift the hold first in their menu, from the Queue or the card's page alone. Not where
an open, unanswered question covers that entry alone (answer that instead), nor where a session is working on
the line, the entry or another entry the line covers. That hides some Lifts that would have worked, on purpose.
