# TD-1829: Lift the hold could not be done, because no unattended session may lift your hold

## What it does

Pressing Lift the hold on an entry you are holding used to write a "go ahead" note for some session to act on
by removing your hold. No unattended session may remove a hold of yours, so nothing ever happened. Now the press
removes the hold itself, at once, as your own act: it is your button, so it counts as done by you. A question a
session sends you can also mark the answers that lift a hold, so choosing one of them lifts it on the spot.

## The key choices, and why

- Only two presses lift: Lift the hold, and an answer a session's question marked as lifting. Any other answer
  you give on a held entry leaves the hold in place, so the entry stays out of the lanes while a session acts
  on your words. Where those words mean the hold should go, that session asks you with a lifting answer.
- Lift the hold writes no note for a session: there is nothing left to do.
- If an open question on the board still asks about that hold alone, the press is refused and points you to
  it, so you answer the question itself. A question shared with other held entries stays for them.
- Only the person the hold names can lift it; a press from anyone else is refused.
- If a session is already working on your earlier answer about that hold, the press is refused rather than
  pulling the work from under it, unless that session is the one that asked you to lift.
- Changing the shared tool's rule so that sessions may lift on your written word was rejected: your press is
  already yours, and holds stay liftable only by you.

## What it rules out

- A question with no lifting answers never lifts, whatever its answers say; "may this go ahead?" needs no
  question at all, since Lift the hold asks it.
- An answer to a question whose asker has gone, a typed reply, or an answer on a closed question lifts nothing.
  A lifting answer whose hold was already gone is still delivered, saying so.
- After Lift the hold the entry can be picked even while a session still acts on an earlier answer of yours
  about it; that earlier answer's work order stays.
- Until the shared tool's weekly check learns about these lifts (asked of it), it lists each one as merged
  outside the usual review.

## What you will see

The Lift the hold button and a lifting answer both say they lift the hold, and ask you to confirm. After the
press the entry leaves what needs you, and the session that asked reads that the hold is lifted.
