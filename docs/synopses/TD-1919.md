# TD-1919: The Focus terminal holds its pane at one grid, and the person resizes it on demand

## What it does

Every session's terminal is fixed at one size, its grid of columns and rows, from the moment it starts. Opening a
Focus page, reloading it, turning a phone, raising the keyboard or attaching from another window no longer resizes
the session. The page draws the terminal at the session's own grid: a smaller window scrolls across and shows the
bottom rows where Claude Code's input box sits, and a larger one leaves empty room. When you move to a new monitor
or window, one press, Fit to window, sets the session's grid to the window you are looking at.

## The key choices, and why

- Resizing a session while Claude Code is drawing is what cut off, dropped and repeated lines in the history.
  Measured on a real session: a width change repeated 5 to 13 paragraphs and a height change lost 3 to 4. With
  the size fixed, every paragraph was there once in every run.
- Fit to window waits until the session is idle with nothing of its own running, then resizes. It never
  interrupts the turn or stops the tool: each of those ways was measured and lost or repeated lines. Even at
  idle, a fit to a smaller window can still repeat or drop the last few paragraphs, once per fit. A shell
  fits at once. If the session stays busy for five minutes, the fit gives up with a note. A second press cancels it.
- A new session starts at your grid, 120 by 40 until you set your own. A fit on the desktop's own Focus page
  becomes that grid for later sessions. A phone's fit, or a popped-out window's, changes that one session only.
  A fit from one window leaves another window open on the same session drawing the new grid, scrolling or
  with empty room, until it fits too.
- A phone measures afresh at every press: it puts the keyboard and sheets down, waits for the screen to settle, then
  measures, so a fit never shrinks the session to a dozen rows. If the keyboard will not go down, it uses its last good
  measurement, or with none sends nothing and says to put the keyboard down and press again. Turning the phone while a
  fit waits cancels it. A tablet's wide page presses the same way, since its fit becomes your grid. A window too small
  or too large for a grid is refused with a note. A pinch still changes how many columns you see, without a new
  measurement.
- A read-only Focus can fit, since fitting types nothing, so a team's unattended session needs no Take over.

## What it rules out

- Any page, phone or reload resizing a session by itself.
- The terminal mark *resized by another client*, which no longer happens. *fitting when idle…* takes its place.
- Fitting while the tool is drawing, by interrupting it or by pausing it.

## What you will see

- A Focus terminal that keeps its size, with a scrollbar when the window is narrower than the session.
- Fit to window in the more menu and in the phone's sheet. Its tooltip names both sizes. It reads Cancel fit
  while it waits.
- Two builds: one makes sessions start at your grid and lets a fit resize them. The other draws the page at the
  session's grid and adds the control.
