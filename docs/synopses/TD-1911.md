# TD-1911: The person ends every design round: the techlead should judge routine designs, under the cadence's guidance

## What it does

When a design's review rounds finish, the cadence counts the reviews and says what comes next: another round, or
Go. A judge then confirms that answer or raises it to you. This design makes the team's techlead seat that judge.
A routine design the judge confirms goes ahead without asking you, and you get a short note saying so. A
far-reaching design still comes to you to decide, as every design does today.

## The key choices, and why

- The techlead judges, not decides. It confirms or raises the counted answer and never picks one of its own. It
  starts a fresh reviewer for each judgment, so the steers it answered on the design do not sway it.
- Where the techlead cannot judge, the cadence's default judge runs instead: when the team has no techlead, when
  you are its question seat, when the question was routed to you, when its tool cannot start the reviewer, or when
  it does not answer within two hours. No judging question ever waits on you.
- Far-reaching means the design changes who may act, answer, merge or decide for you: a role's rights, which seats
  a flow has, a permission or a delegation. The designer marks this from the passages it changes, not from which
  section they sit in. Any doubt counts as far-reaching.
- A list of files makes a design far-reaching however it was marked: the briefs, the built-in roles and flows, the
  glossary, and the org and team schema. The merge check holds a build touching them until you give your Go.
- To have every design come to you, routine ones too, you list every file there. That one line is your whole
  choice, so there is no new setting. This replaces the setting the entry first asked for.

## What it rules out

- A seat answering a far-reaching design for you. The board refuses such an answer from a seat today, so it waits
  for the director's delegation work in TD-1910.
- A judge's Go letting through a build that touches the far-reaching files.

## What you will see

- Fewer end-of-rounds cards: only far-reaching designs, or ones the judge raises, reach you.
- A short note for each routine design that went ahead.
- Two builds: the designer and techlead briefs (TD-1960), and the far-reaching file list (TD-1961). The briefs
  build's own Go is yours, since it changes the briefs.
