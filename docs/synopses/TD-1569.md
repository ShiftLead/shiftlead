# TD-1569: A team whose members wound down cannot be started from its card while a seat is busy

## What it does

A seat (the techlead, a fact-checker, an on-call manager) comes on a question and closes itself once it is idle and its
work is pushed (a seat that comes on a question, the techlead or a fact-checker, lingers about fifteen minutes for the
next one). Until now, if one was still working when you pressed Start on a team whose members had stopped, the whole
start was refused, and the refusal said the seat was idle and offered to close it mid-merge. Now your Start goes ahead:
it starts the members and leaves the busy seat running, and the seat closes on its own once it is idle and pushed; one
holding unpushed work stays until its work is pushed, then closes itself. A seat counts as busy whenever a start would
not close it: working, waiting on a prompt, running a background command, or idle with work not yet pushed.
When a start is still refused, it says why for each session in the way, in the words the team's own end-of-run check
uses, and offers a fix for that one session only: Close it if it is idle and clean, otherwise let it finish or kill that
one session. It never offers to stop the whole team, which would end the busy seat too.

## The key choices, and why

- The same Start button, not a separate "Start members" act: a seat is busy for minutes, so a second button
  would exist for that window alone. Your wording, "Start members", is still open as the label.
- The card and the press read one shared check, so the card offers Start only where pressing it would work. The
  card can still be out of date for a moment; then the start is refused with its reason, and you press again.
- Wind down and Stop now stay beside Start, so you can still end the seat yourself.
- The automatic restart of a wound-down team is unchanged: it waits until the seat has gone.
- Only your own start goes ahead of a busy seat; a start made by a session leaves nothing out, as before.

## What it rules out

- Closing or replacing a seat that is busy: a start never cuts off a seat's work.
- A refusal that offers to stop the whole team, or to kill your own session or a suspended one.
- Leaving out a seat the team's definition renamed or dropped: that start is refused, as today, with its Apply
  line.

## What you will see

  it closes first and which seats it leaves running.
- After the start, a line saying which seat was left running and what it was doing.
- A refused start that lists each session in the way, what it is doing, and its own fix.
