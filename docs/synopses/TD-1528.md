# TD-1528: A bought round's closed question holds its entries as waiting on the round, not on you

## What it does

When you answer One more round on a design, the session that carries the answer closes your question and
files the new round. The Queue is meant to show the design's entries as waiting on that round, not on you.
It did not, because the close was worded "see" where the Queue looks for "carried to". Now both wordings
count, and the close command writes the right words itself so they cannot drift again.

## The key choices, and why

- The Queue accepts "see" as well as "carried to". That alone mends the questions already closed with "see",
  so no closed line has to be rewritten by hand.
- The close command gains an option that writes the words for the session, refusing an entry that does not
  exist. Typed words are what drifted.
- A close that points at an older, unrelated entry still holds nothing. Only an entry filed on or after your
  answer counts as its work, as before.
- Lift the hold stays in the menu on such an item: the link can be wrong, and it is your way out.

## What it rules out

- No change to the shared cadence tool's own close command; it serves repos without ShiftLead and is left as
  it is.
- No new waiting rule: the wait still has no time limit of its own and ends when the round's entry closes.

## What you will see

After you answer One more round, the design's card reads "answered, waiting on" the designer or the round,
with no question asking you again, until the round ends and raises its own question.
