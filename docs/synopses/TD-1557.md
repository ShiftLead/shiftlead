# TD-1557: A board decide line's Answer… cannot write the person's words as Decided, and no code fills a Hold slot

## What it does

- An answer with a gap for your words, like "Hold: <note>" on a design's end-of-rounds item, is a form: the same
  kind of answer as a look's "Not right: <what>".
- A form is drawn as its head and an ellipsis (Hold…). Pressing it opens a box with "Hold: " already typed, and
  sending decides the line. If you typed over the head it is put back, so "drop Dismiss" lands as
  "Hold: drop Dismiss" and never as a different answer.
- On a board line, a Reply or Answer… that starts with a form's head ("Hold: drop Dismiss") decides the line
  too. Any other words stay a reply, and the line still waits on you. The box says so under it.
- A form is never sent with its placeholder: "Hold:" alone or "Hold: <note>" is refused on every road, on a
  board, on a ledger entry's row, in a mail reply, and when a session picks an answer.

## The key choices, and why

- Answer… decides only when its words start with a form's head, so a question back to the asker stays a
  reply. The other road, where anything typed into Answer… decides, was put to the techlead, who chose
  this one.
- The head is matched in any case, so "hold: x" lands as "Hold: x".
- The server decides which words fill a form, through the board format's own library; the page parses nothing.
- The form is defined once, in ledgerboard, the library shiftlead and agent-cadence both carry. Its change
  goes to ledgerboard's team, and the session's own board tool goes to agent-cadence's. The shiftlead work
  waits until that ledgerboard release is brought here.
- A look's "Not right" keeps its hand-off to the techlead; a Hold hands nothing on.

## What it rules out

- A board line decided with a placeholder ("Decided: Hold: <note>").
- Your typed Hold going nowhere while the line stays due, as on 2026-11-07.
- A form marked as the default answer on a board item or in mail, or two forms with one head on a board item.

## What you will see

- On an end-of-rounds item: Go, One more round and Hold…, on the Inbox row and its Queue card.
- After you send "Hold: <your note>", the item shows the line landing, then reads decided with your words, and
  the board line carries them.
