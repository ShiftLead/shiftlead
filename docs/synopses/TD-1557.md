# TD-1557: A board decide line's Answer… cannot write the person's words as Decided, and no code fills a Hold slot

## What it does

- An answer with a gap for your words, like "Hold: <note>" on a design's end-of-rounds item, becomes a form, the
  same kind of answer as a look's "Not right: <what>".
- A form is drawn as its head and an ellipsis (Hold…). Pressing it opens the word box with "Hold: " already
  typed; it is never sent with the placeholder in it.
- On a board line, words you type into Answer… or Reply that start with a form's head ("Hold: drop Dismiss")
  decide the line, written as its Decided answer. Any other words stay a reply, and the line still waits on you.
- On mail, a form opens the reply box the same way, and a reply that is only the placeholder is refused.

## The key choices, and why

- Answer… decides only when its words fill one of the line's forms. That keeps a question back to the asker a
  reply. The other road, where anything typed into Answer… on a decide line decides it, is set aside: the techlead
  answered the steer, Form only.
- The head is matched in any case and written as the form spells it, so "hold: x" lands as "Hold: x".
- The agent decides which words fill a form, reading the board's own format library; the page parses nothing.
- The form is defined once, in ledgerboard, the library both shiftlead and agent-cadence carry. Its change is
  handed to ledgerboard's own team, and the shiftlead work waits until that release is brought here.

## What it rules out

- A board line decided with a placeholder ("Decided: Hold: <note>").
- Your typed Hold going nowhere while the line stays due, as on 2026-11-07.
- A page-side copy of the board format's rules.

## What you will see

- On an end-of-rounds item: Go, One more round and Hold…, on the Inbox row and its Queue card.
- After you send "Hold: <your note>", the item reads decided at once, and the board line carries your words.
