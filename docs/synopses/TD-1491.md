# TD-1491: /shiftlead-feedback: a procedure that reviews seats' transcripts for machinery friction

## What it does

It makes the review you ran by hand on 2026-11-06 a procedure you start from a session you drive. You pick
the seats and a window. A command condenses each seat's conversations in that window into short text, and
one fresh agent per piece looks for where shiftlead, the cadence or ledgerboard got in the seat's way. The
session merges what they find, checks it against the open and archived ledgers of all three repos, groups
it by cause and shows you a report. Nothing is filed until you answer each group.

## The key choices, and why

- The procedure ships with ShiftLead and prints with one command, with a thin skill in this repo pointing at
  it, as the team skill does. The techlead agreed to this over a separate docs file and script.
- The condensing reads through each tool's adapter, so no tool's file layout leaks into the core and a seat
  on a container node is read through the home. It keeps prompts to three lines, the seat's own words whole,
  and failed calls; successful calls become a count; thoughts and subagents' own steps are dropped. A
  contradiction between docs, or a step repeated by hand, is found only where the seat says so.
- A finding counts as known only when an entry's title and Why name the same symptom, checked by reading the
  entry, never by a keyword alone. Ids the seat itself named are checked first, and entries in open PRs too.
  A fixed entry whose symptom came back after its resolved day is filed again as recurred.
- Each finding has a fixed shape: the seat, the days seen, how often, what, evidence, why, the fix and the
  area. The report gives a noise figure for each agent, each seat and the run.
- Evidence quotes stay in the report, since a seat may work on a private repo and a ledger may be public.
- The condensed text goes only to the agents your session starts, on your session's account, which may
  not be the seat's: a Gemini seat's text is read by Claude. A seat's directory may also hold your own
  sessions; the session lists every conversation first so you can leave those out.
- The run is recorded by its filing PR's window, so the next run starts where it ended.

## What it rules out

- A seat running it on a schedule: the last step is your approval.
- Running it anywhere but a shiftlead checkout at the home.
- Reports from other people's installs: that is phase 2 (TD-1718), after a privacy design round.
- Gemini seats' failed calls are not shown until a recorded failing call lands (TD-1719).

## What you will see

A report in the session's chat: the window, the seats, counts by outcome, the noise, then each group with
its proposed entry. You answer each group file, edit, merge or drop, and say which notes on known entries to
write. Shiftlead's entries then arrive in one PR titled with the window; another repo's go to its question
seat; problems with the coding tool itself are listed for you to report.
