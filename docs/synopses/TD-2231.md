# TD-2231: An early question from a session counts on the Queue tab while its card shows it grey with no count

## What it does

A question that waits on other work is drawn grey on its Queue card, with no count of its own. Some of these were
still counted in the Queue tab's number: the person saw a 1 on the tab and nothing on any card said where.

Now everything the Queue draws grey this way counts nowhere. That covers a question from a session about an entry
that is asked early, one about an entry waiting on its own open work, and a board question covering such entries.
It is in no Needs you count, no top bar number and no side rail count. Once the work it waits on closes, it counts
as any question does.

## The key choices, and why

- The same rule for a session's question as for a board question: the design already left an early board
  question out of every count, and the person cannot tell the two apart on a card.
- The question still takes up its sender's place among the open questions a session may have. That limit is about
  how many questions a session has open, not about the person's counts, so the earlier reason for counting it
  does not hold.

## What it rules out

- A number on the Queue tab that no card explains.
- Counting a waiting question so a session's open questions stay in view: it stays drawn on its card, which is
  where the person reads it.

## What you will see

The Queue tab's number and the top bar no longer go up for a grey question on a card. The card itself looks as
it does today: the question in the grey section, answerable early where it is asked early.
