# TD-2231: An early question from a session counts on the Queue tab while its card shows it grey with no count

## What it does

A question that waits on other work is drawn grey on its Queue card, with no count of its own. Some of these were
still counted: the person saw a 1 on the Queue tab and nothing on any card said where.

Now everything marked this way counts nowhere: a session's question about an entry asked early or waiting on its
own open work, and a board question covering such entries. It is in no Needs you count, top bar number, Sessions
rollup, team card's "asked you" line or side rail. Once the work it waits on closes, it counts as any question.

## The key choices, and why

- The rule is by the mark, wherever the item is drawn, so the person cannot meet the same question counted on one
  page and not on another. Every page's top bar, the first paint of the Sessions page among them, reads the marks.
- The same rule for a session's question as for a board question: the person cannot tell them apart on a card.
- A board question covering several entries stays grey and uncounted if any one of them waits, whatever the others.
- The home's own reads stay as they are. The question still holds its sender's place among its open questions,
  still quiets a stuck notice about its entry, and an open one is still told to your phone. Whether the phone
  should be told is a choice put to you; the default is that it is told as today.
- When the ledger cannot be read, such a question counts until the next good read: a count errs toward showing.
- The hover text on the numbers stays as it is, since a grey item is still an open question.

## What it rules out

- A number on the Queue tab that no card explains.
- Counting a waiting question so a session's open questions stay in view: it stays drawn on its card.

## What you will see

The Queue tab's number, the top bar and a team's "asked you" line no longer go up for a grey question on a card.
The top bar's split between Inbox and Queue now matches the Inbox page's from the first paint: a board question
about an entry is counted on that entry's card there too.
The card itself looks as it does today: the question in the grey section, answerable early where it is asked early.
