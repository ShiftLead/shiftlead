# TD-2215: Whether a flow's sessions still read the person's own briefs from their cadence layer

## What it does

Your own additions to a role's brief, kept in your cadence layer repo, keep reaching sessions once teams run on
flows. A session started for a stage, an audit, a job, the tech lead or the sweeper reads the layer file named for
the role it runs in, under its own rules heading. This is already how the code works; the design now says so. The
seat that answers permission prompts reads none, since it runs on the director's brief, which has no such place.

Every stage, audit, job and review session is also told which part wins when they disagree: the repo's rules over
yours, both over the rest, the stage's brief among it, and neither may take away what it is told never to do. A
team member in the reviewer role gets the same sentence, since it shares that template.

## The key choices, and why

- The layer keeps its job beside the Briefs page. A brief there, or a flow's own, is one org's or one flow's
  choice of what a stage is told; your layer follows you to every org and repo, and adds to whichever brief a
  stage picks.
- Files are named by role, not by the stage's kind, so one name serves a team member and a flow's session.
- Your rules and the repo's win over a stage's brief, as they win over a team member's role text today.
- Your start-of-entry, Start session and director preference texts are read as before.
- When the grinder role becomes builder, a layer file still named for grinder is not read in builder's place.
  Each layer holding only that file gives the session one line saying so and asking for the rename. Reading the old
  name as a fallback would be quieter but keeps an old name alive; this notice is the one place it stays.
- A rename of that file takes effect at the session's next start, not mid-run.

## What it rules out

- The Briefs page replacing your layer for briefs.
- Silent loss of your rules when a role is renamed.
- Any change to how the director's preferences are read.

## What you will see

- A stage's session carries your layer's text for its role, as a team member's does today.
- After the rename, a builder session whose layer still has only the old grinder file shows one line saying it was
  not read and should be renamed.
