# TD-2215: Whether a flow's sessions still read the person's own briefs from their cadence layer

## What it does

Your own additions to a role's brief, kept in your cadence layer repo, keep reaching every session once teams run
on flows. A session started for a stage, an audit or a built-in seat reads the layer file named for the role it
runs in (designer, builder, reviewer, auditor, the tech lead, the sweeper), placed under its *Your own rules*,
before the repo's rules. This is already how the code works; the design now says so.

Those sessions are also told, as a team member already is, which part wins when they disagree: the repo's rules
over yours, and yours over ShiftLead's, while neither may take away what the brief says never to do.

## The key choices, and why

- The layer keeps its job beside the Briefs page. A brief on that page, or a flow's own, is one org's or one
  flow's choice of what a stage is told; your layer follows you to every org and repo, and adds to whichever brief
  a stage picks.
- Files are named by role, not by the stage's kind, so one name serves both a team member and a flow's session.
- Your start-of-entry, Start session and director preference texts are read as before; a flow changes none of them.
- When the grinder role becomes builder, a layer file still named for grinder is not read in builder's place.
  Instead the session is told, where your rules would be, that the file was skipped and should be renamed, so the
  miss is visible to the session and to you. The alternative, reading the old name as a fallback, is quieter but
  keeps an old name alive.

## What it rules out

- The Briefs page replacing your layer for briefs.
- Silent loss of your rules when a role is renamed.
- Any change to how the director's preferences are read.

## What you will see

- A stage's session shows your layer's text for its role in its start context, as a team member's does today.
- After the rename, a builder session whose layer still has only the old grinder file shows one line saying it was
  not read and should be renamed. Today your layer has no such file, so nothing changes for you now.
