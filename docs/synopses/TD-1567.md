# TD-1567: An idle interactive holder reads 'stalled' on its Queue card, and its word does not open the session

## What it does

The Queue stops calling your own idle session a stuck team. A card row that a session you started by hand is holding
reads "with you in" and that session's name, never "stalled", however long the session sits idle. Words on a card that
stand for a session ("with you in", "held by", "stalled") become links that open that session, as its name pill does.
And once a design is finished (its builds filed and your go given), the session that ran the design conversation no
longer holds the design entry just because it still sits on the design's branch.

## The key choices, and why

- Your own session is never "stalled", even when an unattended worker also holds the row: you are in the work and
  can see it. "Stalled" stays a team's word, for its techlead.
- On a row that needs nothing from you, an open pull request still shows its own words ("in review", "failing"),
  with your session's pill beside it; "with you in" replaces only "held by". A failing pull request held only by your
  idle session drops "being fixed", since nothing is fixing it.
- "Needs you" is never a link: what makes it, such as a stopped session's question, already has its own Open.
- A finished design lets go of the claim the system inferred from its branch name. The techlead took this as the
  default over keeping the claim until the session leaves the branch, since keeping it is the problem you reported.
- A session that revises a design after your go keeps it by claiming it explicitly; that is never undone.

## What it rules out

- A grey "with you" item on the card for work that asks nothing of you: the row's word is enough.
- Starting a session from a card whose top entry is a finished design does not mark the card In progress; the
  session holds nothing until it claims an entry or switches to a build's branch, though Open session still finds it.
- A design whose builds sit in another repository's ledger keeps its claim, since the home cannot see them.

## What you will see

TD-1529's card would have read "with you in td-1529" instead of "stalled", and after your go it would have read
"waiting" on its builds. Pressing a holder's word on a card or its header opens that session's terminal.
