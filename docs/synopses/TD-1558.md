# TD-1558: The Queue holds still, and a card has a page of its own

## What it does

The Queue stops moving while you read it. When something changes, a word or a count updates where it already is.
When a card would move, arrive or leave, nothing moves. A bar at the top counts the waiting changes instead:
"1 new ask for you · 2 cards moved — Refresh". Press **Refresh** and the moves happen, the cards sliding to their
new places so you can follow them. The card your pointer is on does not change at all until you move away.

Answering moves to a page of its own for each card. On the Queue each item is one line, with at most its
one-press answer (such as **Go**) and a link to answer it on its card. The card's page has every control, and it
never re-orders. While you are typing an answer, nothing on the page changes.

## The key choices, and why

- Values update at once, but positions wait for you. This is the split that sites like X use for "new posts".
  Positions are what make you press the wrong thing.
- One-press answers stay on the Queue: the recommended answer, Done on an act, Allow and Deny on a permission.
  Now that the list holds still, they cannot move under your press.
- Anything with a box or a choice goes to the card's page. The Queue then has no answer box, so words can no
  longer reach the wrong card.
- Old links (Telegram, push) still work, and lead to the card's page.

## What it rules out

- Cards jumping while you read or press.
- The Queue as a place to type answers.

## What you will see

- A bar above the groups: "live · up to date", or what is waiting, with **Refresh**.
- A small *held* mark on the card under your pointer.
- *gone — refresh* on an item that was answered somewhere else.
- **Open card ›** on each card, and **Answer on its card ›** on each item.
- A page for each card, with **‹ Queue** to go back to where you were.
