# TD-2222: A design the person has approved keeps a review record that says the decision is still pending

## What it does

When you press Go on a design, your press lifts the hold on the design and its builds and closes the question.
No session is sent after it, so nothing writes your Go into the design's review record. Sixty-two records still
lack your approval although you gave it, most of them saying the decision is pending.

This design gives that job to the home's daily sweep, which already lifts waiting entries. Once a day it looks
for designs you approved whose record still says pending. It fills in who gave the Go and on what day, in one
pull request that a builder reviews and merges, as it does the sweep's other pull requests. It keeps any note the
record already holds about how the rounds ended. Its first run catches up on the sixty-two.

It also moves one step earlier. The fixes made after the last review round used to be copied into the builds
only after your Go, by a session that never came. Now the designer writes them into the builds when it asks you
the question, each marked as not yet reviewed, so a build you approve already carries them.

## The key choices, and why

- The home writes the record instead of sending a designer a work order. Two lines of record are not worth a
  session and its fact-check, and the sweep already reads the ledger every day.
- The record waits for the next daily pass and a builder's merge. Your press cannot write it, because the rule
  that lets your press land without review allows only the board and the ledger.
- The unread fixes go into the builds when the question is asked, not after your answer. If you answer One more
  round, that round reads them anyway. If you answer Go, nothing is left to do.

## What it rules out

- A session sent to you, or to the designer, after every Go.
- Your press touching the review record.
- A build becoming pickable before the fixes that concern it are written into it.

## What you will see

- Nothing new to press. Go works as it does today.
- Once a day at most, a small pull request from the home that fills in approvals, merged by a builder.
- The end-of-rounds question still lists the unread fixes. Each build already carries them, marked for its builder
  to check.
