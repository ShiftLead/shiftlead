# TD-2222: A design the person has approved keeps a review record that says the decision is still pending

## What it does

When you press Go on a design, your press lifts the hold on the design and its builds and closes the question.
No session is sent after it, so nothing writes your Go into the design's review record. Sixty-one records lack
it although you gave it.

The home's daily sweep, which already lifts waiting entries, takes the job. Once a day it finds designs you
approved whose record lacks the Go, and fills in who gave it and on what day, keeping any note the record holds,
in one pull request a builder checks and merges. Its first run catches up on the sixty-one. It counts a lift
answered Go, go ahead (Go now, Lift the hold) or Yes; a design that asks you a yes-or-no question gives it
answers of its own, so another answer is never taken for a Go.

The fixes made after the last review round now go into the builds when the designer asks you the question, each
marked as not yet reviewed, so a build you approve already carries them. A later round brings them into line
with the design as it then stands, and a Hold revision sets the record back to pending for your next Go.

## The key choices, and why

- The home writes the record, not a session sent after each Go: two lines are not worth a session.
- The record waits for the next daily pass and a builder's merge, since your press may touch only the board and
  the ledger without review.
- If a record pull request is closed unmerged, its records are left alone for thirty days, as the sweep does.
- A Go you gave on an old question that names no hold cannot be lifted by a session. A builder turns it into a
  new Go question on the design and its builds, so you press once more for those.
- This departs from the shared cadence, which still sends a session after the Go. The briefs say so until the
  cadence changes, and a board item asks you to propose the change there, which no session here can do.

## What it rules out

- A session sent to you, or to the designer, after every Go.
- Your press touching the review record, or a session lifting a hold you answered by override.
- A build becoming pickable before the fixes that concern it are written into it.

## What you will see

- At most one small pull request a day from the home that fills in approvals, merged by a builder.
- A board item asking you to propose this change to the shared cadence.
- Rarely, a fresh Go question where an old one named no hold.
- The end-of-rounds question still lists the unread fixes; each build already carries them.
