# TD-1564: A team's Stop is undone by the tick: rule 3 fills a stopped team's question seat for mail waiting on it

## What it does

When you stop a team, or its stop time stops it, its seats now stay closed until you start the team again,
whatever is waiting for them. Before, a question sent to the team's question seat started that seat again on
the next tick, so your Stop did not hold. That is why ao-grind's fact-check seat came back six times on
7 November. Questions sent to a held seat wait in its mailbox and are read when the team starts again.

## The key choices, and why

- The hold looks at how the team ended: nobody in it is running, and the last thing that ended it was you
  (a Stop, a Close or Kill of its last session, or a Wind down its sessions left without saying they were
  out of work) or its stop time. The tick closing a seat
  between questions does not count as an ending, so it never undoes your word.
- A team that ended any other way still has its seats filled as now: one whose members finished their
  work, one that crashed, or one made only of question seats that the tick closed between questions (if
  you closed its idle seat yourself, it holds). Asking another team a question keeps working for them.
- A question to a held seat lands and waits; it is not sent on to the techlead or to you. You chose this
  when asked.
- The sender is told at once that the question waits for the team's Start. The seat's card and the team's
  Inbox row show how many questions wait.
- A question with a time limit still expires on time, as on any closed seat.

## What it rules out

- The home starting a seat of a team you stopped.
- A held seat taking work handed in for any team while another team's techlead could take it.
- Questions starting a team on their own: they wait for your Start.

## What you will see

The seat's card reads *held: team stopped by you — 2 questions wait for Start*. The team's Inbox row adds
*2 questions wait on fact-check-ao-1*. For a team you stopped it sits under Later, not counted. For one
stopped by its stop time it sits under Needs you, and Telegram tells you once. The row's Start starts the
team, and the waiting questions are read.
