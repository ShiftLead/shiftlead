# TD-2240: Pressing Go or Not yet on a held question leaves a change the weekly check calls unreviewed

## What it does

Two of your presses land changes the weekly check has no rule for: Go or Not yet on a held question, where the
home adds the question's line and answers it in one change, and Go now on a design, which lifts the design and its
builds together. The check lists each as merged without review. The design now says so plainly: until agent-cadence
learns both changes, the weekly check lists these presses as merged outside the usual path, and each is your press,
never a bypass.

It also corrects two sentences: one said such a change already passes as an answer to a hold, and one said a plain
lift of a hold had no rule yet, though it now has one.

## The key choices, and why

- The press stays one change. Splitting it into an add and then an answer does not help: the add alone passes no
  rule either, since a question line carries its detail.
- The fix belongs to agent-cadence, which owns the weekly check. Agent-cadence has no seat to take a question, so
  a line on your board asks you to file it there, with the text to paste written on the entry. The text names
  each new rule, so this repo can tell when it has landed.
- A build, TD-2271, waits until both named rules are in agent-cadence, then moves this repo onto them and tests
  that each press passes the check.

## What it rules out

- Calling your press a bypass in the weekly read in the meantime.
- Waiting for a reviewed pull request: your press is its own review, so you accept these presses listed as merged
  outside the usual path until agent-cadence's change is in.

## What you will see

- A new line on your board asking you to file the entry in agent-cadence.
- Until it lands, your Go, Not yet and Go now presses in the weekly list of changes merged outside the usual
  path; afterwards, listed with the other presses the home lands for you.
