# TD-2240: Pressing Go or Not yet on a held question leaves a change the weekly check calls unreviewed

## What it does

When you press Go or Not yet on a held question, the home adds the question's line and answers it in one change.
The weekly check that lists changes merged without review has no rule for that change, so it lists your own press
as if it had skipped review. The design now says so plainly: until agent-cadence learns this change, the weekly
check lists each such press as merged outside the usual path, and it is your press, never a bypass.

It also corrects a sentence that said such a change already passes as an answer to a hold. It does not.

## The key choices, and why

- The press stays one change. Splitting it into an add and then an answer does not help: the add alone passes no
  rule either, since a question line carries its detail.
- The fix belongs to agent-cadence, which owns the weekly check. Agent-cadence has no seat to take a question, so
  a line on your board asks you to file it there, with the text to paste written on the entry.
- A build, TD-2271, waits for that change, then moves this repo onto it and tests that your press passes the
  check; it looks again every few weeks until the change is there.

## What it rules out

- Calling your press a bypass in the weekly read in the meantime.
- A reviewed pull request for each press: you accept the press listed as merged outside the usual path until
  agent-cadence's change is in, since the press is its own review.

## What you will see

- A new line on your board asking you to file the entry in agent-cadence.
- Until it lands, your Go and Not yet presses on held questions in the weekly list of changes merged outside the
  usual path; afterwards, listed with the other presses the home lands for you.
