# TD-1718: /shiftlead-feedback phase 2: reports from other installs, after a privacy design round (TD-1491)

## What it does

Someone who runs ShiftLead but cannot push to its repo can still send their seats' friction here. The same
procedure runs at their install in a report mode: it reads their seats, checks the findings against the three
public ledgers by fresh shallow clones, and writes a report on their machine, which they read and submit
themselves through an issue form on the public repo. At your home, a run reads the open reports beside your
seats, and its PR closes them.

## The key choices, and why

- The mode is chosen by the install: no registered repo pointing at the public repo on GitHub, or no push
  right there, means report mode. A run in your own clone stays as it is and warns that its filings are public.
- A report carries only short findings (what happened, in the agents' words, its fix, counts, days) and
  counts on known entries, with the last public build it ran. Never evidence quotes, transcript text, seat
  names, the window's dates or a commit of the person's own.
- Code scrubs every name the install knows (sessions, teams, projects, repos, hosts, the person's own names),
  plus web addresses, emails, paths, long ids and other repos' entry numbers, then runs agent-cadence's
  publish check. Names spelled another way and private work described in plain words get past it, so the
  person's own read is the last check, and the session says so.
- The person answers submit as is, edit or drop; only then are they given the form's address. The session
  sends nothing, and never changes the text by hand.
- Here, a report is a stranger's text: a command parses it, drops anything outside the fixed fields, scrubs it
  again and cuts it, so the session never reads an issue raw. You pick at most five reports a run.
- A note on a known entry counts the installs that reported it, never their self-reported counts.
- There is no opt-in setting: nothing leaves an install unless its person runs it, approves and submits.
  You confirmed that on 2026-11-13.

## What it rules out

- Any automatic sending, aggregator or labelled queue: the channel is one issue form.
- Recording what a reporter sent: a later run may propose a finding again.
- Reports from a build the person patched: only the last public commit is named.

## What you will see

Issues titled feedback report on the public repo, each the reporter's. In your own runs, a listing of the
open reports by number, login, age and finding count, your pick of up to five, their findings grouped with
your seats', and closes or comments on each in the run's PR. Builds TD-1794, TD-1795 and TD-1796.
