# TD-2182: Check the Queue's live updates, then let the person turn them on or off

## What it does

First a check: the cards you saw change while the bar counted waiting updates were values (titles, priority pills,
state words, counts and ages), which the Queue patches in place by design. Moves, arrivals and leavings waited for
Refresh, as they should. Nothing was broken.

Then a switch at the right end of the Queue's bar: Held or Live. Held is today's page, which holds still until you
press Refresh. Live draws each update as it comes, so the page stays current for someone watching the work move.

## The key choices, and why

- Held is the default. A card moving under a press is what made the page hold still in the first place, so a page
  for answering cards stays still unless you choose otherwise.
- In Live the card you are pointing at, touching or typing in keeps its place on the screen and its values wait,
  while the rest of the page is drawn around it, at the top of the page too. No card you are on slides. When you
  let go, its changes are drawn at once.
- Live never moves what you are pressing: a draw that would land while a press is under way, or while cards are
  sliding, waits until it ends. After a draw a touch begun in the next half second is ignored, so a finger never
  lands on a card that just moved under it; a mouse click or a key is never ignored by that, and during the brief
  slide only a press on a card that is itself sliding is ignored. After Refresh, every press during its slide is
  ignored, as today.
- Live never closes an open menu or box.
- The switch is remembered in the browser, not in the settings file, since a screen you watch and a phone you
  answer on want different things. A browser that remembers nothing is Held.
- The bar's words become connected and not connected, so the word live names the switch alone.
- Held is still the page's pause for accessibility: Live is your own choice and Held, on the bar, stops it.

## What it rules out

- No switch on a card's own page: you answer items there, and your pointer is nearly always on its one card.
- No setting on the Settings page, and no shared setting across browsers.
- Live is not instant push: it draws at the Queue's usual re-reads, a second or two after a change.

## What you will see

The Queue's bar ends in two buttons, Held and Live, with Held pressed. In Held the bar counts what is waiting and
offers Refresh, as today. In Live it reads connected, drawn as it comes, never counts, and shows no Refresh. One
build, TD-2197, makes it, and its help words say why you might want each.
