# TD-1899: An opened Recently completed line shows who built, reviewed and merged each entry

## What it does

When you open a line in the Queue's Recently completed group, each entry gets one quiet line under its resolution
text saying who worked it: which sessions built it, which model reviewed it and with what verdict, and who merged
it. For example: built by grinder-ao-2 · reviewed by sonnet, passed · merged by techlead-ao-1. It lets you see at a
glance that the flow ran as it should.

## The key choices, and why

- The record is the closing merge commit's own message. The cadence's merge step writes who built, reviewed,
  prechecked and merged into it, so nothing new is stored, and the home reads it with the ledger history it
  already reads: no extra history log and no cache, only one small read of the repo's precheck setting.
- Only that closing commit is read. A design whose rounds landed in several PRs, or an entry archived by a PR of
  its own, shows who made that last PR, and the line then opens "closed in #n by …" in place of "built by", since
  whoever archived it did not build it.
- Four oddities are drawn in the warning colour: no review, no precheck (only in a repo that sets a precheck),
  merged past the gate, and a blocked review that no later review passed. A blocked review followed by a pass is
  the flow working, and stays quiet.
- The merger is named as the commit writes it, so your own merge reads with your name, not "you".
- A review's model is shown only where the review line has one of the cadence's own shapes; any free-text or
  hand-typed review line reads as "an unnamed reviewer".
- The precheck is judged against the repo's current setting, so an entry closed before the repo set a precheck
  reads "no precheck". This is accepted rather than reading the setting at every closing commit.

## What it rules out

- No line, and no "missing" note, for an entry closed by a direct push or by a merge the cadence did not write.
- No mark for a builder who merged their own work: a doc-only PR merges on its author's gate.
- A kept card, one still waiting on you, draws none; its line does once it goes back.
- No "built by" where the commit names no session: that is your own work.

## What you will see

On an opened Recently completed line, a small grey line under each entry, the oddities in the warning colour. Two
builds do it: the home's reading first, then the Queue's drawing.
