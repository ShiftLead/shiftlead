# TD-1476: A seat as an ordered pool of members: work goes to the first member able to take it

## What it does

A seat that answers questions can be declared as an ordered pool of two or more profiles, for example Gemini
then Sonnet, in place of its one profile. A question to a member that cannot answer (limited, gated, stalled or
unfillable) goes to the first able member after it, before it leaves the seat for the techlead or you, as
TD-1443's routing around does today.

## The key choices, and why

- Each member is a separate seat record, so the design stays simple: the first keeps the seat's name, the
  others are named after it with a number (qa-ao-1, then qa-ao-1-p2). Everything that names the seat today
  names its first member. Each member has its own mailbox; members share no claims.
- Only the first member starts live; the later ones are written held and filled only when a question waits
  on them, so a backup runs only while the members before it cannot answer. Their fills count toward the same
  six-an-hour ceiling as every other seat.
- A pool is a fallback, never a way to share the load: a working member counts as able and keeps its
  questions. A member whose mailbox is full, or that already holds an open question from the same sender, is
  passed over like an unable one.
- Questions move forward only, never back to an earlier member; one that is able again takes new questions,
  and nothing moved comes back. Your own question to an unable member lands, and the tick moves it on; it
  never comes back to you.
- A reader's question about a PR, and an entry's envelope, never move within a pool: they keep today's roads.
- A changed pool reaches a running team member by member: a member added at the end starts held, one removed
  sits out, and one whose profile changed is relaunched or taken at its next fill. A reorder is just profile
  changes in place.
- A profile may repeat, and any adapter's profile is allowed.

## What it rules out

- Pools only on question-answering seats: not on the techlead or the manager (the techlead's pool is a later
  design), and not on any other trigger. No fallback models beyond the pool, as you chose on TD-1443.
- A pooled seat holds no review stage, since a review has one reader; a team whose flow would need one is
  shown as not followable. That waits for a design of its own.
- A seat that exists to measure one model has no pool: the fact-check trial's seat stays one Gemini seat.

## What you will see

Each member is a seat card under its team on the Sessions page. Members after the first carry a backup mark:
live, fact-checker · backup for qa-ao-1; on call, on call · backup 1 of 1 — comes when qa-ao-1 cannot answer.
The first member's card is unmarked. An unable member's routed-around line names the member its questions go
to, for example its questions go to qa-ao-1-p2 while it cannot answer.
