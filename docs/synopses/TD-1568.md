# TD-1568: A seat taken over and not handed back is easy to miss: colour Hand back and the session's card

## What it does

A team member you took over and have not handed back stops part of its team's work, and today its card
looks like any of your own sessions. This design marks such a member in orange wherever the team is read:
- its card is tinted orange with an orange border, and reads *taken over* where it said *interactive*;
- a team member's small card is tinted too, with a line such as *taken over 3h 12m ago*;
- the team's header counts *1 taken over*, naming the members on hover;
- Hand back is a filled orange button: first on the card's foot, and on the session's own page and phone sheet.

## The key choices, and why

- The whole card is tinted, not just outlined: a border alone was too faint to notice in the render.
- The header count keeps the mark visible when the team is folded.
- On the card, Hand back replaces Close session and the *ready to close* mark: a taken-over member is handed
  back, not closed. Close stays in the card's menu, and the session's own page is unchanged.
- A card that needs your answer keeps its answer buttons first, with Hand back after them.
- The test is the one the question words already use: a live member of a team (or under a lead) that you took
  over, not a session you started yourself. A suspended member is marked too.

## What it rules out

- Your own sessions are never marked, and neither is a lead's member you resumed by hand.
- A member on an unreachable host is not marked, since Hand back cannot reach it.
- The Queue gets no mark here: how a holder reads on a Queue card is TD-1567's to settle.
- The Sessions page's summary table and its needs-you counts do not change.

## What you will see

Orange cards and an orange Hand back for any member you took over, in light and dark, as the review renders
show. Hand back returns the member to its team and the marks go at once.
