# TD-1721: Who reviews a design, and what the designer carries

## What it does

It cuts what a design costs where the money goes, as you chose. The reviewers cost twice what the designer does,
and a Fable review costs about seven times a Sonnet one. So Fable reads a design in its first round and Sonnet in
every round after, with two exceptions where Fable comes back. And the designer's context bound drops to 150k, so
each design starts in a fresh run instead of carrying the last design's context into every turn.

## The key choices, and why

- Fable in round 1, Sonnet after: Fable finds the most that only a design review catches in round 1, and less
  after. A design's reviewers cost about $10 instead of $21 (Sonnet alone, $6, was the third choice). The risk:
  what a round without Fable misses is not measured, and the trial's escapes are where a loss would show. It
  applies from a design's next round after the brief that writes it merges.
- Fable comes back in a long design: past round 4, once Sonnet still finds something that changes the builds,
  Fable reads that round too. And a design for a major release (ledgerboard v3.0, say) gets Fable in every round.
- The 150k bound is set in this repo's own config, which wins over your org file. A run only ends between pieces
  of work, so the bound never stops a round mid-way, though a design waiting on a question may go on in a fresh
  run. It is about a fifth off the designer's spend. It reaches the designer only when you next stop and start the
  team, and a board line will ask you to.
- No second seat applies the fixes: the cost model showed it would save about 2%.

## What it rules out

- Ending the designer's run between a design's rounds on purpose: it would save more, but the next run would not
  know why each fix was made.
- Changing the replays: they keep Fable then Sonnet, testing the checklist and the gate alone.

## What you will see

- Design review records with Fable in round 1 only, unless a design runs long or is for a major release.
- The trial's report saying both changes muddy its cost test, and reading it without them: without the later Fable
  reviews, and split by the designer's bound at the dated team start.
- The designer restarting after each design, once the team has been restarted on 150k.
