# TD-2172: A question whose entry still waits on other work is asked early, and a yes-or-no ledger question offers Go

## What it does

- A question of yours on an entry that still waits on other work is now **asked early**. You see it whole, with its
  answers, and you can answer it now. It does not count as something that needs you now.
- The early question sits in the card's grey section under the line *Asked early: your answer acts once TD-n
  closes*. Where its only wait is a date or an event, the line names that instead.
- The card goes where the rest of its work puts it. Usually that is Waiting, with an amber edge because you are in it.
  A card in progress stays in progress and takes no colour from the question.
- A yes-or-no question on a ledger entry with no answers of its own now offers **Go** and **Not yet**. These are the
  same pair the gate sweep offers. Before, you had only Lift the hold.

## The key choices, and why

- Work an entry waits on comes in two kinds. A part still being written, such as a design's review round, is what
  the question is about, so the question keeps waiting for it as it does today. Anything else, such as a blocker, a
  date, an event, or a gated blocker that itself waits on work, only delays what your answer does. So the question
  is asked early.
- A design's end-of-rounds question is never asked early. Its One more round and Hold act at once, so it is asked as
  any, a need. This was your TD-2164 case.
- Your own gated to-do keeps waiting while it is blocked, since its Done is the doing. So does a build held under a
  design, since the design's question is the one asked.
- Go writes the sweep's decided line and lifts the hold in the same commit. Not yet closes the line as kept waiting.
  It is asked again in 30 days.

## What it rules out

- An orange card or a needs-you count for a question whose answer could start nothing yet.
- Asking a question before the part it is about is finished.
- A second question on a build, beside its design's question.

## What you will see

- Fewer cards among those that need you now. Waiting cards carry an amber-edged early question you may answer.
- Go and Not yet on a ledger question that had only Lift the hold.
