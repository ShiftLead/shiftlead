# TD-1845: Text a person reads is plain and every answer says who acts, checked by code

## What it does

- What waits on you (a session's question or steer, a question a seat passes up, a board decide or act line, a
  ledger entry's question) is checked by code when it is written, never by a model. One check, shared by every
  writer here and in the other repos, refuses three things: a question that is not one plain sentence of at most
  25 words with no code in it; an answer that does not say who acts (I'll, you, a session); and an entry title
  with code or internal labels in it. Code in the context below is only warned about.
- A refused writer gets every problem in one reply, rewrites once and sends again. Nothing is lost.
- What each answer leads to now rides on the answer itself (Pin it, then who does what); a question's second
  paragraph is its context.
- A question a seat passes up to you leads with that seat's own question for you, the original folded under it.
- Almost every such item gets one more answer, Not clear (not a look, and not a question rerouted to you). It
  sends the item back to whoever wrote it to rewrite, and each press is counted by writer and week, which the
  feedback review reads.

## The key choices, and why

- Refuse, not warn, for the first three rules: every writer is a session with a turn in hand or a helper it can run
  again, so a refusal costs one retry. Context is never capped or refused, since it is as long as it needs.
- Where it is, is drawn rather than written: the Queue card's head already names the larger work, so no message
  repeats it. This narrows your answer of 2026-11-14, which asked for the top entry's title first on every item.
- Not clear on a passed-up question hands it back to the seat that passed it, which rewrites and passes it again;
  on a board line or an entry it becomes a work order, so some session always picks it up.
- The check lives in ledgerboard, and agent-cadence wires it into its helpers; both are asked of those repos'
  techleads, to be filed there before this design merges.

## What it rules out

- A model judging plainness, and any seat watching the Queue for clarity.
- Notes, replies, your own words, what the home writes, and anything already filed: none is checked. A look with
  screenshots is checked except for who acts, since its answers are fixed.

## What you will see

- Questions on your Queue that open with one plain sentence, answers that say who does what, and a Not clear
  button last among the answers (after Reply on the Inbox), on every such item but a look or a question
  rerouted to you.
- The builds land in order: the card's drawing (TD-1847), then the briefs (TD-1848), then the refusals once the
  check reaches agent-cadence (TD-1846), then Not clear (TD-1853).
