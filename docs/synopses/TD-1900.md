# TD-1900: A decided design round waits most of a day while its designer sits idle on an early restart nobody presses

## What it does

A run that asks for a restart soon after it began, with nothing reported done, is read as early. The host agent
does not restart an early run, and it waits for you. That rule was meant to catch a session stuck in a loop. On
2026-11-15 it also caught a designer that its own context limit had sent away, and the designer sat idle for about
twenty hours with your One more round on another design waiting behind it. This design fixes three things:

- A run that pushed a commit of its own branch this run has done new work, even with nothing reported done. A design
  round carries one entry across many runs and reports nothing done until its PR merges.
- A run that asks to restart while its context reading is over its limit is not early either, because the host agent
  itself told it to restart.
- When a decided board line sits waiting, its stuck line names the session that should act. For a design's answer
  that is the designer, never a grinder. A session whose restart waits on you reads "waits on your Restart".

## The key choices, and why

- A run that repeats itself still waits for you: the same work reported done again, or an entry claimed and left by
  three runs in a row with nothing pushed. Those are the loop the early rule was written for.
- A pushed restart counts toward a ceiling of its own: six restarts in two hours, where the ordinary ceiling is
  three. So a session that keeps pushing a trivial commit each time still reaches you. A restart that reports new
  finished work counts toward neither ceiling, so a grinder that merges often is never stopped.
- The push is read on the session's own branch only, on this machine. A session on another node, one with no branch
  checked out, or one that pushed somewhere else reads no push, and its restart is judged as before.
- An earlier decision said the context reading must not lift an early restart. This design reverses it: a fresh run
  starts below its limit, and the ceiling still counts every such restart.

## What it rules out

- No seat or other session presses a restart that is still early. That stays your Restart or Resume.
- The stuck line still presses nothing. It names who should act and what cures it.

## What you will see

- Fewer early restarts on the Inbox. A designer between review rounds restarts on its own.
- A new ceiling row, "restarts exhausted · 6 in 2 h", when pushed restarts reach six in two hours. The restarted
  chip's hover shows both counts.
- Stuck lines such as "designer-ao-1 waits on your Restart: restart wanted · early".
