# TD-1920: A person-owned entry that nothing blocks reads ready on the Queue, so the person's own next step goes unsaid

## What it does

An entry you own that nothing holds is your own next step, and the Queue now says so. Its row reads
*yours to design*, *yours to start*, *yours to check* or *yours to evaluate*, by its kind, where it read *ready*,
the word for work a session will pick up. Its card reads *yours*, in indigo. A parent waiting on your step says
*after your TD-n* in indigo, not a bare *after TD-n*, and its card reads *yours* too, so the card that waits on you
says what the next step is and whose it is.

## The key choices, and why

- Indigo is the step's own colour, apart from amber, which asks you something, and teal, which waits on other
  work first. The techlead was asked the colour and the rank and kept both: you see them at the design's Go.
- A card's *yours* ranks just below *steer* and above *stalled*. It counts nowhere; nothing is asked of you.
- Your owner words are two in every repo, your own name and the generic *person* another repo's ledger writes, read
  by one test. A per-repo word was dropped: no repo declares its own, and one test keeps every reader the same.
- The step is read off the row: once a session claims it, or a review or a stall stands, it is no longer your step.
- Its way forward is Start session. Unless the card has a question of yours first, it starts on the step, not on
  the parent it holds up. A step counts as one of the card's items for the header's button, though it has no
  button of its own; a card whose only item is a step still draws the header's.
- This joins the design for an entry held for you while its question waits on open work: where that work is your
  own step, it reads *waiting, then yours* as before, with your step named.

## What it rules out

- No hold: who owns an entry still holds nothing, so lanes and what a session can pick are unchanged. A lane with
  no owner filter may still take your step, and it then reads as that session's work.
- No question and no Done on a step: there is no gate to answer.
- A gated entry another repo writes as owned by *person* becomes yours to do with Done, no longer a decision with
  answers. One gated on a *person* decision rather than yours keeps what it reads today, blocked.

## What you will see

- A row reading *yours to design* in indigo, and a card's left edge and status word indigo.
- *after your TD-n* on what waits on your step, and an indigo segment in a parent's parts meter.
- Start session on the card's header, starting on your step when no question of yours comes first.
- Two builds: the reading first, then the drawing. Round 4's last fix, where the card finds its steps, was not
  read by a review, so the board asks whether to run one more round before the builds start.
