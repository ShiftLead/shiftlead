# TD-1153: A team's definition and flows are edited only in its file, from a page per team

## What it does

Every team gets its own page. It gathers the three things you change about a team: its members, its settings
(schedule, until, reserve priority, balance, flow) and its definition, shown as the text of its block in its
file. The team card's Members button and flow chip open the page, and the Settings page lists one link per team.
A team defined in the org file is edited and saved in place. A team defined in a repo's own file is edited the
same way, but your edit is proposed as a pull request, which the team's own grinders review and merge.

## The key choices, and why

- The definition stays in its file, versioned and reviewed with the briefs it names; the page is the file's
  editor, not a second copy. Only the team's own block of lines is rewritten, so comments and the rest of the
  file are never touched.
- Every edit is checked before anything is written: the block must still be the one you opened, must stay one
  team under its own name, must parse, and must not reach past its block; then the whole org is built with your
  text standing in, and an edit that breaks it, names a session twice or leaves a listed flow unfollowable is
  refused. A brief that does not exist yet is only a warning, since it may arrive in another PR.
- A repo team's PR is opened by the home on your press and never merged by it. It becomes work for the team's
  free-pick lanes at once, at high priority. The grinder that takes it re-runs the checks, reviews it, and merges
  it, or asks you, giving its finding, when it cannot merge as written. The alternative, the home merging on
  your press as a board edit is, would be faster but unreviewed.
- One open definition PR per team at a time; editing is disabled while it is open.
- In a repo no team services, a definition PR is yours to review and merge by hand.

## What it rules out

- Renaming a team, or adding a second team, from the page: both stay hand edits to the file.
- Editing a brief's text or a flow's own file on the page.
- Proposing for a repo whose forge is not GitHub, or whose checkout is not what origin holds.
- Start, Wind down and Apply move nowhere: they stay on the team card.

## What you will see

A new page per team with Members, Settings and Definition sections, Edit then Save or Propose as PR, and each
refusal drawn under the text in plain words. While a PR is open the page links it and says the team still runs
on the old definition. Once it merges and the checkout is pulled, a running team's card reads definition
changed — Apply, and nothing moves until you press Apply or the team next starts.
