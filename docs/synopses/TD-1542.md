# TD-1542: An item on a kept completed card draws no Start session

## What it does

A Do or Decide item about a finished entry is drawn on that entry's completed card, which the Queue keeps at the
head of Recently completed while the item is open. Such an item now draws Start session, as it would on any open
card, so you can always have a session walk you through it. The session starts on the finished entry the item is
about, on that repo's team, and its first words tell it the entry is finished and to read it as the record of
what was done, not as open work.

## The key choices, and why

- The button is on the item, never on the card's header. A kept card asks only through its items, so the
  header stays bare however many items it holds.
- The session starts on the item's own finished entry, which may be a row of the card and not its top entry.
  Its role and profile come from that entry's type, as for any entry.
- The usual session name for an entry is often taken here, because the session that built it left one behind.
  The press then opens the filled New session form with the reason, and you pick another name there.
- Once your own session is on the entry, under whatever name, the button reads Open session and goes there. So a
  second press never starts a second session. Only your own sessions count for this. A worker that happens to
  list the entry does not hide the button, but another session already working on the entry does, and a press
  from a page drawn before it started is refused, naming that session.
- If anything about the card or the item has changed since the page drew it, the press is refused with one
  message, and nothing starts unchecked.
- While that session runs it usually shows as an other work line in In progress, unless the entry's closing
  change was made on the same branch name. Either way it holds up no work, since the entry is finished.

## What it rules out

- No Start session on a completed card's header, and none on a red PR, a fork's PR, a stopped session's row or
  a stuck row, as on an open card.
- No Start session on a loose card, the card made for an item whose entry the Queue cannot find on any card.
- Open cards are unchanged: the rule that a session's lane shows it is yours applies on kept cards only.

## What you will see

On a kept card in Recently completed, each Do or Decide item has a Start session button after its answers. Its
help says it opens on the finished entry the item is about.
