# TD-1832: The Queue draws one answer set, the same way and in the same place, on every item kind

## What it does

- Every item on the Queue and on a card's page ends in one answer bar, whatever the item is: a ledger entry, a
  session's question, a board line, a look, a permission, a red PR, a stuck session, or a grey item that waits on
  someone else. The same controls sit in the same order: the item's own answers (the recommended one outlined),
  Answer…, Start session, then at the right Dismiss where it applies, Open, and the menu last.
- The item's heading holds no buttons: a question's Open moves into the bar, and its separate link to the whole
  message goes, since its sentence opens the message. A slot an item has no use for is left out, never shown disabled.
- A press that did not finish never leaves an item with no buttons or with Answer… alone. The item keeps its whole
  bar, and one sentence above it says what became of the press and what you can do, in one of three colours.

## The key choices, and why

- Blue, still with a session: you answered and the asker is acting on it. Lift the hold is in the bar, Answer…
  sends more words to the asker, and Start session is there when no other session holds the entry.
- Amber, could not be done: a session reported it could not do what you answered, or its asker exited without
  reporting. The bar shows the question's answers again as plain replies, the one you took ticked. Where the
  entry is still held for you, Lift the hold comes first: one press lifts it and tells the asker you did.
- Red, refused: the home's own words, then that nothing was sent, then a redrawn bar to press again.
- One press, one item: while an amber item offers the entry's Lift the hold, the entry's own blue item steps aside.
- Some grey items draw less than a full bar: one kept waiting until a date shows Lift the hold and Open, one whose
  answer is being written shows Open alone, and one handed to a session (once that work is built) shows its entry's
  own answer, Lift the hold or Done.
- A blue item counts toward the card header's Start session only where it draws its own Start session.
- Open is always the bar's. An item whose menu would hold nothing draws no menu; Done… and Back to design… stay on
  the entry's row on the card.

## What it rules out

- No Dismiss on an open question: a question is declined by Delete, and a steer lets go at its time.
- No answer keys on the Queue; the Inbox keeps its own.
- No Lift the hold where a board line already asks about the entry, or on the item of an asker that has exited:
  there the entry's own item carries it.
- The Inbox, the message page and the Repo page keep their controls as they are, and so do the Queue's read-only
  items (waiting on them, answered for you, for your information), which answer nothing.
