# TD-1832: The Queue draws one answer set, the same way and in the same place, on every item kind

## What it does

- Every item on the Queue and on a card's page ends in one answer bar, whatever the item is: a ledger entry, a
  session's question, a board line, a look, a permission, a red PR, a stuck session, or a grey item that waits on
  someone else. The same controls sit in the same order: the item's own answers (the recommended one outlined),
  Answer…, Start session, then at the right Dismiss where it applies, Open, and the menu last.
- The item's heading holds no buttons. A slot an item has no use for is left out, never shown disabled.
- A press that did not finish never leaves an item with no buttons or with Answer… alone. The item keeps its whole
  bar, and one sentence above it says what became of the press and what you can do, in one of three colours.

## The key choices, and why

- Lift the hold is not this design's. Your Hold moved every rule about it to the Lift the hold design (TD-1829):
  where it is drawn, and what a press may lift. The bar draws it where that design marks an entry as liftable,
  and asks nothing of its own, so the two designs no longer overlap.
- The one-press lift-and-reply on a stuck answer (lift the hold, then tell the asker you did) is that design's too,
  in a build of its own (TD-1879). This design draws the result.
- Blue, still with a session: you answered and the asker is acting on it; Answer… sends more words to the asker,
  and Start session is there when no other session holds the entry.
- Amber, could not be done: a session reported it could not do what you answered, or its asker exited without
  reporting. The bar shows the question's answers again as plain replies, the one you took ticked.
- Red, refused: what refused it, that nothing was sent (only where nothing was), then a redrawn bar to press again.
- One press, one item: while an amber item waits for your reply, the entry's own blue item steps aside.

## What it rules out

- No Dismiss on an open question: a question is declined by Delete, and a steer lets go at its time.
- No answer keys on the Queue; the Inbox keeps its own.
- The Inbox, the message page and the Repo page keep their own controls, and the Queue's read-only items answer
  nothing.

## What you will see

- The bar on every Queue item, as in the mockups you agreed, in light, dark and at phone width.
- Two builds: TD-1842 draws the bar with today's controls in it (released by your Go on its own item); TD-1843
  adds the sentence above it and draws the lift from the Lift the hold design's marks, so it waits on TD-1879 and
  on your next answer about this design.
