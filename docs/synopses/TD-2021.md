# TD-2021: A design's end-of-rounds question is asked once, on the design, and its builds wait on it

## What it does

When a design ends its review rounds and asks you to go, one more round, or hold, the card shows that question once,
on the design, and says what Go releases: "Go releases TD-1947, TD-1948 to the grinders". The builds it holds no
longer ask anything of their own. Each reads "waits on TD-1919's question", with no buttons.

## The key choices, and why

- Each build keeps its own hold in the ledger, as today. Making the builds wait on the design through "blocked by"
  would be a build blocked by its own design, which the ledger refuses, and a design only closes with its last
  build, so that wait would never release. Only the drawing changes; the cadence and the board tools are untouched.
- When a session of yours holds the design, its "with you" item no longer sits beside the question with a second
  press: the question is the one item.
- The design's "Lift the hold" is renamed "Go now — build TD-1947, TD-1948" and lifts the design's hold and every
  build's in one press, its confirm naming each one. A build has no lift of its own.
- Go now is drawn where Lift the hold is drawn today, and only there. While a round you bought is running, nothing
  is drawn: the way out is that round's own next question, as you decided for TD-1926.

## What it rules out

- Three rows for one question, and a build's lift that was in effect a Go on the whole design.
- A press that lifts only some of a design's builds. If the builds changed since you read the card, Go now is
  refused and asks you to read it again.

## What you will see

- With the question open: one Decide item on the design naming what Go releases, and builds reading
  "waits on TD-1919's question".
- After you answer One more round: no item; the design's row says who is acting, and the builds still wait on
  its question.
