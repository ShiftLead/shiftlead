# TD-2021: A design's end-of-rounds question is asked once, on the design, and its builds wait on it

## What it does

When a design ends its review rounds and asks you to go, one more round, or hold, the card shows that question once,
on the design, and says what Go releases: "Go releases TD-1947, TD-1948 to the grinders". The builds it holds are
the parts that question lists. They no longer ask anything of their own: each reads "waits on TD-1919's question",
with no buttons, and never makes the card read as waiting or as needing you on its own.

## The key choices, and why

- Each build keeps its own hold in the ledger, as today. Making the builds wait on the design through "blocked by"
  would be a build blocked by its own design, which the ledger check flags, and a design only closes with its last
  build, so that wait would never release. Only the drawing changes; the cadence and the board tools are untouched.
- When a session of yours holds the design, its "with you" item no longer sits beside the question with a second
  press: the question is the one item.
- The design's "Lift the hold" is renamed "Go now — build TD-1947, TD-1948" and lifts the design's hold and every
  held build's in one press, its confirm naming each one. Every other way of lifting the design lifts them too,
  among them your answer in the Inbox to a session's question that lifts the design's hold, which is drawn and
  confirmed as Go now.
- The two builds land in turn: until the second draws Go now, the first draws no plain Lift the hold on the design,
  so nothing lifts the design while leaving its builds held; you answer its question meanwhile.
- Go now is drawn where Lift the hold is drawn today, and only there. While a round you bought is running, nothing
  is drawn: the way out is that round's own next question, as you decided for TD-1926. Likewise, if a held build
  still waits on other open work, the design waits on it too and Go now appears once it closes.
- A design you own yourself holds no builds this way: its builds ask as they do today.

## What it rules out

- Three rows for one question, and a build's lift that was in effect a Go on the whole design.
- A press that lifts only some of a design's builds. If the builds changed since you read the card, Go now is
  refused and asks you to read it again. A lift asked or pressed on one held build alone is refused, pointing you
  to the design's question.

## What you will see

- With the question open: one Decide item on the design naming what its Go answer releases, and builds reading
  "waits on TD-1919's question".
- After you answer One more round: no item; the design's row says who is acting, and the builds still wait on
  its question.
- On the Repo page the held builds still list under "for you", with their word and no buttons.
