# TD-1984: A Queue card shows no Start session while another session holds its entry

## What it does

- Every Queue card for open work now offers Start session, whoever holds its entry: on each Decide and Do item,
  and in the card's header by the usual count. Only a red PR, a fork's PR, a stopped holder's state row and a
  stuck row still draw none.
- When a worker or a seat holds the entry, the press starts your own session beside it, never in its place. The
  worker keeps its claim, its branch and its PR, and is not told.
- The next press anywhere on that card is Open session, to the session you started, and it stays so while that
  session lives, even after the worker lets go: you take the work over in it, or close it to start afresh.
- A board item from another repo on the card still starts on the card's own entry, the prompt naming the item.
- The refusal *held by <name>* is gone from the card. The Inbox's own row opens your session first, else the
  holder's, and reads Open session while your session beside lives, even with nothing holding the entry.

## The key choices, and why

- A session of your own beside the worker, rather than a button that only opens the worker's pane: you asked to
  always be able to start a session on the work in front of you. The techlead agreed with this default.
- The session beside takes nothing from the worker. It gets a name of its own (with-td and the entry's number,
  then -2 up to -9) that the system reads no claim from, and no lane. So the entry stays the worker's on the card
  and in the build lanes, and if the worker lets go the grinders can pick it up again.
- Its opening words say it is there to look into the card with you (an entry that is yours, or waits on your
  decision, keeps its usual opening), then list the card's entries, then name the holder, its branch and its PR,
  and tell it not to push to them or open a PR of its own. To change the worker's course it drafts a message for
  you to agree first; to take the work over, it declares a claim once the worker has let go and you ask.
- Two quick presses on the same entry start one session, not two. Two quick presses on different entries of one
  card can still start two, as they could before; that is left as it is.

## What it rules out

- Two sessions building the same entry at once: the session beside reads and talks, it does not build.
- Starting beside an interactive session: one with a person at it opens as your own, as today.
- A tenth session beside one entry: after nine names are used, the press opens the New session form, which
  gives the session no lane either.

## What you will see

- The card around TD-1977, held by a grinder, shows Start session on its release item; pressing it opens a new
  session of yours, and the card still reads *held by* the grinder.
- The round count: four rounds ran, then the fifth you bought. It read the three fixes no review had read and
  found them right, and found three more gaps: the fallback form gave the session a lane, the prompt did not
  say where the holder's sentence goes, and the Inbox row drew Start session for a session you already had.
  A Sonnet review and then a Fable review read the fixes, and Fable found nothing that changes the build, so
  the end-of-rounds item recommends Go.
