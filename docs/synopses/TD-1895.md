# TD-1895: A team's Wind down leaves its idle members open when no manager is live or one member never declared

## What it does

When a team is wound down, by your Wind down or by its manager, every unattended member it reaches gets a mark with
the wrap-up prompt, or alone where it had already finished. The host agent then closes each marked member once it has
run its last turn and settled idle with its work committed and pushed, whether or not it said it was out of work. No
member has to close itself, and no manager has to be live.

## The key choices, and why

- The host agent does the closing, checking on every tick: the press itself closes nobody, and a wrapped-up member
  sits idle rather than leaving, so nobody else would. A member busy at the press is not closed until the wrap-up's
  own turn has run, or a grace has passed if that turn never comes.
- A member waiting on your answer, or on a reviewer, is closed too: nothing would wake it for the answer, so open it
  would only block the team's next start. Your question stays open in your Inbox; the member keeps its copy a day.
- A message you send a marked member (push it, say) is part of the ending: it stays marked and is closed once it
  settles, its ending yours even where a session pressed. A manager's new instruction drops the mark.
- While a marked member is live the team is not finished, so its manager is told only once the last one is gone.
  Nothing restarts a marked member; a crashed one is left to you. The wrap-up prompt is unchanged.
- The manager gets no mark that counts: it is sent the wrap-up as today, and the host agent closes it after its
  members, saying who wound the team down (on one that closed itself when told, before any seat can refill). So
  the team reads stopped by you, its seats held, even if the manager restarted or its wrap-up failed.
- Accepted gaps: a manager closing itself before it is told, or when told until the next tick, reads by its own
  ending; a press from a node member's shell reads as yours, like a node manager's stray mark from your message there.
- Marks count only for their own run, by the team's last start; while anyone in the team winds down (a Wrap up on
  the manager too), lane news restarts no member that had already finished.

## What it rules out

- A member with unpushed work stays open until it is pushed, and one on a permission dialog is never killed; either
  keeps the team's manager from being told the team is done, with no time limit, until you act.
- A member whose wrap-up could not be sent carries no mark and is named as refused, left to a second Wind down.
- A seat is never marked, and the manager is never closed by this road: it is still ended after its members.
- A team nobody wound down is unchanged: its declared members still wait for the rest before any is closed.

## What you will see

Each closed member's card reads closed by the tick after your Wind down, or after the manager's. Once nobody is live
the team reads as wound down where every member said it was out of work, else as stopped by you where you pressed it,
with Start offered; no note arrives, since you asked for it. Where only a session wound the team down, one note
tells you the host agent closed its members, with what each reported, when the manager closes (or, with no manager,
when the host agent closes the last marked member), unless the manager already reported, or the last of them was
closed by another hand or exited on its own.

The Wind down's toast counts the members the wrap-up reached, the finished ones marked with nothing sent, and names
each refused one. A Start pressed while a marked member is still live is refused naming it, *winding down*, with what
to do: wait for the home to close it and press Start again, or answer its dialog, push its work or let its shell or
helper finish. Once the members are gone, a Start closes the idle manager and starts the team afresh.
