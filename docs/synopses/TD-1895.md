# TD-1895: A team's Wind down leaves its idle members open when no manager is live or one member never declared

## What it does

When a team is wound down, by your Wind down or by its manager, every unattended member it reaches gets a mark with
the wrap-up prompt, or alone where it had already finished. The host agent then closes each marked member once it has
run its last turn and settled idle with its work committed and pushed, whether or not it said it was out of work. No
member has to close itself, and no manager has to be live.

## The key choices, and why

- The host agent does the closing, checking on every tick: the press itself closes nobody, and a wrapped-up member
  sits idle rather than leaving, so nobody else would. A member busy at the press is not closed until the wrap-up's
  own turn has run, or a grace has passed if that turn never comes.
- A member waiting on your answer, or on a reviewer, is closed too: under a wind-down nothing wakes it for the answer,
  so leaving it open would only stop the team from starting again. Your question stays open in your Inbox and its
  answer goes where an orphaned question's answer goes; the member's own copy is kept for its next start for a day.
- A message you send a marked member is part of the ending (push it, say), so the member stays marked and is closed
  once it settles. A new instruction from its manager means the run carries on, so the mark is dropped.
- While a marked member is still live, the team does not count as finished, so its manager is told only once the last
  one is gone. Nothing restarts a marked member, and a crashed one is left to you to start or forget. The wrap-up
  prompt is unchanged and asks for no declaration of being out of work.
- The manager gets no mark: it is sent the wrap-up as today, and rule 9 closes it after its members. Its own close
  comes well after theirs, so where it is the only ending in the short window the team's word is read over, your
  marks on the members say you stopped the team, its seats held, even if the manager restarted or its wrap-up failed.
  Where a member's ending is in that window too, the endings decide as before, as for a run that carried on past it.
- A mark counts only for the run it was made in: the home keeps the time of the team's last start, so a member left
  closed and marked by an earlier wind-down never decides whether a later run's ending gets a note.

## What it rules out

- A member with uncommitted or unpushed work is never closed; it stays open and is closed once it is pushed.
- A member on a permission dialog is never killed; it stays open in your Inbox. Such a member, or one with unpushed
  work, keeps the team's manager from being told the team is done, with no time limit, until you act.
- A member whose wrap-up could not be sent carries no mark and is named as refused, left to a second Wind down.
- A seat is never marked, and the manager is never closed by this road: rule 9 still ends it after its members.
- A team nobody wound down is unchanged: its declared members still wait for the rest before any is closed.

## What you will see

Each closed member's card reads closed by the tick after your Wind down, or after the manager's. Once nobody is live
the team reads as wound down where every member said it was out of work, else as stopped by you where you pressed it,
with Start offered; no note arrives, since you asked for it. Where only a manager wound the team down, one note tells
you the host agent closed its members, with what each reported, unless the manager closed the last of them itself.

The Wind down's toast counts the members the wrap-up reached, the finished ones marked with nothing sent, and names
each refused one. A Start pressed while a marked member is still live is refused naming it, *winding down*, with what
to do: wait for the home to close it, then press Start again, or answer its dialog or push its work. Once the members
are gone, a Start closes the idle manager and starts the team afresh.
