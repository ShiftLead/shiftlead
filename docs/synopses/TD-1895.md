# TD-1895: A team's Wind down leaves its idle members open when no manager is live or one member never declared

## What it does

When a team is wound down, by your Wind down or by its manager, every member it reaches gets a mark. The
host agent then closes each marked member once it has finished its last turn and its work is committed and
pushed, whether or not it said it was out of work. No member has to close itself, and no manager has to be live.

## The key choices, and why

- The host agent does the closing, not the member and not the page's button. A member closing itself depends on
  every brief telling it to, and the designer showed one stopping short. The page's button cannot wait hours
  for a busy member; the host agent checks on every tick.
- A member waiting on your answer, or on a reviewer, is closed too. Under a wind-down nothing wakes it for the
  answer anyway, so leaving it open would only stop the team from starting again. The answer is kept for that
  member's next start, for a day.
- The prompt a member gets at a wind-down is unchanged. It does not ask the member to declare itself out of work,
  since a declaration made because you pressed Wind down would not mean it looked and found nothing.

## What it rules out

- A member with uncommitted or unpushed work is never closed; it stays open, shown as it is today, and is closed
  once its work is pushed.
- A team nobody wound down is unchanged: its declared members still wait for the rest before any is closed.
- The manager is handled as today: once its members are gone it is told to finish and is closed after a grace.

## What you will see

Each closed member's card reads closed by the tick, after Wind down. The team then reads as stopped by you, with
Start offered, and no note arrives telling you the team finished, since you asked for it.
