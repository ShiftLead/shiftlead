# TD-1801: Answer on the Queue again: keep the holding-still and Refresh bar, bring the controls back

## What it does

Every item on the Queue is answered where it is read again. Each decision and each thing to do shows its whole
sentence, its context, all its answers, Answer…, Start session and the ⋯ menu, as the card's own page does, and
each card's header has Start session and ⋯ back. The page still holds still: nothing moves while you read, and
Refresh draws what changed. All the Queue's words are rewritten to read as plain sentences.

## The key choices, and why

- One drawing for the Queue and a card's own page, so the two can never differ.
- Open card › becomes a quieter link, Its own page, for one card in its own tab or a link to send.
  A notification's link still opens the item there, where it is rung.
- An open Answer… box holds its own card still, and Refresh cannot be pressed while it is open. Other cards keep
  updating their words in place, but nothing moves. The mockups said the whole Queue would hold; the reviews
  showed that clashes with how the page already holds, so it keeps the rule already built.
- A reason typed for a Deny is kept across every redraw, so you never lose it by looking away.
- The words: headings say counts in words (One decision for you, 2 things for you to do, 6 to read); items say
  where they come from (Do · from samscrape's board); due dates read due today, due in 3 days, due 45 days ago;
  dates read Nov 19, never 2026-11-19; an id in the card's own repo drops the repo; titles show no raw markup;
  every button starts with a capital; on a phone a card's title sits under its id.

## What it rules out

- One-line previews on the Queue, and a separate page to answer on.
- New words on the Inbox, the Repo page or the command line: they keep theirs.
- Rewording the group headings, the amber pill and the entry counts, which keep their form.

## What you will see

The Queue with every answer back on its cards, the bar and Refresh as now, and the new words. Two builds:
TD-1808 brings the controls back, then TD-1809 rewrites the words, each with shots in light and dark (the words
also at phone width) for you to look at before you say the live Queue reads right.
