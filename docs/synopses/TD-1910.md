# TD-1910: The director seat only answers for the person: it should also work for them, as a chief of staff

## What it does

- With one setting turned on (off by default), the director stops only answering what is passed up to it and
  also works for you: it watches every repo's board, ledger, pull requests and seats, woken by the home
  hourly when something changed, at once when you answer it, and when a pull request it may merge turns ready.
- Everything it tells you is a Queue item, never chat only: a recommendation placed under a question that
  already stands, a question of its own, a report, or a note of what it is waiting on.
- It acts for you only in what you hand it: merging pull requests in your merge line, and sending other
  seats work you approved. It finds gaps nobody has filed and asks you *File this?*, then files them.
- **Brief me** starts your own session with the director's view, to talk things through.

## The key choices, and why

- Nothing stale reaches you: each item is checked against the live state as it is sent, and the same thing
  is never sent twice while an earlier copy is open.
- A merge is bounded by the home, not by the director's judgement: by default only doc changes outside the
  design, the briefs, the decisions and the reviewed paths, at a passing review and a green check. The home
  merges it itself, at the exact version the director read, and records each merge for you.
- How you see its merges is your choice: quietly on the card (the default), in one daily digest you can
  dismiss at once, or each one waiting for you to mark seen.
- Every question it asks ends on a *Not now* answer that does nothing. Work it hands a seat needs your
  answer to one of its questions, at most two weeks old, one send each.
- A gap goes to you only after a fresh independent review checks it and finds nothing already covering it;
  a gap you turn down is not asked again for a month.

## What it rules out

- It never starts or stops sessions or teams, deploys, edits your files, changes an answer you gave,
  approves its own gap, mails an interactive session, or merges its own work, a fork's, or anything outside
  the merge line.
- It does not yet answer board decisions for you: that waits on ledgerboard TD-132, which records an
  answer made on someone's behalf. Until then it only recommends.

## What you will see

- On the Settings page's Director card: turn the work on, choose what it may do, its merge paths, how its
  merges are shown, and the **Brief me** button.
- A Director chip on the Queue and Inbox that filters to its items, as the team chips do.
- *The director recommends* under questions, *File this?* questions, and records of what it merged for you.
