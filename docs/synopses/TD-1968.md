# TD-1968: Start session on a Queue card's header shows nothing for seconds after the press

## What it does

Every button whose press sends a request now changes in the same instant you press it, even where it sits
outside a row: a Queue card's header Start session and Open session, and a session card's and the Focus
page's own buttons (Start now, Restart, Kill, Close, Wrap up, the mode switch and the rest). Rows already
did this. The pressed button greys out, keeps its width, and its label turns into what is under way, such as
starting…, killing… or opening…, until the answer comes.

## The key choices, and why

- One rule for every page, not a fix for one button, so no press ever looks lost.
- The label says the verb under way. Where a button's verb differs from what it sends (Open session, a
  scheduled session's Cancel, Forget), the button carries its own; otherwise a word table; failing that,
  its own label with an ellipsis. Time badges and grant chips keep their words and only grey out.
- Buttons that do the same thing to the same session go off together, and so does the other half of an
  Allow and Deny pair. A press from a menu shows its verb on the menu's button, which still opens.
- The mark lasts until the answer. A press that moves you to another page stays marked until it goes. One
  whose effect shows up on its card or Focus page waits for that redraw, five seconds at most, so Kill or
  Close cannot be pressed twice in the gap. Then the button comes back as the page now draws it, never as
  it was at the press. A refusal brings it back with a red toast naming the button and the server's words.
- A redraw while a press is out draws its button marked again.
- Keyboard focus stays on the marked button, so the page's keys keep working.

## What it rules out

- A press that asks first (a confirm, a prompt, a composer) is marked only once you answer it.
- Copying, popping a window out and opening a shell beside the page show no mark.
- Buttons with their own handling (the transcript's Earlier, a team's Start and Stop, Settings saves) keep it.

## What you will see

On the Queue, Start session in a card's header reads starting… the moment you press it, until the new
session's page opens. On a session card, Kill reads killing… until the card shows it stopped.
