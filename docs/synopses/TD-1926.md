# TD-1926: The Queue asks a held decision while the entry still waits on open work, so a press can lift it mid-round

## What it does

- An entry held for your decision that still waits on open work (an unfinished entry it is blocked by, or an
  unfinished part of it, such as a design's running review round) is no longer asked of you yet.
- On the Queue it reads *waiting, then yours*: a grey item with your question's words, the line *Asked once TD-n
  closes*, and an Open button alone. No answers, no Answer…, no Not clear and no Lift the hold.
- Its card shows no *needs you*, and reads *waiting* when the work it waits on is itself waiting; it leaves the
  Queue's and the rail's counts.
- Once the last of that work closes, the question comes back with its answers as any question does.

## The key choices, and why

- The hold itself is unchanged: lanes, what can be picked, and the hold's own answer stay as they are. Only when
  you are asked changes, so a press can never be a Go in the middle of a round.
- Work that is itself held for your decision does not count. So a design whose only open parts are its held
  builds is asked as today. Work that is itself waiting on the held entry does not count either, so the two never
  wait on each other. Work that is yours to do still counts: the question waits until you have done it.
- A word that already says who acts stays: *with you in a session*, an answer on its way, *answered — waiting on*
  its asker, *handed to* a seat, and a re-ask date. Even then, no Lift the hold is drawn while the work is open.
- A board line covering several entries is grey if any of them waits, since its one answer acts on them all. An
  entry on that line with no open work of its own waits with the rest.
- A press made from a page read before the wait began is refused by the home, with the reason shown.
- The Inbox still shows the board line as any, its due date the board's. A press there is refused in the same
  way. The Repo page and the Inbox's ledger rows show *Asked once TD-n closes* in place of the answers.

## What it rules out

- An unreadable or unknown blocker, and a date or event gate, do not count as open work. The question is asked,
  and the hold still keeps the entry from being picked.
- The wait has no time limit of its own. A stuck piece of work is flagged where stuck work always is.

## What you will see

- On a design whose review round is running, its end-of-rounds question is drawn grey, with no *needs you*,
  until the round's entry closes.
- A session started from such a card is told the question waits for that work.
