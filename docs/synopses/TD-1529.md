# TD-1529: A td-max flow: a team's grinders and seats scale with the work waiting, for the most throughput

## What it does

A team's member lines and its question seats can run more sessions than their size when work waits on every
busy one, and go back toward their size when the work runs out. The size is the floor; a ceiling per line or
seat caps it, drawn together as a range: grinder-ao 3–5, techlead-ao-1 1–3. Each session past the size is an
extra: a record written held at the team's start and filled only when needed, so nothing appears unasked.

## The key choices, and why

- Up on need, never on a timer. A seat adds an extra the moment a question arrives while its own session and
  every extra are busy, and the question goes straight there. A worker line adds one when an entry becomes
  pickable, or a member claims with work still left, while every member holds a claim. One guard: no second
  extra on a line until the first has claimed.
- Down with no idle timer. A worker extra closes when it declares out of work, but stays open while a PR of
  its own is open, so the reader's reply reaches the session that knows the work. A seat extra stays open idle,
  costing no tokens and keeping its context warm, until its host needs the slot.
- One host-wide cap counts every live session on the machine, yours included: about 30 on kmaster. Only extras
  are held back by it; any start at the cap first closes the longest-idle clean seat extra.
- On by default with a ceiling of size + 2, for teams defined after it lands. Every team running today is
  written off once and stays as it is until you raise a line's up to, which turns on that one line.
- A scaled seat's extras are the same reader: a review read by any of them counts as the seat's.

## What it rules out

- No scaling of the manager, the director, seats that sweep the repo on a timer, or pooled seats: one lead,
  one pass over the repo, and a pool is a fallback, not capacity.
- No minimum setting: the size is the floor. No idle-timeout shutdowns.
- The balance mark holds back new worker extras but never a seat's, since a backed-up reader is what it trips on.

## What you will see

The team card's header reads td-max, and each scaling line its range and extras (techlead-ao-1 1–3 +2 idle),
with the newest reason beside it: +1 at 14:02: 4 pickable, every grinder claimed, or +0: host full, 30 live.
A live extra's badge reads grinder · extra 1 of 2. The Members dialog's up to field sets a ceiling (0 turns a
line off); ao team max sets it from the terminal, with off and on for the whole team.
