# TD-1856: A restart waiting on the person reads stalled on its entry's Queue card

## What it does

When a team session holding an entry asks for a restart that only you can press (an early restart, a repeat, the
restart ceiling, a restart held by unpushed work, a brief lost twice, a seat's fills exhausted), that restart row
moves from the Inbox to the Queue card of the entry the session holds. It is a Do item there, with the same
Open, Restart, Resume and Dismiss it has in the Inbox, and the card reads needs you.

The entry's rows stop reading stalled. They say what waits instead: restart wanted · early, restart wanted ·
repeats an entry, restarts exhausted, restart held, brief lost twice or fills exhausted.

## The key choices, and why

- The restart row is treated as one more kind of stopped holder's row, the rule that already moves a holder's
  permission or question to its card. Which card it lands on, and which claim a session holding several entries
  draws on, follow that rule unchanged.
- Every restart reason moves, not only early ones: each waits on your press.
- The row's word comes from the session's own restart mark, not from the Inbox row. Dismissing the row takes it off
  the card, but the rows still read restart wanted rather than stalled, since that is still true.
- Stalled keeps its meaning: a team that is stuck, for its techlead. It is no longer used for work that waits on
  you.

## What it rules out

- A session that has exited or closed holds no live claim, so its restart row stays in the Inbox, as any stopped
  holder's row does.
- There is no Snooze on the card, as for every Queue item; a row snoozed in the Inbox earlier stays snoozed there.
- Other Inbox rows about a session (an idle member of a team with no manager, held mail) do not move.

## What you will see

A card like TD-1711's on 14 November reads needs you, its rows read restart wanted · early, and the Restart and
Resume buttons are on the card, not in the Inbox.
