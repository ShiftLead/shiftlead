# TD-1856: A restart waiting on the person reads stalled on its entry's Queue card

## What it does

When a live team session holding an entry needs a restart that only you can press (an early restart, a repeat,
the restart ceiling, a restart held by unpushed work, a brief lost twice), that restart row moves from the Inbox
to the Queue card of the entry the session holds. It is a Do item there, with Restart and Open, and the
card reads needs you, on the page and in the command line's list.

The entry's rows stop reading stalled. They say what waits instead: restart wanted · early, restart wanted ·
repeats an entry, restarts exhausted, restart held or brief lost twice. The word is amber and opens the session.

## The key choices, and why

- The restart row is treated as one more kind of stopped holder's row, the rule that already moves a holder's
  permission or question to its card. Which card it lands on, and which claim a session holding several entries
  draws on, follow that rule unchanged.
- Every restart reason a live session can carry moves, not only early ones: each waits on your press. In
  practice the early and held ones move most; a session at its ceiling has usually exited.
- The row's word and the card's needs you come from the session's own restart mark, not from the Inbox row. The
  card has no Dismiss or Snooze, and shows the row even if you dismissed or snoozed it in the Inbox earlier: it
  stays until the session's restart mark goes, as the Inbox row does (a restart or a Forget takes it). A notification
  about it still reaches you, linking the card, even if you dismissed or snoozed the Inbox row, when the restart began
  inside the telling window. There is no Resume on the card, since the session is still running and Resume is only for
  one that has ended. A session the Restart button cannot restart (one that is not idle, or is a team seat or
  not supervised) shows only Open there, which opens the session; its own page has no Restart either, so the
  row waits until the session can be restarted, ends (its restart row is then the Inbox's), or the mark goes.
- The card's needs you and its Do item are read a moment apart, so for one reading after a restart mark goes the
  card can still read needs you with nothing to press. This is accepted.
- A session holding entries on two cards draws the row on one; the other card's row reads the restart words but
  that card does not read needs you.
- Stalled keeps its meaning: a team that is stuck, for its techlead. It is no longer used for work that waits on
  you.

## What it rules out

- A session that has exited or closed holds no live claim, so its restart row stays in the Inbox, as any stopped
  holder's row does; so does a seat's fills exhausted row, which is only set once the seat has exited.
- Other Inbox rows about a session (an idle member of a team with no manager, held mail) do not move.

## What you will see

A card like TD-1711's on 14 November reads needs you, its rows read restart wanted · early, and its Restart
button is on the card, not in the Inbox.
