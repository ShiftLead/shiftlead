# TD-1556: A blocked outcome reads as Decide and counts a need twice

## What it does

- When a session reports that it is blocked after you answered, and names its own open question to you as the
  reason, the send is refused. It is told to ask that question on the first question's thread instead, so you
  see one question with your earlier answer above it, not two items.
- A blocked report is drawn as a Do item, never Decide, since it asks you nothing.
- Your reply to a blocked report is now recorded and moves it out of Needs you. Dismissing it tells the session.
- A blocked report is never pruned by age; it waits until you reply or dismiss it, or the session reports again.
- After a blocked report, the session may still finish (done or dropped) or ask on the thread; the old blocked
  line stays visible under the newer one.

## The key choices, and why

- The fix is at the send, not on the page: the misuse that caused TD-1529's two cards is refused, with the cure.
- A blocked report that waits on someone else's question stays its own counted item. Drawing it under that
  question was proposed and set aside by default: answering the other question does not tell this session, and
  making it work needed rules for every way a question can close. The techlead has the choice as a steer.
- While a blocked report stands, the entry it is about stays held on its card, so the card shows one need.
- A session that names nothing but holds an open question to you is warned, not refused: it may be blocked on
  something else. Asking a fresh question after a blocked report is warned the same way.

## What it rules out

- A session reporting blocked beside its own new question, the shape behind TD-1529.
- A blocked report shown as a decision, and an entry's card counting its question and the blocked report twice.
- A reply to a blocked report that leaves it sitting in Needs you.

## What you will see

- Fewer Decide items: a blocked report reads as Do, with Reply and Dismiss, and leaves when you reply.
- In FYI, a replied report says you replied; a report replaced by a later one shows what it was.
- A handed entry that came back blocked keeps its card held while it is blocked, with no day limit.
