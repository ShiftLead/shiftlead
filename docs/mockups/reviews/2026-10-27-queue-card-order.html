What each shape does with a raised row

  • A card is every entry its links join, so a blocker is always on the same card; it can never sit on a card ranked lower.
  • Ranking moves the whole card. Within a card the order comes from the links: TD-909 is High, but in A it sits below TD-916 and TD-917 because it closes after them, and says so. In B and C it heads its group, and 2 of 8 parts open says the same.
  • Where a row is ready, it is the first row in its place in A; in B and C the first open part in each group.
