What the drawing decides

Four groups

Recently completed, In progress, Waiting, Up next. Waiting holds every card with nothing to pick among what is not yours: a date, an event, settling, a blocker past the org's reach (TD-900 moved here after review round 1: its open rows all wait). The card carries its date, or event, or blocked, where Up next draws the number, and says the place it would take; when the wait ends it is back in Up next at that place. Only Up next is numbered.

Three wants
Decidea question with answers, a steer, a board line, a hold of yours
Doan entry that names you as owner
Readnotes, what was answered for you; folded with its count
Three verbs
Answerthe item's answers as buttons, the recommended one outlined, and Answer… for your own words; Done and Lift the hold are answers
Start sessiononce per card, in its header; the Do verb. The session gets the card and the item you came from
⋯the team's controls: Hold (keep the team off this), Back to design, Done on an entry, Open on the Repo page
No dates of yours on a card

No Snooze, no Put on the board, no due date. The rank is the snooze. Waiting is the ledger's own dates. The Inbox keeps its dates for what names no entry.

An item leads with one sentence

A question or a task to you states itself in one sentence a cold reader understands, then what each answer leads to, then its context folded. The rule goes in dev-cadence, the home refuses an ask to you without it, and a backfill rewrites the open entries that name you. The two session questions above were rewritten to show the shape.

The tree has lines

Two pixels down each level, in the branch's colour: green done, teal waiting, amber yours, grey open. Mentions on a row are one phrase, the subjects on hover.
