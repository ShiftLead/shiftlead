What the three share

Same style means same kind of thing. Tabs are the pages, in the order of their keys, and the open page is the lit one. The readings are one monospace strip, dim, no borders, so nothing in it looks like a button; each is still a link where it was one. The tools are three quiet glyphs and the one filled button, New, which stays the only filled thing in the bar. When the bar does not fit, the readings and tools wrap under the tabs; under 720px the readings take a row of their own and the host is left off it, as today.
