Phone Focus: the terminal takes the screen

Design round for TD-003, 2026-11-01. Today a phone gets the desktop page stacked into one column: two rows of top bar, the identity and acts lines, a terminal at half the screen, sixteen soft keys that scroll sideways, an open composer, then every side panel. These frames give the terminal everything from the session strip down to one key bar and one input bar. Every other control moves into sheets.

Each frame is a 375 × 700 viewport, a small phone with the browser's own bars already gone (the page added to the home screen). Terminal text is 12 px, which is 50 columns at that width.
