## §6

- 2026-11-11 (TD-1498 slice 1): *A send that did not land* is built for a home's own sessions. `_unavailable` tells a session that a filing verb did not land (`missed: true` under `--json`), or that a read did nothing. Before exit 3, a filing verb (`_missed_verb`) writes `{at, verb}` to `$SHIFTLEAD_HOME/missed/<session id>-<at>.json` (`_write_missed`, a temporary name then a rename). The home's tick reads the marks every tick (`_missed_pass`), notes the newest record (or an empty seat) and tells the person where no live record is left, then deletes what it read. The skill's and every brief's exit-3 line tell a filing verb from a read. A node's own marks are the next slice.
- 2026-11-11 (TD-1498 slice 2, the last): a node's own marks. A node's tick reads its marks (`_missed_marks`) and sends them to the home over its link as `missed {marks: [{id, at, verb}]}`. It deletes them once the home has taken them, and keeps them while the link is down or the home refuses. The home's `_take_missed` reads each id as `id@<node>`, refuses a batch that holds a mark not three strings or naming another host, and notes each through `_missed_note`, as for its own marks.
