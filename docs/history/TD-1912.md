## §2

- 2026-11-17 (TD-1912): goal 12's Focus pane measures character widths by Unicode 11, through xterm.js's unicode11 addon (0.8.0, vendored beside the other addons), in place of xterm.js's own Unicode 6 tables. Paul saw text cut off and lines repeated in Focus (2026-11-16); emoji, ZWJ sequences and symbols such as ⚠️ took other cells in the browser than tmux gives them, so wrapped lines and box drawing drifted. Read against tmux 3.5a's `#{cursor_x}` in a headless browser on the vendored files: `a😀b`, `✅x`, `中文x` and a mixed line now match; a ZWJ family (`👨‍👩‍👧`) and ⚠ with U+FE0F do not, since xterm.js measures grapheme clusters only through its graphemes addon, published for xterm 6 alone — filed as TD-1918. tmux's own history duplicating lines on a resize is a separate cause, which this does not change.
