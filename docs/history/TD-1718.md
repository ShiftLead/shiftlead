## §4.7

- 2026-11-13 (TD-1718, TD-1491's phase 2): reports from other installs designed. A run of `/shiftlead-feedback` at
  an install whose person cannot push to shiftlead (no `shiftlead` repo registered, or `gh` says no push) runs in
  report mode: it reads that install's seats as phase 1 does, dedupes against the three public ledgers by sparse
  shallow clones, and writes a feedback report, its approved findings scrubbed of the install's names by code
  (`ao feedback report`) and publish-checked, which the person submits themselves through an issue form on
  `ShiftLead/shiftlead`. A file-mode run here reads the open reports beside its seats (`ao feedback intake`), and
  its PR closes them. No setting: the run, the approval and the submission are the person's. Builds TD-1794,
  TD-1795, TD-1796.
