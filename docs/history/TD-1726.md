  the name is taken, with `{**os.environ, **spec.env}`, and writes the node-owned `tool_version` on the record. The
  seat smoke reads through the same method (`smoke_version`), and `seat_run.tool_version` is gone. The home's
  comparison and note are TD-1727's.
