    drop of a work order the home's reading holds (`_release`), and the member's claim of the order clears it.
  - `_lane_news` writes such an order into `lane_seen` untold (`_released_untold`), before its early returns. A
    line answered again stays news, and its `told` moves only where the note is sent.
  - The keep-running pass removes a mark whose order the reading no longer holds (`_released_prune`), on every
    record. `_carry` copies the mark beside `lane_seen`.
