## §4.4

- 2026-11-11 (TD-1589 slice 1, grinder-ao-2): a poll that fails says why in the log: the Claude Code adapter's `usage_for` puts `detail` beside a failed reason (`HTTP <code> <reason>`, or the exception's class and message, at most 200 characters; *a body of no known shape* for a 200 that does not parse), and the core's once-per-change line carries it. Between 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-10 the journal held 19 `error` ↔ `rate_limited` changes with no cause.
- 2026-11-11 (TD-1589 slice 2, grinder-ao-2): the status line sends its report from a child that waits `REPORT_WAIT` (5 s a step), out of the status line's process group and holding none of its pipes, while the status line waits only its second. All 23 reports refused as *unknown* on 2026-11-11 up to 12:30 MST were `unreadable` — the reporter had given up at its 1 s timeout and exited before the host agent read its `/proc` entry — and 22 of them fell within 45 minutes of a host agent start, when the loop lags (no hook, which waits 5 s, was refused so). Since slice 1 went live (09:02 MST) the poll's reason stayed `ok` all day, the account's reports keeping its reading young; the endpoint's small allowance (Oct 25–Nov 10, mostly `rate_limited`) is now written in *The endpoint is the fallback*.

## §4.8a

- 2026-11-11 (TD-1589 slice 1, grinder-ao-2): a request judged *unknown* other than a late hook is logged with its peer and its signal. 88 `usage_report`s were refused as *unknown* between 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-10, and the alarm line could not say whether the peer was gone before the read (`unreadable`), or was in another namespace or a detached cgroup.
