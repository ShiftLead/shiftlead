## §4.4

- 2026-11-12 (TD-1686, grinder-ao-1): built *An entry no lane takes is marked*, slice 1 — the mark and the counts.
  - `ledger.lane_gap(entry, lanes)` gives `(case, why)`: the team case, then the kind word, then the owner words
    of the lanes that take it, sorted and said once. `ledger.mark_no_lane` copies the reading, drops the old marks,
    marks again, and rebuilds `by_kind` with `no-lane`. It sets the reading's `no_lane: [{id, why}]`.
  - The host agent marks on every reading it stores before the compare: `_refresh_repos` (`_mark_lanes`) and
    `_reread_board`. The lanes come from every record of the checkout that carries a lane, live or ended, never
    a superseded one (`_repo_lanes`). An unserved repo marks nothing, and a reading with `error` is left.
  - `teamrun.lane_kinds` and `ao repo`'s first line subtract a settling or waiting entry only when it is not
    marked. The Queue's `_status` and `_wait`'s `far()` read the mark as `NO_LANE`'s kinds.
  - Left: the note to the taker (`no_lane_noted.json`, `NO_LANE_NOTE_AFTER`).
- 2026-11-12 (TD-1686, grinder-ao-1): built *The note to the taker*, slice 2.
  - `sessionorc.no_lane.step` keeps `no_lane_noted.json` (`paths.no_lane_noted_file`), keyed by `settle_key`. Each
    checkout has its own `{from, marked}`, and the origin's spent `ids` are shared. A first pass clocks every mark
    and spends the `owner` ones. After that, an `owner` mark held 30 minutes (`NO_LANE_NOTE_AFTER`) and not
    claimed is spent and due. A `team` or `kind` mark, or a claimed one, keeps its clock running.
  - The host agent's `_mark_lanes` runs the step after marking (`_note_no_lane`). It finds the taker as the newest
    `ids.json` `given` for the id (`agent_ids.given_of`), resolved by `_address` to a record the home holds,
    never the person. It sends that record an uncharged `system` note. `_refresh_repos` drops a gone checkout's
    key (`_drop_no_lane_roots`).
