## §4.6

- 2026-11-17 (TD-1922): *A touch does not select* names no xterm.js release, so the next bump leaves it true. Probed on the vendored `@xterm/xterm` 6.0.0 in headless Chromium with touch on: a tap, a long press, a long press then drag, and a quick drag leave `term.getSelection()` empty, while a mouse drag selects. A double tap selects a word (xterm's double click) only through the browser's emulated mouse clicks; the Focus terminal prevents those on a phone (§4.6).
