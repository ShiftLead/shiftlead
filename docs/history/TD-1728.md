## §4.9

- 2026-11-12 (TD-1728, grinder-ao-2): built TD-1671's *A scaled seat's bounds* for the pair bound.
  `agent_mail._check_bounds` sums each side's `pair:` tallies over both ends' `mail.seat_records` (read through
  `_pair_peek`, which creates no key), refuses when either sum is at `PAIR_BOUND`, marks `bound_hit` only on the read
  tallies with a count, and names a seat record by `mail.seat_name` (lifted from `seat_records`); mail within one
  seat reads its two records' tallies alone and names them by id. A forgotten seat record leaves the pair count on
  both sides, since it is no record of either seat: a sender with 3 reply-less sends to a seat's own record and 3 to an
  extra, the bound 5, is refused, and once the extra is forgotten its next send is not (the Fix's test line said
  *still refuse*; the techlead's answer to the steer, m-ba36fcc85738, held to the design's sums and reworded it).
