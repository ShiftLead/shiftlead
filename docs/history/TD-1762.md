  one card whose own id is the page's (`card_page_find`: whole, then spelled by `_refs`; a loose id by its spelled

  `notify.queue_path` writes `/card/…` for Telegram and push; the Queue's `showAddress` is gone; the opening fallback
  (`card_page_goto`) runs in the page's first client read. A mail item's Back from a card's page is
