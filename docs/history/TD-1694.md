## §4.10

- 2026-11-12 (TD-1694, grinder-ao-1): built *A `blocked` outcome is never a question's stand-in* and *is not the
  last word*, slice 1 — the send (`agent_mail._msg`).
  - A `blocked` outcome whose text names the caller's own open question to the person (its `superseded_by`
    chain's too), or the rerouted original of one, is refused with the thread cure; one sent while the caller
    holds an open question on the outcome's own thread is refused with *wait for its answer*, named or not.
    One that names neither lands, its reply's `warnings` naming each open question the caller holds.
  - An `ask` or `steer` to the person without `--thread`, from a caller holding an unreplied `blocked`
    outcome, lands with *your blocked outcome on m-… is open*.
  - A recorded `blocked` outcome is replaced by a newer `done` or `dropped` and by a question on its thread
    (`asked_again`), kept as the new outcome's `was`; a second `blocked` is refused as settled. On a handed
    entry a question on its thread clears the outcome, so the entry owes again, and keeps it as the entry's
    `was` (`MailEntry.was`), which its next outcome takes and a move carries.
  - Left: the person's Reply recorded (`replied`), the notify row, the thread view, Dismiss, keeping an
    unreplied one, and the briefs and the skill.
- 2026-11-12 (TD-1694, grinder-ao-1): slice 2 — *`blocked` is not a dead end*.
  - A person's reply whose `reply_to` is a question with a `blocked` outcome, or that outcome's reporting
    note, writes `replied: {at, by}` on its outcome on every copy, a first reply only; a handed entry's
    outcome is never stamped. `agent_notify` raises no `blocked:` row for a replied one.
  - Dismiss of an unreplied `blocked` question tells its asker (*they want nothing more on it*) and writes
    `dismissed` with the blocked one as `was` on every copy; a replied one is dismissed silently; a handed
    entry's Dismiss writes `was` beside `dismissed` too.
  - `_keep` keeps an unreplied `blocked` outcome with no age where an owing question is kept; a replied one
    runs FYI's window from `replied.at`. `ao inbox --thread` prints *replied* and *was blocked: <line>*, and
    `_oldest_first` orders a replaced outcome's note after the answer and before its replacement.
  - Left: the briefs and the skill.
