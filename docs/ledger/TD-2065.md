## TD-2065: Session starts obey a team's counts, team limit and login order, and a team has a started mark

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 33c0146a649e4b5da09b4dedfb154a09
**Blocked by:** TD-2060, TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** The page sets how many sessions a stage may have and which logins they use, but the builds that start sessions (TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053) start them with no count and on the launch path they have.

**Ask:** Should the builders make session starts obey a team's counts and login order?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *Sessions at once*, *The logins, in order* and *Start and Wind down*, and §4.9c's support seats that act for a repo: the Offer act and an audit's start hold to the per-stage numbers and the team limit, stages before audits; every launch of a stage, audit or support session takes the first usable login through TD-2060's function; a session whose login stops being usable is relaunched on the next, spending nothing; the started mark written by Start, the schedule and on_work and cleared by Wind down and the stop time, read wherever the tick asks whether a team is started; the three repo-wide support seats run only for the team that looks after the repo. Dark until the cut-over: no team is switched before it.

**Done when:** Tests on fixtures show a stage never holds more sessions than its number, the team limit holds with stage work before an audit, a launch skips an unusable login and waits when none is usable, and a stopped team starts nothing.
