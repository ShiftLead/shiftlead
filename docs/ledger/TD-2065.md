## TD-2065: Session starts obey a team's counts and team limit, a team has a started mark, and a repo's seats run while any of its teams is started

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 33c0146a649e4b5da09b4dedfb154a09
**Blocked by:** TD-2060, TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** The page sets how many sessions a stage may have and whether the team is started, but the builds that start sessions (TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053) start them with no count and with a started reading that is always false.

**Ask:** Should the builders make session starts obey a team's counts and its started mark?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *Sessions at once*, *Start and Wind down*, *Holds new work* of *When it runs* and *A repo's seats belong to the repo, and run while any of its teams is started*, with §4.9c's support seats that act for a repo: the Offer act and an audit's start hold to the per-stage numbers and the team limit, stages before audits; the started mark and who stopped the team, written by Start and the schedule and cleared by Wind down and the stop time, read wherever the tick asks whether a team is started; after the cut-over, no entry without a stage record is offered while the team is over a Balance line; the three repo-wide support seats and the tagging of untagged entries run while any of the repo's teams is started, each start taking its model and login from the first, by name, of the repo's teams that is started and can run its flow, no other team's copy running, with nothing stored; the line that names that team, or says the seats wait because no team of the repo is started or no started one can run its flow (a node's team counting as able unless its node reported otherwise, and a seat's session launching on the host of the team it runs from), on each team page of a repo with several teams and on the Repo page. Not here: taking the team's login at a launch, the move down the list and the handoff are TD-1976's design and its builds; TD-2060's pick function is what they call. Dark until the cut-over: no team is switched before it. The home's team record holds `started` and, when it is cleared, `stopped: {at, by}`: the stop time writes it only when it clears a set mark, Start deletes it, and the schedule's start at a reset starts a team that is not started and has no `stopped` record or one whose `by` is the stop time, never one the person stopped (§6 *Schedule*, after the cut-over).

**Done when:** Tests on fixtures show a stage never holds more sessions than its number, the team limit holds with stage work before an audit, a team stopped by the person starts nothing when work appears, a team over a Balance line is offered no new entry while its entries in stages carry on, a repo-wide seat on a repo with two teams keeps starting when the first team is wound down and the second is started, starts from one team only, and waits when neither is started, and passes over a started team that cannot run its flow.
