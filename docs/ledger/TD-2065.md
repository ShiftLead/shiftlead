## TD-2065: Session starts obey a team's counts and team limit, a team has a started mark, and a repo's seats run while any of its teams is started

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 33c0146a649e4b5da09b4dedfb154a09
**Blocked by:** TD-2060, TD-2066, TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** The page sets how many sessions a stage may have and whether the team is started, but the builds that start sessions (TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053) start them with no count and with a started reading that is always false.

**Ask:** Should the builders make session starts obey a team's counts and its started mark?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *Sessions at once*, *Start and Wind down*, *Holds new work* of *When it runs* and *When they run* of *The built-in seats and their page*, with §4.9c's built-in seats that act for a repo: the Offer act and an audit's start hold to the per-stage numbers and the team limit, stages before audits; the started mark and who stopped the team, written by Start and the schedule and cleared by Wind down and the stop time, read wherever the tick asks whether a team is started; after the cut-over, no entry without a stage record is offered while the team is over a Balance line; the three built-in seats that act for a repo (board answers, pull requests nobody holds, the sweeper) and the tagging call run while any of the repo's teams is started and wait only while none is, a tech lead's and a manager's while their own team is started and on while a wound-down team's sessions finish, each ending as §4.9d *The built-in seats and their page* says when a team is wound down or reaches its stop time; their models and logins are the repo's built-in set's, read through TD-2066's function, and nothing of them is taken from a team. Not here: taking the team's login at a launch, the move down the list and the handoff are TD-1976's design and its builds; TD-2060's pick function is what they call. Dark until the cut-over: no team is switched before it. The home's team record holds `started` and, when it is cleared, `stopped: {at, by}`: the stop time writes it only when it clears a set mark, Start deletes it, and the schedule's start at a reset starts a team that is not started and has no `stopped` record or one whose `by` is the stop time, never one the person stopped (§6 *Schedule*, after the cut-over).

**Done when:** Tests on fixtures show a stage never holds more sessions than its number, the team limit holds with stage work before an audit, a team stopped by the person starts nothing when work appears, a team over a Balance line is offered no new entry while its entries in stages carry on, a repo's seat on a repo with two teams keeps starting when one team is wound down and the other is started, waits when neither is started, and is ended by a stop time only when it stops the repo's last started team, and a wound-down team's tech lead still answers a session that is finishing.
