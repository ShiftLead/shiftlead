## TD-2065: Session starts obey a team's counts and team limit, a team has a started mark, and one team looks after a repo

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 33c0146a649e4b5da09b4dedfb154a09
**Blocked by:** TD-2060, TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** The page sets how many sessions a stage may have and which logins they use, but the builds that start sessions (TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053) start them with no count and on the launch path they have.

**Ask:** Should the builders make session starts obey a team's counts and login order?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *Sessions at once*, *Start and Wind down*, *Holds new work* of *When it runs* and *Where a repo has several teams, one of them looks after the repo*, with §4.9c's support seats that act for a repo: the Offer act and an audit's start hold to the per-stage numbers and the team limit, stages before audits; the started mark and who stopped the team, written by Start, the schedule and on_work and cleared by Wind down and the stop time, read wherever the tick asks whether a team is started; after the cut-over, no entry without a stage record is offered while the team is over a Balance line; the three repo-wide support seats and the tagging of untagged entries run only for the team that looks after the repo, and the Repo page says when that team is stopped. Not here: taking the team's login at a launch, the move down the list and the handoff are TD-1976's design and its builds; TD-2060's pick function is what they call. Dark until the cut-over: no team is switched before it.

**Done when:** Tests on fixtures show a stage never holds more sessions than its number, the team limit holds with stage work before an audit, a team stopped by the person starts nothing when work appears, a team over a Balance line is offered no new entry while its entries in stages carry on, and a repo-wide seat starts only for the team that looks after the repo.
