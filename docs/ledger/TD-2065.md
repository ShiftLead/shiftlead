## TD-2065: Session starts obey a team's counts and team limit, a team has a started mark, and a repo's seats run while any of its teams is started

**Kind:** build

**Why:** The page sets how many sessions a stage may have and whether the team is started, but the builds that start sessions (TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053) start them with no count and with a started reading that is always false.

**Ask:** Should the builders make session starts obey a team's counts and its started mark?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *Sessions at once*, *Start and Wind down*, *Holds new work* of *When it runs* and *When they run* of *The built-in seats and their page*, with §4.9c's built-in seats that act for a repo: the Offer act and an audit's start hold to the per-stage numbers and the team limit, stages before audits; the started mark and who stopped the team, written by Start and the schedule and cleared by Wind down and the stop time, read wherever the tick asks whether a team is started; after the cut-over, no entry without a stage record is offered while the team is over a Balance line; the four built-in seats that act for a repo (board answers, pull requests nobody holds, the prompts seat, the sweeper) and the tagging call run while any of the repo's teams is started and wait only while none is, a tech lead's while its own team is started and on while a wound-down team's sessions finish (the prompts seat likewise for a finishing session of its repo), each ending as §4.9d *The built-in seats and their page* says when a team is wound down or reaches its stop time; their models and logins are the repo's built-in set's, read through TD-2066's function, and nothing of them is taken from a team. Not here: taking the team's login at a launch, the move down the list and the handoff are TD-1976's design and its builds; TD-2060's pick function is what they call. Dark until the cut-over: no team is switched before it. The home's team record holds `started` and, when it is cleared, `stopped: {at, by}`: the stop time writes it only when it clears a set mark, Start deletes it, and the schedule's start at a reset starts a team that is not started and has no `stopped` record or one whose `by` is the stop time, never one the person stopped (§6 *Schedule*, after the cut-over). It also builds design §4.9d *How each one runs* for the three seats of policy rule 3: the home's tick writes the closed seat record for a tech lead (`techlead-<team>`), a sweeper (`sweeper-<repo>`, where `sweep.on`) and a prompts seat (`prompts-<repo>`, only where the org has no director), each marked `builtin` with its seat key and written with its launch (the seat's role, that role's brief from the package with the repo's supplement, the repo's checkout on the record's host, the role's grants, no controllers), the sweeper's mark `{trigger: swept, after: <sweep.every>, by: settings}` and only while `sweep.on` and the repo's own switch is not off; a first-shape record of the same name is superseded and its mail kept, and a record whose host changes is superseded by one on the new host, mail moved; the host is read again only while the record is closed; a record is held while no fill is allowed, and not while a wound-down team's session finishes; deleting a team removes its tech lead's record; the home also writes the director's record and launch, reading `org.yml`'s `director:` and, for its Org block, `projects:`, and nothing else of that file, exactly as a first-shape Start writes them today (its own directory, the Org block, `permit`, unattended and supervised, on the home) and never held; the director is the `prompt` record with no `builtin` and a prompts seat the one with `builtin: prompts`, a test every reader of the director's record makes (`_is_director`, the mail and card readers); the home refuses `ao allow` and `ao deny` from a prompts record on a record of another repo; every tech lead record carries `tags: true` and the `tag` cause is due only on that of the repo's first started team by name; each seat's launch is in a worktree of the seat's own name; the director's Org block reads `org.yml`'s `projects:` as a Start does, and its record is removed once the key is gone and no session of it is live; the pick skips, for the director and a prompts seat, a login whose `unattended_args` leave its prompts on; a record whose *When they run* no longer holds is held as a stopped team's seat is; the `tag` cause sits on the tech lead's record of the first started team in the repo's order; a tech lead's fills are bounded at 25 an hour (`TECHLEAD_FILL_CEILING`), each built-in seat's fills counted for that seat alone; the prompts seat's `prompt` cause reads the unattended sessions of its repo only, its teams' and its built-in seats', with the director seat's rights for a prompt. There is no manager after the cut-over and no record is due by `team`. The tick fires a switched team's schedule on its login (TD-2091 stored the rule and drew the row, and the tick skips such a rule until this build): it resolves the login as the row does, the rule's login while it is in the team's list, else the first, reads that login's account for the reset, and starts nothing where the list is empty or the resolved login is metered, so no reset start spends metered money. `_schedule_starts` then answers the rule's next reset, which it leaves unanswered for a login's rule until this build.

**Done when:** Tests on fixtures show a stage never holds more sessions than its number, the team limit holds with stage work before an audit, a team stopped by the person starts nothing when work appears, a team over a Balance line is offered no new entry while its entries in stages carry on, a repo's seat on a repo with two teams keeps starting when one team is wound down and the other is started, waits when neither is started, and is ended by a stop time only when it stops the repo's last started team, and a wound-down team's tech lead still answers a session that is finishing, and starts nothing once its last session has ended. A started team with no tech lead record gets one on the next tick, marked `builtin`, and an ask sent with `--team` resolves to it; a second tick writes no second record; a wound-down team's record, once no session of the team is left, is held and keeps its mail; a prompts record is written for a repo only where the org has no director, and its cause names a session of its own repo's team with a prompt pending and none of another repo's; the ceiling's count reads a tech lead's record with 24 fills in the hour as under it and one with 25 as at it, and a sweeper's with six as at it; the launch written with a record names the repo's checkout and its role's brief (that a fill is made from it on a login is TD-2068's test); on an org whose `org.yml` has `director:` the home writes the director's record and no prompts record, and the director's reading is false of a prompts record; a seat whose session is live when its host would change keeps its record until that session ends; a deleted team's tech lead record is gone; a sweeper's record is held while either sweep switch is off and a prompts record once the org gains a director; a seat whose host changes leaves one current record of its name; no record is due by `team`. The director's record the home writes is unattended and supervised, in the director's own directory, and is due for a prompt while no team is started; `ao allow` from a prompts record on another repo's record is refused; of two started teams of one repo both tech lead records carry `tags: true`, only the first by name is due by `tag`, and an entry request from the second team resolves to its own tech lead. A switched team's schedule fires at its login's account's reset; with its login removed it reads the first login's; with an empty list, or a metered login first, it starts nothing.
