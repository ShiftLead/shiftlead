## TD-1976: The switch-over from profiles to logins has no procedure: the cut-over window's design

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-19
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 186cf164e9f8433b9ceb625cc12d63c5
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** The frame's *One window, no dual read* (Paul, 2026-11-18) moves every host from profiles to logins and every team repo to the new shape in one window. The Tools round (TD-1953) designed what each host's move makes (§4.2b *What the switch-over makes*) and left how the window runs to its own design, after the Flows (TD-1954) and Team (TD-1153) rounds, whose shapes it carries.

**Ask:** Will you design the switch-over window with a session once the team page design is merged?

**Fix:** To design with Paul, inheriting TD-1953's ledger list (reshaped rounds 2 and 3): the order of the home and the nodes, and a launch or team start across hosts of the other shape refused; a switched host reading an old-shape `.shiftlead.yml` marks the repo not moved over; `ao migrate logins [--check|--name|--back]` as a subcommand beside today's `ao migrate`, run with the host agent stopped and resumable, `logins.yml` written before `profiles.yml` goes; fresh hosts, backups, both files present; `--back` refused while post-window sessions or teams run, stub profiles for post-window logins; a profile shim from logins for every `s.profile` reader, the record's `profile` rewritten to its login name; the rewrite of `org.yml` `director:` and `roles:` and `settings.yml` `sweep:` and `schedule:` to `model:` and `logins:` in one commit; the `defs.TRACKED` swap; writing the record's `login`, `account` and `seat_model` at launch; container-node provisioning from team login lists (§4.4a); the Forget refusals that read team lists; `ao new --login`; the transcript handoff for a relaunch on another login (the frame).

**Done when:** The design round with Paul has written the window's procedure and filed its builds.
