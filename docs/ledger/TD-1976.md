**Why:** The frame's *One window, no dual read* (Paul, 2026-11-18) moves every host from profiles to logins and every team repo to the new shape in one window. The Tools round (TD-1953) designed what each host's move makes (§4.2b *What the switch-over makes*) and left how the window runs to its own design, after the Flows (TD-1954) and Team (TD-1153) rounds, whose shapes it carries.

**Ask:** Should the builders build the switch-over's six parts, starting with the switch that turns the old shape off?

**Fix:** Designed with Paul, 2026-11-22: design 4.9e. No page: three client commands (ao switch-over check, move, undo) and an answers file a session fills by interviewing the person; the window's nine steps with a journal and a backup; repos are no step of it; the window writes nothing for work in flight, an entry's first record taking the open pull request that is its work; undo until the first Start; the first shape is turned off at the marker and its code removed afterwards by TD-2056; the launch on a login, the move down the list, the transcript handoff and the node's side. The builds are TD-2068 to TD-2072 and TD-2076; TD-2073 is the run of the window itself.

**Done when:** The six builds TD-2068 to TD-2072 and TD-2076 have merged.
