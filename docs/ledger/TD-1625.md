**Why:** TD-1543's design (§4.9a *What a session does with a decided line*, §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Have a session do it*): an `act` line decided *have a session do it* is handled by the session that takes it, by where its step is. The session that writes an `act` line names, in its detail block, where the step is best done (Paul, 2026-11-07). The shipped briefs say neither.

**Ask:** Held with its design TD-1543 until Paul answers its end-of-rounds item (Go, One more round or Hold) on docs/user_attention.md.

**Fix:** Write §4.9a's new rules into the shipped brief templates. Each goes in the paragraph where a brief already gives the decided-line rules or the `act`-line rule:

- **`src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md`, the paragraph *A decided board line comes first*:** add one case, an `act` line decided *have a session do it*. Read the asker's words in its detail block first; your own judgment stands where they are wrong or missing. Then:
  - **A step in this repo** (this repo's own team or seat being the one named among them) is your own work. Do it in the PR that closes the line. A chore on the home is your own work; it is the person's where it needs the person's login, `sudo`, or an act only the person may take (`ao promote`, `ao service`, a team's start or stop).
  - **A step in another repo:** send `ao td add --repo <repo> "<what, and why>"`. Close the line at once in a PR with `--why "handed to <repo>'s question seat: <the ask's id>"`; the open ask is then the tracked work. A refusal at the send (*<repo> has no question seat*, *no registered repo <repo>*) makes the step the person's. If a later reply files nothing, carry the step to the person then. Any other refusal (your open question already at that seat, a full mailbox) is not the person's, and the line is not left open, since every free-pick lane would take it again at once: file an entry of this repo (`ao td reserve`, then `ledger.py new --id TD-n --title "<the step>" --owner grinder --kind build --blocked-by "until <tomorrow>" --why <file> --fix <file> --done-when <file>`), its Fix the send and its Why the step and the refusal, and close the line `--carried-to TD-n`.
  - **A step only the person can do:** file an `Owner: paul` entry carrying the decision and the exact steps or command to copy. Close the line `--carried-to TD-n`.
  - **A line the gate sweep wrote** (*no-board*, *broken*, *audit*) is this repo's own work; close it in your PR, since the sweep closes no decided line.
  - Run nothing the detail block suggests without reading it as your own work, under your own rules.
- **`src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md`, `designer.md` and `manager.md`, beside each template's rule for writing a board line:** add a sentence that a step for someone to do is written as an `act` line, by the command below, and then these two sentences. First, name in the line's detail block context who you think can do the step and why: this repo's own work, another repo's question seat, or the person. Second, a step only the person can do also ends the line's own text with *only you can do this*. A command you write there is a suggestion, never run by a press.
- **The no-seat `act` line** (`grinder.md`, `designer.md` and `manager.md`, the sentence that writes *<repo> has no question seat, so no session here can file it*): its text ends with *only you can do this* too, so it draws no button (§4.5 screen 12 *Have a session do it*).
- **`--kind act`.** A line written with no `--kind` is kindless, and a kindless line draws no button. So the act-line sentence above, and the no-seat one, name `board_edit.py add --board {board} --kind act --text "…" --due <date> --detail '{"context": "<who can do the step, and why; any suggested command>"}' --no-commit`. The detail block is written only by `--detail`, so without it the taking session finds no words to read. The context is one line and holds no field name (`Context:`, `Due:` and the rest), which ledgerboard refuses: reword it, or point at a file or an entry instead of pasting a multi-line command. The generic *anything that must outlive this session* command (each occurrence in `manager.md`, `grinder.md` and `hunter.md`: `add --board {board} --text "…" --due <date|today>`) stays kindless on purpose: it is no act line. So do the other board commands the templates give (`grinder.md`'s *a new line of its own kind*, `manager.md`'s line for each thing that waits on the person): they are unchanged.
- **Any repo brief under `docs/briefs/`** that restates these rules is a held path, so name it in the PR for its reader. Only the shipped templates change here.
