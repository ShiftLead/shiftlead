## TD-1516: A restart after the session's own none is taken, and an owed outcome rides the hand-off to the next run (TD-1485 build 1)

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3483c61422a2456ea097c7fc9b14f478
**Part of:** TD-1485
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent.py (_ending, rpc_create, rpc_restart, _close); src/sessionorc/agent_attention.py (_asker_gone); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_replay, the tick's closes); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (the owed line); src/shiftlead/cli.py (cmd_progress docstring); src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md, designer.md; tests; design §4.9a *The third declaration*, §4.10 *Outcomes*

**Why:** `ao progress restart` is refused while the session's own `none` stands (seen 3 times on two seats, each then claiming an entry just to be allowed to restart), and while it owes an outcome, though every replay after a restart keeps the mail and the debt with it (TD-1485). Design §4.9a *The third declaration* now lets the later declaration stand and lets an owed outcome ride the hand-off.

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *The third declaration* (TD-1485's changes to it):
1. **The later declaration stands** (`src/sessionorc/agent.py` `_ending`): a `restart` while `out_of_work` stands clears `out_of_work` and `lane_seen` and writes `restart_wanted`; a `none` while `restart_wanted` stands clears it with `restart_blocked` and `restart_blocked_sent_at` (never `restart_ceiling`) and writes `out_of_work`. A `none` refused by the balance check clears nothing (that check runs before any clearing). The "refuse each other" refusal goes; the docstring, `src/shiftlead/cli.py`'s `cmd_progress` docstring (~3046) and `tests/test_cli.py` (~1995–2019, *already wants a restart*) say the new rule.
2. **An owed outcome rides the hand-off**: `restart` is no longer refused for `s.owed()`. `none` keeps its refusal. The unread refusal stays for both.
3. **A close made for a restart leaves the debt**: `_asker_gone`'s loop over answered-and-owing entries (agent_attention.py, its outcome marking through `agent_mail._mark`) skips a close of a record carrying `restart_wanted`, a close with `rpc_close`'s `restart`, and the tick's closes before a `keep_mail` replay (`_brief_restart`'s `brief`/`flow`, `_cache_restart`, `_lost_brief`) — the tick passes the same mark; only the home's close settles, so a node member's routed close needs none. Open questions still close or orphan at those closes as today (§6 rule 2). The debt is settled `asker_gone` when the name's mail ends: `_forget` (already), or a create under the name that carries neither `keep_mail` nor `succeeds` — `_take_name` replaces the old record in place (and `_take_supersession` for a node member) without settling, so the home calls the owed-entry settle there explicitly, before the replacement.
4. **The home names the debt** — a helper at the home, `owed_prompt(closed, prompt, carries)`, `carries` true for `keep_mail` or `succeeds`, applied to the prompt before any create is launched or routed: in the home's `rpc_create` after `_name_holder` and `_check_keep_mail` (~1555) and before `ad.launch` (reassigning the local `prompt`, which `launch_params(locals())` then stores). The `ARG_LIMIT` check (~1470) runs earlier, before the worktree is made and the holder known, so it reserves the line's bound: the line lists at most eight ids, then *and n more*, so its length has a constant maximum (`OWED_LINE_MAX`) the check adds to the prompt's; and in `_replay`'s and `rpc_restart`'s routed branches before `_route_act("create", …)` (a node's replica holds no mail, so the node's `rpc_create` is unchanged). It reads `owed()` before `_move_mail` empties the closed record's boxes. On every create it strips any `\n\n<OWED_LINE prefix>…` tail; with `carries`, a closed record whose `owed()` names ids (for a seat, those its `FILLED_FOR` line does not already name), and a non-empty prompt, it appends `\n\n` and the fixed line *[shiftlead] your last run owes the person an outcome on <ids> — report each with `ao msg person --outcome done|blocked|dropped "<one line>" --for <id>`*, always last. The prefix is a constant in `agent_common.py`. Nothing is stored on a mark.
5. **The templates**: `src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md` *A run that ends with work left* ("with nothing of this conversation"), `src/shiftlead/cli.py`'s matching line, and the designer template's *A run that ends with work left* sentence (inside its *Out of work* paragraph, line 25) gain one clause: an owed outcome follows you, its ids ending your next prompt.
6. Tests: a restart after the session's own `none` is taken and clears it, and the reverse; a balance-refused `none` clears nothing; a restart with an owed outcome is taken; the successor's prompt ends with the line through rule 2's replay, rule 7's brief restart of an idle member with an answered ask, a person's Restart, a controller's `ao close` then `ao new --keep-mail`, a rename's replay, and a node member's replay; a create without the mail settles the old run's debt `asker_gone`; a prompt at the argument limit less the reserve still launches with the line; a replay of a stored prompt carrying a settled debt's line drops it; a seat's lost-brief replay carries its *filled for* line and no owed line; the successor's `ao` replies carry `mail.owed`, and its `none` is refused until it reports; `none` with an owed outcome is still refused; a create without `keep_mail` gets no line.

**Done when:** A session's `restart` after its own `none` (and `none` after its own `restart`) is taken, the later word standing; a `restart` with an owed outcome is taken, and the next run's prompt names the owed ids in the fixed closing line while its `none` stays refused until they are reported; the tests above pass.
