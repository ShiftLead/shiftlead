## TD-1516: A restart after the session's own none is taken, and an owed outcome rides the hand-off to the next run (TD-1485 build 1)

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3483c61422a2456ea097c7fc9b14f478
**Part of:** TD-1485
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent.py (_ending, rpc_create's keep_mail); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (the owed line); src/shiftlead/cli.py (cmd_progress docstring); src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md, designer.md; tests/test_cli.py and new tests; design §4.9a *The third declaration*

**Why:** `ao progress restart` is refused while the session's own `none` stands (seen 3 times on two seats, each then claiming an entry just to be allowed to restart), and while it owes an outcome, though every replay after a restart keeps the mail and the debt with it (TD-1485). Design §4.9a *The third declaration* now lets the later declaration stand and lets an owed outcome ride the hand-off.

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *The third declaration* (TD-1485's changes to it):
1. **The later declaration stands** (`src/sessionorc/agent.py` `_ending`): a `restart` while `out_of_work` stands clears `out_of_work` and `lane_seen` and writes `restart_wanted`; a `none` while `restart_wanted` stands clears it with `restart_blocked` and `restart_blocked_sent_at` (never `restart_ceiling`) and writes `out_of_work`. A `none` refused by the balance check clears nothing (that check runs before any clearing). The "refuse each other" refusal goes; the docstring, `src/shiftlead/cli.py`'s `cmd_progress` docstring (~3046) and `tests/test_cli.py` (~1995–2019, *already wants a restart*) say the new rule.
2. **An owed outcome rides the hand-off**: `restart` is no longer refused for `s.owed()`. `none` keeps its refusal. The unread refusal stays for both.
3. **The create names the debt** (`rpc_create`, where `keep_mail` moves a closed record's mail): a create handed `keep_mail` that supersedes a closed record first strips from the handed prompt any `\n\n<OWED_LINE prefix>…` tail (a stored prompt or a seat's `FILLED_FOR` tail can carry one), then, when the closed record's `owed()` names any id and the record is not a seat and the prompt is non-empty, appends `\n\n` and the fixed line *[shiftlead] your last run owes the person an outcome on <ids> — report each with `ao msg person --outcome done|blocked|dropped "<one line>" --for <id>`*, always last. The line's prefix is a constant in `agent_common.py`. One place covers every tick replay (`_replay`, `_lost_brief`'s seat tail included), a person's Restart (`rpc_restart` → create) and a controller's `ao new --keep-mail`. Nothing is stored on a mark.
4. **The templates**: `src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md` *A run that ends with work left* ("with nothing of this conversation") and the designer template's same sentence gain one clause: an owed outcome follows you, its ids ending your next prompt.
5. Tests: a restart after the session's own `none` is taken and clears it, and the reverse; a balance-refused `none` clears nothing; a restart with an owed outcome is taken; the successor's prompt ends with the line through rule 2's replay, a person's Restart and a controller's `ao new --keep-mail`; a replay of a stored prompt carrying a settled debt's line drops it; a seat's lost-brief replay carries its *filled for* line and no owed line; the successor's `ao` replies carry `mail.owed`, and its `none` is refused until it reports; `none` with an owed outcome is still refused; a create without `keep_mail` gets no line.

**Done when:** A session's `restart` after its own `none` (and `none` after its own `restart`) is taken, the later word standing; a `restart` with an owed outcome is taken, and the next run's prompt names the owed ids in the fixed closing line while its `none` stays refused until they are reported; the tests above pass.
