**Status:** Open

**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent.py (_ending, rpc_restart, _refill_prompt); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_replay's closing, rule 2's replay); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py; tests; design §4.9a *The third declaration*

**Why:** `ao progress restart` is refused while the session's own `none` stands (seen 3 times on two seats, each then claiming an entry just to be allowed to restart), and while it owes an outcome, though every replay after a restart keeps the mail and the debt with it (TD-1485). Design §4.9a *The third declaration* now lets the later declaration stand and lets an owed outcome ride the hand-off.

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *The third declaration* (TD-1485's two changes to it) in `src/sessionorc/agent.py` `_ending`:
1. **The later declaration stands**: a `restart` while `out_of_work` stands clears `out_of_work` and `lane_seen` and writes `restart_wanted`; a `none` while `restart_wanted` stands clears it and writes `out_of_work` (its balance refusal still applies first). The "refuse each other" refusal goes; the docstring says the new rule.
2. **An owed outcome rides the hand-off**: `restart` is no longer refused for `s.owed()`; it writes the ids as `restart_wanted.owed`. `none` keeps its refusal. The unread refusal stays for both.
3. **The closing line**: every replay that follows a `restart_wanted` carrying `owed` — rule 2's tick replay and a person's Restart (`rpc_restart`'s `_refill_prompt`) — ends the prompt with the fixed line *[shiftlead] your last run owes the person an outcome on <ids> — report each with `ao msg person --outcome done|blocked|dropped "<one line>" --for <id>`*, through `_replay`'s `closing` (beside a seat's `FILLED_FOR`, which a member never has); a constant in `agent_common.py`. Read the ids again from the closed record's `owed()` at the replay, dropping any settled since the declaration.
4. Tests: a restart after the session's own `none` is taken and clears it, and the reverse; a restart with an owed outcome is taken, the successor's prompt ends with the line, its `ao` replies carry `mail.owed`, and its `none` is refused until it reports; `none` with an owed outcome is still refused.

**Done when:** A session's `restart` after its own `none` (and `none` after its own `restart`) is taken, the later word standing; a `restart` with an owed outcome is taken, and the next run's prompt names the owed ids in the fixed closing line while its `none` stays refused until they are reported; the tests above pass.
