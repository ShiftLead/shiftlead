**Why:** `ao progress restart` is refused while the session's own `none` stands (seen 3 times on two seats, each then claiming an entry just to be allowed to restart), and while it owes an outcome, though every replay after a restart keeps the mail and the debt with it (TD-1485). Design §4.9a *The third declaration* now lets the later declaration stand and lets an owed outcome ride the hand-off.

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *The third declaration* (TD-1485's changes to it) and §4.10 *Outcomes*' added sentence:
1. **The later declaration stands** (`src/sessionorc/agent.py` `_ending`): a `restart` while `out_of_work` stands clears `out_of_work` and `lane_seen` and writes `restart_wanted`; a `none` while `restart_wanted` stands clears that dict (its `early`, `decided` and `repeat` with it) and `restart_blocked` and `restart_blocked_sent_at` (never `restart_ceiling`) and writes `out_of_work`. The `other`-refusal block is deleted whole. A `none` refused by the balance check clears nothing (that check runs before any clearing). The docstring's *they refuse each other* (agent.py ~3999–4005), the owed check's comment (~4017–4024, *A restart owes them for a reason of its own*), the owed refusal's message (naming `none`, and a restart of a member that is not supervised), `src/shiftlead/cli.py`'s `cmd_progress` docstring (~3046) and `tests/test_cli.py` (~1995–2019, *already wants a restart*) say the new rule.
2. **An owed outcome rides the hand-off**: `restart` of a supervised member (`s.supervised`) is no longer refused for `s.owed()`; a member that is not supervised is refused as before. `none` keeps its refusal. The unread refusal stays for both. Nothing is added to the next run's prompt: its `mail.owed` on every `ao` reply, rule 4's nudge and its refused `none` already name the debt.
3. **The close made for a replay leaves the debt**: `_asker_gone` (agent_attention.py) takes a parameter, `keep_debt`, that skips its owed loop (the answered-and-owing entries it marks through `agent_mail._mark`); its open-question handling is unchanged. `_close` takes `keep_debt` too and passes it on; it is true only for `restart=True` (a person's Restart's own close) and where the tick's own call sites pass it explicitly — the closes before a replay with the mail (rule 2's `wanted`, `_brief_restart`'s `brief`/`flow`, `_cache_restart`, `_lost_brief`). The `rpc_close` RPC surface is unchanged: no caller of `ao close` can set it, so a person's Close, a controller's, a stop's, a wrap-up's (`_finished_close`), a seat's own (`_seat_done`) and `_forget` settle as today. Only the home's close settles, so a node member's routed close needs nothing.
4. **A create that does not carry the mail settles the debt**: once a create under a name without `keep_mail` or `succeeds` has succeeded — where `s.supersedes = [{…, "mail": kept}]` is written with `kept` false (agent.py ~1689), and `_take_supersession`'s replace in place for a node member (agent_remote.py ~960) — the home settles each owed person-inbox entry whose `from_` is the old run's address `asker_gone`, by a helper split from `_asker_gone`'s owed loop, touching no open question.
5. **The templates**: `src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md` *A run that ends with work left* ("with nothing of this conversation"), `src/shiftlead/cli.py`'s matching line, and the designer template's *A run that ends with work left* sentence (inside its *Out of work* paragraph, line 25) gain one clause: an owed outcome follows you; your first `ao` reply names it in `mail.owed`.
6. Tests: a restart after the session's own `none` is taken and clears it, and the reverse; a balance-refused `none` clears nothing; a restart with an owed outcome is taken, and after rule 2's replay, rule 7's brief restart, a person's Restart and a controller's `ao close` then `ao new --keep-mail` the successor's `ao` replies carry `mail.owed` and its `none` is refused until it reports; a rename's successor owes it; a person's Close of a `restart_wanted` record, a controller's or wrap-up's `ao close` of one, and a forget of one, settle it; a restart by a member that is not supervised is refused while it owes; a second restart carries the debt on (the next run's `mail.owed`, the guaranteed channel); a replay whose create fails after its close leaves the debt on the closed record; a create without the mail settles it after it succeeds and leaves it when the launch fails; `none` with an owed outcome is still refused.
