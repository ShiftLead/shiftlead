**Why:** Asked by the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (2026-11-05). Nothing shows a Queue card whole outside the UI. `ao rank --list` gives one line per card (its `--json` the rows only), `ao board show` one line, `ao inbox` the messages. So a person reading a clipped card, or a session checking what the person will see, rebuilds the card from the ledger files, the board and the PRs. Two things were found only that way on 2026-11-05. The first was the act line under shiftlead#TD-1392's card (lift `decision (paul)` from TD-1392 and its four builds) and four *Lift the hold* presses that wrote decided lines but lifted nothing. The second was contractmatch's board look shown under ledgerboard#TD-051's card, a cross-repo `item_home` match. Its tied-entry field would show it at a glance.

**Fix:** Design first (§4.7, §4.4 *The Queue*, §4.5 screen 12): `ao card <repo>#TD-n [--json]`, the whole Queue card as the UI draws it, from the host agent's reading. It shows the card's top entry and every row (state, page, gate or blocker, holder, PR). Then it shows every board line, ask and ledger row the Queue attached to each row, in full: board key, kind, the Context and detail block the UI folds under *▸ context*, answers, due. Each names the entry `item_home` or `ledger_home` tied it to. The design says what the view reads (the reading's cards and the Inbox items' homes), its text form, and that `--json` is the same shape the UI renders, so neither re-derives the card.

**Done when:** The design round has merged, and the builds it files carry `Part of:` this entry.
