**Design PR:** #4065

**Location:** design §6 rule 2 (early restarts), rule 3 (seat_due causes: open), rule 4 (the idle nudge), rule 6 (save a design's answer), rule 13 (Stuck work); §4.9a Early is decided from the record, What a session does with a decided line; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_idle_nudge, _wanted_restart, _team_causes, _designers, _stuck_pass, _stuck_orders); src/sessionorc/stuck.py; src/sessionorc/workorders.py (join); src/sessionorc/ledger.py (lane_matches)

**Fix:** A design round first: each piece below changes what the tick does, and revisits rules the design chose on purpose (an early restart waiting on the person; rule 13 pressing nothing).

1. **An early restart at a context bound.** §4.9a *Early is decided from the record* says the context reading does not lift an early mark and the `why` is never read. Weigh reversing that for a restart whose context reading is over the role's bound: a run's lack of new work is not a sign of a loop when the context is the reason. Else weigh whether an early restart still waiting after a set wait fills the on-call manager (a fifth `seat_due` cause), or is raised to the person as a Do item, rather than sitting on an Inbox row alone. TD-1856's card placement is the visibility half; this is the acting half.
2. **A member idle behind a restart nobody performs.** Such a member is idle with open work as surely as an undeclared one. Weigh whether rule 4's nudge and rule 3's `open` cause read it (so the manager on call can press what the tick does not), or whether item 1 covers every such case; either way, make rule 4's text say what the code does with `restart_wanted`.
3. **Rule 13's actor.** Find why the stuck line named grinder-ao-1, not designer-ao-1, for an order rule 6 routed to the designer's lane, and correct it, so the line names the record whose cure is the press.
4. **Stuck past a threshold.** Weigh whether rule 13, past a longer wait, routes the work (a fill, a restart of the one able member) rather than only telling the person, since today it rings no session and presses nothing (TD-1470).

**Done when:** The design says, for a member that declared a context-bound restart which no rule performs, and for a decided round on a design, which session acts and when; its builds have landed; and a case like 2026-11-16's (designer-ao-1 early at its context bound holding TD-1895, Paul's One more round on TD-1829 decided) has a round run, or a press on the entry's card, within the tick's window rather than 20 hours of idle sessions.
