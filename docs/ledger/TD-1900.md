## TD-1900: A decided design round waits a day while its designer sits idle on an early restart nobody presses

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-16 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 4933dd13581e487c9047965aa1b6d587

**Why:** On 2026-11-16 at about 19:40Z the session ao-led-tds-ledger, at Paul's request, reported that a decided round had sat about 20 hours with nothing acting on it, and Paul unstuck it by hand. Read from the records the same hour:

- **designer-ao-1 waits on an early restart nobody presses.** It holds TD-1895 (draft #4001). At 2026-11-15 23:11Z it declared `ao progress restart --why "context bound: …"`, seventeen minutes after its previous restart, with nothing reported done in that run, so the home marked the restart `early` (design §4.9a *Early is decided from the record*). An early restart waits on the person's Restart or Resume; neither the tick nor the manager acts on it (§6 rule 2). And because a restart is a declaration, rule 4 sends no idle nudge and writes no `idle_open`, so rule 3's `open` cause never fills the on-call manager (manager-ao-1 closed, `seat_filled` empty). Each rule did what the design says; together they leave a designer at a context bound idle with no session able to move it, and the Inbox restart row is the only signal. TD-1856 (built by TD-1863, gated on Paul's decision) moves that row onto the entry's Queue card; it does not make anything act.
- **The decided One more round on TD-1829 is read by the wrong lane.** Paul decided *One more round* on board item 2026-11-15-f5b4a76c (commit 38b22b845). A round on a design is the designer's work, but rule 13's stuck line reads *grinder-ao-1 has been idle since Sun 15:35 with it open*: the order's actor is the grinder, whose lane takes the work order and who rightly declared itself out of work, since a design round is not its lane. The one record that can run the round (designer-ao-1) is not named, and no round 10 entry or hand-off to the designer was made.
- **Stuck is shown, never acted on.** §6 rule 13 by design tells the person and presses nothing (each cause keeps its own cure). Here the cure for both causes was a person's press, and the stuck line named a cause (an idle grinder) that was not the one to cure.

The harm: a decision of Paul's waited 20 hours with every session idle, and blocked TD-1829's builds, TD-1832's second part and ledgerboard's TD-115 and TD-123 (v3.3 waits on TD-1829's Go). Not TD-1895, which is a person's Wind down leaving members open.

**Location:** design §6 rule 2 (early restarts), rule 3 (seat_due causes: open), rule 4 (the idle nudge, a declaration), rule 8, rule 13 (Stuck work); §4.9a Early is decided from the record, What a session does with a decided line; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_idle_nudge, the seat_due causes, _stuck_pass); src/sessionorc/stuck.py; src/sessionorc/work.py (the lanes a board order is read into)

**Fix:** A design round first: each piece below changes what the tick or a lane does, and two of them revisit rules the design chose on purpose (an early restart waiting on the person; rule 13 pressing nothing).

1. **An early restart at a context bound.** Weigh whether a member's restart whose `why` is a context bound (the context reading over its role's bound) is ever `early`: the run's lack of new work is not a sign of a loop when the context is the reason. Else weigh whether an early restart still waiting after a set wait fills the on-call manager (a fifth `seat_due` cause), or is raised to the person as a Do item, rather than sitting on an Inbox row alone. TD-1856's card placement is the visibility half; this is the acting half.
2. **A member idle and declared, with open work.** A member whose declaration is a restart nobody performs is idle with open work as surely as an undeclared one. Weigh whether rule 4's nudge and rule 3's `open` cause read it (the manager on call can then press what the tick does not), or whether item 1 covers every such case.
3. **A decided order on a design goes to the designer.** A decided board line whose Context names a design entry (`TD-n decision (<person>)`, its Owner `designer`, Kind `design-first`) and whose answer is a round is a work order for the designer's lane, not a free-pick grinder's. Settle which lane `board:<key>` is read into (§4.9a *What a session does with a decided line*, §6 rule 8), so rule 13 names the record that can act.
4. **Stuck past a threshold.** Weigh whether rule 13's stuck line, past a longer wait, routes the work (a fill, a restart of the one able member) rather than only telling the person, since rule 13 today rings no session and presses nothing (TD-1470).

**Done when:** The design says, for a member that declared a context-bound restart which no rule performs, and for a decided round on a design, which session acts and when; its builds have landed; and a case like 2026-11-16's (designer-ao-1 early at its context bound holding TD-1895, Paul's One more round on TD-1829 decided) has a round run, or a press on the entry's card, within the tick's window rather than 20 hours of idle sessions.
