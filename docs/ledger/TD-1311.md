**Why:** TD-1310's design: §4.5 screen 12 *The page's top section is one block* and *Filters* (the switch's bullet), §4.5a row *Queue page: filters, find*. Paul picked mockup B's needs-you hatch and dropped the heading line's *Decide · Do* (2026-11-02, the ask `m-5b5911edecba` and his steer in #2800); the shape is `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-02-td1310-c-1600.png` and `-c-400.png`, drawn by `docs/mockups/reviews/td1310-src/td1310_shots.py` (shape `C`, its `CSS` and `EDIT`).

**Fix:**
- **Counted from the cards shown.** `apply_picks` (which runs after `queue_view`, `app.py` ~4019) sets `view["bar"]` and the summary's counts from the cards it leaves shown: for each group its shown cards and, among them, those whose status is *needs you*; the shown total and the whole Queue's total (`m`); and the switch's `n` (e.g. `view["needs_shown"]`), the cards the chips and the find pass whose status is *needs you*, whatever `filter` is. `summary_bar` takes the per-group `(shown, needs)` pairs and the shown total; `queue_view`'s `bar` goes. The template draws the bar, the legend and `#qfneedsn` from these, not from `queue_view`'s whole-Queue `n`. In `app.js`, `applyPicks` recounts the same from the cards not marked `qoff` (`data-status`, the `.qgroup`'s `data-group`) and redraws the bar, the legend, `#qfneedsn` and the switch's `title` on every apply while `#qcounts` is in the lists; with none (the Queue not served) it recounts nothing, so the hidden parts keep their last numbers. `AO.qDrawCounts` and its call in the re-read go; `#qcounts` keeps no `data-` numbers, only its presence, which still toggles the served parts.
- **The needs-you share.** In each segment with a shown needs-you card, a part at its right end as wide a share of the segment as those cards are of its shown cards, never under 4 px (`min-width`) and never wider than its segment (`overflow: hidden` on the segment), drawn `repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, var(<segment colour>) 0 3px, var(--needs) 3px 6px)` as the mockup's `EDIT` draws it. The segment is a flex row of its plain part and its hatched part. The segment's `title` gains *, k need you* when k > 0 (*1 needs you* at 1). While the switch is on, each segment is hatched whole. One width function serves both sides, as `bar_flex` and `AO.qBarFlex` do now, and a test holds that they agree.
- **The legend:** after the groups, *▨ k need you* (its swatch the hatch on `--ended`, as the mockup's), drawn only while k > 0 (*1 needs you* at 1), its swatch's stripes finer than the bar's (the mockup's 2 px against 3 px); then *· n of m cards* while `AO.qFiltered` (the server's `filtered`), else *· m cards*; at none shown, no bar and *0 cards* or *0 of m cards*; *1 card* singular. The bar stays `aria-hidden`.
- **The switch's hover** leads with its count: *n cards need you — on, only these: it narrows the teams picked, it picks none; the completed lines are hidden while it is on* (*1 card needs you* at 1), redrawn with `n`.
- **No *Decide · Do* in the heading line:** the `qwantsn` span, its CSS (~1148, ~1318–1319), `queue_view`'s `n.decide` and `n.do` and their comment, and the header assertions in `test_ui_queue.py` (~120, the whole `n` dict), `test_queue_wants.py`, `test_queue_with_you.py` and `test_queue_board_item.py` go (each card's own `wants.n` and its pill stay, and so do the assertions on them). The template's head comment, `queue_part.html`'s `#qcounts` line, `app.css`'s two block comments (~1305, ~1353) and `app.js`'s comment over `AO.qDrawCounts`' place say what they now carry.
- **Level with the switch:** `.qhline` aligns its items to `flex-start`; the heading's `line-height`, the switch's and the find box's heights and the bar's top margin are the mockup's `CSS` (26 px rows, the bar 8 px tall with no top margin and `border-radius: 3px`), so the bar's top is the switch's border's top; the legend stays under the bar. At phone width the stacked order is as now, the switch alone on its row.
- **`docs/mockups/gen.py`** (~2150–2163) draws the new heading line (the hatch, the *need you* entry, no *Decide · Do*); regenerate, so `Queue.dc.html` follows.
- **Tests:** the counts follow a pick on both sides — a team chip, the switch (the switch's `n` unchanged by its own press) and a find word — with the hatch's width per segment, the legend's *n of m*, the *need you* entry absent at 0, and the hover's count, each at 1 (*1 needs you*, *1 card needs you*); an unserved re-read leaving the numbers as they were; the `#qcounts`, legend and `qwantsn` assertions in `test_ui_queue.py` (~213, ~264, ~295, ~776) follow the new markup.

**Done when** on the live page with a team picked, the bar, its legend and **Needs you n** count that team's shown cards (*n of m cards*), each segment hatching its needs-you share; pressing the switch leaves its `n` as it was; no *Decide · Do* is on the heading line while each card's pill keeps its own; the bar's top is level with the switch's at desktop width, and at phone width the legend stays under the bar; the tests above pass; and Paul has seen it live.
