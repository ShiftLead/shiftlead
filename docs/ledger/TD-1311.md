**Fix:**
- **The layout.** `#qfilters` holds two lines.
  - **The control line** (`.qhline`) holds **Queue**, then the teams (the *teams* label, **All**, the team chips, the divider, *no team* and the repo chips, which were `.qteams`'s), then `.qhend` with the switch and find at the right (`margin-left: auto`). It wraps as the chips do, and has a rule under it (`.qteams`' `border-bottom`, `padding-bottom: 12px` and `margin-bottom` move to it).
  - **The summary line** (`.qsum`) follows, across the width (no `max-width`, no `flex-basis`), directly above `#queuelists`.
  - `.qhline` keeps `align-items: center`. The bar is 8 px tall (was 6), its `border-radius: 3px` and `gap: 2px` as now.
  - At phone width the control line wraps: heading and chips, *no team* with its repo chips as one unit, then the switch, then find across the width. The rule and the summary line follow. The narrow rules (~1353) say so.
  - Keep the `class="qhend"` and `class="qsleg"` anchors. The tests that slice the legend from `qsleg` to `qhend` (`test_ui_queue.py` ~211, `test_queue_waiting.py` ~170) need a new end, since the legend now comes after `.qhend`: slice to the legend's closing tag or to `id="queuelists"`.
- **The server's counts.** `apply_picks` sets `view["bar"]` from the cards it leaves shown, as a list of segments `{key, word, color, n, needs, flex, needs_flex}`. Here `n` is the group's shown cards and `needs` is those among them whose status is *needs you*. `flex` is `bar_flex(n, shown)`, and `needs_flex` is `needs` inside the segment, never under 4 px (CSS) and clipped by it.
  - It also sets `view["summary"] = {"cards": shown, "needs": k, "of": m}`, where `m` is `q.n.cards`, the whole Queue.
  - It sets `view["needs_shown"]`, the switch's `n`: the cards the chips and the find pass whose status is *needs you*, whatever `filter` is.
  - `summary_bar` takes the per-group `(n, needs)` pairs and the shown total. `queue_view`'s `bar` goes.
  - The template's `summary` macro draws from `view["summary"]` and `view["bar"]`, and `#qfneedsn` and the switch's `title` from `view["needs_shown"]`. `q.n.needs_you` is no longer drawn; `q.n` keeps `cards`, `in_progress`, `waiting`, `up_next` and `needs_you`.
- **The browser's counts.**
  - `applyPicks` recounts the same from the cards: per `.qgroup[data-group]`, the `.qcard:not(.qoff)` and those with `data-status="needs you"`. `m` is every `.qgroup .qcard`, `qoff` included. The switch's `n` counts the cards that pass `AO.qPass` with `filter` cleared and have status *needs you*.
  - It redraws the bar, the legend, `#qfneedsn` and the switch's `title` on every apply while `#qcounts` is in the lists. With none (the Queue not served) it recounts nothing, so the hidden parts keep their last numbers.
  - One width function serves both sides, as `bar_flex` and `AO.qBarFlex` do now, and a test holds that they agree.
  - `AO.qDrawCounts` and its call in the re-read go. `#qcounts` keeps no `data-` numbers, only its presence, which still toggles the served parts.
- **The segment and its hatch.** `.qsbar > span` becomes `display: flex; overflow: hidden`: a plain part (`flex: n − needs`, the segment colour), then the hatched part (`flex: needs; min-width: 4px`, `repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, var(<segment colour>) 0 3px, var(--needs) 3px 6px)`), as the mockup's `EDIT` draws them. A segment with no needs draws one plain part, as now.
  - The segment's `title` is *24 up next, 24 need you*, with *1 needs you* at 1, and just *19 waiting* with none. While the switch is on, each segment is hatched whole.
- **The legend.** After the groups comes *▨ k need you* (*1 needs you* at 1), drawn only while k > 0. Its swatch is the hatch on `--ended` with finer stripes (2 px/4 px). Then *· n of m cards* while `AO.qFiltered` (the server's `filtered`), else *· m cards*. With none shown there is no bar, and the legend reads *0 cards* or *0 of m cards*; *1 card* is singular. The bar stays `aria-hidden`.
- **The switch's hover** reads *n cards need you — press to keep only these: it narrows the teams picked, it picks none; the completed lines are hidden while it is on* (*1 card needs you* at 1), the same on and off, and is redrawn with `n`.
- **No *Decide · Do* count.** These go:
  - the `qwantsn` span, `.qwantsn` (~1148) and `.qhend .qwantsn` / `.qwantsn[hidden]` (~1318–1319);
  - `queue_view`'s `n.decide` and `n.do` and their comment.

  Each card's own `wants.n` and its pill stay.
- **Comments.** These say what they now hold:
  - `queue_filters.html`'s head comment;
  - `queue_part.html`'s `#qcounts` comment (line 8);
  - `app.css`'s two block comments (~1305, ~1353);
  - `app.js`'s comment where `AO.qDrawCounts` stood.
- **`docs/mockups/gen.py`** (~2150–2163) draws the new top section: the control line, the rule, then the summary line with the hatch and the *need you* entry, and no *Decide · Do*. Edit only `gen.py`, then regenerate so `Queue.dc.html` follows.
- **Tests.**
  - **Edits to existing assertions:**
    - `test_ui_queue.py` ~120 drops `"decide"` and `"do"` from the expected `n` dict (the rest stays, as do the `n["needs_you"]` reads at ~362, ~495 and ~523).
    - `test_queue_wants.py` 67, 80 and 123, `test_queue_with_you.py` 136 and `test_queue_board_item.py` 79 drop only their `v["n"]["decide"]`/`["do"]` conjuncts, keeping the cards' `wants.n` ones.
    - `test_ui_queue.py` ~267–279 is rewritten for `summary_bar`'s new signature.
    - The `#qcounts` (~264, ~776) and `qwantsn` (~295) assertions follow the new markup; ~736 and ~824's `<b id="qfneedsn">` stay valid.
  - **New:** the counts follow a pick on both sides — a team chip, the switch (whose `n` its own press leaves unchanged) and a find word. Each covers the hatch per segment, the legend's *n of m* and *need you* entry (absent at 0, *1 needs you* at 1), and the hover's count. A further case covers an unserved re-read, which leaves the numbers as they were.
