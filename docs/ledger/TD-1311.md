**Fix:**
- **The layout.** `#qfilters` holds two lines.
  - **The control line** (`.qhline`) holds **Queue**, then `.qteams` (the *teams* label, **All**, the team chips, the divider, *no team* and the repo chips), moved inside it and drawn `display: contents` so its chips flow in the line while it keeps its `qserved` class (`.qhline`'s `gap` becomes `8px`, the mockup's, for the chips and the rest alike; the *teams* label's `margin-right: 4px` and the divider's margins stay), then `.qhend` with the switch and find at the right (`margin-left: auto`). It wraps as the chips do, and has a rule under it: `.qteams`' `border-bottom` and `padding-bottom: 12px` move to `.qhline`, with `margin-bottom: 10px` before the summary line (the mockup's spacing). The served parts are still three, `.qteams`, `.qhend` and `.qsum`, so `test_ui_queue.py` ~264's count of 3 stands.
  - **The summary line** (`.qsum`, a `<div>` now, holding the bar and the legend) follows, across the width, directly above `#queuelists`: `display: flex; flex-direction: column` with no `max-width` and no `flex` (it is no longer in a flex line). The narrow rule `.qsum { flex-basis: 100%; max-width: none; order: 3; }` (~1355) is deleted.
  - `.qteams`' `margin-top: 10px` and `margin-bottom: 6px` go with its box (`display: contents` draws none).
  - `.qhline` keeps `align-items: center`. The bar is 8 px tall (was 6), its `border-radius: 3px` and `gap: 2px` as now.
  - At phone width the control line wraps: heading and chips, *no team* with its repo chips as one unit, then the switch, then find across the width. The rule and the summary line follow. The narrow rules (~1353) say so.
  - Keep the `class="qhend"` and `class="qsleg"` anchors. The tests that slice the legend from `qsleg` to `qhend` (`test_ui_queue.py` ~211, `test_queue_waiting.py` ~170) need a new end, since the legend now comes after `.qhend`: slice to the legend's closing tag or to `id="queuelists"`.
- **The server's counts.** `apply_picks` sets `view["bar"]` from the cards it leaves shown, as a list of segments `{key, word, color, n, needs, flex}`. `view["bar"]` is set after `view["shown_in"]` (built at `apply_picks`' end, ~862), with `needs` tallied per group key while the card loop (~833, one flat list) walks the cards. Here `n` is the group's shown cards, read from `view["shown_in"]` (which `apply_picks` already counts; never a second count), `needs` is those among them whose status is *needs you*, and `flex` is `bar_flex(n, shown)`; the hatch's width inside the segment is `needs` itself (its 4 px floor is CSS).
  - In `apply_picks`' card loop, keep the chips-and-find result apart from the switch's: `nf = by_repo_find(...)`, then `ok = nf and status == "needs you"` while `filter` is on, else `ok = nf`. `k` counts the cards with `nf` and status *needs you*, so it ignores `filter`; a group's `needs` counts the cards with `ok` and status *needs you*.
  - It also sets `view["summary"] = {"cards": shown, "needs": k, "of": m}`, where `m` is `q.n.cards`, the whole Queue, and `k` the cards the chips and the find pass whose status is *needs you*, whatever `filter` is. `k` is both the legend's *need you* count and the switch's `n` (the switch narrows to needs-you cards alone, so the two are one number): count it once.
  - `summary_bar` takes the per-group `(n, needs)` pairs and the shown total. `queue_view`'s `bar` goes.
  - The template's `summary` macro draws from `view["summary"]` and `view["bar"]`, and `#qfneedsn` and the switch's `title` from `view["summary"]["needs"]`. `q.n.needs_you` is no longer drawn; `q.n` keeps `in_progress`, `waiting` and `up_next`, which the group headings read (`queue_part.html` ~41, ~53, ~63), and `cards`, which `summary["of"]` reads; `needs_you` is kept for the tests that read it (`test_ui_queue.py` ~362, ~495, ~523, `test_queue_wants.py` 67) and drawn nowhere.
- **The browser's counts.**
  - `applyPicks` recounts the same from the cards: per `.qgroup[data-group]`, the `.qcard:not(.qoff)` and those with `data-status="needs you"`. `m` is every `.qgroup .qcard`, `qoff` included. The switch's `n` counts the cards that pass `AO.qPass` with `filter` cleared and have status *needs you*.
  - It redraws the bar, the legend, `#qfneedsn` and the switch's `title` on every apply while `#qcounts` is in the lists. With none (the Queue not served) it recounts nothing, so the hidden parts keep their last numbers.
  - One width function serves both sides, as `bar_flex` and `AO.qBarFlex` do now, and a test holds that they agree.
  - `AO.qDrawCounts` and its call in the re-read go. `#qcounts` keeps no `data-` numbers, only its presence, which still toggles the served parts.
- **The segment and its hatch.** The markup, the same on both sides: a segment is `<span class="qseg" style="flex: F" title="…">`, holding a plain part `<i style="flex: P; background: var(--c)"></i>` (P = n − needs) and, when needs > 0, a hatched part `<i class="qhatch" style="flex: K; --seg: var(--c)"></i>` (K = needs). `.qsbar > .qseg` is `display: flex; overflow: hidden` (the old `.qsbar > span { display: block; }` is deleted: it would outweigh a bare `.qseg`); `.qhatch` is `min-width: 4px` and `background: repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, var(--seg) 0 3px, var(--needs) 3px 6px)` (`--needs`, which `:root` defines and the dark theme inherits, the same amber in both), as the mockup's `EDIT` draws it. The empty bar stays `<span class="qsbar" aria-hidden="true" hidden></span>`. A segment with no needs draws one plain part, as now; a segment all needs (the switch on, or `needs == n`) draws only the hatched part, no 0-flex plain one.
  - The segment's `title` is *24 up next, 24 need you*, with *1 needs you* at 1, and just *19 waiting* with none. While the switch is on, each segment is hatched whole.
- **The legend.** After the groups comes *▨ k need you* (*1 needs you* at 1), drawn only while k > 0, as `<span class="qsneeds"><i class="qhatch"></i><b>k</b>need you</span>`. Its swatch keeps `.qsleg i`'s 8 × 8 px box: `.qsneeds .qhatch { --seg: var(--ended); background: repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, var(--seg) 0 2px, var(--needs) 2px 4px); }`. The groups' entries and `<span class="qscards">` keep today's markup. Then *· n of m cards* while `AO.qFiltered` (the server's `filtered`), else *· m cards*. With none shown there is no bar and no legend entry (no *need you* one either), and the legend reads *0 cards* or *0 of m cards*, with no leading `· `; *1 card* is singular. The bar stays `aria-hidden`.
- **The switch's hover** reads *n cards need you — press to keep only these: it narrows the teams picked, it picks none; the completed lines are hidden while it is on* (*1 card needs you* at 1, *0 cards need you* at 0), the same on and off, and is redrawn with `n`. The server and the browser build the hover, the segments' titles and the legend's words with twin functions: `queue.summary_words(bar, summary, filtered)` and `AO.qSummaryWords(bar, summary, filtered)`, each returning words, never markup: `{titles, entries, cards, hover}`, where `titles` is each segment's `title`, `entries` the legend's `{n, word}` pairs in order (the groups', then the *need you* one while k > 0), `cards` the `qscards` text (*· 47 of 231 cards*, its leading `· ` only while an entry precedes it, so *0 cards* alone), and `hover` the switch's `title`. The template and `applyPicks` draw the swatches and the `<b>` around each `n` themselves. `AO.qSummaryWords` is pure (no `$`, no DOM), so `app.js` still loads under the probe's stubs. AO.qSummaryWords(c.words.bar, c.words.summary, c.words.filtered) : null`, and a test calls it on each case's counts (0, 1, many; filtered and not) and compares it to the server's.
