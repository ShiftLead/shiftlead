## TD-1310: The Queue page's summary bar counts the whole Queue whatever is picked: recount it to the picks, and drop it to the **Needs you** switch's top

**Type:** debt

**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first

**Location:** `docs/design/4.5-ui.md` (screen 12 *The page's top section is one block*, ~1759, its sentence ~1774: *The summary counts the whole Queue, as the sentence it replaces did, whatever is picked*); `docs/design/4.5a-controls.md` (row *Queue page* — **filters**, **find**, ~132); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`n` ~694–704, `summary_bar`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_filters.html` (`summary`, `.qhline`, `#qfneedsn`, `.qwantsn`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_part.html` (`#qcounts`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`AO.qDrawCounts` ~4432, `AO.qPass`, the shares' recount on a pick, TD-829); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.qhline`, `.qsum`, `.qsbar`, `.qhend`, ~1307–1318, the narrow rules ~1355); `docs/mockups/Queue.dc.html`; `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-02-td1293-agreed-{1600,400}.png`

**Why:** Paul (2026-11-02, with a screenshot of the live page, **ao-grind** picked): "lets change the queue progress bar to adjust to the current set of filters. Lets also move the bar itself down so its top aligns to the height of the top of the "Needs you" box. Would it make sense to have the progress bar include the "needs you counts"?" With one team picked the bar and its legend still read *3 in progress · 60 waiting · 167 up next · 230 cards*, the whole Queue's, while the groups below show that team's cards with *(n of m)* shares, so the bar describes cards the page is not showing. TD-1293's design chose this on purpose (the summary replaced a sentence that counted the whole Queue); the live page shows that it reads wrong. The **Needs you** number (*159*) and *Decide 158 · Do 43* are the whole Queue's too (`q.n`, drawn once by the server and redrawn by `qDrawCounts` from `#qcounts`, never by the picks). The bar also sits higher than the switch beside it: `.qhline` centres its items, and the 6 px bar sits at the top of the `.qsum` column above its legend, so the bar's top is above the switch's border.

Paul asked how *159*, *Decide 158* and *Do 43* relate. They count different things. **Needs you** counts **cards** whose status is *needs you* (`_status` in `src/sessionorc/queue.py`: a row with `Owner: paul` or `decision (paul)`; or `_mail_status`: any Decide or Do item attached that is not a running `steer`). *Decide* and *Do* count **items** on those cards, not cards (`want_of`: Decide is an `ask`, a `steer`, a `decide` or `look` board line, a `decision (paul)` entry; Do is an `act` board line, an `Owner: paul` entry, a blocked outcome; a red PR is left out of the header). Those are Inbox mail and board lines attached to cards, plus the person's ledger rows. One card can carry several items, and one item can make a card *needs you* while the card counts once, so the numbers neither add up nor bound each other. The page does not say so beyond the *Decide · Do* hover.

**Fix:** design first, as a UI round with Paul (agree the mockups before model review rounds):
- The summary counts the cards the picks (teams, repos, **Needs you**, find) leave shown: the bar's segments, the legend and *n cards* (e.g. *of 230* while a pick is on, as the group shares do). The browser recounts them on a pick, as it recounts the shares (TD-829). **Needs you n** and *Decide · Do* follow the team and find picks too; the switch's own number ignores the switch's state.
- The bar drops so its top lines up with the top of the **Needs you** switch's border, and the legend stays under it, at desktop width and in the narrow layout.
- The round settles whether the bar shows the needs-you share. The drafter recommends against a fourth segment: *needs you* cuts across the three groups (a needs-you card can be in progress, waiting or up next), so a segment would count those cards twice. Better would be a mark inside each segment (a needs-colour sliver or hatch of that group's needs-you cards), or nothing, leaving the switch's number to say it.
- The round decides whether the page should say that **Needs you** counts cards and *Decide · Do* counts items, beyond the hover (e.g. *159 cards* on the switch's hover, or *items:* before *Decide*).
- §4.5 screen 12 and the §4.5a row say what the build draws. The build entry covers the template, `qDrawCounts`, the CSS, the mockup and the tests that hold the counts.

**Done when** on the live page, picking a team, a repo, **Needs you** or a find word redraws the bar, its legend, the switch's number and *Decide · Do* to the shown cards; the bar's top lines up with the switch's top at desktop and phone width; the design says both; and Paul has seen it live.
