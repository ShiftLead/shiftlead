**Why:** TD-1542's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card*, §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*): a Decide or Do item drawn on a kept completed card draws Start session, on the archived entry it is tied to, so the person can always start a session on a Do or Decide item (Paul, 2026-10-26). Today such an item shows only Done, with Answer… in its ⋯ (seen 2026-11-07 on shiftlead#TD-1025, shiftlead#TD-1044 and agent-cadence#TD-219).

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card* as written:

- `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_completed`, its docstring among them): each completed card carries `types`, `{<repo>#TD-n: type}`, beside `ids` (§4.4 *Completed cards*). Each value is the first word of the member's archived `Type:`, lower-cased, as `ledger.entries` reads an open entry's.

- `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`:
  - `attach`'s `start` callback becomes `start(card, ref, on_row)`, where `on_row` is the item's `on_row` from `item_home`. `item_start(card, item, fleet, on_row="")` draws a button for a Decide or Do item on a completed card (one with no `start`), starting on `on_row`, the archived entry the item is tied to. Red PRs, fork PRs, stopped holders' state rows and stuck rows still draw none, and an open card's items are unchanged.
  - `card_session` keeps filtering archived rows on an open card. On a completed card, which has no open rows, it reads the card's `ids`, its archived entries, as the entries a holder may hold. So the person's own session on the item's entry, or on any of the card's entries, gives **Open session**, and another holder of the item's entry gives no button.
  - A kept card's header (`_kept_card`) still draws no Start session, whatever its items count. A loose card is unchanged.
- `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`:
  - On the page, `card_mail` gets `[*cards, *completed]` from the `queue` reading as its `cards`, so `by_id` holds a kept card's id and its items reach `item_start`.
  - `/api/ledger/start`, with `card` naming a completed card:
    - It reads `queue` with `omit=["completed.entries", "completed.titles"]`, as `Readings.queue_cards` does, so `ids` and `types` stay. It builds its `card_index` with `completed`, so it has a kept item's `kept:<ref>` key and `on_row`, and it reads the card's mail through `card_mail` as the page does.
    - It accepts the press only where the card is kept at the press (an open item that needs the person is attached to it), `item` is one of its Decide or Do items, and `id` is that item's `on_row` now. Every failed check refuses 409 *the item is no longer open on the card around TD-n — nothing was started*: no `item`, an item no longer open on the card, an `id` that is not the item's `on_row`, a card that is now neither open nor completed, and a mail read that raises. A kept press never starts unchecked: here the read is not under `suppress`. The *no longer open* check (`entry is None`) is not made on this path.
    - The entry is archived, so it is not in the repo facts' open entries. `ref` is the card's normalised `TD-NNN`, and the name is `ref.lower()` as today. `Type:` is read from the completed card's `types`. The road keeps it where it is in `ENTRY_TYPES` and else takes `ENTRY_TYPES[0]`, as `app.py` does today. The session's `lane` is the archived id, since `_lane` checks form alone. The claim then names no open entry, so it is an *other work* line in In progress, and that is the designed outcome. `why_key` is *card*, and the `owner` and `decided_by` reads are skipped.
    - The holder rules and the existing refusals stand. A name that is not free is among them and answers `{form, why}`; it is the common case for an entry the person's own session built, which leaves an exited `td-<n>` record or branch. The tick's derived claim may read `done #<old PR>` where the closing PR's head was `td-<n>`, and the holder rules still match by name. The session starts in worktree and branch `td-<n>` on the entry's repo's team.
- `src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py`:
  - `WORK_ITEM_KEPT` sits beside `WORK_ITEM`. It is `WORK_ITEM`'s text with *the completed card* in place of *the card*, followed by *{id} is archived: read it as the record of what was done, not as open work* (no path: a v1 ledger has one archive file).
  - `work_text(..., kept: bool = False)` uses it in `WORK_ITEM`'s place when `item` and `kept` are both given, filling the same slots (`{read}`, `{item}`, `{id}`, `{repo}`, `{ledger}`). *Waits on me* is the kept card's Decide and Do items by id, and *the rest* is *none*.
- `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py`: the help entry `card-item-start` becomes: *Starts your own session about this item: it opens on the card's entry, on this entry where the item is one, or on a completed card on the archived entry the item is about, with the item as its subject, to read first and walk through with you. Reads Open session while yours is on the card: the press goes there and copies the item's id for you to paste.* Run the doc-bound help tests.
