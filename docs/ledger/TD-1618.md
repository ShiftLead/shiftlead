**Why:** TD-1542's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card*, §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*): a Decide or Do item drawn on a kept completed card draws Start session, on the archived entry it is tied to, so the person can always start a session on a Do or Decide item (Paul, 2026-10-26). Today such an item shows only Done, with Answer… in its ⋯ (seen 2026-11-07 on shiftlead#TD-1025, shiftlead#TD-1044 and agent-cadence#TD-219).

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card* as written:

- `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`: `item_start` draws a button for a Decide or Do item on a completed card (it has no `start`), starting on the archived entry the item is tied to (`item_home`'s `on_row`; `attach` passes it to `start`, which reads only the card and the item's id today). Red PRs, fork PRs, stopped holders' state rows and stuck rows still draw none. `card_session` reads a completed card's archived entries for the holder rules, since that card has no open ones: the person's own session on the item's entry, or on any of the card's entries, gives **Open session**, and another holder of the item's entry gives no button. A kept card's header (`_kept_card`) still draws no Start session, whatever its items count. A loose card is unchanged.
- `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`: `card_mail` builds its cards by id from the open cards and the completed ones, so a kept card's items reach `item_start`. `/api/ledger/start` with `card` naming a completed card reads the `queue` reading's completed cards (their ids and entries) and accepts the press only where the card is kept at the press, `item` is one of its Decide or Do items, and `id` is the entry that item is tied to now. The entry is archived, so it is not in the repo facts' open entries, and `why_key` is *card*. With no `item`, or with an item no longer open on the card, the press is refused 409 *the item is no longer open on the card around TD-n — nothing was started*. The holder rules and the existing refusals stand, and the session starts in worktree and branch `td-<n>` on the entry's repo's team.
- `src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py`: `WORK_ITEM_KEPT`, the kept sentence, sits beside `WORK_ITEM`. It is the item's sentence with *the completed card* in place of *the card*, then *{id} is archived, under the ledger's `archive/`: read it as the record of what was done, not as open work*. `work_text` takes it for an item on a completed card. *Waits on me* is the kept card's Decide and Do items by id, and *the rest* is *none*.

**Done when:** Tests: a Do item (an `act` board line) and a Decide item (an open `ask` to the person) on a kept card each draw Start session, posting `{repo, id, card, item}` with `id` the archived entry the item is tied to, on that repo's team. The road starts the session in `td-<n>`, and its prompt carries the kept sentence, *waits on me* the items and *the rest* `none`. The kept card's header draws none with one item or two. The person's own session on the item's entry turns the button into Open session. Another session holding it removes the button, and a stale press gets 409 *held by <name>*. A press with no `item`, or one whose item has closed, gets 409. A plain completed line, an open card's items, and a loose card are unchanged.
