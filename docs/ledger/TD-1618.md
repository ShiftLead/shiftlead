**Why:** TD-1542's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card*, §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*): a Decide or Do item drawn on a kept completed card draws Start session, on the archived entry it is tied to, so the person can always start a session on a Do or Decide item (Paul, 2026-10-26). Today such an item shows only Done, with Answer… in its ⋯ (seen 2026-11-07 on shiftlead#TD-1025, shiftlead#TD-1044 and agent-cadence#TD-219).

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card* as written:

- `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_completed`, its docstring among them): each completed card carries `types`, `{<repo>#TD-n: type}`, keyed by the same `qid`s as `ids` (§4.4 *Completed cards*). Each value is the member's archived `Type:`, normalised exactly as `ledger.entries` normalises an open entry's (the first word, lower-cased, else `TYPES[0]`), so it is always an entry type.

- `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`:
  - `attach`'s `start` callback becomes `start(card, ref, on_row)`, where `on_row` is the item's `on_row` from `item_home`. `item_start(card, item, fleet, on_row="")` draws a button for a Decide or Do item on a completed card (one with no `start`), starting on `on_row`, the archived entry the item is tied to. Red PRs, fork PRs, stopped holders' state rows and stuck rows still draw none, and an open card's items are unchanged.
  - `card_session` keeps filtering archived rows on an open card. A card with no `start` is a completed card: the page passes the `queue` reading's completed card, and `_kept_card`'s card also carries `ids`. On such a card `card_session` reads the card's `ids` as the entries a session may hold.
  - On a completed card `own` also counts the person's own live session (`unattended: false`) of the entry's repo whose `lane` names one of those entries, compared with `same_td`. The repo is checked as `holders()` checks it, because a lane's refs are bare. It counts beside the name `td-<n>` and the `claimed` claims. This is the button's marker alone: `other` stays claims and names, and the lane of an unattended session marks nothing.
  - The person's own session on the item's entry, or on any of the card's entries, gives **Open session**. Another session holding the item's entry by claim or name gives no button.
  - A kept card's header (`_kept_card`) still draws no Start session, whatever its items count. A loose card is unchanged.
- `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`:
  - On the page, `card_mail` gets `[*cards, *completed]` from the `queue` reading as its `cards`, so `by_id` holds a kept card's id and its items reach `item_start`.
  - `/api/ledger/start`:
    - The road chooses its path from the reading at the press. A `card` found among the completed cards takes the kept path. So does a `card` found nowhere while `id` is not an open entry. A `card` found nowhere while `id` is an open entry keeps today's refusal, *the card around TD-n is no longer open*.
    - It reads `queue` with `omit=["completed.entries", "completed.titles"]`, as `Readings.queue_cards` does, so `ids` and `types` stay. It builds its `card_index` with `completed`, so it has a kept item's `kept:<ref>` key and `on_row`. It reads the card's mail through `card_mail` as the page does.
    - The kept path accepts the press only where three things hold at the press:
      - the card is kept (an open item that needs the person is attached to it);
      - `item` is one of its Decide or Do items;
      - `id` is that item's `on_row`.

      Each failed check refuses with 409 *the item is no longer open on the card around TD-n — nothing was started*. The failures are: no `item`, an item no longer open on the card, an `id` that is not the item's `on_row`, a card found nowhere, and a mail read that raises. Here the read is not under `suppress`, so a kept press never starts unchecked. The `entry is None` check is not made on this path.
    - The entry is archived, so it is not in the repo facts' open entries. `ref` is the card's `qid` id, its normalised `TD-NNN`, and the name is `ref.lower()`. That name can differ from the one an open start took from an unpadded ledger spelling. The `on_row` check and the `types` lookup compare `qid`s. `Type:` is read from the completed card's `types`. Where `types` lacks the id, the road takes `ENTRY_TYPES[0]` as a guard, as `app.py` does today. The session's `lane` is the archived id, since `_lane` checks form alone. `why_key` is *card*, and the `owner` and `decided_by` reads are skipped.
    - On the kept path, the road's own check for the person's session also reads the lane, by the same marker: only the person's own live session, and only its lane. So a second press goes to that session, even one named on the form, and never starts a second one. `holder`'s refusal of another session (409 *held by <name>*) stays claims and names alone.
    - The other holder rules and refusals stand. A name that is not free answers `{form, why}`, and that is the common case here. On the form the person may pick any name, and the lane holds the entry.
    - What the tick makes of the session:
      - A derived claim reads `claimed` unless a merged PR with that head is among the PRs the derivation reads. While it reads `claimed` it names no open entry, so it is an *other work* line in In progress, which is the designed outcome.
      - Otherwise it reads `done #<old PR>`, and the old PR's derived findings land on the new record. That is accepted.
- `src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py`:
  - `WORK_ITEM_KEPT` sits beside `WORK_ITEM` and is the opening in its place, exactly: `"I opened this session from the Queue about {item}, on the completed card around {id} of {repo}'s ledger ({ledger}). {id} is archived: read it as the record of what was done, not as open work. Read the item first{read}, and walk me through its goal and what each answer leads to."` `{read}` is filled by the item's kind, as for `WORK_ITEM`. Only board lines and mail items reach a kept card, since a ledger item is an open entry's row. The constant names no archive path, since a v1 ledger has one archive file.
  - `work_text(..., kept: bool = False)` uses it in `WORK_ITEM`'s place when `item` and `kept` are both given, filling the same slots (`{read}`, `{item}`, `{id}`, `{repo}`, `{ledger}`). *Waits on me* is the kept card's Decide and Do items by id, and *the rest* is *none*.
- `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py`: the help entry `card-item-start` becomes: *Starts your own session about this item: it opens on the card's entry, or on this entry where the item is one (on a completed card, the archived entry the item is about), with the item as its subject, to read first and walk through with you. Reads Open session while yours is on the card: the press goes there and copies the item's id for you to paste.* Run the doc-bound help tests.
