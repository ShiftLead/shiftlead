**Why:** TD-1542's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card*, §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*): a Decide or Do item drawn on a kept completed card draws Start session, on the archived entry it is tied to, so the person can always start a session on a Do or Decide item (Paul, 2026-10-26). Today such an item shows only Done, with Answer… in its ⋯ (seen 2026-11-07 on shiftlead#TD-1025, shiftlead#TD-1044 and agent-cadence#TD-219).

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: An item on a kept card* as written:

- `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`:
  - `attach`'s `start` callback becomes `start(card, ref, on_row)`, where `on_row` is the item's `on_row` from `item_home`. `item_start(card, item, fleet, on_row="")` draws a button for a Decide or Do item on a completed card (one with no `start`), starting on `on_row`, the archived entry the item is tied to. Red PRs, fork PRs, stopped holders' state rows and stuck rows still draw none, and an open card's items are unchanged.
  - `card_session` keeps filtering archived rows on an open card. On a completed card, which has no open rows, it reads the card's `ids`, its archived entries, as the entries a holder may hold. So the person's own session on the item's entry, or on any of the card's entries, gives **Open session**, and another holder of the item's entry gives no button.
  - A kept card's header (`_kept_card`) still draws no Start session, whatever its items count. A loose card is unchanged.
- `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`:
  - On the page, `card_mail` gets `[*cards, *completed]` from the `queue` reading as its `cards`, so `by_id` holds a kept card's id and its items reach `item_start`.
  - `/api/ledger/start`, with `card` naming a completed card:
    - It reads `queue` with `omit=["completed.entries", "completed.titles"]`, as `Readings.queue_cards` does, and builds its `card_index` with `completed`, so it has a kept item's `kept:<ref>` key and `on_row`.
    - It accepts the press only where the card is kept at the press (an open item that needs the person is attached to it), `item` is one of its Decide or Do items, and `id` is that item's `on_row` now. With no `item`, or with an item no longer open on the card, it refuses 409 *the item is no longer open on the card around TD-n — nothing was started*. The *no longer open* check (`entry is None`) is not made on this path.
    - The entry is archived, so it is not in the repo facts' open entries. `ref` is the id as the card's row spells it, and the name is `ref.lower()` as today. `Type:` is read from the archived file under the ledger's `archive/`, an unreadable or unknown one taking `ENTRY_TYPES[0]`. `why_key` is *card*, and the `owner` and `decided_by` reads are skipped.
    - The holder rules and the existing refusals stand, a name not free among them, which answers `{form, why}`. The session starts in worktree and branch `td-<n>` on the entry's repo's team.
- `src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py`:
  - `WORK_ITEM_KEPT` sits beside `WORK_ITEM`. It is `WORK_ITEM`'s text with *the completed card* in place of *the card*, followed by *{id} is archived, under the ledger's `archive/`: read it as the record of what was done, not as open work*.
  - `work_text(..., kept: bool = False)` uses it in `WORK_ITEM`'s place when `item` and `kept` are both given, filling the same slots (`{read}`, `{item}`, `{id}`, `{repo}`, `{ledger}`). *Waits on me* is the kept card's Decide and Do items by id, and *the rest* is *none*.

**Done when:** Tests:
- A Do item (an `act` board line) and a Decide item (an open `ask` to the person) on a kept card each draw Start session. Each posts `{repo, id, card, item}` with `id` the archived entry the item is tied to, on that repo's team.
- The road starts the session in `td-<n>`, with the role from the archived entry's `Type:`. Its prompt carries the kept sentence, with *waits on me* the items and *the rest* `none`.
- A taken name answers the filled form.
- The kept card's header draws none, with one item or with two.
- With the person's own session on the item's entry, the button is Open session.
- With another session holding the item's entry, there is no button, and a stale press gets 409 *held by <name>*.
- A press with no `item`, or one whose item has closed, gets 409.
- A plain completed line, an open card's items and a loose card are unchanged.
