## TD-1245: A Queue card's board item leads with its task: an `act` line is a Do with Done, its bold head is the sentence, *in <repo>* where it names another repo, its standing in Answer…'s title, and no footer

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (the designer, from TD-1066's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1066
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`want_of` ~:194: a board row is `decide` unless `watch`/`fyi`; `attach`'s `put` ~:225 sets each item's `want` from it, which `card_wants` ~:259 reads); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the board item's sentence ~:819, its standing ~:806, the *on the board —* footer ~:873, Done in ⋯); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`board_text` ~:1164, `board_head` ~:1325); `src/sessionorc/ledgerboard.py` (`bold_head` ~:1053); `src/shiftlead/ui/render.py` (`fold_head` ~:239)

**Why:** design §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* (Do), *Three verbs* (Answer) and *An item's shape* (*A board item says where its task is*), §4.5a rows *Queue card: what needs the person*, *Queue card: answers, Answer…*, *Queue card: ⋯* and *Inbox board row: standing*, §4.10's one-sentence table, ADR 2026-10-19 §9 (TD-1066). Today an `act` line is drawn under Decide with Answer… as its verb and Done hidden in ⋯; its sentence is the line up to its bold head, so it leads with *act 2026-10-26 (session … on kmaster) —*; its head carries the *standing* text, which a card has no Reply for; and its footer explains the write-back.

**Fix:**
1. `want_of`: a board line of kind `act` is `do` (counted in the pill's *Do*, the card amber as for any Do); `decide` and `look` stay Decide; `watch` and `fyi` stay Read.
2. On an `act` item the Answer row is **Done** alone, outlined, the write-back's Done (the line checked off, `Closed:` appended — the road the Inbox board row's Done already takes); **Answer…** moves into the item's ⋯ menu.
3. In `card` mode a board item's sentence is its bold head (`bold_head`); a line with none keeps the line before `Context:`. With no `Answers:`, the line's next sentence after the head is drawn as its consequence. Under *context*: the prefix (kind, date, *(session … on <host>)*), the rest of the line, its `board:<key>` and its sub-bullets.
4. The kind line reads *<kind> · in <repo> · board line · on TD-n*: *in <repo>* when the line's `Context:` names an id or PR of a repo other than the card's (`<repo>#TD-n`, `<repo>#n`), the first such; absent otherwise.
5. On a card the *standing* is not drawn in the item's head; it is Answer…'s `title`. The Inbox and the Repo page keep it as today.
6. On a card a board item draws no *on the board —* footer; its *each one edit landed on <repo>'s origin by shiftlead's own pull request* words are Done's `title`. The Inbox row keeps its footer.

**Done when:** a test renders a card with an `act` line whose bold head and `Context: agent-cadence#TD-151, TD-1033` give *do · in agent-cadence*, the head as the sentence, Done outlined in the Answer row, Answer… only in ⋯, the prefix and key under *context*, no standing in the head and no footer; the card's pill and the page header read *Do 1* and the *Do* kind pick finds the card; `want_of`'s docstring says an `act` line is Do; a `decide` line on the same card stays Decide with its answers; the Inbox's board row is unchanged (its existing tests pass); `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass.
