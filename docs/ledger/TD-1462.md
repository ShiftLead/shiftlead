**Fix:** - **The worked reading.** For each instant in the home's team record's `work_started` not yet in its new `work_worked` list: the start is *worked* when a record of the team (the method groups `self._graph().values()` by `team` itself, live and ended, and returns at once unless `self.mode == "home"`, as `_work_marks` does, ~2183) has as its newest `restarts` entry the start's own (`why == "work"` and `start` equal to the instant) and holds a `progress` entry with `source == "declared"`, `status` `claimed` or `done`, and `at` strictly after that entry's `at`, compared as parsed instants (a same-second stamp reads as not after: accepted). `_replay` restamps the waiting claims it carries with the entry's `at` (~3081–3086, *a renewal of the lease*), so `>` excludes them; a member the start did not replay, and a record a later restart (a person's Start, a schedule's, rule 2's) has superseded, never credit it. Append each newly worked instant to `rec["work_worked"]`.
- **Where it runs.** A method of its own, `_work_worked(now)`, called in `tick()` immediately before `_keep_running` (~363, whose crash, wanted and scheduled restarts and seat fills replay), over every team whose home record holds `work_started`, whatever its `on_work`, (not `_work_marks`' loop, ~2206, which runs after `_keep_running` and visits only teams with records or a `work_waiting`): a replay supersedes the record in place and its progress with it (`tests/test_work_start.py` ~361), so the reading must see a claim before any replay that tick erases it — that is why `work_worked` is kept. A member that claims and crashes between two ticks is read before rule 1 replaces it; one a person restarts by hand in that interval reads idle (accepted). The same step prunes both lists to `WORK_DAY` (today only `_work_start` prunes `work_started`, ~2461, and only under a standing mark), `work_worked` to the instants still in `work_started`, pops each when empty, and, when either changed, saves the home's record itself (`host_store.save` and `_push_changes`, as `_work_marks`' dirty block does, ~2236–2241), since that block's check compares `work_started` alone. `_work_start`'s own prune (~2461–2465) is left as it is; a `work_worked` it leaves stale goes at the next tick's step. The tests call `_work_worked(now)` directly, as the cap tests call `_work_marks(now)`. Node records are read from the home's copy (`_graph()`): a down link reads the last snapshot, and the start stays counted until the claim arrives.
- **An unattended seat never credits.** A start replays an unattended seat held (`held=bool(r.seat and r.unattended)`, ~2494) and rule 3's fill appends a `why: fill` entry (`_replay(s, "fill", …)`, ~3483), so its newest entry is never the start's own: the rule excludes it with no special case. A seat that is not unattended is replayed live and read as a member is.
- **The cap.** `_work_start` reads `worked = rec.get("work_worked") or []` and passes it to `_work_held` as a new parameter; `_work_held` (~2554): `if len([t for t in started if t not in worked]) >= agent_common.WORK_STARTS_DAY` holds `{why: day, count: <that number>}`. `WORK_EARLY` still reads `started[-1]`, every start. `WORK_STARTS_DAY` stays 3; the comment at `agent_common.py:89` says idle starts.
- **The words.** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (~953): the `day` hold reads *started 3 times today and found no work* (`started {n} time(s) today and found no work`), as §4.5a's *Inbox row: team start* now says; the §4.5a help paragraph **when work appears** (Settings page: Teams) says *three starts in a day*: reword it there and in `help.py` together to *three starts in a day that found no work* (the help text is bound to the design's list; the design PR left it for this build). `tests/test_ui_work_row.py` pins the old words at 124 (a dict key that feeds `work_held`), 261, 301, 349: update them.
- Tests (`tests/test_work_start.py`, where the cap's tests are, ~119–130; its existing `day` cases keep a claim-free setup): three starts each followed by a declared claim on a member record leave a fourth start open (no `day` hold); three starts with no claim hold at `{why: day, count: 3}`; a claim stamped before a start, a `derived` entry, a carried claim restamped by the replay, another member's claim on a member-by-member start, and a claim after a person's Start superseded the record and a seat's claim after its fill do not make it worked; the carried claim marked `done` later, or declared again with a fresh `at` (`_upsert`), does, and so does a claim by a member that crashed before the next tick (read at that tick's first step, before rule 1 replays it); a claim between start 1 and start 2 makes only start 1 worked; a worked start's record closed and pruned keeps the start in `work_worked`; `WORK_EARLY` still holds a start inside thirty minutes of a worked one; `work_worked` drops an instant past the day with `work_started`.
