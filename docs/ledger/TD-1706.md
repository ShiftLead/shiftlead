## TD-1706: A work order the member dropped itself is not told back to it after each none

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 48978909993c425482212488543db46b
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1590
**Location:** design §6 rule 6 (save one the member itself released); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_lane_news, _closed_untold, a per-tick pass), agent.py (rpc_progress drop and claim, succeeds), models.py (Session.released)

**Why:** Rule 6 never puts a work order in a declaration's first write, and only a line the member itself reported `done` is kept out (TD-1389). So a line or a red PR the member *dropped*, its reason on the record, is told again after every `ao progress none`: ledgerboard's grinder was rung four times in about ten minutes for one line it had dropped (2026-11-10, TD-1590), a wasted turn and a unit of the wake budget each time. TD-1590's design (§6 rule 6 *save one the member itself released*) keeps such an order out of the member's news.

**Ask:** Will you let TD-1590's design (a decided line answering a design's end-of-rounds item goes to that design's designer instead of the grinders, and a member's own dropped work order stops ringing it after each none), whose four rounds ended at the cap with every build-changing fix read by a later review, go to its builds TD-1705 and TD-1706, or buy one more round, or hold it with a note?

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 6 *save one the member itself released* (TD-1590), in `agent_tick._lane_news` beside `_closed_untold` (TD-1389):

1. **`released`, on the record.** A new `Session` field, in `HOME_OWNED` (`models.py` ~137) and not in `lane_seen` (which a claim and a `none` clear): `released: {ref: {"at_drop": decided, "told": decided}}`. The `ao progress drop` handler writes it only when the caller, as the home receives it (a node's forwarded caller included), is the record's own address (`self._address(s)`) — never a person's drop of its claim, nor a lead's or any other session's (`rpc_progress` lets another drop a claim), since both read `source: declared` and the entry alone cannot tell them apart. The tick's closure of an extra (`agent_scale.py` ~348, `agent_tick.py` ~1520) never passes the handler and needs no guard. For a `board:<key>` both values are `{"text": <Decided: text>, "date": <its date>}` from the repo reading's stored order at the drop; for a `pr:<n>` both are `null`. `at_drop` never changes after the drop (TD-1705's *to reach* reads it); `told` is the last answer told to the member. It is written only where the drop applied (`applied`, not a repeat), on a `board:` or `pr:` ref, and where the home's reading holds the order; otherwise none is written and the order is told as before. A node's member's `progress` is forwarded to the home (design §4.4a *A node forwards; the home answers*, `modes.REPORTS`), so the home's handler writes every mark. The member's next claim of that ref clears it. A per-tick pass on the home over every record of the repo, at work or not, removes a mark whose ref the reading no longer holds (the line closed); a board or PR reading that carries `error`, or none, removes no mark; TD-1705's join later runs in the same pass. `_carry` (`agent.py` ~2082) copies the mark beside `lane_seen`, so a rename, and a restart that renames, keep it; a restart that keeps its name starts a record with none.
2. In `agent_tick._lane_news`, beside `_closed_untold` (TD-1389), a work order the record carries a `released` mark for is kept out of the news and **written into** `lane_seen` untold. Unlike a `done` one, which `_closed_untold` leaves out of `lane_seen`, it goes in, because `_lane_drops` reads only ids `lane_seen` holds and a sibling's release must still be told. Write it before `_lane_news`'s early returns (`if not (new or drops)` and the gates), so a tick that tells nothing still writes it. On a reading whose `Decided:` text or date differs from the mark's `told`, the id is told once as news, and `told` takes the new answer **only in the step that sends the note**, after the suspended, wrap-up, gated and balance returns and `_leased` — so a gated member is told once its gate clears — and a third answer is told again. A `pr:<n>` is told again only by a sibling's release.
3. A sibling's later claim and drop is told through the existing dropped-lease path (`_lane_drops`).

Tests: a member that dropped `board:<key>` and declared `none` is not told the line on later ticks. A re-decided `Decided:` is told to it once (and to a gated member once its gate clears), including one answered while it held another claim between two declarations, and a third answer once more. A sibling's later claim and drop is told once. A person's drop of the member's claim, another session's drop of it, and an extra's closure write no mark. A member that claimed the order again after its drop is told as before. A mark whose line closed is removed by the per-tick pass, for a member at work too, and a failed board reading removes none. A node member's own drop writes its mark. A repeat drop, a drop of a `TD-n` ref, and a drop of an order the reading does not hold write no mark. A renamed record, and one restarted under a new name, keep the mark; one restarted under its own name has none.

**Done when:** A member that dropped a work order itself is not told it again after its `none`, unless the person answers the line again or a sibling claims and drops it; tests pin each case.
