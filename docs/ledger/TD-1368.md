## TD-1368: A held entry whose board decide line the person answered reads answered — waiting on its holder, not needs you

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1364's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1364
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/workorders.py` (`read_boards`, which builds each board's record and keeps no closed line today: the boards' `carried`, and the archive read, which is new); `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_read_boards`, which passes it on); `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`answered_hold` and the `reading` that calls it); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`ledger_for_you`, `BOARD_WAIT_DAYS`, `board_waiting_on`); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (the answered item's foot); `tests/test_queue_answered_hold.py`, `tests/test_ui_answered_hold.py`; `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` (the end-of-rounds item's *closes the line pointing at it*: the close's `--why` begins *carried to TD-n*, design §4.9a — a held path, reviewed by the techlead)

**Why:** TD-1364: a `decision (paul)` entry whose question is a board `decide` line still reads *needs you* after the person answers the line, while a session acts on the answer — today the hold's answered word (TD-1316) is linked by mail alone.

**Fix:** As design §4.4 *The card's status* (the paragraph *A question the person answered on the board holds the member the same way*), *The boards* (`carried`), §4.5 screen 6 (the Inbox's ledger rows and their count), screen 11 *For you*, screen 12 *Three wants* (*A held entry the person has answered*: the board answer's foot), §4.5a row *Queue card: what needs the person*, and §4.9a *What a session does with a decided line* (the close's *carried to TD-n*):
- **the boards reading carries `carried`**: each closed `decide` line with a `Decided:` whose `Context:` names an entry, of the board and its archive (`<board stem>_archive.md` beside the board's file, `docs/user_attention_archive.md` here, read here for the first time, from the same copy as the board — origin's where the board is read from origin), as `{key, ref, decided, closed, to, at}`. Cut the line's trailing `Closed:` (`CLOSED_RE`) before reading its `Decided:` and `Context:`: v2.3's `DECIDED_RE` is anchored at the line's end and matches nothing while `Closed:` follows (or build on v2.4's `parse_closed_items` if TD-1363 has landed). `ref` the `Context:`'s first ref as `item_home` attaches it (TD-1355) and `to` the ids a leading *carried to* in the `Closed:` reason lists, joined only by `,`, `and` or `&` and ending at the first other word, dash or sentence end (*carried to TD-1310 and TD-1311*; *carried to TD-9 — see TD-4* is TD-9), each read as a bare id on that board (its own repo's entry, TD-1355); an id of a repo the home reads no ledger of holds nothing; `at` one index over both files, the board's lines in file order and then the archive's. A `Closed:` with no reason, or *— Go*, parses with `to` empty;
- **`answered_hold`** (one pure function, as TD-1331 made it, called by `reading` and `ledger_for_you` alike, both passing the same new arguments: the reading's boards — open items and `carried` — by repo, the live claims by ref, and the open entries' `Added:` and `Part of` through the open-member index) also links a member to a decided `decide` line that **covers** it — names it, or names the entry it is `Part of` while it carries `decision (paul)`, or lists it in the line's `Parts:` (v2.3's `parse_parts`). An open line holds for as long as it is open (its stale return to the Inbox's Needs you after `BOARD_WAIT_DAYS`, built, is the bound, and it then draws in the member's place on the card); `BOARD_WAIT_DAYS` bounds the other-words closed line below (the constant moves to `sessionorc`, since `sessionorc` never imports `shiftlead`, and `shiftlead.ui.inbox` reads it from there); a closed line with `to` while any id of it is open, not a covered member, neither `Owner: paul` nor `decision (paul)`, and `Added:` on or after the line's `Decided:` date; a closed line without `to` while such a live record claims a covered member, within `BOARD_WAIT_DAYS` of its `Closed:` date. Its result names the word's `<asker>` — the order's holder, else a covered member's holder, else the line's live `session`, else *a pick* (open); the carried entry's holder, else `TD-m` (closed, with `to`) — and picks one line over every covering decided line, open or closed: the newest by `Decided:`, on a tie an open line before a closed one, then the later `Closed:`, then the smaller `at`. That line's test alone decides, so a newest line whose test has ended returns the hold to the person. A mail question that also holds wins. A board answer's result is `{kind: "board", asker, closed_at: its Decided: date, outcome: None, line: {key, open, to}}` with no `mid`, so the foot and the Lift road tell it from mail;
- **the Queue**: a stale open line (past `BOARD_WAIT_DAYS`, back in the Inbox's Needs you) is the card's Decide item, and `attach`'s `asked` set strips the member's own block, as it already does for the entry a board Decide item's `Context:` names. That set is widened here to every member the line covers (a `decision (paul)` part of the named design, an id in its `Parts:`), which is new: today it strips by `on_row` alone, the line's first ref. An undecided covering line does the same, so there is one row for one answer. A closed line that holds draws the grey answered item with the foot *you answered "<Decided: words>" on the board, <age> ago* then *carried to TD-m* or *<holder> acts on it*. **Lift the hold** stays in its ⋯, and the line it writes makes the entry read *answered — waiting on a pick*. There are no answers, no Answer… and no Start session, as in the mail case;
- **the Inbox and the Repo page**: TD-565 stands. An entry an open decided line covers (names, or names its parent while it carries `decision (paul)`) is left out of the Inbox's ledger rows and *For you* (`ledger_for_you`'s `decided` filter, widened from *names* to *covers*), and its line is the *Waiting on them* row. After the line closes, the row holds *answered — waiting on <asker>* while a closed line holds. The repo's count leaves out the entries an open decided line covers.

**Done when:** tests show each of these:
- a member covered by an open decided `decide` line reads *answered — waiting on <holder>* and gives the card no *needs you* while a live record claims the line's `board:<key>`, and also while one claims the member itself;
- unclaimed, the same line does so while open, and once stale it is the card's one Decide item, standing in the member's place;
- with an open `go ahead` line (Lift the hold) and an older closed carried line on the same `Decided:` date, the open line is the one picked;
- a newest line whose test has ended returns the need, even with an older line that would hold;
- a build `Part of` the named design that carries its own `decision (paul)` reads the same word as the design;
- a line closed *carried to TD-m* (and *carried to TD-m and TD-k*) holds while either is open and not the person's, and stops holding once they archive;
- a line closed in other words holds only while a live record claims a covered member, and not past `BOARD_WAIT_DAYS` after its `Closed:` date;
- an old carried line whose TD-m was added before its `Decided:` date does not hold;
- a covered build is left out of the Inbox and *For you* while an open line covers it;
- a new undecided `decide` line covering the member (naming it or its parent design) makes it *needs you* again;
- a claimed open line stale by date returns to Needs you as the card's Decide item, the member still *answered — waiting on <holder>*;
- *carried to TD-9 — see TD-4* holds on TD-9 alone;
- a member listed in a line's `Parts:` is covered;
- a mail question that holds wins over a board line;
- the foot reads *carried to TD-m* and *<holder> acts on it* in their cases;
- after a Lift the hold press the entry reads *answered — waiting on a pick*;
- a closed line's `Decided:` is read with its `Closed:` cut;
- the *For you* row and the Inbox's *Later* row draw a closed holding line as *answered — waiting on <asker>* and leave an entry out while an open decided line covers it.

Then a live read of the next such hold on the Queue, recorded in this entry before it archives.
