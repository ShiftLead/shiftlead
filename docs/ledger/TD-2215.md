## TD-2215: The Flows design does not say whether a stage's session still reads the person's own briefs and texts from their cadence layer

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 01ded30c8f8a40be8fd71bed7803a4da

**Why:** TD-1016 (built, its live check open) gives the person's own texts one home that follows them, their cadence layer repo: `shiftlead/briefs/<role>.md`, `entry.md`, `work.md` and `preferences.md`, read into a member's brief under *Your own rules*. The Flows design (4.9c *Where a brief lives*) names three places a brief lives (the package's standard briefs, the org's on the Briefs page at `~/.shiftlead/briefs/<name>.md`, and a flow's own directory), names briefs by kind (design, build, review, audit) and renames the grinder role builder. It does not say whether a stage's session still reads the person's layer, so after the switch-over the layer's `shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md` may be read by nothing, and nothing says where the person's own additions to a stage's brief go. Found by a read of the open ledger against the new designs (Paul, 2026-11-25).

**Design PR:** #4658

**Location:** docs/design/4.9c-flows.md (Where a brief lives; What a stage's session is told)

**Fix:** Design first, in 4.9c *What a stage's session is told* and *Where a brief lives*, with the ADR of 2026-10-25. Decide whether a stage's, an audit's and a built-in seat's session reads the person's layer texts after the switch-over, and if so under which names (by kind, by brief name, or the old role names carried over) and where in what the session is told; or say that the Briefs page replaces the layer for briefs, and what then becomes of `entry.md`, `work.md` and `preferences.md`. Say what TD-1016's live check and TD-662 (the director's preferences, which reads the layer) then need. File the builds.

**Done when:** Design 4.9c says where the person's own texts are read from under a flow and what a file under the old role names does, TD-1016 and TD-662 are reworded or closed to match, and the builds are filed.
