## TD-2251: Nothing tests that filling a built-in seat reads the real logins and their reserves

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** c663f9799fd142318e38c20acfb055d5

**Why:** Audit of #4717 (TD-2068 slice 4). `_builtin_login` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`) builds the pick's rows from the home's own reads: `teamstore.login_rows(read, self._projected(gate["accounts"], doc, now), 0, now)`, with `read` the `tools` reply and `gate` the `gate` reply. Design §4.9d *The login a seat starts on* has the fill start on the first usable login of the set's list, and a login at its reserve is not usable.

The `rows` fixture of `tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py` replaces all three (`rpc_tools`, `rpc_gate` and `teamstore.login_rows`, the last with `lambda *a, **kw: held`), so every test hands the pick rows it wrote itself and none reads the arguments. Nothing says that a login a real reading shows at its reserve is passed over by the fill.

With that line changed to `rows = teamstore.login_rows({}, {}, 0, now)`, so the fill reads no login and no account at all:

- at b6f5c8850, the 47 test modules that mention `cut_over`, `builtin` or `unfillable` passed (1239 passed);
- at 28715c324, tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py, test_builtinstore.py, test_teamstore.py and test_team_show.py passed (122 passed).

Probe (a temporary test over the module's own `_held_seat`, leaving `login_rows` real and giving `rpc_tools` one card, `{"adapter": "hookstub", "accounts": [{"key": "acct-1", "models": {"families": ["opus"]}}], "logins": [{"name": "first", "state": "signed_in", "account": "acct-1"}]}`, and `rpc_gate` `{"accounts": {}}`): the unchanged code fills the tech lead on `hookstub:first`; with the change it fills nothing and the record holds no login.

A break here would either leave every built-in seat unfilled after the cut-over, or, where only the accounts are lost, start one on a login at its reserve.

With that change made together with the other changes that TD-2251 to TD-2255 name, the whole suite at b6f5c8850 (`pytest -n 4`) passed (10259 passed, 24 skipped).

**Location:** tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py _builtin_login

**Fix:** In tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py, add a fill that leaves `teamstore.login_rows` real: give `rpc_tools` and `rpc_gate` replies of the shape the host agent sends (or let the real calls read a scratch `logins.yml` and usage reading), with the first login's account over its reserve in the gate's windows. Assert the seat is filled on the second login.

**Done when:** Handing `login_rows` an empty `tools` read, or an empty accounts mapping, in `_builtin_login` fails a test.
