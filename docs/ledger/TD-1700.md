**Why:** TD-1568's design: §4.5 *A taken-over member is marked*, §4.5a *card: taken over mark*, *team header: taken over count* and *Focus: Hand back*. A member the person took over and never handed back stops its team's stage, and the page shows it no differently from the person's own sessions (2026-11-10: ao-grind's fact-check seat stayed interactive after Paul allowed its permission prompts, and the team's fact-checks did not run). Paul asked for the Hand back button and the session's card in orange.

**Fix:** Design §4.5 *A taken-over member is marked* (TD-1568), rendered in `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-11-td1568-taken-over-light.png` and `…-dark.png`.
- **One predicate.** Add `mail.taken_over(record, agent)` to `src/sessionorc/mail.py`. It is true when all of these hold: `agent` (the caller's `adapters.has_agent`, so never a shell); the state is not `exited`, `closed` or `scheduled`; `team` is set or `controllers` is non-empty; `mail.by_person(record)` is false (the derived reading, not the raw field); `mail_wakes` is false. Its three readers call it in place of their own expressions: `unanswered.py`'s `taken_over` branch, `agent_serve._held_mail`, and `agent_serve._view`. The mark reads this base fact, so a member that is also suspended, stop-held or at its ceiling is marked as well, beside its own lines.
- **The view.** `agent_serve._view` carries `taken_over` (bool) and, when it is true, three fields:
  - `taken_lead`: *taken over* after a flip's stamp, *held interactive from its start* after a start's (`mode_via: start`), *held interactive* with no stamp;
  - `taken_at`: `mode_at`, or none with no stamp;
  - `taken_tail`: *by `<you | name>` — its mail does not wake it, and its team's work waits; Hand back returns it*. The *by* part comes only after a flip. Its name follows `taken_name`'s rule (§4.9b), read from `_view`'s `graph`, or the id where the stamp's record is not in it.

  The page composes the hover from them, counting *`<age>` ago* from `taken_at` the way the card's clock is counted. The compact line is the lead and the age alone.
- **The full card** (`src/shiftlead/ui/templates/card.html`, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css`): with `taken_over`, the card takes the class `tko`. Row 2's mode reads *taken over* with the `person` icon, in `--warn-fg`, in place of *interactive*. The card's ground is `--warn-bg` and its border `--warn-line`; `.sc.s-needs`'s amber border wins over it. The state bar is unchanged.
- **The compact card** (the same template's compact branch): the person glyph `--warn-fg`, the same ground and border, and a line of its own after the card's line, placed as the *held* line (`stop_held_line`) is: *taken over `<age>` ago*, its hover `taken_words`.
- **The team header** (`src/shiftlead/ui/templates/group_head.html`, and the group's view where `ready` is counted, `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py`): *n taken over* in `--warn-fg`, after *ready to close*, folded or not. It counts the team's records whose view carries `taken_over`, and its hover names each (*fact-check-ao-1, taken over 3h ago by you*). It is never pressable and absent at 0.
- **Focus** (`src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html`): this covers the acts slot `#fmodeact` and the phone sheet's acts (`focus.html` near line 455, drawn `act next` today), plus `app.js`'s live update of `mode_act` near the TD-964 comment. While the view carries `taken_over`, **Hand back** takes the class `tko` (`btn sm tko`: `--warn-line` ground and border, `--warn-fg` text at full weight). Never use the class `warn`: its global banner rule (`app.css`, `.warn`) would apply. On the sheet the class replaces the `next` look. Otherwise Hand back stays plain. Plain covers, for example, a lead's member the person resumed, which reads `by_person`. It is never `next`.
- **Live.** The marks follow the record's deltas: a Hand back, an end or any flip to unattended clears them on the next one. The groups payload's count follows the same re-read as the lane count (`HEADS_EVERY`).

**Done when:** Tests pin each of these:
- `mail.taken_over` is true for an idle interactive member (team, not `by_person`) and false for a person's own session, a `by_person` record with `controllers`, an unattended member, an ended record and a shell.
- `unanswered`'s `taken_over` state and `_held_mail` read it.
- A suspended taken-over member is marked.
- The rendered full card and compact card of a taken-over member carry `tko`, *taken over* and the hover. The person's own interactive session carries none of them.
- The team header reads *1 taken over* with the member's name in its hover, and nothing at 0.
- Focus's Hand back, in the acts slot and in the phone sheet, carries `tko` on a taken-over member and not on a `by_person` record with `controllers`.
- The hover reads *taken over … by you — …* after a flip and *held interactive from its start …* after a start's stamp.

The build also renders a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`) with a taken-over member, light and dark. Its shot is committed and matches the design's mockups.
