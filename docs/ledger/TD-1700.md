**Location:** design §4.5 A taken-over member is marked, §4.5a card: taken over mark, team header: taken over count, Focus: Hand back; src/sessionorc/mail.py, unanswered.py, agent_serve.py (_view, _held_mail); src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py, repo.py; src/shiftlead/ui/templates/card.html, group_head.html, focus.html; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css, app.js

**Why:** TD-1568's design: §4.5 *A taken-over member is marked*, §4.5a *card: taken over mark*, *team header: taken over count* and *Focus: Hand back*. A member the person took over and never handed back stops its team's stage, and the page shows it no differently from the person's own sessions (2026-11-10: ao-grind's fact-check seat stayed interactive after Paul allowed its permission prompts, and the team's fact-checks did not run). Paul asked for the Hand back button and the session's card in orange.

**Fix:** Design §4.5 *A taken-over member is marked* (TD-1568), rendered in `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-11-td1568-taken-over-light.png` and `…-dark.png`.


- **One predicate.** Add `mail.taken_over(record, agent)` to `src/sessionorc/mail.py`. It is true when all of these hold:
  - `agent` is true (the caller's `adapters.has_agent`, so never a shell);
  - the state is not `exited`, `closed` or `scheduled`;
  - `team` is set or `controllers` is non-empty;
  - `mail.by_person(record)` is false (the derived reading, not the raw field);
  - `mail_wakes` is false.
  Its three readers call it in place of their own expressions: `unanswered.py`'s `taken_over` branch, `agent_serve._held_mail` and `agent_serve._view`. The mark reads this base fact, so a member that is also suspended is marked as well, beside its *suspended* mark. Two readers change slightly, each pinned by a test: `_held_mail` now also leaves a `scheduled` record out, and `unanswered` now reads the derived `mail.by_person`, so a record from before the field reads as the view already reads it.
- **The view.** `agent_serve._view` carries `taken_over` (bool) and, when it is true, three more fields. It sets them only at the home (`self.mode != "node"`, as `_held_mail` guards) and only while the view's state is not `unreachable`.
  - `taken_lead`: *taken over* after a flip's stamp, *held interactive from its start* after a start's (`mode_via: start`), *held interactive* with no stamp.
  - `taken_at`: `mode_at`, or none with no stamp.
  - `taken_tail`: *by `<you | name>` — its mail does not wake it; Hand back returns it*. The *by* part comes only after a flip. Its name follows `taken_name`'s rule (§4.9b), read from `_view`'s `graph` (`self._graph()` when none is passed), whose keys are home-form ids as `mode_by` is. `_view` puts the id in where the record is not in the graph. It reuses `_view`'s `down` for the `unreachable` test.
- **The hover.** The page composes it from the three fields: the lead, *`<age>` ago* in `fmtAge`'s form counted from `taken_at`, then the tail.
  - It replaces two titles: the full card's mode word's (*interactive: yours — no policy acts on it; switch it from more ⋯*), and the compact card's glyph's and line's (*yours: an interactive session*).
  - Each element that carries it holds `data-taken-at`, `data-taken-lead` and `data-taken-tail`. The ticker that keeps the card's clock (`app.js`, `.age[data-since]`) rewrites those titles on each tick too.
  - The compact line's age is an `.age[data-since]` span of its own. The server draws the first title and the first age with the card's own age helper, as the card's clock is first drawn, and the ticker keeps both.
- **`cards.py`.** The card's view passes `taken_over`, `taken_lead`, `taken_at` and `taken_tail` from the record's view into the card dict and Focus's `v`. `repo.py` reads them off the members (`m`) for the header's `taken`.
- **The full card** (`src/shiftlead/ui/templates/card.html`, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css`):
  - With `taken_over`, the card takes the class `tko`.
  - Row 2's mode reads *taken over* with the `person` icon, in `--warn-fg`, in place of *interactive*, whatever the stamp.
  - The ground is `--warn-bg` and the border `--warn-line`. On a `needs-you` card the ground stays, and `.sc.s-needs`'s amber border and ring win.
  - The state bar is unchanged.
  - `cards.py`'s `own` leaves a taken-over member out: `(not team or not unattended) and not taken_over`. So the card reads no *ready to close ✓*, and the header's `ready` (`repo.py`) does not count it.
  - The foot's first act is **Hand back** (`btn sm tko`, the same `set_mode` call Focus's Hand back makes), in place of Close session. Close session stays in more ⋯. The compact card's foot does the same.
- **The compact card** (the same template's compact branch):
  - The person glyph is `--warn-fg`, with the same ground and border.
  - The card's last line, after the *routed around* and *held* lines, reads the lead and the age: *taken over 3h 12m ago*, *held interactive from its start, 3h 12m ago*, or *held interactive*.
- **The team header** (`src/shiftlead/ui/templates/group_head.html`, and the group's view where `ready` is counted, `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py`):
  - The groups payload carries `taken`: the count and the names of the team's records whose view carries `taken_over`.
  - The header draws *n taken over* in `--warn-fg` after *ready to close*, folded or not. Its hover is *<names> — taken over and not handed back; each card says since when*, with no age.
  - It is never pressable, is absent at 0, and appears only on a team's header. A lead's worker with no team is marked on its card alone.
  - The rollup and the team summary draw nothing of it.
- **Focus** (`src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html`, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`):
  - While the view carries `taken_over`, the acts slot's **Hand back** (`#fmodeact`) takes the class `tko` (`btn sm tko`: the `--taken` ground and border and `--taken-fg` text at full weight. These are two new tokens in `app.css`'s light and dark blocks: light `#fdba74` on `#7c2d12`, dark `#fdba74` on `#3a2410`, so the label keeps its contrast in dark).
  - `app.js`'s live update of `mode_act` (near the TD-964 comment) toggles `tko` with each view.
  - The phone sheet's visible Hand back is the `fmodemenu` entry, at the toggle's place. The sheet's `fill()` toggles `tko` on it both ways from `#fmodeact`, so the class comes off once the slot reads Take over. The sheet's own buttons are styled for it (`.act.tko`), not only `btn sm`.
  - Never use the class `warn`: the global banner rule (`.warn` in `app.css`) would apply to it.
  - Hand back is never `next`, and stays plain otherwise, for example on a lead's member the person resumed (`by_person`).
  - Focus's mode words (the Session card's *mode* row, the sheet's heading) are unchanged.
  - A taken-over member keeps the card's `data-mine`, so it still sorts and filters with the person's own sessions.
- **Live.** The marks follow the record's deltas: a Hand back, an end or any flip to unattended clears them on the next one. The groups payload's `taken` follows the same re-read as `ready`.

**Done when:** Tests pin each of these:
- `mail.taken_over` is true for an idle interactive member (team, not `by_person`), and for one also suspended. It is false for a person's own session, a `by_person` record with `controllers`, an unattended member, an ended record and a shell.
- `unanswered`'s `taken_over` state and `_held_mail` read it. `_held_mail` draws no row for a `scheduled` member, and `unanswered` reads a record from before `by_person` as `mail.by_person` does.
- `_view` carries `taken_over` with `taken_lead`, `taken_at` and `taken_tail`, for a flip's stamp, a start's stamp and no stamp. It carries none on a node or while `unreachable`.
- The rendered full card of a taken-over member carries `tko`, *taken over* and the composed hover. The compact card carries `tko` and its last line in each of the three forms. The person's own interactive session carries none of them.
- A taken-over `needs-you` card keeps the tint under its amber border.
- A taken-over member's card, full and compact, leads its foot with Hand back (`tko`), never Close session. It reads no *ready to close ✓*, and the header's *ready to close* does not count it.
- The full card's first render carries the hover's title with a server-drawn age.
- The team header reads *1 taken over* with the member's name in its hover, nothing at 0, and nothing on a non-team header. The rollup draws nothing of it.
- Focus's Hand back, in the acts slot and in the sheet's `fmodemenu` entry, carries `tko` on a taken-over member. It does not on a `by_person` record with `controllers`. The class comes off both after a live Hand back (`app.js`'s `mode_act` update and the sheet's `fill()`).

The build also renders a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`) with a taken-over member, light and dark. Its shot is committed and matches the design's mockups.
