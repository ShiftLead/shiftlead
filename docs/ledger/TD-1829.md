**Status:** Open

**Why:** **Lift the hold** writes a decided line, `go ahead`, with `Context: TD-n`. Design §4.5 screen 12 and §4.4 *The card's status* say the session that takes this work order drops `decision (paul)`. agent-cadence's `ledger.py set` refuses to: removing `decision (paul)` needs `--by <person>`, and `--by` or `--override` from an unattended session is refused with *only a person's own session lifts a person's gate* (`scripts/agent_sessions.py`, pin acf46ead). So no lane can do a `go ahead` order.

Seen on board:19274ec5, the go ahead on TD-1796 (2026-11-13). grinder-ao-2 claimed it and the helper refused. Paul then answered *Lift it — a session may drop decision (paul) with --by paul on my go ahead* (m-861f98d4d042), and the helper refused again. The line was carried to this entry so lanes stop picking it. TD-1796 still carries `decision (paul)`.

**Design PR:** #3820

**Location:** design §4.4 The card's status, §4.5 screen 12 Three wants; agent-cadence scripts/ledger.py set (agent_sessions guard)

**Fix:** Pick one road for a `go ahead` and make the design and the helper agree.

(a) The press lifts the gate itself, from the home on the person's behalf, as TD-1375's decide lift does. Its line then names the gate (`Context: TD-n decision (paul)`), and the *answered — waiting on a pick* state is dropped.

(b) agent-cadence's guard accepts a decided board line by the person as the lift's authority: `set --by paul --answer "go ahead"` from any session, where an open or carried `decide` line `Decided: go ahead (by paul)` names the entry. Hand this to agent-cadence with `ao td add --repo agent-cadence`.

Then drop TD-1796's `decision (paul)` and make TD-1796 Owner: paul. Its last step is the Done when's by-hand check of the issue form, which needs Paul signed in to GitHub.

**Done when:** A **Lift the hold** press on a `decision (paul)` entry ends with the gate dropped, by the home or by a lane's session, and no helper refuses it. A test or a walk holds this, and TD-1796's `decision (paul)` is gone.
