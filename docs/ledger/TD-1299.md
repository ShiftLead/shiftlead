## TD-1299: Narrow Focus: one screen — the strip, the tray, the input bar with the composer as a sheet, the ⋯ sheet, the switcher, the grid rules and the home-screen manifest

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-01 (phone-view, from TD-003's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — the design's review rounds run first, then Paul's go.
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-003
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html`, `base.html` (the viewport meta, the manifest link), a new `static/manifest.webmanifest` and icons; `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (the `@media (max-width: 720px)` Focus rules: `.termbox { height: 50vh }`, the static composer, `.focus .side`, the rail row), `app.js` (`AO.focus`, `fill()`, the composer, the resize sender, the read-only line, `openTerm()`'s reconnect); `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py`; tests `test_ui.py`, `test_ui_focus_rail.py`, `test_design_doc.py`

**Ask:** Now the phone layout's review rounds have converged, should a grinder build the narrow Focus?

**Why:** TD-003's design: §4.5 *Phone layout* and §4.5a *the strip*, *the tray*, *the input bar · ⌨*, *the sheet (⋯)*, *the switcher*.

**Fix:**
1. **The screen**: under the narrow query (`(max-width: 720px), (pointer: coarse) and (max-height: 500px)`, one `matchMedia` the CSS and `app.js` share; a popped-out window excluded) Focus is pinned to the visual viewport and never scrolls — `position: fixed; inset: 0`, `100dvh`, on iOS the `visualViewport`'s height and `offsetTop` from its `resize` and `scroll`; Focus's own viewport meta adds `interactive-widget=resizes-content` and `viewport-fit=cover` (other pages keep `base.html`'s); the safe-area insets as padding. The top bar, the identity line, the acts line and the side panel are not drawn; `AO.holdBar` does not run (`--barh` 0), `AO.termFill` and `fill()` leave the terminal to the screen's column, and `.termbox`'s 360 px `min-height` is lifted; the terminal runs from the strip (and the existing banners) to the key bar.
2. **The strip**, **the tray**, **the input bar · ⌨** and the composer as a sheet, **the sheet (⋯)** with each side-panel card opened as a sheet, **the switcher**, each as its §4.5a row says; kept current from the pushed delta as the header is.
3. **The grid**: rows only for the keyboard, columns only for a turn or a pinch, each through the 150 ms rule; the tray and every sheet lie over the terminal and send nothing, the terminal refitting when a sheet closes; the terminal's helper text box at 16 px (no iOS zoom); **a tap** on the terminal has its default prevented in the touch handler (§4.6), so only ⌨ focuses it.
4. **A link** opens on a press held still 500 ms: a timer from `pointerdown` on the terminal, cancelled by 10 px of travel or a second finger, at whose end the cell under the press is looked up through the terminal's link providers (as a hover would) and its link opened in a new tab, as Ctrl+click does; the long press's `contextmenu` stays prevented (§4.6).
5. **Home screen**: `manifest.webmanifest` (`display: standalone`, `start_url` and `scope` `/`, name, theme colour, icons at 192 and 512), and for iOS `<link rel="apple-touch-icon">`, `apple-mobile-web-app-capable` and `apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style` `black-translucent`; the `/events` and `/term` sockets reconnect on `visibilitychange` and `online`; the disconnected banner opens with *is Tailscale on?*.
6. **Help**: §4.5a help entries for the strip, the tray, the input bar, the sheet and the switcher, written in `help.py` and the design's list in the same PR.
7. Tests: the narrow template draws the strip, the input bar and the sheet's rows for each card the record draws and nothing of the header's two lines; the sheet draws no Copy, copy on select, Pop out or VS Code; a record with no agent's input bar reads *Type a command…* and its sheet has no Wrap up, Message or agent cards; an unattended record draws the tray's Take over and the input bar's *Message* with no ⌨; an ended or scheduled one neither bar; the manifest and the touch icon are served. Screenshots at 375 × 700 under `docs/mockups/reviews/`, beside the mockup's frames.

**Done when** Focus at 375 px matches the mockup's frames 1, 3–7 (frame 2's key bar is TD-1300's), the tests hold it, and Paul has opened it from his phone's home screen.
