## TD-2265: An early question from a session stops counting on the Queue tab when its card draws it grey

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 6fc755c770504fd5b942768ab1e70e62
**Part of:** TD-2231

**Why:** Design TD-2231 (§4.4 *A question whose entry only waits is asked early*, §4.5 screens 6 and 12): an item the Queue draws grey on a card with no pill — an open `ask --lift` from a session about an entry asked early or *waiting, then yours*, and a `decide` line covering only such entries — counts in no **Needs you (n)**, top bar number or rail. Today `inbox_sections` (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`) leaves out of `count` and `queue_n` only an early board line, so an early or *waiting, then yours* `ask --lift` (and a *waiting, then yours* line) puts a 1 on the Queue tab that no card shows.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py; tests/test_queue_asked_early_items.py; design §4.4, §4.5 screens 6 and 12

**Fix:** 1. In `inbox_sections` (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`), `counted` leaves out every `needs` row carrying `early` or `then_yours`, a board line and a mail item alike (today: `not (e.get("early") and e.get("row") == "board")`); `count`, `queue_n` and `overdue_n` read it as today. Update the comment above it to cite §4.4 *A question whose entry only waits is asked early* and TD-2231.
2. Nothing else changes: the card's drawing (`rewrite` in `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`), the rail (`rail_rows`, which already passes over both marks) and §4.10's depths (an open question still holds its sender's slot) stay as they are.
3. Tests, beside `tests/test_queue_asked_early_items.py`'s fixtures: an open early `ask --lift`, an open `ask --lift` whose entry is *waiting, then yours*, and an undecided line covering a member *waiting, then yours* each give `count` 0 and `queue_n` 0 through `inbox_sections`, and an overdue undecided line covering a member *waiting, then yours* gives `overdue_n` 0 (the Sessions rollup's *m overdue* and `ao`'s `overdue` read it), and an ordinary open `ask` still counts 1; the card's item is drawn as before.

**Done when:** Through `inbox_sections`, an open `ask --lift` about an entry asked early or *waiting, then yours*, and a line covering only such entries, count 0 in `count` and `queue_n` (an overdue such line 0 in `overdue_n`); an ordinary open ask counts 1; the tests in Fix item 3 pass.
