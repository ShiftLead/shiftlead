## TD-2102: End the Gemini processes a conformance walk starts

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d479a6810d64484fbb956ab18ca5b62a
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: Gemini CLI processes started by the adapter's walks and probes outlive them. On 2026-11-23, six were orphaned, 27 to 36 days old, in directories since deleted, holding 0.58 GB of swap while swap was full. They were killed by hand that day, along with 122 orphaned `tests/_agent_child.py` processes that all predate TD-2067's fix.

**Location:** scripts/conformance.py, scripts/look_home.py, src/sessionorc/tmux.py

**Fix:** The cause is not yet found: `scripts/conformance.py`'s `finally` already stops its agent and kills its private tmux server, and `look_home.py` says a SIGKILL of the script cannot tear down, so the likely case is an interrupted walk or a tool that ignores SIGHUP. Reproduce it, then make the teardown end the whole process tree, in the walk's `finally` and on an interrupted run, by the process group or the private tmux server that holds it, never by matching a process name.

**Done when:** After a conformance walk of the Gemini adapter that ends normally, and one interrupted partway, no process it started is still running.
