## TD-1898: The home closes each member a Wind down reached once it settles

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** bcb32db9bc8340efa9ff1436f7d3d8fb
**Part of:** TD-1895

**Why:** A person's Wind down leaves its members open where no manager is live or a member never declared (TD-1895): the page's press closes nobody, and rule 9 closes nobody while one member is undeclared. The design (TD-1895) has the home close each member a Wind down reached once it settles.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (stop_members); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_finished_pass, _finished_close, _finished_tell); src/sessionorc/work.py (_how_stopped); src/sessionorc/models.py; src/sessionorc/agent.py (the mark's RPC, the send's clear); src/shiftlead/ending.py; design §4.9a, §6 rule 9

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *A wind-down closes its members* and §6 rule 9 *A wind-down's close* (TD-1895):

1. **The mark.** A home-owned record field `wind_down: {at, by, waits}` (§4.4a's home fields). `teamrun.stop_members`, when not `--now`, writes it through one home RPC on every member it acts on, before its send: `by` from the caller as `closer` is; `waits` true for a member sent the wrap-up and for a finished member left idle for the doorbell with unread mail, false for a finished member sent nothing. Never on the manager, a seat or a person's session. Routed to a node's member as the home's other writes are. Cleared by the next send to the record from a person or a session not marked `wrapup` (beside `wrapup_at`'s clear), never by the home's own sends (doorbell, resume, nudge, rule 9's line).
2. **The close.** In the tick's rule 9 pass (`agent_tick._finished_pass`, at the home), for each team: every live unattended record with `wind_down`, not a seat, not the team's manager (`work.manager_of`), that is idle with no live subagent and no live shell, and whose mark has `waits` false, or `turn_at` ≥ `wind_down.at`, or is `WRAPUP_GRACE` old, is closed through `_finished_close(s, why="wind_down")` — whatever `work.finished` reads, and before it. One left open (work left, git unknown, link down) is tried again each tick. No `FINISHED_SETTLE`, nothing taken back.
3. **The stop reading.** `work._how_stopped` reads a closer `{by: tick, why: wind_down}` as `person` when the record's `wind_down.by` is `person`, else None (§6 rule 8 *A team that stopped*).
4. **No note.** `_finished_tell` writes nothing for a team whose members carry a `wind_down` mark by the person.
5. **The card's words.** `ending.TICK_WHY` gains `wind_down` → *after Wind down* (§4.5 row 5 (b)).
6. **Tests.** ao-grind's case: a team with its manager closed, two declared idle members and one idle undeclared member, each marked by the person's Wind down, all clean and pushed: after one tick all three are closed `{by: tick, why: wind_down}` and the team's stop reads `person`. A member with unpushed work stays open and is closed the tick it reads pushed. A member sent the wrap-up and still idle from before it (`turn_at` before the mark) is not closed until a turn ends or `WRAPUP_GRACE` passes. A plain send from the person clears the mark; a doorbell does not. A member without the mark beside an undeclared one is not closed (rule 9 unchanged).

**Done when:** After a Wind down — the person's, with no live manager — every member that settles idle, clean and pushed is closed by the tick within a tick of settling, declared or not, its card reading *closed by the tick · after Wind down*, and the team reads stopped by the person; one with unpushed work is left open and closed once it reads pushed; the tests in the Fix pass.
