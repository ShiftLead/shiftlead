## TD-1898: The home closes each member a Wind down reached once it settles

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** bcb32db9bc8340efa9ff1436f7d3d8fb
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1895

**Why:** A person's Wind down leaves its members open where no manager is live or a member never declared (TD-1895): the page's press closes nobody, and rule 9 closes nobody while one member is undeclared. The design (TD-1895) has the home close each member a Wind down reached once it settles.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the way the host agent closes a wound-down team's members, or should a session run one more review round first?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (stop_members, _stop_one, _close_settled); src/sessionorc/agent.py (rpc_send's stamp, mark_wind_down; the _finished_owed dict); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_finished_team, _finished_view, _finished_close, _finished_tell, _idle_nudge, _context_line, _cadence_line, _held_line, _lane_news, _lost_brief, _brief_restart, _crashed, _extra_hold, _wanted_restart); src/sessionorc/work.py (_how_stopped, _last_word, finished, finished_alone); src/sessionorc/models.py (the host-side field list, the view); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (tick_ready), src/sessionorc/agent_scale.py, src/sessionorc/seat_send.py (the winding predicate); the RPC dispatcher, the node route table and ao's client (mark_wind_down); src/sessionorc/agent_wake.py (the doorbell's hold); src/sessionorc/mail.py (the stop-time line, only where it reads the predicate's fields); src/shiftlead/cli.py (container --wind-down); src/shiftlead/ending.py (closer_words and its read of `wind_down.by`); src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; tests/test_cli_teams.py; design §4.9a, §4.9, §4.10, §6 rules 8 and 9, §4.5a help

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *A wind-down closes its members* and §6 rule 9 *A wind-down's close* (TD-1895):

1. **The mark.** A record field `wind_down: {at, by, waits}`, on the host's side beside `wrapup_at` (§4.4a), carried in the record's view. It rides the wrap-up send: `rpc_send(wrapup=True, wind_down=True)` stamps it once `_submit` returns (`wrapup_at` is stamped before it), so a refused or failed send leaves no mark, `waits` true (a `_submit` failure after `wrapup_at`'s stamp leaves that stamp — the send waits on no turn, so it never stalls — so the member reads `winding`, but no mark: it is never closed by this road); a member refused is named *refused: <why>* in the press's result and left to a second press or `--close`; `teamrun._stop_one` passes `wind_down=True` for a member. A finished member that `stop_members` sends nothing (declared and settled, with unread mail or without) is marked by a new `mark_wind_down` RPC under the same caller check as `send`, `waits` false; it is no longer *left idle for the doorbell*: its line reads *out of work, n unread — marked, closed once it settles, mail kept*, or *finished (out of work), marked* with none (§4.9; `tests/test_cli_teams.py`'s *left idle for the doorbell* case changes with it). `by` is read by `closer_of`, as `wrapup_by` is: `person` for the page and for `ao` outside a session. Only an unattended record is marked; never on the manager (`split`'s lead, nor the record `work.manager_of` names), a `seat` record or a person's session; a sub-lead is marked as a member. Callers: the page's stop route, `ao team stop`, a manager's own `ao team stop`, the container `--wind-down` (`cli.py`). Register the field on the host's side in `models.py` beside `wrapup_at`, carry it in the view, forward `wind_down=True` on a node's send in the route table, and add `mark_wind_down` to the dispatcher and `ao`'s client. A person's send (`mail.as_person`) to a marked record moves `at`, stamped after `_submit` as the mark is, and sets `waits` true, and, being no wrap-up, clears `wrapup_at` and `wrapup_by` as today (so `work.py`'s person-wrap-up reading no longer sees it; the mark's `by` carries it); a session's controller `send` that is not a wrap-up clears it (and `wrapup_at` with it, as today), making the member an ordinary member again — a manager's *also push X* to a sub-lead mid-wind-down among them; a session's send to its own record (`act_gate` allows it) never touches the mark, though it clears `wrapup_at` as today; mail (`ao msg`, lane news) never touches it; a plain `wrapup=True` send with no `wind_down` (a card's Wrap up) moves `at` where it is the person's and otherwise leaves the mark; the home's own sends never touch it. A second Wind down keeps a `by` of `person`, a person's press on a session's mark takes `by` as `person`, and `at` moves only where a new wrap-up is sent.
2. **The close.** First in `agent_tick._finished_team` (rule 9's pass, at the home), before the `finished_sent_at` branch and `_finished_owed`, the team's members and views read again after it: every live unattended record with `wind_down`, not a seat, not the team's manager (`work.manager_of`), idle with no live subagent and no live shell, and settled — `waits` false; or `turn_at` > `wind_down.at` and idle for `SEND_STALL_SECONDS` measured on the home's clock from the first tick that read it idle after that turn (kept in memory, as `_finished_first` is, and started again by any tick that reads it not idle; a member's own turn ending between `_submit` and the stamp reads `turn_at` before `at` and only waits for the wrap-up's turn or `WRAPUP_GRACE`); or `WRAPUP_GRACE` past the first tick that read the mark (or its moved `at`), on the home's clock, kept in memory beside the idle window — is closed through `_finished_close(s, why="wind_down")`, whatever `work.finished` reads. One left open (work left, git unknown, link down, on a dialog) is tried again each tick, never killed; an `exited` marked record is not this close's and no rule restarts it (item 3). No `FINISHED_SETTLE`, nothing taken back. `_finished_owed` is not written, and `_finished_tell` writes nothing, for a team where any record carries a mark whose `by` is `person`, and `_finished_owed` is never written for a team with any mark, so the wound-down note is the one path. Where only a session pressed it, the note is written once, on the tick whose `wind_down` close leaves the team with no live marked record and nobody live in its crew: `_finished_tell` gains a wound-down form (its words §4.9a's: *<team> was wound down by <presser>, and the host agent closed its members once each settled. Nothing is asked of you.*, then the PRs and each member's `out_of_work.why` or *did not declare*), its `since` the earliest `wind_down.at` among the team's records; a last marked member closed by another hand (`--close`, a person's Close) gets none. While a marked member is live, `work.finished` carries its clause, so a live manager is neither told nor closed. A manager the reading then finds alone gets the line *your team was wound down by <presser>; its members are closed* in place of *every member has declared*, after `FINISHED_SETTLE` from the last member's close, and is closed `WRAPUP_GRACE` after it. The manager's half (the `finished_sent_at` branch's member-at-work check) passes over a marked member.
3. **One predicate.** `winding(s)`: `wrapup_at`, `wrapup_sent_at` or `wind_down` set. Every pass that now holds off a record on `wrapup_at` reads it: the doorbell's hold (`agent_wake.py`; `mail.py`'s stop-time line builds note text and changes only if it reads the predicate's fields), `_idle_nudge`, `_context_line`, `_cadence_line`, `_held_line`, `_lane_news`, `_lost_brief` (rule 1's lost-brief replay), `_brief_restart` (rule 7's changed-brief restart), `_crashed` (rule 1) and `_extra_hold` (`agent_tick.py`), the cache restart's `tick_ready` (`agent_common.py`), `agent_scale`'s fill hold (which reads `wrapup_at` alone today and widens to all three) and `seat_send`'s *stopping*. `_wanted_restart` (rule 2) holds off no wrap-up today and gains the predicate, so neither rule 1 nor rule 2 restarts a marked record. `work.finished` passes over an **ended** (`closed` or `exited`, by state) record carrying `wind_down`, as it passes over a sat-out one, and reads a **live** one as a clause *<name> winding down*; `_finished_view` carries the field.
4. **The stop reading.** `work._how_stopped` reads a closer whose `by` is `tick` and whose `why` is `wind_down` or `finished` as `person` when the record's `wind_down.by` or `wrapup_by`, as it stands, is `person` (a member's `wind_down` close, a manager's rule 9 close after the person's wrap-up), and a closer whose `by` is the record's own id (a manager closing itself) the same way; a manager, which carries no mark, whose `wrapup_by` a person's later send cleared reads `person` where any member of its team carries `wind_down.by` `person` — `_how_stopped` takes one record, so it gains the team's records (passed from `_last_word`, which holds them); else None (§6 rule 8 *A team that stopped*).
5. **The member-wise reading.** `work.finished_alone` (rule 8 *A member that finished while its team runs on*) leaves out a record whose closer's `why` is `wind_down`.
6. **`--close`.** `teamrun._close_settled` reports a member it finds closed with closer `why: wind_down` as closed (*closed by the tick after the wind-down*).
7. **The card's words.** `ending.closer_words` special-cases a tick closer whose `why` is `wind_down`: it reads the presser from the record's `wind_down.by` — *after your Wind down* for the person, *after <name>'s Wind down* for a session (its name where `names` knows the id) — and *after a Wind down* where the mark is missing or malformed (§4.5 row 5 (b)); `TICK_WHY` alone cannot, since it reads only `closer.why`.
8. **The help text.** Wind down's help (`ui/help.py`, §4.5a's help list) reads: *Sends the wrap-up to every member and then to the manager: each finishes what it holds and pushes, and the home closes each member once it settles; one on a dialog stays open, in the Inbox. Press it when the team should stop after the work in hand, not in the middle of it. It kills nothing — Stop now does — but for a member whose brief is not in yet, which it stops, and forgets nothing.* — §4.5a's help list changed with it in the same PR.
9. **Tests** (in `tests/test_cli_teams.py`, whose *left idle for the doorbell* case at its stop-words test changes, and the tick's and `work`'s own test files). ao-grind's case: a team with its manager closed, two declared idle members and one idle undeclared member, each marked by the person's Wind down, all clean and pushed: after one tick all three are closed `{by: tick, why: wind_down}` and the team's stop reads `person`. A live manager: its members closed, the manager closed by rule 9 after the line, the team's stop `person`. A member with unpushed work stays open and is closed the tick it reads pushed. A member `working` at the press whose own turn ends is not closed before the wrap-up turn has run (idle for less than `SEND_STALL_SECONDS` after a turn); one sent the wrap-up and idle from before it is not closed until a turn ends or `WRAPUP_GRACE` passes. A refused send leaves no mark. A waiting member (an open `ask` about a reference) is closed, its question left orphaned. A node member waits for its link. A seat and the manager are never marked. A second press by a session keeps the person's `by`. A person's send moves the mark; a session's plain send clears it; a doorbell does not touch it. A marked finished member is not rung. A member without the mark beside an undeclared one is not closed (rule 9 unchanged). A member closed `why: wind_down` is not replayed by rule 8's member-wise news. A marked member with `restart_wanted` is not restarted by rule 2; a marked crashed one is not restarted by rule 1 and, ended, does not block the reading; a live marked member reads *winding down* and holds the manager's line until it is closed, the manager's settle running from its close. A manager already told is not taken back by a marked member. The idle nudge and the lane news skip a marked record whose `wrapup_at` a person's send cleared. A `_submit` failure leaves no mark. A session-pressed, manager-less team gets the wound-down note once, on the tick its last marked member closes, and reads `other`; one whose last marked member a `--close` closed gets none; a team with a person's mark gets none. A `_how_stopped` tick close for another cause (`brief`, `seat`) is not read as the person's. A card closed `wind_down` with no mark reads *after a Wind down*; the manager's rule 9 close reads *team finished*. A manager's controller send to a marked member clears the mark and `wrapup_at`; an `ao msg` to it does not. An `exited` marked record with no `ended` or `closer` does not block the reading. An attended record in the team is not marked. A node member's `WRAPUP_GRACE` runs from the home's first reading of its mark. A member busy at the press on a node whose clock is skewed is not closed before its wrap-up runs. A manager that closes itself after the person's wrap-up reads `person`. The `--close` race: the first close stands and `_close_settled` reports it. A sub-lead under a manager → lead → worker team is marked and closed. An `exited` marked record is left alone. A member on a dialog is left open and named. The container `--wind-down` marks its members. A node member's mark is stamped on the node and read from the view. A member's send to itself leaves its mark. A person's send to a marked member clears its `wrapup_at` and `wrapup_by` and keeps the mark; a person's send to a finished (`waits` false) marked member sets `waits` true. A manager whose `wrapup_by` a person's later send cleared reads `person` from its members' marks. A member read busy inside its idle window starts the window again. A member whose own turn ends between `_submit` and the stamp is closed only after the wrap-up's turn or `WRAPUP_GRACE`.

**Done when:** After a Wind down — the person's or a manager's, with or without a live manager — every member that settles idle, clean and pushed is closed by the tick within a tick of settling, declared or not, its card reading *closed by the tick · after <presser>'s Wind down*; once nobody is live the team reads *wound down* where every member declared and otherwise stopped by the person where the person pressed it and `other` where a session did; one with unpushed work is left open and closed once it reads pushed; Wind down's help says the home closes each member; the tests in the Fix pass.
