## TD-1898: The home closes each member a Wind down reached once it settles

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** bcb32db9bc8340efa9ff1436f7d3d8fb

**Why:** A person's Wind down leaves its members open where no manager is live or a member never declared (TD-1895): the page's press closes nobody, and rule 9 closes nobody while one member is undeclared. The design (TD-1895) has the home close each member a Wind down reached once it settles.

**Ask:** The wind-down's close design ran an eighth review round and dropped the manager's mark; should a session run one more round?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (stop_members, _stop_one, _close_settled, _holder_words); src/sessionorc/agent.py (rpc_send's stamp, mark_wind_down; the _finished_owed dict); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_finished_team, _finished_view, _finished_close, _finished_tell, _idle_nudge, _context_line, _cadence_line, _held_line, _lane_news, _lost_brief, _brief_restart, _crashed, _extra_hold, _wanted_restart); src/sessionorc/work.py (_how_stopped, _last_word, stopped_by, seats_held_by, team_stopped, held_for, held_words, finished, finished_alone); the callers passing started_at: src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py, src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py, src/sessionorc/agent_serve.py, src/sessionorc/stuck.py (team_reason); src/sessionorc/models.py (the host-side field list, the view); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (tick_ready), src/sessionorc/agent_scale.py, src/sessionorc/seat_send.py (the winding predicate); the RPC dispatcher, the node route tables (`agent_common.NODE_ACTS`, `modes.NODE_ACTS`); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py's `_mark_team_start` (`teams.<team>.started_at`); ao's client (mark_wind_down); src/sessionorc/agent_wake.py (the doorbell's hold); src/sessionorc/mail.py (`ACTING_RPCS`; the stop-time line, only where it reads the predicate's fields); src/shiftlead/cli.py (container --wind-down); src/shiftlead/ending.py (closer_words and exit_words, their read of `wind_down.by`); src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (api_team_stop's toast count); src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; tests/test_cli_teams.py; design §4.9a, §9 (the `ACTING_RPCS` list), §4.9, §4.10, §6 rules 8 and 9, §4.5a help

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *A wind-down closes its members* and §6 rule 9 *A wind-down's close* (TD-1895):

1. **The mark.** A record field `wind_down: {at, by, waits}`, on the host's side beside `wrapup_at` (§4.4a), carried in the record's view. It rides the wrap-up send: `rpc_send(wrapup=True, wind_down=True)` stamps it once `_submit` returns (`wrapup_at` is stamped before it), so a refused or failed send leaves no mark, `waits` true (a `_submit` failure after `wrapup_at`'s stamp leaves that stamp — the send waits on no turn, so it never stalls — so the member reads `winding`, but no mark: it is never closed by this road); a member refused is named *refused: <why>* in the press's result and its toast (`ui/app.py`'s `api_team_stop` counts in *wrap-up sent to N sessions* only the members the wrap-up reached and adds *, m finished and marked* for those marked with nothing sent and *, n refused: <name> (<why>)*; a member killed because its brief was not in is reported *killed (brief not in)* by `_stop_one`, never *wrap-up sent*, and named apart; the text `teamAct` toasts; Stop now's toast takes the same tail for its refusals; `ao team stop` already prints each entry's action) and left to a second press or `--close`; `teamrun._stop_one` passes `wind_down=True` for a member. A finished member that `stop_members` sends nothing (declared and settled, with unread mail or without) is marked by a new `mark_wind_down` RPC under the same caller check as `send` (it joins `mail.ACTING_RPCS`, so `act_gate` checks it; it and `send(wind_down=True)`'s stamp refuse a record with `seat`; outside that set a session's call would pass unchecked; a session marking its own record passes, as its own send does, and is harmless: the tick closes only it once it settles, `by` its own id), `waits` false; it is no longer *left idle for the doorbell*: its line reads *out of work, n unread — marked, closed once it settles, mail kept*, or *finished (out of work), marked* with none (§4.9; `tests/test_cli_teams.py`'s *left idle for the doorbell* case changes with it). `by` is read by `closer_of`, as `wrapup_by` is: `person` for the page and for `ao` outside a session. Only an unattended record is marked; never a `seat` record or a person's session; a sub-lead is marked as a member. The manager (the record `work.manager_of` names, and `split`'s lead where that differs) is never marked: `stop_lead` sends it the wrap-up as today (`wrapup_by` the presser), a manager's own `ao team stop` sends itself nothing (`_own_end`), and `mark_wind_down` has no `lead` parameter; a `wind_down` on the manager's record (only a session's `mark_wind_down` on that record, its own or one it controls, makes one) is not a member's mark and is inert (item 2). A restart of the manager (`_replay` under any `why`, `rpc_restart`) makes a new record with no `wrapup_by`, as today, and no `_replay` or `rpc_restart` change is made; the team's word still reads the person's from its members' marks (item 4). Callers: the page's stop route, `ao team stop`, a manager's own `ao team stop`, the container `--wind-down` (`cli.py`). Register the field on the host's side in `models.py` beside `wrapup_at`, carry it in the view, forward `wind_down=True` on a node's send (`_route_act` passes the params whole), route `mark_wind_down` to a node's member by adding it to `agent_common.NODE_ACTS` (the table `agent_remote` and `agent_link` route by) and to `modes.NODE_ACTS` (which feeds `PERSON_NODE_BOUND`, a person's request bound to a node's records, and `offline_refusal`), as `send` is in both, and add `mark_wind_down` to the dispatcher and `ao`'s client. A person's send (`mail.as_person`) to a marked record moves `at`, stamped after `_submit` as the mark is, and sets `waits` true, and, being no wrap-up, clears `wrapup_at` and `wrapup_by` as today (so `work.py`'s person-wrap-up reading no longer sees it; the mark's `by` carries it); a session's controller `send` that is not a wrap-up clears it (and `wrapup_at` with it, as today), making the member an ordinary member again — a manager's *also push X* to a sub-lead mid-wind-down among them; a session's send to its own record (`act_gate` allows it) never touches the mark, though it clears `wrapup_at` as today; mail (`ao msg`, lane news) never touches it; a plain `wrapup=True` send with no `wind_down` (a card's Wrap up) moves `at` where it is the person's and otherwise leaves the mark; the home's own sends never touch it. A second Wind down keeps a `by` of `person`, a person's press on a session's mark takes `by` as `person`, and `at` moves only where a new wrap-up is sent.
2. **The close.** First in `agent_tick._finished_team` (rule 9's pass, at the home), before the `finished_sent_at` branch and `_finished_owed`, the team's members and views read again after it: every live unattended record with a member's mark (a `wind_down` on a record that is not the one `work.manager_of` names; a `wind_down` on the manager's record is inert, passed over here and by `finished`'s clause), not a seat, idle with no live subagent and no live shell, and settled — `waits` false; or `turn_at` > `wind_down.at` and idle for `SEND_STALL_SECONDS` measured on the home's clock from the first tick that read it idle after that turn (kept in memory, as `_finished_first` is, and started again by any tick that reads it not idle; a member's own turn ending between `_submit` and the stamp reads `turn_at` before `at` and only waits for the wrap-up's turn or `WRAPUP_GRACE`); or `WRAPUP_GRACE` past the first tick that read the mark (or its moved `at`), on the home's clock, kept in memory beside the idle window — is closed through `_finished_close(s, why="wind_down")`, whatever `work.finished` reads. One left open (work left, git unknown, link down, on a dialog) is tried again each tick, never killed; an `exited` marked record is not this close's and no rule restarts it (item 3). No `FINISHED_SETTLE`, nothing taken back. `_finished_owed` is not written, and `_finished_tell` writes nothing, for a team where any member carries a mark whose `by` is `person`, and `_finished_owed` is never written for a team with a marked member, so the wound-down note is the one path. Where only a session pressed it, the note is written once, on the tick whose `wind_down` close leaves the team with no live marked member and nobody live in its crew: `_finished_tell` gains a wound-down form (its words §4.9a's: *<team> was wound down by <presser>, and the host agent closed its members once each settled. Nothing is asked of you.*, then the PRs and each member's `out_of_work.why` or *did not declare*), its `since` the earliest `wind_down.at` among the members' marks; a last marked member closed by another hand (`--close`, a person's Close) gets none. While a marked member is live, `work.finished` carries its clause, so a live manager is neither told nor closed. A manager the reading then finds alone gets the line *your team was wound down by <presser>; its members are closed* in place of *every member has declared*, after `FINISHED_SETTLE` from the last member's close, and is closed `WRAPUP_GRACE` after it. The manager's half (the `finished_sent_at` branch's member-at-work check) passes over a marked member. Every team-level read of a mark here — no note where the person pressed, `_finished_owed`, the note's `since` — counts this run's marks only (§4.9a *A mark counts for its run only*): `_mark_team_start` writes `teams.<team>.started_at`, its own instant, on the home's host record beside `usage_at_start` each time it counts a start (its existing test unchanged: no unattended record of the team running but a seat, so a member-wise replay, `write_extras`' Apply and `set_max` calls and the later creates of one start never count), and a member's mark counts where its record is live, or its end (`work.record_end`) is at or after `started_at` or is none, every mark where the host record has none (a node's end on its own clock against the home's `started_at`: skew accepted); `_finished_team` holds the host record, so no record field, create param or view changes. Nothing is cleared or stamped on a record, so a node's record needs no call and an earlier run's card keeps its words.
3. **One predicate.** `winding(s)`: `wrapup_at`, `wrapup_sent_at` or a `wind_down` set. Every pass that now holds off a record on `wrapup_at` reads it: the doorbell's hold (`agent_wake.py`; `mail.py`'s stop-time line builds note text and changes only if it reads the predicate's fields), `_idle_nudge`, `_context_line`, `_cadence_line`, `_held_line`, `_lane_news`, `_lost_brief` (rule 1's lost-brief replay), `_brief_restart` (rule 7's changed-brief restart), `_crashed` (rule 1) and `_extra_hold` (`agent_tick.py`), the cache restart's `tick_ready` (`agent_common.py`), `agent_scale`'s fill hold (which reads `wrapup_at` alone today and widens to all three) and `seat_send`'s *stopping*. `_wanted_restart` (rule 2) holds off no wrap-up today and gains the predicate, so neither rule 1 nor rule 2 restarts a marked record. The *winding down* clause sits in `finished`'s member branch only, after the manager and seat `continue` and before the waiting, held-ask and declaration tests, and reads only a member's mark (never the manager's record), and a marked member reads *winding down* in their place. `work.finished` passes over an **ended** (`closed` or `exited`, by state) record carrying a member's mark, as it passes over a sat-out one, and reads a **live** one as a clause *<name> winding down*; `_finished_view` carries the field.
4. **The stop reading.** `work._how_stopped` reads a closer whose `by` is `tick` and whose `why` is `wind_down` or `finished` as `person` when the record's `wind_down.by` or `wrapup_by`, as it stands, is `person` (a member's `wind_down` close, a manager's rule 9 close after the person's wrap-up), and a closer whose `by` is the record's own id (a manager closing itself) the same way; for a record that ends naming nobody (its own exit), where its `wrapup_by` is not `person` and its `wind_down.by` is, `mine` is `wind_down.at` (the stamp a person's later send leaves when it clears `wrapup_at`), and the existing comparison of `mine` with `wrapup_sent_at`'s clock then runs as today, so a stop-time clock before the person's mark still reads `person`; `ending.exit_words` takes `wrap = wrapup_at or wind_down.at`, the latter only from a member's mark, for its *· after wrap-up*; the manager, never marked, reads by its `wrapup_by` as today, so `_how_stopped` keeps its one-record signature; an unmarked record is left as today (§6 rule 8 *A team that stopped*). The team's reading: `work._last_word`, where every end in its `FINISHED_SETTLE` window is the record `work.manager_of` names, also reads `person` where any current record carries a member's mark whose `by` is `person` and which counts for the run (item 2's test: live, or ended at or after `started_at`, or no end); a window whose newest end is a member's reads as today. `_last_word` and every reader on it take the team's `started_at` (None, counting every mark, only where the host record has none): `stopped_by`, `seats_held_by`, `team_stopped`, `held_for`, `held_words` and `stuck.team_reason`, and every caller at the home passes it from the host record's `teams.<team>.started_at` — `agent_tick`'s (rule 3's fill hold and `stop_held`, rule 8's start and its team readings), `agent_mail`'s (`held_for`, `held_words`), `agent_inbox`'s, `agent_serve`'s and `stuck`'s — so the tick, the mail, the Inbox and the work orders read one word; the page's `ui/cards.py` `_held_words` reads the stored `stop_held` and changes nothing. The manager's rule 9 close falls `FINISHED_SETTLE` and `WRAPUP_GRACE` after its last member's, so without it a manager with no `wrapup_by` (restarted, its wrap-up refused) decides the team's word alone and the seats are not held.
5. **The member-wise reading.** `work.finished_alone` (rule 8 *A member that finished while its team runs on*) leaves out a record whose closer's `why` is `wind_down`.
6. **`--close`.** `teamrun._close_settled` reports a member it finds closed with closer `why: wind_down` as closed (*closed by the tick after the wind-down*).
7. **The card's words.** `ending.closer_words` special-cases a tick closer whose `why` is `wind_down`: it reads the presser from the record's `wind_down.by` — *after your Wind down* for the person, *after <name>'s Wind down* for a session (its name where `names` knows the id) — and *after a Wind down* where the mark is missing or malformed (§4.5 row 5 (b)); `TICK_WHY` alone cannot, since it reads only `closer.why`.
8. **The help text.** Wind down's help (`ui/help.py`, §4.5a's help list) reads: *Sends the wrap-up to every member and then to the manager: each finishes what it holds and pushes, and the home closes each member once it settles; one on a dialog stays open, in the Inbox. Press it when the team should stop after the work in hand, not in the middle of it. It kills nothing — Stop now does — but for a member whose brief is not in yet, which it stops, and forgets nothing.* — §4.5a's help list changed with it in the same PR.
