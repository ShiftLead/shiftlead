## TD-1898: The home closes each member a Wind down reached once it settles

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** bcb32db9bc8340efa9ff1436f7d3d8fb
**Part of:** TD-1895

**Why:** A person's Wind down leaves its members open where no manager is live or a member never declared (TD-1895): the page's press closes nobody, and rule 9 closes nobody while one member is undeclared. The design (TD-1895) has the home close each member a Wind down reached once it settles.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (stop_members, _stop_one, _close_settled); src/sessionorc/agent.py (rpc_send's stamp, mark_wind_down); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_finished_team, _finished_close, _finished_tell, _finished_owed, _wanted_restart, rule 7's pass); src/sessionorc/work.py (_how_stopped, finished_alone); src/sessionorc/models.py; src/sessionorc/mail.py, src/sessionorc/agent_wake.py (the doorbell's hold); src/shiftlead/cli.py (container --wind-down); src/shiftlead/ending.py; src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; design §4.9a, §6 rules 8 and 9, §4.5a help

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *A wind-down closes its members* and §6 rule 9 *A wind-down's close* (TD-1895):

1. **The mark.** A record field `wind_down: {at, by, waits}`, on the host's side beside `wrapup_at` (§4.4a), carried in the record's view. It rides the wrap-up send: `rpc_send(wrapup=True, wind_down=True)` stamps it where it stamps `wrapup_at` and `wrapup_by`, after the send's refusals (a refused send leaves no mark), `waits` true; `teamrun._stop_one` passes `wind_down=True` for a member. A finished member that `stop_members` sends nothing (declared and settled, with unread mail or without) is marked by a new `mark_wind_down` RPC under the same caller check as `send`, `waits` false; it is no longer *left idle for the doorbell*. `by` is read from the caller as `closer` is. Never on the manager (`split`'s lead), a `seat` record or a person's session; a sub-lead is marked as a member. Callers: the page's stop route, `ao team stop`, a manager's own `ao team stop`, the container `--wind-down` (`cli.py`). The mark holds the doorbell as `wrapup_at` does (`mail.py`, `agent_wake.py`). A person's send to a marked record moves `at` to now and sets `waits` true; a session's non-wrap-up send clears it; the home's own sends never touch it. A second Wind down keeps a `by` of `person` and moves `at` only where it sends a new wrap-up.
2. **The close.** First in `agent_tick._finished_team` (rule 9's pass, at the home), before the `finished_sent_at` branch and `_finished_owed`, the team's members and views read again after it: every live unattended record with `wind_down`, not a seat, not the team's manager (`work.manager_of`), idle with no live subagent and no live shell, and settled — `waits` false; or `turn_at` > `wind_down.at` and idle since that turn for `SEND_STALL_SECONDS`; or `WRAPUP_GRACE` past `wind_down.at` — is closed through `_finished_close(s, why="wind_down")`, whatever `work.finished` reads. One left open (work left, git unknown, link down, on a dialog) is tried again each tick, never killed; an `exited` marked record is not this close's. No `FINISHED_SETTLE`, nothing taken back. `_finished_owed` is not written, and `_finished_tell` writes nothing, for a team whose members carry a mark by the person. A manager the reading then finds alone gets the line *your team was wound down by <presser>; its members are closed* in place of *every member has declared*.
3. **Rules 2 and 7.** `_wanted_restart` (rule 2) and rule 7's brief pass pass over a record carrying `wind_down`.
4. **The stop reading.** `work._how_stopped` reads a closer whose `by` is `tick` as `person` when the record's `wind_down.by` or `wrapup_by` is `person` (a member's `wind_down` close, a manager's rule 9 close after the person's wrap-up), else None (§6 rule 8 *A team that stopped*).
5. **The member-wise reading.** `work.finished_alone` (rule 8 *A member that finished while its team runs on*) leaves out a record whose closer's `why` is `wind_down`.
6. **`--close`.** `teamrun._close_settled` reports a member it finds closed with closer `why: wind_down` as closed (*closed by the tick after the wind-down*).
7. **The card's words.** `ending.TICK_WHY` gains `wind_down` → *after <presser>'s Wind down*, *your* for the person, the session's name otherwise (§4.5 row 5 (b)).
8. **The help text.** Wind down's help (`ui/help.py`, §4.5a's help list) reads: *Sends the wrap-up to every member and then to the manager: each finishes what it holds and pushes, and the home closes it once it settles. Press it when the team should stop after the work in hand, not in the middle of it. It kills nothing — Stop now does — but for a member whose brief is not in yet, which it stops, and forgets nothing.* — §4.5a's help list changed with it in the same PR.
9. **Tests.** ao-grind's case: a team with its manager closed, two declared idle members and one idle undeclared member, each marked by the person's Wind down, all clean and pushed: after one tick all three are closed `{by: tick, why: wind_down}` and the team's stop reads `person`. A live manager: its members closed, the manager closed by rule 9 after the line, the team's stop `person`. A member with unpushed work stays open and is closed the tick it reads pushed. A member `working` at the press whose own turn ends is not closed before the wrap-up turn has run (idle for less than `SEND_STALL_SECONDS` after a turn); one sent the wrap-up and idle from before it is not closed until a turn ends or `WRAPUP_GRACE` passes. A refused send leaves no mark. A waiting member (an open `ask` about a reference) is closed, its question left orphaned. A node member waits for its link. A seat and the manager are never marked. A second press by a session keeps the person's `by`. A person's send moves the mark; a session's plain send clears it; a doorbell does not touch it. A marked finished member is not rung. A member without the mark beside an undeclared one is not closed (rule 9 unchanged). A member closed `why: wind_down` is not replayed by rule 8's member-wise news.

**Done when:** After a Wind down — the person's or a manager's, with or without a live manager — every member that settles idle, clean and pushed is closed by the tick within a tick of settling, declared or not, its card reading *closed by the tick · after <presser>'s Wind down*; once nobody is live the team reads *wound down* where every member declared and stopped by the presser otherwise; one with unpushed work is left open and closed once it reads pushed; Wind down's help says the home closes each member; the tests in the Fix pass.
