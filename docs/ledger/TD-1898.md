## TD-1898: The home closes each member a Wind down reached once it settles

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** bcb32db9bc8340efa9ff1436f7d3d8fb
**Part of:** TD-1895

**Why:** A person's Wind down leaves its members open where no manager is live or a member never declared (TD-1895): the page's press closes nobody, and rule 9 closes nobody while one member is undeclared. The design (TD-1895) has the home close each member a Wind down reached once it settles.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (stop_members, _stop_one, _close_settled); src/sessionorc/agent.py (rpc_send's stamp, mark_wind_down); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_finished_team, _finished_view, _finished_close, _idle_nudge, _context_line, _cadence_line, _held_line, _lane_news, _brief_restart, _finished_tell, _finished_owed, _wanted_restart, rule 7's pass); src/sessionorc/work.py (_how_stopped, finished, finished_alone); src/sessionorc/models.py (the host-side field list, the view); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py, src/sessionorc/agent_scale.py, src/sessionorc/seat_send.py (the winding predicate); the RPC dispatcher, the node route table and ao's client (mark_wind_down); src/sessionorc/mail.py, src/sessionorc/agent_wake.py (the doorbell's hold); src/shiftlead/cli.py (container --wind-down); src/shiftlead/ending.py; src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; tests/test_cli_teams.py; design §4.9a, §4.9, §4.10, §6 rules 8 and 9, §4.5a help

**Fix:** Build design §4.9a *A wind-down closes its members* and §6 rule 9 *A wind-down's close* (TD-1895):

1. **The mark.** A record field `wind_down: {at, by, waits}`, on the host's side beside `wrapup_at` (§4.4a), carried in the record's view. It rides the wrap-up send: `rpc_send(wrapup=True, wind_down=True)` stamps it once `_submit` returns (`wrapup_at` is stamped before it), so a refused or failed send leaves no mark, `waits` true; a member refused is named *refused: <why>* in the press's result and left to a second press or `--close`; `teamrun._stop_one` passes `wind_down=True` for a member. A finished member that `stop_members` sends nothing (declared and settled, with unread mail or without) is marked by a new `mark_wind_down` RPC under the same caller check as `send`, `waits` false; it is no longer *left idle for the doorbell*: its line reads *out of work, n unread — marked, closed once it settles, mail kept*, or *finished (out of work), marked* with none (§4.9; `tests/test_cli_teams.py`'s *left idle for the doorbell* case changes with it). `by` is read from the caller as `closer` is. Never on the manager (`split`'s lead, nor the record `work.manager_of` names), a `seat` record or a person's session; a sub-lead is marked as a member. Callers: the page's stop route, `ao team stop`, a manager's own `ao team stop`, the container `--wind-down` (`cli.py`). Register the field on the host's side in `models.py` beside `wrapup_at`, carry it in the view, forward `wind_down=True` on a node's send in the route table, and add `mark_wind_down` to the dispatcher and `ao`'s client. A person's send to a marked record moves `at` to now and sets `waits` true; a session's non-wrap-up send clears it; the home's own sends never touch it. A second Wind down keeps a `by` of `person` and moves `at` only where it sends a new wrap-up.
2. **The close.** First in `agent_tick._finished_team` (rule 9's pass, at the home), before the `finished_sent_at` branch and `_finished_owed`, the team's members and views read again after it: every live unattended record with `wind_down`, not a seat, not the team's manager (`work.manager_of`), idle with no live subagent and no live shell, and settled — `waits` false; or `turn_at` > `wind_down.at` and idle since that turn for `SEND_STALL_SECONDS`; or `WRAPUP_GRACE` past `wind_down.at` — is closed through `_finished_close(s, why="wind_down")`, whatever `work.finished` reads. One left open (work left, git unknown, link down, on a dialog) is tried again each tick, never killed; an `exited` marked record is not this close's. No `FINISHED_SETTLE`, nothing taken back. `_finished_owed` is not written, and `_finished_tell` writes nothing, for a team whose members carry a mark by the person; where the mark is a session's, the note is written on the tick that closes the team's last live marked member, `since` the earliest mark, naming the presser. A manager the reading then finds alone gets the line *your team was wound down by <presser>; its members are closed* in place of *every member has declared*, after `FINISHED_SETTLE` from the last member's close, and is closed `WRAPUP_GRACE` after it.
3. **One predicate.** `winding(s)`: `wrapup_at`, `wrapup_sent_at` or `wind_down` set. Every pass that now holds off a record on `wrapup_at` reads it: the doorbell (`mail.py`, `agent_wake.py`), `_idle_nudge`, `_context_line`, `_cadence_line`, `_held_line`, `_lane_news`, `_brief_restart` and `_wanted_restart` (`agent_tick.py`), the cache-restart precondition (`agent_common.py`), `agent_scale`'s fill hold and `seat_send`'s *stopping*. `work.finished` passes over a record carrying `wind_down`, live or ended, as it passes over a sat-out one (`_finished_view` carries the field).
4. **The stop reading.** `work._how_stopped` reads a closer whose `by` is `tick` as `person` when the record's `wind_down.by` or `wrapup_by` is `person` (a member's `wind_down` close, a manager's rule 9 close after the person's wrap-up), and a closer whose `by` is the record's own id (a manager closing itself) the same way, else None (§6 rule 8 *A team that stopped*).
5. **The member-wise reading.** `work.finished_alone` (rule 8 *A member that finished while its team runs on*) leaves out a record whose closer's `why` is `wind_down`.
6. **`--close`.** `teamrun._close_settled` reports a member it finds closed with closer `why: wind_down` as closed (*closed by the tick after the wind-down*).
7. **The card's words.** `ending.TICK_WHY` gains `wind_down` → *after <presser>'s Wind down*, *your* for the person, the session's name otherwise (§4.5 row 5 (b)).
8. **The help text.** Wind down's help (`ui/help.py`, §4.5a's help list) reads: *Sends the wrap-up to every member and then to the manager: each finishes what it holds and pushes, and the home closes it once it settles. Press it when the team should stop after the work in hand, not in the middle of it. It kills nothing — Stop now does — but for a member whose brief is not in yet, which it stops, and forgets nothing.* — §4.5a's help list changed with it in the same PR.
9. **Tests.** ao-grind's case: a team with its manager closed, two declared idle members and one idle undeclared member, each marked by the person's Wind down, all clean and pushed: after one tick all three are closed `{by: tick, why: wind_down}` and the team's stop reads `person`. A live manager: its members closed, the manager closed by rule 9 after the line, the team's stop `person`. A member with unpushed work stays open and is closed the tick it reads pushed. A member `working` at the press whose own turn ends is not closed before the wrap-up turn has run (idle for less than `SEND_STALL_SECONDS` after a turn); one sent the wrap-up and idle from before it is not closed until a turn ends or `WRAPUP_GRACE` passes. A refused send leaves no mark. A waiting member (an open `ask` about a reference) is closed, its question left orphaned. A node member waits for its link. A seat and the manager are never marked. A second press by a session keeps the person's `by`. A person's send moves the mark; a session's plain send clears it; a doorbell does not touch it. A marked finished member is not rung. A member without the mark beside an undeclared one is not closed (rule 9 unchanged). A member closed `why: wind_down` is not replayed by rule 8's member-wise news. A marked member with `restart_wanted`, and a marked crashed one, do not block the reading, and are not restarted. The idle nudge and the lane news skip a marked record whose `wrapup_at` a person's send cleared. A `_submit` failure leaves no mark. A session-pressed, manager-less team gets its note on the tick its last marked member closes, and reads `other`. A manager that closes itself after the person's wrap-up reads `person`. The `--close` race: the first close stands and `_close_settled` reports it. A sub-lead under a manager → lead → worker team is marked and closed. An `exited` marked record is left alone. A member on a dialog is left open and named. The container `--wind-down` marks its members. A node member's mark is stamped on the node and read from the view.

**Done when:** After a Wind down — the person's or a manager's, with or without a live manager — every member that settles idle, clean and pushed is closed by the tick within a tick of settling, declared or not, its card reading *closed by the tick · after <presser>'s Wind down*; once nobody is live the team reads *wound down* where every member declared and otherwise stopped by the person where the person pressed it and `other` where a session did; one with unpushed work is left open and closed once it reads pushed; Wind down's help says the home closes each member; the tests in the Fix pass.
