**Fix:** - **The edge** (design §4.10 *The message gate*, *an answer, to the `ask` that reached it*). In `message_edge` (`src/sessionorc/mail.py`), the last branch (`kind == "reply"`, `replied` an `ask` from the sender's own inbox, `target` on the asker's `superseded_by` chain) drops `asks_seat(me)` and returns its own name, `"asked"` — no caller keys the reply on `"question seat"` (the only reader, `edge == "question seat" and kind == "ask"` ~590, is the ask's). - Nothing else widens: a `reply` on any thread but an `ask` in the sender's own inbox, and every other kind, between unrelated projects, is still refused; depth and pair bounds hold.
- Tests: a reply from a record with no seat, on the thread of an `ask` from another project's record in its inbox, is accepted as `asked` (and to a resumed asker on its chain; and from a record the `ask` was copied to, as `--reply-to` resolves a copy); the same reply on a `note`'s thread, or to a record off the chain, is refused; a seat's reply passes as `asked` too, and the tests that asserted `"question seat"` for a reply are updated.
