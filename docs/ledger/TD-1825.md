## TD-1825: A board line with Context: none, or an archived Context, is drawn nowhere

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-13 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger's ask m-5c88a7dd2d53; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 1552fb7103754389b5ca7c9db83ab975

**Why:** Four `act` lines that only the person could do, each due within a day, were drawn nowhere in the UI: not in the Inbox, not on any Queue card (`ao card` for the entries they serve, TD-1637, TD-1550 and TD-1648, holds none of them). Paul could not see them or press **Done**, and learned of them only through a session that told him (reported 2026-11-12 and again on 2026-11-14 by the ledgerboard session ao-led-tds-ledger, ask m-5c88a7dd2d53, at Paul's request). Three are shiftlead's own board lines with `Context: none`: *Sign in Grok Build's two homes…* and *Log in again on the conformance profile…* (2026-11-12), and *Turn off the grok.com hosted connector Automations…* (2026-11-13). The fourth is ledgerboard's *Release v2.8 … or fold it…*, whose `Context:` is TD-067, archived in ledgerboard. Lines whose `Context:` names an open entry do show.

The design already says where each goes. §4.5 screen 6 *One place for each thing that needs the person*: "An `act` or `do` line that names no entry and no PR" has the Inbox as its primary place, "the person's to-do list". *Where a row is drawn* (TD-1400): a line none of whose refs an open card holds "stays in the Inbox — unless it needs the person, when it draws on a kept or a loose card", and *Every item that needs the person draws on a card* "whatever that entry's state". So `Context: none` belongs in the Inbox, and an archived `Context:` on the kept or loose card of that entry. The build does neither: such a line falls between the Inbox's `home: inbox` rows and the Queue's cards. The harm is that a person-only step with a due date silently never reaches the person, which is the one thing the board exists to prevent.

**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`board_rows`, `inbox_sections`, `horizon_left`), `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`item_home`, the kept and loose cards); design §4.5 screen 6 *Where a row is drawn* and *One place for each thing that needs the person*.

**Fix:** Find where these lines are dropped. Likely suspects: a `Context: none` read as a ref, or a line removed from the Inbox's horizon (`horizon_left`) because a card is expected to draw it while no card does. Then make the build follow the design. A board line that names no entry and no PR is a `home: inbox` row in the Inbox. A line whose `Context:` names an entry its repo holds only archived, and that needs the person, draws on that entry's kept card, else a loose card, as *Every item that needs the person draws on a card* says. Either way it is drawn exactly once and counted in exactly one tab. Add tests that build a board with each shape (`Context: none`; `Context: TD-n` where TD-n is archived) and check where the line is drawn, so that it can be pressed **Done**.

**Done when:** An open, due `act` line with `Context: none` is drawn in the Inbox, counted in the Inbox tab, with **Done**. One whose `Context:` names an archived entry is drawn on a kept or loose Queue card, counted in the Queue tab. A test holds each, and each fails if the line is dropped.
