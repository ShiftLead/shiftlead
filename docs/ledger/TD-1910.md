## TD-1910: The director seat only answers for the person: it should also work for them, as a chief of staff

**Priority:** High
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-16 (Paul, approved in an interactive session; drafted by ao-led-tds-ledger, filed by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** b2f73e8893ab449184563349be2e9ad3

**Why:** The planned director (TD-636, TD-657) answers permission prompts and techleads' questions in the person's place. During the ledgerboard v3 migration, an interactive session did a wider job for 16 days: it watched every repo, put decisions to the person with a recommendation, carried out their answers, merged within their merge line, and above all found gaps and filed them. Paul, 2026-11-16, approved this entry (*"file"*): he wants that role in the director while the Queue and Inbox mature, and expects it to shrink as cards improve.

Evidence from that session: about 175 recommendations, of which 41 (23%) differed from the card's own; the person followed the session in 31. Almost all of those were on design-round endings, which TD-1911 and agent-cadence's round-ending guidance move to the techlead. Most of the gaps it raised were approved. Its errors (stale status lists, two misdiagnoses) were caught by the person or an independent review. Full draft with evidence: https://claude.ai/artifact/K1mUpgqKkXZKmyEx3g6ZvH (entry 2).

**Design PR:** #4083

**Location:** design §4.9b (the director seat, TD-636; passing up, TD-657), §4.5 and §4.5a (Queue and Inbox filters), §4.8 (grants); src/shiftlead/briefs/

**Fix:** Design first, widening TD-636's and TD-657's director. What the director does:
- watches every repo's board, ledger, PRs and seats from the home's own events (tick, mail), not a session-bound script, and checks against the live board mechanically before it lists anything;
- records what it tells the person as Queue items (decide for answers, fyi for reports, watch for what it waits on), so nothing lives only in chat;
- has a Queue and Inbox filter that works like the team filters (on shows, off hides, combines with them);
- acts for the person by delegation, recorded as made for them (ledgerboard TD-132): merges within the person's merge line first (cleanup, docs and self-contained fixes at SHIP and green), more kinds as preferences grow;
- has a review setting for its delegated decisions: none (default; recorded, nothing asks), digest (one summary card with Dismiss all), or each;
- raises a *file this?* card for each gap, with evidence and an independent review's verdict, and on approval files it in the right repo through a filing path that does not queue behind other questions;
- raises a card-quality gap when it has to explain a card interactively;
- can brief the person in an interactive session, with state on the cards and discussion in chat;
- directs other seats on the person's behalf within its delegation;
- never starts or stops sessions or teams, deploys, edits the person's paths, changes an answer the person gave, or approves its own gap.

Later idea, not work: show more session dialogue in the Prompts panel.

Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director it widens), TD-1911 (round endings move to the techlead), ledgerboard TD-132 (delegated answers).
