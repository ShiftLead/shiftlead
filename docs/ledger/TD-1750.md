## TD-1750: A hook-limited session loses its lift marks at a host-agent restart and lifts to working on a stale reading

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-13
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** f57bc1e283f8422bb5af5ce55ab19980

**Why:** TD-1748 (#3642) keeps two maps in memory only: `_pre_limited`, which a hook's `limited` sets to `idle`, and `_hook_limited`, when that `limited` landed. After a host-agent restart (a promote), a session whose turn ended on a `rate_limit` `StopFailure` still reads `limited` (`hook`), but both marks are gone. The first usage reading with no cap then lifts it, even one fetched before the cap, and it lands on `_pre_limited`'s default `working`, not `idle`. Design §4.2's row says the lift is never to `working`. The poll's own `limited` already loses `_pre_limited` the same way. Found by the techlead's read of #3642.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_usage_limits, _hook_lift_due); design §4.2

**Fix:** Keep what the lift needs across a restart. Either persist both marks (on the record, or in the host agent's saved state), or have the lift read the record itself: a hook-confirmed `limited` whose pending is the adapter's turn-ended limit lifts to `idle`, and only on a reading fetched after its `since`. Write the choice in design §4.2's `rate_limit` row. Add a test that clears both maps (as a restart does) and then shows the lift waits for a later reading and lands on `idle`.

**Done when:** After a host-agent restart, a hook-limited session is lifted only by a reading fetched after its `limited`, and lands on `idle`. A test holds it.
