## TD-2069: A session whose login runs out moves to the next login, handed over from its transcript

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 79a85486e9bd41daabf617ee34fd3eff
**Blocked by:** TD-2068, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1976

**Why:** A session whose login reaches its reserve or a limit is to carry on, on the next login of its list, from where it stopped (the frame, A stopped seat moves; design 4.9e, A session moves down the list). Nothing moves a session today, and no adapter reads a transcript into a handoff.

**Ask:** Should the builders build a session's move to the next login and the handoff from its transcript?

**Fix:** Build design 4.9e A session moves down the list and The handoff is one adapter method. The tick of the host a session runs on, for each unattended session with a seat_model whose login is no longer usable, finds the next usable login of its list that offers the model, reads the handoff text, ends the old session and launches the new one under the same record, told what its stage's session is told followed by the handoff text; the record's login and account are rewritten and the move is added to login_moves. It spends no offer, limit or cap; with no later login usable the session waits; an interactive session is never moved. The adapter contract gains the optional handoff(transcript), written into design 4.3's Protocol block, implemented for Claude Code, Gemini and Grok with a conformance row each; the core keeps the newest 24,000 characters, cut at a turn's start.

**Done when:** On a fixture: a stage's session whose login is marked limited is relaunched on the next login under the same record with the handoff text in what it is told, its stage record's holder and counts unchanged; with no later login it waits; an interactive session stays. Each of the three adapters returns a fixture transcript's turns as text, newest last, and an adapter without the method hands over nothing.
