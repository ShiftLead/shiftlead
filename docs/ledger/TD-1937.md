**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *A gap becomes a File this? question* and *A card it had to explain is a gap*, with §4.7 (`ao td add --approved`):
- The director's brief: before sending a gap, a fresh review of the claim against the repo by `fact-check-model`; a gap the review blocks is not sent; the `ask`, `--about <repo>`, names the gap, its evidence, the reviewer's verdict quoted and the repo, with *File it — the director files it in <repo>* (default) and *Not now — nothing is filed*; a card the person had to have explained is a gap against shiftlead naming the card and what it lacked.
- `ao td add --repo <repo> --approved <ask id> "<what, and why>"`: the director's alone; the host agent checks the ask was the director's and the person answered *File it*, then hands the entry to that repo's question seat as the person's Add entry with *Hand to the techlead* does (§4.10 *An entry handed to a seat*), marked `handed`, so it waits behind no other question and the seat owes the person its outcome; with no question seat, an `act` line on that repo's board. Refused otherwise, naming why. The director never answers its own ask (the person's answer is required by the check).
