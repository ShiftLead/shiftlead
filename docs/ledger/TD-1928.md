## TD-1928: A team's own page gathers its members and settings, and shows its definition

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 50cfefcc94e24ac29a937172566d110d
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** TD-1153's design (§4.9 *A team's own page*, §4.5 screen 14): a team's members and settings are on two surfaces today, the Members dialog on the team card and a card on the Settings page, and its definition is on none. The page gathers them at `/team/<team>`; this build makes the page with the two sections that move, and the Definition section's read-only view.

**Ask:** Do the grinders build the team page design now, or does it get one more review round first?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/team_page.py (new), src/shiftlead/ui/app.py, src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py, src/shiftlead/org.py; design §4.9 *A team's own page*, §4.5 screen 14, §4.5a *Team page*

**Fix:** 1. A route `GET /team/{name}` in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, built by a new `src/shiftlead/ui/team_page.py`: the header (name, repo, source with the checkout's branch where it is not the default, the card's state words, the flow chip, **‹ Sessions** to the team's group), then three sections with anchors `#members`, `#settings`, `#definition` (§4.5 screen 14). The org read as the Sessions page reads it (`org.with_repos`, then `with_settings`). A name the org does not define answers 404 listing the defined teams as links; a name in `with_repos`' `refused`, or whose repo file is skipped, answers that 404 with the refusal's words; a team `org.yml` defines over a repo's (`merge_repo_teams`' `shadowed`) is the org's page, its Definition naming the shadowed file; on a node the route refuses and names the home as the existing team routes do (§4.9 *A team's own page*).
2. **Members**: the Members dialog's content drawn in place, its routes unchanged (`/api/teams/{name}/members`, the size and max writes); on a repo's team, until TD-1930, **Add member** and **Remove** stay disabled with the dialog's reason and **Open file**, as the route's `editable`, `note` and `open_file` give them; the dialog itself goes, and the team card's **Members…** links to `/team/<team>#members` (§4.5a *Sessions: team card: Members…*, its pointer).
3. **Settings**: the Settings page's team card (§4.5a *Settings page: Teams*) drawn here, its fields, writes, refusals and Save unchanged; the Settings page's Teams section becomes one line per team (name, source, flow) linking to `/team/<team>`; the flow chip on the Sessions page lands on `/team/<team>#settings` in place of `/settings#team-<team>` (both links in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/group_head.html`).
4. **Definition**, read-only: the team's block of its file, in the mono face with line numbers, its source above it. A helper `org.team_block(text, team) -> tuple[int, int, str] | None` (first line, end line, the block's indent-stripped text) serves this and TD-1929. The block runs from the team's key under `teams:` to its last line that is neither blank nor a comment before the next key at that indent or less, comments inside it included; blank and comment lines between two teams belong to neither (`_quiet`'s reading); a team in flow style on one line is that line; the key matched by parsing it, so a quoted key is found. Where it answers `None` (`teams:` in flow style, a team by anchor or merge key, a key the parse finds and the lines do not hold) the section reads *<team>'s definition cannot be edited as text: edit the file by hand* with **Open file**, and TD-1929 draws no **Edit**; the page computes that link itself (`editor_file_link(source)`, absent under `none`) for an `org.yml` team as for a repo's, since the route's `open_file` is set only for a repo's team.
5. On a phone the sections stack (§4.5 screen 14).
6. Help text (`help.py`, bound to §4.5a's help list): the team card's Members… entry says it opens the team's page; the flow chip's says it opens the team's Settings section, in place of *Flow on Settings →*. The wording is the build's, the list's pointer row unchanged.
7. Tests: the route for an `org.yml` team and a repo team; the 404, a refused name, a shadowed team and the node's refusal; the block's bounds (a comment inside the block, a comment and blank lines before the next team, a following team, end of file, a flow-style one-line team, a quoted key, a flow-style `teams:` and an anchored team answering `None` and the section's words); the Settings page's line per team and its link; the card's Members… and flow chip links. The Members dialog's existing tests move to the page's Members section, asserting what they asserted, not rewritten.
8. Look at the page as design §4.9b says a page change is looked at: rendered in headless Chromium, the shot committed under `docs/mockups/reviews/` and named in the PR, showing the three sections with the parts the review shot `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-17-td1153-team-page.png` shows.

**Done when:** `/team/<team>` draws Members, Settings and a read-only Definition for an `org.yml` team and a repo team, and the 404, refused and shadowed cases as §4.9 says; the Members dialog and the Settings page's team cards are gone, their links landing on the page; the help text is reworded; the tests above pass, and the PR's shot shows the three sections the review shot shows.
