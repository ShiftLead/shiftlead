## TD-1928: A team's own page gathers its members and settings, and shows its definition

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 50cfefcc94e24ac29a937172566d110d
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** TD-1153's design (§4.9 *A team's own page*, §4.5 screen 14): a team's members and settings are on two surfaces today, the Members dialog on the team card and a card on the Settings page, and its definition is on none. The page gathers them at `/team/<team>`; this build makes the page with the two sections that move, and the Definition section's read-only view.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/team_page.py (new), src/shiftlead/ui/app.py, src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py, src/shiftlead/org.py; design §4.9 *A team's own page*, §4.5 screen 14, §4.5a *Team page*

**Fix:** 1. A route `GET /team/{name}` in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, built by a new `src/shiftlead/ui/team_page.py`: the header (name, repo, source with the checkout's branch where it is not the default, the card's state words, the flow chip, **‹ Sessions** to the team's group), then three sections with anchors `#members`, `#settings`, `#definition` (§4.5 screen 14). The org read as the Sessions page reads it (`org.with_repos`, then `with_settings`). A name the org does not define answers 404 listing the defined teams as links.
2. **Members**: the Members dialog's content drawn in place, its routes unchanged (`/api/teams/{name}/members`, the size and max writes); the dialog itself goes, and the team card's **Members…** links to `/team/<team>#members` (§4.5a *Sessions: team card: Members…*, its pointer).
3. **Settings**: the Settings page's team card (§4.5a *Settings page: Teams*) drawn here, its fields, writes, refusals and Save unchanged; the Settings page's Teams section becomes one line per team (name, source, flow) linking to `/team/<team>`; the flow chip on the Sessions page lands on `/team/<team>#settings` in place of `/settings#team-<team>`.
4. **Definition**, read-only: the team's block of its file (from its key under `teams:` to the line before the next key at that indent or less, comments inside it included), the key's indent taken off every line, in the mono face with line numbers, its source above it. A helper `org.team_block(text, team) -> tuple[int, int, str] | None` (first line, end line, the block's indent-stripped text) serves this and TD-1929.
5. On a phone the sections stack (§4.5 screen 14).
6. Tests: the route for an `org.yml` team and a repo team, the 404, the block's bounds (a comment inside the block, a following team, end of file, a team in flow style on one line), the Settings page's line per team and its link, the card's Members… and flow chip links; help text for the moved controls where `help.py` holds it.
7. Look at the page as design §4.9b says a page change is looked at: rendered in headless Chromium, the shot committed under `docs/mockups/reviews/` and named in the PR, compared with `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-17-td1153-team-page.png`.

**Done when:** `/team/<team>` draws Members, Settings and a read-only Definition for an `org.yml` team and a repo team; the Members dialog and the Settings page's team cards are gone, their links landing on the page; the tests above pass and the shot is in the PR.
