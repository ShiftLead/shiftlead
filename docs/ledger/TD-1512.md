## TD-1512: _mend_launch leaves a corrupt launch record unmended with nothing said

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3a836559c7c84445a8864c8756609ffe
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent.py (_mend_launch)

**Why:** `_mend_launch` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py`, TD-1455, #3142) wraps its read, set and write in `contextlib.suppress(OSError, ValueError)`, so a launch record that exists but cannot be read or parsed is left unmended with nothing said: `ao mode unattended` (`set_mode`'s mend) and a Resume's seat carry report success while the record keeps its old fields, and the next Resume reads them. A missing record is the designed quiet case (*a record with none writes none*); a corrupt or unreadable one is not. Raised by the techlead's read of #3142 (m-989b4e3f02e1, note a).

**Fix:** In `_mend_launch`, keep a missing launch record (`FileNotFoundError`) silent and log any other `OSError` or a `ValueError` (unparseable JSON) with the record's address at warning level, writing nothing. A test: a launch record holding invalid JSON is left as it was and a warning names the address.

**Done when:** A corrupt or unreadable launch record is reported in the host agent's log when `_mend_launch` meets it, a missing one is not, and a test holds both.
