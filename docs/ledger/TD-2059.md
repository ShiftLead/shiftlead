**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *Where the settings pages sit*, the head and *When it runs* of *The page*, and the *Before* case of *Before and after the cut-over*, with their §4.5a rows: the rail on Settings and Tools (and on Flows and Briefs where those pages exist), the gear lit on each; `/teams` and `/teams/<team>` with the team tabs; the head and the five running-settings rows, live on today's keys, Stops offering never; those rows taken off the Settings page's team card, which keeps the first shape's flow pick and Apply and gains a link; the Sessions card's flow chip linked to the page; the flow, counts and logins bands drawn as *after the switch-over*. For a team that is not switched the tab draws no cannot-run dot, the head shows the Sessions card's state word, and **+ New team** is drawn disabled with *after the switch-over*.

**Done when:** Every team has a page reached from the gear and from its Sessions card; a running setting saved there reads back from `ao` and takes effect as it does today; the Settings page's team card no longer draws those rows; nothing about how a team runs has changed.
