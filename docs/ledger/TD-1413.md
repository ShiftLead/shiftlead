## TD-1413: The UI cuts one Queue card from its own view, serves it with the person's mail cut to ids, and says how each attached item found its entry

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-05
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ee6bcbf973aa4d768f10fb38f203926d
**Part of:** TD-1411
**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (item_home, ledger_home, attach, new card_view); src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (GET /api/queue/card); design §4.7 ao card, §4.5 screen 6 What tied it, §4.4 One owner

**Why:** TD-1411's design (§4.7 *`ao card`*, §4.4 *One owner*, §4.5 screen 6 *What tied it*): a card's attachments are the UI's (`card_mail`, `attach`), so the one card `ao card` prints is cut from the page's own view by one function, served by the UI with the person's mail cut to ids, and every attached item says how its entry was found. This is the UI half; TD-1414 is the command.

**Fix:** As design §4.7 *`ao card`* and §4.5 screen 6 *What tied it* say:

1. **`tied`**: `item_home` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`) returns `tied: {by, ref}` beside `home`, `on_card`, `on_row` and `mentions`.
   - `by` is where its first held ref, the one that sets `on_card`, was read:
     - `context` for a board line;
     - for mail, `about` when the ref came from `about`, else `words` (subject and text, read as one, as today);
     - `pr` for a red PR (branch and title, read as one);
     - `title` for a fork's PR.
   - `ref` is that ref as the index matched it: normalised by `refs`, and for a board line a bare `TD-n` qualified with the line's `repo_name`.
   - `ledger_home` returns `tied: {by: "entry", ref: "<repo>#TD-n"}`.
   - An `inbox` home has `tied: None`. Nothing on the page draws it.
2. **`attach(…, raw=False)`**. With `raw=True`:
   - Each item also carries:
     - `row`, the Inbox row it was drawn from (the `e` passed to `put`, without rendered markup);
     - `tied`, from that row;
     - `repo`: a board line's `repo_name`, a ledger row's `name`, a PR row's `repo`, and `""` for mail.
   - `mentioned` becomes `{<row qid>: [{item, kind, words}]}`:
     - `item` is the naming item's `item_ref`, else its row's `id`;
     - `kind` is its row's `kind`;
     - `words` is its first eight words, as today.

   The page's call is unchanged (`raw=False`), and so is the page.
3. **`card_mail(…, raw=False)`** passes `raw` to `attach`.
4. **`card_view(queue, mail, id, now, fleet, *, words)`** in `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` returns `{card, wants, mentioned, mail, at}`.
   - **Finding the card.** It normalises `id` with `refs(id)[0]` and finds its card with `card_index(queue)`.
   - **What it returns:**
     - `card` is `queue_view`'s shaped card for it, every key but `lines` and `wants`. Each row's and the top's `mentioned` is replaced by the list form from step 2, for that row.
     - `wants` is its `card_wants`. Each item is keyed by `item` (`item_ref`); its `html` is dropped and its `row` stands in its place.
     - `mentioned` is the step 2 lists for this card's rows.
     - `at` is the `queue` reading's.
   - **Ids no open card holds:**
     - None for an id no open card holds.
     - `{ambiguous: [<repo>#TD-n, …]}` for a bare `TD-n` that two or more repos' open cards hold, found by the cards' rows.
     - `{completed: {closed, pr}}` for a `<repo>#TD-n` among the reading's `completed` cards' entries, `pr` possibly None.
   - **With `words=False`:** each item whose `row` is mail is cut to `{id, kind, from, at}`, `kind` being the row's and `from` its `from_name`. Mail means a `row` kind other than `board`, `ledger`, `red_pr` and `fork_pr`. Each `mentioned` entry of a mail item drops `words`. `mail` is then `ids`; else `mail` is `full`.
5. **`Readings.card(id, words)`** in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` is the one read behind both roads.
   - **`ledger_view` moves.** Today it is a closure in `_inbox_routes`, set as `h.ledger_view`. It becomes a `Readings` method, keeping its `@timing.timed("ledger")`, and the closure's callers use the method. So `card_mail` runs on a `Readings`.
   - **Before its reads,** `Readings.card` sets what the `person_settings` middleware and `queue_page` set: `uiconf.set_read` from the `settings` RPC, a repo memo, and `repos_omit(REPOS_OMIT_INBOX)`. On the endpoint road it uses the request's memo; it holds its own only where none exists, as `counts_now` does.
   - **Then it reads,** in order:
     - the `queue` and `list` RPCs;
     - `card_mail(self, fleet, card_index(queue), None, cards, raw=True)`;
     - `card_view(…, words=words)`.
   - **It suppresses nothing.** A read's `HTTPException` becomes `CardRefused(kind, why)`:
     - `down` for a 503 from any read;
     - `no_queue` for the `queue` RPC's refusal;
     - `refused` for any other read's refusal, the `person` read's among them, `why` being the host's words.
6. **`GET /api/queue/card?id=<id>`** answers `await h.card(id, words=False)` as JSON. It never carries a mail's words.
   - 200 with the card, or with `completed` or `ambiguous`.
   - 404 `{error}` for None.
   - 503 `{agent_down: true}` for `down`.
   - 502 `{error: "the home did not serve the Queue: <why>"}` for `no_queue`.
   - 400 `{error: <why>}` for `refused`.

**Done when:** Tests show that:
- **`item_home`** gives `tied` for each case:
  - a board line's `context`;
  - mail's `about` and `words`;
  - a red PR's `pr`;
  - a fork's PR's `title`;
  - a bare ref on another repo's board, its `ref` qualified with the line's own repo;
  - `inbox`, with None.
- **`ledger_home`** gives `entry`.
- **`attach(raw=True)`** gives each kind's `row`, `tied` and `repo`, and the list form of `mentioned`.
- **`card_view`** finds a card by its top entry, a row, an archived row, an unnormalised `TD-5`, a bare `TD-n` and an origin path. It answers None, `ambiguous` and `completed` as above.
- **Under `words=False`** no mail item, `mentioned` entry or row carries a mail's words, while a board line keeps its text, detail and answers. `mail` reads `ids` against `full`.
- **`Readings.card`** gives the card the Queue page draws for the same readings, and raises each of the three `CardRefused` kinds from a stubbed call.
- **The endpoint** answers its five statuses.
- **The Queue page's and the Inbox's existing tests** pass with `ledger_view` moved.
