**Why:** TD-1411's design (§4.7 *`ao card`*, §4.4 *One owner*, §4.5 screen 6 *What tied it*): a card's attachments are the UI's (`card_mail`, `attach`), so the one card `ao card` prints is cut from the page's own view by one function, served by the UI with the person's mail cut to ids, and every attached item says how its entry was found. This is the UI half; TD-1414 is the command.

**Ask:** Held with its design TD-1411 by decision (paul): Go on the board's 2026-11-05 decide item for TD-1411 lifts it (Go | One more round | Hold).

**Fix:** As design §4.7 *`ao card`* and §4.5 screen 6 *What tied it* say:

1. **`tied`**: `item_home` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`) returns `tied: {by, ref}` beside `home`, `on_card`, `on_row` and `mentions`.
   - `by` is where its first held ref, the one that sets `on_card`, was read:
     - `context` for a board line;
     - for mail, `about` when the ref came from `about`, else `words` (subject and text, read as one, as today);
     - `pr` for a red PR: its title, and its branch where a row carries one (the Inbox's red PR row carries none), read as one;
     - `title` for a fork's PR.
   - `ref` is that ref as the index matched it: normalised by `refs`, and for a board line a bare `TD-n` qualified with the line's `repo_name`.
   - `ledger_home` returns `tied: {by: "entry", ref: "<repo>#TD-n"}`.
   - An `inbox` home has `tied: None`. Nothing on the page draws it.
2. **`attach(…, raw=False)`**. With `raw=True`:
   - Each item also carries:
     - `row`, the Inbox row it was drawn from (the `e` passed to `put`, without rendered markup);
     - `tied`, from that row;
     - `repo`: a board line's `repo_name`, a ledger row's `name`, a PR row's `repo`, and `""` for mail.
   - `attach` also returns `mentioned_items`, `{<row qid>: [{item, row_kind, kind, words}]}`, beside `mentioned`, which keeps its shape; one entry per item, as `mentioned` holds one, in `attach`'s order:
     - `item` is the naming item's id by the same fallback as `wants` (step 4): `item_ref`, else *fork PR <repo>#<n>*, else its row's `id`;
     - `row_kind` is its row's `row` value (`board`, `red_pr` or `fork_pr`; `""` for mail, which has none; a ledger row names no mention);
     - `kind` is its row's `kind`, else *red PR* or *fork PR* for a PR row, which has none;
     - `words` is its first eight words, as today.

   The page's call is unchanged (`raw=False`), and so is the page.
3. **`card_mail(…, raw=False)`** passes `raw` to `attach`.
4. **`card_view(queue, mail, id, now, fleet, *, words)`** in `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` returns `{card, wants, mentioned, mail, at}`.
   - **Finding the card.** It normalises `id` with `(refs(id) or [id])[0]` (`refs` is imported as `_refs` in `ui/queue.py`) and finds its card with `card_index(queue)`.
   - **What it returns:**
     - `card` is `queue_view`'s shaped card for it, every key but `lines` and `wants`. `queue_view` is called with the mail as `attach` returned it and `promotes=None` (its completed lines' live mark, which `card_view` drops); afterwards each row's and the top's `mentioned` is replaced by that row's `mentioned_items` list.
     - `wants` is its `card_wants`. The groups stay `card_wants`' lists. Each item's `item` is `item_ref`, else *fork PR <repo>#<n>* for a fork's PR (`<n>` its row's `pr`, so two repos' cannot collide), else its row's `id`; its `html` is dropped and its `row` stands in its place.
     - `mentioned` is `mentioned_items` for this card's rows.
     - `at` is the `queue` reading's.
   - **Ids no open card holds:**
     - None for an id no open card holds.
     - `{ambiguous: [<repo>#TD-n, …]}` for a bare `TD-n` that two or more repos' open cards hold, found by the cards' rows.
     - `{completed: {closed, pr}}` for a `<repo>#TD-n` among the reading's `completed` cards' entries: the matched entry's own `closed` and `pr`, else the card's, `pr` possibly None.
   - **With `words=False`:** each item whose `row` is mail is cut to whichever of `{id, kind, from, from_name, at}` are present (a handed row has `from_name` *you* and may have no `from`), the row's own keys: `kind` the row's, `from` the sender's id and `from_name` its name, as `person_inbox` sets them. Mail means a `row` kind other than `board`, `ledger`, `red_pr` and `fork_pr`. Each `mentioned` entry whose `row_kind` is mail (`""`) drops `words`. Every `answered` loses the words it carries, of either kind, one rule with no branch on kind (a board answer's *you answered "…"* goes too): a ledger row's (`_held_answered`'s) keeps whichever of `{asker, name, kind, age, mid, until, board}` are present and drops `words` and `outcome`; the queue reading's on `card.top` and each of `card.rows` keeps whichever of `{mid, asker, name, kind, until, closed_at}` are present and drops `outcome` and `line`. `mail` is then `ids`; else `mail` is `full`.
5. **`Readings.card(id, words)`** in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` is the one read behind both roads.
   - **`ledger_view` moves.** Today it is a closure in `_inbox_routes`, set as `h.ledger_view`. It becomes a `Readings` method, keeping its `@timing.timed("ledger")`, and the closure's callers use the method. So `card_mail` runs on a `Readings`.
   - **Before its reads,** `Readings.card` sets what `queue_page` sets, `repos_omit(REPOS_OMIT_INBOX)`, over a repo memo. On the endpoint road the `person_settings` middleware has already set the memo and `uiconf.set_read`. On the in-process road, which has no request, it holds its own memo, as `counts_now` does, and reads `settings` for `uiconf.set_read` as `_counts` does: display settings, suppressed, so a failed read keeps the defaults. It tells the roads apart by `_REPOS_MEMO.get() is None`, and on the in-process road resets the memo's token in a `finally`, as `counts_now` does. `h.ledger_view` and `h.card` are wired on `h` from the `Readings` (`h.ledger_view = rd.ledger_view`, `h.card = rd.card`).
   - **Then it reads,** in order:
     - the `queue` RPC whole, as `queue_page` reads it, never `queue_cards`' read, which omits `completed`; and the `list` RPC;
     - `card_mail(self, fleet, card_index(queue), None, cards, raw=True)`;
     - `card_view(…, words=words)`.
   - **`CardRefused(Exception)`** is defined in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` beside `Readings`, with `kind` and `why` attributes.
   - **It adds no suppression of its own.** The reads' own tolerance stays: `ledger_view` suppresses a failed `repos()` or `defs()` and returns no rows, so a card may then lack its ledger rows, as the page does.
   - **A refused read**: A read's `HTTPException` becomes `CardRefused(kind, why)`:
     - `down` for a 503 from any read;
     - `no_queue` for the `queue` RPC's refusal;
     - `refused` for any other read's refusal, the `person` read's among them, `why` being the host's words.
6. **`GET /api/queue/card?id=<id>`** answers `await h.card(id, words=False)` as `JSONResponse(json.loads(json.dumps(got, default=str)))`, since rows carry dates. It never carries a mail's words.
   - 200 with the card, or with `completed` or `ambiguous`.
   - 404 `{error: "no open card holds <id>"}` for None.
   - 503 `{agent_down: true}` for `down`.
   - 502 `{error: "the home did not serve the Queue: <why>"}` for `no_queue`.
   - 400 `{error: <why>}` for `refused`.

**Done when:** Tests show that:
- **`item_home`** gives `tied` for each case:
  - a board line's `context`;
  - mail's `about` and `words`;
  - a red PR's `pr`;
  - a fork's PR's `title`;
  - a bare ref on another repo's board, its `ref` qualified with the line's own repo;
  - `inbox`, with None.
- **`ledger_home`** gives `entry`.
- **`attach(raw=True)`** gives each kind's `row`, `tied` and `repo`, and `mentioned_items`, while `mentioned` keeps its shape and the page's `queue_view` runs on it unchanged.
- **`card_view`** finds a card by its top entry, a row, an archived row, an unnormalised `TD-5`, a bare `TD-n` and an origin path. It answers None, `ambiguous` and `completed` as above.
- **Under `words=False`** a fixture whose message has text and whose answered rows carry `words` and `outcome.text` (a ledger row's `answered`, `card.top.answered`, `card.rows[*].answered`) shows none of those words anywhere in the JSON, and the in-process road shows them; no mail item or `mentioned` entry carries a mail's words, while a board line keeps its text, detail and answers. `mail` reads `ids` against `full`.
- **`Readings.card`** gives the card the Queue page draws for the same readings, and raises each of the three `CardRefused` kinds from a stubbed call.
- **The endpoint** answers its five statuses.
- **The Queue page's and the Inbox's existing tests** pass with `ledger_view` moved, those that compare an `item_home` result or an Inbox row exactly updated for `tied`; the Inbox's payload carries `tied` on each row.
- **The result is JSON-serialisable** (the endpoint's answer for a fixture card with dates in its rows).
