## TD-2280: A forgotten login's records lose their transcript, though the design says they keep it

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 0259f7e0233f42d08eb225774b4bd3de

**Why:** §4.2b *Where it lives* says an ended record is meant to resolve its folder through `logins.yml`'s `forgotten:`, so its transcript stays readable, and that no reader gives that folder yet. None does: `profiles._from_logins` lists `logins:` alone, and nothing in `src/` outside `logins.py` reads `forgotten:` (found by TD-2232's round 1 Fable review, F7 in `docs/reviews/TD-2232.md`). Once a login is forgotten, its records' transcript, conversations, prompts and links read *unknown profile*.

TD-2232 tried to design the read in its rounds 1–3 and each round widened it (F7, F15, F21, F29, F30, F33, and round 3's fact-check), so it was taken out of TD-2232 (F34) to be designed on its own. What those rounds found:
- every launch path calls `profiles.get`: `agent.py`'s new-session path, each adapter's `launch`, `ao new --profile`, a relaunch, the team pre-flights in `teams.py` and `stage_start.py`; and the relaunch check `_relaunch_profile` reads `doctor_profiles` over `load()`, not `get`;
- the readers that would need the folder are `_last_turns`, `read_transcript`, `conversations`, `links` and `prompts` in the Claude Code adapter (the last run for every non-closed record by `agent_tick._refresh_prompts`), and Gemini's and Grok's module-level `_profile()`;
- usage, billing, the gate and reserves (`usage_for`, `billing_for`, `_account_key`, `_reserve_accounts`, `_gate_profiles`) must not read a forgotten login, and a kept record under a forgotten name already makes an orphan `<adapter>:<name>` gate row;
- the refusal sites strip `"` from `str(KeyError)`, so a message with no `'` stays wrapped in quotes.

**Design PR:** #4784

**Location:** src/shiftlead/profiles.py, src/shiftlead/adapters/; docs/design/4.2b-tools.md, docs/design/4.9e-switch-over.md

**Fix:** Design how a forgotten login's records are read while it is forgotten — for instance a `forgotten=True` opt-in on `profiles.get` for the record readers alone, or a folder read of its own — keeping it absent to every launch path, usage, billing, the gate and reserves, with the points the Why lists. Write it in §4.2b *Where it lives* and §4.9e *A record's `profile`*, and file the build. Or, if no one needs a forgotten login's transcript before a take-back, make §4.2b say so in place of its promise.

**Done when:** The design says whether and how a forgotten login's record's transcript is read, and §4.2b and §4.9e agree; a build is filed, or §4.2b's promise is withdrawn.
