**Why:** td-max's seat half (TD-1529, Paul 2026-11-07: *if the techlead is busy, and another item is coming to it, a new techlead seat would be spun up instead*): today a second question to a working seat queues behind its turn, and the techlead seat, which reads every held PR, is the team's bottleneck when several grinders hand it PRs at once.

**Ask:** Go once you and the designer agree td-max's shape (floor, ceiling, what triggers scaling, what the card says) and its review rounds end?

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *An extra seat*, §4.9 *Mail to a scaled seat* and *A scaled seat holds its review stage*, §4.9b's routing-around pointer:
- the send to a seat goes to its first free live record in place order (the seat, then its extras), else the seat's own record;
- **up, at the send** (no `SEAT_SCALE_WAIT`): a send to the seat that finds every live record of it `working` or `needs-you` fills the seat's lowest-placed held extra at once (`create` from its launch record with `keep_mail`) and delivers the mail to it — except mail on a thread a live record sent or answered and an `ask` carrying a `pr` a live record already holds an ask about, which stay with that record; a send that cannot fill one (every extra live and busy, the host at `scale.live`, gated, balance) leaves it on the seat's own record, and rule 3's pass each tick fills an extra for it once one can be filled and moves the waiting mail there (oldest first, by `_reroute_waiting`'s road);
- an extra's fills count toward a `FILL_CEILING` group of their own, the seat's extras together, apart from the seats sharing their controller;
- routing around an unable seat (`seat_able`, `_route_lead`) reads the seat's extras first: a free live one, else a held one its mail would fill;
- **down, only when the slot is needed**: a live extra seat is never closed by the `asks` linger; when its host is at `scale.live` and a start needs the room there (an extra, a member's fill, a team's start, a person's new session), the home first closes the host's longest-idle extra seat that is idle at the composer and clean (nothing dirty or unpushed, no live subagent or shell, no PR of its own waiting within `SEAT_PR_WAIT`) back to held, then starts; a wind-down or stop closes it with the rest;
- a record whose `scale.line` names a seat answers for the seat's name in `held.read_by` and a chain link's `addressed` (so `ao pr held`, rule 11's crossing and the *unasked* tag take an extra's reply as the reader's) and in `shape.stage_of` (so the shape samples the extras in the seat's review stage, its queue the union of their `prs_waiting`); *one open question per sender per seat* counts over the seat's records;
- `scale.last` written on each fill and close.

**Done when:** A techlead seat with `max: 2`, working, that receives a second ask has `<techlead>-2` filled at the send and the ask delivered there, with no wait; a follow-up on the first ask's thread stays with the first record; a PR read by the extra counts as the reader's for `ao pr held`, the *unasked* tag and the merge; the extra stays open idle past `SEAT_ASK_LINGER`, and is closed back to held only when the host is at `scale.live` and a start needs the room, the longest-idle first; tests hold each.
