**Why:** td-max's seat half (TD-1529, Paul 2026-11-07: *if the techlead is busy, and another item is coming to it, a new techlead seat would be spun up instead*): today a second question to a working seat queues behind its turn, and the techlead seat, which reads every held PR, is the team's bottleneck when several grinders hand it PRs at once.

**Ask:** Go once you and the designer agree td-max's shape (floor, ceiling, what triggers scaling, what the card says) and its review rounds end?

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *An extra seat*, §4.9 *Mail to a scaled seat* and *A scaled seat holds its review stage*, §4.9b's routing-around pointer:
- the send to a seat goes to its first free live record in place order (the seat, then its extras), so a warm idle extra takes it while the own record is held; where none is free and the own record is not live (held, never filled, or `exited` awaiting rule 1), the own record takes it and is filled on its cause or restarted;
- **up, at the send** (no `SEAT_SCALE_WAIT`): a send to the seat that finds the seat's own record live and busy (`working` or `needs-you`) and each live extra busy fills the seat's lowest-placed held extra at once (`create` from its launch record with `keep_mail`): the mail written to the held extra's mailbox and the fill started in the same call, the reply naming where it went, the send not waiting for the pane; a seat whose own record is not live takes the mail there and is filled on its cause as today — except mail on a thread a live record sent or answered and an `ask` carrying a `pr` a live record already holds an ask about, which stay with that record; **each tick, by the state**: a seat whose own record is live and busy, each live extra busy, holding unread movable mail (however it got there: a send that could not fill, or a second ask that landed on the closed seat before its fill) fills its lowest-placed held extra once one can be filled (not every extra live and busy, the host below `scale.live`, not gated, no balance mark) and moves that mail there (oldest first) — a mover of its own beside `_reroute_waiting`, which moves only from a seat `seat_unable` says cannot answer and passes over `entry` envelopes and a reader's `pr` ask, both of which this one moves;
- rule 3's cause pass (`seat_due`, a seat's fill on its cause) never fills a record carrying `scale`;
- an extra's fills count toward a `FILL_CEILING` group of their own, the seat's extras together, apart from the seats sharing their controller;
- routing around an unable seat (`seat_able`, `_route_lead`) reads the seat's extras first — a free live one, else a held one its mail would fill — only where the seat is unable as `stalled?`, `exited` or closed; `limited` and the usage gate's pause pass the extras over (same profile); a reader's `pr` ask may go to an extra;
- mail on a thread, or a `pr` ask, held by a record that is now a held extra is routed as any other;
- **down, only when the slot is needed**: a live extra seat is never closed by the `asks` linger; when its host is at `scale.live` and a start needs the room there (an extra, a member's fill, a team's start, a person's new session), the home first closes the host's longest-idle extra seat that is idle at the composer and clean (nothing dirty or unpushed, no live subagent or shell, no PR of its own waiting within `SEAT_PR_WAIT`, no unread mail) back to held, then starts — the eviction is TD-1545's reader (never an extra of the starting extra's own seat), this build's part that the linger never closes an extra; a wind-down or stop closes it with the rest;
- a record whose `scale.line` names a seat answers for the seat's name in `held.read_by` and a chain link's `addressed` (so `ao pr held`, rule 11's crossing and the *unasked* tag take an extra's reply as the reader's) and in `shape.stage_of` (so the shape samples the extras in the seat's review stage, its queue the union of their `prs_waiting`); *one open question per sender per seat* counts over the seat's records;
- `scale.last` written on each fill and close.

**Done when:** A techlead seat on call (its own record held) with a live idle extra sends the ask to the extra; with none live, to its own record, filled on its cause, no extra; a techlead seat with `max: 2`, working, that receives a second ask has `<techlead>-2` filled at the send and the ask delivered there, with no wait; a follow-up on the first ask's thread stays with the first record; a PR read by the extra counts as the reader's for `ao pr held`, the *unasked* tag and the merge; the extra stays open idle past `SEAT_ASK_LINGER`, and is closed back to held only when the host is at `scale.live` and a start needs the room, the longest-idle first; two asks that landed on the closed seat, the second unread once it works, move the second to a filled extra at the next tick; the cause pass never fills an extra; tests hold each.
