**Why:** td-max's seat half (TD-1529, Paul 2026-11-07: *if the techlead is busy, and another item is coming to it, a new techlead seat would be spun up instead*): today a second question to a working seat queues behind its turn, and the techlead seat, which reads every held PR, is the team's bottleneck when several grinders hand it PRs at once.

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *An extra seat* and §4.9 *Mail to a scaled seat* and *A scaled seat holds its review stage*:
- the send to a seat goes to its first free live record in place order (the seat, then its extras), else the seat's own record;
- the tick fills the seat's lowest-placed held extra when mail has waited unread a minute (`SEAT_SCALE_WAIT`) and every live record of the seat is `working` or `needs-you` (`seat_due: {by: scale}`; not suspended, not gated, under `FILL_CEILING` and `scale.live`), and moves the waiting mail to it oldest first, except mail on a thread a live record sent or answered and a `pr` ask whose PR a live record already holds an ask about; one fill per seat per pass; routing around an unable seat (TD-1443) runs first;
- a live extra closes back to held by rule 3's `asks` close (the linger, clean, no PR of its own waiting);
- the seat's records read together: *one open question per sender per seat*, `prs_waiting` (union, oldest), the balance's `review` line, the shape's review queue, `TECHLEAD_WAIT`, the repo's *unasked* tag, and the merge on a held path by any record whose `scale.line` names the stage's seat;
- `scale.last` written on each fill and close.

**Done when:** A techlead seat with `max: 2`, working, that receives a second ask has it moved to `<techlead>-2`, filled, within a pass of the minute; a follow-up on the first ask's thread stays with the first record; a PR read by the extra counts as the reader's for `ao pr held`, the *unasked* tag and the merge; the extra closes back to held after the linger; tests hold each.
