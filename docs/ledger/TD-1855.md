## TD-1855: Every PR the gate sweep opens is a work order at once: lift PRs of every gate, and its board PRs

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-14
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 25038f6bb0294d0c941b517899e13389
**Part of:** TD-1854

**Why:** TD-1854's design (design §4.4 *A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once*): only a live check's lift PR was a work order, so the gate sweep's other lift PRs and its board PRs (#3757, #3760, #3891 on 2026-11-14) sat open with nobody to read them. This builds the order for every one of them.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/reports.py (lift_of, sweep_of, pr_reading); src/sessionorc/workorders.py (is_lift_order, is_sweep_order, filing_row, red_prs, pr_order, pr_note, lift_context); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_red_prs); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (red_pr_rows); src/shiftlead/cli.py (ao repo, _lifts); src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md; docs/briefs/

**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once* and its pointers (§4.4 *The gate sweep*, the filing rule's heads, the pick order; §4.5a *Inbox row: red PR*; §4.9b *No anchor seat*):
1. **The reading** (`reports.pr_reading`): `lift_of(branch) -> (TD-n, gate, live) | None` reads every lift head as `gatesweep.lift_branch` writes it — `until-YYYY-MM-DD` (a calendar date, matched whole) → `until <date>`, a legacy dateless `until` → `until` (its date read from the entry at the taker's step), any other name → `event (<name>)` (`until-<not a date>` among them), `decision-…` → None — `live` true only for `live`, `live-<digits>`, `live-td<digits>`; every caller updated (`workorders.lift_context`, `cli._lifts`, whose live-check line reads `live` alone); `lift` and `lift_since` stamped on each. A new `sweep_of` reads `sweep/<date>` and `notices/<repo>-sweep-<date>` (`gatesweep.SWEEP_BRANCH`, and the notices prefix as a constant beside it), stamping `sweep` and `sweep_since` as `lift_since` is kept. The filing read (`filing`, `filing_head`) is skipped on all three heads.
2. **The orders** (`workorders`, `agent_tick._red_prs`): `is_lift_order` takes every lift head; a new `is_sweep_order` beside it; `_red_prs` in an unserved repo lists the lift and sweep orders and only those (so the Inbox row draws there); `filing_row` refuses both; `red_prs` orders a sweep PR at once as a lift, keyed on `sweep_since`, with the lift and sync ones; `pr_order` marks a sweep order `sweep` with `sweep_since`, Medium, its `Context` the URL and *the gate sweep's board items*; `lift_context` names the gate (*lifts TD-n until 2026-11-20*); `pr_note` says *sweep PR #n has been open since …: the gate sweep's board items — take it or say why*.
3. **The pages**: `ui/inbox.red_pr_rows` draws a sweep order's row (*`<repo>` #<n> · sweep 2h · the gate sweep's board items · <author>*) as it does a lift's; `ao repo` (`cli.py`) counts and prints a sweep order with the lift orders; the live-check line's *lift #n open* reads only a lift whose `live` is true.
4. **The grinder's brief** (`src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md`, and `docs/briefs/` where it says the same): the `pr:<n>` order list and the pick order name the gate sweep's PRs, and the taker's steps by head as §4.4 writes them — a live lift §4.9b's, unchanged; another event's re-run by `event_checks.py --json` (passed merges; not yet or impossible closes unmerged with a comment; error, no row or no outcome drops the order); an `until` lift merged where `origin/main`'s entry still carries the gate and its day has come, else closed with a comment; a sweep PR merged where its diff is the board and its archive alone, a `sweep/` one closed when the board has moved past it, a `notices/` one never closed (replayed and merged); a conflict resolved by main's copy and `ledger.py lift` again (a lift), or `board_edit.py replay` (a sweep PR).
5. Update the docstrings and comments that name *A live check's lift PR is the team's at once* (`workorders.py`, `reports.py`, `agent_tick.py`, `tests/`) to the rule's new name.
Tests: a reading test per head (`until-<date>`, `until-foo` as an event, dateless `until`, an event, a live name, `decision-…` read as none, `sweep/…`, `notices/…`); `red_prs` ordering each at once whatever its checks, none while a live record holds it, none as a draft or a fork, and none as a filing order; `red_pr_rows` drawing the sweep row in a repo no team services and none in a serviced one.

**Done when:** Every open PR on a `lift/`, `sweep/` or `notices/` head the gate sweep writes is a `pr:<n>` order from its first reading, in the grinders' pick list or, in a repo no team services, an *Inbox row: red PR*; the grinder's brief carries the taker's steps by head; the tests above pass; and the next lift the sweep opens after this is live is merged or closed by a grinder within a working day with no person's prompt.
