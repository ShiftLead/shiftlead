## TD-1855: Every PR the gate sweep opens is a work order at once: lift PRs of every gate, and its board PRs

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-14
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 25038f6bb0294d0c941b517899e13389
**Part of:** TD-1854

**Why:** TD-1854's design (design §4.4 *A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once*): only a live check's lift PR was a work order, so the gate sweep's other lift PRs and its board PRs (#3757, #3760, #3891 on 2026-11-14) sat open with nobody to read them. This builds the order for every one of them.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/reports.py (lift_of, sweep_of, pr_reading); src/sessionorc/workorders.py (is_lift_order, is_sweep_order, filing_row, red_prs, pr_order, pr_note, lift_context); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_red_prs); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (red_pr_rows); src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (pr_orders, red_prs); src/shiftlead/cli.py (ao repo, _lifts); src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md; src/shiftlead/briefs/sweeper.md; docs/briefs/

**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once* and its pointers (§4.4 *The gate sweep*, the filing rule's heads, the pick order; §4.5a *Inbox row: red PR*; §4.9b *No anchor seat*):
1. **The reading** (`reports.pr_reading`): `lift_of(branch) -> (TD-n, gate, live) | None` reads every lift head as `gatesweep.lift_branch` writes it — `until-YYYY-MM-DD` (a calendar date, matched whole) → `until <date>`, a legacy dateless `until` → `until` (its date read from the entry at the taker's step), any other name → `event (<name>)` (`until-<not a date>` among them), `decision-…` → None — `live` true only for `live`, `live-<digits>`, `live-td<digits>`; every caller updated (`workorders.lift_context`, `cli._lifts`, whose live-check line reads `live` alone); `lift` and `lift_since` stamped on each. A new `sweep_of(branch) -> "sweep" | "notices" | None` reads `sweep/YYYY-MM-DD` and `notices/<slug>-sweep-YYYY-MM-DD`, matched whole with a calendar date by new `reports.SWEEP_HEAD_RE` and `NOTICES_HEAD_RE` (the slug `[A-Za-z0-9._-]+`; `gatesweep.SWEEP_BRANCH` and `NOTICES_BRANCH` moved to `reports` as `LIFT_PREFIX` was), `lift_of`'s event name `[a-z0-9-]+`, stamping `sweep` (`sweep_of`'s value) and `sweep_since` as `lift_since` is kept; an `until-<date>` head's `lift_since` is stamped from the first reading on or after its day. The filing read (`filing`, `filing_head`) is skipped on exactly the heads `lift_of` or `sweep_of` reads — one predicate, so `lift/TD-n/decision-x` or `sweep/foo` is filing-eligible — and `held` is read on them as today.
2. **The orders** (`workorders`, `agent_tick._red_prs`): `is_lift_order` takes every lift head; a new `is_sweep_order` beside it; `_red_prs` in an unserved repo lists the lift and sweep orders and only those, running `red_prs` with no `filing_after` and keeping the lift and sweep orders with their notes and `told` alone, returned by a new `teamrun.person_pr_orders(r)` (`teamrun.pr_orders` still none there, so `lane_kinds` counts nothing pickable); a PR on a head `lift_of`/`sweep_of` reads never a red or filing order in `red_prs` or `red_holders`, whether or not it is an order now; `red_prs` (with `now` and `reads`) leaves out an `until-<date>` lift before that UTC day and a held one with a read open, `is_lift_order`/`is_sweep_order` staying pure; the reading carries `pr_orders.gate`, this rule's numbers holders aside, and `red_holders` names their holders at once, with no `after`; `workorders.red_holders` lists a lift or sweep PR's holders keyed on its stamp, whatever its checks, in an unserved repo too; a lift or sweep order whose `held` names a path stops being an order once a read of it is open, through a new `workorders.read_open(pr, reads)` that `is_filing_order` calls too; `filing_row` refuses both; `red_prs` orders a sweep PR at once as a lift, keyed on `sweep_since`, with the lift and sync ones; `pr_order` marks a sweep order `sweep` with `sweep_since`, Medium, its `Context` the URL and *the gate sweep's board items*; `lift_context` names the gate (*lifts TD-n until 2026-11-20*); `pr_note` says *sweep PR #n has been open since …: the gate sweep's board items — take it or say why*.
3. **The pages**: `ui/inbox.red_pr_rows` draws a sweep order's row (*`<repo>` #<n> · sweep 2h · the gate sweep's board items · <author>*) as it does a lift's, and draws an unserved repo's from `person_pr_orders` whatever `teamrun.serviced` says; `ao repo` (`cli.py`) prints a sweep order *· sweep since <t>: the gate sweep's board items*, counts *n lift PRs to take* and *n sweep PRs to take* apart from the red ones, an unserved repo's lines *the person's · …*, and prints an unserved repo's under its *unserved:* line as *the person's*, never a *pickable … grinders* line; `teamrun.red_prs` draws a lift or sweep order from its `lift`/`sweep` mark, read from `pr_orders.gate`, winning over a red or conflicting row for the same PR, `whose` *the person's* in an unserved repo (`word` *lift*/*sweep*, `since` its stamp, `whose` *nobody's — the grinders' order* or *held by <names>*), out of the facet's red count; the live-check line's *lift #n open* reads only a lift whose `live` is true.
4. **The grinder's brief** (`src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md`, and `docs/briefs/` where it says the same): the `pr:<n>` order list and the pick order name the gate sweep's PRs, and the taker's steps by head as §4.4 writes them — a live lift §4.9b's, unchanged; every lift but a live one first reads `origin/main`'s entry and closes the PR with a comment where the gate is gone; a `host-log` gate's — known by its section's `check:` line on `origin/main`, as `gatesweep.host_log_gates` reads it — merged on its Log line's evidence, never re-run; another event's re-run by `event_checks.py --json` (passed merges; not yet or impossible closes unmerged with a comment; error, no row or no outcome drops the order; a taker's own setup error is mended or a `restart`, never a drop); an `until` lift merged (a dated one is an order only from its day), a legacy dateless one whose date is later closed with a comment; a sweep PR merged where its diff is the board and its archive alone (else reviewed as any PR), a `sweep/` one closed when main's board or archive already holds an item of the same `Context:` by `gatesweep._contexts`' fold or has closed one it closes, else replayed and merged, a `notices/` one replayed and merged, closed with a comment only where the replay refuses; a held one: the taker posts its `cadence-review:` evidence, asks its reader as its author and ends its claim `done --pr`; a conflict resolved by main's copy and `ledger.py lift` again (a lift), or `board_edit.py replay` (a sweep PR).
5. Update the docstrings and comments that name *A live check's lift PR is the team's at once* (`workorders.py`, `reports.py`, `agent_tick.py`, `tests/`) to the rule's new name.
Tests: a reading test per head (`until-<date>`, `until-foo` as an event, dateless `until`, an event, a live name, `decision-…` read as none and filing-eligible, `sweep/<date>`, `sweep/foo` filing-eligible, `notices/…`); a sweep PR red or conflicting being one sweep order keyed on `sweep_since`; `pr_note`'s and `pr_order`'s sweep words; in an unserved repo, the reading's `pr_orders.orders` holding only its lift and sweep orders, `teamrun.pr_orders` none and `person_pr_orders` those, and `red_pr_rows` drawing them; a not-yet-due `until-<date>` lift red past `ORPHAN_AFTER` being no red order; `red_prs` ordering each at once whatever its checks, none while a live record holds it, none as a draft or a fork, and none as a filing order; `red_pr_rows` drawing the sweep row in a repo no team services and none in a serviced one.
6. **The sweeper seat's branch** moves to `stranded/<date>` (design §4.9b *The sweep*): `src/shiftlead/briefs/sweeper.md` and `tests/test_sweep_seat.py`, so no other writer opens a PR on `sweep/<date>`.

**Done when:** Every open PR on a head `reports.lift_of` or `sweep_of` reads is a `pr:<n>` order from its first reading, in the grinders' pick list or, in a repo no team services or an unserved one, an *Inbox row: red PR*; every other head under those prefixes is read by the filing rule; the grinder's brief carries the taker's steps by head; and the tests in the Fix pass.
