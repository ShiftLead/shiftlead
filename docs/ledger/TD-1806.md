**Why:** Found by the test-audit of #3764 (TD-1637 slice 8). `_late_turn` (src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py) empties `s.turns` on a new `adapter_id` only `and event.get("state") not in ("exited", "closed")`, so a dying run's own `SessionEnd` cannot clear the turns of the conversation that replaced it. tests/test_turns.py never sends an event with a new `adapter_id` and state `exited` or `closed`. Probe: the clause `and event.get("state") not in ("exited", "closed")` replaced by nothing (`:`) left `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pdm run pytest -q tests/test_turns.py` at 7 passed. Every other branch of `_late_turn` I mutated (the clear, the exited-no-turn drop, the stale start, the eight kept, the subagent guard) fails a test.

**Location:** tests/test_turns.py

**Fix:** Add a case to tests/test_turns.py: a record with turns ["p1"] and adapter_id "a1" receives `state="exited", adapter_id="a2"`; its `turns` stay ["p1"]. Re-run the probe: it must fail.

**Done when:** Done when removing the exited/closed exemption fails a test.
