**Fix:** 1. **Find the cap.** The answer as written to the board is "Hold: " plus the note, 303 characters. A look at origin/main on 2026-11-14 found no 297- or 300-character cut in: `sessionorc/board.py` (its `ANSWER_MAX` = 80 and `SUBJECT_MAX` = 250 cut only the commit subject and PR title, as they should), `sessionorc/mail.py`'s caps, the vendored `ledgerboard` (`fill_form` cuts nothing), and the Queue's templates and `app.js` (no `maxlength` on an Answer… box or a form's box). Still to read: the press's road from the Queue's form box to `rpc_ledger_decide` / `_board_answer` (`fill_form`) and the decided line's writer, any input that came by a phone push action, and the pinned agent-cadence `board_edit.py decide`. Reproduce the cut on a scratch home with a 400-character Hold note before changing anything.
2. **Never cut a person's answer silently.** Keep the note whole on the board's Decided text and in the ledger Log line the press writes. Where a real limit has to stand, refuse the press before anything is sent: the box shows the limit and a count as the person types, and the refusal names the limit with the person's words kept in the box (§4.5's refusal rules, *Nothing was sent.*). Only a commit subject or PR title may be shortened, with its `…`, since the whole text is in the body.
3. **Tests:** a Hold note of more than 300 characters, pressed from the Queue, reaches the board's `Decided:` and the ledger Log whole; any limit kept is refused at the box with the words kept.
4. **Design:** §4.4 *Board write-back* or §4.10 gains one sentence, that a person's answer or note is written whole or refused, never cut, in the same PR.
