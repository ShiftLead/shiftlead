## TD-1276: §4.6 *A path in the pane is a link* points to "(below)" for a run's pieces, which the paragraph states above

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (docs-audit-ao-1, auditing the last ten merged PRs)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Location:** `docs/design/4.6-transport.md` line 292 (*at most eight with their pieces (below), each bounded, eight paths an ask*)

**Why:** #2722 (TD-1262) added the sentence at lines 284-290 that defines a run's pieces (*A run holding one of those is asked with its pieces without them … and the first the host resolves is the link*) and, in the next sentence block at line 292, wrote *with their pieces (below)*. `grep -n "pieces" docs/design/4.6-transport.md` finds the definition only above line 292 (lines 287, 292), and nothing below it in the paragraph defines them, so the pointer sends the reader the wrong way. The sentence is also run-on: *at most eight with their pieces … eight paths an ask* says the cap twice, while the host (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py:921`, `PATHS_RUNS_MAX = 8`) refuses more than eight runs in one ask, so the page sends the runs and their pieces eight at a time, which the sentence does not make plain. That line is also the only one of the paragraph past 100 columns.

**Fix:** change *(below)* to *(above)* and say once: at most eight runs an ask, a row's runs with their pieces sent in asks of eight; wrap the line.

**Done when:** line 292 points the right way and says the cap once.
