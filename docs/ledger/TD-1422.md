## TD-1422: A seat at the fill ceiling stays down until a person notices, and no command brings it back

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-05 (ao-led-tds-ledger, through the techlead seat; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open — designed: builds TD-1428, TD-1429; class in-repo (cap 4), since it changes only shiftlead's tick, restart RPC, CLI and UI
**Id take:** dede92056a884c3494f5c38b83835632
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (`_fill`, the fill ceiling); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (`FILL_CEILING`, `FILL_WINDOW`); src/sessionorc/agent.py (`ao restart`'s seat refusal); design §6 rule 3 *The fill ceiling*, §4.9b, §4.7

**Why:** Asked by the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-422eba651afb). On 2026-11-05, `techlead-ao-1` was filled at 14:04, 14:11, 14:19, 14:28, 14:46 and 15:00Z. Each fill answered one ask, sat idle for `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE`, was closed by rule 3 and was filled again for the next ask. At 15:04:38Z it reached `FILL_CEILING` (6 in `FILL_WINDOW`, an hour; `src/sessionorc/agent_common.py:137`) and got `restart_ceiling {why: fill}`. `_fill` returns at once on any `restart_ceiling` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`), so the seat was never filled again. Seven hours later 8 asks waited on it, two of them held-PR reads (#2963, #2970) that went to the person past their 2h bound. Its only sign was an Inbox restart row (*fills exhausted · 6 in 1 h*), cleared when the person pressed Resume at 22:22Z. `ao restart` refuses a seat (*a seat: rule 3 fills it when it is due, and Resume is the way to it*, `src/sessionorc/agent.py`), so a person at a terminal has no command to bring it back. Design §6 rule 3 *The fill ceiling* writes this as intended: the tripping seat takes the ceiling, its siblings are refused only until the hour rolls. So a seat that answers a steady trickle of asks one at a time trips its own ceiling and stays down.

**Fix:** Design first (§6 rule 3 *The fill ceiling*, §4.9b *A seat is empty or filled*, §4.7 `ao restart`). Choose among:
- the tripping seat's ceiling lapses when `FILL_WINDOW` rolls, as its siblings' refusal already does;
- a seat filled for an ask stays open while asks keep arriving within the grace, rather than closing and being filled again;
- both.

Separately, give the person a command to bring back a seat at a ceiling: `ao restart` or `ao resume` on a seat whose `restart_ceiling` is set, doing what the Inbox row's Resume does.
