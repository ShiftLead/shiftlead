## TD-1920: A person-owned entry that nothing blocks reads ready on the Queue, so the person's own next step goes unsaid

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17 (Paul, through his session ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** c160e75080b740578a6ebe14e988054a

**Why:** An open entry whose `Owner:` is the person and that nothing blocks reads *ready* on the Queue, the word a lane's pickable work reads, but no lane takes it, so it waits on the person and nothing says so. Since TD-1375 an `Owner:` holds nothing and the gate is what makes an entry *for you*; once the person answers a decision and its gate lifts, an entry they own reads as if a lane would pick it up. Seen 2026-11-17: agent-cadence#TD-281 (`Owner: person`, no gate) after Paul answered Yes to designing it with him first — its row reads *ready*, and TD-1911, which it blocks, reads only *waiting*, so neither card tells Paul the next step is his. A second edge: `ledger.PERSON_OWNER` is the literal `paul`, while agent-cadence's ledger writes `Owner: person` and declares no `Fields:` line (its cadence-changes say an Owner names any person), so another repo's person-owned entry is not read as the person's at all.

**Design PR:** #4097

**Location:** design §4.4 *The card's status* and *A row's state* (docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md), §4.5 screen 12, §4.5a Queue card rows; src/sessionorc/queue.py (the status words), src/sessionorc/ledger.py (PERSON_OWNER, kind_of)

**Fix:** Design first (§4.4 *The card's status*, *A row's state*; §4.5 screen 12; §4.5a's Queue card rows):
- an open entry owned by the person with nothing blocking it reads as the person's own step, never *ready* — words from its Kind and Fix, e.g. *yours: start a session to design it* — and its card reads that it waits on the person;
- a parent blocked by such an entry, in this repo or another (`<repo>#TD-n`), says it waits on the person's step, not a bare *waiting*;
- the step has its own colour or indicator, distinct from the orange of a session asking the person something (Paul's ask, 2026-11-17);
- the person's owner word is read per repo rather than as one literal, so another repo's word (agent-cadence's `person`, which no `Fields:` line declares) is the person too; the design says where that word comes from.
Check against TD-1375's rule that an Owner holds nothing: this is a reading of the row, not a hold, so lanes and pickable are unchanged.

**Done when:** the design says how a person-owned, unblocked entry and a parent waiting on one read on the Queue, with its colour, and how each repo's person word is read; its builds are filed with `Part of:` this entry.
