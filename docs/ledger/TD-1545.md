## TD-1545: td-max: a team's max setting, and the held extra sessions each scaling line and seat keeps ready

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-07

**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open

**Part of:** TD-1529
**Location:** design §4.9 *Scaling with the work*, §5 teams.<team>.max and scale.live, §4.7 ao team max, §4.5a Members dialog up to; src/sessionorc/settings.py, src/sessionorc/agent.py (create held), src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (LAUNCH_KEYS), src/sessionorc/models.py, src/sessionorc/work.py, src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_work_replays, the tagging seat), src/shiftlead/org.py, src/shiftlead/teams.py, src/shiftlead/teamrun.py

**Why:** td-max (TD-1529) needs its extras to exist before the tick can fill them: the home reads no team definition (design §6 *Keeping a team running*), so a session it starts past a line's size must be a record a press already wrote, as a pool's later members are (TD-1476). Without the setting, the held extras and the one road that writes them, rules 3 and 14 have nothing to fill.

**Fix:** Build design §4.9 *Scaling with the work*'s setting and records (and §5 `teams.<team>.max`, `scale.live`; §4.7 `ao team max`; §4.5a's Members dialog **up to** row):
- **Settings:** `sessionorc.settings` takes `max` in a team's keys beside `size`, validated as `size` is, and a top-level `scale: {live}` (six when absent) with a reader the tick can call; `org.with_settings` merges `max` onto `TeamDef`.
- **Refusals:** the write's refusals and `ao org check`'s set-aside lines for the manager, `plain` roles, a seat on `prs`, `every` or `swept`, a pooled seat, a worker line whose compiled lane is in no stage, and a name the definition does not give, each with its reason; `0` or the size clears it.
- **Records:** `create(held=True)` takes a record carrying `scale` (today an unattended seat's alone); `scale: {line}` is a record field carried by the launch record's keys (`LAUNCH_KEYS`) and every replay. `ao team start` and Apply's *starts* write `max − size` held extras per line or seat, each with the launch record the line's next kept member would get, unattended, supervised; a seat's extra carries `seat: {trigger: asks}` (no `tags`, no `pool`), and the tagging seat's choice and the `--team` / `--repo` candidates pass over it. A flow switch or Apply rewrites every held extra's launch record.
- **Names:** `TeamDef.extra_names(name)` (a line's sized names past its size up to `max`; a seat's `<name>-2`…), read by `session_names`, the started names and the twice-named check; `TeamDef.next_free` and `resize`'s step-up read a record carrying `scale` as free for its line, and **+** adopts it (clears `scale`, keeps the record, fills it on a live team); **−** never winds down an extra.
- **Passed over:** `_work_replays` (rule 8 and a schedule's start) skips a record carrying `scale`; `work.wound_down` and `work.finished` pass over a held extra as over a seat; `ao team stop` and a wind-down close a live extra and leave it held.
- `teamrun.set_max(call, org, team, name, n)`: writes the setting; on a live team writes the new extras held, or sits out the highest-placed by *A member renamed or removed*'s form. `ao team max <team> <name> <n>` (person-only) and the dialog's field call it; `ao team list` and the dialog read *<line> <size> +<live extras> · up to <max>*.

**Done when:** A team with `max: {grinder-ao: 5}` and size 3 started by `ao team start` has `grinder-ao-4` and `-5` as held records carrying `scale: {line: grinder-ao}`; `ao team max` and the dialog's field raise and lower them on a live team; each refused case is refused with its reason; **+** adopts `grinder-ao-4`; tests hold each.
