## TD-1545: td-max: a team's max setting, and the held extra sessions each scaling line and seat keeps ready

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** cc32a4bacbe7419090add31ca92f3005
**Part of:** TD-1529
**Location:** design §4.9 *Scaling with the work*, §5 teams.<team>.max and scale.live, §4.7 ao team max, §4.5a Members dialog up to; src/shiftlead/org.py, src/shiftlead/teams.py, src/shiftlead/teamrun.py

**Why:** td-max (TD-1529) needs its extras to exist before the tick can fill them: the home reads no team definition (design §6 *Keeping a team running*), so a session it starts past a line's size must be a record a press already wrote, as a pool's later members are (TD-1476). Without the setting, the held extras and the one road that writes them, rules 3 and 14 have nothing to fill.

**Fix:** Build design §4.9 *Scaling with the work*'s setting and records (and §5 `teams.<team>.max`, `scale.live`; §4.7 `ao team max`; §4.5a's Members dialog **up to** row):
- `max: {<name>: n}` merged onto `TeamDef` by `org.with_settings` as `size` is; the write's refusals and `ao org check`'s set-aside lines for the manager, `plain` roles, a seat on any trigger but `asks`, a pooled seat, a worker line whose compiled lane is in no stage, and a name the definition does not give, each with its reason; `0` or the size clears it.
- `ao team start` and Apply's *starts* write `max − size` **held** extras per line or seat (closed with no pane, as a pool's later member), named as the line's next sized names (a seat's `<name>-2`, `-3`), each with the launch record the line's next kept member would get, unattended, supervised, `scale: {line}`; the name checks cover them.
- `teamrun.set_max(call, org, team, name, n)`: writes the setting; on a live team writes the new extras held, or sits out the highest-placed by *A member renamed or removed*'s form. `ao team max <team> <name> <n>` (person-only) and the Members dialog's **up to** field call it.
- **+** adopts an extra holding the next sized name (clears `scale`, keeps the record, fills it on a live team); **−** never winds down an extra; rule 8 never replays one; `ao team stop` and a wind-down treat a live extra as a member and leave it held.
- `ao team list` and the dialog read *<line> <size> +<live extras> · up to <max>*.

**Done when:** A team with `max: {grinder-ao: 5}` and size 3 started by `ao team start` has `grinder-ao-4` and `-5` as held records carrying `scale: {line: grinder-ao}`; `ao team max` and the dialog's field raise and lower them on a live team; each refused case is refused with its reason; **+** adopts `grinder-ao-4`; tests hold each.
