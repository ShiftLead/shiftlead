**Why:** td-max (TD-1529) needs its extras to exist before the tick can fill them: the home reads no team definition (design §6 *Keeping a team running*), so a session it starts past a line's size must be a record a press already wrote, as a pool's later members are (TD-1476). Without the setting, the held extras and the one road that writes them, rules 3 and 14 have nothing to fill.

**Ask:** Go once you and the designer agree td-max's shape (floor, ceiling, what triggers scaling, what the card says) and its review rounds end?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9 *Scaling with the work*'s setting and records (and §5 `teams.<team>.max`, `scale.live`; §4.7 `ao team max`; §4.5a's Members dialog **up to** row):
- **Settings:** `sessionorc.settings` takes `max` in a team's keys beside `size`, validated as `size` is, and a top-level `scale: {live: {<host>: n}}` with a reader the tick and the send can call: a host's cap on every live session there, its default the machine's (half its memory at ~0.5 GB a session, capped at twice its cores, read by the host agent at its start; 30 on kmaster), and a count of that host's live sessions of every kind (a record `starting` or `scheduled` counted, one tick's fills counted as they are made), and **the eviction**, run in the host agent's `create` (which every start goes through: a fill, a team's start, the person's `ao new`, the UI's New session) when the host's live count is at or above the cap as it starts, the start itself never refused, and never evicting an extra of the starting extra's own seat — an extra, a member's fill, a team's start, a person's new session: close the host's longest-idle extra seat that is idle at the composer and clean (nothing dirty or unpushed, no live subagent or shell, no PR of its own within `SEAT_PR_WAIT`, no unread mail) back to held, its `scale.last` *back to held: host full, room for <name>*; a team with no `max` key reads the **default ceilings** (each line or seat that may scale, its size + 2: a worker line of 3 up to 5, a seat up to 3), and `max: off` none; **a one-time migration** run by the host agent at its first start on this build, finding no `scale.migrated` stamp in `settings.yml`: it writes `max: off` for every team it knows (a key under `teams:` in `settings.yml`, or a record carrying `team`) and stamps `scale.migrated: <date>` in the same write (the validator takes the key beside `live`); with the stamp present it writes nothing, so a team defined or first started later is on td-max, while existing teams stay unscaled until the person raises an **up to**, which replaces the `off` with that one ceiling; `org.with_settings` merges `max` onto `TeamDef`.
- **Refusals:** the write's refusals and `ao org check`'s set-aside lines for the manager, `plain` roles, a seat on `prs`, `every` or `swept`, a pooled seat, a worker line whose compiled lane is in no stage, and a name the definition does not give, each with its reason, said by `ao org check` and by the team's settings line (the one `ao team on-work` prints); `0` or the size is written as that line's ceiling and nothing clears a key; `ao team max <team> off` writes the team's `max: off`, replacing any written ceilings, and `ao team max <team> on` removes it, every line then on its default; on a live team `off` removes every extra and `on` writes the default ones held.
- **Records:** `create(held=True)` takes a record carrying `scale` (today an unattended seat's alone); `scale: {line}` is a record field carried by the launch record's keys (`LAUNCH_KEYS`) and every replay. `ao team start` and Apply's *starts* write `max − size` held extras per line or seat, each with the launch record the line's next kept member would get (its controllers among it: the manager, none on a person-led team), unattended, supervised; a seat's extra carries `seat: {trigger: asks}` (no `tags`, no `pool`), and the tagging seat's choice (`agent_tick`, `teamrun`) and the `--team` / `--repo` candidates (`asks_seat`, `agent_mail._question_seat` and `_pool_next`) pass over it. A flow switch or Apply rewrites every held extra's launch record.
- **Names:** `TeamDef.extra_names(name)` (a line's sized names past its size up to `max`; a seat's `<name>-2`…), read by `session_names`, the started names and the twice-named check; `resize`'s step-up (and `TeamDef.next_free` on the keep step alone) reads a record carrying `scale` as free for its line, and **+** adopts it (clears `scale`, keeps the record, fills it on a live team); **−** turns the line's highest-numbered kept member into its lowest extra (wound down on a live team, never killed, then held with `scale`), so `extra_names` and `session_names` give the same names before and after; under the default ceiling **+** also writes a held extra past the line's last and **−** also removes the highest-placed extra, so the line keeps size + 2; a run-only start (`ao team add` without `--keep`, the form's team pick) never adopts an extra and takes the first name past them.
- **Passed over:** `_work_replays` (rule 8 and a schedule's start) skips a record carrying `scale`, and every start of the team (`ao team start`, Apply's *starts*, rule 8's, a schedule's) first reconciles its held extras to the merged `max` (writes the missing, removes the surplus); `work.wound_down` and `work.finished` pass over a held extra as over a seat; `ao team stop` and a wind-down close a live extra and leave it held.
- `teamrun.set_max(call, org, team, name, n)`: writes the setting; on a live team writes the new extras held, or removes the highest-placed (a held one's record dropped, a live one wound down first, never killed, and dropped once closed; never a sit-out); `off` and `on` as above. `ao team max <team> <name> <n>` (person-only) and the dialog's field call it; `ao team list` and the dialog read the line as its range and extras, *<line> <size>–<max> +<live extras>* (TD-1548 draws the rest).

**Done when:** A team with `max: {grinder-ao: 5}` and size 3 started by `ao team start` has `grinder-ao-4` and `-5` as held records carrying `scale: {line: grinder-ao}`; `ao team max` and the dialog's field raise and lower them on a live team; each refused case is refused with its reason; a team with no `max` key writes its default ceilings' extras (size + 2 per line or seat: a seat's extras `-2` and `-3`); the migration writes `max: off` for every team then known (a `teams:` key or a record carrying `team`), and `ao team max` replaces it with one ceiling; the host cap's default reads 30 for 30 GB and 16 cores; **+** adopts `grinder-ao-4` (and under the default ceiling writes the next held extra); `ao team add` without `--keep` takes a name past the extras; a team restarted after its `max` changed while down has its held extras reconciled; `ao team max <team> off|on` writes and removes the `off`; at the cap a start first closes the longest-idle clean extra seat; a second run of the migration with the stamp present writes nothing; **−** on size 3 with extras `-4`, `-5` under the default ceiling leaves size 2 with extras `-3`, `-4`; a lowered `max` drops held extras' records; a seat's extra carries `seat: {trigger: asks}` with no `tags` and no `pool`, and is never the tagging seat nor a `--team` / `--repo` candidate; tests hold each.
