**Why:** #3281 (TD-1545 slice 5) added to `src/sessionorc/agent_remote.py` `_route_act`: `if held.scale and not params.get("scale"): made["scale"] = dict(held.scale)`, so a node's replica of a Resumed extra keeps its `scale` (the PR's history line says "a node's replica too"). Replacing the condition with `False` leaves every test passing: `tests/test_td_max_readings.py`, `test_link.py`, `test_seat_resume.py`, `test_closer.py`, `test_work_start.py`, `test_td_max_records.py` (75 and 49 passed). `test_a_resume_of_an_extra_keeps_its_scale` exercises only the local `rpc_create` path. A Resume routed to a node would write the launch record without `scale`, and the extra would come back a plain member.

Found by: test-audit-ao-1, audit of the 10 PRs merged to 2026-11-11.

**Fix:** Add a test that routes a Resume of a held extra to a node through `_route_act` and asserts the launch record written for the replica carries `scale`; revert the condition and watch it fail.
