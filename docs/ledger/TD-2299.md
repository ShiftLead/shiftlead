**Why:** Test audit of #4790 (TD-2069 slice 2, the tick's move down the login list). `_movable` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`) passes over a superseded, suspended, already moving, at-ceiling or scheduled record, and `_login_moves` clears a stale `moving` mark only on an ended session (design §4.9e *A session moves down the list*, *A mark is never left standing*).

Each change below, made alone at ff1c0ef8d, left tests/test_login_move.py green (27 passed). Each probe (a temporary test on a stage's session whose login is `limited`, with a usable login below it, removed after) answered as said. Unchanged, every probe left the same record in place and launched nothing.

- `and not s.suspended` removed from `_movable`: the suspended session is closed and launched again on `hookstub:second`, and its new record holds no suspension. This one was probed again at 7ff76f1ae with the mark as a person's suspension writes it, `{at, by, why}` (design §4.8a *An alarm's answers*).
- `and not s.superseded_by` removed: closed and launched again.
- `and not s.moving` removed: closed and launched again.
- `and not s.restart_ceiling` removed: closed and launched again.
- `, "scheduled"` removed from its state tuple: the record, its state set to `scheduled` by the probe, is gone from the host's sessions afterwards and nothing is launched. The probe's record had no launch record for a scheduled start.
- `s.state in ("exited", "closed") and` removed from the stale mark's clear: a live session with an eleven-minute-old mark is closed and launched again.

Two cases that look as if they hold this pass for another reason:

- `test_what_does_not_move[suspended]` sets `s.suspended = True`, where the field is a dict or None (`src/sessionorc/models.py`). With its guard removed the replay then raises, and the case's assertions (same record, same login, no mark, nothing launched) do not read the state, so a session the move has closed passes.
- `test_a_mark_with_no_live_session_ten_minutes_on_is_cleared[11-limited-False]` asserts `s.moving` on the record it held before the pass. A move leaves that old object marked, so a session that was moved passes as *the mark stands*.

With these, the changes of the entries filed beside this one and a few more this audit filed nothing on, made together at ff1c0ef8d, the whole suite passed (10475 passed, 26 skipped).
