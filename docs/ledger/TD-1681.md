## TD-1681: Focus draws Hand back on the person's own session in a team, which it never took from anyone

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ada89b0c16494d9d969b4215314a071c
**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py (mode_act, mode_place); design §4.5 (the slot rule), §4.5a (Focus (interactive, with controllers or a team), more ▾)

**Why:** Focus draws **Hand back** on the person's own session when it is in a team. Seen 2026-11-11 on `ao-shiftlead-td-1551`, a designer session Paul opened from a Queue card (record: `by_person: true`, `team: ao-grind`, `role: designer`, `unattended: false`, `mode_via: start`, `controllers: []`): the acts line's slot read Hand back (Paul: *why is there a "hand back" button on this focus terminal?*). `src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py` draws `mode_act = "Hand back"` for any interactive record with `controllers` or a `team` and never reads `by_person`. Design §4.5 *A taken-over member* already says a session the person starts in a team is interactive from its start by design and leaves it out of the member rule (`by_person`), but §4.5a's row *Focus (interactive, with controllers or a team)* and the slot rule (TD-964) do not. Pressing it would flip the person's own working session to unattended and hand it to the team's manager and the policies, which nobody asked for, and the label says it was taken from someone.

**Fix:** Make the mode toggle read `by_person`, as §4.5 *A taken-over member* already does. On an interactive record that is the person's own (`by_person`), whatever its team or controllers, the acts line's slot does not read Hand back: the toggle is *Switch to unattended* in more ▾, as on a session with no team and no controllers, and the slot follows its existing rule for an own session (Close session when the checklist passes, else nothing). Hand back stays for a member the person took over or resumed attended (not `by_person`). Update §4.5a's two rows (*Focus (interactive, with controllers or a team)* and the more ▾ row) and §4.5's slot sentence to say so; change `cards.py`'s `mode_act` and `mode_place`; test the card for a `by_person` team record (no Hand back, Switch to unattended in the menu) beside the existing taken-over member case. Whether *Switch to unattended* on the person's own team session should hand it to the team's manager is the design's to say in the same sentence: today it would, as Hand back does.

**Done when:** Focus shows no Hand back on a session the person started (`by_person`), in a team or not; a taken-over or attended-resumed member still shows it; §4.5 and §4.5a say so; a test holds both.
