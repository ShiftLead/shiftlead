## TD-1681: Focus draws Hand back on the person's own session in a team, which it never took from anyone

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ada89b0c16494d9d969b4215314a071c
**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py (mode_act, mode_place); design §4.5 (the slot rule), §4.5a (Focus (interactive, with controllers or a team), more ▾)

**Why:** Focus draws **Hand back** on the person's own session when it is in a team. Seen 2026-11-11 on `ao-shiftlead-td-1551`, a designer session Paul opened from a Queue card (record: `by_person: true`, `team: ao-grind`, `role: designer`, `unattended: false`, `mode_via: start`, `controllers: []`): the acts line's slot read Hand back (Paul: *why is there a "hand back" button on this focus terminal?*). `src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py` draws `mode_act = "Hand back"` for any interactive record with `controllers` or a `team` and never reads `by_person`. Design §4.5 *A taken-over member* already says a session the person starts in a team is interactive from its start by design and leaves it out of the member rule (`by_person`), but §4.5a's row *Focus (interactive, with controllers or a team)* and the slot rule (TD-964) do not. Pressing it would flip the person's own working session to unattended and hand it to the team's manager and the policies, which nobody asked for, and the label says it was taken from someone.

**Fix:** Draw Hand back only where there is someone to hand the session back to: a member the person took over, or a member a lead started that the person resumed attended. Not on a session the person started themselves, in a team or not; there the toggle is *Switch to unattended* in more ▾, as on a session with no team and no controllers, and the slot follows its existing rule for an own session (Close session when the checklist passes, else nothing).
- **The discriminator is not `by_person` alone.** §4.5 *Resume on a member confirms too* makes a member a lead started read as `by_person` once the person resumes it, and ends *Hand back reads `team` and `controllers`, not `by_person`*. So the build first settles what tells *the person started it* from *the person took over or resumed a lead's member*: a field the record already carries for who started it, if one is reliable (check `create`'s and resume's writes), else one written at `create` and kept through a resume. `mode_via` is display only and gates nothing (§4.5 *Every mode change is stamped*), so it is not the test.
- **Docs.** Update §4.5's slot sentence (TD-964) and the *Resume on a member* line that says Hand back reads `team` and `controllers`, and §4.5a's rows that state the Hand back rule: the card/Focus mode badge row, *Focus (interactive, with controllers or a team)*, and *more ▾*.
- **Code and test.** Change `cards.py`'s `mode_act` (and so `mode_place`); test the card for a session the person started in a team (no Hand back, Switch to unattended in the menu), a member the person took over, and a lead-started member the person resumed (both Hand back).
- Whether *Switch to unattended* on the person's own team session should hand it to the team's manager is the design's to say in the same sentence: today it would, as Hand back does.

**Done when:** Focus shows no Hand back on a session the person started, in a team or not; a member the person took over, or a lead-started member the person resumed, still shows it; §4.5 and §4.5a say so; a test holds all three.
