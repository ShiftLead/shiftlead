## TD-2058: A held build's blocker that waits on the design itself makes the design wait forever

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** c9fe958322d24fac9e805e66fd333847

**Why:** The techlead's read of #4327 (TD-2026 slice 1, m-a4bfa55394e0): `with_held` adds a held build's counted blocker to its design's open work even when that blocker's own `Blocked by:` chain reaches the design (build TD-2 blocked by TD-4, TD-4 blocked by the design). `open_work` drops such an id for the member itself ("its answer comes first"), so the design waits on TD-4 while TD-4 waits on the design, and the design's question is never drawn. **Location:** design §4.4 A design's question is asked once, on the design; src/sessionorc/queue.py with_held, open_work

**Fix:** 1. Design §4.4 *A design's question is asked once, on the design*: the `with_held` sentence says a held build's counted id whose own open `Blocked by:` chain reaches the design is not added to the design's (as `open_work` drops one whose chain reaches the member), with a history line in `docs/history/TD-2058.md`.
2. `with_held` in `src/sessionorc/queue.py` skips such an id: it reads the open entries' `Blocked by:` chain as `open_work` does (one `reaches` helper shared by both, `xref` passed for a `<repo>#TD-n`), so the three callers pass the resolver they already hold.
3. A test: build TD-002 blocked by TD-004, TD-004 blocked by the design TD-001: the design's `open_work` is empty and its question is drawn; and TD-004 blocked by nothing still makes the design wait.

**Done when:** A held build's blocker whose own chain reaches the design makes the design wait on nothing, as `open_work` reads a member's own, in the design and under a test.
