## TD-1302: Push notifications to the phone's home-screen app, beside Telegram, each device turned on or off by the person

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (phone-view; Paul, 2026-11-02)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open — to design.
**Location:** design §4.10 *Told on Telegram when nobody is looking*, §4.5 *Phone layout* (*Home screen*), §4.5a Settings page *You*, §4.5b (HTTPS), §5 `notify:`

**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-02, on TD-003's phone layout: *Push notifications are a good idea, obviously with the ability to turn them on/off for the user.* TD-003 adds a home-screen app (TD-1299's manifest) but left push notifications out. Today a phone hears that a session needs the person only through Telegram (TD-092), a third party's app, and only once Telegram is set up. A notification on shiftlead's own icon would open the row in one tap.

**Fix:** design it as a second channel of §4.10 *Told on Telegram when nobody is looking*, not a new set of rules:
1. **What is told, when, and what it says** are that section's: the same subset of *Needs you*, the same `NOTIFY_HOLD`, *looking*, `NOTIFY_BURST` and `NOTIFY_LATE`, and the same words from structured fields, never the text a session wrote (a push goes through the browser vendor's servers to a lock screen). The link is the row's address.
2. **On / off by the person, per device**: a switch on the Settings page's *You* card (*tell me on this device*), which asks the browser for permission and subscribes this browser, and turns it off again. Also an off for all devices, and whether Telegram and push both send or push replaces Telegram. Say where the subscriptions live (the home's state, not `settings.yml`) and what a revoked or expired subscription does.
3. **Mechanics**:
   - Web Push with VAPID keys, held as the Telegram token is (Doppler).
   - A service worker scoped `/`, which TD-1299's manifest does not yet have.
   - The home sends; a tap opens the row in the installed app.
   - Needs HTTPS: the `tailscale serve` road of §4.5b. Say what a plain-HTTP reach shows in place of the switch.
   - iOS delivers only to an app added to the Home Screen (16.4+). Say what the switch reads in a Safari tab.
4. Mockup of the Settings card and of a notification, then review rounds (cadence §4.8), then a build entry `Part of: TD-1302`.

**Done when** the design is merged and its build entry filed.
