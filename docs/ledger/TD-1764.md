## TD-1764: The design cost model: what share of the designer's spend on a design is applying a round's fixes, measured on five past designs

**Priority:** high
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-13
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 76d31066c863475f89d9b0afb1eebf99
**Part of:** TD-1722

**Why:** The cheaper way to design (TD-1711) moves the applying of a review round's fixes from the Fable designer to a Sonnet seat, design-coord (TD-1721). Whether that saves anything depends on a number nobody has: what share of the designer's spend on a design is applying fixes, as against drafting, waiting on reviews and bookkeeping. The plan's own review said so (docs/reviews/TD-1711.md, F22: the saving does not follow from the evidence), and the plan puts this measure first (docs/decisions/2026-11-11-fable-reset-plan.md, The order). Paul, 2026-11-12 (session fabplan): file it, and hold TD-1721 on it, so the second seat is not designed before it is known to pay.

**Location:** docs/decisions/ (a new document and its data), docs/reviews/, the designer sessions' transcripts (read only)

**Fix:** A measurement, read-only on everything it reads. Use Sonnet agents for the reading; commit numbers and method, never transcript text.

1. The sample: five past designs with a review record (`docs/reviews/TD-*.md`, Source: rounds) whose rounds have ended, spread over short, median and long, and none of TD-1557, TD-1569 or TD-1470 (TD-1722's replays).
2. For each, find the designer session's transcript turns from the design's first turn to its rounds' end (`agent-cadence run list_sessions.py`, `ao transcript`; the review record's dates and the design PR's commits bound it). Put each turn in one phase: drafting (before the first review is sent), applying fixes (after a review's report, until the next review is sent), sending and waiting on reviews, bookkeeping (the ledger, the board, the review record, the PR and its merge), other.
3. For each phase of each design: turns, tokens by kind (input, cache read, cache write, output), the cost by `ao spend`'s weights, and the median context a turn carries. Give the share of the design's spend that is applying fixes, for each design and pooled, and the spend for each design across days set beside what `ao spend --entries` reports for it.
4. Two estimates, their assumptions stated: what the same fix turns would cost on Sonnet from a fresh context holding the design and its review record; and what one question to Fable would cost in a fresh session given a span and the rules it cites, and in the designer's own session at its context then.
5. Write it as `docs/decisions/<date>-design-cost-model.md` with its script and data beside it, as docs/decisions/2026-11-11-design-review-findings/ does, a row in AGENTS.md's documentation map, and a `ledger.py note` with the headline numbers on TD-1721 and on TD-1722.
6. If applying fixes is under a quarter of a design's spend pooled over the sample (a line proposed here, Paul's to move), do not let TD-1721 start on this entry's close: set TD-1721 `Blocked by: decision (paul)` with a board decide item that gives the numbers and asks whether design-coord is still worth designing.

**Done when:** Done when the document gives, for five designs, the share of the designer's spend that is applying fixes and the two estimates, a second reader has re-derived its numbers from its data, TD-1721 and TD-1722 carry the headline numbers, and TD-1721 is either free to start or held on Paul's decision as step 6 says.
