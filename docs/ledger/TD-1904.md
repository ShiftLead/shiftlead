## TD-1904: The usage poll throws away the endpoint's answers whose shape lacks the five-hour window, and logs them only as no known shape

**Priority:** high
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3350b9d0fff24e629cca655ee2ed8886

**Why:** The usage poll (design §4.4 *Usage*) is the gate's fallback for an account whose sessions are not reporting. On kmaster it logs `error (was …) — a body of no known shape` as often as it logs `rate_limited`: eight changes of reason between 2026-11-15 13:30 and 2026-11-16 17:00 local, every `error` among them that detail, and no change back to `ok` from the poll itself (each `was ok` follows a host-agent restart, whose held reading came from reports).

That detail is the one failure the adapter does not name. `ClaudeCodeAdapter.usage` returns `parse_usage(...)` for any HTTP 200, and `parse_usage` answers `None` unless the body carries both `five_hour` and `seven_day` with a number in each; `usage_for` turns `None` into `error` with the fixed words *a body of no known shape*. So a 200 that the endpoint answers in a shape the parser does not expect is logged with no trace of what it held.

The likely shape is a missing or `null` `five_hour`: the gate's reading of `claude-code:paul` on 2026-11-17 00:07 UTC (`ao gate --json`) carries a `week` window only, from the sessions' own reports, so the account seems to have no five-hour window at present. If so, every successful answer of the endpoint is thrown away, and the poll never fills a reporting gap. This was found on TD-1589's live check, whose Done when needs the poll's errors to log their cause.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/adapters/claude_code/__init__.py (parse_usage, usage_for); design §4.4 Usage

**Fix:** 1. In `src/shiftlead/adapters/claude_code/__init__.py`, make `parse_usage` read each window on its own: a `five_hour` or `seven_day` that is missing, `null` or carries no number is left out, as a per-model window already is; the result is `None` only when no window at all could be read.
2. When the body still yields no window, `usage_for`'s `detail` names the body's top-level keys and the type of `five_hour` and `seven_day` (never their values beyond that), so the next look reads the shape from the journal.
3. Check what a reading with no `5h` window does downstream: the chip, the gate's labels and reserves, the projection and `limited`'s reset (design §4.4 *Usage*, §6 *Usage gate*); state in §4.4 that a window the endpoint leaves out is absent, not an error.
4. Tests: a body with `five_hour: null`, one with no `five_hour`, and one with neither window (its detail names the keys).

**Done when:** Done when an endpoint answer with any window parses to a reading, an answer with none logs its keys as the cause, and the tests above pass; live, the journal shows no `a body of no known shape` after the fix is promoted, and TD-1589's live check is read again.
