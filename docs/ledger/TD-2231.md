## TD-2231: An early question from a session is counted as needing you while its card shows it grey and uncounted

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d9b1699150904876abd54334fd6b7d24

**Why:** Audit of #4691 (TD-2184 slice 3). The Queue draws a session's lifting question about an entry asked early (an open `ask --lift`) grey and on no card pill, while the top bar and the Queue tab still count it. The design says neither half.

- Design §4.4 *A question whose entry only waits is asked early* says of that message only that it is "drawn whole with the early line where it is asked early". Its count rule names a line alone: "An open undecided `decide` line all of whose covered members are asked early is drawn early in the same way, in their place, and counts in no **Needs you (n)**, top bar or rail".
- Design §4.5 screen 12 *A question asked early is no want while its entry waits*, which says what goes in the grey section, names two things: "a `decision (paul)` entry whose row reads *asked early* ... or an open undecided line covering only such entries, in their place", and of them "It is counted on no pill and puts the card in no **Needs you (n)**". The message is not named.
- Screen 12 also says "the Queue tab counts only the counted rows drawn on cards".
- Code: `rewrite` in `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` sends every early item to the grey section (`if e.get("early") and want in WANTS:`), the message among them; `inbox_sections` in `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` leaves out only a board line (`counted = [e for e in out["needs"] if not (e.get("early") and e.get("row") == "board")]`).
- The choice is written in the body of PR #4691 alone: "An early `ask --lift` mail stays counted in the top bar (an open ask holds its slot, §4.10); on the Queue it is grey with no pill."

Probe at a5f15d01c, with the fixtures of `tests/test_queue_asked_early_items.py`, one early lifting question through `inbox_sections`, `attach` and `card_wants`:

    count (Needs you (n)): 1  queue_n: 1
    card item want: [('answered', True)]  pill n: {'decide': 0, 'do': 0, 'read': 0, 'early': 1}

So on an In progress card the person reads *Needs you (1)* and a 1 on the Queue tab, with no card edge or pill saying where. A lifting question held as *waiting, then yours* reads the same counts in the same probe (TD-1926's, older than this PR), and the design is as silent about it.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py, src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py; docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md, docs/design/4.5-ui.md

**Fix:** Decide whether an open lifting question that is asked early (and one *waiting, then yours*) is counted in the top bar, **Needs you (n)** and the Queue tab, and write the answer in §4.4 and screen 12, naming the message among what the grey section holds. The reason given in #4691 for counting it (an open ask holds its slot, §4.10) goes in if it stands. File the build if the counts change.

**Done when:** §4.4 and §4.5 screen 12 say where a lifting question about a waiting entry is drawn and which counts read it, and a build entry is filed or the code already matches.
