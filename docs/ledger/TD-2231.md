## TD-2231: An early question from a session counts on the Queue tab while its card shows it grey with no count

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d9b1699150904876abd54334fd6b7d24

**Why:** Audit of #4691 (TD-2184 slice 3). The Queue draws a session's lifting question about an entry asked early (an open `ask --lift`) grey and on no card pill, while the Queue tab still counts it. The design's general rules count an open question; its rules for an early question do not say it is drawn grey with no pill, or whether it is an exception to the count.

- Design §4.5 screen 6 counts in **Needs you**, and so in the Queue tab's number for a row drawn on a card, "an open `ask` to the person".
- Design §4.4 *A question whose entry only waits is asked early* says of the message only that it is "drawn whole with the early line where it is asked early". The exception it writes names a line alone: "An open undecided `decide` line all of whose covered members are asked early is drawn early in the same way, in their place, and counts in no **Needs you (n)**, top bar or rail".
- Design §4.5 screen 12 *A question asked early is no want while its entry waits*, which says what goes in the grey section, names two things: "a `decision (paul)` entry whose row reads *asked early* ... or an open undecided line covering only such entries, in their place", and of them "It is counted on no pill and puts the card in no **Needs you (n)**". The message is not named.
- Code: `rewrite` in `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` sends every early item to the grey section (`if e.get("early") and want in WANTS:`), the message among them; `inbox_sections` in `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` takes out of the count only a board line (`counted = [e for e in out["needs"] if not (e.get("early") and e.get("row") == "board")]`).
- The choice is written in the body of PR #4691: "An early `ask --lift` mail stays counted in the top bar (an open ask holds its slot, §4.10); on the Queue it is grey with no pill." No design section says it.

Probe at a5f15d01c, with the fixtures of `tests/test_queue_asked_early_items.py`, one early lifting question through `inbox_sections`, `attach` and `card_wants` (`count` is the two tabs' numbers together, `queue_n` the Queue tab's):

    count: 1  queue_n: 1
    card item want: [('answered', True)]  pill n: {'decide': 0, 'do': 0, 'read': 0, 'early': 1}

So the person reads a 1 on the Queue tab, and on an In progress card no amber edge and no pill says where; the Queue's **Needs you (n)** switch, which counts cards by their status, does not count it. A lifting question held as *waiting, then yours* gives the same two counts in the same probe (TD-1926's, older than this PR), and no rule for it names the count either.

**Design PR:** #4753

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py, src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py; docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md, docs/design/4.5-ui.md

**Fix:** Decide whether an open lifting question that is asked early (and one *waiting, then yours*) is counted in the Queue tab's number while its card shows it grey with no pill, and write the answer in §4.4 and screen 12, naming the message among what the grey section holds. The reason given in #4691 for counting it (an open ask holds its slot, §4.10) goes in if it stands. File the build if the count or the drawing changes.

**Done when:** §4.4 and §4.5 screen 12 say where a lifting question about a waiting entry is drawn and whether the Queue tab counts it, and a build entry is filed or the code already matches.
