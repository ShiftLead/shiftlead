## TD-2088: Two teams of one repo on one flow would show each other's work, because a stage record names no team

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 9e46df089d0c49b78f38b5f23a890220
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** Design §4.9d *What a team is* calls the team a field of every stage record, but §4.4's list of a record's fields and `new_record` have no such field. `team_show` therefore matches a stage's entries by flow name, so two teams of one repo on one flow would show each other's entries. No repo runs two teams on a format-3 flow yet, so nothing reads wrongly today. Found by the techlead's read of TD-2060's second slice (#4373).

**Fix:** Settle the contradiction in the design first: either §4.4's field list and `new_record` gain the team, or §4.9d stops saying a record carries it and says how two teams of one repo on one flow keep their entries apart. Then build that, and have `team_show` match a stage's entries by it.

**Done when:** §4.4 and §4.9d say the same thing about a record's team, and on a fixture of two teams of one repo on one flow, each team's `ao team show` lists only its own entries.
