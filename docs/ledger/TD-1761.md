**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (reread, AO.reopenMenus), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue.html, src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css, src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; design §4.5 screen 12 *The page holds still*

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 12 *The page holds still* on the Queue page, and the §4.5a rows *Queue page: the bar, Refresh*, *Queue card: held* and *Queue card: gone — refresh*:
- The page keeps the drawn copy and the latest reading. A re-read patches values in place by key (`data-card`, `data-qid`, `data-msg`) and never re-marks `qoff` on a patch. It counts the difference between the two copies in the bar, by the design's parts, order and once-per-card rule, as a `role="status"` line.
- An item or row the reading no longer holds keeps its place and height, with *gone — refresh* in place of its controls. An item the person's own press answered is never *gone*: its result is drawn at once, as today's keep draws it, and the re-read confirms it. TD-1436's keep is restated for a patch (`AO.qcardReapply`, `restoreRowErrs` adapted, not dropped).
- **Refresh** draws the latest reading whole, with no request. The first card in view keeps its screen position, and the top of the page stays at the top. Cards in view slide in 200 ms (FLIP: First, Last, Invert, Play), at once under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and presses during the slide are ignored. Focus goes to the next header if its card left.
- Load, coming back from a card's page (the Queue's side of that is TD-1763's), and the reload on the first served read of a page loaded unserved (`location.reload()` in `reread`) draw whole. A rank move does not: `moveCard` keeps placing the card on the drawn copy at once (and now on the stored reading too) and releases nothing; its neighbours follow the rank-move bullet below. Picks, find and a group's fold stay in the browser on the drawn copy and release nothing.
- Inside a card: an item moving between blocks, items or rows re-ordering, and a block appearing or emptying are place changes (*moved*); edge colour, status classes and pill follow the patched status; group heading counts are recounted from the drawn cards.
- A refused press on the Queue (409, a permission's `call` check) draws its refusal and ends its card's hold.
- *gone* is judged per drawn block and withdrawn when a later reading holds the item there again; an own press's consequences inside its card (item leaving, block count, pill, Read count) draw at once and are outside the difference; a lapsed keep puts the item back as the press's result.
- A failed or unserved re-read (throws; 200 with no `#qcounts`) never replaces the latest reading; the bar reads *not live — last read <age>*. A re-read whose request left before the last accepted rank move is dropped. `reread` keeps skipping while a card is `.dragging`.
- The find submit's one request for `#qfindmiss` stays and draws that note alone; where the id is on a card not drawn, the note reads *TD-n is new — Refresh* (on find's Enter and on a `/queue#TD-n` alike).
- The Repo page's board (its `data-scope`, its own `reread`) is unchanged: neighbours from the latest reading apply only where the page keeps one; `moveCard` and `placeCards` keep the board's path.
- Recently completed's window replaces only that group's lines in both copies and releases nothing; its lines (`data-key`) and `.qother` lines (given a key, the hold's ref; today only `data-other`) patch by key, and their arrivals and age-outs are that group's own places, drawn by Refresh and not counted.
- *gone* (item or row) is drawn at once even on a held card. A place is relative order among cards in both copies; Up next numbers (`.qplace`/`.qpn`, per repo) and group heading counts (today server-drawn: `data-of`, `c.places`) are recounted from the drawn order — new code.
- Factor the patch, bar and hold code out of the Queue's closure in `app.js` so TD-1762's card page reuses it.
- Same card: a **re-top** — a drawn card whose id is no card's in the reading matches the reading's card holding any of its open members (`data-ids`) as its top or a row, when no drawn card has that card's id; one-to-many and many-to-one resolve greedily, best pair first (most shared open members, then best key), each card used once (the others *new*, *joined* or left). The header is found by the card's key; the new top's drawn row is neither *gone* nor patched and waits for Refresh (*moved*). Keys — `data-card`, `data-ids`, the `data-upnext` entry (Up next cards), anchors, `data-fold` keys, the in-page fold sets' entries keyed by the old card id (`foldsOpen`/`foldsShut`, prefix = old id), the stored `qfold` entries, the header's `data-qid`, the Start/Open session buttons' `data-card` (their `data-ref`/`data-repo` patch as values and wait; the road's card check refuses a stale one), and **Open card ›**'s href — are rewritten at once even on a held card; only shown words and pills wait. A *done* or *joined* card's items stay drawn, each *gone* where no longer held in its block. A **join** — the reading's card is itself drawn — is a place change (*moved* on the card joined, *done* for the one that left). Only an unmatched card, or one that has joined, counts *done*.
- An item's or row's controls and their data (`call`, answers, *held by* → **Start session**) patch as values under the same holds.
- A row whose entry was archived keeps its place and height marked *done*, its controls hidden, its move into **✓ n done** a *moved* place change; *done* is withdrawn as *gone* is; the fold's *n* is recounted from the drawn rows; *gone* on a row with no controls sits at its line's end.
- Rank moves: *drawn* is the drawn `data-upnext` order (hidden cards included); after A sends `prev` A and its latest successor, before B sends `next` B and its latest predecessor; a drop on a card's upper half is before it, lower half after; ↑/↓ are after the drawn card that will precede it (before the first at the top); ⤒ is before the first; a move that leaves the card where it is sends nothing; the other neighbour anchors if one left, else nothing is sent (*the order changed — Refresh first*); a refusal puts that one card back in both copies. Drop re-reads that left before the last move was sent.
- Refresh closes open menus first. A hidden-page draw places a held card's node where the reading puts it (else where it was). `visibilitychange` to visible re-reads at once and draws as a hidden page's re-read (a card with a menu or box open excepted; no menu closed), unless a hold began before the reading arrived, when it is an ordinary visible re-read.
- Dismiss all sends drawn rows that are not *gone*; its count is theirs. The window may draw a *done* card twice until Refresh (accepted).
- The bar is never held; *n new asks* counts asks, the other parts cards.
- `AO.refreshInboxPage = reread` (a press on an Inbox-drawn item) and the `queue` event's 1.5 s debounce feed the patch path, not the swap.
- *held*: a mouse pointer over the card (on touch, `pointerdown` until the press ends), `:focus-visible` inside it, or a menu or box open on it. Its changing values wait, but a press's own result on that card is never held. The mark is drawn only while something waits, in reserved width, and is not announced. Browser clocks keep ticking.
- While `document.hidden`, pointer, touch and keyboard-focus holds end (focus back to the same card's header after the draw) and a re-read draws whole, except a card with a menu or box open.
- **Until TD-1763 lands**, the Queue still draws boxes: an open **Answer…**, form or *why?* box holds its card as an open menu does (its own item waits for *gone* until the box closes or its send is refused, as on the card's page), and **Refresh** cannot be pressed while it is open, so close-and-keep cannot fire on the Queue.
- Only `AO.reopenMenus` and the swap-then-patch path of a plain re-read go; every whole draw (Refresh, load, the window, coming back, a hidden page) still swaps the lists and keeps the fold (`opened` `.qdline` bodies, `AO.reopenFolds`), row-error (`restoreRowErrs`) and keep (`AO.qcardReapply`) restores.
