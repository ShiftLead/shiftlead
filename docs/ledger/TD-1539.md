**Why:** TD-1528's design (§4.4 *The boards* `to`, *The card's status* — *A closed decided line*): a bought round's end-of-rounds line is closed *see TD-n: …* by the sessions that carry it, which the reader's *carried to* parse misses, so its held entries read *held until your decision* while the round they bought runs (TD-1470's card, Paul's screenshot, m-87c0a64dfefc). The reader takes *see TD-m* as the same words.

**Fix:** Build §4.4 *The boards* (`to`) and *The card's status* (*A closed decided line*) as TD-1528 widened them:
- `src/sessionorc/workorders.py` (held path, its PR waits for its reader): `CARRIED_RE` takes a leading *see* as well as *carried to* (either case of the first letter, as the design's *The boards* says, after the same leading space and dashes), the ids joined as today and ending at the first other word, dash, colon or sentence end — *see TD-1525: the bought sixth round, the designer's* is `["TD-1525"]`, *see TD-9 — carried to TD-4* is `["TD-9"]`, *See TD-9* and *— see TD-9* are `["TD-9"]`, *see TD-5, TD-6 and TD-7* is `["TD-5", "TD-6", "TD-7"]`, *seen TD-9*, *done, see TD-9* and *see the PR* are `[]`. The Inbox foot and the row word (*then carried to TD-m*, `inbox_row.html`) are built from `to`, never the reason's text, so a *see* close reads *carried to TD-m* there with no template change. The comment over it and `carried_to`'s docstring say both forms. Nothing else in the hold changes: the *filed for the answer* test (`Added:` on or after the line's `Decided:`, a day's slack) still decides whether the named entry holds, so a *see TD-m* pointing at an older, unrelated entry holds nothing.
- `src/sessionorc/queue.py`'s comment at the carried hold (~739) names both forms.
- **Tests** (`tests/test_board_carried.py`, the carried hold's own, and `tests/test_workorders.py`): `carried_to` on the cases above; a closed decided line `Context: TD-n decision (paul). Parts: TD-a.` closed *see TD-m: the bought round*, TD-m open, `Part of` TD-n and `Added:` on the `Decided:` day, holds TD-n and TD-a as *answered — waiting on TD-m* (its holder's name where a live record claims it), with no *held until your decision* item on the card; the same with TD-m archived returns the hold to the person; *see TD-k* naming an open entry added before the line's `Decided:` holds nothing.

**Done when:** A closed end-of-rounds line whose reason begins *see TD-m* holds its covered entries as *answered — waiting on <holder>* (or *on TD-m*) while TD-m, filed for the answer, is open, exactly as *carried to TD-m* does; the tests pass.
