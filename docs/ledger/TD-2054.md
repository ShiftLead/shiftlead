**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *The Flows page* and the Flows page rows of §4.5a, from `docs/mockups/reviews/td1954-src/prototype.html`: the flow list, Copy, Delete and Save through `flow_save`, the three tabs, the kinds of stage, a stage's fields, each outcome's Next and limit, Audits, Support, Walk an entry and Does it flow?; the help texts for the new rows. It also owns what the page does to entries already in a flow (design §4.9c *A switch or a rename re-keys the records*): a saved rename of a stage re-keys the open stage records in that stage in the same save, and deleting a stage or changing its kind is refused while entries are in it.

**Done when:** A person copies a built-in, changes it and saves it, and the saved file passes `ao flow check`; Save is refused while the check fails; a repo's flow is read-only with The file to copy. A move to the person can offer answers that each name a stage, which the prototype lacks. `flow_save` is on the person-only list and the test that counts that list is updated. Renaming a stage that holds entries leaves each of their records on the new key after the save; deleting that stage, or changing its kind, is refused naming the entries.
