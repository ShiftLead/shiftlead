**Owner:** grinder

**Status:** Open

**Why:** #4687 (TD-2184 slice 2) marks a row *asked early* only where no earlier word stands (docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md, *A question whose entry only waits is asked early*: *with you in <name>*, a press's mark, an *answered — …* and the rest keep theirs), and leaves an early row out of the rail's *Later* count. The conditions that do this have no test.

Over `tests/test_queue*.py`, `tests/test_go_now_*.py` and `tests/test_ui_inbox*.py` (637 passed at 44e59d8dd), each change below left every test green:

- `reading` in src/sessionorc/queue.py: dropping `not mine` from `early = … if needs and not mine and not answered and not held_word`, and separately `not answered`.
- `ledger_for_you` in src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py: dropping from `early_of`'s condition `not got_q`, separately `applying is None`, and separately the test for the person's own session holding the entry.
- `rail_rows` in the same file: deleting `and not e.get("early")`.

Probes of real readings passed on the unchanged code and failed with the line removed: an entry the person's own session holds then reads *asked early* in place of *with you*; one with an answered board line or mail answer reads it in place of *answered — …*; the *Later* row is marked early while a press is landing or a mail answer stands; and `rail_rows` returns the early row, so the rail counts it.

With TD-2233 to TD-2238's reversions applied together, `pytest -n 8` over the whole suite passed at 44e59d8dd (10197 passed, 24 skipped), and with the same edits on de7a00b47 (after #4691) the Queue, Go now, Inbox UI, profile, switch-over, settings, launch, Gemini and Grok modules passed (1093 passed). In that run the `reading` and `early_of` conditions were dropped together.

**Location:** tests/test_queue_asked_early.py

**Fix:** In tests/test_queue_asked_early.py, for an entry that would otherwise be asked early: one test each for the person's own session on it, a mail answer, an answered board line and a press landing, asserting the Queue row and the *Later* row keep their own word and are not `early`; and one asserting `rail_rows` leaves an early row out.

**Done when:** Dropping any one of those conditions, or the `rail_rows` line, fails a test.
