## TD-1529: A td-max flow: a team's grinders and seats scale with the work waiting, for the most throughput

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-07 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by Claude Code session td-max)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e21b1e98b7204cc0a52bad996ffed833
**Location:** design §4.9 *A team's size* (TD-1149), §4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool* (TD-1476) and the techlead seat, §4.9c flows, §5 `teams.<team>.size`, §6 rules 3 (`FILL_CEILING`), 6 and 8; src/shiftlead/org.py (`TeamDef.size`, `next_free`), src/shiftlead/teams.py, src/shiftlead/teamrun.py

**Why:** A person who wants the most work done per hour is held today by a team's fixed headcount. A team runs the sessions its member lines and their `size` say (design §4.9 *A team's size*, TD-1149): when every grinder holds a lease and the ledger still has pickable entries, those entries wait, and the line runs the same number whether its lanes are full or empty (a team out of work winds down whole, §4.9a). The techlead seat is one session filled on call (rule 3, `FILL_CEILING`): a second question that arrives while it is at work queues behind the first. Paul wants a *td-max* flow, for people who want to maximise throughput, where the team's seats load-balance — *if the techlead is busy, and another item is coming to it, a new techlead seat would be spun up instead. If the grinders are busy and there is work that can be picked, another grinder would spin up. Of course they would spin down when work was no longer available* (Paul, 2026-11-07).

This is not TD-1476's ordered pool (§4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool*), which routes a question past a member that cannot answer and runs one member at a time, and which refuses `pool:` on `techlead:` and `manager:`: td-max adds capacity while every member is busy.

**Fix:** Design first. Settle:
- what *td-max* is: a §4.9c flow the team lists and the person picks as its current flow, a team setting beside `size` (§5 `teams.<team>.size`), or a mode of both, and where the person turns it on;
- when a grinder line scales up — every live member of the line holds a lease and pickable work in its lane is left over (rule 6's reading, `ao repo`'s pickable list) — and when it scales down: a member out of work (`none`) closed past the line's floor, the names it takes and gives back (`TeamDef.next_free`, §4.9 *A team's size*);
- when a seat scales up — a question or ask arrives while each of its sessions is at work — and down: an extra idle with nothing waiting; how an added techlead shares the seat's mail, rank and record with TD-1476's pool members, and whether a pool and a load-balanced seat are one mechanism;
- the bounds: a floor and a ceiling per line or seat, and how they sit with `FILL_CEILING` (§6 rule 3) and `RESTART_CEILING` (rule 1), the host's capacity, and the person's usage window, so a burst of work never starts sessions without limit;
- what the person sees on the team card, Sessions page and `ao team list` when a line or seat has grown past its size, and why.

**Done when:** A design round names what td-max is, its scale-up and scale-down rules for grinder lines and seats, their bounds and what the person sees, and files its builds with `Part of: TD-1529`; a team on td-max then starts another grinder when pickable work waits on busy grinders and another techlead when a question waits on a busy one, and closes them when the work runs out.
