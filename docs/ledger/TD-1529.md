## TD-1529: A td-max flow: a team's grinders and seats scale with the work waiting, for the most throughput

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-07 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by Claude Code session td-max)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e21b1e98b7204cc0a52bad996ffed833
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Location:** design §4.9 *A team's size* (TD-1149), §4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool* (TD-1476) and the techlead seat, §4.9c flows, §5 `teams.<team>.size`, §6 rules 3 (`FILL_CEILING`), 6 and 8; src/shiftlead/org.py (`TeamDef.size`, `next_free`), src/shiftlead/teams.py, src/shiftlead/teamrun.py

**Why:** A person who wants the most work done per hour is held today by a team's fixed headcount. A team runs the sessions its member lines and their `size` say (design §4.9 *A team's size*, TD-1149): when every grinder holds a lease and the ledger still has pickable entries, those entries wait, and the line runs the same number whether its lanes are full or empty (a team out of work winds down whole, §4.9a). The techlead seat is one session filled on call (rule 3, `FILL_CEILING`): a second question that arrives while it is at work queues behind the first. Paul wants a *td-max* flow, for people who want to maximise throughput, where the team's seats load-balance — *if the techlead is busy, and another item is coming to it, a new techlead seat would be spun up instead. If the grinders are busy and there is work that can be picked, another grinder would spin up. Of course they would spin down when work was no longer available* (Paul, 2026-11-07). The grinder and the techlead are his examples, not the scope: the design covers every member line and every seat a team can declare (Paul, 2026-11-07: *it should consider other seats as well*).

This is not TD-1476's ordered pool (§4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool*), which routes a question past a member that cannot answer and runs one member at a time, and which refuses `pool:` on `techlead:` and `manager:`: td-max adds capacity while every member is busy.

**Ask:** Go once you and the designer agree td-max's shape (floor, ceiling, what triggers scaling, what the card says) and its review rounds end?

**Design PR:** #3220

**Fix:** Design first. Settle, for **every** kind of member a team can declare, not only the grinder and the techlead (§4.9c: a role's kind is `worker`, `seat`, `manager` or `plain`):
- what *td-max* is: a §4.9c flow the team lists and the person picks as its current flow, a team setting beside `size` (§5 `teams.<team>.size`), or a mode of both, and where the person turns it on;
- **worker lines** — every role that gives a lane (grinder, hunter, a designer line, any repo's own): when a line scales up — every live member holds a lease and pickable work in its lane is left over (rule 6's reading, `ao repo`'s pickable list) — and when it scales down: a member out of work (`none`) closed past the line's floor, the names it takes and gives back (`TeamDef.next_free`, §4.9 *A team's size*);
- **seats** — the `techlead:` seat and every `seats:` entry (an `asks` seat, one holding a review stage): when one scales up — a question, ask or review arrives while each of its sessions is at work — and down: an extra idle with nothing waiting; how an added session shares the seat's mail, rank and record with TD-1476's pool members, and whether a pool and a load-balanced seat are one mechanism; and how a scaled seat holds a review stage, which §4.9c gives to *one seat of its role* and from which §4.9b refuses a pooled seat (a reader's read is its stage's seat's);
- **the manager and `plain` roles**, and the org's director seat (§4.9b, TD-636): whether each scales, or stays one, and why;
- the bounds: a floor and a ceiling per line or seat, and how they sit with `FILL_CEILING` (§6 rule 3) and `RESTART_CEILING` (rule 1), the host's capacity, and the person's usage window, so a burst of work never starts sessions without limit;
- what the person sees on the team card, Sessions page and `ao team list` when a line or seat has grown past its size, and why.

**Done when:** A design round names what td-max is, its scale-up and scale-down rules for every worker line and every seat (the techlead, `asks` seats and review-stage seats among them), whether the manager, `plain` roles and the director seat scale, their bounds and what the person sees, and files its builds with `Part of: TD-1529`; a team on td-max then starts another member of any line or seat when work waits on all of its busy members, and closes them when the work runs out.
