## TD-1791: A card's page loses the address's ring at its first re-read

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-13
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 8c828275adb949cc846acb134423ff22

**Why:** A card's page opened at an item (`/card/<id>?row=…&item=…`: Telegram's link, a push, and since TD-1763 every Queue preview's **Answer on its card ›**) scrolls to the item and rings it in `showAddress` at load, but the page's first re-read (`reread()` → `patch()`), which follows at once, replaces the item's node and the ring with it: on a scratch home the ring was on the page with the re-read held, and gone 300 ms after load without the hold (found while checking TD-1763's UI). §4.5 screen 12 *Addresses* says the address "rings the item".

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (AO.cardPage: showAddress, patch)

**Fix:** Keep the address's ring across the card page's patch, as the Queue's whole draw carries `qring` onto a replaced node (`app.js`, the class list copied in the Queue's patch): in `AO.cardPage`'s patch (and `drawWhole`), copy `qring` from a replaced unit to its new node, or re-run the ring after the first read. Test it under node with the card page's small DOM (`test_ui_card_page_diff.py`'s fixtures).

**Done when:** A card's page opened at an item keeps the item ringed after its first re-read; a test holds it. Merged.
