## TD-1380: One board decide line that covers several entries is one Queue item, with a holds line, and none of the covered entries draws its own block

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1378's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1368
**Part of:** TD-1378
**Location:** design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs* (*One line is one item, however many entries it covers*), *What is attached to a card*, screen 11 *For you*; §4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*. Code: `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`board_rows`, which builds the board row the Queue and the Repo page read: it copies `refs` and `text` and carries no `parts`), `src/sessionorc/workorders.py` (`orders`, which drops `parts` too), `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`CONTEXT`, `item_home`, `asked_entries` as TD-1368 widens it, `attach`), `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the `board()` macro: its `e.card` block draws a board item on a Queue card, its `e.lead` branch on the Repo page, which renders no card mode); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`inbox_sections`, which calls `item_home` with its `cards` index; `ledger_for_you`, which builds the open entries' map), `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (the Repo page's `fy_boards`) and its board template.

**Why:** an end-of-rounds `decide` line holds its design and every build it filed, so it covers several entries (§4.4 *The card's status*, TD-1364), and the person should see and answer it once. On 2026-11-04 TD-1286's line was drawn as six Decide items with one question (TD-1378's Why). TD-1368 widens the reading so a covered entry's own block gives way to the line on the line's own card; this entry makes the line one item on the whole page and says what it holds.

**Fix:** 1. **The order of what a line covers.** The board row carries the record's `parts` (`board_rows` copies it beside `refs` for every open line, decided or not; the reading's items already carry it from ledgerboard, `item.parts`), and one reader in `queue.py`, `covered(e, entries)` — `e` the board row, `entries` the open entries by qid with their `Part of` and `Blocked by:` as TD-1368 passes them to `answered_hold` — gives an open board line's covered entries as an ordered list, not a set: its `Context:` refs in the order written, gate words read past (a bare `TD-n` the line's own repo's, every ref, as TD-1368 covers them; `covered()` returns qualified ids only, and `item_home`'s bare fallback (another repo's id unique on a card) stays for its home lookup of the first ref alone, as today), then its `Parts:` ids in the order written (v2.3's `parse_parts`, the record's `parts`; a bare id the line's own board's repo's, as a bare `Context:` ref is), then each open member that carries `decision (<person>)` (the home's person, as `answered_hold` reads the gate) and is directly `Part of` one of the `Context:` refs' entries (§4.4 *The card's status*: the gate is the member's, not its parent's), ref by ref in the `Context:`'s order and by number within each as the ledger orders them (TD-9 before TD-10); each once. `item_home` and `asked_entries` gain the `entries` argument, and their callers pass it: `inbox_sections` (which calls `item_home(e, cards)` with its `cards` keyword, None for the snoozed section), `split_later(…, entries)` (which calls `item_home` itself) and its three callers in `app.py`, and the Repo route the repo's for `fy_boards`; **Where `entries` comes from**: `ledger_for_you` (as TD-1368 widens it) builds the map once from its repo facts — from `r["ledger"]["entries"]` for every repo, before its per-repo `continue` on a repo with no for-you entry and ahead of its for-you and `decided` filters, each entry's `part_of` and `blocked_by` — and `h.ledger_view` returns it beside its rows — one repos read per page load, as today; `card_mail` and the Inbox route read `ledger_view` before `inbox_sections` (today after) and pass it on; the Org route's and the top bar's counts poll's `inbox_sections` read no ledger and place by `Context:` and `Parts:` alone (their counts may place a `Part of`-only line differently, accepted); the Inbox JSON read already reads `ledger_view` first; the inbox-entry route passes no `cards` and is unchanged; the Repo route builds it from its `r["ledger"]["entries"]` (each entry's `part_of` and `blocked_by`, as the repo facts carry them — `part_of_raw` is ledgerboard's raw read alone). With `entries` None (no repo facts, or the agent down) `covered()` gives the `Context:` refs and `Parts:` ids alone, no `Part of` members, and *holds* lists those as read, less the home row (no open test then). `item_home`, `asked_entries` (TD-1368's widened set is this list's) and the Repo page's `fy_boards` all read it, and none reads `Parts:` out of the line's text: today `CONTEXT` stops only at `Due:`, `Answers:`, `Decided:` or `Closed:`, so on the Queue a `Parts:` line's ids are read as `Context:` refs by accident, while the Repo page's `refs` (`item_refs`) stop before `Parts:`; `CONTEXT` takes ledgerboard's stop list (`CONTEXT_RE`: `Parts`, `Due`, `Hide until`, `Answers`, `Lifts`, `Decided`, `Closed`) and keeps its own `re.S` flag.
2. **One home.** `item_home` places a board line on the card of the first entry in that list that the index holds (the first ref's bare fallback tried only once no id of the list is in the index) (today: its `Context:`'s first ref alone, so a line whose parent sits on no open card fell to the Inbox while its parts were on one). `item_home` returns the list on its home dict as `covers`, beside `mentions`, carried on the row as `on_card` and `mentions` are; `attach` reads `e["covers"]`.
3. **No covered block anywhere.** `attach` leaves out a ledger item whose entry an open Decide or Do line placed on the page covers, on every card, not only the line's own (TD-1368 strips within the card) — a Decide or Do item only: a *with you* or *answered* item stands, as `attach` keeps it today. The strip set is page-wide: before its first `drawn(…)` call `attach` runs a pre-pass over every placed Decide or Do board line and every ledger item (after `put`'s *with you* and *answered* rewrites, which the pre-pass applies the same way), giving the page's covered entries that have a Decide or Do ledger item — the strip set only; `holds` is `item_home`'s (step 4), drawn as it is; then it draws. On a card other than the line's, the entry's row carries the item in its *mentioned in n items* phrase (`mentioned`, TD-915): for a board line (the `board` kind only: mail, a red PR and a fork's PR keep `refs[1:]` as today), `item_home`'s `mentions` are the covered list's rows on cards other than the home card (today `refs[1:]` of the text with only `row != home row` filtered, which would also name the same card's parts, already in `holds`; and no `Part of` member is in the text), and `attach` records a `coming` (not yet due) line's mentions as it does a due one's. That card's status is unchanged (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs*): its amber edge stands, its pill draws no count, the **Needs you** switch keeps it, and its header's Start session stands by the header's own rule (a card with no item that draws one). An entry another open undecided line covers in its own right (its `Context:` names it) keeps that line as its own item and is still listed in this line's `holds`. A covered row no open card holds is left out of *Later* by `split_later`'s `asked` (TD-1362, widened by TD-1368); one a card holds reaches `attach` as a `later` item, and this strip is what removes it.
4. **The holds line.** `item_home` returns `holds` beside `covers` (`covers` less the home row), so every page that draws the line — a Queue card, the Repo page, and the Inbox's own row of a line no card holds — draws the same *holds* line: `item_home` returns `covers` and `holds` in its `inbox` results too, from the text and `entries` without the index, and `split_later` merges them into the rows it keeps (`rest`, `unshown`); the snoozed section (index None) draws none. The item carries `holds`: `covered()`'s list less the item's home entry, open entries only, whatever each draws (stripped, *with you*, *answered*, on another card or on none), in the list's order — the same set on both pages; the strip set is a subset of it, so nothing stripped goes unnamed. `inbox_row.html`'s `board()` macro draws from `e.holds`, after the item's body and before its fold — in the `e.card` block (the Queue card, after `sentence` and `leads`) and in the `e.lead` branch alike (the Repo page's For you and Waiting on you, which render no card mode) — one line *holds TD-1356, TD-1357, TD-1358* (each id as the card's rows name it: bare for the line's own repo, `<repo>#TD-n` for another's), each id a link to its row's anchor where the page draws the row at all (the picks never drop a card, `apply_picks` marks it `qoff`; a folded card still holds its rows), else to the Repo page on the id (`/repo/<name>#TD-n`); the link carries the Repo page's address too, and the browser, at the press, follows that one where the anchor's card is hidden by the picks (`qoff`), since a pick changes without a reload (`AO.qPass`); none when `holds` is empty. The item keeps its answers, default, **Answer…** and Start session.
5. **The Repo page.** The Repo route leaves out of `fy_rows` every entry `covered()` lists for an open undecided Decide or Do line of the horizon (its due lines and `hz["ahead"]`, the lines `fy_boards` places), so `brow` falls to `qrow` for it: the route calls neither `split_later` nor `asked_entries` today, and `ledger_for_you`'s `decided` filter reads decided lines only. `fy_boards` places a line at the first For you row it covers in the list's order (today the first of its `refs`), draws the same *holds* line (compared with the repo's own prefix removed, as `fy_ids` and `hits` are bare, a ref to another repo matching no For you row; a placed line is taken out of `boards` and `hz["ahead"]` as today, and a line none of whose covered entries is in For you is placed nowhere and stays where it is drawn today, among Waiting on you's due lines or its rows coming up, its *holds* line drawn there too), and the other For you rows it covers stay in the column as its existing plain row (`brow`'s `qrow`, as the column draws any row, as it already does today for an open decided line's covered row — this widens it to undecided lines; no ⋯, since a Repo-page row carries `ext` and `row` draws `menu` only without it — Open, Done… and Back to design… are not offered while the line covers the row, its answers being the line's) with no `ledger_for_you` block — not left out of For you: TD-1368's *left out of … For you* is the entry's `ledger_for_you` row, which `brow` then does not find, and the column keeps the plain row (screen 11 *For you*).
