## TD-1380: One board decide line that covers several entries is one Queue item, with a holds line, and none of the covered entries draws its own block

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1378's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1368
**Part of:** TD-1378
**Location:** design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs* (*One line is one item, however many entries it covers*), *What is attached to a card*, screen 11 *For you*; §4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*. Code: `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`board_rows`, which builds the board row the Queue and the Repo page read: it copies `refs` and `text` and carries no `parts`), `src/sessionorc/workorders.py` (`orders`, which drops `parts` too), `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`CONTEXT`, `item_home`, `asked_entries` as TD-1368 widens it, `attach`), `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html` (the item's first paragraph), `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (the Repo page's `fy_boards`) and its board template.

**Why:** an end-of-rounds `decide` line holds its design and every build it filed, so it covers several entries (§4.4 *The card's status*, TD-1364), and the person should see and answer it once. On 2026-11-04 TD-1286's line was drawn as six Decide items with one question (TD-1378's Why). TD-1368 widens the reading so a covered entry's own block gives way to the line on the line's own card; this entry makes the line one item on the whole page and says what it holds.

**Fix:**
1. **The order of what a line covers.** The board row carries the record's `parts` (`board_rows` copies it beside `refs`; `orders` keeps it), and one reader in `queue.py` gives an open board line's covered entries as an ordered list, not a set: its `Context:` entry (a bare `TD-n` the line's own repo's, as `item_home` reads it), then its `Parts:` ids in the order written (v2.3's `parse_parts`, the record's `parts`), then each open member directly `Part of` the `Context:` entry that carries `decision (paul)`, by id; each once. `item_home`, `asked_entries` (TD-1368's widened set is this list's) and the Repo page's `fy_boards` all read it, and none reads `Parts:` out of the line's text: today `CONTEXT` stops only at `Due:`, `Answers:`, `Decided:` or `Closed:`, so on the Queue a `Parts:` line's ids are read as `Context:` refs by accident, while the Repo page's `refs` (`item_refs`) stop before `Parts:`; `CONTEXT` stops at `Parts:` too.
2. **One home.** `item_home` places a board line on the card of the first entry in that list that the index holds (today: its `Context:`'s first ref alone, so a line whose parent sits on no open card fell to the Inbox while its parts were on one).
3. **No covered block anywhere.** `attach` leaves out a ledger item whose entry an open Decide or Do line placed on the page covers, on every card, not only the line's own (TD-1368 strips within the card); on a card other than the line's, the entry's row carries the item in its *mentioned in n items* phrase (`mentioned`, TD-915). A covered row no open card holds is left out of *Later* by `split_later`'s `asked` (TD-1362, widened by TD-1368); one a card holds reaches `attach` as a `later` item, and this strip is what removes it.
4. **The holds line.** The item carries `holds`: the covered entries but the one it is drawn on, open and carrying `decision (paul)`, in the list's order. `queue_row.html` draws under the item's first paragraph one line *holds TD-1356, TD-1357, TD-1358*, each id a link to its row's anchor where the page draws it, else to the Repo page on the id (`/repo/<name>#TD-n`); none when `holds` is empty. The item keeps its answers, default, **Answer…** and Start session.
5. **The Repo page.** `fy_boards` places a line at the first For you row it covers in the list's order (today the first of its `refs`), draws the same *holds* line, and the other For you rows it covers draw no **Lift the hold** / **Answer…** block (screen 11 *For you*).

**Done when:** tests (beside TD-1368's, e.g. `tests/test_queue_answered_hold.py` or a new `tests/test_queue_covers_once.py`) show: a parent's open `decide` with `Parts:` naming three held builds draws one Decide item on the parent's card, its *holds* line listing the three in order, and none of the builds' own blocks; the same line with no `Parts:` covers the parent's `decision (paul)` members by `Part of` the same way; a covered entry on another card draws no block and carries *mentioned in 1 item*; a line whose `Context:` entry is on no open card is drawn on the first part's card; the Repo page's For you draws the line once with its *holds* line. The design text matches what was built (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs*, screen 11, §4.5a row). Then a live read on the next design whose builds are held with it, recorded here before it archives.
