**Why:** After the cut-over no file defines a team, so a team is made and deleted on the page.

**Ask:** Should the builders build New team and Delete on the team page?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *New team* and *Delete*, with their §4.5a rows: `team_new` and `team_delete` with their refusals, each gated on the `cut_over` marker. New team for a repo's second team says its name must be declared on the Team vocabulary of that repo's own ledger by a pull request to that repo, shows the line to write, and marks the team undeclared until it merges; Start of an undeclared team is refused as today. The form asks nothing about the built-in seats: a new team's repo uses the built-in set it already uses, `default` where it names none (§4.9d *The built-in seats and their page*). Both calls are on the person-only list.

**Done when:** A team made on the page is stopped with an empty login list and says it cannot run; Delete names the open records that refuse it; a repo's second team is drawn undeclared until its ledger pull request merges; all are refused before the marker.
