## TD-2063: Making and deleting a team on its page, and the pick of the team that looks after a repo

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e3ef8bb983d647ffb0ab7305c24f0549
**Blocked by:** TD-2060, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** After the cut-over no file defines a team, so a team is made and deleted on the page; and where a repo has several teams, one must be named for what belongs to the repo.

**Ask:** Should the builders build New team, Delete, and the pick of the team that looks after a repo?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *New team*, *Delete* and *Where a repo has several teams, one of them looks after the repo*, with their §4.5a rows: `team_new` and `team_delete` with their refusals, each gated on the `cut_over` marker; `repos.<repo>.own_team`, asked when a repo's second team is made, never unset on a repo with several teams, a Delete of the team it names refused until the pick is moved, cleared when one team remains. New team for a repo's second team says its name must be declared on the ledger's Team vocabulary by a pull request, and Start of an undeclared team is refused as today. Both calls are on the person-only list.

**Done when:** A team made on the page is stopped with an empty login list and says it cannot run; Delete names the open records that refuse it; the pick's refusals each have a test; all are refused before the marker.
