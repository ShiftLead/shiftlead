## TD-1569: A team whose members wound down cannot be started from its card while a seat is busy, and the refusal says the seat is idle

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-10 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** b81bba5d8c3f403ab8c8b09d493155f3
**Location:** design §4.5a *Start / Wind down / Stop now*; §4.9a *A person's Start on a concluded team*; §4.9b; src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (`NamesHeld`, `_close_concluded`, `concluded`); src/sessionorc/work.py (`finished`)

**Why:** A team whose workers wound down cannot be restarted from its card while one of its seats is busy. ao-grind's card offered only **Wind down** and **Stop now** while its grinders and designer were exited with work in their lanes (grinder: 3 pickable, 2 live check; designer: 11 design). `ao team start ao-grind` refused: *team ao-grind was not started — techlead-ao-1 is idle … `ao team stop ao-grind --close` closes a settled member, or Close it on its card* (Paul, through `ao-led-tds-ledger`, m-1f43099fd62f, 2026-11-10).

What the design says and what probably happened. §4.5a *Start / Wind down / Stop now* draws Start on a team with nothing live, and on a **concluded** team (§4.9a *A person's Start on a concluded team*), whose seats are idle or gone, where it closes them first. The techlead seat, `ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1`, was answering Paul's previous request. At 03:46Z its turn had ended but its background precheck-and-merge of PR #3259 was still running. `sessionorc.work.finished` reads a seat that is idle with a shell running as *idle, shell live*, so the team is not concluded (`teamrun._close_concluded` then raises `NamesHeld`). The card draws a live, unconcluded team, with Wind down. The build should confirm this against the home's log for that minute.

So the code most likely follows the design, but the outcome is wrong in two ways:
- **A seat stands in front of the team's work.** A seat comes on a question and leaves (§4.9b). That one is mid-answer should not keep the person from starting the members whose lanes hold work. Today only waiting for the seat to finish, or killing it, gets the team going again.
- **The refusal misleads.** It says the seat *is idle* and offers Close, when the seat was busy (a shell live) and closing it would cut off its merge. The concluded test's own clause (`why`) is never shown.

**Fix:** Design first (§4.5a *Start / Wind down / Stop now*, §4.9 `ao team start`, §4.9a *A person's Start on a concluded team*, §4.9b seats).

1. **Starting the members while a seat is live.** When every non-seat member is exited or closed, or concluded, and the lanes hold work, the card offers **Start** (*Start members*, Paul's alternative wording) beside Wind down. The start creates the members and leaves a live seat alone, never closing a seat that is working or has a shell live. Settle whether this is the existing Start with seats exempt from the name check, or a separate act, and what rule 8 does in the same case.
2. **The refusal says why.** A `NamesHeld` refusal on a team that is not concluded names the concluded test's clause for that holder (*techlead-ao-1 idle, shell live*), not the bare state, and offers Close only for a holder it would be safe to close.

**Done when:** The design says what the card offers when only seats are live and lanes hold work, and a build makes it so: a team in ao-grind's case can be started from its card without closing a busy seat, and a refused start names the clause that kept the team from being concluded.
