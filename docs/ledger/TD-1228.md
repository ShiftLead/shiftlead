## TD-1228: *Unseen* becomes a mark beside the pill: a `turn_at` stamp, the ● unseen chip, and no pill gives way to it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-29 (the designer, from TD-1222's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1227
**Part of:** TD-1222
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/models.py` (`set_state`, ~:1594; the `turn_at` field and its place in the record's lists beside `since`) and `src/sessionorc/agent.py` (`rpc_seen`'s docstring) — held paths, so the PR waits for the techlead's read; `src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py` (`unseen` ~:256–266, `pill_word` ~:299, the team header's counts ~:1350–1370), `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py` (`LIGHT_KEYS`, the rollup's count spec ~:658), the card templates and `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` / `app.css` (the chip, `focusTitle`), `docs/mockups/gen.py` (*States & badges*); `tests/test_ui.py` (`test_unseen_idle_until_focused`, the header counts ~:1533), `tests/test_ui_popout.py`

**Why:** design §4.2 *Unseen*, §4.5 *The card's anatomy*, §4.5a *card: unseen mark* (TD-1222). *Unseen* is a mark beside whatever the pill reads, not the composed pill *idle · unseen*, so a session that answered and then started a background shell reads *working* and unseen. Today's test, `since > seen_at` on an `idle` record, cannot carry it, because `since` moves on every state change.

**Fix:**
- **The stamp.** Inside `set_state`'s changing branch (`if state != self.state or (since is not None and since != self.since)`, `models.py` ~:1599), when the new state is `idle`, it writes `turn_at = max(filter(None, (turn_at, self.since)))`, so the first stamp compares no `None` (a `tests/test_models.py` case covers it). A same-state call that changes nothing never re-stamps, so a tick re-reading `idle` cannot bring the mark back after a look, so a state taken back with an older `since` (TD-950) leaves it. It joins `NODE_OWNED`, as `subagent_at` does (with its line in `tests/test_models.py`, which pins every field to one owner set), so a node's reading carries home, and it is in neither `GROWS` nor `WAKE_FIELDS`. A `--resume`'s first `SessionStart` and the tick's launch reading stamp it too, as `since` did.
- **The light keys.** In `org.py`'s `LIGHT_KEYS`, `seen_at` replaces `unseen` (`turn_at` moves only with `state`, which `SHAPE_KEYS` carries), which no agent view carries — `app.py`'s `fan_step` compares `LIGHT_KEYS` on the raw session event, which never had `unseen` (it is computed in `cards.py`), so the key was already dead — so a Focus or Inbox stream re-reads when Focus clears the mark on a working record.
- **The test.** It reaches an `exited` record as well as a live one (§4.2 *Unseen*). `unseen` is `not unattended and state != "closed" and turn_at and (not seen_at or turn_at > seen_at)`, so a session on its first turn (no `turn_at`) carries no mark and nothing compares `None`. A record with no `turn_at` that is `idle` falls back to `since`, the record written before the stamp.
- **The pill.** The pill no longer reads *unseen*: `state_label` and `pill_word` keep the state's or the composed pill's word, and no rank is lowered for it. `unseen` is computed once, below `d["seat"]` (~:272, so after ~:297), by the test above and false on a seat drawn *on call*, and nothing after it reads or writes it to compose a pill: `:264–265` (the rank lowered by 0.5, `state_label` rewritten), `:458`'s `not d["unseen"]` (so *waiting* no longer yields to it) and `:477`'s `d["unseen"] = False` (so the mark shows on a working record) all go. `COUNT_ORDER`'s `("unseen", …)` entry (~:1354) goes with the pill.
- **The view fields.** The card view carries `unseen` and `unseen_age` (from `turn_at`, else `since`), which the chip and its hover read. `inbox.py`'s session row dict (~:328–345) gains both, and so does the view the transcript route hands `transcript.html`.
- **The sort.** The key becomes (rank, unseen first, interactive first, name) at its two sites: `cards.card_order` (~:1343) and the client re-sort in `app.js` (~:2115, reading `data-rank` and `data-mine`, and `data-unseen`, which `card.html` already emits). The Repo page's session list sorts through `card_order` (`repo.py` ~:717), so it follows with no edit (§4.5 *One order, no control*).
- **The chip.** Every card, full or compact, draws the chip *● unseen* after the pill: accent blue (`--new`), unfilled, display only, hover *a turn ended <age> ago, after you last looked* — as `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-10-29-td1222-working-waiting-unseen-1600.png` draws it.
- **Counts.** A team header's folded counts are by pill, then *n unseen* in the accent blue. `state_counts` keeps returning the pill counts as strings, and the group dict gains `unseen_n` beside `counts`. `templates/group_head.html` (~:90, `g.counts | join(' · ')`) draws *n unseen* after them in the accent blue, and `repo.py` (~:794) fills `unseen_n` for the Repo page's team groups. Each *n unseen* counts the mark on the records its own pill counts already count: the header's cards, `exited` ones included, and the rollup's counted set, which leaves ended sessions out. Seats are not counted.
- **The filter.** The filter box takes the whole word `unseen` alone, as it takes `mine`: only cards with the mark show. Its placeholder becomes *filter… team: state: mine unseen kind:command* (§4.5a *filter…*). The Sessions rollup's member blocks and its **Agents (n)** pills both read `ROLLUP_STATES` (`org.py` ~:658, ~:699, ~:825): its *unseen* row leaves the blocks, and the Agents line ends with *unseen (n)* in the accent blue after the pills, a press filtering to `unseen` (§4.5a *rollup*).
- **Keep and reword.** `app.js` (~:6657) calls `act(id, "seen")` when `ev.session.unseen`: keep `unseen` on the card's `session` event. Reword `rpc_seen`'s docstring (`src/sessionorc/agent.py` ~:3211, held) and `seen_at`'s field comment (`models.py` ~:1099, *last time a person looked*: now the last turn the person saw) and the comments at `app.py` ~:5091 (*unseen idle sorts above idle*) and `tests/test_ui.py` ~:1421 (*§4.2's unseen-idle rule*).
- **Seen only while visible.** The `GET /focus/{sid}` route reads the record with `call("get", id=sid)` in place of its `seen` (`app.py` ~:1969), its 503 redirect to `/` unchanged: loading the page is not the look. The page sends `seen` once from the rendered record's `unseen`, at once if `document.visibilityState` is `visible`, else on the first `visibilitychange` to visible. Then it sends on each delta whose `session.unseen` is true while visible (`app.js` ~:6657, which checks no visibility today), and on every `visibilitychange` to visible while the last rendered record is unseen, never from a hidden tab (§4.5a *card: unseen mark*). Tests cover a page loaded hidden that clears the mark only once shown, and a turn that ended while the tab was hidden, cleared on the switch back.
- **One clock for the test.** `turn_at` is the clock of the host that ran the turn (it is `since`). So a look records the turn it saw: `rpc_seen` stamps `seen_at = max(old seen_at, turn_at)` where the record has a `turn_at`, else its `since` where it is `idle` (a record written before the stamp), else leaves `seen_at` unchanged (a first turn, which a missing `seen_at` already reads unseen once it ends). Any later turn on that host then compares greater, whichever way its clock is skewed, and equal reads seen, as the same-second rule does today. Every other `seen` call stays: the card controls' (`app.py` ~:3475, 3521, 3526, 3541), the Message action's (~:3561) and the post-action one (~:3578).
- **The Inbox row and the transcript page.** `inbox.py` (~:336) and `templates/inbox_row.html` (~:600), and `templates/transcript.html` (~:15), draw the chip after their pill as the card does. The transcript page has no event stream, so its chip is the record as the page was drawn.
- **The window title.** Focus's window title appends *· unseen* after the state word as the pill reads it (§4.5a *Focus: title*), in both `app.js`'s `focusTitle` and `templates/focus.html`'s title block (~:4), so `tests/test_ui_popout.py` pins `w · idle · unseen` from the mark, not from `state_label`.
- **The mockups.** In `docs/mockups/gen.py`, the legend comment (~:26), the *unseen* legend entries (~:209–210, 392–400), `RANK["unseen"]`, and the old pill's renderings (~:335, ~:613, ~:1005–1017) become the chip beside the pill; regenerate (§4.5a *state icon*: the ● is the chip's, not a state glyph).

**Done when:**
- An interactive record that finished a turn after its last Focus shows the mark whether its pill reads *working*, *waiting* or *idle*.
- An unattended one never shows it, and a session still on its first turn (no `turn_at`) never shows it.
- Focus clearing the mark on a working record redraws a light stream's card (a test).
- A visible Focus clears it on its first send, so the chip may draw for one round trip; a Focus loaded into a background tab does not clear it until it is shown; opening the transcript page never does.
- A turn that started after an unseen one keeps the mark.
- A test pins each case.
