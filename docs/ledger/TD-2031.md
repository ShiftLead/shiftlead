**Why:** TD-1948's design (§4.6 *Fit to window*) bounds a phone press's hold at one second for the keyboard's fall and asks that the fall be measured on an iPhone and an Android phone, both figures recorded and the limit raised if either is longer. Only real phones show it: headless Chromium has no soft keyboard, so the build kept the one second unmeasured.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (AO.HOLD_LIMIT); docs/history/TD-1948.md; design §4.6 Fit to window

**Ask:** Will you time how long your phone's keyboard takes to fall after you press Fit to window, on an Apple phone and an Android phone?

**Context:** Fit to window on a phone waits up to one second for the keyboard to go down before it measures the screen; that second was never measured on a real phone, since the test browser has no keyboard. If a keyboard takes longer, the press falls back to the size kept before.
