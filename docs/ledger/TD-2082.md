**Why:** Part of TD-2080: temporary files keep filling the space every session depends on, and nothing reacts until it is full (design §4.6).

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that gives unattended seats their own temporary space and clears it before it fills?

**Context:** Temporary files have repeatedly filled the disk every session shares. This design moves unattended seats to a pool of their own, clears what can be rebuilt when it runs low, and warns you before it is full. Its review rounds ended with nothing left that changes the builds.

**Location:** design §4.6 *Temporary space is a pool of its own*; `src/sessionorc/agent.py`, `agent_tick.py`, `scratch.py`, `hosts.py`, `diskbook.py`, `src/shiftlead/cli.py`, `src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py`

**Fix:** Build design §4.6 *Temporary space is a pool of its own*. In `hosts.py`: the `seat_root` field, its template comment, and a parse of its own that reads empty as unset (`_root` returns the default). The start check (`agent.py`, `_check_scratch_root`) refuses its length and overlaps and calls `ensure_parent` for `scratch_root` alone. `_run_scratch` gains the `unattended` its caller has and places such a run under `seat_root`; both `_scratch_root()` copies (`agent.py`, `agent_tick.py`) gain a twin. `scratch.kept` weighs a carried path against the root it lies under; the hourly sweep and `keep_alive` run once per distinct root under one tally. The reading carries a second free-space key, `seat_free`, for `seat_root`, through `diskbook.write` and `read`, and `ao disk` prints it with the same tenth warning, or *not mounted* where `seat_root` is neither a mount point nor holds its `shiftlead-<uid>/` (§4.7); the sweep skips such a root without making it. The Settings page's Hosts card gains a `seat_root` row (§5). On merge, write an `act` board line ending *only you can do this*, carrying §4.6's `mkdir`, its mount line, `seat_root: /aot`, the host agent's restart and the teams' stop and start, in that order.

**Done when:** With `seat_root` unset, every run's directory and env are as before. With it set, a run started unattended has its scratch under it and an interactive run under `scratch_root`; a restart, a Resume and `ao mode` keep the path; the sweep ages and touches directories under both roots, and once where the two are equal; an overlapping `seat_root` is refused at start; a missing `seat_root` directory stops no host agent and fails only a seat's create, with why; `ao disk` prints `seat_free` with its warning, or *not mounted* where `seat_root` has no reading, which the sweep then skips without making it; the Settings page's Hosts card shows `seat_root`; and the board line is on the board.
