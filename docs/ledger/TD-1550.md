**Why:** Paul (2026-11-07, through Add entry): integrate xAI's Grok Build CLI, and add Grok as a fact-checker behind Gemini to get trial runs with it, on his SuperGrok subscription. A third adapter tests the adapter contract again (TD-112's aim, which Gemini CLI first tested, §4.3 *The second adapter*). It also gives the fact-check, the job a lesser model can hold (§4.9b *The fact-check*), a second non-Claude model to measure against Claude's verdicts.

Paul's words: *I believe the install command is `curl -fsSL https://x.ai/cli/install.sh | bash`. I have a supergrok subscription that we can use (presuming using a sub is allowed).* The 2026-10-13 Grok Bot decision (docs/decisions/2026-10-13-grok-bot.md) found no CLI for Grok Bot, a different product; this is xAI's own CLI.

**Fix:** A design round, as TD-112's was for Gemini CLI (§4.3), then its builds:
- **Read the CLI before designing.** Its install (Paul's command is a remote script piped to bash; the round reads it before anyone runs it, and the install on kmaster is Paul's step), its login and whether a SuperGrok subscription may drive it (xAI's terms; Paul presumes so, and the round confirms it in xAI's own words or asks him), its hooks or other state signals, its per-profile config home, its headless and permission modes.
- **The adapter** under `src/shiftlead/adapters/grok/`, held to the conformance suite (TD-784) and walked live, as Gemini's was (TD-785, TD-786).
- **"Behind Gemini" for the fact-check.** Two readings, which the round settles with Paul. (a) A pool on the fact-check seat, Gemini first and Grok after (§4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool*, TD-1476): Grok then runs only when Gemini cannot answer, which gives few trial runs. The design today also keeps the trial seat `fact-check-ao-1` one Gemini seat with no pool, and a pooled seat holds no review stage (§4.9b). (b) A second fact-check seat on Grok, read beside Gemini's on the same PRs, so its verdicts can be measured. The round recommends one.

**Done when:** A merged design for the Grok CLI adapter and its place in the fact-check, with its builds filed `Part of` this entry; xAI's terms on driving the CLI with a subscription are quoted in the design or answered by Paul.
