**Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *Recently completed*, its **who closed it** (TD-1899), and §4.5a's row *Queue page, Recently completed | who closed it*. Mockup: `docs/mockups/QueueWhoClosed.dc.html`, shot `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-17-td1899-who-closed.png`.

- Draw *no review*, *no precheck* and *merged past the gate* in the warning colour where §4.5 says. Draw no line where `by` is None.
- A line opens when any of its entries has a `Resolved:` text or a `by`.
- Tests: the page's words over a `by` with every part, one with no session and no review, one with an override, and an entry with no `by`. Take a Playwright shot of an opened line, committed under `docs/mockups/reviews/` and named in the PR.
