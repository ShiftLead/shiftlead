## TD-1933: An opened Recently completed line says who built, reviewed and merged each entry

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 66ef2eb8205a4e9cb00da7b56a4250fc
**Blocked by:** TD-1932
**Part of:** TD-1899

**Why:** Part of TD-1899's design: an opened Recently completed line shows, under each entry's `Resolved:` text, who built, reviewed and merged it, so the person sees at a glance that the flow ran as it should.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (queue_view); src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_part.html (qdbody); design §4.5 screen 12 Recently completed, §4.5a

**Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *Recently completed*, its **who closed it** (TD-1899), and §4.5a's row *Queue page, Recently completed | who closed it*. Mockup: `docs/mockups/QueueWhoClosed.dc.html`, shot `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-17-td1899-who-closed.png`.

- `queue_view` passes each entry's `by` through on both kinds of line: a completed card's (`kind: "card"`) and an open card's archived row's (`kind: "row"`). `queue_part.html`'s `qdbody` draws one quiet line under the entry's `Resolved:` text, in the words §4.5 gives. *built by* lists the sessions joined by *and*, and is left out where none. *reviewed by* gives each model and its verdict in words (*passed*, *passed after fixes*, *blocked*, or the model alone), joined by *;*. *merged by* names the merger as written. A review with no model reads *reviewed, passed*. Where `by.pr` is not the PR drawn beside the entry, the line opens *in #n: by …* in place of *built by …*.
- Draw *no review*, *no precheck* (False only, never None), *merged past the gate*, and a *blocked* that no later review passed, in the warning colour (`.warnish`) where §4.5 says. Draw no line where `by` is None. An entry listed for its `by` alone draws its line in place of the *no Resolved line* words. A kept card (`_kept_card`) draws no line.
- Which entries a line lists, and whether it opens: a card line's per-entry filter becomes *a `Resolved:` text, or a `by`, or more than one entry*; a row line lists its entry where it has a `Resolved:` text or a `by`. A line opens when it lists any entry.
- Tests: the page's words over a `by` with every part, one with no session and no review, one with an override, one with `precheck` None (no mark), one *blocked* then *passed* (quiet) and one ending *blocked* (marked), one with a model None, one whose `by.pr` differs from the drawn PR, and an entry with no `by`; a kept card draws none; a card line whose only entry has a `by` and no `Resolved:` opens; a row line the same. Take a Playwright shot of an opened line, committed under `docs/mockups/reviews/` and named in the PR.

**Done when:** An opened Recently completed line on the Queue shows each entry's who-closed-it line as design §4.5 screen 12 says, with the marks, and nothing for an entry whose closing commit has no `Merged-by:`. The tests the Fix lists pass, and the PR carries the shot.
