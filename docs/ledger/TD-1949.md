**Why:** TD-1926's design (§4.4 *The card's status*, *A `decision (paul)` member that still waits on open work is no need*): an entry held by `decision (paul)` that also waits on open work — an open id in its `Blocked by:` or an open entry `Part of` it, less the person's own — is asked a question whose answer cannot act yet, and on a parent a lift frees it the moment its last open part closes (ledgerboard#TD-132, 2026-11-17). This build is the reading half: the word, the need, the counts and Lift.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/queue.py; src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (ledger_for_you)

**Fix:** In `src/sessionorc/queue.py` and `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`ledger_for_you`), per §4.4 *The card's status* (TD-1926's paragraph), *A row's state*, *Waiting* and *Where Lift the hold is drawn*:
- one helper, `open_work(e, entries, …)` in `queue.py`: the member's open `Blocked by:` ids (this repo's, and `<repo>#TD-n` the reading reads open) and the open entries naming it in `Part of:`, less each that carries `decision (paul)` itself; an unresolved or unknown id and an `until`/`event` gate count for nothing; sorted by id. Both readings call it: the Queue's `row()` and `ledger_for_you`'s rows, which are what the Queue's wants are built from;
- the mark goes on the entry where the reading builds it (e.g. `e["then_yours"] = [ids]`), so the tests that read entries see it, and on both rows: in `row()`, after the *with you*, answered, handed, *waits until* and applying words (each keeps its word), a member that needs the person and has open work reads `waiting, then yours` — the slot after *answered — waiting on <asker>* in *A row's state*'s order — and **keeps `needs`** (`card_entries` reads it for *waits on me*), the row carrying the counted ids as `then_yours`; its *after TD-n* relation words are its open `Blocked by:` ids alone (`after`, as today), its counted parts drawn in the parts meter;
- every test of need excludes a marked row as it excludes `with_you`/`answered`: `_status`'s *needs you*, `branch()`, `_seg`, and `queue_row.html`'s `needsyou` class and needs word;
- `_status`'s inner `waits(e)` returns true for a marked entry (its `decision (` id no longer stops it), and `_own(e)` is false for it, so `_wait` sets it with the rest and reads it waiting through its counted ids, which are members of the same card;
- `lift_here` (the row's ⋯) and `lift_item` (`ledger_for_you`, and the Repo page's ⋯ through it) are unset on a marked row, and its `answered` stamp carries no `lift`;
- the Queue's per-repo *for you* count, taken from `ledger_for_you`'s rows, leaves a marked row out (§4.5 screen 6), while `kind_of` is unchanged and the row stays in `ledger_for_you`'s list, drawn grey;
- an open, undecided `decide` line that covers only marked members (`covered`) carries the same mark on its item (`then_yours`: the union of their counted ids), so the card draws it grey.
Tests: a held design whose only open parts carry the gate still needs the person; one with an open round part reads the word, keeps *waits on me* in the id lists and has no Lift; a lone marked member whose blocker waits on a date gives its card *waiting*, and one whose round part is in progress does not; a cross-repo open blocker counts, an unresolved one does not; an answered, *with you*, *waits until* or applying member keeps its word; the word ends when the last counted id archives; a line covering only marked members is marked, one covering one unmarked member is not.
