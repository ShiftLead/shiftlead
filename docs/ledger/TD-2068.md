## TD-2068: Sessions start on a login: the first usable one in the team's or the built-in set's list that offers the model

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 892e4dba52524c7e9d39b6053edc2778
**Blocked by:** TD-2060, TD-2066, TD-2049, TD-2051, TD-2053, TD-2065
**Part of:** TD-1976

**Why:** After the switch-over every session a team or a built-in set starts runs on a login, and no build of the Tools, Flows or team page designs launches one: each left it to the switch-over's design (design 4.9e, The launch on a login).

**Ask:** Should the builders build the launch of a session on a login?

**Fix:** Build design 4.9e The launch on a login, for the home's own host, read only after the cut-over marker. Every launch the home makes for a stage, an audit, a built-in seat or the director calls TD-2060's pick with the list (a team's or a built-in set's) and the model; with a login, launch in its folder (the adapter's home_env) on the tool's word for the model with the login's extra_args, unattended_args and permission_wait; with none usable, wait under the usage gate, read as design 6 Usage gate, After the cut-over says (a session is held only when no login in its list is usable); with none offering the model, launch nothing and let cannot run say why. Write the record's login and account (node-owned) and seat_model (home-owned), and the login's name into the record's profile. On a switched host the profile reader answers from logins.yml (design 4.9e, A record's profile): a lookup of a profile by name and of its account gives the login of that name, so today's readers of a record's profile keep working; a shell profile is answered as today. ao new and the + New form take --login <tool>:<name>, defaulting to the login last used there, and --model; after the marker --profile is refused with the design's words. org.yml's director: reads model: and logins:. Write the record's new fields into design 4.4's record lists, add the 4.5a rows the + New form's pick needs, and the help text.

**Done when:** On a fixture home with the marker written: a stage's session, an audit's, a tech lead's, a sweeper's and the director's each start on the first usable login of the right list that offers the model, and their records hold login, account, seat_model and the list they were launched with; a list whose first login is at its reserve starts the session on the second; a list with no login offering the model starts nothing; ao new --login and the + New form's pick start a session on that login and ao new --profile is refused; the usage gate, the spend and the Sessions card read a record whose profile is a login's name with no profiles.yml on the host. On a home without the marker every launch is unchanged.
