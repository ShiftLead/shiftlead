**Why:** On 2026-11-14 the home's gate sweep opened lift PRs #3757 (TD-1661's `event (agent-cadence-doer)`) and #3760 (TD-1803's `event (agent-cadence-v28)`) at 00:00 UTC, and nobody read them all day. Design §4.4 says the home opens those PRs and merges none, since they take ordinary review, but nothing hands one to a reviewer. Only a live check's lift (`lift/TD-n/live…`) is a team's work order (§4.4 *A live check's lift PR is the team's at once*, TD-1437). An event or `until` lift reaches nobody unless a person notices it. TD-1803 (the ledgerboard v2.8 sync) stayed blocked until Paul's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` found both PRs, reviewed them and merged them that evening, and asked for this entry (ask m-0421ca32e937). A gate that has lifted is work that can start, and a lift PR nobody reads holds it back without anyone knowing.

**Location:** docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md (the gate sweep; A live check's lift PR is the team's at once); docs/design/4.9b-techlead.md; src/sessionorc/gatesweep.py; src/sessionorc/workorders.py (lift_since); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py

**Fix:** A design round on how a gate sweep's lift PR reaches a reader, in design §4.4 (the gate sweep, and *A live check's lift PR is the team's at once*) and §4.9b. Two shapes to weigh, the round recommending one:
- **An ask to the repo's techlead seat**, carrying the PR (`--pr`), sent by the home when it opens the lift, so the seat reads it as a held PR and merges it, the asker being the home.
- **A work order**: an open lift PR on `lift/TD-n/<gate>` (event and `until`, not only `live…`) becomes the servicing team's order from its first reading, as a live check's lift is today, its taker prechecking, reviewing and merging it.
Either way: what happens in a repo with no servicing team or seat (today's silence must at least reach the person), what a red precheck on a lift does, and that the home still merges nothing itself. The sweep's own `sweep/<date>` board PR (#3891 today) is in scope too, if it waits the same way.

**Done when:** The design says who reads an event or `until` lift PR and how it reaches them without a person noticing it; its build has landed; and a lift the sweep opens is merged or answered within a working day with no person's prompt.
