## TD-1414: ao card <id>: the whole Queue card in the terminal, with every line the Queue attached to it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-05
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ee6bcbf973aa4d768f10fb38f203926d
**Blocked by:** TD-1413
**Part of:** TD-1411
**Location:** src/shiftlead/cli.py (new ao card); src/shiftlead/skill.md; design §4.7 ao card

**Why:** TD-1411's design (§4.7 *`ao card`*): nothing shows a Queue card whole outside the UI. `ao rank --list` gives one line per card, `ao board show` one line, `ao inbox` the messages. So a person reading a clipped card, or a session checking what the person will see, rebuilds the card from the ledger, the board and the PRs. On 2026-11-05 that is how two things were found: TD-1392's act line, and contractmatch's board look drawn under ledgerboard#TD-051's card.

**Fix:** As design §4.7 *`ao card`* says, `ao card <id> [--ui <url>] [--json]` in `src/shiftlead/cli.py`:

1. **The roads.** Outside a pane (no `SHIFTLEAD_SESSION`) and without `--ui`, it builds the card in-process with `await Readings().card(id, words=True)` (TD-1413), as `_counts`' local road builds a `Readings`. Its `inbox` read names `person`. Under a pane, or with `--ui`, it reads `/api/queue/card?id=<id>` at `--ui` or `http://127.0.0.1:<service.DEFAULT_PORT>`, with no proxy, as `_ui_counts` does, giving `mail: ids`. Under a pane with no UI answering, it is refused with §4.7's words, exit 1. An install without `[ui]` outside a pane is refused naming the extra, exit 1. A host agent that does not answer exits 3. A host refusal of the `person` read is printed as the refusal, exit 1.
2. **The id.** A bare `TD-n` is passed as typed; `card_index` resolves it.
   - An id no open card holds: *no open card holds <id>*, exit 1.
   - A completed card: *<id> is on a completed card: closed <date>, #<pr>*, exit 1.
3. **The ids**: each item's id is its `item` (`item_ref`); a fork's PR, which has none, prints *fork PR #<n>*.
4. **The text form**, §4.7's order:
   - The header: the id and title, then group and place, status, *d/m done* and *start*.
   - `rows`: indented by depth, each line with id, priority, page, state or *needs you (decision|owner)*, holder, `PR #n`, *after TD-n*, and *done #n* with its Resolved text.
   - Each want with its count: decide, do, read by line, with you, answered. Each item's first line carries its id, kind, *on TD-n*, *board of <repo>* for a board line, *tied by <by>: <ref>* and its due. The body follows indented: the whole text, the detail's context, question and recommended, the answers with *(default)*, a ledger row's Ask, a PR's title, branch and checks.
   - A mail item on the UI road ends its line with *its words are the person's to read*.
   - Then `mentioned`.
5. **`--json`** prints `card_view`'s dict as it is.
6. `src/shiftlead/skill.md` (`ao --skill`'s text) lists `ao card <id>` among the read-only commands.

**Done when:** Tests, over a stub UI and a stub host, show that:
- each road is taken as §4.7 says: a pane uses the UI, `--ui` uses the UI, outside a pane runs in-process, and a pane with no UI is refused with the words;
- the text form of a fixture card holds the header, the rows tree, each want's items with *tied by*, a board line's unfolded detail and answers, and a mail item cut on the UI road;
- the two refusals and exit 3 hold;
- `--json` equals `card_view`'s output.

`ao --skill` names the command.
