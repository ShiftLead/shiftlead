## TD-1414: ao card <id>: the whole Queue card in the terminal, with every line the Queue attached to it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-05
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ee6bcbf973aa4d768f10fb38f203926d
**Blocked by:** TD-1413
**Part of:** TD-1411
**Location:** src/shiftlead/cli.py (new ao card); src/shiftlead/skill.md; design §4.7 ao card

**Why:** TD-1411's design (§4.7 *`ao card`*): nothing shows a Queue card whole outside the UI. `ao rank --list` gives one line per card, `ao board show` one line, `ao inbox` the messages. So a person reading a clipped card, or a session checking what the person will see, rebuilds the card from the ledger, the board and the PRs. On 2026-11-05 that is how two things were found: TD-1392's act line, and contractmatch's board look drawn under ledgerboard#TD-051's card.

**Fix:** As design §4.7 *`ao card`* says, `ao card <id> [--ui <url>] [--json]` in `src/shiftlead/cli.py`:

1. **The roads.**
   - Outside a pane (no `SHIFTLEAD_SESSION`) and without `--ui`, it builds the card in-process with `await Readings().card(id, words=True)` (TD-1413), as `_counts`' local road builds a `Readings`. Its `inbox` read names `person`.
   - Under a pane, or with `--ui`, it reads `/api/queue/card?id=<id>` at `--ui` or `http://127.0.0.1:<service.DEFAULT_PORT>`, with no proxy, as `_ui_counts` does, giving `mail: ids`.
2. **The refusals**, §4.7's words, each exit 1 but the last:
   - no open card holds the id (the endpoint's 404, or None in-process);
   - a completed card (`completed`): *closed <date>*, and *#<pr>* where it has one;
   - under a pane with no UI answering;
   - `--ui <url>` with nothing answering there, with no fall-back;
   - outside a pane with no `[ui]` extra;
   - the host's refusal of the `person` read, as the host words it;
   - a home with no `queue` RPC (the endpoint's 502, or the RPC's refusal in-process);
   - a host agent that does not answer: exit 3 (the endpoint's 503, or the in-process call's).

   A bare `TD-n` is passed as typed; `card_index` resolves it.
3. **The ids**: each item's id is its `item` (`item_ref`); a fork's PR, which has none, prints *fork PR #<n>*.
4. **The text form**, in §4.7's order:
   - **The header:** id and title, group and place, status, the card's `done`/`of` as *d/m done* on every group, and *start*.
   - **`rows`:** in `_tree`'s order, indented by depth, every fold opened (each archived row in its place). Each line gives id, priority, page, state or *needs you (decision|owner)*, holder, `PR #n`, *after TD-n*, and *done #n* with its Resolved text.
   - **The wants**, each with its count: decide, do, read under `READ_LINES`' labels, with you, and answered.
   - **Each item's first line:** its id, kind, *on TD-n*, *board of <repo>* for a board line, *tied by <by>: <ref>*, and its due.
   - **Its body, indented:**
     - a board line's `card` text, its `detail` and its answers with *(default)*;
     - a message's subject, text, and an `ask`'s or `steer`'s answers with *(default)*;
     - a ledger row's title and Ask;
     - a PR's title and its row's text.
   - A mail item on the UI road ends its first line with *its words are the person's to read*, and has no body.
   - **Then `mentioned`.**
5. **`--json`** prints `card_view`'s dict as it is.
6. `src/shiftlead/skill.md` (`ao --skill`'s text) lists `ao card <id>` among the read-only commands.

**Done when:** Tests, over a stub UI and a stub host, show that:
- each road is taken as §4.7 says: a pane uses the UI, `--ui` uses the UI, outside a pane runs in-process, and a pane with no UI or a `--ui` with nothing there is refused with the words;
- the text form of a fixture card holds the header, the rows tree with an archived row opened in its place, each want's items with *tied by*, a board line's card text, detail and answers, and a mail item cut on the UI road;
- each refusal and exit 3 holds;
- `--json` equals `card_view`'s output.

`ao --skill` names the command.
