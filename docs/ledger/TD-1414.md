## TD-1414: ao card <id>: the whole Queue card in the terminal, with every line the Queue attached to it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-05
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ee6bcbf973aa4d768f10fb38f203926d
**Blocked by:** TD-1413
**Part of:** TD-1411
**Location:** src/shiftlead/cli.py (new ao card); src/shiftlead/skill.md; design §4.7 ao card

**Why:** TD-1411's design (§4.7 *`ao card`*): nothing shows a Queue card whole outside the UI. `ao rank --list` gives one line per card, `ao board show` one line, `ao inbox` the messages. So a person reading a clipped card, or a session checking what the person will see, rebuilds the card from the ledger, the board and the PRs. On 2026-11-05 that is how two things were found: TD-1392's act line, and contractmatch's board look drawn under ledgerboard#TD-051's card.

**Fix:** As design §4.7 *`ao card`* says, `ao card <id> [--ui <url>] [--json]` in `src/shiftlead/cli.py`:

1. **The roads.**
   - **In-process.** Outside a pane (no `SHIFTLEAD_SESSION`) and without `--ui`, it calls `await Readings().card(id, words=True)` (TD-1413), as `_counts`' local road builds a `Readings`. Its `inbox` read names `person`. A `CardRefused` is its refusal: `down` exits 3; `no_queue` and `refused` exit 1 with their words.
   - **The UI.** Under a pane, or with `--ui`, it reads `/api/queue/card?id=<the id, URL-quoted>` at `--ui` or `http://127.0.0.1:<service.DEFAULT_PORT>`, with no proxy, as `_ui_counts` does, giving `mail: ids`. It parses an `HTTPError`'s body:
     - 404, 400 and 502 are refusals in their `error` words, exit 1;
     - 503 exits 3;
     - only a failure to connect (a `URLError` that is not an `HTTPError`, or a refused connection) is *no UI answers*.
2. **The refusals**, in §4.7's words:
   - no open card holds the id;
   - an `ambiguous` bare id, naming its repos;
   - a `completed` card: *closed <date>*, and *#<pr>* where it has one;
   - under a pane with no UI answering;
   - `--ui <url>` with nothing answering there, with no fall-back;
   - outside a pane with no `[ui]` extra;
   - the host's refusals as above.
3. **The text form**, in §4.7's order. Ids are printed whole (`<repo>#TD-n`) throughout.
   - **The header:** the id and title; group and place (a Waiting card's `wait` phrase and `would_be`); status; the card's `done`/`of` as *d/m done* on every group; and *start*.
   - **`rows`:** in `_tree`'s order, indented by depth, every fold opened so each archived row is in its place. Each line gives the id, priority, page, the state word where it has one (*with you in <name>*, *answered — waiting on <asker>* among them), else *needs you (decision|owner)* where `needs` is set, the holder, `PR #n`, *after <id>*, and for an archived row *done #n* with its Resolved text.
   - **The wants, each with its count:** decide, do, read under `READ_LINES`' labels, with you, then answered.
   - **Each item's first line** gives:
     - its id, the item's key in `wants` (TD-1413);
     - its row's `kind` (*red PR* or *fork PR* for a PR row, which has none);
     - *from <sender>* for a message;
     - *on <on_row>*, the top entry's included;
     - *board of <repo>* for a board line;
     - *tied by <by>: <ref>*;
     - its due.
   - **Its body, indented:**
     - a board line's `card` (`board_card`'s dict): its `sentence`, then its `leads`, then its `context`; then its `detail`, and its answers with *(default)*;
     - a message's subject, its text, and an `ask`'s or `steer`'s answers with *(default)*;
     - a ledger row's title and its Ask;
     - a PR's title and its row's text.
   - **A mail item on the UI road** ends its first line with *its words are the person's to read*, and has no body.
   - **Then `mentioned`:** per row, each item's id, its `kind` and its words (words absent on the UI road for mail).
4. **`--json`** prints `card_view`'s dict as it is (`default=str`), and for every refusal `{"error": "<its words>"}` with the text form's exit code, as every `ao --json` does.
5. **`src/shiftlead/skill.md`** (`ao --skill`'s text) lists `ao card <id>` among the read-only commands.

**Done when:** Tests, over a stub UI and a stubbed `Readings.card`, show:
- **The roads** are taken as §4.7 says:
  - a pane and `--ui` read the UI;
  - outside a pane runs in-process;
  - a pane with no UI, or a `--ui` with nothing there, is refused in its words;
  - the UI's 404, 400 and 502 are refused in their words, and its 503 exits 3;
  - `CardRefused`'s three kinds exit as in step 1;
  - a `#` in the id reaches the endpoint quoted;
  - the `completed`, `ambiguous` and no-`[ui]` refusals print their words, and a UI's 503 exits 3;
  - `--json` prints `{"error": …}` for a refusal.
- **The text form of a fixture card** holds, as substrings named in the test:
  - the header, a Waiting card's phrase included;
  - the rows with an archived row opened in its place;
  - each want's items with their row kind, *from*, *on* and *tied by*;
  - a board line's card text, detail and answers;
  - a mail item cut on the UI road;
  - `mentioned`.
- **`--json`** equals `card_view`'s output.
- **`ao --skill`** names the command.
