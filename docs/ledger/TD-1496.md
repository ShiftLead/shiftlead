**Fix:** As §6 rule 13 *Stuck work* says:
- `sessionorc.stuck`: the three kinds of work and their actors — an open `ask` to a session (every addressee, each walked by `_successor`; never a `steer`, nor one whose `to` includes the person, nor one with `passed_up` set, a handed entry's passed-up copy included); a work order `board:<key>` read from the lanes as `ao repo` reads them (every record of a lane that takes it — no seat, which has no lane — the claimant alone once one holds it; a team with no live member that `work.team_wound_down` reads wound down or `work.team_stopped` stopped read by its `work_waiting` (else each member's own state), each team's where two take it, the first's reason in `teams` order; *no lane takes it* where none does); a PR, green or not, that records' newest claims not `done` name by a derived `review_pr` or a declared `pr` (every such record an actor), unless that claim is a waiting claim (rule 5, TD-820): on an `ask`, read as a question instead; on a `steer` alone, not read until the steer closes.
- Acting: `unanswered.read` (called, not copied; `question_at` the work's open time; its other inputs as `MailMixin._unanswered` gathers them) gives `working` (or `idle` with `agent_common.on_its_own`, §4.2 *Working on its own*) with `stalled_ticks` < 2, or a supervised, unattended, not-live seat with no `unfillable` word this tick (`seat_unable`'s exited/closed branch); `_stuck_pass` runs after rule 3's pass. Every other actor is not acting, its state the reason in `unanswered.words`, with rule 13's own words for `stalled` and `idle` (`words` not called for them); a team's `work_waiting.held` in rule 13's words per `why`, *<team> waits for your Start* with no `held`, *<team> is not running* with none, *<team> wound down with it seen and unpicked* where the line is in its members' `lane_seen` (members: the team's records no newer one supersedes; these words replace the members' `ended`/`not_filled`); *released by <name>* for a PR with no actor.
- Never stuck: work whose only actors are the person's own live sessions (`by_person`); a taken-over member is `taken_over`.
- The clock: open time (the ask's `at`; the first tick the line was among `workorders.orders()`, kept with a PR's head in `stuck_open: {<key>: {at, oid?}}` on `host.json` (shape-checked by `HostStore.load`): a line's `at` moved to a claim's `at` and to a release, dropped when the line closes or is undecided; a PR's `at` the first tick that read its current `headRefOid`, moved also to a claim's `at` and a release; the claim's `at`); `since` the later of the open time and the newest state change among the actors (`agent_common.idle_from`; an `idle` actor gives instead, for a question, the later of the `read_at` of the copy in the walk's last record's mailbox and that record's `created`, unread that record's `since`, and for an order or a PR its `stuck_open` `at` — each then the later of that and `subagent_at`/`shells_at`; a `stalled` actor gives the later of `work_at` and `screen_at` (§4.2 *Liveness cross-check*), also its stamp's `since`; every actor's figure floored at the open time), recomputed every tick and stored as recomputed; a row drops when an actor acts, the work closes or `since` is no longer past the wait, recomputed while none acts; stuck when none acts and `since` is older than `STUCK_ASK_AFTER` 60 min, `STUCK_ORDER_AFTER` 60 min, `STUCK_PR_AFTER` 2 h (constants beside `ROUTE_AROUND_AFTER`).
- `_stuck_pass` at the home keeps `stuck: {<key>: {since, reason, actor}}` on `host.json` for the stuck work (keys `ask:<id>`, `board:<key>`, `pr:<repo>#<n>`; `HostStore.load` (src/sessionorc/store.py) and its docstring keep `stuck` and `stuck_open`, shape-checked as `ci_spells`), dropped the tick an actor acts or the work closes; `reason` the first actor's `unanswered` stamp (rule 13's own `{state: stalled|idle, since}`, `{state: team, team, held}`, `{state: released, by}`, `{state: no_lane}`) and `actor` `{id, host, name, more}` (first by an ask's `to` order, an order's lane order then id, a PR's claim `at`), re-read each tick, `needs_you` among the reasons; the sentence and `+n` made at render. It rings no session and presses nothing.
- `waiting_until`: where every actor not acting has a reason with an instant (`scheduled`'s `start_at`, the record's `limited_reset`, `gated`'s `gate.resets`, a team held `until`, `early` as last start plus `WORK_EARLY`, `usage` with `resets`), the earliest, when it is ahead of now by no more than the wait, read from each actor's own reading, never the stored `reason`; recomputed each tick, served with the reading, never stored.
- Serve the stuck reading to the Queue's and the Inbox's readers.

**Done when:** The home's tick keeps `stuck` on `host.json` as §6 rule 13 says. Tests: an open ask whose only actor is a seat `limited` with no reset known is stuck `STUCK_ASK_AFTER` after the later of its `at` and the seat's turn to `limited`, reason `limited`; one whose actor is `working` is not; a multi-addressee ask with one addressee working is not; an ask to a seat idle with it open past the wait reads `idle`, also when other mail woke the seat in between (clocked from the ask's `read_at`); an idle actor with a live background shell is acting; an idle PR holder woken by other mail stays stuck, clocked from its head's last push; one actor `idle` beside one `limited` with a near reset is a need, not waiting; an ask its seat passed up is not read, nor a steer, nor an ask whose `to` includes the person; a team whose members crashed out keeps their own states as reasons; a malformed `stuck_open` entry is dropped on load; an idle actor whose shell ended a minute ago is not stuck; an idle reader that just claimed a PR whose head is hours old is not stuck; a record just flagged `stalled?` is stuck one wait after its stall began, not at once; a seat's fresh fill, its copy carrying the predecessor's `read_at`, is clocked from its `created`; a seat `limited` with a reset ten minutes off gives `waiting_until`; work whose only actor is the person's own session is never stuck; a PR whose holder's claim is a waiting claim is not read as a PR (on a steer alone, not read at all); a work order's `stuck_open` survives a home restart; a work order whose team is held at `day` reads that reason; a PR whose claim was dropped reads *released by <name>*; the entry survives a home restart; the key drops the tick an actor acts.
