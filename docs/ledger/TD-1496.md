**Fix:** As §6 rule 13 *Stuck work* says (the rule is the text: where a bullet here restates it and the two differ, the rule wins). An open PR is not work this build reads (Paul's Hold on TD-1470, 2026-11-10; TD-1571): no `pr:` key, no PR clock, no *released* reading — that is TD-1572's.
- `sessionorc.stuck`: the two kinds of work and their actors — an open `ask` to a session (every addressee, each walked by `_successor`; never a `steer`, nor one whose `to` includes the person, nor one with `passed_up` set, a handed entry's passed-up copy included); a work order `board:<key>` read from the lanes as `ao repo` reads them (every record of a lane that takes it — no seat, which has no lane, nor a member its flow sat out, `work.sat_out` — its holders alone while one holds it: each record `mail.lease_holders(f"board:{key}", records, now)` returns over the board reading's repo's records (declared `claimed` on that ref, the record not in `mail._NOT_LIVE`, `at` inside `LEASE_TTL`), whatever its kind, two under a `--force` both actors, the claim `at` its `claimed` entry's; a claim that is no lease (drop, `done`, exit, untaken restart, lapsed lease) leaves the line to its lanes' records; a team with no live member that `work.team_wound_down` reads wound down or `work.team_stopped` stopped read by its `work_waiting` (else each member's own state), each team's where two take it, the first's reason in `teams` order; *no lane takes it* where none does).
- Acting: `unanswered.read` (called, not copied; `question_at` the work's open time; its other inputs as `MailMixin._unanswered` gathers them) gives `working` (or `idle` with `agent_common.on_its_own`, §4.2 *Working on its own*) with `stalled_ticks` < 2, or a supervised, unattended, not-live seat with no `unfillable` word this tick and not `stop_held` (`seat_unable`'s exited/closed branch); `_stuck_pass` runs after rule 3's pass and reads `_repos` as the tick finds it (the detached repo pass's last readings, loaded from disk at a restart, stale but good), so a reading is absent only for a repo never yet read well, or whose board reading a raised pass left out (a raise drops the board reading, absent, which only the board's own failed read keeps errored; absent and errored read alike). Every other actor is not acting, its state the reason in `unanswered.words`, with rule 13's own words for `stalled` and `idle` (`words` not called for them) and `{state: "unfillable", why}` for a seat the exited or closed branch finds unfillable; a team's `work_waiting.held` in rule 13's words per `why`, *<team> waits for your Start* with no `held`, *<team> is not running* with none, *<team> wound down with it seen and unpicked* where the line is in its members' `lane_seen` (members: the team's records no newer one supersedes; these words replace the members' `ended`/`not_filled`).
- Never stuck: work whose only actors are the person's own live sessions (`by_person`); beside other actors, a person's own session is passed over: not in the acting test, nor `since`, nor the choice of the first actor and its reason, nor the waiting test, nor `more`; a taken-over member is `taken_over`; a line whose `Context:` names a `TD-n` that an open `ask` or `steer` in the person inbox is about, from any sender (`work.waiting_of` over the person inbox; an `about` that is no bare `TD-n` names nothing), is not read while one is open, whatever holds it: its `stuck` entry drops and `stuck_open` is kept, still moved by claims and releases, `asked` set; a `Context:` naming no entry has no such question.
- The clock: open time the ask's `at`, or the first tick the line was among `workorders.orders()`, kept in `stuck_open: {<key>: {at, repo, holder?, asked?}}` on `host.json` (shape-checked by `HostStore.load`, an entry that fails it dropped): a line's `at` the latest of its stored `at` (never moving back), its holders' latest claim `at` while one holds it, the release — the first tick reading no holder where `holder` (`true`, written each tick the line has one; a change of holder is no release) is set, the tick's own instant, `holder` cleared then — and the answer — the first tick reading no such open question where `asked` (`true`, written each tick one is open) is set, the tick's own instant, `asked` cleared then; a tick that is both moves `at` once and clears both —, the entry dropped when a reading that holds the line finds it closed or undecided — a repo whose board reading is absent (a restart before its first pass), errored or fell back to the checkout's (`fetch_case` *skipped*, or no `fetch_case` at all, null: the board pass's plain checkout read) is not read that tick, its `stuck_open` and `stuck` entries kept exactly as stored (on disk, so a restart has them; each `board:` entry of both maps carrying its `repo`, the repo's `root.name` it was read from, since a `board:<key>` names none) and nothing of that repo opening; a repo that leaves the home's roster (`hosts.local_host().repos()`: the registry, `repos_registry` else the home's `repos.txt`, then agent-cadence's imported roster where no registry is set; left when no roster path's basename is the entry's `repo`) drops its entries; a board read that fell back to the checkout may close a line it finds closed or archived there (a closed decided line naming an entry, the only kind the reading's `carried` lists), never one absent from both `items` and `carried`. `since` the later of the open time and the newest state change among the actors (`agent_common.idle_from`; an `idle` actor gives instead, for a question, the later of the `read_at` of the copy in the walk's last record's mailbox and that record's `created`, unread that record's `since`, and for an order its `stuck_open` `at` — each then the later of that and `subagent_at`/`shells_at`; a `stalled` actor gives the later of `work_at` and `screen_at` (§4.2 *Liveness cross-check*), also its stamp's `since`; every actor's figure floored at the open time), recomputed every tick and stored as recomputed (an unread repo's works excepted: as stored); a row drops when an actor acts, the work closes or `since` is no longer past the wait; stuck when none acts and `since` is older than `STUCK_ASK_AFTER` 60 min or `STUCK_ORDER_AFTER` 60 min (constants beside `ROUTE_AROUND_AFTER`).
- `_stuck_pass` at the home keeps `stuck: {<key>: {since, reason, actor, repo?}}` (`repo` on `board:` keys) on `host.json` for the stuck work (keys `ask:<id>`, `board:<key>`; `HostStore.load` (src/sessionorc/store.py) and its docstring keep `stuck` and `stuck_open`, shape-checked as `ci_spells`), dropped the tick an actor acts or the work closes; `reason` the first actor's `unanswered` stamp (rule 13's own `{state: stalled|idle, since}`, `{state: unfillable, why}`, `{state: team, team, held}`, `{state: no_lane}`) and `actor` `{id, host, name, more}` (first by an ask's `to` order, an order's lane order then id; none for a team's), re-read each tick, `needs_you` among the reasons; the sentence and `+n` made at render. It rings no session and presses nothing.
- `waiting_until`: where every actor not acting has a reason with an instant (`scheduled`'s `start_at`, the record's `limited_reset`, `gated`'s `gate.resets`, a team held `until`, `early` as last start plus `WORK_EARLY`, `usage` with `resets`), the earliest, when it is ahead of now by no more than the wait, read from each actor's own reading, never the stored `reason`; recomputed each tick, served with the reading, never stored.
- Serve the stuck reading to the Queue's and the Inbox's readers.
