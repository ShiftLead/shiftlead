**Ask:** Go with TD-1496, a build of TD-1470's design, after its bought sixth round? Answered on the board's end-of-rounds item for TD-1470 (One more round | Go | Hold: <note>).

**Fix:** As §6 rule 13 *Stuck work* says:
- `sessionorc.stuck`: the three kinds of work and their actors — an open `ask` to a session (every addressee, each walked by `_successor`; never a `steer`, nor one whose `to` includes the person, nor one with `passed_up` set, a handed entry's passed-up copy included); a work order `board:<key>` read from the lanes as `ao repo` reads them (every record of a lane that takes it — no seat, which has no lane — the claimant alone once one holds it; a team with no live member that `work.team_wound_down` reads wound down or `work.team_stopped` stopped read by its `work_waiting` (else each member's own state), each team's where two take it, the first's reason in `teams` order; *no lane takes it* where none does); a PR, green or not, that records' newest claims not `done` name by a derived `review_pr` or a declared `pr` (every such record an actor), unless that claim is a waiting claim (rule 5, TD-820): on an `ask`, read as a question instead; on a `steer` alone, not read until the steer closes.
- Acting: `unanswered.read` (called, not copied; `question_at` the work's open time; its other inputs as `MailMixin._unanswered` gathers them) gives `working` (or `idle` with `agent_common.on_its_own`, §4.2 *Working on its own*) with `stalled_ticks` < 2, or a supervised, unattended, not-live seat with no `unfillable` word this tick (`seat_unable`'s exited/closed branch); `_stuck_pass` runs after rule 3's pass and reads `_repos` as the tick finds it (the detached repo pass's last readings, loaded from disk at a restart, stale but good), so a reading is absent only for a repo not yet read or whose read raised. Every other actor is not acting, its state the reason in `unanswered.words`, with rule 13's own words for `stalled` and `idle` (`words` not called for them); a team's `work_waiting.held` in rule 13's words per `why`, *<team> waits for your Start* with no `held`, *<team> is not running* with none, *<team> wound down with it seen and unpicked* where the line is in its members' `lane_seen` (members: the team's records no newer one supersedes; these words replace the members' `ended`/`not_filled`); *released by <name>* for a PR with no actor, `by` the PR's `stuck_open` entry's `by`: the name of its first actor on the last tick it had one, written each tick it has one (a dropped claim's entry, `ao progress drop` with no `--pr`, no longer names the PR); an entry with no `by` reads *released* alone.
- Never stuck: work whose only actors are the person's own live sessions (`by_person`); beside other actors, a person's own session is passed over: not in the acting test, nor `since`, nor the choice of the first actor and its reason, nor the waiting test, nor `more`; a taken-over member is `taken_over`.
- The clock: open time (the ask's `at`; the first tick the line was among `workorders.orders()`, kept with a PR's head in `stuck_open: {<key>: {at, oid?, repo, by?, rel?}}` on `host.json` (shape-checked by `HostStore.load`): a line's `at` moved to a claim's `at` and to a release (the drop's `at`), dropped when a reading that holds the line finds it closed or undecided, a PR's when a good PR reading no longer lists it open (one listing 200, `pr_reading`'s cap, drops none) or, on a tick with no actor, when the record its `by` names holds a `done` naming the PR (no work; any other PR with no actor is *released*) — a repo whose board or PR reading is absent (a restart before its first pass), errored or fell back to the checkout's (`fetch_case` *skipped*, or none: the board pass's plain checkout read) is not read that tick, its `stuck_open` and `stuck` entries kept as they were (the works their `stuck_open` and `stuck` entries name, on disk, so a restart has them — each `board:` and `pr:` entry of both maps carrying its `repo`, the repo's `root.name` it was read from, since a `board:<key>` names none; each kind unread by its own reading — a `board:` entry while the board reading is absent, errored or skipped (a skipped board read may only close, below), a `pr:` entry while the PR reading is absent or errored; a repo that leaves the home's roster (`hosts.local_host().repos()`: the registry, `repos_registry` else the home's `repos.txt`, then agent-cadence's imported roster where no registry is set; left when no roster path's basename is the entry's `repo`) dropping its entries: every work of that repo, a PR's and a board line's alike (a fallback read's close aside), exactly as stored until a good read, a PR's row dropping a tick late, and nothing of that repo opens — a PR a claim first names is read once a PR reading says it is open); a board read that fell back to the checkout may close a line it finds closed or archived there (in the reading's `carried`), never one absent from both `items` and `carried`; a PR's open time the newest claim among its actors' claims, derived each tick, else (a released PR) its `stuck_open.at`, which is read only for that and an idle actor's figure; a PR's `at` the latest of the first tick that read its current head (the PR row's `head`) (moved on every push), the newest claim's `at` among its actors, and its release, `rel` (written on the first tick that reads it with no actor, cleared on any tick that reads one)); `since` the later of the open time and the newest state change among the actors (`agent_common.idle_from`; an `idle` actor gives instead, for a question, the later of the `read_at` of the copy in the walk's last record's mailbox and that record's `created`, unread that record's `since`, and for an order or a PR its `stuck_open` `at` — each then the later of that and `subagent_at`/`shells_at`; a `stalled` actor gives the later of `work_at` and `screen_at` (§4.2 *Liveness cross-check*), also its stamp's `since`; every actor's figure floored at the open time), recomputed every tick and stored as recomputed (an unread repo's works excepted: as stored); a row drops when an actor acts, the work closes or `since` is no longer past the wait, recomputed while none acts; stuck when none acts and `since` is older than `STUCK_ASK_AFTER` 60 min, `STUCK_ORDER_AFTER` 60 min, `STUCK_PR_AFTER` 2 h (constants beside `ROUTE_AROUND_AFTER`).
- `_stuck_pass` at the home keeps `stuck: {<key>: {since, reason, actor, repo?}}` (`repo` on `board:` and `pr:` keys) on `host.json` for the stuck work (keys `ask:<id>`, `board:<key>`, `pr:<repo>#<n>`; `HostStore.load` (src/sessionorc/store.py) and its docstring keep `stuck` and `stuck_open`, shape-checked as `ci_spells`), dropped the tick an actor acts or the work closes; `reason` the first actor's `unanswered` stamp (rule 13's own `{state: stalled|idle, since}`, `{state: team, team, held}`, `{state: released, by}`, `{state: no_lane}`) and `actor` `{id, host, name, more}` (first by an ask's `to` order, an order's lane order then id, a PR's claim `at`), re-read each tick, `needs_you` among the reasons; the sentence and `+n` made at render. It rings no session and presses nothing.
- `waiting_until`: where every actor not acting has a reason with an instant (`scheduled`'s `start_at`, the record's `limited_reset`, `gated`'s `gate.resets`, a team held `until`, `early` as last start plus `WORK_EARLY`, `usage` with `resets`), the earliest, when it is ahead of now by no more than the wait, read from each actor's own reading, never the stored `reason`; recomputed each tick, served with the reading, never stored.
- Serve the stuck reading to the Queue's and the Inbox's readers.
