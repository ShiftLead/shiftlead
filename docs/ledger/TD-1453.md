## TD-1453: What needs the person lands in the Inbox or on the Queue by whether its entry holds an open card; one primary place per kind

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger and the techlead seat; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 7fa3eb3a35b34d5fb2c9a61b782593f6
**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (`item_home`, `attach`, `asked_entries`); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (`inbox_sections`); the Queue's rank read (`ao rank --list`, docs/ledger/rank.yml per repo); design §4.5 screen 6 *Where a row is drawn*, screen 12 *What is attached to a card*, §4.4 *The card's status*, §4.10

**Why:** Asked by Paul through the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-6b440cdfdbb0): review what reaches the person where and when, across the Inbox, the Queue and the board.

Seen 2026-11-06. agent-cadence's grinder asked *TD-221: merge #164?* (m-ab094b32f47e, about TD-221), and the ask showed only in the Inbox, with no Queue card. `ao rank --list` held no `agent-cadence#TD-221` card while the entry was being worked. It listed only 6 agent-cadence entries, and no ledgerboard ones while TD-053 and TD-046 were in flight. Earlier the same day, shiftlead's *merge #2996?* ask on TD-1388 slice 1 did show on its Queue card. So the person sees merge asks in two places, depending on the repo or the entry's state. Paul expected the Queue.

The rule as built: `item_home` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py:46`; §4.5 screen 6 *Where a row is drawn*, screen 12 *What is attached to a card*) puts a question, steer or note on the Queue only when its `about` (or the first `TD-n` in its text) names an entry that an open card holds. Everything else goes to the Inbox: *an entry on a completed card or found nowhere*. Design §4.4 *The card's status* says *needs you* is an attached question. So an ask about an entry with no open card, because the entry is in flight and unranked, archived by the very PR the ask is about, or in a repo the Queue under-reads, never makes a card read *needs you*.

**Fix:** Design first (§4.5 screens 6 and 12, §4.4 *The card's status*, §4.10). Set one rule for each kind of thing that needs the person:
- a merge ask (a PR's reader, a held path);
- a decide item on a board;
- an act or do item on a board;
- an answer to the person's own **Add entry**;
- a seat that is limited or unable (TD-1443).

Each kind gets one primary place, and any other place only mirrors it. Every *needs you* lands on a Queue card even when its entry is not ranked: an entry in flight, one archived by the PR in question, or one in a repo the Queue under-reads. The design says how that card is found or made: a card for an unranked entry, or a placeholder card per repo. It also says why the Queue lists only part of a repo's entries (six of agent-cadence's, none of ledgerboard's in flight) and whether that is the cause or a separate gap. Paul expects the Queue to be the primary place.

**Done when:** The design names, for each kind of item that needs the person, its one primary place and its mirrors, and says how an item about an unranked entry reaches a Queue card. Its builds carry `Part of: TD-1453`. A test: an ask about an in-flight entry that has no rank row draws on a Queue card as *needs you*.
