**Fix:** Design first (§4.5 screens 6 and 12, §4.4 *The card's status*, §4.10). **Paul's starting principle** (m-b3f3df732ba0, the rule the design starts from): anything that affects current work goes to the Queue, on its entry's card. That includes merge asks, decide items and a stuck seat. Reminders and things done outside a team go to the Inbox, which then works as his to-do list.

From that, set one rule for each kind of thing that needs the person:
- a merge ask (a PR's reader, a held path);
- a decide item on a board;
- an act or do item on a board;
- an answer to the person's own **Add entry**;
- a seat that is limited or unable (TD-1443).

Each kind gets one primary place, and any other place only mirrors it. Every *needs you* lands on a Queue card even when its entry has no open card: an entry the asked-about PR archives, an entry already archived, or an entry in a repo the Queue does not read. The design says which card that is: the completed card the entry was on, kept in view while an ask about it is open, or a card for the PR itself. The ask came before #164 merged, so a card was expected. Find out which way it missed:
- whether `about` held `agent-cadence#TD-221` or a bare `TD-221`. `card_index` (`src/sessionorc/queue.py:81`) keys a bare id only when one repo's card holds it, archived rows on open cards included, and shiftlead has its own archived TD-221;
- `item_home` reads only the first ref (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py:83`);
- whether the agent-cadence checkout the Queue read was behind origin when the ask arrived.

Also settle how an ask leaves *Needs you* once nothing waits on it (the session's suggestion):
- an ask that carries a PR (its `pr` field, or `#n` in its subject) closes itself with the outcome *merged* when that PR merges, whoever merges it;
- an ask whose entry is archived or dropped is settled too: it closes, or moves out of *Needs you* into a list of settled asks the person can clear in one press.

Paul corrected this (m-ddbb6b494291): an asker being gone is not a reason to close or demote an ask, since a question can still be valid after its session winds down. Only the question being settled closes it: its PR merged, or its entry archived or dropped. TD-213's rule, which keeps an orphaned ask about a PR or an entry open, stands.

**Done when:** The design names, for each kind of item that needs the person, its one primary place and its mirrors, and says which card an ask about an archived or unread entry draws on.
