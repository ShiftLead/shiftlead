## TD-1212: A fact-check seat preset: a seat that reads a doc-only PR it is asked about and answers with a verdict and its findings

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-29 (the designer, from TD-857's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — build
**Part of:** TD-857
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py` (`PRESETS`, beside `auditor` and `sweeper`); `src/shiftlead/briefs/fact-checker.md` (new); design §4.9b *The fact-check*, §4.8 roles table

**Why:** TD-857: Gemini's first job is the doc-only fact-check, held by a seat. No preset reads a PR on request and answers without posting, editing or merging.

**Fix:** as design §4.9b *The fact-check* (*The preset*, *What it does*) says. (1) `PRESETS["fact-checker"]`: `kind: seat`, `lane: []`, `grants: []`, `context: WORKER_CONTEXT`, `label: "Fact-check"`, an icon from §4.8's fixed set, and a `message` naming what it is asked (a doc-only PR to fact-check). (2) `fact-checker.md`, the built-in brief. It reads each waiting ask (`ao inbox --unread --json`) and works in its home, `<repo>/.claude/worktrees/<name>` (§4.9 *Home and reach*). It fetches, reads the named head by refs (`git show`, `git diff origin/<default>...<sha>`) and never the author's tree, then re-derives each claim the ask names and each one the diff makes. It edits nothing, opens no PR, posts nothing on the PR, and runs no `ao` but `ao inbox` and `ao msg`. It replies with `ao msg --reply-to <id>`: first `cadence-review: SHIP|BLOCK · <adapter>/<model> · docs · <n> findings`, its `<adapter>/<model>` the first line of `$AGENT_MODEL_FILE`, else `$AGENT_MODEL`, used unchanged; then each finding (the claim, file:line, what the repo says). A head not on origin is `BLOCK` with that finding. It ends when no ask waits. (3) Tests: the preset resolves with a `trigger: asks` seat line, `ao roles` lists it, and the brief renders with its slots filled.

**Done when** a team's `seats:` line `{name: …, role: fact-checker, profile: …, trigger: asks}` starts a seat on the built-in brief, and the brief says what §4.9b *The fact-check* says the seat does.
