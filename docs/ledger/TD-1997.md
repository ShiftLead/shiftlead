## TD-1997: A press outside a row shows nothing until its answer: mark the pressed control at once

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-19
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 03b8624583fa482fa6966d20805af7a3
**Part of:** TD-1968

**Why:** Paul pressed **Start session** in the header of the Queue card around TD-794 on 2026-11-18, and for 6 to 8 seconds nothing on the page changed before the new session's Focus opened. The press looked lost. The cause is general: the page's press handler marks a press only inside a row (`pressed()` in `app.js` returns at once with no enclosing `.mailrow`), so every control that sits in no row — a Queue card header's Start session and Open session, a session card's and the Focus page's controls, the Repo page's Promote — draws nothing until its answer. TD-1968 designed what such a press shows.

**Location:** design §4.5 screen 6 rule 2 (A press with no row), screen 12 Three verbs: Start session; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the press handler, pressed(), PRESS_VERBS)

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 6 rule 2's *A press with no row* and screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*'s *Its press answers in its frame* (TD-1968), as §4.5a's *every page: a control's look*, *Inbox row: pending* and *Queue card: Start session / Open session* rows say:
- In `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`'s document click handler, where `pressed()` finds no `.mailrow`, mark the control itself in the press's frame, before its request leaves: disabled, `aria-busy`, its width held (set its `min-width` to its width before the label changes), its label (kept in a data attribute) giving way to its verb — the button's own `data-verb` where it carries one, else `AO.pressVerb(key)`, else its own label with `…` (never a second `…` on a label already ending in one, and never the wire name, the row's fallback). A badge or chip (`.badge`, a grant's chip; the `start` and `stop` time badges with `data-ask`) keeps its words, only disabled and `aria-busy`.
- Twins: also mark every control on the page with the same `data-act` and the same target (the Focus page's Close in its bar, its ⋯ and `#closebtn`; a card's two Restarts), and the other half of an Allow/Deny pair.
- A control inside a `details.more` menu (a Queue card's ⋯ is `details.more.qdots`, its toggle `summary.qdotsbtn`) puts its verb on the menu's `summary` with `aria-busy` (a `<summary>` has no `disabled`, so keep it openable), and disables the pressed entry and its twins inside the menu.
- Keyboard focus: a disabled focused control drops the page's keys, so move focus to the marked control again after marking (a menu entry's to its `summary`), or mark with `aria-disabled` plus an in-flight guard in the handler; either way the page's keys still answer.
- `PRESS_VERBS` gains `start: "starting…"` (Start now), `kill: "killing…"`, `close: "closing…"`, `remove: "removing…"`, `stop: "stopping…"`, `wrapup: "sending…"` and `message: "sending…"`. Buttons whose verb is not their wire name's carry `data-verb`: Open session (`queue_card.html`'s header, `inbox_row.html`'s item and ledger-row buttons) `data-verb="opening…"`, a scheduled session's Cancel (`data-act="close"` in `focus.html` and `card.html`) `data-verb="cancelling…"`, Forget (`data-act="remove"` in `card.html`) `data-verb="forgetting…"`. A row's mark takes `data-verb` first too.
- The answer ends the mark, whatever its shape. A press whose answer moves the page (`ledger_start`'s Focus or form, Forget on its own Focus) leaves it marked; a back-forward return to the page draws it unmarked (drop the key on the answer even after `location.href` is set; restore from the bfcache with the mark cleared, e.g. on `pageshow` with `persisted`). One that keeps the page — a toast, or none, where `kill`, `close`, `mode`, `remove` answer by the card's `/events` swap or removal — ends it: a control the answer takes away just goes; one still on the page comes back on with the label the page draws now (a toggle such as `#fmodeact`, `#closebtn`, `#fclosemenu` or a grants chip, which the Focus page redraws in place on each delta, takes its new label, never the one kept at the press). A refusal puts it back and toasts the handler's `<named control> failed: <words>`, red: add `named` entries (near `app.js` line 2163) for the no-row controls and Open session so the toast names the control, never its wire name.
- In flight: hold the press in `AO.inflight` under a prefixed key, `press:<wire name>:<target>`, the target the session's id for a session's control (`data-id` not `person`), else `data-repo`/`data-ref`/`data-card`/`data-item` where `data-id` is `person` (every Queue header Start/Open session shares that `data-id`, so it alone would collide), and the repo for Promote. Drop the key on the answer. Make sure the existing scans of `AO.inflight` (the ones near the Queue's re-read and the `qcard:` loops) skip that prefix. Re-mark the matching control at the three places the page redraws a press in flight: `AO.restoreRowErrs` (whose rows branch is `if (f.leaves) r.remove(); else AO.markPending(r, f.verb)` — not `AO.redraw`, which only redraws decided and reply rows) for the Inbox and the Queue, the session card's swap on its `/events` delta, and the Focus page's redraw of its controls on its delta (around `app.js` lines 8905 and 9096–9123).
- Out of scope: a press that asks first (`data-confirm`, a prompt such as Stop's, a composer such as Message's) is marked once it is answered; `data-copy`, `popout` and `shell-here` mark nothing; a control with its own listener keeps its own mark — the transcript's Earlier (`transcript-earlier`, its listener in `AO.transcript`), a team's Start and Stop, Apply flow, the member form's Start, a Settings save; a Queue card ⋯ press that `qpressed()` draws keeps that drawing and no second mark.
- Tests that pin the handler's text: `tests/test_ui_inbox.py` (around line 3715, the `pressed(key === "board_decide" ? …)` / `act(...)` lines and the waiting toast; the restore branch's `markPending` line) and `tests/test_ui_queue.py` (around line 1155, `if (qt) qpressed(); else pressed();`). Keep those lines intact, or update the pins with the change; `test_every_inbox_control_has_its_verb_under_way` parses `PRESS_VERBS`.
- New tests (tests/ui): the Queue card header's Start session is marked — disabled, `aria-busy`, *starting…*, its width unchanged — before its `fetch` leaves, and a refusal puts its label back with a toast *Start session failed: …*; two Queue headers' Start sessions on different cards hold separate in-flight keys; Open session reads *opening…*; a session card's Kill reads *killing…* while its request is out, its twin is off too, it stays marked across a card swap, and the mark ends when the card's delta answers with no toast; a ⋯ entry's press shows its verb on the `summary`, which still opens.

Fixed, not reviewed; the builder checks it: none yet.
