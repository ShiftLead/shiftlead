## TD-1997: A press outside a row shows nothing until its answer: mark the pressed control at once

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-19
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 03b8624583fa482fa6966d20805af7a3
**Part of:** TD-1968

**Why:** Paul pressed **Start session** in the header of the Queue card around TD-794 on 2026-11-18, and for 6 to 8 seconds nothing on the page changed before the new session's Focus opened. The press looked lost. The cause is general: the page's press handler marks a press only inside a row (`pressed()` in `app.js` returns at once with no enclosing `.mailrow`), so every control that sits in no row — a Queue card header's Start session and Open session, a session card's and the Focus page's controls, the Repo page's Promote — draws nothing until its answer. TD-1968 designed what such a press shows.

**Location:** design §4.5 screen 6 rule 2 (A press with no row), screen 12 Three verbs: Start session; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the press handler, pressed(), PRESS_VERBS)

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 6 rule 2's *A press with no row* and screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*'s *Its press answers in its frame* (TD-1968), as §4.5a's *every page: a control's look*, *Inbox row: pending* and *Queue card: Start session / Open session* rows say:
- In `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`'s document click handler, where `pressed()` finds no `.mailrow`, mark the control itself in the press's frame, before its request leaves: disabled, `aria-busy`, its label (kept in a data attribute) giving way to `AO.pressVerb(key)`, or to its own label with `…` where `PRESS_VERBS` has no key (never the wire name, the row's fallback). A control inside a `details.more` menu (a Queue card's ⋯ is `details.more.qdots`, its toggle `summary.qdotsbtn`) marks the menu's `summary` instead, since the menu folds on the press.
- `PRESS_VERBS` gains `start: "starting…"` (Start now), `kill: "killing…"`, `close: "closing…"`, `remove: "removing…"`, `stop: "stopping…"`, `wrapup: "sending…"` and `ledger_open: "opening…"`. The Open session buttons (`queue_card.html`'s header, `inbox_row.html`'s item and ledger-row buttons) gain an attribute such as `data-open="1"`, and the `ledger_start` press uses the `ledger_open` key where it is set — never by reading the label. Controls with no key (`mode`, `drop`, `grants`, `uncontrol`, …) take the label-with-`…` fallback.
- The mark holds until the answer. A press whose answer moves the page (`ledger_start`'s Focus or form, `remove` on its own Focus) leaves it marked; one that keeps the page puts the label back and the control on again once its toast is up; a refusal does the same and toasts the server's words, red.
- A redraw while the request is out draws the control marked again. Hold the press in `AO.inflight` under a prefixed key, `press:<wire name>:<target>` (the target `data-id`, else `data-repo`/`data-ref`/`data-card`), and make sure the existing scans of `AO.inflight` (the ones near the Queue's re-read and the `qcard:` loops) skip that prefix. Re-mark the matching control where the page's swaps restore a press in flight: `AO.restoreRowErrs` (whose rows branch is `if (f.leaves) r.remove(); else AO.markPending(r, f.verb)` — not `AO.redraw`, which only redraws decided and reply rows) for the Inbox and the Queue, and the session card's swap on its `/events` delta.
- Out of scope: a press that asks first (`data-confirm`, a prompt such as Stop's, a composer) is marked once it is answered; `data-copy`, `popout` and `shell-here` mark nothing; the transcript's Earlier (`transcript-earlier`) keeps its own listener in `AO.transcript`, which already disables it while it loads; a Queue card ⋯ press that `qpressed()` draws keeps that drawing and no second mark.
- Tests that pin the handler's text: `tests/test_ui_inbox.py` (around line 3715, the `pressed(key === "board_decide" ? …)` / `act(...)` lines and the waiting toast; the restore branch's `markPending` line) and `tests/test_ui_queue.py` (around line 1155, `if (qt) qpressed(); else pressed();`). Keep those lines intact, or update the pins with the change; `test_every_inbox_control_has_its_verb_under_way` parses `PRESS_VERBS`.
- New tests (tests/ui): the Queue card header's Start session is marked — disabled, `aria-busy`, *starting…* — before its `fetch` leaves, and a refusal puts its label back; Open session reads *opening…*; a session card's Kill reads *killing…* while its request is out and stays marked across a card swap.
