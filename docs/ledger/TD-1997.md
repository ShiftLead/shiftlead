## TD-1997: A press outside a row shows nothing until its answer: mark the pressed control at once

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-19
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 03b8624583fa482fa6966d20805af7a3
**Part of:** TD-1968

**Why:** Paul pressed **Start session** in the header of the Queue card around TD-794 on 2026-11-18, and for 6 to 8 seconds nothing on the page changed before the new session's Focus opened. The press looked lost. The cause is general: the page's press handler marks a press only inside a row (`pressed()` in `app.js` returns at once with no enclosing `.mailrow`), so every control that sits in no row — a Queue card header's Start session and Open session, a session card's and the Focus page's controls, the Repo page's Promote — draws nothing until its answer. TD-1968 designed what such a press shows.

**Location:** design §4.5 screen 6 rule 2 (A press with no row), screen 12 Three verbs: Start session; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the press handler, pressed(), PRESS_VERBS)

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 6 rule 2's *A press with no row* and screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*'s *Its press answers in its frame* (TD-1968), as §4.5a's *every page: a control's look*, *Inbox row: pending* and *Queue card: Start session / Open session* rows say:
- In `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`'s press handler, where `pressed()` finds no row, mark the control itself in the press's frame, before its request leaves: disabled, `aria-busy`, its label (kept in a data attribute) giving way to `AO.pressVerb(key)`, or to its own label with `…` where `PRESS_VERBS` has no key (never the wire name, the row's fallback). A control inside a `details.more` or ⋯ menu marks the menu's `summary` toggle instead, since the menu folds on the press.
- `PRESS_VERBS` gains `kill: "killing…"`, `close: "closing…"`, `remove: "removing…"`, `stop: "stopping…"`, `"transcript-earlier": "loading…"`, `wrapup: "sending…"` and `ledger_open: "opening…"`; the `ledger_start` press uses the `ledger_open` key when the button reads *Open session*.
- The mark holds until the answer. A press whose answer moves the page (`ledger_start`'s Focus or form, `remove` on its own Focus) leaves it marked; one that keeps the page puts the label back and the control on again once its toast is up; a refusal does the same and toasts the server's words, red.
- A redraw while the request is out (a session card's swap on its `/events` delta, a Queue re-read) draws the control marked again: hold the press in `AO.inflight` keyed by the wire name and target (`data-id`, else `data-repo`/`data-ref`/`data-card`), and re-mark the matching control after the swap, as `AO.redraw` does for rows.
- A press that asks first (`data-confirm`, a prompt such as Stop's, a composer) is marked once it is answered; `data-copy`, `popout` and `shell-here` mark nothing; a Queue card ⋯ press that `qpressed()` draws keeps that drawing and no second mark.
- A test (tests/ui) holds that the Queue card header's Start session is marked — disabled, `aria-busy`, *starting…* — before its `fetch` leaves, and that a refusal puts its label back; another that a session card's Kill reads *killing…* while its request is out.

Fixed, not reviewed; the builder checks it: none yet.

While there, not part of done: note in the PR where `/api/ledger/start`'s seconds go (the worktree's creation, the branch, the session's launch), in case one of them can answer sooner.
