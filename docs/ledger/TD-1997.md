## TD-1997: A press outside a row shows nothing until its answer: mark the pressed control at once

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt

**Why:** Paul pressed **Start session** in the header of the Queue card around TD-794 on 2026-11-18, and for 6 to 8 seconds nothing on the page changed before the new session's Focus opened. The press looked lost. The cause is general: the page's press handler marks a press only inside a row (`pressed()` in `app.js` returns at once with no enclosing `.mailrow`), so every control that sits in no row — a Queue card header's Start session and Open session, a session card's and the Focus page's controls — draws nothing until its answer. TD-1968 designed what such a press shows.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design for marking every press outside a row, or run one more review round first?

**Location:** design §4.5 screen 6 rule 2 (A press with no row), screen 12 Three verbs: Start session; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the press handler, pressed(), PRESS_VERBS)
