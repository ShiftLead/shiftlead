## TD-1997: A press outside a row shows nothing until its answer: mark the pressed control at once

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-19
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 03b8624583fa482fa6966d20805af7a3
**Part of:** TD-1968

**Why:** Paul pressed **Start session** in the header of the Queue card around TD-794 on 2026-11-18, and for 6 to 8 seconds nothing on the page changed before the new session's Focus opened. The press looked lost. The cause is general: the page's press handler marks a press only inside a row (`pressed()` in `app.js` returns at once with no enclosing `.mailrow`), so every control that sits in no row — a Queue card header's Start session and Open session, a session card's and the Focus page's controls — draws nothing until its answer. TD-1968 designed what such a press shows.

**Location:** design §4.5 screen 6 rule 2 (A press with no row), screen 12 Three verbs: Start session; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the press handler, pressed(), PRESS_VERBS)

**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 6 rule 2's *A press with no row* and screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*'s *Its press answers in its frame* (TD-1968), as §4.5a's *every page: a control's look*, *Inbox row: pending* and *Queue card: Start session / Open session* rows say:
- In `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`'s document click handler, where `pressed()` finds no `.mailrow`, mark the control itself in the press's frame, before its request leaves: `aria-disabled="true"` (styled as `:disabled` is, and the click handler and the page's keys sending nothing from a control whose press is in flight — the guard placed before `data-confirm` (app.js ~1651) and the Stop and Start prompts, so a second press on a marked control never asks again — never the `disabled` property, which drops keyboard focus), `aria-busy`, its width held (set its `min-width` to its width before the label changes), its label (kept in a data attribute) giving way to its verb — the button's own `data-verb` where it carries one, else `AO.pressVerb(key)`, else its own label with `…` (never a second `…` on a label already ending in one, and never the wire name, the row's fallback). A badge or chip (any `.badge` — the Start time badge with `data-ask`, the Stop badges with `data-until` — and a grant's chip) keeps its words, only disabled and `aria-busy`.
- Twins: also mark every control on the page with the same `data-act` and the same target (the Focus page's Close in its bar, its ⋯ and `#closebtn`, and a scheduled session's Cancel, also `close`; a card's two Restarts), and the other half of an Allow/Deny pair, each twin showing its own verb by the same order (its `data-verb`, the table, its own label with `…`).
- A control inside a `details.more` menu (a Queue card's ⋯ is `details.more.qdots`, its toggle `summary.qdotsbtn`) puts its verb on the menu's `summary` with `aria-busy` (a `<summary>` has no `disabled`, so keep it openable), and disables the pressed entry and its twins inside the menu.
- Keyboard focus stays on the marked control (a menu entry's moves to its `summary`), so the page's keys still answer.
- `PRESS_VERBS` gains `start: "starting…"` (Start now), `kill: "killing…"`, `close: "closing…"`, `remove: "removing…"`, `wrapup: "sending…"` and `message: "sending…"`. Buttons whose verb is not their wire name's carry `data-verb`: Open session (`queue_card.html`'s header, `inbox_row.html`'s item and ledger-row buttons) `data-verb="opening…"`, a scheduled session's Cancel (`data-act="close"` in `focus.html` and `card.html`) `data-verb="cancelling…"`, Forget (`data-act="remove"` in `card.html`) `data-verb="forgetting…"`. A row's mark takes `data-verb` first too: every site that computes a row's verb from `AO.pressVerb(key)` (in and around `pressed()`, app.js ~1593-1629, and ~2042-2046) reads `b.dataset.verb` first, or an item's Open session still reads *starting…*. (`stop` gets no entry: its only control is the time badge, which keeps its words.)
- The answer ends the mark; its end event, per shape: A press whose answer moves the page (`ledger_start`'s Focus or form, Forget on its own Focus, any other press whose answer sets `location.href`) leaves it marked: set a flag on the press before `location.href` is set, and have the handler's `finally` (~2176-2182) and the toast skip the unmark when it is set; a back-forward return to the page draws it unmarked (drop the key on the answer even after `location.href` is set; restore from the bfcache with the mark cleared, e.g. on `pageshow` with `persisted`). One that keeps the page ends at the response (the handler's `finally`, app.js ~2176-2182), as its toast comes up. One whose effect its card's or the Focus page's `/events` delta draws (`kill`, `close`, `mode`, `start`, `remove` off its own Focus) ends at the first redraw of that card or page after the response, or 5 s after the response where none comes — toast or not (Start now toasts *starts on the next tick*, app.js ~2121, and still waits for the redraw) — never at the response alone, which can beat the delta and leave Kill or Close pressable again before the card shows the effect. Unmarking restores the state the page last drew, never "enabled": the Focus delta writes `#closebtn.disabled`, `#fclosemenu.disabled`, `#fclose`'s and `#fmodeact`'s `hidden`, and the text and `data-unattended` of both mode twins `#fmodeact` and `#fmodemenu` itself (app.js ~8903-8910, ~9096-9123), so keep the mark in `aria-disabled`/`aria-busy`/a data attribute apart from those writes, let the delta write them while the press is out without clearing the mark, and on unmarking remove only what the mark added (the label comes back as the page last drew it, a toggle's new state, never the one kept at the press). When `AO.remarkPresses` re-marks a control whose text the delta has just written (`#fmodeact`, `#fmodemenu`), it takes that fresh text as the kept label before putting the verb on. A control the answer takes away just goes. A refusal puts it back and toasts the handler's `<named control> failed: <words>`, red: add `named` entries (near `app.js` line 2163) for the no-row controls and Open session, and make the fallback the control's kept label, never its wire name (`named[action] || label`).
