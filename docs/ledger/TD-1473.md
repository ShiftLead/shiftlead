**Why:** TD-1443's design, first build. Seen 2026-11-06: `fact-check-ao-1`, limited on Gemini's daily quota for about a day, took `ao td add`'s ask overnight (m-e5f321db6cd6) and never answered it. `_question_seat` skips only a closed or superseded record; `_fillable` passes any record not closed; `_one_seat` ranks a live (limited, stalled?) record over an exited one; `rpc_entry_add` takes the team's fixed techlead id whatever its state; nothing re-routes a waiting ask.

**Fix:** Design §4.9b *A seat that cannot answer is routed around*, §4.10 *to a question seat*, §4.5a (*Inbox row: routed around*, the card's *routed around* line), §4.7 (`ao inbox`, `ao msg`'s reply) (TD-1443). Moving a question already waiting is TD-1480's.
- One predicate, `seat_able(record, now)` (src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py, beside `_fillable`): false for `stalled?` whose `stalled_ticks` (TD-1475's count, node-owned on the record) is at least 2 — the stall test compares no clock across hosts; for `limited` whose reset (`limited_reset`, TD-1474's, kept for the spell) is unknown, more than `ROUTE_AROUND_AFTER` (30 min — `TECHLEAD_WAIT`'s figure, which lives in the briefs and design only; a new constant in src/sessionorc/mail.py beside `ASK_BOUND`) away, or already passed while the state stays `limited`; for a live seat the usage gate paused (§6) whose `gated.next` (when the line next moves or the window resets, whichever is sooner) is unknown or past `ROUTE_AROUND_AFTER` (a reading under the line may lift it sooner; while the mark is set the seat reads unable unless `gated.next` is within `ROUTE_AROUND_AFTER`, and it is able again when the gate's resume clears the mark, no sooner than `RESUME_MIN`); for `exited` or `closed` that rule 3 would not fill this tick — not a supervised unattended seat, suspended or sat out (`_seat_pass`), its own `restart_ceiling` held, its profile gated (`_profile_gated`), or the fill ceiling reached (`_fill`, `agent_tick.py`) — the home's tick, where rule 3 runs, keeps in memory `unfillable: {record_id: why}` (why one of *suspended*, *sat out*, *restart ceiling*, *profile gated*, *fill ceiling*; an exited or closed record with no `supervised` mark reads unable as *not supervised* without an entry, `_fill`'s pass never visiting it), written each tick for every seat by `_seat_pass` (which returns before `_fill` for a suspended or sat-out seat, and calls `_fill` only when the seat is due) and by `_fill`, and read by `seat_able`; empty before the first tick means fillable; `seat_able` runs only on the home, as `_msg` does, reading node-owned fields from the record's replica (an unreachable node's seat from its last replica); true otherwise. `_one_seat` is a `@staticmethod` today: it gains the clock and the settings it needs.
- `_question_seat` and `_one_seat` order able records first, then `tags`, then live over exited over closed.
- **A general question** (no `pr`, no `entry`) to a seat that is not able — by `--team`/`--repo`, or by the seat's id (`rpc_msg` → `_msg`, the `asks_seat` edge in src/sessionorc/mail.py) — goes to its team's techlead seat when able, else to the person: an `ask` to the person like any other (`bound` none, its answers kept, its Queue item led by the text's first sentence), never to another `asks` seat. Its envelope carries `routed: {seat, name, why, at}`, `why` the state, the reset for `limited` or a gated pause, and for an exited or closed seat its `unfillable` word. At the send, a routed ask to the person is refused as any send to the person is (the sender's open question there on the same `about`, a full depth), the refusal naming it.
- **A reader's `ask`** (carrying `pr`) is never re-seated: with its reader unable it goes to the person as a new ask on its own thread, as one past `review.bound` does, carrying `routed`; the PR reads as asked (`ao pr held`, the Repo page), the ask being out; it carries no suggested answers and owes no outcome, the person's reply being the PR's read and closing it.
- **A handed entry** (`rpc_entry_add`, src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py): a blank Team pick goes to the first team of `teams` whose techlead is able (`entry.team` and the returned `team` that team's, and `entry.picked` false; `entry.picked` is true only when the caller supplied `team`, false otherwise even with one servicing team), else lands on the first team's seat and waits; a pick the person made is kept (the CLI already puts it first, cli.py) and waits on that team's techlead. A session's own ledger request (TD-995: `entry`, no `handed` mark) waits on its seat, its reply saying so: the `entry` envelope, handed or not, waits; it never takes the general question's road. (Moving a waiting one when a techlead turns able is TD-1480's pass.)
- **The words**: the Inbox row's line *routed around <name>: <why>* (src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py, the Queue's mail item and the message page alike), *waits: <name> <state>, resets HH:MMZ* under *Waiting on them*, `ao inbox`'s indented line and `ao msg`'s reply line (src/shiftlead/cli.py), and the seat card's *its questions go to <the techlead | you> while it cannot answer* (src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py).

**Done when:** Tests: a general ask to a team whose techlead is `limited` with no reset goes to the person carrying `routed` with `bound` none, never to the team's review seat; a seat `limited` with a reset 10 minutes away still takes it; one whose `limited_reset` passed while still `limited` is routed around; a seat with `stalled_ticks` 1 still takes it, with 2 it is routed around; a live seat the usage gate paused with no `gated.next` is routed around, one whose `next` is 15 minutes away is not; an exited seat that is suspended, or whose restart ceiling holds, or whose profile is gated, or that the fill ceiling holds, is routed around, one rule 3 can fill is not; a worker's `ao msg <techlead> --kind ask` to a `limited` techlead goes to the person carrying `routed`; an `ask` carrying `pr` whose reader is `stalled?` goes to the person as a new ask with no suggested answers, and `ao pr held` reads the PR as asked and, after the person's reply, read; `ao td add` with a blank pick and the first team's techlead limited goes to the next servicing team's able techlead (`entry.team` that team's), one with the person's own pick waits, and a session's own ledger request waits; the person's row, `ao inbox` and the sender's reply print *routed around <name>: <why>*, and the seat's card its *routed around* line while unable. A routed ask the sender already holds an open copy of with the person is refused at the send, naming it; an exited seat's routed line names its `unfillable` word. A seat whose gate mark is set after a reading under the line, the resume not yet run, is still unable; `ao td add` with no `--team` and one servicing team writes `entry.picked` false. `seat_able` reads TD-1474's parsed `limited_reset`.
