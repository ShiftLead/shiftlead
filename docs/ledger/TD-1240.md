## TD-1240: With every team card folded, the repo's line draws the whole Repo facet; it should be one or two rows with Add entry…

**Type:** debt

**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Designed (2026-10-30) — Paul's answer to m-418219cd59de, *Yes, as drawn: two rows, no-team repos too, no selector*; the design is §4.9 *The Sessions page* (*the compact facet*) and §4.5a; build TD-1248.
**Location:** design §4.5 screen 1 (the Repo facet, 4.5-ui.md ~:287), §4.9 *The Sessions page* (*The repo's line*: *the line draws the compact facet in its place*); §4.5a rows *team card: fold*, *team card: Repo facet* and *Repo page, team card: Repo facet* → **Add entry…**; `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/repo_line.html` (includes `repo_facet.html` whole); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`AO.lineFacetShown`, the `.rsum` show/hide); the review shots `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-10-30-td1240-td1240-{1600,400}.png` (source `td1240-src/td1240_shots.py`)

**Why:** Paul's words (2026-10-29): *Right now the collapsed cards on the Sessions page show a large display of the team TDs. Lets instead show only one or two rows of info, including the add entry button.* A folded team card is the header alone (§4.5a *team card: fold*). But once every team card of a repo is folded, §4.9 has the repo's line take over the **whole** Repo facet: two stacked bars for Technical debt by priority and by kind, its legend, the period selector, and the Pull requests blocks. So folding a team, which is the person's way to make it small, puts a large block of the repo's numbers above the folded row. The page reads as unfolded, and a page of folded teams stays long.

**Fix:** design it first, then a build. In §4.9 *The repo's line* and §4.5a, the repo's line under folded team cards (and wherever else the round decides it stands in for the facet) draws a **compact** form of the Repo facet in one or two rows instead of the whole facet. That form holds the repo's open count with its kind or priority figures inline, the open PRs and those waiting on review, the lanes *i*, and **Add entry…** (the same form as the facet's, §4.9 *Add an entry to the ledger*). Its link is *open →* to the Repo page, where the full facet stays. The round settles three things: what the one or two rows hold; whether a repo with no team also takes the compact form; and whether the line keeps the facet's *open · day · week · month* selector. Render the folded and unfolded pages for Paul before the review rounds (cadence §4.8 as layered: agree the shape first). Then file the build as `Part of: TD-1240`.

**Done when:** the design names the repo line's compact form under folded team cards, with **Add entry…** on it, and a build entry that implements it is filed as its part.
