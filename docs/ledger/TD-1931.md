## TD-1931: A team definition pull request is work for the team's lanes as soon as it opens

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 50cfefcc94e24ac29a937172566d110d
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** TD-1153's design (§4.4 *A definition PR is the team's at once*: *Which PRs*, *The order*, *The taker*): a definition PR the home opens on the person's press waits for review and merge under the repo's cadence, so the repo reading makes it a work order of the team's lanes at once, as a sweep PR is.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/reports.py, src/sessionorc/workorders.py, docs/briefs/grinder-ao-*.md; design §4.4 *A definition PR is the team's at once*

**Fix:** 1. `reports.TEAM_HEAD_RE` and `reports.team_of(branch) -> str | None` reading `team/<team>/<YYYYMMDD-HHMM>` whole; the repo reading stamps a PR whose head it reads and whose one changed file is the repo's `.shiftlead.yml` (`reports.pr_files`, sharing the held and filing reads' call and budget) as `team: <team>`.
2. The order: `pr:<n>` at once (no `ORPHAN_AFTER`), priority *High*, `team: <team>`, its Context the PR's URL and *definition of <team> proposed on its page: review and merge it, or say why it cannot merge*; routed as a sweep PR's order (every `free-pick` lane of the teams servicing the repo; a wound-down team started for it per §6 rule 8), in the pick order beside lift and sweep orders; held by a live claim, no order.
3. The taker's step in the grinder briefs (`docs/briefs/grinder-ao-*.md`, a held path: its reader reads it): review at the head with `review-model`, re-running the page's checks at the head and checking the named briefs exist; precheck; merge on the cadence. It never rewrites the definition: one that cannot merge as written, or a `.shiftlead.yml` conflict, gets the review posted, the PR left open and an `ask` through the techlead — *Merge as it is — a session merges it | Close it — a session closes the PR and the page's edit is gone*, `Merge as it is` the default only for a missing brief — and the claim dropped with the ask's id.
4. Tests: `team_of` on good and bad heads (no date, extra segments, a `team/` head changing another file too); the order's fields and lane; a held claim removes it; a PR on such a head changing two files is no definition order and is read as any PR.

**Done when:** A definition PR is in the team's free-pick lanes as `pr:<n>` the tick after it opens, the grinder brief's taker step is written, and the tests above pass.
