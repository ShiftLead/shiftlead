2. The order, in `src/sessionorc/workorders.py`, by `is_definition_order(pr, reads)`, which the reading calls (`pr_orders.definition`, beside `pr_orders.gate`: the tick's repo reading in `agent_tick` builds `definition` as it builds `gate`, keeps it in the `new` it saves and in the equality check before the save, so a definition PR alone is saved and broadcast; in an unserved repo, whose reading keeps only lift and sweep orders, it is no order, a *definition* row whose `whose` reads *the person's*), built on `is_definition_row(pr)`, the head and files test alone, which `workorders.red_prs` and `workorders.red_holders` call to pass the PR over: `pr:<n>` at once (no `ORPHAN_AFTER`), priority *High*, `team: <team>`, its Context the PR's URL and *definition of <team> proposed on its page: review and merge it, or say why it cannot merge*; routed as a sweep PR's order (every `free-pick` lane of the teams servicing the repo; a wound-down team started for it per §6 rule 8), in the pick order beside lift and sweep orders; held by a live claim, no order; never a red order (`red_prs` and `red_holders` pass over every row `is_definition_row` accepts, red or conflicting, whether or not it is an order now, as a gate head is no order of any rule: one order, never two); `teamrun.red_prs` (`src/shiftlead/teamrun.py`, the facet and the Repo page's tags) draws a row the reading stamps `team: <team>` as it draws a gate row, whatever its checks: `word` *definition*, `what` *definition of <team>*, `whose` as a gate row's, *waiting on an answer* while a read of it is open, left out of the red count; and no order while any read of it is open (`read_open`, whatever its `held`, as a filing order's), so a reader's ask under `held:` or the taker's ask to the person holds it until answered.
3. The taker's step in the shipped grinder brief, `src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md`, its pick-order sentence gaining the definition order beside the lift and sweep orders (this PR touches `src/sessionorc/**`, a held path, so its reader reads it): review at the head with `review-model`, re-running the page's checks at the head by `ao org check --root <its worktree at the head>` (parse, `_team`, no name twice, every listed flow followable through `teams.plan`, the named briefs present); precheck; merge on the cadence. It never rewrites the definition: one that cannot merge as written, or a `.shiftlead.yml` conflict, gets the review posted, the PR left open, an `ask` through the techlead carrying `--pr <n>`, and the claim dropped with the ask's id. The answers (§4.4 *The taker*): a missing brief alone — *Merge as it is — a session merges it (default) | Close it — a session closes the PR and the page's edit is gone*; a definition that does not parse, a team `_team` refuses, an unfollowable flow, a red precheck or a conflict — *Close it — a session closes the PR and the page's edit is gone (default) | Fix it yourself — you change the PR's branch, and a session reviews it again*. A taker of a PR whose ask is answered reads the answer from its reads and does it.
4. Tests: `team_of` on good and bad heads (no date, extra segments, a `team/` head changing another file too); the order's fields and lane; a held claim removes it; an open read removes it and its answer brings it back; a red or conflicting definition PR is one order, the definition one, never a red one too; a red or conflicting definition PR with a read open, past `ORPHAN_AFTER`, is no order at all; `teamrun.red_prs` draws a red definition PR as a *definition* row, outside the red count; `pr_orders.definition` lists a definition PR alone, saved when it is the only change, and the Repo page and the facet read it; in an unserved repo it is a *definition* row, the person's, and no order; the rows are hand-made `team/` heads, since TD-1930's RPC comes after; a PR on such a head changing two files is no definition order and is read as any PR.

**Done when:** A definition PR is in the team's free-pick lanes as `pr:<n>` the tick after it opens and out of them while a read of it is open; the grinder brief's taker step, its two answer pairs and its reading of an answer are written; the tests above pass.
