## TD-2205: A built-in seat's keep-on-while-a-session-finishes clauses are not tested

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 11cd3bbec3ed4c05a74c8725a2c506e9

**Why:** Audit of #4611 (TD-2065 slice 12). `agent_tick._builtin_hold` keeps a wound-down team's tech lead (and the prompts seat) on while one of the team's sessions finishes, and its docstring says a seat is counted as no team's finishing session. The predicate's clauses `r.seat is None`, `not r.builtin`, `not r.superseded_by` and `r.unattended` have no test: with `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pdm run pytest -q tests/test_builtin_when_they_run.py tests/test_first_shape_tick.py` (61 passed), deleting `and r.seat is None` and `and not r.builtin` together, or `and not r.superseded_by`, or `r.unattended` alone each still pass. The tests' tech lead record is `closed`, which `DEAD` already excludes, so a *live* built-in seat counted as its own team's finishing session — a lead held on forever by its own record — ships green.

**Fix:** In `tests/test_builtin_when_they_run.py` add a wound-down team a whose tech lead record is `working` and whose only other records are a superseded unattended member, an attended member and a live seat of the team: assert `_builtin_hold` returns the hold. Revert each clause to confirm a failure.

**Done when:** Removing any one of the four clauses fails a test.
