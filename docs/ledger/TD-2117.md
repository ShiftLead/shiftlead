## TD-2117: The Queue's Refresh stays in reach while the page is scrolled down

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 6c29517225fd4567a261b916ac0f465d

**Why:** When the Queue changes, its bar says what is waiting (*1 card moved*) and offers **Refresh**, but the bar sits in the page above the first group. A person scrolled down cannot see that anything changed, or press Refresh, without scrolling back to the top. The Queue deliberately holds still while it is read (design 4.5 screen 12, *The page holds still*), so this bar is the only sign that what is on screen is out of date: out of sight, a person can go on reading and acting on an old picture. The person reported it on 2026-11-23 with a screenshot of the bar. A card's page draws the same bar in the same way.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css (.qbar), src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Queue's and card page's bars), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue.html, src/shiftlead/ui/templates/card_page.html, design 4.5 screen 12 The page holds still, design 4.5a Queue page: the bar, Refresh

**Fix:** Keep the bar in reach while something waits. The person left the shape to this entry's call: the bar itself floats, and no new control is added to the top menu, since the bar already holds the words and the button, and a second copy would be one more place to keep in step.

- While the bar has something to refresh, `.qbar` is `position: sticky` just under the top bar, with a solid background and a thin edge so cards do not read through it. Its `top` is `var(--barh)`, and 0 on a phone while the top bar has slid away (`.topbar.away`), where `--barh` keeps the bar's height. Its `z-index` sits below the top bar's 30 and above the cards and overlay composer (5 and below), so a card's fixed menu (40) and the toasts (50) stay above it.
- While the page is up to date, or not live, the bar stays in the page's flow and does not follow the scroll, so an idle page keeps its whole height for reading. Sticky positioning keeps the bar's place in the flow, so nothing under it moves when it starts or stops following.
- The same holds on a card's page, which shares the bar (`queue.html`, `card_page.html`, `.qbar` in `app.css`, the two bar renderers in `app.js`).
- **Change what counts as in view.** Refresh keeps the first card at least partly in view in place, and today that is measured from the top of the viewport, so a card under the top bar counts. While the bar floats, both pages' measurements start at the floating bar's lower edge, so a card hidden behind it does not count.
- **Change the jump to a card.** `scroll-padding-top` covers only the top bar today. While the bar floats it grows by the bar's height, also while `.topbar.away`, so `/queue#<id>` lands below the bar and not behind it.

Change design 4.5 screen 12 *The page holds still* and the 4.5a rows for the Queue's and the card page's bar in the same PR. Those texts say the bar is *never held*, meaning it never holds still for a reader; keep that meaning and say separately that it floats. The change is a page's, so its builder verifies it on a scratch home with screenshots, wide and at phone width, scrolled down with a change waiting (design 4.9b *A UI change is verified by its builder*).

**Done when:** On the Queue and on a card's page, scrolled down with a change waiting, the bar's words and **Refresh** are on screen under the top bar at wide and phone widths, and pressing Refresh keeps the first visible card in place. With nothing waiting the bar scrolls away with the page. No card's menu, toast or dialog is drawn under the bar, and a jump to a card lands below it.
