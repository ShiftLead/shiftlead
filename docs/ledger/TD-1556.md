**Why:** One thing the person had to do showed on the Queue as two Decide cards, and answering it cleared only one (Paul, 2026-11-07: *if its not asking me to do something, why is it listed as a decide item?*). On TD-1529, designer-ao-1 sent a steer (`m-040a11963aa5`); Paul answered it; the designer then reported its outcome as `blocked` (*the review rounds wait on your shape answer (ask m-998693d16e2a)*) and opened that shape question as a separate `ask`. Design §4.10 *Outcomes* puts a `blocked` outcome in *Needs you*, counted, and `inbox.py` draws it as a Decide beside the ask it names, so the card carried an orange *Decide 1* for a question already answered, offering only Reply or Dismiss; it stayed after Paul answered the ask it was waiting on, since only a Reply or Dismiss on the card itself clears it.

Two halves. **The sender's:** §4.10 lists a new ask on the thread (`--thread <ask id>`) as the follow-up when more direction is needed, and the grinder and hunter briefs say *more direction on the same question* is not an outcome but a new ask on its thread (`ao msg person … --kind ask --thread <id>`, so the person sees their own answer above it); the designer used `--outcome blocked` plus a fresh ask instead, and nothing stopped it. **The card's:** a `blocked` outcome is not a decision, yet it reads as one, and when what blocks it is another item already in front of the person it counts the same need twice and never clears with it.

**Design PR:** #3504

**Fix:** Design first (§4.10 *Outcomes*, §4.5a's Inbox/Queue rows), then build:
- **Refuse or steer the misuse at the send.** `ao msg person --outcome blocked --for <id>` whose sender holds an open `ask` or `steer` to the person (or whose line names one) answers with what to do instead: ask on the question's thread with `--thread <id>`. Decide in the design whether it refuses or warns, and how a blocked outcome that genuinely waits on something else the person must do (not a question of its own) is still sent.
- **Say it in the briefs that teach outcomes:** `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md`, `grinder.md`, `hunter.md`, `manager.md` and `src/shiftlead/skill.md` each carry the outcome paragraph; `grinder.md` and `hunter.md` already say a follow-up question goes on the thread, `designer.md` only as a clause and `manager.md` and `skill.md` not at all: add the sentence there, and to all five that it is never `blocked` plus a new ask.
- **Draw a blocked outcome as what it is.** Its chip and heading read *Blocked* (or the design's word), not *Decide* or *Do* (`ui/queue.py`'s `want_of` gives *decide* to any `ask` or `steer`, which a blocked outcome rides on, and *do* to anything else); a `blocked` outcome whose line names an item still open in front of the person is drawn under that item, uncounted, and leaves when that item is answered or closed; one that names nothing stays counted as today, with Reply and Dismiss.
- **The Queue's count** (`ui/inbox.py`'s `needs`, and the Queue card's *Decide n*) counts one need once.

**Done when:** A sender that needs more direction after an answer is told at the send to ask on the thread, and the briefs that teach outcomes say so; a `blocked` outcome on the Queue reads as blocked rather than Decide, and one waiting on another item already in front of the person is drawn under it, uncounted, and clears when that item is answered — read on the live Queue with a TD-1529-shaped pair (a steer answered, a blocked outcome naming an open ask).
