**Fix:** The lead candidate is the tick's retention sweep (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, the attachments loop after `if live_ids is None`). A sweep whose snapshot predates the test's `agent.sessions[s.id] = s` can therefore remove the new empty folder between those two calls. This test, unlike the retention test in the same file, does not park the fixture's ticks.

Confirm the cause by forcing a sweep between the mkdir and the open. Then park the ticks in this test, as the retention test does. Also judge whether the same window is open in production for a session created just after a sweep's snapshot. If it is, make `_start_part` retry its mkdir once on `FileNotFoundError`, or make the sweep skip a folder holding a `.part` or one created within the sweep.
