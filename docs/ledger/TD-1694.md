## TD-1694: A blocked outcome that names the sender's own open question is refused, and the person's Reply on a blocked outcome is recorded

**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open

**Part of:** TD-1556
**Location:** design §4.10 *Outcomes*; src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py (`_msg`: the outcome path, the person's reply), src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py, src/shiftlead/briefs/{designer,grinder,hunter,manager}.md, src/shiftlead/skill.md, tests

**Why:** TD-1556's sender's half. On TD-1529 a designer reported `--outcome blocked` naming its own fresh `ask` (m-998693d16e2a) instead of asking on the answered steer's thread, and the person saw one need as two Decide cards. Design §4.10 *Outcomes* now refuses that at the send and warns when the caller holds an open question the outcome does not name. It also records the person's Reply on a `blocked` outcome. *Reply answers it and the row leaves* was written and never built: nothing stores the reply, so the row stays.

**Fix:** Design §4.10 *Outcomes* holds the rules, in the paragraphs *A `blocked` outcome is never a question's stand-in* and *`blocked` is not a dead end*. Build them:
- **The refusal.** In `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`'s `_msg`, once `state` is `blocked` and `_owing_question` has found the entry (a question, or a handed entry from its first branch), read the `m-[0-9a-f]{12}` ids in the whole `text` as sent, before `_clean` caps it. For each id, take the person-inbox copies and the caller's own copies of it. Where the id is a question of the caller's, meaning its `from_` is the caller or a record on the caller's `superseded_by` chain (as `rpc_msg_withdraw` reads the sender), and its thread (`root`) holds an open `ask` or `steer` in `self.person_inbox` (a routed or moved copy among them), refuse with §4.10's words, naming that open copy's id. Where that open copy's `root` is the outcome's own entry's `root` (a seat's `--thread` question about a handed entry), use §4.10's second words instead (*wait for its answer*). Nothing is written on a refusal.
- **The warning.** Where the text names none of them but the caller (on the same chain) holds an open `ask` or `steer` in the person inbox, the outcome lands. The reply's `warnings` gains one line naming each such open id, with the first words.
- **The reply recorded.** Where the person's `reply` lands on the thread of an entry whose outcome is `blocked` (the Inbox's **Reply** on the row, `reply_to` that entry's id), `_mark` adds `replied: {at, by}` to that outcome on every copy, `by` being the reply's id. `MailEntry.outcome` (`src/sessionorc/models.py`) is a plain dict, so the field round-trips as it is. `src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py`, which raises a `blocked:<id>` row for every `blocked` outcome (a question's and a handed entry's), skips one carrying `replied`.
- **The briefs and the skill.** `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md`, `grinder.md`, `hunter.md`, `manager.md` and `src/shiftlead/skill.md` each carry the outcome rule. Say in each that more direction after an answer is a new `ask` on the question's thread (`ao msg person --kind ask --thread <id> …`), never `--outcome blocked` beside a new ask, and that `blocked` is for work that waits on something else only the person can move. grinder.md and hunter.md already say the first half, and skill.md says `--thread <its id>` asks again: add the second half to each. designer.md, whose outcome line says neither half, and manager.md gain both. `manager.md` is what a manager reads about its members, so it states the rule a member follows.
- **Tests,** beside the outcome tests (`grep -ln "asked_again" tests/*.py`):
  - refused when the text names the caller's own open ask, its own open steer, or a routed copy's original id;
  - refused when it names a question sent by a record the caller superseded;
  - the second words for a handed entry's own-thread question;
  - lands with the warning when the caller holds an open question it does not name;
  - lands with no warning when it names another sender's open ask;
  - a person's reply on a blocked outcome writes `replied`;
  - a replied blocked outcome raises no `blocked:` notify row.

**Done when:** Each of these holds, and the tests above pass:
- `ao msg person --outcome blocked "…m-X…" --for <id>` is refused with the thread cure when m-X's thread holds the caller's own open question to the person.
- It is refused with *wait for its answer* when that question is already on the outcome's own thread.
- It lands with a `warnings` line when the caller holds an open question the text does not name.
- The person's Reply on a blocked outcome writes `outcome.replied`, and that outcome is no longer told.
- The four briefs and the skill say a follow-up is an ask on the thread, never `blocked` plus a new ask.
