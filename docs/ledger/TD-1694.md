**Why:** TD-1556's sender's half. On TD-1529 a designer reported `--outcome blocked` naming its own fresh `ask` (m-998693d16e2a) instead of asking on the answered steer's thread, and the person saw one need as two Decide cards. Design §4.10 *Outcomes* now refuses that at the send and warns when the caller holds an open question the outcome does not name. It also records the person's Reply on a `blocked` outcome. *Reply answers it and the row leaves* was written and never built: nothing stores the reply, so the row stays.

**Ask:** Held with its design TD-1556 until Paul answers its end-of-rounds item (Go, One more round or Hold) on docs/user_attention.md.

**Fix:** Design §4.10 *Outcomes* holds the rules, in the paragraphs *A `blocked` outcome is never a question's stand-in* and *`blocked` is not a dead end*, with the words to use. Build them, all in `src/sessionorc/` unless named:
- **The refusal** (`agent_mail.py` `_msg`, once `state` is `blocked` and `_owing_question` has found the entry: a question, or a handed entry from its first branch).
  - The caller's open questions are the open `ask`s and `steer`s of `self.person_inbox` whose `from_` is the caller or a record on the caller's `superseded_by` chain, read as `rpc_msg_withdraw` reads the sender.
  - Read the `m-[0-9a-f]{12}` ids in the whole `text` as sent, before `_clean` caps it, in text order.
  - First, any of those open questions whose `root` is the settled entry's `root` refuses with the own-thread words below, named or not. Then the first id that is one of those questions refuses with §4.10's first words, naming it and the `--for` id as the `--thread` target. So does an id of an entry of the caller's outbox (on the same chain) closed `rerouted` whose `root` holds one of them in the person inbox. `_reroute_waiting` closes the original in the asker's outbox and appends the moved copy to `person_inbox` only when the target is the person; a copy moved to a lead refuses nothing.
  - The own-thread words (*wait for its answer*) are for a seat's `--thread` question about a handed entry.
  - An answered or closed id refuses nothing. Nothing is written on a refusal.
- **The warnings.** Where none is named and none is on the own thread, but the caller holds such an open question, the outcome lands. The reply's `warnings` gains §4.10's warning line for each. The other order is warned too: an `ask` or `steer` to the person without `--thread`, from a caller (on the same chain) that holds a question or handed entry whose outcome is `blocked` and unreplied, lands with §4.10's *your blocked outcome on m-… is open* line.
- **A `blocked` outcome is not the last word.** `_owing_question`'s *is settled* refusal, in both of its branches (the handed one and the person-inbox one), stays for every recorded outcome except `blocked`, and for a second `--outcome blocked`; `_owing_question(sender, mid)` takes no state today, so pass it the caller's intent (the outcome's state, or `thread`). On a question:
  - a newer `--outcome done|dropped` writes that outcome with `was: <the blocked outcome as it was>`;
  - a `--thread` follow-up lands as any does, and the `settle` mark writes `outcome: {state: "asked_again", text, at, by, was: …}`.
  On a handed entry:
  - a newer `done` or `dropped` outcome replaces it the same way, with `was`;
  - a `--thread` question (the `onto` path) clears `outcome`, so the entry `owes` again, and keeps the blocked one as the entry's own `was` field, which the entry's next outcome takes as its `was` (the field then cleared), and which `_move_handed`'s copy carries (`MailEntry` gains `was: dict | None = None`; `to_dict` is `asdict` and `from_dict` keeps the dataclass's fields, so nothing else changes; `_mark` with `outcome=None` skips the look-back).
  An outcome that is no longer `blocked` leaves *Needs you* with no `ui` change, since `inbox_sections` reads only `blocked`. TD-1695 draws `was`.
- **The reply recorded.** A person's `reply` whose `reply_to` is the id of a person-inbox question carrying a `blocked` outcome, or that outcome's reporting note (`outcome.by`), makes `_mark` write the question's outcome merged with `replied: {at, by}` on every copy (`_mark` sets whole fields), `by` being the reply's id. The reply may come from the row (`reply_to` the question's id, `reply_btn`), from the message page or from `ao msg --reply-to`; the note may be pruned before the question, and a reply to it then is refused as today. A second reply leaves the first standing. A reply to another entry on the thread, or to a handed entry, stamps nothing. A reply to a closed question is accepted today (`closes` is False, and it goes to `from_`), a closed asker's lands in its mailbox and is stamped; a forgotten one's is refused (*no session*), as today, and Dismiss is its way out. `MailEntry.outcome` (`models.py`) is a plain dict, so the field round-trips as it is. `agent_notify.py`, which raises a `blocked:<id>` row for every `blocked` outcome, skips one carrying `replied`.
- **The thread's readers.** `ao msg --thread`'s view (`src/shiftlead/cli.py`, which prints `outcome: <state>`) adds *replied* and *was blocked* where the outcome carries them; `agent_common.py`'s `_oldest_first` orders a replaced note by `was.by` as it orders the current one by `outcome.by`.
- **Dismiss.** `agent_inbox.py`'s `rpc_inbox_dismiss` writes the `dismissed` outcome and sends the asker its `system` note only for an entry that `owes`, plus the handed branch. A question whose outcome is `blocked` and unreplied gets §4.10's note (*the person dismissed m-…: they want nothing more on it*), and `dismissed` (with `was`) is written on every copy, as the handed branch writes it, so the outbox copy is no longer kept as `blocked`; a replied one is dismissed silently. The handed branch keeps its own words and tests, and writes `was` (the `blocked` one) beside `dismissed` too. Today such a question owes nothing (`MailEntry.owes`), so its Dismiss tells nobody, though `inbox_row.html` says *is told*.
- **Kept until it is answered.** `agent_inbox.py`'s `_keep` keeps an open or owing entry only, and a `blocked` question owes nothing, so it is pruned 12 h after its answer. Keep a `blocked` outcome without `replied` on every copy, as an owing question is kept (`MailEntry.owes_for`'s copies: the person inbox and the asker's outbox; a handed entry in its holder's inbox is open and already kept), with no age, since only the person's Reply or Dismiss or the asker's newer send ends it. A replied one runs FYI's window from `replied.at`.
- **The briefs and the skill.** Each of these carries the outcome rule: `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md`, `grinder.md`, `hunter.md`, `manager.md`, `techlead.md` (the handed-entry rule, *`blocked` with what stopped you*) and `src/shiftlead/skill.md`. Say in each that more direction after an answer is a new `ask` on the question's thread (`ao msg person --kind ask --thread <id> …`, which takes a `blocked` outcome's place too), never `--outcome blocked` beside a new ask. Also say that `blocked` is for work that waits on something else only the person can move. Per brief:
  - grinder.md and hunter.md already say both halves: add only that it is never `blocked` beside a new ask.
  - skill.md says `--thread <its id>` asks again: add the rest.
  - designer.md, whose outcome line says neither half, and manager.md (the rule a member follows, as a manager reads its members) gain both.
  - techlead.md says that a question about a handed entry goes on its thread and that the entry's outcome waits for its answer.
- **Tests,** beside the outcome tests (`grep -ln "asked_again" tests/*.py`):
  - refused when the text names the caller's own open ask, its own open steer, a rerouted original's id, or an open question sent by a superseded record;
  - the own-thread words for a handed entry's open `--thread` question, named and unnamed;
  - not refused when it names the caller's answered question, or another sender's open ask;
  - lands with the warning when the caller holds an open question it does not name;
  - an `ask` to the person without `--thread` while the caller holds an unreplied `blocked` outcome lands with the other-order warning;
  - `--thread` on a `blocked` question is accepted and writes `asked_again` with `was`, while it is still refused on a `done` one;
  - `--outcome done` after `blocked` is accepted with `was`, while a second `blocked` is refused;
  - on a handed entry, `--thread` after `blocked` leaves it owing with `was`;
  - a person's reply with `reply_to` the blocked question, or its outcome note, writes `replied`, while one to another entry on the thread does not;
  - a replied blocked outcome raises no `blocked:` notify row;
  - Dismiss sends the note for an unreplied blocked question, and stays silent for a replied one;
  - an unreplied blocked question past 12 h is kept.

**Done when:** Each of these holds, and the tests above pass:
- `ao msg person --outcome blocked "…m-X…" --for <id>` is refused with the thread cure when m-X is the caller's own open question to the person, or the rerouted original of one.
- It is refused with *wait for its answer* when the caller holds an open question on the outcome's own thread, named or not.
- It lands with a `warnings` line when the caller holds another open question the text does not name.
- `--thread <id>` and a newer `done` or `dropped` on a blocked question or handed entry are accepted, and take the outcome's place.
- The person's Reply on a blocked question writes `outcome.replied`, and that outcome is no longer told.
- Dismiss of an unreplied one tells the asker.
- An unreplied blocked outcome is never pruned by age.
- The five briefs and the skill say a follow-up is an ask on the thread, never `blocked` plus a new ask.
