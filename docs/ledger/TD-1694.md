## TD-1694: A blocked outcome that names the sender's own open question is refused, and the person's Reply on a blocked outcome is recorded

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** b9805dbd226d44c0820a06cff27c94f3
**Part of:** TD-1556
**Location:** design §4.10 *Outcomes*; src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py (`_msg`, `_owing_question`, the person's reply), src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py, src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py (`rpc_inbox_dismiss`, `_keep`), src/shiftlead/briefs/{designer,grinder,hunter,manager,techlead}.md, src/shiftlead/skill.md, tests

**Why:** TD-1556's sender's half. On TD-1529 a designer reported `--outcome blocked` naming its own fresh `ask` (m-998693d16e2a) instead of asking on the answered steer's thread, and the person saw one need as two Decide cards. Design §4.10 *Outcomes* now refuses that at the send and warns when the caller holds an open question the outcome does not name. It also records the person's Reply on a `blocked` outcome. *Reply answers it and the row leaves* was written and never built: nothing stores the reply, so the row stays.

**Fix:** Design §4.10 *Outcomes* holds the rules, in the paragraphs *A `blocked` outcome is never a question's stand-in* and *`blocked` is not a dead end*, with the words to use. Build them, all in `src/sessionorc/` unless named:
- **The refusal** (`agent_mail.py` `_msg`, once `state` is `blocked` and `_owing_question` has found the entry: a question, or a handed entry from its first branch).
  - The caller's open questions are the open `ask`s and `steer`s of `self.person_inbox` whose `from_` is the caller or a record on the caller's `superseded_by` chain, read as `rpc_msg_withdraw` reads the sender.
  - Read the `m-[0-9a-f]{12}` ids in the whole `text` as sent, before `_clean` caps it, in text order.
  - The first id that is one of those questions refuses with §4.10's first words, naming it and the `--for` id as the `--thread` target. So does an id of an entry of the caller's outbox (on the same chain) closed `rerouted` whose `root` holds one of them in the person inbox. `_reroute_waiting` closes the original in the asker's outbox and appends the moved copy to `person_inbox` only when the target is the person; a copy moved to a lead refuses nothing.
  - Any of those open questions whose `root` is the settled entry's `root` (a seat's `--thread` question about a handed entry) refuses with §4.10's own-thread words, named or not.
  - An answered or closed id refuses nothing. Nothing is written on a refusal.
- **The warning.** Where none is named and none is on the own thread, but the caller holds such an open question, the outcome lands. The reply's `warnings` gains §4.10's warning line for each.
- **`--thread` takes a `blocked` outcome's place.** `_owing_question`'s *is settled* refusal stays for every recorded outcome except `blocked`, when called for `--thread` (not for a second `--outcome`). The follow-up lands as any `--thread` question does, and the `settle` mark writes `outcome: {state: "asked_again", text, at, by, was: <the blocked outcome as it was>}`. The `asked_again` outcome needs no `ui` change to leave *Needs you*: `inbox_sections` reads only `blocked`. The `fyi` macro draws `outcome_line`; TD-1695 draws `was` under it.
- **The reply recorded.** A person's `reply` whose `reply_to` is the id of a person-inbox question carrying a `blocked` outcome makes `_mark` add `replied: {at, by}` to that outcome on every copy, `by` being the reply's id. A second reply leaves the first standing. A reply to another entry on the thread, or to a handed entry, stamps nothing. A reply to a closed question is accepted today (`closes` is False, and it goes to `from_`), except for an orphaned one, which stays refused, so its way out is Dismiss. `MailEntry.outcome` (`models.py`) is a plain dict, so the field round-trips as it is. `agent_notify.py`, which raises a `blocked:<id>` row for every `blocked` outcome, skips one carrying `replied`.
- **Dismiss.** `agent_inbox.py`'s `rpc_inbox_dismiss` writes the `dismissed` outcome and sends the asker its `system` note only for an entry that `owes`, plus the handed branch. A question whose outcome is `blocked` and unreplied gets §4.10's note (*the person dismissed m-…: they want nothing more on it*); a replied one is dismissed silently. Today such a question owes nothing (`MailEntry.owes`), so its Dismiss tells nobody, though `inbox_row.html` says *is told*.
- **Kept until it is answered.** `agent_inbox.py`'s `_keep` keeps an open or owing entry only, and a `blocked` question owes nothing, so it is pruned 12 h after its answer. Keep a `blocked` outcome without `replied` as an owing question is kept: the person-inbox copy, and a handed entry in its holder's inbox. A replied one runs FYI's window from `replied.at`.
- **The briefs and the skill.** Each of these carries the outcome rule: `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md`, `grinder.md`, `hunter.md`, `manager.md`, `techlead.md` (the handed-entry rule, *`blocked` with what stopped you*) and `src/shiftlead/skill.md`. Say in each that more direction after an answer is a new `ask` on the question's thread (`ao msg person --kind ask --thread <id> …`, which takes a `blocked` outcome's place too), never `--outcome blocked` beside a new ask. Also say that `blocked` is for work that waits on something else only the person can move. Per brief:
  - grinder.md and hunter.md already say both halves: add only that it is never `blocked` beside a new ask.
  - skill.md says `--thread <its id>` asks again: add the rest.
  - designer.md, whose outcome line says neither half, and manager.md (the rule a member follows, as a manager reads its members) gain both.
  - techlead.md says that a question about a handed entry goes on its thread and that the entry's outcome waits for its answer.
- **Tests,** beside the outcome tests (`grep -ln "asked_again" tests/*.py`):
  - refused when the text names the caller's own open ask, its own open steer, a rerouted original's id, or an open question sent by a superseded record;
  - the own-thread words for a handed entry's open `--thread` question, named and unnamed;
  - not refused when it names the caller's answered question, or another sender's open ask;
  - lands with the warning when the caller holds an open question it does not name;
  - `--thread` on a `blocked` question is accepted and writes `asked_again` with `was`, while it is still refused on a `done` one;
  - a person's reply with `reply_to` the blocked question writes `replied`, while one to the outcome note does not;
  - a replied blocked outcome raises no `blocked:` notify row;
  - Dismiss sends the note for an unreplied blocked question, and stays silent for a replied one;
  - an unreplied blocked question past 12 h is kept.

**Done when:** Each of these holds, and the tests above pass:
- `ao msg person --outcome blocked "…m-X…" --for <id>` is refused with the thread cure when m-X is the caller's own open question to the person, or the rerouted original of one.
- It is refused with *wait for its answer* when the caller holds an open question on the outcome's own thread, named or not.
- It lands with a `warnings` line when the caller holds another open question the text does not name.
- `--thread <id>` on a blocked question is accepted and takes the outcome's place.
- The person's Reply on a blocked question writes `outcome.replied`, and that outcome is no longer told.
- Dismiss of an unreplied one tells the asker.
- An unreplied blocked outcome is never pruned by age.
- The five briefs and the skill say a follow-up is an ask on the thread, never `blocked` plus a new ask.
