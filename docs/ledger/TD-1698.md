**Why:** TD-1557's design (§4.4 *Decide*, *A Reply that fills a form decides*; §4.10 *A form is filled, never pressed*). On 2026-11-07 Paul typed his `Hold: <note>` into **Answer…** on a board end-of-rounds item. It took the Reply road and left the line due, because `_board_answer` fills only ledgerboard's `Not right:` form. Today a `Hold: <note>` button pressed as written would write the placeholder as `Decided:`. This build is the agent's half: forms in general, read through ledgerboard v2.7's `is_form` and `fill_form`.

**Event (ledgerboard-v27):** ledgerboard v2.7 (the slot form generalised: `is_form`, `fill_form`, and `decide_lift` reading a filled form as its form) is vendored into this repo's wheel by the agent-cadence bump that brings it.

**Fix:** Design §4.4 *Decide* and §4.10 *Suggested answers* (TD-1557). Every reading goes through ledgerboard. It needs ledgerboard v2.7 vendored here (`is_form`, `fill_form`, and `decide_lift` reading a filled form as its form for `Lifts:`), which this entry's event gate waits on. In v2.7, `Not right:`'s head is matched in any case and its refusal reads *Not right needs its words*. Update the tests here that bind the old *say what is off*.
- **`_board_answer`** (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`): an offered answer word for word first. Then any form among the item's answers, filled through `ledgerboard.fill_form`: the head is compared in any case and written as the form spells it. A form as written, its head alone, or its head with a placeholder is refused with ledgerboard's *<head> needs its words*. `Not right:` becomes this rule's look case, with nothing special left for it here. Correct the docstrings that say only `Not right:` fills.
- **`rpc_board_reply`**: it takes no `answers` from the page. It finds the line as it does today and reads that line's own kind and `Answers:` through ledgerboard (`parse_board` on the found line). On an open, undecided `decide` or `look` line, words that fill one of the line's forms are written by the decide's road: `board_mod.write_back(..., "decide", answer=<filled>)`, the decide lift on a gated item, one commit, with no lease holder mailed. It answers `{decided: <answer>, ...}` beside what the decide answers. Words that fill no form are the Reply as today, and so are any words on a decided line or on a line of another kind. A refused fill (a form left empty) is refused, never written as a reply.
- **The reader hands `forms`**: beside each board item's and each mail entry's `answers`, which stay a list of strings, a parallel list of booleans says which answers are forms (`ledgerboard.is_form`). The page reads that flag and never matches the shape itself (`ui/inbox.py`'s `live_look` keeps its own pair check).
- **The person's mail reply** (`src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`, the reply road that checks `answer` against `answers`): a reply to an `ask` or a `steer` whose text is one of its offered forms unfilled is refused with *<head> needs its words*. The check comes first, in one helper that both the orphan road (`_answer_orphan`) and the `answer` index check reach. `ao msg --kind steer --default <a form>` is refused at the send. A typed reply that fills the form carries no `answer` index.
- `ao board edit decide` (the person's CLI) takes the same `_board_answer`; nothing more is needed there.
Tests: a `Hold: <note>` item decided by `Hold: drop Dismiss` through `board_edit` and through `board_reply`. `hold: x` is written `Hold: x`. `Hold: <note>`, `Hold:` and `Hold: <b>` are refused by both roads. A Reply that fills no form stays a reply, and the line stays due. On a gated item, a filled `Hold:` lifts where `Hold: <note>` is in `Lifts:`, and otherwise writes what any other answer to a decision writes. It is never a waiting-only answer. A look's `Not right: x` typed as a Reply decides it. A form-filling Reply on a decided line stays a reply. A mail reply of `Hold: <note>` is refused, and `Hold: x` is accepted with no index. A steer with a form `--default` is refused.

**Done when:** On a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`), a board `decide` line offering `Go | Hold: <note>` is decided by a Reply `Hold: drop Dismiss`: the line reads `Decided: Hold: drop Dismiss` in one commit, and the RPC answers `decided`. `Hold: <note>` sent as written, by either road, is refused with *Hold needs its words*. A mail ask with a `Hold: <note>` answer refuses that reply as written. The tests above pass.
