- **`_board_answer`** (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`): it takes an offered answer word for word first. Then it tries any form among the item's answers, filled through `ledgerboard.fill_form`, written `<head>: <words>`. A form as written, its head alone, or its head with a placeholder is refused with ledgerboard's *<head> needs its words*. `Not right:` becomes this rule's look case. Correct the docstrings that say only `Not right:` fills.
- **The Reply that fills a form**: `rpc_board_reply` takes no `answers` from the page. The reading happens inside the one locked write-back (`board._write_back`, under `_EDIT`, after its fetch of origin's head). There, the `reply` action finds the line and reads the line's own kind and `Answers:` through ledgerboard (`parse_board` on the found line). On an open, undecided `decide` or `look` line, or a `watch` carrying the look's pair, words that fill one of its forms turn into the decide in that same write. The result is `Decided: <filled>`, written by the decide lift on a gated item, in one commit. No lease holder is mailed and no reply trail line is written. The call answers `{decided: <answer>, ...}`. These stay the Reply they were: words that fill no form, and any words on a decided line or a line of another kind. Every refusal of the decide refuses the press with the decide's own words, and nothing is written: an empty fill, one ending `(by …)`, one holding a field's name, a read-back failure, or the decide lift's `People:` or answerer refusal. The applying mark (TD-1508) reads `reply` until the line is read, then `decide` with the filled answer.
- **`rpc_ledger_decide`**: an `answer` index, or `go`, that names a form is refused with *<head> needs its words*. The Reply's words are written as today.
- **The readers hand `forms`**: beside the `answers` of each board item, each ledger entry for the person and each mail entry, which stay lists of strings, a parallel list of booleans says which answers are forms (`ledgerboard.is_form`). The page reads it and never matches the shape itself. `ui/inbox.py`'s `live_look` keeps its own pair check.
- **Every reply to a question** (`src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`): an `answer` index that names a form is refused, whatever words follow it. A reply whose first paragraph is an offered form unfilled is refused with *<head> needs its words*: the form as written, `<head>:` alone, or the head and a placeholder, with the head compared in any case. This check comes first, in one helper that both the orphan road (`_answer_orphan`) and the `answer` index check reach, for a session's reply as much as the person's. `ao msg --kind steer --default <a form>` is refused at the send. `ao inbox` prints a form answer as *<head>…: fill it, do not pick it*.
- **Handed upstream with the format**: agent-cadence's `board_edit.py decide` takes the same `fill_form` there. That is the ledgerboard part's, not this repo's.
Tests: a `Hold: <note>` item decided by `Hold: drop Dismiss` through `board_edit` and through `board_reply`. `hold : x` is written `Hold: x`. `Hold: <note>`, `Hold:` and `Hold: <b>` are refused by both roads. A Reply that fills no form stays a reply, and the line stays due. A line decided between the page's read and the lock takes the Reply. A fill ending `(by x)` is refused, never written as a reply. On a gated item, a filled `Hold:` lifts where `Hold: <note>` is in `Lifts:` and closes the item as a lift does; otherwise it is any other answer. A look's `Not right: x` typed as a Reply decides it. A ledger entry's index naming a form is refused. A mail reply of `Hold: <note>` is refused, and so is `--pick <form> "words"`. `Hold: x` is accepted with no index. A steer with a form `--default` is refused.
