- **`_board_answer`** (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`): it takes an offered answer word for word first. Then it tries any form among the item's answers, filled through `ledgerboard.fill_form`. The fill is written `<head>: <words>`, with the words collapsed to one line. `rpc_board_edit` takes `form`, the index of the form pressed, and hands it to `_board_answer`. `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`'s `/api/person/board` `decide` branch passes it through. **With `form` the order changes:** first that form's head is put back where the words do not begin with it and its colon (any case), so `Holding it` becomes `Hold: Holding it` and `Go` becomes `Hold: Go`. Then that form is filled, and no other form. It never falls through to a plain answer: `fill_form(..., form=)` does this. The call returns the filled answer. A form as written, its head alone, or its head with a placeholder is refused with ledgerboard's *<head> needs its words*. `Not right:` becomes this rule's look case. Correct the docstrings that say only `Not right:` fills.
- **The Reply that fills a form**: `rpc_board_reply` takes no `answers` from the page. The reading happens inside the one locked write-back (`board._write_back`, under `_EDIT`, after its fetch of origin's head). There, the `reply` action finds the line and reads the line's own kind and `Answers:` through ledgerboard (`parse_board` on the found line). On an open, undecided `decide` or `look` line, or a `watch` carrying the look's pair (a new `board.live_look_pair(kind, answers)` in sessionorc: a kind in `board.LOOK_KINDS`, moved there from `ui/inbox.py`, which imports it, and exactly *Works* and the look's own form, `is_not_right_form`. `ui/inbox.py`'s `live_look` calls it. Any other form on a `look` or `watch` is an ordinary form), words that begin with one of its forms' heads and a colon (any case) are that form's press, in that same write. Filled, they turn into the decide. An empty fill (the head alone, or the head with a placeholder) is refused with *<head> needs its words*, and nothing is written. Only words that begin with no form's head are a Reply. The result is `Decided: <filled>`, written by the decide lift on a gated item, in one commit. No lease holder is mailed and no reply trail line is written. The call answers `{decided: <answer>, ...}`. When the reading decided, `rpc_board_reply` returns before `_board_reply_mail`. The decide's author is the person as `answerer`, as a decide's is, not the reply's `author`. These stay the Reply they were: words that begin with no form's head, and any words on a decided line or a line of another kind. Every refusal of the decide refuses the press with the decide's own words, and nothing is written: an empty fill, one ending `(by …)`, one holding a field's name, a read-back failure, or the decide lift's `People:` or answerer refusal. The applying mark (TD-1508) reads `reply` until the line is read, then `decide` with the filled answer. `board.write_back` takes a callback, called with the filled answer when the reading turns the reply into a decide. It is idempotent, since `_twice` may run the reading again. From the worker thread it hops to the agent's loop (`call_soon_threadsafe`) and swaps the mark's act through a new `_remark(root, key, act, words)`, which replaces the act and words of `_applying_mark`'s entry for that key.
- **`rpc_ledger_decide`**: an `answer` index, or `go`, that names a form is refused with *<head> needs its words*. It takes `form` and fills it through `fill_form` as `board_edit` does. The Reply's words are written as today, except where they are an offered form unfilled (its head in any case, alone or with a placeholder), which is refused the same way.
- **The readers hand `heads` and `reply_decides`**: board items in `ui/inbox.py`'s board item reading, mail in `mail.read_answers`, and ledger entries in `ledger.answers_of`, whose return tuple gains `heads` as a fourth member. They are carried through `ui/common.py`'s `suggested_answers`; `app.py`'s `answer` press reads the offered answers too. beside the `answers` of each board item, each ledger entry for the person and each mail entry, which stay lists of strings, a parallel list `heads` gives each answer's form head (`ledgerboard.form_head`), empty for a plain answer. It is filtered in step wherever the answers are clipped or blanks are dropped (`suggested_answers`). Each board item also gets `reply_decides`: open, undecided, a `decide` or `look` line or a `watch` with the pair, and carrying a form. Every reader hands no default where the marked answer is a form, on boards, in mail and on ledger entries alike. The page reads it and never matches the shape itself.
