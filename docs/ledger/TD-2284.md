Each change below, made alone at d70555bf3, left tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py, test_director_on_login.py and test_launch_on_login.py green (90 passed). Each probe (a temporary test on a held seat's record moved to a node, removed after) answered as said.

Probes, a launch record naming a login beside its list: unchanged, sent under a mark and its refusal counted; changed, held, or counted nowhere. I know of no path that writes such a record for a node's seat today.

A break would let any other restart of a node's seat fail without counting toward its ceiling, hold a restart or a fill the design says is sent, ask a node again on every tick, or lose the count of a restart whose launch record cannot be read.
