**Why:** A decided board line is a work order in every `free-pick` lane, and a line has no owner (design §6 rule 6, §4.9a *What a session does with a decided line*). So when the person answers a design's end-of-rounds item with *One more round*, the answer goes to the grinders, and the designer who owns the round is never told. Seen in ledgerboard on 2026-11-10 (reported by `ao-ledgerboard-lb-grinder` through `ao td add --repo shiftlead`). Board line `a86efd98` read *Decided: One more round* on TD-058, a design-first entry held by `lb-designer`. The grinder claimed the line and dropped it as the designer's work. Meanwhile `lb-designer` sat idle with *waits on Paul's end-of-rounds answer*, until the grinder mailed it by hand.

A second cost compounds the first. Rule 6 never puts a work order in a declaration's first write, and only a line the member itself reported `done` is kept out (TD-1389). A line the member *dropped*, with its reason on the record, is told again after every `ao progress none`. The grinder was rung four times in about ten minutes for the same line, a wasted turn and a unit of the wake budget each time.

**Ask:** Will you let TD-1590's design (a decided line answering a design's end-of-rounds item goes to that design's designer instead of the grinders, and a member's own dropped work order stops ringing it after each none), whose four rounds ended at the cap with every build-changing fix read by a later review, go to its builds TD-1705 and TD-1706, or buy one more round, or hold it with a note?

**Design PR:** #3526

**Fix:** A design round for rule 6 (§6), §4.9a *What a session does with a decided line* and the end-of-rounds item (cadence §4.8):
- **Who a decided line goes to.** A decided line whose Context names a `design-first` entry's gate, such as the end-of-rounds item `TD-<plan> decision (<person>)`, goes to the lane that holds or owns that entry (its holder's lease, else its `Owner:`), not to `free-pick`. Alternatively, rule 6 tells that entry's holder with one note when the line is decided. Weigh this against §4.9a's *the answer is someone else's* path, where the grinder carries the line to the entry with `--carried-to`, and against the designer brief's own reading of its item. §4.4 (`4.4-host-agent.md` ~1941) already reads a line carried to a design-first entry `Part of` the design as holding, as *One more round* does, so the defect may be only that the grinder brief does not say to carry such a line. The round decides.
- **A line the member dropped.** A work order the member itself released with `ao progress drop <ref> --why` is kept out of `lane_seen`'s news after its next `none`, as a `done` one is (TD-1389). Define when it is told again: the line changes, or a sibling's release.

**Done when:** An answered end-of-rounds line reaches its design's own designer (or, with none, any designer the repo can reach) without a grinder's hand-off, and a member is not rung again after `none` for a work order it dropped itself. The design (§6 rule 6, §4.9a) says both.
