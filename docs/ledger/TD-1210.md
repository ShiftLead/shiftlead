## TD-1210: No test would catch `seat_names` listing `person` as a stub seat of a stopped team's rollup row

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-28 (test-audit-ao-1, from #2620)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py` (`seat_names`), `tests/test_ui_rollup_rows.py` (`test_a_rows_members_are_its_counted_cards_in_urgency_order_and_a_stopped_team_stubs_its_empty_seats`)

**Why:** #2620 made `seat_names` drop `person` ("never `person`, a person-held manager"), and the test's docstring says stubs are "matched by name, never `person`". The test never exercises it: the only stopped team in its `DEFS` (`design`) has `manager: "lead"`; the two teams with `manager: "person"` (`grind`, `review`) are live, and their rows draw no stubs. Probe: in `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py` change `n != "person" and n not in names[:i]` to `n not in names[:i]` and run `tests/test_ui_rollup_rows.py`, `test_ui_team_summary.py`, `test_ui_inbox.py`, `test_ui_card_completed.py`: 178 passed. A stopped team with a person-held manager would then draw a stub named *person* in its Members cell.

**Fix:** give the fixture a stopped team with `manager: "person"` (or a `seats` entry) and assert its `stubs` hold no `person`; `seat_names` unit-tested directly on a row naming `person` and a repeated name.

**Done when** the probe above fails the file and `pdm run test` passes.
