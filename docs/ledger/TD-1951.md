## TD-1951: A person's own unheld entry reads as theirs on the Queue, and the person's owner word is read the same in every repo

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 34f5776cd39e43cfba4a498a0a67f000
**Part of:** TD-1920

**Why:** TD-1920's design (design §4.4 *The card's status*, *A row's state*, *The page's kinds*, *Waiting*) reads a person's step — an open entry the person owns that nothing holds — as theirs on the Queue, never *ready*, and reads the person's owner words the same in every repo. This builds the reading in `sessionorc`; TD-1952 draws it.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/ledger.py, src/sessionorc/queue.py, src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py, src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py (the lift helper), src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py, src/shiftlead/ui/app.py, src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (row words), src/shiftlead/team_skill.md; design §4.4

**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *The page's kinds* (the person's owner words), *A row's state* (*yours to <verb>*, *after your TD-n*), *The card's status* (*A person's step reads as theirs, never ready*), *The Queue*'s `start`, and *Waiting* (a person's step is the person's own):
- `ledger.is_person_owner(owner)` (not `mail.is_person`, the caller test): the owner lower-cased is in `PERSON_WORDS` — `PERSON_OWNER` (`paul`) and `person`; replace every `Owner:` compare against `PERSON_OWNER` with it (`ledger.lane_gap`, `queue.py`'s owner tests — the lift test (`owner == PERSON` beside `decided_by`), the covered test, `_need`'s `owner`/`decision` and `_start`'s `owned`, each `str(…owner…).lower() == PERSON` —, `agent_inbox.py`, `shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`, `shiftlead/ui/app.py`, the lift helper's *yours to do* in `agent_mail.py`); `decided_by` and every `decided_by(…, PERSON)` call in `queue.py`, `board.py`'s gate test and every `decision (<person>)` written stay on `PERSON_OWNER`; the comment above `PERSON_OWNER` (it says the word comes from the `Fields:` line) and `team_skill.md`'s owner line say the two words.
- `queue.py`: one step predicate, read off the row: an entry whose row would read *ready* (no `decision (…)`, no open or unresolvable blocker, no open part, not settling, no `waiting` mark, no claim, no PR, no restart word) and `is_person_owner(owner)`; `row()` stamps it as `step` on the row and on the reading's entry (as TD-1949 stamps `open_work`), and `_own`, `_wait`, `waits` and `start` read the stamp; `_state_word` returns *yours to <verb>* in *ready*'s place (verb from `Kind:`: design-first → design, live-check → check, evaluation → evaluate, else start — `decision` too), every earlier word standing first. Every consumer below reads this predicate, so a claimed, reviewed, stalled or *with you* step is no step.
- the relation words: the reading gains `after_yours`, the qids among a row's `after` (its `Blocked by:` blockers alone; a step that is a part shows in its parent's meter) whose entry is a step; a `<repo>#TD-n` blocker is tested on that entry's row in its repo's reading where the home reads the repo, else not a step.
- `_status`: *yours* between *steer* and *stalled* (`STATUSES`): a card with no higher status some open member of which is a step (a card is a component of blocker and part edges, so a step and what it blocks share it); `_own` (the *Waiting* test's person's own) also holds a step itself — never a member blocked by it — so a member blocked only by steps, once they are set aside, does not wait; TD-1926's counted open work (TD-1949's `open_work`) keeps a step as open work, its fact and teal *waiting, then yours* unchanged, its blocker in `after_yours`.
- `start` and the header count: after the card's Do entry and its first `decision (paul)` entry, its first step by key, else the top entry; the card's wants gain a `step` list beside `decide` and `do` (`card_wants`, `items_n` in `ui/queue.py`), counted in the header's *two or more* rule with no button of its own — the header draws where two or more count or where no counted item draws its own button (`queue_card.html`'s `items_n == 1` test reads only items with a button), so a card whose one item is a step draws it; `card_entries` (sessionorc), `waits_on_me` and `walk_mine` (`app.py`) put each step in the prompt's *waits on me* list with its word (*yours to design*); a lone step's press opens with `why_key`'s *waits on me (its owner is me)* through `is_person_owner`; the walk names a step as work to start (*tell me its goal and how I start it*), no answers to wait for.
- the rank: a step row never draws the *passed over* line.
- no count reads it: the Queue tab, the top bar, the rail and a repo's count are unchanged.
- tests: an `Owner: person` and an `Owner: paul` entry with no gate read *yours to start* / *yours to design*, each word above *ready* standing first (a claim, a PR, settling, an `until` gate, *stalled*); a claimed step's card is not *yours* and its dependants' relation word is *after TD-n*; a parent blocked only by one reads *waiting* with the step in `after_yours`, its card *yours*, not Waiting; a card with a step and other open work is *yours*; a gated member whose only counted open work is a step reads *waiting, then yours* with the step in `after_yours`, its card *yours*, not Waiting; `start` on a card whose top entry is blocked by a cross-repo step is that step; a card with a step and one Do item draws the header's Start session and walks both; a card holding only a step draws the header's Start session on it; a cross-repo step from a read repo is a step, an unread repo's is not; `lane_gap` never marks an `Owner: person` entry; a gated `Owner: paul` entry is unchanged (*for you*, Do), and a gated `Owner: person` entry now reads Do (*yours to do*, **Done**) where it read Decide with its answers and Lift the hold (`_need`'s `decision`, the lift test's `owner` compare).

**Done when:** `ledger.is_person_owner` is the one owner predicate; a person's step reads *yours to <verb>*, its card *yours* and its blockers' dependants list it in `after_yours` in the Queue reading, a started step is no step, the card's `start` reaches a step, `pdm run test` passes with the tests above, and no count changes.
