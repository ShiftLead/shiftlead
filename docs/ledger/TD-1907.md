## TD-1907: A hidden answered item has no test for an outcome the Queue does not draw

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build

**Why:** #4026 (TD-1843 slice 4) built `unreplied` in `rewrite` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`) only from outcome items with `e.get("home") == "queue"`, and its comment says "an outcome the Queue draws, on the card it is drawn on: one in the Inbox alone, or on another card, hides nothing". Its tests, `tests/test_queue_outcome_lift.py`, `tests/test_queue_press_refused.py` and `tests/test_ui_qcard_press.py`, have no case for an outcome that is in the Inbox alone. Evidence: on `origin/main`, change the condition to `if blocked_unreplied(e)` (dropping the `home` test). Those three modules still pass (24 passed), and so do the 878 tests selected by `-k "queue or qcard or inbox or attach or lift"`. The other three conditions in that function (the card match, `replied`, `handed`) each fail a test when reverted. With the filter gone, an answered item would give way to an outcome the person cannot see on the Queue, and nothing would flag it.

**Location:** tests/test_queue_outcome_lift.py; src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (rewrite, unreplied)

**Fix:** In `tests/test_queue_outcome_lift.py`, add a case with a `blocked` outcome whose `home` is not `queue`, and assert the entry's *answered — waiting* item is still drawn and not hidden. Add one for an outcome on another card.

**Done when:** Dropping the `home == "queue"` test from the `unreplied` set in `queue.rewrite` fails a test.
