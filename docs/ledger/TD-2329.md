## TD-2329: Forgetting a login goes ahead although the director lists it

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 18f5bc21bb8a49d89c4503ba244f6fc4

**Why:** Design §4.2b *⋯ on a login* (`docs/design/4.2b-tools.md`, lines 132 to 134 at `69bc29a9a`): *Forget is refused, naming them, while a live session runs on the login, or a team's login list, a built-in set's, `director:` or a team's `schedule.login` names it*. Line 353 leaves a node alone out: *Forget on a node is not refused for the home's lists (a team's, `director:`), which it cannot read.*

The home checks no `director:` list. `rpc_login_forget` (`src/sessionorc/agent_logins.py`) passes `in_use=[*self._login_users(lk), *self._login_listed(lk)]`; `_login_users` reads live sessions only, and `_login_listed` returns `builtinstore.login_holders` over `settings.yml`, which reads team lists, built-in sets' lists and a switched team's `schedule.login`. After the cut-over the director's logins are `org.yml`'s `director.logins`, which the home writes on the director's record as its `login_list` (`stage_seats._rewrite_director`, `_launch_of`), and rule 3's fill picks from that list.

Probe at `69bc29a9a`, a test on the `agent` fixture (mode `home`) with the logins of `tests/test_login_verbs.py` and one record in state `exited` with `login_list: ["claude-code:grind"]` and `seat_model: "opus"`, the shape of the director between fills: `login_forget {login: "grind"}` replied `claude-code:grind forgotten`.

This was seen coming: TD-2068's log, 2026-11-23, *director: holds no logins yet. The build that gives it logins adds them to builtinstore.login_holders' caller, so a login the director lists cannot be forgotten.* TD-2068 gave the director its logins (slice 8, #4737) and was archived without that.

So the person can forget a login the director lists with no refusal; the director's next fill passes over it, and where it was the only usable login of the list the director cannot be filled. Found while auditing #4861 (TD-2320), which changed the other half of the same refusal.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_logins.py _login_listed; src/sessionorc/builtinstore.py login_holders; design 4.2b

**Fix:** Have the home's Forget refuse a login the director lists, in words beside `builtinstore.login_holders`' (*director*). The host agent reads no `org.yml` in this check and `sessionorc` never imports `shiftlead`, so the list at hand is the director record's `login_list` (the `prompt` record with no `builtin`, `_is_director`'s two marks, whether or not it can be filled now). Where `org.yml`'s key changed and the record is not yet written again, the record lags the key; say which one the refusal reads in §4.2b if it reads the record. A node stays unrefused, as the design says. A test beside `test_forget_and_naming_an_account_read_what_runs_and_what_is_retired`.

**Done when:** On the home, Forget of a login the director lists is refused, naming the director, while no session of the director is live, and a test fails without the check. A node's Forget is unchanged.
