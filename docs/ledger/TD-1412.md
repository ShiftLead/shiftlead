**Why:** Asked by agent-cadence's `ac-grinder` (2026-11-05). agent-cadence TD-233 (TD-177 build 3, resolved) makes a cadence merge the procedure's step 5 line. That line writes the squash's trailer paragraph, which ends in `Merged-by:`. It runs `check_cadence.py --pr <n> --trailers` into a `mktemp -d`, then `forge.py squash-message`, then `gh pr merge <n> --squash --match-head-commit … --subject … --body-file …` (on GitLab, `glab mr merge`). Once shiftlead's pin (`docs/cadence.toml`) carries it, two things here are out of step. First, briefs that say a bare `gh pr merge <n> --squash` teach a merge with no trailer: the techlead's merge of a held PR, the grinder and flow briefs under `src/shiftlead/briefs/` and `docs/briefs/`. Second, agent-cadence TD-234's `check_cadence.py --since` will list every squash with no `Merged-by:` as merged outside step 5. That includes the merges ao makes in its own code rather than through a session's command line (the board-edit tool's PRs, the `queue:` rank edits, any others). Design: agent-cadence `docs/plans/2026-10-31-merged-by-trailer.md`, Designs 2, 4 and 6.

**Fix:** Design first. Bump the pin past TD-233 (`agent-cadence bump`, a reviewed PR). List every merge ao makes in its own code, with where each is made. Then decide, for each, whether it goes through step 5's line or is named as a tool merge in the form Design 4 of the plan gives. Separately, change every brief's merge wording (the shipped briefs under `src/shiftlead/briefs/` and the repo's under `docs/briefs/`, a held path) to name step 5's line.

**Done when:** The design round has merged, and the builds it files carry `Part of:` this entry. Its builds are done when no brief names a bare `gh pr merge <n> --squash` outside the `{merge}` slot's own fallback sentence, and when `check_cadence.py --since` over a week of shiftlead's merges (after the first `Merged-by:` commit, the read's cutoff) lists nothing outside step 5 except a person's own web-UI merges and, only if agent-cadence declines TD-237, the board adds' PRs; a decided append that lifts a gate passes as a decide lift or not at all (TD-1421).
