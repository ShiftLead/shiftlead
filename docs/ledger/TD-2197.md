## TD-2197: A switch on the Queue's bar between a page that holds still and one that draws updates as they come

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 78897c9af5dc4e51ad8d51413968f50f
**Part of:** TD-2182

**Why:** Part of TD-2182 (design §4.5 screen 12 *Held or Live*, §4.5a *Held*, *Live*). Paul asked for a switch that lets the Queue draw its updates as they come, rather than holding every move for **Refresh**, with words saying why a person would want each. The check TD-2182 ran found the held page working as designed, so this is the switch alone: **Held** keeps today's page for answering cards, **Live** keeps a page current for someone watching the work move.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js; src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue.html, base.html; src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; docs/design/4.5a-controls.md (help list); tests

**Fix:** 1. **The switch** (`queue.html`, `app.js`). At the Queue bar's right end, apart from **Refresh**, draw a two-way switch, **Held** and **Live**: a `role="group"` with `aria-label="updates"`, outside the `role="status"` element (move the status to an inner element of the bar so the switch is not in it), its buttons styled as `.qwins`/`.qwin` are (`aria-pressed` on the pressed one) but under classes of their own (`.qlivesw`, `.qlive[data-live]`) with a click handler of their own, since `.qwin[data-win]`'s handler and `apply()` would take buttons of that class. It never moves when **Refresh** appears, when the bar floats, or on a narrow bar (it wraps to the end of the bar's last line). Its value is `ao.qlive` through the page's `store`, applied before the first paint: `base.html`'s Queue head script (the one that reads `ao.qwin`) also reads `ao.qlive` and sets `data-qlive` on `<html>`, and the switch's pressed look and `aria-pressed` follow it from the template's first draw (CSS keyed on `html[data-qlive]`, `aria-pressed` set from it before `app.js` draws anything), so a stored Live never flashes Held; read again on `pageshow` with `persisted`; a missing or unknown value is **Held**. A press stores it: **Live** draws the latest reading at once by the Live draw below; **Held** holds from the next re-read.
2. **The connection words.** On the Queue's bar only, *● live* becomes *● connected* and *not live — last read <age>* becomes *not connected — last read <age>* (`app.js` where `.qbarlive` is written for the Queue, and `queue.html`). The card's page keeps *● live* and *not live*.
3. **The Live draw** (`app.js`, the Queue's re-read). In Live, a served re-read whose places differ from the drawn copy is drawn whole. Places differ when `AO.qBarParts(diff)` is not empty (the parts Held would count), or when the keys or the order of Recently completed's lines (`.qdline`) or of the *other work* lines (`.qother`) differ from the drawn ones; add one helper that answers this (`AO.qPlacesDiffer(diff, reading)`), since a held card's values always differ and a plain compare would draw at every re-read. The test leaves out what a holding card owes (its own new, gone or moved items and rows, and its own move), so a card that holds never makes each later re-read draw again; those wait in the reading and `release()`'s Live draw draws them. Such a re-read is drawn whole by `drawWhole({keepOpen: true})`, with `keepOpen` extended from a card with a menu or box open to every card that holds (`holds()`: a mouse pointer over it, a touch from `pointerdown` to the press's end, keyboard focus inside it, a menu or box open on it): its node placed where the reading puts it (where it was, if it left), its values waiting as today, and `data-waits` set on each kept card whose values differ from the reading (today `drawWhole` sets none). The scroll anchor is one holding card (the one under the pointer or the touch, else the focused one, else the one with a menu or box open), kept where it was on the screen; another holding card keeps its node and values and may shift. Where a kept node is taken out and put back, the element that had focus inside it gets focus back, with its selection and caret (a *why?* input keeps what is typed and where), never only the card's header; with none holding, the first card in view, as **Refresh**'s. Slides, `prefers-reduced-motion` and focus to the next header where a focused card left are as **Refresh** does them. It closes no menu and no box: a menu or dialog not on a card holds nothing and the draw happens under it. A re-read during a rank drag is skipped as today (`.qcard.dragging`); one landing while a slide runs is drawn when the slide ends (a new guard: a timer set to the slide's end, drawing once with the newest reading, however many arrived during the slide). A re-read with no place changed draws nothing but patches values as today; the person's own press is drawn at once, as today. **A hold that ends in Live**: `release()` runs a Live draw where Held runs `patch()` (the kept card's values patched, and any place owed drawn), so a card that lets go never waits for the next re-read. The hidden page's rule, a rank move, the picks, a fold and Recently completed's window are unchanged.
4. **A touch lands where it was aimed.** After a Live draw that moved, added or removed a card in view, touch and pen presses on the Queue's lists in the next 500 ms are ignored, under `prefers-reduced-motion` too: record `pointerType` at `pointerdown` and extend the `sliding` capture listener so that in that window it swallows a click only when the press began as `touch` or `pen` with its `pointerdown` after the draw (a finger already down when the draw lands is on the anchor, which did not move, and its press lands), the recorded type cleared at each click, so a click with no fresh `pointerdown` (a key) is never swallowed. A mouse click and a keyboard Enter or Space are never swallowed by it (the slide's own swallow is unchanged). **A *gone* item on a holding card in Live** is drawn at once as in Held but reads *gone* (no *— refresh*, since Live draws no **Refresh**), and its row goes at the Live draw when the card lets go.
5. **The bar in Live** reads *● connected · drawn as it comes* (a holding card's own changes wait, as its *held* mark says), never floats, counts nothing and draws no **Refresh** (`drawBar` ignores the diff in Live, so a `patch()` there never draws a count); *not connected* is as in Held. The bar's `role="status"` moves from `#qbar` to an inner span holding the words and **Refresh**; `#qbar` stays what `AO.qbarFloat` and the CSS float. A Live draw that drew anything sets a visually hidden line inside that status to *drew <the parts' words>* (*drew 1 card moved, 1 new*), once per draw, written after `drawWhole`'s `drawBar(null)`, and `drawBar` leaves that line alone.
6. **Help** (`src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` and §4.5a's help list, word for word, in this PR; `tests/test_help.py` holds the two equal). Two new entries, keys `bar-held` and `bar-live`, each listed once in the Queue group of `SCREENS` after `queue-bar`:
   - `bar-held`, **Held** (Queue page: the bar): *Changes wait for Refresh, so nothing moves while you read or answer. Pick it when you are working through cards.*
   - `bar-live`, **Live** (Queue page: the bar): *Changes are drawn as they come, for watching the work move. The card you are pointing at, touching or typing in keeps its place while the rest moves around it.*
   The two switch buttons carry these as their `title` through `help_title`, as **Refresh** carries `queue-bar`'s.
   And three amended:
   - `queue-bar`: *In Held, counts what changed since the page was drawn — new asks for you, cards done, moved, new — without moving anything; Refresh draws it. In Live, changes are drawn as they come and it counts nothing.*
   - `card-gone` (its label *gone — refresh*, unchanged): *This item was answered or closed elsewhere; Refresh takes it away. In Live it reads gone, and goes when you leave the card.*
   - `card-held`: *Nothing on this card changes while your pointer, keyboard focus or an open menu is on it. In Live it also keeps its place on the screen while the page moves around it.*
7. **Tests**: the switch is drawn with **Held** pressed when nothing is stored, outside the status element; a stored **Live** is applied on load and on a back-forward return; the bar reads *● connected* / *not connected* on the Queue and *● live* on a card's page; in Live a re-read that moves a card, adds a card, adds an ask, or adds a Recently completed line draws it without **Refresh**, the bar reading *drawn as it comes* and the hidden line *drew …*; with the pointer over a card that the reading moves, that card stays at its screen position and its values wait; keyboard focus anchors the same way; an open menu is not closed; a re-read during a drag draws nothing, one during a slide draws after it; a touch press within 500 ms of a draw that moved a card in view is ignored, and a mouse click and a keyboard press in that window are not, a key press just after a touch tap among them; a touch begun before a draw lands; a holding card's own changes make no later re-read draw again, and draw when it lets go; a focused input in a kept card keeps focus and caret through a draw; a *gone* item on a holding card in Live reads *gone*; several readings during a slide draw once, the newest; the switch's buttons carry their help's first sentence as `title`. Existing tests updated, not deleted: `tests/test_ui_holds_still.py`'s pins of `id="qbar" role="status"` and of `AO.qbarFloat(bar, !!parts.length);`'s count, and the pins of *● live* and *not live* on the Queue, for the moved status, the connection words and `drawBar`'s Live path; a card that lets go in Live has its values patched and its place drawn at once, the bar counting nothing; pressing **Live** draws at once and pressing **Held** holds the next re-read; the hidden page draws as today in both.
8. **Look at it** (design §4.9b): on a scratch home, a shot of the bar in each state (Held up to date, Held counting, Live), in the PR (the design's mockup is `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-25-td2182-held-live.png`; its card's-page panels are superseded: the card's page has no switch).

**Done when:** The Queue's bar ends in **Held** | **Live**, **Held** by default and remembered in the browser, and reads *● connected* and *not connected* in place of *● live* and *not live* (the card's page keeping its words). In **Live** each re-read that changes a place is drawn without **Refresh**, the card the person is on keeping its place on the screen while the rest moves, never closing a menu or a box, a touch just after a draw ignored, each draw read out once (*drew …*), and a card that lets go drawn at once. The choice is stored as `ao.qlive`, applied before the first paint and on a back-forward return. In **Held** the page holds still as before. The card's page is unchanged. The help words for each say why a person would want it. Tests cover each of these, and the shots are in the PR.
