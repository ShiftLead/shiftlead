## TD-2197: A switch on the Queue's bar between a page that holds still and one that draws updates as they come

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 78897c9af5dc4e51ad8d51413968f50f
**Part of:** TD-2182

**Why:** Part of TD-2182 (design §4.5 screen 12 *Held or Live*, §4.5a *Held*, *Live*). Paul asked for a switch that lets the Queue draw its updates as they come, rather than holding every move for **Refresh**, with words saying why a person would want each. The check TD-2182 ran found the held page working as designed, so this is the switch alone: **Held** keeps today's page for answering cards, **Live** keeps a page current for someone watching the work move.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js; src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue.html, card_page.html; src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; docs/design/4.5a-controls.md (help list); tests

**Fix:** 1. **The switch.** At the end of the Queue's bar (`queue.html`, after **Refresh**) and the card's page's bar (`card_page.html`), draw a two-way switch, **Held** and **Live**, as the Recently completed window's buttons are drawn (`aria-pressed` on the pressed one). It is drawn in both the floating and the in-page bar. Its value is one key in `localStorage`, read by both pages; a missing or unknown value is **Held**. A press stores it and takes effect at once: pressing **Live** while something waits draws the latest reading whole, as the next live draw would (the hold rule below still applies), and pressing **Held** stops the next draw.
2. **Live on the Queue** (`app.js`, the Queue's re-read, beside today's `reread`). In Live, a served re-read that differs from the drawn copy is drawn whole by the same code **Refresh** runs (scroll anchor on the first card in view, the 200 ms slide or none under `prefers-reduced-motion`, focus to the next card's header where its card left), except that it closes no menu and no box. It waits while any card holds (the existing holds: a mouse pointer over a card, a touch from `pointerdown` until the press ends, keyboard focus inside a card, a menu or box open on one). Meanwhile the page behaves as Held: values patched but on the held card, the bar counting. When the last hold ends, a 1 s timer starts, cancelled by any new hold, and when it fires the latest reading is drawn whole. A re-read equal to the drawn copy draws nothing. The person's own press is drawn at once, as today. The hidden page's rule, a rank move, the picks, a fold and Recently completed's window are unchanged. While Live has nothing waiting, the bar reads *● live · drawn as it comes* in place of *● live · up to date*, and does not float. *not live* is unchanged.
3. **Live on the card's page.** In Live, a served re-read is drawn as the card's page's **Refresh** draws it, while no item or row holds and no box is open, with the same 1 s timer after the last hold ends. Where that **Refresh** would open another page (*no longer on the Queue*, a completed card's find), Live does not navigate: the bar counts it as today. In Live the page's line reads *● live — drawn as it comes* in place of *● live — values update in place; nothing here moves*.
4. **Help** (`src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` and §4.5a's help list, word for word, in this PR):
   - **Held** (Queue page and Card page: the bar): *Changes wait for Refresh, so nothing moves while you read or answer. Pick it when you are working through cards.*
   - **Live** (Queue page and Card page: the bar): *Changes are drawn as they come, for watching the work move. A card you are pointing at, typing in or have a menu open on holds the page until you leave it.*
   Also amend the bar's help entries (Queue page, Card page) to say *Held* counts and Refresh draws, and *Live* draws as it comes.
5. **Tests**: the switch is drawn on both bars with **Held** pressed when nothing is stored; a stored **Live** is read on both pages; in Live a re-read that moves a card, adds a card and adds an ask draws them without **Refresh** and the bar reads *drawn as it comes*; a hold (pointer over a card, an open menu) defers the draw and the bar counts, and the draw lands about 1 s after the hold ends; an open menu is not closed by a live draw; in Live the card's page never navigates on its own; switching back to **Held** holds the next re-read's places.
6. **Look at it** (design §4.9b): on a scratch home, a shot of each bar in each state, in the PR.

**Done when:** On the Queue and on a card's page the bar ends in **Held** | **Live**, **Held** by default and remembered in the browser. In **Live** a re-read is drawn whole without **Refresh**, waiting while the person is on a card and drawing about a second after they leave it, never closing a menu or a box and never navigating. In **Held** the page holds still as before. The help words for each say why a person would want it. Tests cover each of these, and the shots are in the PR.
