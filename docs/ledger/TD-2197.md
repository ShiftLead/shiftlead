## TD-2197: A switch on the Queue's bar between a page that holds still and one that draws updates as they come

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 78897c9af5dc4e51ad8d51413968f50f

**Part of:** TD-2182

**Why:** Part of TD-2182 (design §4.5 screen 12 *Held or Live*, §4.5a *Held*, *Live*). Paul asked for a switch that lets the Queue draw its updates as they come, rather than holding every move for **Refresh**, with words saying why a person would want each. The check TD-2182 ran found the held page working as designed, so this is the switch alone: **Held** keeps today's page for answering cards, **Live** keeps a page current for someone watching the work move.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the switch that lets the Queue draw its updates as they come, or run one more review round first?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js; src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue.html, base.html; src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; docs/design/4.5a-controls.md (help list); tests

**Fix:** 1. **The switch** (`queue.html`, `app.js`). At the Queue bar's right end, apart from **Refresh**, draw a two-way switch, **Held** and **Live**: a `role="group"` with `aria-label="updates"`, outside the `role="status"` element (move the status to an inner element of the bar so the switch is not in it), its buttons styled as `.qwins`/`.qwin` are (`aria-pressed` on the pressed one) but under classes of their own (`.qlivesw`, `.qlive[data-live]`) with a click handler of their own, since `.qwin[data-win]`'s handler and `apply()` would take buttons of that class. It never moves when **Refresh** appears, when the bar floats, or on a narrow bar (it wraps to the end of the bar's last line). Its value is `ao.qlive` through the page's `store`, applied before the first paint: `base.html`'s Queue head script (the one that reads `ao.qwin`) also reads `ao.qlive` and sets `data-qlive` on `<html>`, and the switch's pressed look and `aria-pressed` follow it from the template's first draw (CSS keyed on `html[data-qlive]`, `aria-pressed` set from it before `app.js` draws anything), so a stored Live never flashes Held; read again on `pageshow` with `persisted`; a missing or unknown value is **Held**. A press stores it: **Live** draws the latest reading at once by the Live draw below; **Held** holds from the next re-read.
2. **The connection words.** On the Queue's bar only, *● live* becomes *● connected* and *not live — last read <age>* becomes *not connected — last read <age>* (`app.js` where `.qbarlive` is written for the Queue, and `queue.html`). The card's page keeps *● live* and *not live*.
3. **The Live draw** (`app.js`, the Queue's re-read). In Live, a served re-read whose places differ from the drawn copy is drawn whole. Places differ when `AO.qBarParts(diff)` is not empty (the parts Held would count), or when the keys or the order of Recently completed's lines (`.qdline`) or of the *other work* lines (`.qother`) differ from the drawn ones; add one helper that answers this (`AO.qPlacesDiffer(diff, reading)`), since a held card's values always differ and a plain compare would draw at every re-read. The test leaves out what a holding card owes (its own new, gone or moved items and rows, and its own move), so a card that holds never makes each later re-read draw again; those wait in the reading and `release()`'s Live draw draws them. `AO.qDiff`'s counts are totals, so it takes the holding card ids (from `holds()`) as a set to leave out: their own items and rows (new, gone, moved), their own move and their leaving are not counted, and every other card's place is compared with the holding cards taken out of both the drawn and the reading's order, so a holding card's move counts no other card *moved*. `qPlacesDiffer` reads that filtered diff; Held calls `qDiff` with an empty set, as today. The re-read's dispatch (today `document.hidden || whole` choosing `drawWhole` or `patch()`) gains Live's branch ahead of `patch()`: it first applies the picks to the reading (`offIn(reading)`) and reads `folded` (the `.qgroup.qgfolded` groups) and `own`, as `patch()` does today ahead of its `qDiff`, so a card the picks hide or a folded group's card counts nothing; then it computes the filtered diff once and passes it to `qPlacesDiffer` and on to `patch()` where nothing is drawn whole, `patch()` taking a diff given to it rather than computing it again and skipping its own `offIn` and `qDiff` lines. Such a re-read is drawn whole by `drawWhole({keepOpen: true, slide: true, live: true})` (the same options for the two draws the person causes, pressing **Live** and `release()`'s Live draw), with `keepOpen` extended from a card with a menu or box open to every card that holds (`holds()`: a mouse pointer over it, a touch from `pointerdown` to the press's end, keyboard focus inside it, a menu or box open on it): its node placed where the reading puts it (where it was, if it left), its values waiting as today, and `data-waits` set on each kept card whose values differ from the reading (today `drawWhole` sets none). The scroll anchor is one holding card (the one under the pointer or the touch, else the focused one, else the one with a menu or box open), kept where it was on the screen; another holding card keeps its node and values and may shift. In a Live draw the holding anchor's branch runs ahead of `drawWhole`'s `atTop` branch and of a back-forward `anchorOn`, so a card arriving above it at the top of the page scrolls the page rather than pushing it down. Where the scroll cannot make up the difference (a card leaving above the anchor while `scrollY` is smaller than its height, or the page's end), the anchor moves with the page by what the scroll could not take up, placed at once like every holding card. No holding card is ever slid: `slide()` gives none a transform and puts none in `moving`, whatever its `dy`, and a `dy` under 1 px counts as no move for every card (the anchor's scroll lands on a whole pixel, so it can be a fraction off). Where a kept node is taken out and put back, the element that had focus inside it gets focus back, with its selection and caret (a *why?* input keeps what is typed and where), never only the card's header; with none holding, the first card in view, as **Refresh**'s. Slides, `prefers-reduced-motion` and focus to the next header where a focused card left are as **Refresh** does them. It closes no menu and no box: a menu or dialog not on a card holds nothing and the draw happens under it. A re-read during a rank drag is skipped as today (`.qcard.dragging`); one landing while a slide runs is drawn when the slide ends (a new guard: a timer set to the slide's end, drawing once with the newest reading, however many arrived during the slide). **A press in progress holds the draw too**: in Live a press is in progress on the lists from its `pointerdown` there until its click, or until `pointercancel`, or 350 ms after its `pointerup` when no click comes; a Live draw that would land while one is in progress waits, with the slide's guard, and is drawn once when the press ends, with the newest reading, so a kept node is never taken out between a press's down and its click (a browser fires no click on a node removed mid-press) and a tapped card never moves before its click lands. A touch or pen hold in Live likewise ends at its press's end so defined, not at `pointerup`'s 0 ms timer (Held keeps today's timer). The one guard serves all three Live draws: a re-read's, pressing **Live**'s and `release()`'s; and in Live the whole dispatch waits on it, a value `patch()` too, so `swapIn` never replaces a sliding card's node mid-slide. A re-read with no place changed draws nothing but patches values as today; the person's own press is drawn at once, as today. **A hold that ends in Live**: `release()` runs the re-read's own Live dispatch on the reading it holds — `offIn`, `folded` and `own`, the diff filtered by the cards that hold now, then `qPlacesDiffer` choosing a Live draw or `patch(diff)` — where Held runs `patch()` behind today's `data-waits` guard; so a whole draw happens only when a place is owed by a card that no longer holds, a card still holding draws nothing for it, and `data-waits` alone means `patch()`. The card that let go has its values patched and its owed place drawn, so it never waits for the next re-read. The hidden page's rule, a rank move, the picks, a fold and Recently completed's window are unchanged.
4. **A touch lands where it was aimed.** Every Live draw that is drawn whole (the places test true, with or without a slide, under `prefers-reduced-motion` too) sets one time, `touchGuardUntil` = the draw's start + 500 ms. A `pointerdown` listener on the Queue's lists records the press's `pointerType` and its time; the `sliding` capture listener swallows a click when the recorded press is `touch` or `pen` and its `pointerdown` time is at or after the draw's start and before `touchGuardUntil` (a press already in progress when the draw would land holds the draw until it ends, item 3, so the window never meets it). The time compared is the `pointerdown`'s, never the click's, so a tap begun at 490 ms is swallowed whenever its click comes. The record is cleared at each click and at `pointercancel` (a touch that became a scroll), and a click with `detail === 0` (a key's Enter or Space) is never swallowed by the window, whatever is recorded. A mouse click is never swallowed by the window. **The slide's swallow in Live.** The slide's 200 ms swallow stays as it is after **Refresh**, every press on the lists, since the person asked for that draw; a Live slide, which the person did not ask for, swallows a press only when its target is inside a card that is sliding (`slide()` keeps its `moving` cards for the slide's 200 ms, and `drawWhole`'s `live` option tells the listener to test the target against them), so a press on the anchor, on another holding card or on anything not moving lands. No slide runs under `prefers-reduced-motion`, so nothing is swallowed by it there, and the mid-slide guard reads `sliding` as before. **A *gone* item on a holding card in Live** is drawn at once as in Held but reads *gone* (no *— refresh*, since Live draws no **Refresh**), and its row goes at the Live draw when the card lets go.
5. **The bar in Live** reads *● connected · drawn as it comes* (a holding card's own changes wait, as its *held* mark says), never floats, counts nothing and draws no **Refresh** (`drawBar` ignores the diff in Live, so a `patch()` there never draws a count); *not connected* is as in Held. The bar's `role="status"` moves from `#qbar` to an inner span holding the words alone (`.qbarlive`, `.qbarw` and the visually hidden *drew …* line below), **Refresh** and the switch beside it, outside the status; `#qbar` stays what `AO.qbarFloat` and the CSS float. A Live draw that drew anything sets a visually hidden line inside that status to *drew <the parts' words>* (*drew 1 card moved, 1 new*), once per draw, written after `drawWhole`'s `drawBar(null)`, and `drawBar` leaves that line alone.
6. **Help** (`src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` and §4.5a's help list, word for word, in this PR; `tests/test_help.py` holds the two equal). Two new entries, keys `bar-held` and `bar-live`, each listed once in the Queue group of `SCREENS` after `queue-bar`:
   - `bar-held`, **Held** (Queue page: the bar): *Changes wait for Refresh, so nothing moves while you read or answer. Pick it when you are working through cards.*
   - `bar-live`, **Live** (Queue page: the bar): *Changes are drawn as they come, for watching the work move. The card you are pointing at, touching or typing in keeps its place while the rest moves around it.*
   The two switch buttons carry these as their `title` through `help_title`, as **Refresh** carries `queue-bar`'s.
   And three amended:
   - `queue-bar`: *In Held, counts what changed since the page was drawn — new asks for you, cards done, moved, new — without moving anything; Refresh draws it. In Live, changes are drawn as they come and it counts nothing.*
   - `card-gone` (its label *gone — refresh*, unchanged): *This item was answered or closed elsewhere; Refresh takes it away. In Live it reads gone, and goes when you leave the card.*
   - `card-held`: *Nothing on this card changes while your pointer, keyboard focus or an open menu is on it. In Live the card you are pointing at, touching or typing in also keeps its place on the screen while the page moves around it.*
