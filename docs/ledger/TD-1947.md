**Why:** TD-1919's design (design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present*, *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand*): a session's window follows its clients today, and every resize or reattach makes Claude Code repaint over lines tmux has kept — 5 to 13 of 30 streamed paragraphs repeated on a width change, 3 to 4 lost on a height change; pinned, none in 8 of 8 runs. It is the pin, the grid the bridge attaches at and reports, the fit it carries out, and the Focus page drawing at the pane's grid (moved here from TD-1948 by F113), so the pin and the page that draws at it land together.

**Ask:** The terminal size design ran a seventh review round, and three late fixes are unreviewed; should a session run one more round?

**Location:** design §4.6 Attach behaviour with another client present, Fit to window; §5 person.terminal.grid; src/sessionorc/tmux.py (new_session, status_off), src/sessionorc/settings.py (TERMINAL_KEYS), src/sessionorc/agent_settings.py, the host agent's start, src/shiftlead/ui/pty_bridge.py, src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (the terminal websocket, api_settings_person), tests/test_resize_repaint_live.py, src/sessionorc/agent.py (new_session's two callers, status_off), tests/test_pty_bridge_resize_log.py (`term fit`), tests/test_ui.py (clients_frame), tests/test_ui_term_mark.py, tests/test_ui_term_resize.py, tests/test_ui_term_look.py, tests/test_ui_pinch_swipe.py, tests/test_ui_phone_focus.py, src/shiftlead/ui/static/vendor/addon-fit.js, src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Focus terminal's drawing), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html, src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css (.termbox, .xterm)

**Fix:** As design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present* and *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand* say, and §5 `person.terminal.grid`:
1. **The pin.** `Tmux.new_session` (`src/sessionorc/tmux.py`; both callers in `src/sessionorc/agent.py`, the session start and the tools shell, so a tools shell is pinned too) passes `-x <cols> -y <rows>` to `new-session` and chains `set-option -w … window-size manual` into the same invocation as `remain-on-exit` (no `resize-window`, so the tool is never resized while it starts), the grid from `person.terminal.grid` (absent, 120 × 40) passed in by the host agent; the host agent's start sets `window-size manual` on every `ao-*` window at the size it has, unconditionally (a server whose global option is `manual` reads the same), beside `status_off`, never through the server's global options.
2. **The setting.** `person.terminal.grid` in `settings.yml` (§5): `TERMINAL_KEYS` (`src/sessionorc/settings.py`) gains `grid`, validated as two ints, columns 20–500 and rows 5–200, beside the other keys' checks; `src/sessionorc/agent_settings.py`'s list of the person's terminal keys with it; the Focus page's `/api/settings/person` route (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, `api_settings_person`) accepts `{terminal: {grid: [cols, rows]}}` beside `copy_on_select`; the host agent reads it at each session start.
3. **The bridge** (`src/shiftlead/ui/pty_bridge.py` and the terminal websocket in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`): read the window's size before the attach and open the attach's pty at it (a reading that fails, bounded at three seconds, opens the pty at `person.terminal.grid`, else 120 × 40, and sends that grid as the `grid` frame; the five-second reading goes on, and the first good one that differs resizes the pty and sends its frame as any change does); the terminal websocket ignores `cols` and `rows` on its address (the `_int_param` reads go; the page sends none); send `{"grid": [cols, rows]}` before the first pane byte and whenever the five-second reading of the window changes (`clients_argv`, `clients_frame`, `watch_clients` and `CLIENTS_EVERY` are rewritten for it: the reading drops `#{session_attached}` and keeps `#{window_width} #{window_height} #{status}`, the rows counted with the status bar's lines, which are none, and their docstrings lose the retired mark); a `{"fit": [cols, rows]}` message is bounded by the setting's bounds (one constant in `settings.py` beside `TERMINAL_KEYS`, used by both), refused and logged outside them, else runs `resize-window` on the session's window, reads the window again and resizes the pty to that reading (`pty.resize`), then sends that reading as the `grid` frame, so a failed command answers with the grid the window kept; a changed reading of the window resizes this bridge's own pty before its `grid` frame goes, so a fit from another browser leaves no client at the old size; logged as `term fit <name> <cols>x<rows>` in place of today's `term resize` line; the window's reading moves before the `PtySession` is made, and the pump gains a way to send a text frame, since the fit's read, resize and answer run inside it; a fit and the five-second watcher share one last-sent grid and one lock, so a watcher reading taken before a fit is dropped rather than sent after it; a `{"resize": …}` message is ignored (logged once per attach), and the pump's docstring says so. A read-only attach passes a `fit` (it types nothing), so an `unattended` session is fitted without a Take over.
4. **Draw at the pane's grid** (`src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, the Focus template and its CSS; moved here from TD-1948 so the pin and the page that draws at it land together, since a page still fitting xterm to its box against a pinned pty wraps every narrower Focus): The terminal is sized by the bridge's `grid` frame (`term.resize`), never by `FitAddon.fit()` on its box: every `fit.fit()` call in `app.js` goes — `AO.setTermLook`'s, `AO.termFont`'s, the open's (`term.open(…); fit.fit()`), `pinchEnd`'s, `phone.relaid`'s, the box's `ResizeObserver`'s (the observer itself stays: it is the box change that sets the vertical view and, in TD-1948, keeps the pixels) and `phone.closed(() => fit.fit())` — and `AO.termFont` takes no `fit` (once the face lands it sets the font again and xterm measures its cell anew, the grid unchanged), as do `AO.resizeSender`, `sendGrid`, the `term.onResize` wiring and the `cols` and `rows` on the terminal socket's address; the terminal opens at the first `grid` frame's size; a face or size change changes how many of the grid's columns the box shows, never the grid. The put-away, the phone's keyboard, turn, pinch and sheets send nothing. A box smaller than the grid as §4.6 says: `.termbox` the scroll container (`overflow-x: auto`, `overflow-y: hidden`) holding `.xterm` at the grid's pixel size (today's `height: 100%` on `.xterm` and the viewport's clip go); vertically the box-height run of rows ending at the lower of the cursor's row (xterm's `term.buffer.active.cursorY`, copy mode's cursor included) and the last row with any text on it (`translateToString(true)` not empty, a row of coloured blanks empty), a run that would start above the top row starting at it and one that would leave the cursor's row above it starting at that row, set on every cursor move, box change, face or size change (Settings, the bundled face landing) and `grid` frame; sideways from the left, moved by a sideways wheel, Shift+wheel or a sideways finger (the TD-986 handler, which today tracks only a touch's vertical start, tracks both axes, locks a drag to the axis on which it first travels 10 px and sets `scrollLeft` on a sideways one, a touch that moves less than 10 px on either axis staying a tap), the cursor's column kept in view while the terminal has focus, the helper text box focused with `preventScroll`; the wheel and the vertical drag still send `scroll`, a page (Shift+PageUp, Shift+PageDown, today a bare `{scroll}` that tmux pages by the pane's height) sent as `{scroll, lines}` of the box's rows less one (at least one) while the box is shorter than the grid; their lines counted over the renderer's CSS cell height (`term._core._renderService.dimensions.css.cell.height`, the cell the fit addon reads), never today's `cellH` (`$("#term").clientHeight / term.rows`), which divides the box rather than the grid; the selection's edge test (`AO.pastEdge`) takes the same cell height. Each `grid` frame received is logged to the console as §4.6's terminal events are; until TD-1948 rewrites the pinch's note it reads the pane's columns. A larger box leaves the room empty. The fit addon goes with its calls (its script in the Focus template, `vendor/addon-fit.js`, `AO.terms`' `fit` member); the page's `clients` branch and the *resized by another client* mark go too, since no `clients` frame feeds them once item 3 lands. A mouse's selection drag measures *went past the edge* against the rows shown (`.termbox`'s edge where the box is shorter than the grid, else the screen's).
5. **A plain Fit to window**, so a window pinned at whatever size it had when the host agent started (a phone's, a popped-out window's) can be resized before TD-1948 lands: on the wide layout's own Focus (not a phone, not a Pop out) *more ▾* gains **Fit to window**, whose press computes the box's grid by `AO.boxGrid()` (§4.6: `.termbox`'s rendered size less its padding and borders over the renderer's CSS cell, each floored) and sends `{"fit": [cols, rows]}` at once, a grid outside the bridge's bounds sending nothing; it waits for nothing, measures no phone and writes no setting — TD-1948 grows it into the whole control. It is not drawn on an ended record, nor when the box's grid is the pane's already, and a press whose grid equals the pane's sends nothing and notes *already this size*. On a wide page on a coarse pointer (a tablet) it measures the live box, keyboard up included, until TD-1948's blur and hold land (accepted).
6. **Tests:** the unit tests of the pin's argv, a started pane's size equal to the grid with the status line off (private tmux socket; tmux 3.5a measured so, 2026-11-17), the start's pin of an unpinned session (private tmux socket), the bridge's grid frame and fit; the live test's pinned mode (`tests/test_resize_repaint_live.py`) runs through the real `new_session` rather than its own `set-option`, a client attached at another size than the window while the reply streams leaves every paragraph once, and a fit at idle that grows the grid leaves every paragraph once; the page's terminal sized by the `grid` frame and never by its box, with the scroll container's sideways and vertical views; and the look first's shots (design §4.9b) of a wide Focus whose box is smaller and larger than the grid, in the PR.

**Done when:** A new `ao-*` session starts at `person.terminal.grid` (`new-session -x -y`, no `resize-window`) with its window reading `window-size manual` and its pane exactly the grid; a host agent start sets `window-size manual` on every `ao-*` window at its size; the setting is validated (columns 20–500, rows 5–200) and accepted by the Focus page's settings route; the bridge attaches at the window's size and sends the `grid` frame (a failed reading, bounded at three seconds, opening the pty at the person's grid, else 120 × 40, and sending that grid as the frame before the first pane byte, the first good reading that differs resizing it and sending its frame; `cols` and `rows` on the address ignored); a `fit` within the bounds resizes the window and the pty, one outside them is refused, a read-only attach passes it, a changed reading resizes the bridge's pty before its frame, and a `resize` changes nothing; a Focus draws the pane at the bridge's grid, opening at the first frame's, with no resize sent by any box change and no `cols` or `rows` on the socket's address, a smaller box scrolling sideways from the left and showing the box-height run ending at the lower of the cursor's row and the last drawn row (the cursor's row always shown), set again on a face, size or `grid` change, a page scrolling the box's rows less one, a scroll into history passing through it; a finger's drag locked to the axis on which it first travels 10 px (a shorter touch a tap, a sideways drag setting `scrollLeft`), and the wheel's and the drag's lines counted over the renderer's cell height, not the box's height over the rows; the fit addon, the `clients` branch and the *resized* mark gone; the wide layout's plain Fit to window sending the box's grid as a `fit`; each under a test, the live test's pinned mode passes through `new_session`, and the shots are in the PR.
