## TD-1947: A session's window is pinned at one grid, and the bridge attaches at it and carries out the person's fit

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e7d54c8dd4164fefbf11a193a3266980
**Part of:** TD-1919

**Why:** TD-1919's design (design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present*, *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand*): a session's window follows its clients today, and every resize or reattach makes Claude Code repaint over lines tmux has kept — 5 to 13 of 30 streamed paragraphs repeated on a width change, 3 to 4 lost on a height change; pinned, none in 8 of 8 runs. This is the host side: the pin, the grid the bridge attaches at and reports, and the fit it carries out.

**Location:** design §4.6 Attach behaviour with another client present, Fit to window; §5 person.terminal.grid; src/sessionorc/tmux.py (new_session, status_off), src/sessionorc/settings.py (TERMINAL_KEYS), src/sessionorc/agent_settings.py, the host agent's start, src/shiftlead/ui/pty_bridge.py, src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (the terminal websocket, api_settings_person), tests/test_resize_repaint_live.py

**Fix:** As design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present* and *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand* say, and §5 `person.terminal.grid`:
1. **The pin.** `Tmux.new_session` (`src/sessionorc/tmux.py`) passes `-x <cols> -y <rows>` to `new-session` and chains `set-option -w … window-size manual` into the same invocation as `remain-on-exit` (no `resize-window`, so the tool is never resized while it starts), the grid from `person.terminal.grid` (absent, 120 × 40) passed in by the host agent; the host agent's start sets `window-size manual` on every `ao-*` window at the size it has, unconditionally (a server whose global option is `manual` reads the same), beside `status_off`, never through the server's global options.
2. **The setting.** `person.terminal.grid` in `settings.yml` (§5): `TERMINAL_KEYS` (`src/sessionorc/settings.py`) gains `grid`, validated as two ints, columns 20–500 and rows 5–200, beside the other keys' checks; `src/sessionorc/agent_settings.py`'s list of the person's terminal keys with it; the Focus page's `/api/settings/person` route (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, `api_settings_person`) accepts `{terminal: {grid: [cols, rows]}}` beside `copy_on_select`; the host agent reads it at each session start.
3. **The bridge** (`src/shiftlead/ui/pty_bridge.py` and the terminal websocket in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`): read the window's size before the attach and open the attach's pty at it; send `{"grid": [cols, rows]}` before the first pane byte and whenever the five-second reading of the window changes (`clients_argv`, `clients_frame`, `watch_clients` and `CLIENTS_EVERY` are rewritten for it: the reading drops `#{session_attached}` and keeps `#{window_width} #{window_height} #{status}`, the rows counted with the status bar's lines, which are none, and their docstrings lose the retired mark); a `{"fit": [cols, rows]}` message is bounded by the setting's bounds (one constant in `settings.py` beside `TERMINAL_KEYS`, used by both), refused and logged outside them, else runs `resize-window` on the session's window and resizes the pty with it (`pty.resize`), then sends the `grid` frame; a changed reading of the window resizes this bridge's own pty before its `grid` frame goes, so a fit from another browser leaves no client at the old size; logged as `term fit <name> <cols>x<rows>` in place of today's `term resize` line; a `{"resize": …}` message is ignored (logged once per attach), and the pump's docstring says so. A read-only attach passes a `fit` (it types nothing), so an `unattended` session is fitted without a Take over.
4. **Tests:** the unit tests of the pin's argv, a started pane's size equal to the grid with the status line off (private tmux socket; tmux 3.5a measured so, 2026-11-17), the start's pin of an unpinned session (private tmux socket), the bridge's grid frame and fit; the live test's pinned mode (`tests/test_resize_repaint_live.py`) runs through the real `new_session` rather than its own `set-option`, a client attached at another size than the window while the reply streams leaves every paragraph once, and a fit at idle that grows the grid leaves every paragraph once.

**Done when:** A new `ao-*` session starts at `person.terminal.grid` (`new-session -x -y`, no `resize-window`) with its window reading `window-size manual` and its pane exactly the grid; a host agent start sets `window-size manual` on every `ao-*` window at its size; the setting is validated (columns 20–500, rows 5–200) and accepted by the Focus page's settings route; the bridge attaches at the window's size and sends the `grid` frame; a `fit` within the bounds resizes the window and the pty, one outside them is refused, a read-only attach passes it, a changed reading resizes the bridge's pty before its frame, and a `resize` changes nothing; each under a test, and the live test's pinned mode passes through `new_session`.
