## TD-1947: A session's window is pinned at one grid, the bridge attaches at it and carries out the person's fit, and the Focus terminal draws at it

**Why:** TD-1919's design (design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present*, *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand*): a session's window follows its clients today, and every resize or reattach makes Claude Code repaint over lines tmux has kept — 5 to 13 of 30 streamed paragraphs repeated on a width change, 3 to 4 lost on a height change; pinned, none in 8 of 8 runs. This is the host side: the pin, the grid the bridge attaches at and reports, and the fit it carries out.

**Ask:** The terminal size design ran a fifth review round, and five late fixes are unreviewed; should a session run one more round?

**Location:** design §4.6 Attach behaviour with another client present, Fit to window; §5 person.terminal.grid; src/sessionorc/tmux.py (new_session, status_off), src/sessionorc/settings.py (TERMINAL_KEYS), src/sessionorc/agent_settings.py, the host agent's start, src/shiftlead/ui/pty_bridge.py, src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (the terminal websocket, api_settings_person), tests/test_resize_repaint_live.py, src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Focus terminal's drawing), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html

**Fix:** As design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present* and *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand* say, and §5 `person.terminal.grid`:
1. **The pin.** `Tmux.new_session` (`src/sessionorc/tmux.py`) passes `-x <cols> -y <rows>` to `new-session` and chains `set-option -w … window-size manual` into the same invocation as `remain-on-exit` (no `resize-window`, so the tool is never resized while it starts), the grid from `person.terminal.grid` (absent, 120 × 40) passed in by the host agent; the host agent's start sets `window-size manual` on every `ao-*` window at the size it has, unconditionally (a server whose global option is `manual` reads the same), beside `status_off`, never through the server's global options.
2. **The setting.** `person.terminal.grid` in `settings.yml` (§5): `TERMINAL_KEYS` (`src/sessionorc/settings.py`) gains `grid`, validated as two ints, columns 20–500 and rows 5–200, beside the other keys' checks; `src/sessionorc/agent_settings.py`'s list of the person's terminal keys with it; the Focus page's `/api/settings/person` route (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, `api_settings_person`) accepts `{terminal: {grid: [cols, rows]}}` beside `copy_on_select`; the host agent reads it at each session start.
3. **The bridge** (`src/shiftlead/ui/pty_bridge.py` and the terminal websocket in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`): read the window's size before the attach and open the attach's pty at it (a reading that fails, bounded at three seconds, opens the pty at `person.terminal.grid`, else 120 × 40, and sends that grid as the `grid` frame; the five-second reading goes on, and the first good one that differs resizes the pty and sends its frame as any change does); the terminal websocket ignores `cols` and `rows` on its address (the `_int_param` reads go; the page sends none); send `{"grid": [cols, rows]}` before the first pane byte and whenever the five-second reading of the window changes (`clients_argv`, `clients_frame`, `watch_clients` and `CLIENTS_EVERY` are rewritten for it: the reading drops `#{session_attached}` and keeps `#{window_width} #{window_height} #{status}`, the rows counted with the status bar's lines, which are none, and their docstrings lose the retired mark); a `{"fit": [cols, rows]}` message is bounded by the setting's bounds (one constant in `settings.py` beside `TERMINAL_KEYS`, used by both), refused and logged outside them, else runs `resize-window` on the session's window, reads the window again and resizes the pty to that reading (`pty.resize`), then sends that reading as the `grid` frame, so a failed command answers with the grid the window kept; a changed reading of the window resizes this bridge's own pty before its `grid` frame goes, so a fit from another browser leaves no client at the old size; logged as `term fit <name> <cols>x<rows>` in place of today's `term resize` line; a `{"resize": …}` message is ignored (logged once per attach), and the pump's docstring says so. A read-only attach passes a `fit` (it types nothing), so an `unattended` session is fitted without a Take over.
4. **Draw at the pane's grid** (`src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, the Focus template and its CSS; moved here from TD-1948 so the pin and the page that draws at it land together, since a page still fitting xterm to its box against a pinned pty wraps every narrower Focus): The terminal is sized by the bridge's `grid` frame (`term.resize`), never by `FitAddon.fit()` on its box: the `fit.fit()` calls in `AO.setTermLook` and `AO.termFont`'s path, the box's `ResizeObserver` and `phone.closed(() => fit.fit())` go, as do `AO.resizeSender`, `sendGrid`, the `term.onResize` wiring and the `cols` and `rows` on the terminal socket's address; the terminal opens at the first `grid` frame's size; a face or size change changes how many of the grid's columns the box shows, never the grid. The put-away, the phone's keyboard, turn, pinch and sheets send nothing. A box smaller than the grid as §4.6 says: `.termbox` the scroll container (`overflow-x: auto`, `overflow-y: hidden`) holding `.xterm` at the grid's pixel size (today's `height: 100%` on `.xterm` and the viewport's clip go); vertically the lowest box-height run of rows holding the cursor's row (xterm's `term.buffer.active.cursorY`, copy mode's cursor included), set on every cursor move and box change; sideways from the left, moved by a sideways wheel, Shift+wheel or a sideways finger (the TD-986 handler sets `scrollLeft`), the cursor's column kept in view while the terminal has focus, the helper text box focused with `preventScroll`; the wheel and the vertical drag still send `scroll`. A larger box leaves the room empty.
5. **Tests:** the unit tests of the pin's argv, a started pane's size equal to the grid with the status line off (private tmux socket; tmux 3.5a measured so, 2026-11-17), the start's pin of an unpinned session (private tmux socket), the bridge's grid frame and fit; the live test's pinned mode (`tests/test_resize_repaint_live.py`) runs through the real `new_session` rather than its own `set-option`, a client attached at another size than the window while the reply streams leaves every paragraph once, and a fit at idle that grows the grid leaves every paragraph once; the page's terminal sized by the `grid` frame and never by its box, with the scroll container's sideways and lowest-rows views; and the look first's shots (design §4.9b) of a wide Focus whose box is smaller and larger than the grid, in the PR.

**Done when:** A new `ao-*` session starts at `person.terminal.grid` (`new-session -x -y`, no `resize-window`) with its window reading `window-size manual` and its pane exactly the grid; a host agent start sets `window-size manual` on every `ao-*` window at its size; the setting is validated (columns 20–500, rows 5–200) and accepted by the Focus page's settings route; the bridge attaches at the window's size and sends the `grid` frame (a failed reading, bounded at three seconds, opening the pty at the person's grid, else 120 × 40, and sending that grid as the frame before the first pane byte, the first good reading that differs resizing it and sending its frame; `cols` and `rows` on the address ignored); a `fit` within the bounds resizes the window and the pty, one outside them is refused, a read-only attach passes it, a changed reading resizes the bridge's pty before its frame, and a `resize` changes nothing; a Focus draws the pane at the bridge's grid, opening at the first frame's, with no resize sent by any box change and no `cols` or `rows` on the socket's address, a smaller box scrolling sideways from the left and showing the lowest run of rows holding the cursor's row, a scroll into history passing through it; each under a test, the live test's pinned mode passes through `new_session`, and the shots are in the PR.
