## TD-1218: A first-prompt test failed once under the full suite and passes alone, so a precheck can go red for a reason unrelated to the PR

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-29 (grinder-ao-1, seen on #2647's precheck)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Waits:** event — the test fails again in a precheck or a full run; its assertion now names what the tick saw
**Status:** Open — seen once; not reproduced. Read 2026-10-29 (grinder-ao-1): 25 runs alone, 20 of them beside a second full suite on 16 cores at load ~10, and three full suites all passed. The failed run logged neither *the brief … was not sent* nor *did not submit*, so the tick never reached the mark. `_send_first_prompts` skips a record while its first-prompt task is in flight (`_prompting`), and that task's `capture_tail` is a tmux call bounded at 15 s (`tmux.run`), longer than the test's 10 s wait, so one slow capture would explain it; a tick that did not run for 10 s would too. The assertion now prints (`_unmarked` in the test file, both of its waits) whether a task was in flight, the state, `hook_seen_at`, the tries and the times, so the next failure names which.
**Location:** `tests/test_first_prompt.py` (`test_a_composer_that_never_reads_empty_is_marked_at_the_bound`); the bound it waits on, `agent_common.FIRST_PROMPT_BOUND` (patched to 2.0 s), and the tick that marks `first_prompt_error`

**Why:** #2647's precheck on 72e2b1d (2026-10-29, a clean worktree running the whole suite) failed with `AssertionError: never marked at the bound` at `tests/test_first_prompt.py:208`: `wait_for(lambda: _marked(rec), timeout=10)` saw no `first_prompt_error` within 10 s, where the test sets `FIRST_PROMPT_HOOK_WAIT` 0.2 s and `FIRST_PROMPT_BOUND` 2.0 s. The PR changed only `tests/test_start_time.py` and a ledger file, and the same head passed its next precheck. Run alone five times on the same machine it passed in about 3 s each. So something in the full suite's order or load can keep the mark from landing for 10 s. That may be a tick parked or slowed by an earlier test, or a scraped `idle` overwritten before the bound. A precheck red for that reason costs a rerun of the whole suite (about 3 min) and reads as the PR's fault.

**Fix:** reproduce under load first: the whole suite in its own order, as the precheck runs it, or this file run after `tests/test_agent*.py`, or beside a second suite run. Then read what the tick (`agent_wake.py`, where it sets `first_prompt_error`) does with the record between the scraped `idle` and the bound. Fix the cause, not the timeout: raise the timeout only if the bound is shown to be reached late for a reason the test does not own.

**Done when:** the cause is named in this entry, and the test passes in the full suite under load, or this entry says why the flake cannot recur.
