**Why:** `_id_alarm` (src/sessionorc/agent_identity.py) logs `identity alarm (%s): %s claimed %r on %s`; since TD-1644 the method is bounded to 60 characters, but `claimed` — the caller's own `caller` field, normalised by `_addr` — is printed whole, and is the caller's word as much as the method is.
