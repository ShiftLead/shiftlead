**Location:** src/sessionorc/board.py (the lift write); src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py rpc_ledger_decide; src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js; tests/

**Fix:** 1. **The write** (`src/sessionorc/board.py`): a new write-back that lands, in one commit of the host agent's tree on origin's head (subject and PR title `shiftlead: lift decision (<p>) on TD-n (session n/a)`), the lift and any lifted close (§4.4 *Board write-back*: *Lift the hold is a lift on the press*). Do these in order:
   - **Gone check.** Read the entry in the tree. Archived, or no `decision (<p>)`: return `{gone: true}`, write nothing.
   - **`by`.** `lb_board.who_answered(board_text, answerer(root, board_text))`. Refuse *only <p> lifts <p>'s hold* where it is not the gate's `<p>` (any case). Pass no `people` or `answered_by`.
   - **Cases.** Run `covers()` for the gate, given the entry's own repo's board alone. Try the cases in this order:
     - (d) an open undecided item lists no other gated entry: `Refused("TD-n's hold is asked on board:<key>: answer it there")`.
     - (b) open decided items list no other gated entry. If any is among the `board:<key>`s the caller hands in as held: `Refused("board:<key> is being acted on by <holder>: …")`. Else `plan_lift(..., answer="go ahead")` with no suffix, plus `lb_board.plan_edit` `done`, why `lifted`, on each such item (`Closed:` ending `— lifted.`).
     - (c) an open item lists another gated entry: the lift with suffix `shared board item left open`.
     - (a) nothing covers: the lift with suffix `no board item`.
   - **Lists another gated entry** means the item's `Context:` entry, or a bare `Parts:` id, is open in the tree's ledger, is not the pressed entry, and carries the same gate. A `<repo>#TD-n` is not counted.
   - **Return** `{lifted: true}`. Any other ledgerboard `Refused` is the press's refusal, in its words.
