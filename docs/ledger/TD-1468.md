**Why:** TD-1453's design, second build: every item that needs the person draws on a Queue card, and a stuck seat is current work (Paul's principle, m-b3f3df732ba0: what affects current work goes to the Queue, on its entry's card).

**Ask:** Should TD-1453's builds (one primary place for each thing that needs you, kept and loose Queue cards, a stopped seat's permission, question, plain needs you, limited and stalled? rows as Do items on its entry's card, asks closing themselves when their PR merges or their entry is archived) go ahead as written after the seventh round you bought, whose reviews raised two build-changing findings (F114, the tie between one seat's claims, read by Sonnet; F118, a stuck line and its holder's row both in the Inbox are one item, fixed, not reviewed)? Answered on the board's end-of-rounds item for TD-1453 (One more round | Go | Hold: <note>).

**Fix:** Design §4.5 screen 6 *One place for each thing that needs the person*, *Every item that needs the person draws on a card*; screen 12 *Three wants* (what is attached to a card), *Recently completed* (a kept card), *In progress* (a loose card) (TD-1453).
- **A PR-only item**: an item that needs the person and carries only a PR (`pr` with `repo`, TD-1467, or an `about` of shape `#n`) names the entry its PR names — in the repo's open PRs by title or branch, else in its month (`recent`) by title, and by branch where the reading keeps one — qualified by the item's `repo` (`tied.by: pr`, as a red PR's row; `red_pr`'s `_refs` qualifies nothing today), and the tied entry then takes the open, kept or loose card as any entry ref does; with none tied, the PR is its loose card's ref. A PR-only item with no `repo` takes one through TD-1467's helper, which owns the order (*fixed, not reviewed*: F59); with none it stays in the Inbox.
- **Kept card**: the Queue's reading (`src/sessionorc/queue.py`) adds to `card_index` the completed cards' entries for items that need the person (an open `ask` or `steer` to the person, an undecided Decide or Do line): such an item is `home: queue` on that completed card; the page draws the card as a card (not a line), status *needs you*, at the head of Recently completed whatever the window and outside its count, opening the group as a find does; it returns to its line once no such item is open. An FYI row, a note or a closed question naming a completed card's entry stays `home: inbox`, as now.
- **Loose card**: an item that needs the person and names a ref no card (open or completed) holds — an unread repo's entry, an id found nowhere — draws on a card keyed `loose:<ref>`, the ref its first entry ref with its PR (`<repo>#<n>`) a mention, else — an item whose only anchor is a PR no entry is tied to — that PR, so two items about one entry share one card; head the ref and the first item's subject, no members, stripe, number or **⋯** beyond the items' own, status *needs you*, at the end of In progress after the *other work* lines' cards; counted in the needs-you filter; gone once none of its items is open.
- **A stopped holder's state rows**: the state rows of a permission, a question on its pane, the plain *needs you* (`needs`, drawn as a question is: the text, **Open**, no Allow / Deny), `limited` and `stalled?` — those five and no other (an exited session with unpushed work, a restart, *idle · open work*, *held · mail waiting*, alarms, cadence and CI rows stay in the Inbox) — of a session — the person's own among them — whose live claim is an entry id an open card holds — the claim the holder pill reads, the reading's `held` (`src/sessionorc/queue.py`): a `claimed` progress entry, declared or derived, no `LEASE_TTL`, so it reads the claims the pill reads (Open session meets a seat holding by its `td-<n>` name sooner, TD-678: the one surface that differs until the tick derives the claim), never §4.8's lease (`lease_holders`, which the orphan standing reads); a session holding by its `td-<n>` name alone keeps its row in the Inbox until the tick derives its claim; each seat that holds the entry moves its own row, the pill naming one of them (a `board:`, PR or qualified-ref (`<repo>#TD-n`, which `held` keys as other, `src/sessionorc/queue.py` ~842) claim keeps the row in the Inbox; a seat holding two or more, its declared claims first — the newest such claim an open card holds, by the claim's `at`, one with no readable `at` the oldest, a tie by the later in the record's `progress` list, its insertion order (`_upsert` updates a re-declared claim in place, so it keeps its place) — and only with no declared claim on an open card its derived one (`reports.derive` rebuilds it each pass, `at` the pass's, `src/sessionorc/reports.py` ~114, so it never weighs against a declared claim; several derived, the newest by `at`, then the same tie; a re-declare moves its claim's `at` to the re-declare), so the row is drawn once, the other cards showing the holder pill alone; a newer claim on an archived entry, a `board:` ref or a PR passes to the next) are `home: queue` on that card, `tied: {by: "holder", ref: <the claim's ref, qualified by the record's repo through TD-1467's helper>}` (`inbox_sections`, `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`; `attach`, `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`), drawn as a **Do** item in the card's header (§4.5 screen 12 *Three wants*: it makes the card *needs you*, §4.4 *The card's status*, is counted on the card's pill, shown on a folded card, and kept by the needs-you filter; the person's own session's row too) with the controls of §4.5a's new row *Queue card: holder's state row* (Allow / Deny where the adapter has `permission_channel`, else *answer it in the terminal*; Open; no Snooze, the Queue has none, `stalled?`'s Inbox Snooze among it: the row ends when the seat moves or is restarted), counted on the Queue tab and not the Inbox's; the holder's pill is its mirror. Where `seat_able` reads the seat unable (TD-1443), the row carries the *routed around* line, as the Sessions card does. In a group the person folded it is hidden as an ask is. It carries none of the card's per-item verbs — no Answer, no Start session, no ⋯ — and does not count toward the header's Start session rule (two or more items), as a red PR does not (F86). The card's item is built from the Inbox's state row — its record id, `call`, `deadline`, time left and subagent-held text — and its Allow / Deny posts the same request as the Inbox row's, with the same `call` check, so a permission pushed after the page was drawn is never answered unseen. A `stalled?` row snoozed in the Inbox before its seat held the entry stays in **Snoozed (n)** until its time, then draws on the card; on the card a `stalled?` row ends when the seat moves or is restarted. A seat holding no entry an open card holds (an archived entry, an unread repo's, one found nowhere: no kept or loose card is made for a state row), an `unreachable` host, a promote row and a team start stay in the Inbox. (The techlead's answer to the designer's steer, m-88a25174a84a; Paul's Go on the end-of-rounds item confirms it.)
- **The needs-you switch** (screen 12 *Filters*): while on, it hides the Recently completed lines and the *other work* lines but keeps kept and loose cards. A loose card answers the find; a team chip keeps it only where its ref's repo is one the chip's team services; one whose repo no team services shows only with no chip picked.
- **Addresses**: a stopped holder's state row rings by its row key in `/queue?card=…&row=…&item=<key>`; a loose card's address is `/queue?card=loose:<ref>&item=<entry id>` with no `row=`; Telegram's link (§4.10, `notify`) for a `home: queue` state row is its card's address, not `/inbox?row=`.
- **Counts**: the needs-you filter counts cards and the Queue tab items, so a card with a holder's row adds one to each, and two rows on one card two to the tab and one to the filter; a kept card is outside Recently completed's count but in the needs-you filter's, as a loose card is — two counts computed apart, so the heading may read *(0)* above a kept card; a loose card is in *In progress (n)*. The Queue tab counts a stopped holder's row, the Inbox tab does not; the Sessions rollup's *answer needed* is a count of sessions (screen 1), not a tab's number: two stopped holders on one card are two on the rollup and two rows on the Queue's number. `ao inbox --counts` and the rail read the same split.
- A board line that needs the person and names an archived entry, or an id found nowhere, draws on the kept or loose card, as mail does (screen 6 *A board line's refs*).
- An entry archived outside every completed card the reading builds has no card to keep: its item draws on a loose card.
- Order: only the loose card's `<repo>#<n>` key reads TD-1467's `repo`; the kept card and the holder's rows do not wait on it, but the build follows TD-1467 because both change `item_home`.
- A handed entry and its thread stay in the Inbox until the seat names the entry, then draw on its card by the mail rule; nothing new to build beyond TD-1467's read, but a test pins it.
- **Fixed, not reviewed (round 4, Sonnet, after the cap's last review; the builder checks each):**
