**Part of:** TD-1296
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/settle.py` (`hold`, `prehold`, the `held` records, the held branch of `claim_refusal`); `src/sessionorc/agent_ids.py` (`rpc_td_hold` ~243, `_clear_hold`, `_hold_notice`, `_hold_tell` ~360–372, the `--held` prehold in `td_next`); `src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py` (`_ledger_road` ~965); `src/sessionorc/mail.py` (`PERSON_ONLY` ~147, `PERSON_SESSION` ~171); `src/sessionorc/modes.py` (`HOME_EDITS` ~37, ~76); `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (*held (person)* ~708, the `DESIGNER` cases ~358, ~704); `src/sessionorc/ledger.py` (`DESIGNER_OWNER` in `lane_matches` / `in_lanes`, ~292, ~360, ~456); `src/sessionorc/agent_rank.py`; `src/shiftlead/cli.py` (`cmd_td_hold` ~3549, ~5173, `ao td reserve --held`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (the `td_hold` route ~3137–3149); `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py` (~405, ~467, ~543); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (~2439, ~2513); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (~260); templates `queue_row.html`, `inbox_row.html` (~1177); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (~1320, ~4700, ~4733); `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py`; `src/shiftlead/briefs/entry.md`; `docs/briefs/README.md`; `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (~3397, the *held (person)* mark's title); `src/shiftlead/cli.py` (~2056, `decided_by(e, DESIGNER_OWNER)`); the *passes two of them* citations (`src/sessionorc/agent_common.py` ~347, `src/sessionorc/board.py` ~674, and others); the designer's lane as written in `src/shiftlead/team_skill.md` (~239), `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md` (~12) and `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` (~5); `docs/glossary.md` (~75, *designer*)

**Why:** TD-1296's design (Paul, 2026-10-31, ledgerboard's target design §0 and §3): a hold lived in one home's settle clock, so agent-cadence and ShiftLead on another machine picked a held entry anyway. The ledger's `decision (<person>)` gate with an `**Ask:**` says the same "not yet" where every reader sees it. The design (§4.4 *Holding an entry is a decision on it*, *The tell*, *An entry can land held*; §4.5 screens 6, 11, 12; §4.5a; §4.7; §4.8 *A person's session passes one of them*) now has no Hold.

**Fix:**
1. **Migrate first.** Before any removal, read every hold still recorded (`ao repo --json`, an entry's `held`), and for each write `**Blocked by:** decision (<person>)` with an `**Ask:**` (the hold's `why`, else *Go?*) in that repo's ledger by PR, one PR per repo; a hold the person no longer wants is released instead. Say each in the PR body. So no clock hold and decision gate stand side by side, and none is lost.
2. **The clock keeps settling alone**: `settle.py` drops `hold`, `prehold` and the `held` records (an old record's `held` is ignored on load); `claim_refusal` refuses a settling entry only.
3. **`td_hold` goes whole**: `rpc_td_hold`, `_clear_hold`, `_hold_notice`; `PERSON_ONLY` and `HOME_EDITS` drop it; `PERSON_SESSION` is `{"ledger_decide"}`. The tell stays as `_ledger_road`'s first step (renamed from `_hold_tell`), which no longer clears a hold.
4. **CLI**: `ao td hold` and `ao td reserve --held` go (an old `--held` is an unknown option, argparse's own refusal); `ao repo` prints no *held 2h*, and an entry carries no `held` in its `--json` (a PR's `held`, its matched paths, §4.7, stays).
5. **`<person>` is held once**: every place that writes or reads the gate's person word uses `ledger.PERSON_OWNER`, the ledger's `Fields:` person word, never a `"paul"` literal of its own.
6. **Drop the `decision (designer)` cases** in `queue.py` (~358, ~704), `ledger.py`'s lane match and `cli.py` (~2056): a design-first entry routes by `Kind: design-first`. The designer's lane as the briefs and skill word it (`team_skill.md`, `briefs/designer.md`, `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md`: *and any entry blocked by `decision (designer)`*) and the glossary's *designer* drop that clause too; `docs/briefs/**` is a held path, so that slice waits for its reader.
7. **UI**: no **Hold…** or **Release** in any ⋯ (Queue card, Inbox ledger row, Repo page row); the Queue card's *held (person)* status and want, the *Inbox row: held entry* and its `ledger · held` kind go; the ⋯'s title reads *Done… · Open*; the Repo page row draws *settles in 7m* with no press. **Lift the hold** on a `decision (paul)` entry is unchanged.
8. **Help and briefs**: delete **Hold…** from §4.5a's help list and from `help.py` together, and the **⋯** help line's *hold or release it* (`tests/test_help.py` binds them word for word); the ⋯'s `title` / `aria-label` strings read *Done… · Open* as §4.5 screen 12 says; `entry.md` says *an interactive session filing an entry the person is still deciding writes `Blocked by: decision (<person>)` with an Ask*, in place of `--held`; `docs/briefs/README.md` likewise.
9. **Tests**: remove or rewrite the hold tests (`test_settle.py`, `test_settle_pages.py`, `test_row_controls.py`, `test_ui_inbox_ledger.py`, `test_person_only.py`, `test_td_next.py`, `test_lane_news.py`, `test_ledger_derived.py`, `test_repo_facts.py`, `test_board.py`, `test_queue*.py`, `test_ui_queue*.py`); add one that a clock record carrying `held` loads as settled and withholds nothing, and one that `ao td hold` is no command. Update the *passes two of them* citations in src and tests to *passes one of them*; remove the *held (person)* mark in `agent_tick.py`.

**Done when:** no hold is recorded on the live home and none is drawn; every hold that was recorded is a `decision (<person>)` gate with an Ask in its ledger (or was released, said in the PR); `ao td hold`, `--held`, Hold… and Release exist nowhere; the clock records only settling; and the tests pass.
