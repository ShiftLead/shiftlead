## TD-2296: Nothing tests that a session's move to the next login leaves its stage record's holder, offers and counts as they were

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d057786c44904351bd4577667693bd7f

**Why:** Found by the Grok fact-check trial on #4794 (TD-2069's archive), confirmed by re-reading at e56164d. TD-2069's Done when says a stage's session moved to the next login keeps "its stage record's holder, offers and counts unchanged". `tests/test_login_move.py` `test_a_session_on_an_unusable_login_moves_to_the_next_under_the_same_record` asserts the same session id and name, and its message says the stage record's holder stands because of that, but no test builds a stage record (`src/shiftlead/stage_job.py`) and reads its holder, offers and counts after a move. A move that spent an offer or rewrote the holder would pass every test today (design 4.9e A session moves down the list: "A move spends no offer, no limit and nothing of the total cap").

**Location:** tests/test_login_move.py; src/shiftlead/stage_job.py

**Fix:** In `tests/test_login_move.py`, add a test that starts a stage's session through the stage pass (so a stage record names it as holder, with its offers and counts), marks its login limited, runs the tick's move, and reads the stage record again: the same holder, the same offers spent, the same counts.

**Done when:** A move that spends an offer, or that leaves the stage record naming another holder, fails a test.
