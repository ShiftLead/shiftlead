**Why:** Build TD-1435, slice 1 of 3 (design §4.9b *A seat stays a seat when it is resumed*). On 2026-11-06 a one-press Resume of the ended techlead seat `ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1` (05:39:57Z) created its record and rewrote its launch record with no `seat`: the UI's `resume_create` (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` ~621–684) sends no `seat`, and `rpc_create` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py` ~1635–1639) carries `supervised` from the resumed holder but not `seat`. Every replay since reads the seatless launch record, so the tick never marks it due, rule 4 never names its questions, and its replies outside its project are refused.

**Fix:** - **The carry** (design §4.9b *A seat stays a seat when it is resumed*). In `rpc_create` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py`), when the call gives no `seat` and `resume` names the conversation of a holder record of the same name (the test `supervised`'s carry uses, `holder.adapter_id == resume`, and the `any(r.adapter_id == resume …)` arm beside it), rebind the local `seat` to a copy of that record's `seat` — where `holder` is known and before the `seat` guard (~1411), `Session(...)` (~1608) and `launch_params(locals())` (~1681), so the record and the launch record `_write_launch` writes both carry it and `by_person` reads it. A `seat` the call gives wins. The UI's `resume_create` is unchanged: it still sends `unattended: False` (§4.5a *Focus (exited / closed)*, never carried), so the resumed seat is the person's and its `seat` inert until Hand back.
- **The mode on the launch record** (§4.5a *Hand back*). `rpc_set_mode` writes `unattended` on the record's launch record as well as on the record, either way, as `rpc_keep` writes `run_only` (the home's alone; a node member's launch record is the home's, `launch/<id>@<host>.json`; a record with no launch record writes none). So after Hand back a fill or a restart replays unattended, not the Resume's `unattended: false`. TD-1439 (TD-1434's mode stamp) edits the same function: rebase onto whichever lands first.
- **The repair** (§4.9c *Switching*, the `relaunch` RPC). `seat` joins `RELAUNCH_KEYS` (`src/sessionorc/agent_common.py`), its kind `dict` in `rpc_relaunch`'s `kinds` (validated as `rpc_create` validates `seat`), not in `RELAUNCH_KEPT`: handed `None` removes it (the pop-then-update removes a key handed `None`). Unlike the other keys, `rpc_relaunch` also writes it on the record at once (`s.seat`, or `None`), since the tick's seat machinery reads the record; and when `seat` is the only key whose handed value differs from the launch record's, it leaves `s.relaunch` as it was (nothing to restart). `relaunch_params` (`src/shiftlead/teamrun.py`) hands `seat` always: the definition's whole dict as `Launch.create_params` writes it (`{**trigger, tags?}`, the sweeper's `by: settings` included), or `None`.
- **The difference.** `differences` (`teamrun.py`) adds a seat line in both branches (with and without `flows:`), for every member it reads that is no person's: live, ended (`DEAD`) or closed. It compares the record's `seat` with the definition's dict, whole; where they differ, `what` gains `seat <was> → <now>` (each the trigger, `none` for no seat) and the member is relaunched. `profile_only` (~953) stays true only when every `what` is a profile line; `ao team flow` and the card's *definition changed — Apply* read the new line as they read the others.
- Tests: a Resume (`create` with `resume` of an ended seat record, no `seat` given) keeps `seat` on the record and the launch record, and a tick replay after Hand back still has it and is unattended; a `seat` given on the call wins; `set_mode` writes `unattended` on the launch record both ways; Apply on a team whose unattended seat record lost `seat` writes it back on both, without a `relaunch` mark; Apply on a member that is no seat but carries one removes it; Apply on an unchanged team writes nothing; Apply passes over a person's (attended) record.

**Done when:** A Resume of an ended seat keeps its `seat` on the record and the launch record; `set_mode` writes the mode on the launch record too; and Apply restores a member's `seat` that differs from the definition, on the record at once.
