**Why:** Build TD-1435, slice 1 of 3 (design §4.9b *A seat stays a seat when it is resumed*). On 2026-11-06 a one-press Resume of the ended techlead seat `ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1` (05:39:57Z) created its record and rewrote its launch record with no `seat`: the UI's `resume_create` (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` ~621–684) sends no `seat`, and `rpc_create` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py` ~1635–1639) carries `supervised` from the resumed holder but not `seat`. Every replay since reads the seatless launch record, so the tick never marks it due, rule 4 never names its questions, and its replies outside its project are refused.

**Fix:** - **The carry.** In `rpc_create`, when the call gives no `seat` and `resume` names the conversation of a holder record of the same name (the test `supervised`'s carry uses, `holder.adapter_id == resume`, and the `any(r.adapter_id == resume …)` arm beside it), the new record takes the holder's `seat`; the launch record `_write_launch` writes then carries it (it is in `LAUNCH_KEYS`). A `seat` the call gives wins. Nothing else changes in the UI's `resume_create`.
- **The repair.** Apply (`src/shiftlead/teamrun.py`, the differences it computes for a member with a live or ended record, ~1185) compares the record's `seat` with the definition's (`teams._trigger` plus `tags: true` for the techlead, as `Launch.create_params` writes it); where they differ it writes the definition's on the record and its launch record (a relaunch difference that carries `seat`: `seat` joins what `rpc_relaunch` may write, `RELAUNCH_KEYS` in `agent_common.py`, replaced as handed). A member that is no seat in the definition and carries one has it removed the same way.
- Tests: a Resume (`create` with `resume` of an ended seat record) keeps `seat` on the record and the launch record, and a tick replay after it still has it; a `seat` given on the call wins; Apply on a team whose seat record lost `seat` writes it back on both, and Apply on an unchanged team writes nothing.

**Done when:** A Resume of an ended seat keeps its `seat` on the record and the launch record, and Apply restores a member's `seat` that differs from the definition. Once live, Paul's Apply on ao-grind gives `ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1` its `seat` back. Tests cover each case above.
