**Fix:** - **The carry** (design §4.9b *A seat stays a seat when it is resumed*). In `rpc_create` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py`), when the call gives no `seat` and `resume` names the conversation of the holder record of the same name (`isinstance(holder, Session) and holder.adapter_id == resume`, the first arm of `supervised`'s carry — never its `any(r.adapter_id == resume …)` arm, which would make a seat of a record under a name the definition does not give), rebind the local `seat` to a copy of that record's `seat` — immediately after `holder` is bound (~1516), before the `_schedule(locals(), …)` call (~1537), `Session(...)` (~1608) and `launch_params(locals())` (~1695); the guard (~1411) is not run again, the record's `seat` having passed it when written — so the record and the launch record `_write_launch` writes both carry it and `by_person` reads it. A `seat` the call gives wins. The UI's `resume_create` is unchanged: it still sends `unattended: False` (§4.5a *Focus (exited / closed)*, never carried), so the resumed seat is the person's: every reader of `seat` (the mail gate, entry tags, held PRs) treats it as the seat, and only rules 3 and 4 and Apply pass over it until Hand back.
- **The mode on the launch record** (§4.5a *Hand back*). `rpc_set_mode` to unattended writes `unattended: true`, and the `pause_prompt` and `resume_prompt` it is handed (both of its callers hand `gate_prompts(True)`: the page's Hand back and `ao mode`), on the record's launch record as well as on the record — also when the record is already unattended, so `ao mode <id> unattended` mends a launch record a Resume left reading `unattended: false` — as `rpc_keep` writes `run_only`, with its guard (`self.mode != "node"`) and its read-modify-write under `suppress(OSError, ValueError)`, so no partial launch record is written (the home's alone; a node member's launch record is the home's, `launch/<id>@<host>.json`); a record with no launch record (not supervised: `_drop_launch` removed it) writes none, and a switch to interactive (Take over) writes the record alone, since nothing replays an attended record. So after Hand back a fill or a restart replays unattended, not the Resume's `unattended: false`. A Resume hands no gate texts (`resume_create` sends none, and `rpc_create` sets them only when given), so the mend is what gives a resumed seat its usage gate back, on the record and on the launch record every later replay reads; the texts are constants, so a seat that had them gets the same. If the launch record is written through `_write_launch` rather than the key-by-key read-modify-write, it also refreshes the launch record's `at` and copies the record's `controllers` over it, which only syncs it with the record. It writes no mode stamp: `rpc_set_mode` stamps only a change.
- **The repair** (§4.9c *Switching*, the `relaunch` RPC). `seat` joins `RELAUNCH_KEYS` (`src/sessionorc/agent_common.py`), its kind `dict` in `rpc_relaunch`'s `kinds` (validated as `rpc_create` validates `seat`), not in `RELAUNCH_KEPT`: handed `None` removes it (the pop-then-update removes a key handed `None`). Unlike the other keys, `rpc_relaunch` also writes it on the record at once (`s.seat`, or `None`) for a member of the home, since the tick's seat machinery reads the record — a write of `None` clears `seat_due` with it, since `_seat_pass` returns before touching the seat fields of a record with no `seat`; a node member's record is the replica's, so its `seat` is the launch record's alone, taken at its next replay. And it compares the launch record before and after the pop-then-update with `seat` left out and `profile` read `or ""` on both sides: where nothing else changed (`name` left out too, so an earlier pending rename is not read as a change) it writes the launch record and `s.seat` alone and skips the rest — the `relaunch` mark, the brief's re-record (`s.brief`, `brief_changed`, `_brief_differs`) and the rename logic — so a pending `brief_changed` or rename stands; a `prompt` composed differently is a real difference and writes the mark as now. `relaunch_params` (`src/shiftlead/teamrun.py`) hands `seat` always: the definition's whole dict as `Launch.create_params` writes it (`{**trigger, tags?}`, the sweeper's `by: settings` included), or `None`.
- **The difference.** `differences` (`teamrun.py`) adds a seat line in both branches (with and without `flows:`), for every member of `plan.launches` with a record that is no person's: after the `persons`, `sit_out` and `sat_out` skips, and before the `DEAD and not seat` skip of the flow-less branch, the flow branch's `closed` arm (which today compares a profile for a seat alone) and its live compare, so a live, ended or closed record is compared — the `DEAD and not seat` skip stays for the profile compare, only the seat compare runs on a dead record — and one that is no seat in the definition but carries a stray `seat` reads *seat asks → none*. It compares the record's `seat` with the definition's dict, whole; where they differ, `what` gains `seat <was> → <now>` (each the trigger, `none` for no seat) and the member is relaunched. `Difference.definition` (~948) is true when every `what` is a profile line or a seat line (the seat is `org.yml`'s, no flow's), so the header reads *definition changed — Apply*; `Difference.line` ends a definition difference *(written now)* when every line is a seat's and the member is the home's (`rec["host"]` the home or absent), and *(next restart)* when a profile's is among them or the member is a node's, never *relaunched —*.
- Tests: a Resume (`create` with `resume` of an ended seat record, no `seat` given) keeps `seat` on the record and the launch record, and a tick replay after Hand back still has it and is unattended; a `seat` given on the call wins; a scheduled Resume (`start_at`) writes its launch record with the `seat`; a seat-only Apply leaves a pending `brief_changed` and rename standing; `set_mode` to unattended writes it and the gate's two texts on the launch record, and on a record already unattended whose launch record reads `unattended: false` with no texts too, after which a replayed seat's gated pause types the pause text; Apply on a team whose unattended seat record lost `seat` writes it back on both, without a `relaunch` mark; Apply on a member that is no seat but carries one removes it and its `seat_due`; Apply on an unchanged team writes nothing; Apply passes over a person's (attended) record; a closed record that is no seat in the definition but carries one has it removed; `set_mode` to interactive leaves the launch record as it was, and one on a record with no launch record writes none; a seat difference on a record `rpc_relaunch` refuses (`closed_for.why == "run_ended"`) is skipped and said, as a profile difference is; a node member's seat-only line reads *(next restart)*; a seat-only difference reads *definition changed* and *(written now)*; a Resume of a record under another name holding the seat's conversation carries no `seat`.
