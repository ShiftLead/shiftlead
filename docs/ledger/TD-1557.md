**Why:** Split from TD-1555 (techlead's answer to grinder-ao-2's steer, m-742329aa2fc1, 2026-11-07). On 2026-11-07 Paul typed a `Hold: <note>` answer into **Answer…** on a board `decide` line. A board line's Answer… has nowhere to write the person's own words as the line's `Decided:`, because two sections disagree:
- §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Answer* and §4.5a *Queue card: answers, Answer…* say every answer, Answer… among them, takes *a board line's decided line*.
- §4.4 *Decide* says a decide records one of the item's own answers word for word, and that a typed answer of any other shape is a Reply.
- §4.5 screen 6's board row says so too: *A board line's Reply — the row's Reply, or a Queue card's **Answer…** on a board item — … decides nothing*, so the item keeps its answers and stays a Decide item.

The code follows §4.4. `rpc_board_edit`'s `_board_answer` (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`) accepts only an offered answer, or a filled `Not right: <what>` form through vendored ledgerboard's `fill_not_right` (`src/sessionorc/ledgerboard/__init__.py`), and refuses anything else as a Reply. The board item's Answer… (`board_answer`, `data-act="board_reply"`) therefore takes the Reply road, which leaves the line due. No code fills a slot answer such as an end-of-rounds item's `Hold: <note>`; ledgerboard knows only the `Not right:` form. Paul's Hold was carried to the board by hand (TD-1554).

**Handed upstream:** Two hand-offs, sent at the rounds' end with `ao td add --repo <repo>`. Their ids are recorded here and in TD-1557's Status.
- **ledgerboard: the general form** (a release, v2.8 or later). It carries:
  - `is_form(label)`: the label is taken after the consequence split. It is a form when it is `<head>:` alone, or `<head>:` followed by one placeholder `<[^<>]*>`, with whitespace collapsed. The head is the non-empty text before the label's first colon.
  - `form_head(label)`: the head, or None for a plain answer. The agent reads heads only through it.
  - `fill_form(said, offered, form=None)`. `offered` is the item's labels, taken after the consequence split with the ` (default)` mark off. A `said` that is not a string, or a `form` index out of range or naming a plain answer, raises `Refused`.
    - It returns None when no offered form's head (any case, spaces before the colon ignored) begins the words. With `form` (an index), it first puts that form's head back where the words do not begin with it, then fills that form and no other.
    - It raises `Refused("<head> needs its words")` on an empty fill: the head alone, or the head and a placeholder.
    - Otherwise it returns `<head>: <words>`, with the head as offered, one space, and the words collapsed to one line.
    - The first form whose head matches wins on a standing line.
  - `_lifting` reads a filled form as its form, so `decide_lift`, `closes()`, `edit_line`'s close, the gate sweep and the tripwires agree.
  - The item check: `item_problem()` (so `board_problems()`) and the writer `item_line()` refuse two forms whose heads match in any case, and a form marked `(default)`. The rule sits in `item_problem`'s open-`decide` branch and leaves decided lines alone. `decided_line()` does not refuse a ledger entry's answers it copies (`board.add_decided`): it writes a form there with its ` (default)` mark stripped, since the readers already hand no default for a marked form.
  - `is_not_right_form` and `fill_not_right` become the look's case: the head is matched in any case, and the refusal reads *Not right needs its words*. Update `fixtures/edit/expected.json`'s `not_right_fills` and `not_right_forms`, and their manifest. `"not right: <what>": false` becomes true under the any-case head.
  - New fixtures for `is_form`, `form_head` and `fill_form`: the head put back with `form`, two forms on one item, an any-case head and spaces before the colon, an empty fill, a multi-line fill collapsed, a bad `form` index, and a standing line with two same-head forms (the first wins).
  - SPEC *The board*'s `Answers:` bullet defines the general form, and the grammar appendix (`look-answers` and the answer rule) follows it.
  - The version call: `closes()` now reads a filled form as its form on lines that already exist, a reading change the release notes must state. Whether that is v2.8 or a major is ledgerboard's to decide.
- **agent-cadence: the session's decide** (`scripts/board_edit.py decide`). It fills through `fill_form` as shiftlead's `_board_answer` does, and its `reply` follows shiftlead's rule too: words beginning with a form's head are that form's press. The same pin bump vendors the release into shiftlead (`scripts/vendor_ledgerboard.sh`), which is what TD-1698's event waits on.

**Design PR:** #3511

**Fix:** Settle in the design which road a board line's **Answer…** takes, and how a slot answer is filled:
- **Answer… on a `decide` line:** either it writes the person's words as `Decided:` (§4.4 *Decide* widened), or it stays a Reply (§4.5 screen 12 and §4.5a narrowed, and the template comment in `answer_row` corrected).
- **A slot answer** (`<label>: <slot>`, like `Hold: <note>`): say whether it is filled as `Not right: <what>` is, from the person's words. If so, ledgerboard's form check (`is_not_right_form`, `fill_not_right`) generalises, as a change in ledgerboard upstream vendored here, never edited in place.
- Name the builds: shiftlead's decide road and page, and ledgerboard's upstream part.
