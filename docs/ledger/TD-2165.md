## TD-2165: After the switch-over the seat for pull requests nobody holds also takes sync, lift and sweep pull requests

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 815bc56f52d0403fb0171dbb9a0a9bb7
**Blocked by:** TD-2053
**Part of:** TD-2164

**Why:** After the cut-over no lane exists, so the sync, lift and sweep pull requests that today are the free-pick lanes' work orders (design §4.4 *A sync PR is the team's at once*, *A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once*) would be taken by nothing: TD-2053's trigger, `stage_job.pr_objects`, leaves them out, as §4.9c read before TD-2164. TD-2164's design makes each a job of the seat for pull requests nobody holds.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/stage_job.py, src/shiftlead/stage_end.py, the job brief

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *Two of the five are not seats today* (the bullet *Pull requests nobody holds* and *The job keeps §4.4's judgment and leaves the merge to the flow*), §4.9d *How each one runs* (*Offer*'s order and *The session ends its job*), the §4.9c outcome table's `pr-closed` row and §4.7 `ao stage end`, as TD-2164 wrote them:

1. **The trigger.** `stage_job.pr_objects` takes the sync, lift and sweep orders (`sync`, `lift`, `sweep` on `teamrun.pr_orders`' rows) beside the red and filing ones, each keyed `pr-<n>` and mapped to its own stamp (`sync_since`, `lift_since`, `sweep_since`), with the same exclusions (a stage record or a board answer's open job naming it, a pickable entry's work). Its docstring's last sentence goes.
2. **The order.** Of the seat for pull requests nobody holds, an object whose order is a sync's is offered before every other object with no record (records already begun still come first), the rest oldest first by their stamp; the board-answers seat's order is unchanged. The trigger's result carries which keys are syncs, or the order function is given them.
3. **The brief.** The job session's brief (TD-2053's job brief) is the order's words by its kind: a red or filing one's as today; a sync's, what §4.4 has a sync PR's owner do; a lift's or a sweep's, what §4.4 *What the taker does* has the taker do by the head — but for the claim, the reader's ask and the merge: it never merges and never asks a reader, ends `work-pushed` where §4.4 has it merge, `pr-closed` where §4.4 has it close the PR unmerged with a comment, and `other` with what it read for a lift check's *error* or its own setup's failure.
4. **`pr-closed`.** `ao stage end pr-closed --reason "…"` is accepted from a pull request's job alone (refused from a board answer's job, a stage's or an audit's session): the home checks a reason is given and reads the forge that the pull request the record names is closed and not merged, then closes the job's record as Reconcile closes one whose object is gone, and ends the session. `stage_end`'s outcome list and `ao stage end --help` name it.

Tests: each of the three new kinds asks for a job and keys on its own stamp; a sync's job is offered before an older red one's; a lift PR whose entry is pickable is still the job's (its files are the ledger's alone, so never the entry's work); `pr-closed` is refused on an open or merged PR, with no reason, and from a board answer's job.

**Done when:** After the cut-over a sync, a lift and a sweep pull request no session holds each start a job of the seat for pull requests nobody holds, a sync's first; its session is told the order's words by its kind and ends `work-pushed` or `pr-closed` (checked against the forge) or `other`; and the tests above pass.
