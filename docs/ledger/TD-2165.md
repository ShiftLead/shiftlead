## TD-2165: After the switch-over the seat for pull requests nobody holds also takes sync, lift and sweep pull requests

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 815bc56f52d0403fb0171dbb9a0a9bb7
**Blocked by:** TD-2053
**Part of:** TD-2164

**Why:** After the cut-over no lane exists, so the sync, lift and sweep pull requests that today are the free-pick lanes' work orders (design §4.4 *A sync PR is the team's at once*, *A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once*) would be taken by nothing: TD-2053's trigger, `stage_job.pr_objects`, leaves them out, as §4.9c read before TD-2164. TD-2164's design makes each a job of the seat for pull requests nobody holds.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/stage_job.py, src/sessionorc/workorders.py, src/sessionorc/agent_sync.py, a new shiftlead.home_calls module and its pyproject.toml line, src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py, the job-end path and job brief (TD-2053)

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *Two of the five are not seats today* (the bullet *Pull requests nobody holds* and *The job keeps §4.4's judgment and leaves the merge to the flow* with its three kinds and three costs), §4.9d *How each one runs* (*Offer*'s order and *The session ends its job*), the §4.9c outcome table's `closed-unmerged` row, §4.7 `ao stage end`, §6 *Sync*'s first test and §4.5a *Inbox row: red PR*, as TD-2164 wrote them:

1. **The trigger.** `stage_job.pr_objects` takes the sync, lift and sweep orders (`sync`, `lift`, `sweep` on `teamrun.pr_orders`' rows) beside the red and filing ones, each keyed `pr-<n>` and mapped to its own stamp (`sync_since`, `lift_since`, `sweep_since`), with the same exclusions (a stage record or a board answer's open job naming it, a pickable entry's work). **A fork's sync order is left out.** `workorders.pr_order` copies the PR reading's `fork` onto a sync's order row (`fork: true|false`; today the row carries no fork, and `is_sync_order` orders a fork's), and a sync row with no `fork` key is read as a fork, so a stranger's PR never reaches a session. Another login's sync and a sync draft are the seat's. **The job record keeps `kind`** (`red`, `filing`, `sync`, `lift`, `sweep`), and for a sync `reason` (the order's `reason` at the first offer), written by `new_job` at the first offer and again when a closed record's object comes back (§4.4 *The state branch*'s `jobs/<key>.json`); `pr_objects` returns, per key, its stamp and its kind (`dict[str, tuple[str, str]]`, or a small named tuple), and the caller passes `new_job` the kind and `order`'s `first` the sync keys from it; the brief reads the kind from the record, never from the reading again, since the session's push changes the row (a new head reads *pending*). The lift and sweep orders are already never a fork's or a draft's. The docstring says so, and its last sentence goes.
2. **A fork's sync goes to the person.** After the cut-over (read by `teamstore.first_shape_off()`, which falls back to `host.json`; TD-2076, which wired the marker through, is archived), `ui/inbox.py`'s `red_pr_rows` draws a fork's sync order as the *Inbox row: red PR* in a served, serviced repo too (an unserved repo's sync is never an order and stays as it is) — one predicate with item 1's, so a sync row with no `fork` key draws the row too — (today it skips every `teamrun.serviced` repo), its line the sync's (*`<repo>` #<n> · sync · <reason>*), leaving as a sync PR's row does; a non-fork sync in a repo nothing services keeps today's row, unchanged.
3. **The order.** `stage_job.order` takes a new argument, `first: Collection[str]`, the keys offered before every other object with no record (records already begun still come first); the seat for pull requests nobody holds passes its sync keys, the board-answers seat none; the rest stay oldest first by their stamp. A sync waits, as any object, while the seat's one job session is live.
4. **The Sync policy leaves a job's PR alone.** After the cut-over the home's Sync policy (§6 *Sync*, `agent_sync.py`) skips, once the host agent's `_first_shape_off` reads the cut-over, a sync PR an open job record names (`pr-<n>` on `shiftlead/state`, not closed, and not waiting on the person), as `_sync_held_by` skips one a live record holds, so the flow's merge and the policy's never both act on one. The numbers come from a new module on the `shiftlead.home_calls` entry point (a new line in `pyproject.toml`, as `agent_stage` reaches `shiftlead.stage_end`), since `sessionorc` never imports `shiftlead`: read once per repo per pass, in a thread, before `sync_mod.candidates`, and kept in a local that `_sync_merge_in`'s two re-checks read too; a call that fails is no reading, and the pass judges none of that repo's sync PRs that turn.
5. **The brief.** The job session's brief (TD-2053's job brief) is the order's words by its kind: a red or filing one's as today; a sync's, what §4.4 has a sync PR's owner do; a lift's or a sweep's, what §4.4 *What the taker does* has the taker do by the head — but for the claim, the reader's ask and the merge: it ends `work-pushed` where §4.4 has it merge (a person's deliberate early dateless lift among them) and `closed-unmerged` where §4.4 has it close the PR unmerged with a comment (the sweep's legacy dateless lift before its day, its body opening with the sweep's fixed sentence, among them); `other` with what it read for a lift check's *error* or its own setup's failure. And the three kinds §4.9c names: a live check's lift reading *not yet* is turned on its own branch into the fresh gate — its diff against the default branch exactly §4.9b's fresh-gate PR's (the fresh gate's section under §4.9b's naming rule, the lifted gate replaced in `**Blocked by:**`, the lift's section kept, no *gate lifted* Log line), retitled *TD-n: a fresh gate <new> in place of <old>*, its head kept — ending `work-pushed`, and a session offered it again that finds the diff already a re-gate carries it to `work-pushed` without re-running the check, resolving a conflict with main by writing the same fresh gate again on main's copy, never `ledger.py lift`; a pin bump held on *no review of the compare* is reviewed by the session, which posts its `cadence-review:` comment naming the head (the independent review too where the flow has no always-run review stage), then ends `work-pushed`; a sync draft is marked ready before `work-pushed`.
6. **`closed-unmerged`.** A job outcome alone, open to a pull request's job of any kind (a red or filing job whose cure is to close its PR ends so too, its brief saying when), in the job-end path TD-2053 builds, **never in `flow3.OUTCOMES`**, so no flow file can name or route it; its name is apart from Reconcile's own case of a board answer's PR closed (`stage_job`'s `own_pr == "closed"`), which goes to the person. `ao stage end closed-unmerged --reason "…"` is accepted from a pull request's job: the home checks a reason is given and reads the forge that the pull request the record names is closed and not merged, then closes the job's record as Reconcile closes one whose object is gone, and ends the session. From a board answer's job, a stage's or an audit's session it is refused, *closed-unmerged ends a pull request's job alone*; on a PR still open or merged, refused naming the PR's state; from the session the record last named after Reconcile closed the record, answered *the job is closed*, exit 0. `ao stage end --help` names it.

Tests: each of the three new kinds asks for a job, keys on its own stamp and writes its `kind` on the record; a fork's sync (and a sync row with no `fork`) asks for none and draws the Inbox row in a serviced repo after the cut-over, and another login's and a draft sync ask for one; a sync's job is offered before an older red one's, and waits while a job session is live; the Sync policy skips a sync PR an open job names after the cut-over (not one whose job waits on the person), and judges none of a repo's when the call fails; a lift PR whose entry is pickable is still the job's (its files are the ledger's alone, so never the entry's work); `closed-unmerged` is refused on an open or merged PR, with no reason, from a board answer's job and from a stage's session, and a flow file naming it is refused; a `closed-unmerged` after Reconcile closed the record exits 0.

**Done when:** After the cut-over a sync (but a fork's), a lift and a sweep pull request no session holds each start a job of the seat for pull requests nobody holds, a sync's first; a fork's sync draws the person's Inbox row; the Sync policy leaves a job's PR alone; the job session is told the order's words by its kind and ends `work-pushed`, `closed-unmerged` (checked against the forge) or `other`; and the tests above pass.
