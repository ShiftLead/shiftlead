## TD-2165: After the switch-over the seat for pull requests nobody holds also takes sync, lift and sweep pull requests

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 815bc56f52d0403fb0171dbb9a0a9bb7
**Blocked by:** TD-2053
**Part of:** TD-2164

**Why:** After the cut-over no lane exists, so the sync, lift and sweep pull requests that today are the free-pick lanes' work orders (design §4.4 *A sync PR is the team's at once*, *A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once*) would be taken by nothing: TD-2053's trigger, `stage_job.pr_objects`, leaves them out, as §4.9c read before TD-2164. TD-2164's design makes each a job of the seat for pull requests nobody holds.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/stage_job.py, the job-end path (TD-2053), the job brief, ui/inbox.py

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *Two of the five are not seats today* (the bullet *Pull requests nobody holds* and *The job keeps §4.4's judgment and leaves the merge to the flow*), §4.9d *How each one runs* (*Offer*'s order and *The session ends its job*), the §4.9c outcome table's `pr-closed` row and §4.7 `ao stage end`, as TD-2164 wrote them:

1. **The trigger.** `stage_job.pr_objects` takes the sync, lift and sweep orders (`sync`, `lift`, `sweep` on `teamrun.pr_orders`' rows) beside the red and filing ones, each keyed `pr-<n>` and mapped to its own stamp (`sync_since`, `lift_since`, `sweep_since`), with the same exclusions (a stage record or a board answer's open job naming it, a pickable entry's work). **A sync order that is a fork's, another login's than the home's, or a draft is left out** (`workorders.is_sync_order` orders those, and `pr_objects` has no fork or draft test of its own today, so the test is new here, read from the order's row); the lift and sweep orders are already never a fork's or a draft's. Its docstring says so, and its last sentence goes.
2. **Such a sync goes to the person.** After the cut-over, a sync order the trigger leaves out for a fork, a login or a draft is drawn as the *Inbox row: red PR* (§4.5a) that a sync PR of a repo no team services has today (`ui/inbox.py`), its reason the order's `Context`, in a served repo too.
3. **The order.** `stage_job.order` takes a new argument, `first: Collection[str]`, the keys offered before every other object with no record (records already begun still come first); the seat for pull requests nobody holds passes its sync keys, the board-answers seat none; the rest stay oldest first by their stamp. A sync waits, as any object, while the seat's one job session is live.
4. **The brief.** The job session's brief (TD-2053's job brief) is the order's words by its kind: a red or filing one's as today; a sync's, what §4.4 has a sync PR's owner do; a lift's or a sweep's, what §4.4 *What the taker does* has the taker do by the head — but for the claim, the reader's ask and the merge: it never merges and never asks a reader, ends `work-pushed` where §4.4 has it merge (a person's deliberate early dateless lift among them), `pr-closed` where §4.4 has it close the PR unmerged with a comment (the sweep's legacy dateless lift before its day, its body opening with the sweep's fixed sentence, among them), and `other` with what it read for a lift check's *error* or its own setup's failure.
5. **`pr-closed`.** It is a job outcome alone, in the job-end path TD-2053 builds, and **never in `flow3.OUTCOMES`**, so no flow file can name or route it. `ao stage end pr-closed --reason "…"` is accepted from a pull request's job: the home checks a reason is given and reads the forge that the pull request the record names is closed and not merged, then closes the job's record as Reconcile closes one whose object is gone, and ends the session. From a board answer's job, a stage's or an audit's session it is refused, *pr-closed ends a pull request's job alone*; on a PR still open or merged, refused naming the PR's state; from the session the record last named after Reconcile closed the record, answered *the job is closed*, exit 0. `ao stage end --help` names it.

Tests: each of the three new kinds asks for a job and keys on its own stamp; a forked, other-login or draft sync asks for none and draws the Inbox row; a sync's job is offered before an older red one's, and waits while a job session is live; a lift PR whose entry is pickable is still the job's (its files are the ledger's alone, so never the entry's work); `pr-closed` is refused on an open or merged PR, with no reason, from a board answer's job and from a stage's session, and a flow file naming it is refused; a `pr-closed` after Reconcile closed the record exits 0.

**Done when:** After the cut-over a sync, a lift and a sweep pull request no session holds each start a job of the seat for pull requests nobody holds, a sync's first; its session is told the order's words by its kind and ends `work-pushed` or `pr-closed` (checked against the forge) or `other`; and the tests above pass.
