## TD-1644: The identity alarm log line prints a caller's method unbounded, beside the judged-unknown line that bounds it

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ce7fa6c0012942fb86c7e5bc57368147
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_identity.py _id_alarm; tests/test_identity.py

**Why:** `_id_alarm` in src/sessionorc/agent_identity.py logs `identity alarm (%s): %s claimed %r on %s` with the request's method unbounded, while #3332's *judged unknown* line beside it bounds the same method to 60 characters because "the method is the caller's word". A caller that names a 200-character method gets all 200 into the journal on the alarm line (seen writing TD-1606's test, which had to read the judged-unknown record alone). The alarm entry's `rpc` is also stored on the record's alarms.

Found by: grinder-ao-2, writing TD-1606's test.

**Fix:** Bound the method on the alarm log line as the judged-unknown line does (`[:60]`), and decide whether the stored alarm entry's `rpc` is bounded too (design §4.8a); add a test with a long method that fails with the bound reverted.

**Done when:** A long method logs at most 60 of its characters on the alarm line, and a test fails with the bound reverted.
