**Fix:** Build design §4.6 *Told before the pool is full*: each pool's *low* state in `disk.json` (`seats` and `shared`; a host with no `seat_root` has only `shared`, reclaimed; with one on another file system, `shared` is `scratch_root`, read and told but never reclaimed from; with one on the same file system, only `seats`), its three largest holders from one walk bounded by `PRESSURE_WALK_S`, `pressure`, a map by pool name, in the host's `disk` reading and printed by `ao disk` (§4.7); the home's detached read, one `disk` call per host at once every `PRESSURE_EVERY`, and its `disk_low` spells per host and pool in the host record, kept across a load by `HostStore.load` in `store.py` and put in the host view in `agent.py`, as `ci_spells` is; the row `disk_low:<host>:<pool>` (§4.5a), drawn through `ui/app.py` as the CI row is; Telegram and push (§4.10), with a line in `notify.py`. Add the row's help to §4.5a's list and `help.py` together, word for word (`tests/test_help.py`): **Snooze ▾** (Inbox row: temporary space low) — Sets this row aside until the time you pick: +1 day, +1 week or a date. It frees no space and changes nothing on the host, and the row is not told on Telegram or push while it is snoozed.

**Done when:** A pool held under 15%, after a reclaim where the pool is reclaimed and on the reading alone for a `shared` beside `seats`, on the home or on a node, raises one row and one message naming its three largest holders. Moving between 15% and 35% neither ends nor repeats it, nor does a host-agent restart; over 35% ends it, a later fall raises a new one, and a node that stops answering neither raises nor clears it.
