## TD-1433: After a Go on a decide item, the Queue offers Lift the hold until the checkout's pull catches the merged lift

**Why:** Asked by the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-3c66aa9b9572). After the person answers **Go** on a decide item, the Queue keeps offering **Lift the hold** on the entries it lifted for several minutes, though the lift has merged. Seen twice on 2026-11-05 (local): Go on TD-1411, TD-1413 and TD-1414 merged at 03:06Z on 2026-11-06 (#2999–#3001, each entry with its `gate lifted` Log line), yet each card read *needs you* with **Lift the hold** until 03:11Z, when the pull fast-forwarded the main checkout (behind `origin/main` by exactly those three commits); then TD-1423, TD-1430 and TD-1431 the same (#3004–#3006, the checkout behind by 3). The person is shown a hold they just lifted and invited to lift it again: a second decided line, or a confusing no-op.

The cause, read from the code: after a press, `_reread_board` (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`, TD-615) re-reads the repo's **board** at once from the write-back's own tree, so the decide line leaves at once. The decide lift also edits the **ledger** (ledgerboard `decide_lift`: the gate out of `Blocked by:`, the `gate lifted` Log line), but the reading the Queue takes the entry's gate from is the checkout's ledger, which changes only when the pull (`promote.pull`, design §6 *Pull*) fast-forwards it, and the pull waits while any session in the checkout is mid-turn. So the board half is fresh and the ledger half lags by up to the pull's next pass.

**Fix:** Read the ledger the same way the board is read after a press: when the host agent's write-back changed ledger entries (a decide lift, or any board-edit road that edits an entry), `_reread_board`, or a ledger twin of it, re-reads those entries from the write-back's tree (origin's head, the press landed) and puts them in the next `repos` push, so the Queue's reading of the entry's gate follows the merge the home itself made, not the checkout. The press mark that keeps a tick's older board read from putting the old board back covers the ledger read too. Do not fast-forward the checkout as part of the merge: the pull's mid-turn rule (design §6 *Pull*) stands. Say it in design §4.4 *Each reading has one owner* (the ledger half of TD-615's re-read) before the build. If the reading cannot take the entry from the tree, the fallback is the card's word: an entry whose answer's PR is merged but not yet read reads *answered — lifting* with no **Lift the hold**.

**Done when:** A test: a Go on a decide item whose lift PR has merged, with the checkout's ledger still holding the gate, gives the entry's card no *needs you* and no **Lift the hold** in the reading pushed right after the press. Live: the next Go on the Queue drops the card's **Lift the hold** within seconds of the merge, recorded here before archiving.
