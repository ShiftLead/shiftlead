**Why:** Build TD-1436 (design §4.5 rule 4 *A Queue card's own state follows the press*). Paul's ask (m-417b7f8b20c7, 2026-11-03): an answer on a Queue card took about 10 s to show. Rule 4 already draws the item decided or takes it out in the press's frame, but the card's pill (*Decide n · Do n*, `pill()` in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html`), its `data-status` and the Queue's summary (`AO.qDrawSummary`, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`) followed the re-read, after the board's PR round trip (3–4 s to the PR, 2–5 s to the merge, presses 11–13 s apart).

**Fix:** - **Which presses.** A rule 4 press (one that writes origin) that takes an item out — `pressed()` drawing a board decide or a row leaving with `r4`, and `qpressed()` exactly where it removes the card's `ledger:<repo>:<ref>` `.mailrow` (the ⋯ road's Done… and Back to design…) — when the row sits in `row.closest(".qsec[data-sec]")` reading `decide` or `do` inside a `.qcard`. Take the block and the `.qcard` **before** the row is removed. A board line's Reply (`board_reply`, in `drawn`), a Suspend, an `ask`'s answer and a home-answered Go with it take nothing off. Read, *With you* and *Answered* blocks take nothing off. On the Queue and on the Repo page alike (both draw `queue_card.html`).
- **At the press.** Take one off that block's number in the card's `.qwpill` (a part at nought left out with its ` · `, as `pill()` draws it: *Decide 1 · Do 1* → *Do 1*) and in the block's heading (`Decide (n)` / `Do (n)`, the `want` macro's `qsech`; the last one's reads `Decide (0)`, the block kept as drawn). A pill with neither left is removed, and — unless the card carries `data-lneed` — the card drawn plain: drop `st-needs-you` and `wants`, replace `edge-needs` with the group's edge as `card_edge` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` ~536) orders it (`edge-waiting` in the Waiting group, `edge-working` In progress, else `edge-ended`), and clear `data-status`; draw no status word.
- **The server's mark.** `queue_card.html` draws `data-lneed` on a card whose status is *needs you* for its ledger status apart from its Decide and Do items (`queue.py`'s `_mail_status` / the card's status reading: a *needs you* that holds with no non-running item). A card with it keeps its look and status at a press; only its pill and headings change.
- **The recount.** `AO.qDrawSummary` needs the Queue closure's locals (`AO.queue`, ~app.js 4831): the Queue exposes a recount of its own (`AO.qRecount`) that redraws the summary bar, the legend and the switch's number only, never `applyPicks`' `qoff` pass; the next `apply()` (a pick's press or the re-read) hides the card as the picks say. Where it is absent (the Repo page) nothing is recounted.
- **The keep.** `AO.inflight["qcard:<data-card>"]`: per block (`decide`, `do`) the keys of the items taken off (`data-msg`; `ledger:<repo>:<ref>` for the ⋯ road), each press adding its own, and, taken at the first press, the card as drawn (the pill's text, `data-status`, the edge class and the `st-`/`wants` classes). The click handler's `finally` never deletes it. A re-read (the Queue's, `lists.innerHTML` replaced ~5047, and the Repo page's, ~5162) applies it **before** `AO.restoreRowErrs` (which removes a fresh left row itself, ~3736–3746), finding the card by `data-card`: a kept key whose `data-msg` row is still in the fresh card's Decide or Do block is taken off again (count, heading, pill, look as at the press); a key drawn nowhere in those blocks — gone, or a decided board line now under Read — leaves the keep. `answered()` and `qanswered()` mark the press's key answered; once every key is answered the keep gets `until` (a minute on), and the re-read's pass deletes it when it is past `until` or holds no key.
- **On a refusal.** Take that press's key out of the keep at once, without touching the others: restore its block's number in the pill and the heading; once no key is left, restore the pill's text, the classes, the edge and `data-status` from the keep's record and delete the keep. Find the card by `data-card`. Recount.
- Tests (the page's script tests, as TD-1316's): a Decide press on *Decide 2* reads *Decide 1* in the pill and heading in the press's frame; *Decide 1 · Do 1* reads *Do 1* after its Decide; the last Decide on a card with no Do drops the pill, the needs-you classes and edge (to the group's) and the summary's count, and the card stays shown with the switch on until the next apply; a card with `data-lneed` keeps its look; a ⋯ Done… that removes the card's ledger item takes one off; a board Reply, a Read item's press and an ask's answer take nothing off; a re-read while out keeps the change; a re-read drawing a decided board line under Read drops it and leaves the fresh count; two presses on one card, the card drawn plain, refused one after the other: the first refusal restores one count and the card stays plain, the second restores the look; the Repo page's card follows a press with no recount.

**Done when:** A press of an answer on a Queue card changes that card within the press's frame: its pill's number drops, a pill left empty and the card's *needs you* go, and the summary recounts; a re-read while the press is out keeps it, and a refusal puts it back. Tests cover each case above. The live check: a press on the Queue reads so at once, recorded on TD-1436 before archiving.
