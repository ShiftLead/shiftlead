**Why:** Build TD-1436 (design §4.5 rule 4 *A Queue card's own state follows the press*). Paul's ask (m-417b7f8b20c7, 2026-11-03): an answer on a Queue card took about 10 s to show. Rule 4 already draws the item decided or takes it out in the press's frame, but the card's pill (*Decide n · Do n*, `pill()` in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html`), its `data-status` and the Queue's summary (`AO.qDrawSummary`, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`) followed the re-read, after the board's PR round trip (3–4 s to the PR, 2–5 s to the merge, presses 11–13 s apart).

**Fix:** - **At the press.** In the click handler's `pressed()` (an item row's rule 4 press, drawn decided or left) and in `qpressed()` exactly where it removes the card's `ledger:<repo>:<ref>` `.mailrow` (the ⋯ road's Done… and Back to design…), when the row sits in `row.closest(".qsec[data-sec]")` reading `decide` or `do` inside a `.qcard`: take one off that block's number in the card's `.qwpill` and in the block's heading (`Decide (n)` / `Do (n)`, the `want` macro's `qsech`). A pill with neither left is removed, and the card drawn plain: drop the `st-needs-you` and `wants` classes and the `edge-needs` edge class, and clear `data-status`; draw no other status (the server's reading comes with the re-read). Read, *With you* and *Answered* blocks take nothing off.
- **The recount.** `AO.qDrawSummary` needs the Queue closure's locals (`AO.queue`, ~app.js 4831): the Queue exposes a recount of its own (e.g. `AO.qRecount`) that redraws the summary bar, the legend and the switch's number only — never `applyPicks`' `qoff` pass, so a card the press drew plain is not hidden by the *needs you* switch until the re-read.
- **Across re-reads.** Keep the change in `AO.inflight` under its own key, `qcard:<the card's data-card>`, with the blocks and counts it took off; the click handler's `finally` never deletes it. The Queue's re-read (`lists.innerHTML` replaced, ~5047) reapplies it after `AO.restoreRowErrs(lists, errs)` (beside its `qrow` pass, ~3736) and before `apply()`, while the press is out, and after the answer while the re-read still draws the item, for at most a minute; a re-read that draws the card without the item drops it.
- **On a refusal** (rule 4's rollback), put the pill, the heading's number, the classes, `data-status` and the counts back with the item, and recount.
- Tests (the page's script tests, as TD-1316's): a Decide press on a card with *Decide 2* reads *Decide 1* in the pill and the heading in the press's frame; the last Decide on a card with no Do drops the pill, the needs-you classes and the summary's count, and the card stays shown with the switch on; a ⋯ Done… that removes the card's ledger item takes one off; a Read item's press takes nothing off; a re-read while out keeps the change; a refusal puts it back.

**Done when:** A press of an answer on a Queue card changes that card within the press's frame: its pill's number drops, a pill left empty and the card's *needs you* go, and the summary recounts; a re-read while the press is out keeps it, and a refusal puts it back. Tests cover each case above. The live check: a press on the Queue reads so at once, recorded on TD-1436 before archiving.
