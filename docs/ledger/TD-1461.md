**Why:** Build TD-1436 (design §4.5 rule 4 *A Queue card's own state follows the press*). Paul's ask (m-417b7f8b20c7, 2026-11-03): an answer on a Queue card took about 10 s to show. Rule 4 already draws the item decided or takes it out in the press's frame, but the card's pill (*Decide n · Do n*, `pill()` in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html`), its `data-status` and the Queue's summary (`AO.qDrawSummary`, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`) followed the re-read, after the board's PR round trip (3–4 s to the PR, 2–5 s to the merge, presses 11–13 s apart).

**Fix:** - **At the press.** In the click handler's `pressed()` (a row's rule 4 press, drawn decided or left) and `qpressed()` paths, when the row is an item of a Queue card's Decide or Do block (`.qcard` ancestor, the block from the item's own markup), take one off that block's number in the card's `.qwpill`; a pill with neither left is removed and the card's `data-status` cleared (it read *needs you*). Then recount: `AO.qDrawSummary` as `apply()` calls it (TD-1311). The card is not moved: its group and place are the re-read's.
- **Across re-reads.** Keep the change in `AO.inflight` under the card's key (`AO.qKey` of the card), with the items it took off, beside the item's entry; a re-read that draws the card while the press is out, or within the minute after the answer while it still draws the item, applies it again, and one that draws the card without the item drops it (the server's reading wins).
- **On a refusal** (rule 4's rollback), put the pill, `data-status` and the counts back with the item, and recount.
- Done… and Back to design… from a card entry row's ⋯ take nothing off (the entry stays open until its session acts).
- Tests (the page's script tests, as TD-1316's): a Decide press on a card with *Decide 2* reads *Decide 1* in the press's frame; the last Decide on a card with no Do drops the pill and the card's *needs you* and the summary's count; a re-read while out keeps it; a refusal puts it back; a Done… press changes no pill.

**Done when:** A press of an answer on a Queue card changes that card within the press's frame: its pill's number drops, a pill left empty and the card's *needs you* go, and the summary recounts; a re-read while the press is out keeps it, and a refusal puts it back. Tests cover each case above. The live check: a press on the Queue reads so at once, recorded on TD-1436 before archiving.
