**Why:** Build TD-1436 (design §4.5 rule 4 *A Queue card's own state follows the press*). Paul's ask (m-417b7f8b20c7, 2026-11-03): an answer on a Queue card took about 10 s to show. Rule 4 already draws the item decided or takes it out in the press's frame, but the card's pill (*Decide n · Do n*, `pill()` in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html`), its `data-status` and the Queue's summary (`AO.qDrawSummary`, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`) followed the re-read, after the board's PR round trip (3–4 s to the PR, 2–5 s to the merge, presses 11–13 s apart).

**Fix:** - **Which presses.** A rule 4 press (one that writes origin, `drawn` or `PRESS_LEAVES` with `r4` in the click handler) on an item row, in `pressed()`, and in `qpressed()` exactly where it removes the card's `ledger:<repo>:<ref>` `.mailrow` (the ⋯ road's Done… and Back to design…), when the row sits in `row.closest(".qsec[data-sec]")` reading `decide` or `do` inside a `.qcard`. An `ask`'s answer and a home-answered Go with it are not changed. Read, *With you* and *Answered* blocks take nothing off.
- **At the press.** Take one off that block's number in the card's `.qwpill` and in the block's heading (`Decide (n)` / `Do (n)`, the `want` macro's `qsech`; the last one's reads `Decide (0)`). A pill with neither left is removed and the card drawn plain: drop `st-needs-you` and `wants`, replace `edge-needs` with the group's edge as `card_edge` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` ~536) orders it — `edge-waiting` for a card in the Waiting group, `edge-working` In progress, else `edge-ended` — and clear `data-status`; draw no status word.
- **The recount.** `AO.qDrawSummary` needs the Queue closure's locals (`AO.queue`, ~app.js 4831): the Queue exposes a recount of its own (e.g. `AO.qRecount`) that redraws the summary bar, the legend and the switch's number only, never `applyPicks`' `qoff` pass; the next `apply()` (a pick's press or the re-read) hides the card as the picks say.
- **The keep.** `AO.inflight["qcard:<data-card>"]`: per block (`decide`, `do`) the keys of the items taken off (`data-msg`; `ledger:<repo>:<ref>` for the ⋯ road), each press adding its own; the click handler's `finally` never deletes it. The Queue's re-read (`lists.innerHTML` replaced, ~5047) applies it **before** `AO.restoreRowErrs(lists, errs)` (which removes a fresh left row itself, ~3736–3746): for each kept key, a row with that `data-msg` still in the fresh card's Decide or Do block is taken off again (count, heading, pill, look as at the press); a key drawn nowhere in those blocks — gone, or a decided board line now under Read — drops from the keep. After the answer the keep stands so for at most a minute; then it is dropped.
- **On a refusal.** Take back that press's one key at once from the keep — restore its block's number in the pill and the heading, and the pill, the classes, the edge and `data-status` if it had drawn the card plain — and recount; the refresh then draws the board's.
- Tests (the page's script tests, as TD-1316's): a Decide press on a card with *Decide 2* reads *Decide 1* in the pill and the heading in the press's frame; the last Decide on a card with no Do drops the pill, the needs-you classes and edge (to the group's) and the summary's count, and the card stays shown with the switch on until the next apply; a ⋯ Done… that removes the card's ledger item takes one off; a Read item's press and an ask's answer take nothing off; a re-read while out keeps the change; a re-read drawing a decided board line under Read drops it from the keep and leaves the fresh count as drawn; two Decide presses on one card and one refused leave one off; a refusal puts its one back at once.

**Done when:** A press of an answer on a Queue card changes that card within the press's frame: its pill's number drops, a pill left empty and the card's *needs you* go, and the summary recounts; a re-read while the press is out keeps it, and a refusal puts it back. Tests cover each case above. The live check: a press on the Queue reads so at once, recorded on TD-1436 before archiving.
