## TD-2051: The tick's first five acts on a stage record, and the home's archive of an entry no pull request will archive

**Why:** The design gives the home six acts on a stage record for what no session is placed to do. This entry builds five of them: reconciling the record with the forge and the ledger, applying the person's answer, passing a skipped review, offering again after a lost or silent holder, and offering work. The sixth, starting an audit, is TD-2053's.

**Ask:** Should the builders start on the tick's acts that move an entry when no session will?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *The home acts only where no session will* (the six acts with their conditions and order, and reconcile's cases), *Ways in*, *Who archives the entry* and *A switch or a rename re-keys the records*. It builds acts 1 to 5 (reconcile, the person's answer, pass, offer again, offer) and leaves act 6, start an audit, to TD-2053. It also owns the removal of a closed record after thirty days, and running a team's last good copy of its flow while the flow fails its check, telling the person once.

**Done when:** A pass takes at most one act on a record, in the stated order. A pickable entry is offered and its record written at the offer; a lost or silent holder's entry is offered again once, and the second loss goes to the person under the offer-again act; a holder ended by a Stop or a hold spends no offer. A pull request merged by hand ends its entry. The person's lifted answer is mapped to a move by the record's answer map, and a label not in the map is treated as Something else. Settled and written down: what a turn is for the silent test. A record closes only when the ledger reads its entry archived. Lands with no team running a format-3 flow. The acts that start a session run only while the team is started, inside its schedule and not held by the usage gate. A closed record is gone from the branch's head after thirty days. A team whose flow stops passing its check keeps running its last good copy, and the person is told once. Where an open pull request is a pickable entry's work (its branch name holds the id as a whole token, whatever its case, and holds no second entry's id; never a fork's or a filing pull request), the entry's first record is written with that branch and pull request and the seat for pull requests nobody holds leaves it alone; an entry whose pull request is a draft, or is held by a live session, is not offered while that lasts, and one whose only pull request is a fork's is offered afresh; the function that says which pull request is an entry's work is the one the seat's build (TD-2053) and the switch-over's check (TD-2071) call (design 4.9e, Work in flight).
