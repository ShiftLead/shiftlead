**Why:** TD-1581's design (§4.7 `ao gate`, §4.5 screen 8, §4.5a *Settings page: Usage* and the usage chip): once the reserve is the account's (TD-1609), the person sets it once per account, from the CLI or the Settings page, and the chip shows the account's one line.

**Fix:** As §4.7 `ao gate`, §4.5 screen 8 and §4.5a say:
- The `gate` RPC (`rpc_gate`, src/sessionorc/agent_settings.py, with `_amount_rows`) answers `{accounts: {<key>: {profiles, billing, reserves | amounts, windows}}}` (§6 *The readers*): a key under `usage_gate:`, each account the adapters' `profiles()` name, each a node has reported under; `windows: []` with no reading; an orphan key with `profiles: []`. Its readers below change in this same build, so no promote leaves one reading the other's shape.
- `ao gate` (src/shiftlead/cli.py `cmd_gate`, its parser): with no arguments, one line per account, naming its profiles, then its reserves and lines; `ao gate <account> <label>=<reserve>…` sets them through `set_settings {account, reserves}`; a profile's name in place of the account resolves to it, the reply naming the account and every profile it moved. `ao settings`' `usage_gate` print (cli.py) is by account, and the commit-message example in src/shiftlead/defs.py (`usage_gate.grind.week`) names an account key. `ao gate --json` is the `gate` RPC's `{accounts: …}` reply.
- Settings page (src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py `usage_cards`, ui/templates/settings.html, ui/app.py `/api/settings/usage`): one card per account of the `gate` RPC's accounts (the home's `profiles.yml`'s, and a node's once it has reported), its badge listing the profiles on it, an account whose profiles disagree on billing saying so, a reserve (or, metered, an amount) field per window, **Save** posting `{account, reserves}`.
- The usage chip (src/shiftlead/ui/common.py `usage_accounts`, its app.js mirror): the account's one line, not the lowest of its profiles'; the hover gives the account's reserve and line.
- §4.5a's help text for these rows, where help.py carries it.

**Done when:** `ao gate claude-code:paul week=5/day` and `ao gate grind week=5/day` both set `claude-code:paul`, the second's reply naming the account and its four profiles; `ao gate` alone prints one line per account; the Settings page draws one card per account and its Save posts the account; the chip shows the account's line.
