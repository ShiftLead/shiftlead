## TD-1264: ShiftLead's readers of agent-cadence's reviewer keys take round one's reviewer with `--first`, the fact-checker from `fact-check-model`, and the design-round caps from `design-rounds` and `design-rounds-wide`

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (ao-agent-cadence-ac-grinder, through `ao td add --repo`, from agent-cadence TD-203; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open — agent-cadence TD-203 is built; waits for the pin bump its Waits line names (`docs/cadence.toml`'s `[agent-cadence]`)
**Waits:** event — the pin bump that brings agent-cadence TD-203 here: `docs/cadence.toml`'s `[agent-cadence]` `ref` at or past d7aa6a1 (TD-203, archived in agent-cadence, #29); read 2026-10-30 (the designer): the pin is 29210e6, which does not carry it
**Location:** design §4.9b *The fact-check* (the author's fact-check "on the repo's `review-model`", and an expired ask covered by the author's `review-model`); `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` (*How a design is reviewed here*: Fable and Sonnet rounds alternating, the three-Fable-round close; a held path); `docs/briefs/techlead-context.md` (the cheaper-model review and fact-check; a held path); `src/shiftlead/briefs/techlead.md` and `entry.md` (an entry's fact-check); `src/shiftlead/briefs/fact-checker.md`, `src/shiftlead/team_skill.md`; TD-857's builds TD-1213 and TD-1214 (the trial beside the author's own fact-check)

**Why:** agent-cadence TD-203 (ADR 2026-10-30 *design review rounds*) makes `review-model` an ordered, comma-separated list (`"sonnet, fable"`; a round is one pass through it, one review at a time; `cadence_config.py get review-model --first` prints round one's), adds `fact-check-model` (a model or `<adapter>/<model>`; unset, the first `review-model`), and caps a design plan's review rounds with `design-rounds` / `design-rounds-wide` (default 4 / 6), in the *Design review rounds* paragraph TD-203 adds to agent-cadence cadence.md §4.8. ShiftLead's design and briefs assume one model: §4.9b names "the repo's `review-model`" for the author's fact-check, and the designer's brief writes its own round order and close (Paul, 2026-10-15). Once the pin brings TD-203, a plain `get review-model` may print a list, the fact-checker TD-857 seats is a model the repo now names, and the round caps are settings rather than prose, so a reader that keeps assuming one model asks for the wrong reviewer.

**Fix:** a design round once the pin carries TD-203: §4.9b *The fact-check* names `fact-check-model` for the author's fact-check (and what TD-857's trial compares it with), each brief that asks for a review takes round one's reviewer with `--first` and a fact-check from `fact-check-model`, and the designer's brief takes its round order and caps from `review-model` and the two round keys — where that would change the designer's brief's rounds as Paul set them on 2026-10-15, the round asks him. Then the build entries.

**Done when** the design and the briefs name the reviewer, the fact-checker and the round caps by agent-cadence's keys, no shiftlead text reads `review-model` as one model, and the builds are filed.

**Related:** agent-cadence TD-203 and ADR 2026-10-30 *design review rounds*; TD-857 (the Gemini fact-checker) and its builds TD-1212, TD-1213, TD-1214; TD-129 (the pin).
