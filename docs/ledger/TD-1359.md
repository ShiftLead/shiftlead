## TD-1359: ShiftLead's code writes the ledger through ledgerboard's ledger writer, once v2.4 ships it

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1286's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Waits:** event — ledgerboard v2.4 released with its `ledger.py` writer (ledgerboard target design §7.2 step 4)
**Part of:** TD-1286
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/ledger.py` and its callers; `scripts/vendor_ledgerboard.sh`; `src/sessionorc/ledgerboard/`

**Why:** ShiftLead's code writes ledger entries itself — `src/sessionorc/ledger.py`'s edits (a hold, a decide's write-back, a Done… with words) — where Paul's rule of 2026-10-30 asks every ledger edit to go through the helpers. ledgerboard's target design §1.1 makes its own `ledger.py` the writer, called in-process, and ships it in v2.4 (target design §7.2 step 4); the board's half is built (TD-1330, v2.3) (TD-1286).

**Fix:** Once ledgerboard v2.4 is released: vendor it (`scripts/vendor_ledgerboard.sh`, as TD-1330 vendored v2.3), and convert every ledger write in `src/sessionorc/ledger.py` and its callers to ledgerboard's ledger writer, in-process, as `src/sessionorc/board.py` calls its board writer (design §4.4 *A session writes the ledger and the board only through the helpers*, *Board write-back*).

**Done when:** No code under `src/` writes a ledger entry's text but through ledgerboard's ledger writer, and the vendored ledgerboard is v2.4 or later.

**Related:** TD-1286; agent-cadence#TD-206; TD-1330.
