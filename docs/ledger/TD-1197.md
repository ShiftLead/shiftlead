## TD-1197: The Add entry form as TD-1190 designed it: the Open a session line without the name, no plan read blanks a line, one sentence a line, the two ways out side by side

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-28 (the designer, from TD-1190's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — build
**Part of:** TD-1190
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py` (`entry_line` 81); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`/api/entry/plan`, its `line` 2962); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`openEntry` 3557, `plan()` 3591); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/base.html` (`#entrydlg` 164–199); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.maildlg` 185); `tests/test_ui_entry.py`, `tests/test_ui_entry_host.py`

**Why:** TD-1190: the lines under the form's buttons blank and redraw on every pause in typing a Name, the Open a session line repeats the name the box shows, and the form's text is long sentences in the mono face, stacked in one column.

**Fix:** as design §4.5a's *Add entry…* row (*Its layout*) and the *Add entry form* rows say, and as the mockup `AddEntry.dc.html` and the review shots `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-10-28-td1190-add-entry-{wide,taken,phone}.png` draw. (1) `entry_line` drops the name: *Starts an interactive <role> session in a new worktree[ on <host>], with your words in its composer, not sent.*, the *no team services this repo* tail kept; the Inbox's **Start session** line keeps its own name, so it gets its own words or a flag, not this change. `/api/entry/plan` still answers `name_why` and the default as now. (2) `plan()` blanks nothing before its answer: it writes `#entryline`, `#entryseat`, `#entrywhen` and the hand state from the answer, each only where its text changed, and a stale answer (`mine !== asked`) still writes nothing. The page appends no text to `v.line` any more. (3) `base.html`: the one-sentence notes of the *Add entry…* row (What's note under its label, Attach's note, Hand's *Drafts the entry …* line); the *what the session is told besides …* sentence moves to **Open a session**'s `title`; the seat's name beside the Hand button; the lines in `.note` (sans), not `.meta` (mono); Type and Priority as bordered options; the two ways out in a two-column grid under a rule, stacked Hand first below 560px; the dialog widened from 560px to 720px (`min(720px, 94vw)`). (4) Tests: `test_ui_entry*.py`'s `line` asserts take the new words, and one test asserts the line holds no name, typed or default.

**Done when** the form draws as the shots do on a wide screen and a phone; typing a Name leaves the lines under both buttons standing unless the name's verdict changes; the Open a session line holds no session name; `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass; and the PR carries a headless-Chromium shot of the built form (design §4.9b).
