## TD-1197: The Add entry form as TD-1190 designed it: the Open a session line without the name, no plan read blanks a line, one sentence a line, the two ways out side by side

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-28 (the designer, from TD-1190's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — build
**Part of:** TD-1190
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py` (`entry_line`, `entry_hand`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`entry_plan`, its `line`; `/api/entry/plan`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the form's `openEntry(repo)`, not `AO.openEntry`, the Inbox's; its `plan()` and `nameBox.oninput`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/base.html` (`#entrydlg`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.maildlg`); `tests/test_ui_entry.py`, `tests/test_ui_entry_host.py`

**Why:** TD-1190: the lines under the form's buttons blank and redraw on every pause in typing a Name, the Open a session line repeats the name the box shows, and the form's text is long sentences in the mono face, stacked in one column.

**Fix:** as design §4.5a's *Add entry…* row (*Its layout*) and the *Add entry form* rows say, and as the mockup `AddEntry.dc.html` and the review shots `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-10-28-td1190-add-entry-{wide,taken,phone}.png` draw (made by `docs/mockups/reviews/td1190-src/td1190_shots.py`; the *before* shot is the form as built, 2026-10-28).
(1) **The Open a session line is the server's whole sentence.** `entry_line` returns *Starts an interactive <role> session in a new worktree[ on <host>], with your words in its composer, not sent.* — no name, so its `name` parameter goes, with its callers' argument; and no *no team services this repo* tail, which **Hand to the techlead**'s disabled reason already says. `app.js` writes `v.name_why` or `v.line` as they come, wrapping nothing. The Inbox's **Start session** line is drawn by `templates/inbox_row.html` and does not change.
(2) **No plan read blanks a line.** `plan()` writes `#entryline`, `#entryseat`, `#entrywhen` and the hand state from the answer, each only where its text changed, and a stale answer (`mine !== asked`) still writes nothing.
(3) **The Hand line is the server's sentence too.** `entry_hand` returns `line` in the form's voice: where the seat is on call — no record, or its record `exited` or `closed`, `mail.read_when`'s own on-call test — *No techlead is running: one starts on the next tick and reads it first.*; else the record's `refill` sentence with a capital first letter and a full stop. `mail.read_when` and the Message composer are unchanged. `app.js` drops the *Hand to the techlead: * and *When it is read: * prefixes: `#entrywhen` holds `h.why` or `h.line` bare, and `#entryhandwhat` stays hidden while there is a `why`.
(4) **`base.html` and `app.css`.** The one-sentence notes of the *Add entry…* row: What's note under its label, Attach's note, Hand's *Drafts the entry …* line. The *what the session is told besides …* sentence moves to **Open a session**'s `title`. The seat's name sits beside the Hand button. The lines are `.note` (sans), not `.meta` (mono). Type and Priority are bordered options. The two ways out sit in a two-column grid under a rule. The dialog widens from 560px to 720px (`min(720px, 94vw)`). Below 640px of viewport (the dialog then under about 600px): the ways out stack, Hand first; **Close** stays at the heading's right, with the repo and ledger wrapping under the title; and Type's and Priority's options share one row in equal columns, each hint under its word.
(5) **Tests.** `test_ui_entry.py`'s `line` asserts take the new words: the no-team case asserts the line has no tail, and the seat case asserts the on-call sentence. Add one test that the Open a session line holds no name, typed or default, and one that a live seat's `refill` comes back capitalised with a full stop.

**Done when** the form draws as the shots do at 760px and 390px; typing a Name leaves the lines under both buttons standing unless the name's verdict changes; the Open a session line holds no session name; the heading shows the repo's resolved ledger (`docs/ledger` in this repo) once the plan answers; `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass; and the PR carries a headless-Chromium shot of the built form (design §4.9b).
