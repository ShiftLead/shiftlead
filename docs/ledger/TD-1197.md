## TD-1197: The Add entry form as TD-1190 designed it: the Open a session line without the name, no plan read blanks a line, one sentence a line, the two ways out side by side

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-28 (the designer, from TD-1190's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — build
**Part of:** TD-1190
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py` (`entry_line`, `entry_hand`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`entry_plan`, its `line`; `/api/entry/plan`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the form's `openEntry(repo)`, not `AO.openEntry`, the Inbox's; its `plan()` and `nameBox.oninput`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/base.html` (`#entrydlg`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.maildlg`, shared by five dialogs: `#entrydlg`'s own width goes beside it); `tests/test_ui_entry.py`, `tests/test_ui_entry_host.py`, `tests/test_ui_repo_org.py`

**Why:** TD-1190: the lines under the form's buttons blank and redraw on every pause in typing a Name, the Open a session line repeats the name the box shows, and the form's text is long sentences in the mono face, stacked in one column.

**Fix:** as design §4.5a's *Add entry…* row (*Its layout*) and the *Add entry form* rows say, and as the mockup `AddEntry.dc.html` and the review shots `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-10-28-td1190-add-entry-{wide,taken,phone}.png` draw (made by `docs/mockups/reviews/td1190-src/td1190_shots.py`; the *before* shot is the form as built, 2026-10-28).
(1) **The Open a session line is the server's whole sentence.** `entry_line` returns *Starts an interactive <role> session in a new worktree[ on <host>], with your words in its composer, not sent.* — no name, so its `name` parameter goes, with its callers' argument; and no *no team services this repo* tail, which **Hand to the techlead**'s disabled reason already says. `app.js` writes `v.name_why` or `v.line` as they come, wrapping nothing. The Inbox's **Start session** line is drawn by `templates/inbox_row.html` and does not change.
(2) **No plan read blanks a line.** `plan()` writes `#entryline`, `#entryseat`, `#entrywhen` and the hand state from the answer, each only where its text changed, and a stale answer (`mine !== asked`) still writes nothing. `plan()` no longer sets `handWhy = "…"; handState();` before it asks, so a name read never disables **Hand to the techlead** while it waits; the first read after the form opens still starts from `handWhy`'s initial `"…"`.
(3) **The Hand line is the server's sentence too.** `entry_hand` returns `line` in the form's voice: where the seat is on call — the same test as `read_when`'s `on_call`, read from the record view: no record for the seat, or its `state` `exited` or `closed` (an `unreachable` seat keeps its `refill` sentence) — *No techlead is running: one starts on the next tick and reads it first.*; else the record's `refill` sentence with a capital first letter, and a full stop where it has none. `mail.read_when` and the Message composer are unchanged. `app.js` drops the *Hand to the techlead: * and *When it is read: * prefixes: `#entrywhen` holds `h.why` or `h.line` bare, and `#entryhandwhat` stays hidden while there is a `why`.
(4) **`base.html` and `app.css`.** The one-sentence notes of the *Add entry…* row: What's note under its label, Attach's note, Hand's *Drafts the entry …* line. The *what the session is told besides …* sentence moves to **Open a session**'s `title`. The seat's name sits beside the Hand button. `#entryline`'s rule in `app.css` keeps `white-space: normal` and its comment loses *its second sentence says where the rest is read*. The lines, and Type's hints *something wrong now* and *something new*, are `.note` (sans), not `.meta` (mono). Type and Priority are bordered options. The two ways out sit in a two-column grid under a rule. The Add entry dialog alone widens from 560px to 720px (`#entrydlg.maildlg { width: min(720px, 94vw); }`): `.maildlg` and the other dialogs that share it (`#mailbox`, `#startdlg`, `#membersdlg`, `#boardadd`) keep 560px. The Team pick (`#entryteamrow`, drawn where two or more teams service the repo) sits between Priority and the rule, its note the sentence *Its techlead takes the entry.* in `.note`, the row keeping its `row` class so `.maildlg .row[hidden]` still hides it (or a `[hidden]` rule of its own); the mockup does not draw it, and the design row's placement governs. Below 640px of viewport (the dialog then under about 600px): the ways out stack, Hand first; **Close** stays at the heading's right, with the repo and ledger wrapping under the title; and Type's and Priority's options share one row in equal columns, each hint under its word.
(5) **Tests.** The `line` asserts of `test_ui_entry.py` (the plan's lines with a name, ~87 and ~389; the no-team case, ~118) and `test_ui_entry_host.py` (~123) take the new words, as do the hand asserts (`test_hand_to_the_techlead_sends_the_words…`, ~205, the on-call sentence): the no-team case asserts the line has no tail; the hand asserts take the on-call sentence; and the host case reads *Starts an interactive techlead session in a new worktree on node1, with your words in its composer, not sent.* Add one test that the Open a session line holds no name, typed or default, and `test_a_live_seat_says_its_own_sentence` takes the capitalised form (*Read when its turn ends.*), the test that a live seat's `refill` comes back with a capital and a full stop. The markup-bound asserts keep passing: `test_ui_entry.py`'s order and tag asserts (What, Attach, Type, Priority, `entryhand`; `entrygo` before `entryname maxlength="32"` before `entryline`; `entrywhat`, `entryerr`) hold as long as the ids, attributes and Hand-before-Open order survive; `test_ui_repo_org.py`'s `entryteamrow` literal is updated to the new tag. Priority's options are `<label>`/`<span>`, with no `</div>` before the third radio, or the slice in `test_ui_entry.py`'s Priority test (from `aria-label="Priority"` to the next `</div>`) is widened.

**Done when** the form draws as the shots do at 760px and 390px; typing a Name leaves the lines under both buttons standing unless the name's verdict changes; the Open a session line holds no session name; the heading shows the repo's resolved ledger (`docs/ledger` in this repo) once the plan answers — already built (`#entrywhere` from `cfg.ledger`): keep it and assert it in a test; `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass; and the PR carries a headless-Chromium shot of the built form (design §4.9b).
