**Fix:** as design §4.9b *The fact-check* says. (1) ao-grind's `seats:` gains `{name: fact-check-ao-1, role: fact-checker, profile: gemini-reader, trigger: asks}`, which runs from the team's next start (Paul's). (2) The members' repo part of the brief gains the trial's rule. On a doc-only PR, once its head is pushed, send `ao msg --kind ask --bound 1800 --about '#<n>' fact-check-ao-1 "fact-check PR #<n> at <sha>: <the claims>"` beside the usual fact-check, never in place of it. The run that posts the trial line re-checks each of the seat's findings against the asked `<sha>` by refs, and compares them with the author's own reviewer's findings as the evidence comment and its FIXED follow-up record them, to count caught, missed and false, and never wait on it to merge; an ask refused because the sender's last one to the seat is still open is not retried, and the PR's trial line is `fact-check-trial: skipped · one ask open`. When the seat's reply lands, the author posts one comment on the PR, merged or not; if the author has exited or closed, the name's next run, which finds the reply in the mail it keeps (§4.10 *The name coming back adopts it*), posts it. An ask that expires (its bound passes with no reply) delivers nothing, so the author posts `none` at its own next `ao` reply after the bound. For a reply, the comment is `fact-check-trial: SHIP|BLOCK · <adapter>/<model> · caught <c> · missed <m> · false <f> · <minutes> min`; with none, `fact-check-trial: none · <the seat's state at the bound>` or `skipped · one ask open`, with no cells; with a sentence for each missed or false finding, and the string `cadence-review:` nowhere in it (cadence §4.9 reads that line from any line); `none` says why from the seat's record (`limited`, fills exhausted, never filled, or still `working`). (3) `ao team list --json` on a scratch home shows the seat with trigger `asks`.

**Done when** ao-grind's definition holds the seat, the members' brief carries the trial's rule, and the first `fact-check-trial:` comment is on a PR.
