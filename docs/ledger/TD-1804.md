**Why:** A ledger-filing PR whose author's run has ended has no owner, so it stands open and its entry never reaches main. On 2026-11-13 shiftlead had 19 such PRs, opened 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-12, mergeable and never merged: #2629, #2669, #2686, #2717, #2731, #2737, #2824, #2852, #2884, #3002, #3057, #3092, #3201, #3250, #3261, #3288, #3350, #3437 and #3584 (found by `ao-led-tds-ledger` for Paul, mail m-7bfb52fe6889). Fourteen carry a `cadence-review:` comment (thirteen with a SHIP verdict, #2824 with FIXED); five have none. Their entries (TD-1210 to TD-1731, 24 in all) are on no lane's pick list, because a lane reads main's ledger.

The rule is that the author merges a PR that touches no held path (design §4.9b *The reader*; the audit briefs `docs/briefs/docs-audit.md` and `docs/briefs/test-audit.md`, *A finding's entry*). The author can end its run between opening the PR and the merge: the precheck takes about 12 minutes, and main moves under it. A grinder's run can end with no bound at all. A seat (an audit seat is one) that waits on a PR of its own is closed once it has been idle for `SEAT_PR_WAIT` (two hours), "and the PR stands among the repo's open ones" (design §6 rule 3, *Seats*). Nothing after either owns it:
- the session-start hook lists PRs open past two days, but only as information to a session that may not merge them;
- the *unasked* tag marks only a held PR (design §4.4 *A held PR nobody asked is marked, never ordered*);
- the techlead merges only a PR handed to it with an `ask` carrying `pr` (design §4.9b).

**Design PR:** #3777

**Location:** design §6 rule 3 (SEAT_PR_WAIT); §4.9b The reader; §4.4 A held PR nobody asked is marked, never ordered; docs/briefs/docs-audit.md and docs/briefs/test-audit.md (A finding's entry); the stranded-work procedure (agent-cadence path procedures/stranded-work.md)

**Fix:** Design who owns a ledger-only PR whose author is gone, then build it. Two shapes were suggested (m-7bfb52fe6889); the design round picks one or a better one:
- **The home merges it**: a PR that touches only `docs/ledger/` (and the board), is green, carries a `cadence-review: SHIP` on its head and has `check_cadence.py --pr N` exiting 0, merged through cadence step 5's line once its author's record is closed or past a bound. This could be a policy of the tick, or the techlead seat filled for it.
- **A sweep raises it**: the stranded-work sweep, or the Repo page's reading, names a ledger-only PR open past two days whose author is closed, to the techlead or the person, as an item that leads to its merge, a re-review or a close.

Either way, the design says who closes one whose entry is already superseded on main, and a filing PR with no review comment is reviewed before it merges, never merged unreviewed. The 19 PRs above are triaged by hand on m-7bfb52fe6889's thread, not by this entry.

**Done when:** A ledger-only PR whose author's record is closed does not stand open past the bound the design sets: it is merged at SHIP and green, or raised to someone who merges, re-reviews or closes it. A test holds the rule.
