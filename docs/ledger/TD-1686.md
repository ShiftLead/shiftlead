## TD-1686: The home marks an entry no lane of its repo's teams takes (no_lane), and notes the session its id was given to

**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** c530ae34c83a4b85b9bdb52f0a0d7115
**Part of:** TD-1573
**Location:** src/sessionorc/ledger.py (lane_gap, in_lanes rest); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (a mark step after _settle_ledger); src/sessionorc/queue.py (_status, _wait far); src/sessionorc/agent_ids.py (given); no_lane_noted.json; design §4.4 An entry no lane takes is marked

**Why:** TD-1573's design (§4.4 *An entry no lane takes is marked*): an open entry of a kind a lane word takes that no lane of its repo's teams takes is counted under its kind and named nowhere (ledgerboard TD-058: `Kind: design-first`, `Owner: techlead`, drawn *1 design* while Start read *designer: 0*). This build is the home's half: the test, the mark, and the once-only note to the session the id was given to.

**Fix:** As design §4.4 *An entry no lane takes is marked* says:
- **`ledger.lane_gap(entry, lanes) -> str | None`** in `src/sessionorc/ledger.py`, `lanes` a list of `(lane words, team)`: None where some lane takes the entry, else the `why` words in the design's order (the team, then the kind word, then the owner words). It tests only an entry whose `for_page` is in `LANE_KINDS`, not marked `needs_team`, and which reads `pickable: yes` once `_unwaited` and a dropped `settling` mark have read it — so an entry held by a decision, an open id or an open part is never marked.
- **The mark step** on the host agent's instance (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`), on every reading it keeps of a repo, fresh (`_read_repo`'s result) or carried (`_reread_repo`), after `_settle_ledger`: drop any `no_lane` the reading carries; take the repo's lanes from the records — live or ended, `repo` the checkout, never `superseded_by` (the selection `shiftlead.teamrun.in_repo` makes, written in `sessionorc`); mark `no_lane: {why}`; move each marked entry out of its kind in the reading's `by_kind` into `no_lane`; set the repo reading's `no_lane: [{id, why}]`. Nothing on an unserved repo or one no record carries a lane for. `entries_before`'s readings are not marked.
- **`in_lanes`** skips a marked entry in `rest`, as it skips a withheld one.
- **The Queue** (`src/sessionorc/queue.py`): `_status`'s all-members test and `_wait`'s `far()` also read the `no_lane` mark.
- **The note**: `no_lane_noted.json` beside `ids.json`, keyed by origin, `{from, ids}`. On the first pass, fresh or carried, that marks an id not in it, where `ids.json`'s `given` (`agent_ids.py`) holds the id with a `by` that is a session's record (not the person's, not forgotten; ended is fine), a `system` note to that record in the design's words; the id recorded as noted whether a note went or not. A repo with no key: record every marked id as noted, send nothing.
- **Tests**: ledgerboard TD-058's case — a `design-first` entry with `Owner: techlead`, a repo whose one team's records carry `[design-first, owner:designer]` and `[free-pick, owner:grinder]` — is marked with *Owner techlead: the lanes that take design-first take owner:designer* and counted in `by_kind`'s `no_lane`, not `design`; a settling and a waiting entry a lane would take once settled are not marked; a design-first entry blocked by `decision (designer)` and a live check blocked by a decision are not marked; a team-tagged entry of a team with no record, and a kind no lane takes, give their own words; a marked pickable entry is in no `rest`; a mark is dropped at the next pass once a record's lane takes it; the note is sent once, to the taker, and not on a repo's first pass.

**Done when:** The home's reading marks an entry no lane of its repo's teams takes with `no_lane: {why}`, naming the field that keeps it out; `by_kind` counts it under `no_lane`; the Queue reads it as no lane takes it; the session its id was given to gets one `system` note; the tests above pass.
