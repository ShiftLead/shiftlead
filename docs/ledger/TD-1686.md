**Why:** TD-1573's design (§4.4 *An entry no lane takes is marked*): an open entry of a kind a lane word takes that no lane of its repo's teams takes is counted under its kind and named nowhere (ledgerboard TD-058: `Kind: design-first`, `Owner: techlead`, drawn *1 design* while Start read *designer: 0*). This build is the home's half: the test, the mark, and the once-only note to the session the id was given to.

**Fix:** As design §4.4 *An entry no lane takes is marked* says:
- **`ledger.no_lane(entry, lanes) -> str | None`** in `src/sessionorc/ledger.py`, `lanes` a list of `(lane words, team)`: None where some lane takes the entry, else the `why` words in the design's order (the team, then the kind word, then the owner words). It tests the entry as `in_lanes` reads a wait (`_unwaited`), with its `settling` mark dropped too; an entry marked `needs_team`, or whose `for_page` is not in `LANE_KINDS`, is never marked.
- **The mark** on the home's reading, after `_settle_ledger` in `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (the static pass has no records, so it is a step of the instance with `self`'s records): the repo's lanes from its records live or ended, never one superseded (the selection `shiftlead.teamrun.in_repo` makes, written in `sessionorc`, which never imports `shiftlead`); `no_lane: {why}` on each entry `no_lane` returns words for; nothing on an unserved repo or one no record carries a lane for. The reading's `by_kind` (`ledger.py`, `read`) gains `no_lane`, a marked entry counted there and not under its kind, and the repo's reading `no_lane: [{id, why}]`. Mark carried readings too (the `fresh=False` road), so the counts never disagree between passes.
- **The Queue** (`src/sessionorc/queue.py`): the two `NO_LANE` tests (`_status` and `waits`) also read the `no_lane` mark.
- **The note**: on the first pass that marks an id, where `ids.json`'s `given` (`agent_ids.py`) holds it with a `by` that is a session's record and not the person, a `system` note to that record in the design's words; the id recorded as noted in `ledger_seen.json` beside its clock (`settle.py`'s store), never noted twice; a repo's first marking pass after the build records every marked id as noted and sends nothing.
- **Tests**: ledgerboard TD-058's case — a `design-first` entry with `Owner: techlead`, a repo whose one team's records carry `[design-first, owner:designer]` and `[free-pick, owner:grinder]` — is marked with *Owner techlead: the lanes that take design-first take owner:designer*; a settling and a waiting entry a lane would take once settled are not marked; a team-tagged entry of a team with no record, and a kind no lane takes, give their own words; `by_kind` counts it under `no_lane`; the note is sent once, to the taker, and not on the first pass.

**Done when:** The home's reading marks an entry no lane of its repo's teams takes with `no_lane: {why}`, naming the field that keeps it out; `by_kind` counts it under `no_lane`; the Queue reads it as no lane takes it; the session its id was given to gets one `system` note; the tests above pass.
