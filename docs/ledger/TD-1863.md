## TD-1863: A restart that waits on the person is drawn on its entry's Queue card, and the rows read it instead of stalled

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 6da78b655ae1452d905bb1bbcb2465e2
**Part of:** TD-1856

**Why:** TD-1856's design: a restart that waits on the person, for a session holding an entry an open Queue card holds, is drawn on that card and not only in the Inbox, and the entry's rows read what waits instead of *stalled*. On 2026-11-14 TD-1711's card read *stalled* for a day while grinder-ao-1's early restart waited on Paul's press in the Inbox.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/queue.py (_state, _state_word); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (the restart mark's reader); src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py (_restart_at, the told restart row); src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (HOLDER_ROWS, item_home); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (restart_mark); src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; the Queue card's holder row template

**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *A row's state* (*A holder waiting on a restart is not stalled*) and *The card's status*, §4.5 screen 6 *One place for each thing that needs the person* (the stopped holder's row, its restart row among them) and screen 12 *Three wants: Do*, and §4.5a *Queue card: holder's state row* and *Inbox row: restart* (TD-1856).

1. **The restart mark, read in `sessionorc`.** The row word is computed in `sessionorc/queue.py`, which never imports `shiftlead`, so the test of whether a record carries a restart mark and its short word move to one `sessionorc` reader (`agent_common` is where the tick's readers live). Today the test has three copies: `restart_mark` in `shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (not superseded; a `restart_ceiling` dict, a `restart_blocked` dict, or an `early` `restart_wanted` while the record reads idle or exited), `_restart_at` in `sessionorc/agent_notify.py` (the same, a lapsed plain fill mark left out, `fill_lapses`), and now the Queue's row word; all three call the one reader, which leaves a lapsed plain fill mark out, so they never disagree. The short word, from the mark's fields: *restart wanted · repeats <ref>* where `restart_wanted.repeat.ref` is set, else *restart wanted · early*; for `restart_ceiling`, *brief lost twice* (`why: lost-brief`), *fills exhausted* (`why: fill`), else *restarts exhausted*; *restart held* for `restart_blocked` (the `RESTART_SUBJECTS` kinds). The Inbox row's full words (`fill_words` in `cards.py` and the rest) stay in `shiftlead`; only the test and the short word move.
2. **The row word.** In `_state_word` / `_state` (`sessionorc/queue.py`), on a row no live record with `unattended: false` claims, before the *stalled* test: where any claim's live record carries a restart mark, the state is that record's restart word and its holder that record (the first by id among several), the pill beside it; *stalled* and the PR words are not read on that row. It reads the mark, never the attention store: a dismissed or snoozed restart row leaves the word standing. `answered`, `applying` and *with you* keep their place before it, as for *stalled*.
3. **The row on the card.** In `shiftlead/ui/queue.py`, `HOLDER_ROWS` gains `restart`, so `item_home` ties the Inbox's restart row by `holder_claims` exactly as a stopped holder's state row (`tied: {by: "holder", ref}`), a Do item in the header, counted on the pill and the Queue's tab, the card *needs you*. It draws the restart row's words and caption (the *yours now* / *fills again after the hour* caption the Inbox row draws) and its controls through the same requests: **Open**, **Restart** where the row's `restartable` says, **Resume** (confirming on a member, TD-1477), **Dismiss**; no Snooze. Every reader of `HOLDER_ROWS` takes it as it takes a `stalled?` row: §6 rule 13's stuck line goes under it (`inbox.py`'s one-item-for-one-cause pass; a session's pending row first where it has one, as now), `pr_row` and the item's `holder: True` in `ui/queue.py` (no Start session), and the *routed around* line under it where `seat_able` reads the seat unable. `NEEDS_YOU_ROWS` is unchanged (the Sessions rollup's *answer needed* counts no restart row, as it counts no `limited` or `stalled?` row). **The told row follows it** (§4.10: an item `home: queue`, a stopped holder's state row among them, is told by its card's page): `agent_notify`'s restart row, keyed `{sid}|restart`, looks up `queue.HOLDER + sid` as the state rows' notification does and links the card's page where the row is homed there, else the Inbox. A restart row snoozed in the Inbox before its record held the entry stays in the *Snoozed (n)* fold until its time, as a `stalled?` row does. A record that exited or closed has no live claim, so its restart row stays in the Inbox.
4. **Help text.** The **Restart** help entry (§4.5a's help list, `help.py`) reads *(the Inbox restart row, a card's more ▾, the ending banner)*; change it to *(the restart row, in the Inbox or on its entry's Queue card, a card's more ▾, the ending banner)* in §4.5a and `help.py` together, with its doc-bound test (a design PR does not reword the help list).

Tests: TD-1711's case — an idle unattended record with an `early` `restart_wanted` claiming an entry an open card holds: the row reads *restart wanted · early*, not *stalled*; the card is *needs you*; the restart row is a Do item on that card with Restart and Resume and is in no Inbox section; a `repeat` reads *restart wanted · repeats TD-n*; a live (idle, not exited) holder with a `restart_ceiling` (each `why`) and one with a `restart_blocked` read their words, and a lapsed plain fill mark reads none; a dismissed row leaves the word and takes the item off the card (the card no longer *needs you* for it); an exited holder's restart row stays in the Inbox; a record with no mark still reads *stalled*; the Inbox's `restart_mark`, `agent_notify`'s `_restart_at` and the Queue's word read the one `sessionorc` reader; the told restart row links the card's page where the row is homed there; a stuck line goes under a restart row on the card.

**Done when:** A live (not exited or closed) session holding an entry on an open Queue card whose restart waits on the person (an early or repeat restart wanted, the ceiling, a held restart, a second lost brief, fills exhausted) draws its restart row on that card as a Do item with Open, Restart, Resume and Dismiss, not in the Inbox; the entry's rows read the restart's words (*restart wanted · early*, …) instead of *stalled*; the card reads *needs you*; the tests above pass.
