**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *A row's state* (*A holder waiting on a restart is not stalled*) and *The card's status*, §4.5 screen 6 *One place for each thing that needs the person* (the stopped holder's row, its restart row among them), screen 12 *Three wants: Do*, *One answer set* and *A holder's word opens the holder*, and §4.5a *Queue card: holder's state row*, *Queue card: holder's word* and *Inbox row: restart* (TD-1856).

1. **The restart mark, read in `sessionorc`.** The row word is computed in `sessionorc/queue.py`, which never imports `shiftlead`, so the test of whether a record carries a restart mark and its short word move to one `sessionorc` reader (`agent_common`, where the tick's readers live). Today the test has two copies: `restart_mark` in `shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (not superseded; a `restart_ceiling` dict, a `restart_blocked` dict, or an `early` `restart_wanted` while the record reads idle or exited) and `_restart_at` in `sessionorc/agent_notify.py` (the same, every plain fill mark left out — listed, never told, §4.10). The reader takes the five fields as arguments — `restart_wanted`, `restart_ceiling`, `restart_blocked`, `superseded_by` and `state` — so each caller passes its own: `restart_mark` from the view dict by `.get`, `_restart_at` and the Queue's row word from the `Session`'s attributes (no `getattr` fallbacks: a `Session` always carries them), and is the one test of *this record's mark draws a restart row* — `restart_mark`'s today: not superseded; a `restart_ceiling` or `restart_blocked` dict whatever the record reads; an `early` `restart_wanted` only while it reads idle or exited — and returns, for every such mark (a plain fill mark included), the mark's time, its short word and a `lapses` flag (`fill_lapses`); `restart_mark`, `_restart_at` and the Queue's row word all call it, and `_restart_at` alone skips `lapses` (a fill mark is a seat's alone — `agent_tick` writes `why: fill` only on a seat — so its seat test goes). The short word, from the mark's fields: *restart wanted · repeats <ref>* where `restart_wanted.repeat.ref` is set, else *restart wanted · early*; for `restart_ceiling`, *brief lost twice* (`why: lost-brief`), *fills exhausted* (`why: fill`), else *restarts exhausted*; *restart held* for `restart_blocked` (the `RESTART_SUBJECTS` kinds). The Inbox row's full words (`fill_words` in `cards.py` and the rest) stay in `shiftlead`; only the test and the short word move.
2. **The row word and the status.** In `_state` / `_state_word` (`sessionorc/queue.py`), on a row no live record with `unattended: false` claims, before the *stalled* test, inside `_state_word` under the same `own is None` guard *stalled* has (so a row the person's own session claims keeps *with you in <name>*): where a claim's live record's mark draws a restart row (the reader of step 1, never `_holder_stuck`: the word is read also on an idle holder with a live subagent, or a working one at its ceiling), the state is that record's restart word and its holder that record (the first by id among several), which `_state` returns as the row's holder, as it does for *stalled*, overriding `row()`'s default `claim[0][0]`, so the pill beside it is that record's; *stalled* and the PR words are not read on that row. `answered`, `applying` and *with you* keep their place before it, as for *stalled*. Apart from the word, and whatever word a row reads (a need, *with you in <name>* for a taken-over member, *answered — waiting on <asker>*, *applying*): for every live record whose mark passes the reader, the row whose claim `holder_claims` picks for it carries `restart_home: true` — computed in `reading()` after `holds` is filled and before `_card`, as `holder_claims(holds, open_)` (every `holds` qid is in `open_`, so the pick is the page's), passed into `row()` — and the card's ledger status (`_status`, which reads row dicts alone) reads *needs you* from that flag first, before the `needs` test, so `ao rank --list` and `ao card` say it. The card itself carries `restart_home: true` where any of its rows carries the flag (the homed row need not be the top entry), the field the header reads (step 4). Any other card the record's claims hold takes nothing from that row — neither *needs you* nor *stalled* (its state is a restart word, not `stalled`, so `stalled_holder` passes it) — and reads as a held row's card does; its word links the record's Focus, where *more ▾* has Restart.
3. **The row on the card.** In `shiftlead/ui/queue.py`, `HOLDER_ROWS` gains `restart`, so `item_home` ties the Inbox's restart row by `holder_claims` exactly as a stopped holder's state row (`tied: {by: "holder", ref}`), a Do item in the header, counted on the pill and the Queue's tab. It draws the restart row's words and caption (*yours now* / the rest, as the Inbox row draws them) and, through the same requests, **Restart** where the record's own `restartable` says (`cards.py`: supervised, idle, exited or closed, not a seat, not superseded — never the Inbox row's `restartable`, whose *fills exhausted* term draws a mark-clearing Restart; a fill mark is only an exited seat's, so it never reaches a card, and a seat's homed row draws Open alone whatever its mark), in the answer bar's first slot, not outlined, then **Open**; no **Resume** (the record is live, and `resume_blocked` refuses a one-press Resume on a live record); no Dismiss, no Snooze, no ⋯, no Not clear. A restart row homed on a card is drawn there whatever the attention store says of its Inbox row (a `dismissed:<at>` or a snooze is the Inbox's alone): `inbox_sections` reads the attention store before `item_home` runs, dropping a dismissed state row and folding a snoozed one into **Snoozed (n)**, so for `row == "restart"` it looks up `cards and cards.get(queue.HOLDER + sid)` first (its `cards` parameter, `card_index`'s output; with no `cards` passed there is no bypass) and, where it is set, skips both branches and passes the row on to be homed. Every reader of `HOLDER_ROWS` takes it as it takes a `stalled?` row: §6 rule 13's stuck line goes under it, on the card and in the Inbox alike (`inbox.py`'s one-item-for-one-cause pass, `holders` chosen by an explicit priority, not `setdefault`'s section order: a `NEEDS_YOU_ROWS` row first, then the restart row, then any other holder row), `pr_row` and the item's `holder: True` in `ui/queue.py` (no Start session), and the *routed around* line under it where `seat_able` reads the seat unable. A restart row and a `limited` or `stalled?` row of one record are two items, each counted. The Sessions rollup's `NEEDS_YOU_ROWS` is unchanged. **The told row follows it** (§4.10: an item `home: queue`, a stopped holder's state row among them, is told by its card's page): `agent_notify`'s restart row, keyed `{sid}|restart`, looks up `queue.HOLDER + sid` as the state rows' notification does and links the card's page where the row is homed there, else the Inbox; where it is homed on a card, `_notify_snoozed` does not read the Inbox row's `dismissed:<at>` or snooze as its end, since the card draws it whatever they say.
4. **The word as drawn.** A row whose state is a restart word carries `restart_word: true`, set in `row()` from the row's final state (after the answered and applying overrides), and `lay_held`'s `mark()` writes `restart_word: False` with the state it replaces (`restart_home` stays, so the card is still *needs you*); `queue_row.html` adds it to its `opens` test, so the word links its record's Focus as *stalled* does, and draws it with the class `s-restart` (amber with a small mark) forced, never derived from the word's first word. In `queue_card.html`, where the top entry's own row carries `restart_home` (step 2) and reads a restart word, the header draws no word beside its pill (a homed top entry reading another word — *with you in <name>*, *answered — waiting on <asker>*, *applying* — keeps its header word as today): the card's amber pill counts the Do item, as for any Do item, with no status word of its own, and the item's line is the restart row's full words (the Inbox row's subject, which opens with the short word). Any other top entry's restart word — on a card whose `restart_home` row is a child's, or on any other card — is drawn beside its pill as *stalled* is. The card's own `restart_home` field (step 2) is what the ledger status and the pill read, not the header's word. A homed row whose record is not `restartable` (a seat, an unsupervised record, one working at its ceiling) draws **Open** alone; the row leaves with the mark, as the Inbox row does, and the card offers nothing that clears a mark and leaves the record running.
5. **Help text.** The **Restart** help entry (§4.5a's help list, `help.py`) reads *(the Inbox restart row, a card's more ▾, the ending banner)*; change it to *(the restart row, in the Inbox or on its entry's Queue card, a card's more ▾, the ending banner)* in §4.5a and `help.py` together, with its doc-bound test (a design PR does not reword the help list). The manager's brief (`src/shiftlead/briefs/manager.md`, *the person's press is the Inbox row's Restart*) and `src/shiftlead/team_skill.md` (*on its Inbox restart row*) read *its restart row's Restart* / *on its restart row*, as §6 rule 2 now writes the manager's words.
