## TD-1863: A restart that waits on the person is drawn on its entry's Queue card, and the rows read it instead of stalled

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 6da78b655ae1452d905bb1bbcb2465e2
**Part of:** TD-1856

**Why:** TD-1856's design: a restart that waits on the person, for a session holding an entry an open Queue card holds, is drawn on that card and not only in the Inbox, and the entry's rows read what waits instead of *stalled*. On 2026-11-14 TD-1711's card read *stalled* for a day while grinder-ao-1's early restart waited on Paul's press in the Inbox.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/queue.py (_state, _state_word); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (the restart mark's reader); src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py (_restart_at, the told restart row); src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (HOLDER_ROWS, item_home); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (restart_mark); src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; src/shiftlead/briefs/manager.md; src/shiftlead/team_skill.md; the Queue card's holder row template

**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *A row's state* (*A holder waiting on a restart is not stalled*) and *The card's status*, §4.5 screen 6 *One place for each thing that needs the person* (the stopped holder's row, its restart row among them), screen 12 *Three wants: Do*, *One answer set* and *A holder's word opens the holder*, and §4.5a *Queue card: holder's state row*, *Queue card: holder's word* and *Inbox row: restart* (TD-1856).

1. **The restart mark, read in `sessionorc`.** The row word is computed in `sessionorc/queue.py`, which never imports `shiftlead`, so the test of whether a record carries a restart mark and its short word move to one `sessionorc` reader (`agent_common`, where the tick's readers live). Today the test has two copies: `restart_mark` in `shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (not superseded; a `restart_ceiling` dict, a `restart_blocked` dict, or an `early` `restart_wanted` while the record reads idle or exited) and `_restart_at` in `sessionorc/agent_notify.py` (the same, every plain fill mark left out — listed, never told, §4.10). The reader is the one test of *this record's mark draws a restart row* — `restart_mark`'s today: not superseded; a `restart_ceiling` or `restart_blocked` dict whatever the record reads; an `early` `restart_wanted` only while it reads idle or exited — and returns, for every such mark (a plain fill mark included), the mark's time, its short word and a `lapses` flag (`fill_lapses`); `restart_mark`, `_restart_at` and the Queue's row word all call it, and `_restart_at` alone skips `lapses`. The short word, from the mark's fields: *restart wanted · repeats <ref>* where `restart_wanted.repeat.ref` is set, else *restart wanted · early*; for `restart_ceiling`, *brief lost twice* (`why: lost-brief`), *fills exhausted* (`why: fill`), else *restarts exhausted*; *restart held* for `restart_blocked` (the `RESTART_SUBJECTS` kinds). The Inbox row's full words (`fill_words` in `cards.py` and the rest) stay in `shiftlead`; only the test and the short word move.
2. **The row word and the status.** In `_state` / `_state_word` (`sessionorc/queue.py`), on a row no live record with `unattended: false` claims, before the *stalled* test: where a claim's live record's mark draws a restart row (the reader of step 1, never `_holder_stuck`: the word is read also on an idle holder with a live subagent, or a working one at its ceiling), the state is that record's restart word and its holder that record (the first by id among several), the pill beside it; *stalled* and the PR words are not read on that row. `answered`, `applying` and *with you* keep their place before it, as for *stalled*. The row whose claim `holder_claims` picks for that record carries `restart_home: true` (set where the reading holds the open-card index, beside `holds`), and the card's ledger status (`_status`, which reads row dicts alone) reads *needs you* from that flag, beside `needs` and `stalled`, so `ao rank --list` and `ao card` say it; any other card the record's claims hold reads its status as if the word were not there (its word links the record's Focus, where *more ▾* has Restart).
3. **The row on the card.** In `shiftlead/ui/queue.py`, `HOLDER_ROWS` gains `restart`, so `item_home` ties the Inbox's restart row by `holder_claims` exactly as a stopped holder's state row (`tied: {by: "holder", ref}`), a Do item in the header, counted on the pill and the Queue's tab. It draws the restart row's words and caption (*yours now* / the rest, as the Inbox row draws them) and, through the same requests, **Restart** where the row's `restartable` says and **Resume** (confirming on a member, TD-1477) in the answer bar's first slot, neither outlined, then **Open**; no Dismiss, no Snooze, no ⋯, no Not clear. A restart row homed on a card is drawn there whatever the attention store says of its Inbox row (a `dismissed:<at>` or a snooze is the Inbox's alone). Every reader of `HOLDER_ROWS` takes it as it takes a `stalled?` row: §6 rule 13's stuck line goes under it, on the card and in the Inbox alike (`inbox.py`'s one-item-for-one-cause pass, `holders` chosen by an explicit priority, not `setdefault`'s section order: a `NEEDS_YOU_ROWS` row first, then the restart row, then any other holder row), `pr_row` and the item's `holder: True` in `ui/queue.py` (no Start session), and the *routed around* line under it where `seat_able` reads the seat unable. A restart row and a `limited` or `stalled?` row of one record are two items, each counted. The Sessions rollup's `NEEDS_YOU_ROWS` is unchanged. **The told row follows it** (§4.10: an item `home: queue`, a stopped holder's state row among them, is told by its card's page): `agent_notify`'s restart row, keyed `{sid}|restart`, looks up `queue.HOLDER + sid` as the state rows' notification does and links the card's page where the row is homed there, else the Inbox; where it is homed on a card, `_notify_snoozed` does not read the Inbox row's `dismissed:<at>` or snooze as its end, since the card draws it whatever they say.
4. **The word as drawn.** `queue_row.html` links a restart word to its record's Focus as it links *stalled*, with one class, `s-restart` (amber with a small mark), whatever its first word; in the header, where the top entry reads a restart word, the header draws the status, *needs you*, and the Do item's line carries the word.
5. **Help text.** The **Restart** help entry (§4.5a's help list, `help.py`) reads *(the Inbox restart row, a card's more ▾, the ending banner)*; change it to *(the restart row, in the Inbox or on its entry's Queue card, a card's more ▾, the ending banner)* in §4.5a and `help.py` together, with its doc-bound test (a design PR does not reword the help list). The manager's brief (`src/shiftlead/briefs/manager.md`, *the person's press is the Inbox row's Restart*) and `src/shiftlead/team_skill.md` (*on its Inbox restart row*) read *its restart row's Restart* / *on its restart row*, as §6 rule 2 now writes the manager's words.

Tests: TD-1711's case — an idle unattended record with an `early` `restart_wanted` claiming an entry an open card holds: the row reads *restart wanted · early*, not *stalled*; the card is *needs you* on the page and in `ao rank --list`; the restart row is a Do item on that card with Restart and Resume and is in no Inbox section; a `repeat` reads *restart wanted · repeats TD-n*; an idle holder with a `restart_ceiling` (`why` unset and `lost-brief`) and one with a `restart_blocked` read their words; a working holder at its ceiling and an idle early-marked holder with a live subagent read the word; a told restart row homed on a card is told although its Inbox row was dismissed; an Inbox restart row (an exited holder's) takes an actor's stuck line, the pending row first; a holder claiming entries on two cards: the homed card is *needs you*, the other's row reads the word and its status does not; a restart row dismissed in the Inbox before the claim is drawn on the card; an exited holder's restart row stays in the Inbox; a record with no mark still reads *stalled*; a plain fill mark is listed and worded by the reader, never told; the Inbox's `restart_mark`, `_restart_at` and the Queue's word read the one `sessionorc` reader; the told restart row links the card's page where the row is homed there; a stuck line goes under a restart row on the card.

**Done when:** A live (not exited or closed) session holding an entry on an open Queue card whose restart waits on the person (an early or repeat restart wanted, the ceiling, a held restart, a second lost brief) draws its restart row on that card as a Do item with Restart, Resume and Open, not in the Inbox; the entry's rows read the restart's words (*restart wanted · early*, …) instead of *stalled*; the card reads *needs you* on the page and in `ao rank --list`; the tests above pass.
