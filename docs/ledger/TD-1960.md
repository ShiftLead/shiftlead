## TD-1960: The designer and techlead briefs do not yet say who judges a design's verdict

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-19
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3df7631a412e4f3cb11e3e8143ac9051
**Part of:** TD-1911

**Why:** TD-1911's design (§4.9c *Who ends a design's rounds*, §4.9b *It judges a design's verdict*) makes the team's techlead seat the judge of a design's counted verdict, with the cadence's default judge standing in where the seat cannot judge. The seat answers no end-of-rounds item for the person: a seat answering one waits on TD-1910's `decide` delegation. The designer's and the techlead's briefs do not say this yet: the designer brief here still raises the item by hand-applied rules written before agent-cadence TD-281 and TD-288 counted the verdict.

**Location:** docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/techlead.md (not techlead.stage.md), docs/briefs/techlead-context.md, tests/test_briefs_layout.py; design §4.9c *Who ends a design's rounds*, §4.9b

**Fix:** Write the design's rules into the briefs, as §4.9c *Who ends a design's rounds* and §4.9b *It judges a design's verdict* say them:
- **The designer** (the built-in template `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md` and this repo's `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md`, its *How a design is reviewed here* paragraphs): mark the design routine or far-reaching beside its class, far-reaching wherever its own PR changes a section §4.9c lists or any of its builds names a file `[far-reaching]`'s `paths` match; after the closing fact-check run `review_stats.py --verdict TD-<design>` and follow its `next:`; on `next: judge` send the techlead seat the `judge TD-<design>` ask (the PR's number and head, the record's path, the design's section files as the plan, and `--verdict`'s output pasted whole) and end the turn while it waits; copy the reply's `cadence-judge:` line into `Verdict:` with a `judge` row, never asking again; run the cadence's default judge at once where the question seat is the person or absent, the send's reply carries `routed` or the ask closes `rerouted` (then `ao msg --withdraw <id>`), the seat declines, or the ask lapses; on the judge's Go file the builds with no item and send the person one uncounted `note`. Retire the hand-applied end-of-rounds rules the counted verdict replaces.
- **The techlead seat** (`src/shiftlead/briefs/techlead.md`, with its other answering rules, and `docs/briefs/techlead-context.md`'s primer where it lists what the seat answers): a `judge TD-<design>` ask is answered by a fresh subagent on `review-model`'s last model with the cadence's fixed judge prompt, the section files as its plan and `--verdict`'s output written to a file of the seat's own as its verdict, the section files saved beside it from the PR head, read by ref; its `cadence-judge:` line sent verbatim with `--source` the record's path; a `CONFIRM` raised only on a decision of the person's it names with where it is written, the line kept in the judge line's form; a seat whose tool cannot start that subagent declines. It answers no end-of-rounds item for the person.
- **A test** in `tests/test_briefs_layout.py` (or beside it): the rendered techlead brief carries the `judge TD-` ask and the `cadence-judge:` line, and the rendered designer brief carries `--verdict`'s `next:` and the default judge's fallbacks.
Both briefs are held paths (`docs/briefs/**`): the PR waits for its reader. This build's Go is the person's either way, since the design is marked far-reaching; the `reach` row holds it until that Go is recorded only once TD-1961's table is on main (either may merge first).

**Done when:** The designer brief and template end a design's rounds by `--verdict`'s `next:`, with the techlead as judge and the default judge as its fallback in each case §4.9c names; the techlead's brief judges by the fixed prompt; the new brief test passes, and `pdm run test` passes.
