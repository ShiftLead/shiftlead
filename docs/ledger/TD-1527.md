**Why:** Paul wants to see a design from a high level and stay out of its minutiae (Paul, 2026-11-07, through ao-led-tds-ledger, m-5817cb52379e). Today the design text, the review record (`docs/reviews/TD-n.md`) and the board item's `Context` are all at finding level — F-numbers, function names, line refs — so to judge an end-of-rounds item or a design steer he must read the record a reviewer reads. His example, for TD-1476: *each pool member is a separate record, so the design stays simple; members show on the team as seats; later members carry a backup indicator; pools only on question seats, the techlead's is a later design.*

The ask: a short, formatted Markdown synopsis per design — what it does, the key choices and why, what it rules out, what the person will see — in plain words with no F-numbers or code refs, kept current through the rounds, and linked from the design's Queue card. Paul reads it in his editor, so the card links it through ShiftLead's existing editor link (§5 `person.open_in`, his amendment of 2026-11-07, m-87c0a64dfefc) to a checkout where the file exists (the design branch's worktree before the merge, the home's checkout after), with the GitHub blob link as a fallback for the phone.

Bears on TD-1378 (the Queue's Parts display) and on TD-1526 (the same card's link to the design PR), which may share the design round. Where the synopsis lives is the designer's choice — Paul suggests beside the per-entry history TD-1482 introduced (`docs/history/TD-<n>.md`, TD-1513).

**Design PR:** #3203

**Fix:** Design first. Settle:
- where a design's synopsis lives (per entry, e.g. beside `docs/history/TD-<n>.md` or `docs/reviews/TD-<n>.md`), its sections (what it does, the key choices and why, what it rules out, what the person will see) and its writing rule (plain words, no finding numbers, function names or line refs; §4.10 *How a message to a person is written*);
- who writes and keeps it current: the designer each round (the designer brief, `docs/briefs/`, a held path), and whether the closing fact-check reads it against the design; the cadence's part (`cadence.md` §4.8, the end-of-rounds item) asked of agent-cadence on its own ledger;
