## TD-2106: Decide what removes old session and build leftovers from temporary space

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d479a6810d64484fbb956ab18ca5b62a
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: its Fix item 4 asks what happens to temporary files with no owner. Read on 2026-11-23: `/tmp/claude-1000` held about 86,000 inodes across 814 project directories, with nothing ageing them out, and 382 `/tmp/pdm-build-*` directories were left by interrupted builds, likely because `pdm sync` runs before the precheck sets `TMPDIR`.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/scratch.py, docs/cadence.toml, design §4.6 *The disk sweep*

**Fix:** Design which leftovers ShiftLead may remove. `/tmp/claude-<uid>` is Claude Code's, and a person's live or resumable session may depend on it, so settle how a project directory is proven unused, by age and no live session. Run the precheck's `pdm sync` under the run's `TMPDIR` and remove `pdm-build-*` directories past a day that nothing holds.

**Done when:** An unused project directory past the agreed age and an old `pdm-build-*` are removed. A directory of a live or recent session is kept. A killed precheck's sync leaves nothing in `/tmp`.
