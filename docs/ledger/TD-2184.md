**Why:** Design §4.4 *A question whose entry only waits is asked early* (TD-2172) splits a `decision (paul)` member's counted open work into open parts and waits, and counts a gated blocker that itself waits. The reading must stamp the split so every surface reads one fact.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/queue.py (open_work); design §4.4 A question whose entry only waits is asked early

**Fix:** In `src/sessionorc/queue.py`, after `open_work` and `with_held` (unchanged), a third step `with_gated_waits(work, entries, xref)` adds to each member each `Blocked by:` id that carries `decision (paul)` where that blocker's own merged work (its held builds' ids among it) is non-empty, repeated until nothing is added (a visited set guarding a cycle, whose gated ids count for nothing; `until`/`event` gates and unresolved ids still count for nothing); both stamp sites (`queue.py`'s two `with_held(open_work(…))` calls) run the three in that order. The reading stamps beside `open_work` a second list, `open_parts`: the counted ids from `open_work`'s own parts that name the member in `Part of:` (a held build's ids are never parts). The fact (TD-1926) becomes `open_parts` non-empty; every reader of the fact reads that one test, never `open_work` alone — the row word *waiting, then yours*, `lift_here`/`lift_item`, the home's stale-press 409 (`agent_inbox._refuse_waiting` on each road that calls it; `agent_inbox._open_work` hands `open_parts` beside what it returns, and `agent_mail`, which indexes its result, reads it), `ui/queue.line_then_yours`, `ui/queue.mail_then_yours` (an `ask --lift` mail grey with answers withheld only where the entry has the fact, drawn whole with the early line where asked early), the Inbox's `then_yours` reads (`ui/inbox.py`) and `ledger_for_you`. A member with `open_work` and no fact reads the new row word *asked early*, in *A row's state*'s order beside *waiting, then yours* (relation words *after TD-n*, *after your TD-n* for a step blocker; teal row and segment titled *asked early*), keeps its need marked (no *needs you* on the card, no needs-you filter or **Needs you (n)**, out of the repo count and the rail's *Later* count, set aside in the card's `waits` test as a fact member is), and draws its question whole: its answers, **Lift the hold** where stamped, and a press written as any. A covering undecided line over early members and no fact member reads early too. Design §4.4 *The card's status* (*A question whose entry only waits is asked early*).
