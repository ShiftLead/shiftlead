**Why:** Design §4.4 *A question whose entry only waits is asked early* (TD-2172) splits a `decision (paul)` member's counted open work into open parts and waits, and counts a gated blocker that itself waits. The reading must stamp the split so every surface reads one fact.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that moves early questions to Waiting and gives yes-or-no questions a Go, or run another round first?

**Location:** src/sessionorc/queue.py (open_work); design §4.4 A question whose entry only waits is asked early

**Fix:** In `src/sessionorc/queue.py`, one helper `counted_work(entries, xref, held)` runs `open_work` once (unchanged) and then repeats `with_held` and a gated-wait step — adding to each member each `Blocked by:` id that carries `decision (paul)` whose merged work, or date or event wait, is non-empty — until nothing is added (a visited set guarding a cycle, whose gated ids count for nothing; unresolved ids still count for nothing). It returns the work (stamped `open_work`), `open_parts` (the counted ids naming the member in `Part of:`, a held build's own open parts among its design's) and `early_gates` (the member's own unreached `until` or `event (<name>)` gate words; a gated blocker carrying one counts as a wait id). Every site that builds the set calls it in place of `with_held(open_work(…))`: `queue.py`'s `lay_held` and `reading`, the Inbox's and the Repo page's rows (`ui/inbox.py`) and `agent_inbox._open_work` (which returns `open_parts` as a fifth element of its tuple: its three callers in `agent_inbox` that unpack four take five, and `agent_mail`'s index of the fourth stands). The fact (TD-1926) becomes `open_parts` non-empty, or `open_work` non-empty on a gated `Owner: paul` entry; every reader of the fact reads that one test, never `open_work` alone — the row word *waiting, then yours*, `lift_here`/`lift_item`, the home's stale-press 409 (`agent_inbox._refuse_waiting` on each road that calls it), `ui/queue.line_then_yours`, `ui/queue.mail_then_yours` (an `ask --lift` mail grey with answers withheld only where the entry has the fact, drawn whole with the early line where asked early), the Inbox's `then_yours` reads (`ui/inbox.py`) and `ledger_for_you`. A member with a wait (`open_work` or `early_gates`), no fact, no design with a question (a design with held builds: with no open part it is asked as any, a need, since its One more round and Hold act at once), no held build (a `held_by` row never carries `early`: it reads *waits on TD-<design>'s question*) and no gated `Owner: paul` entry (which keeps the fact with counted work of either kind and, with only a date or event wait, is drawn as a need, as before) reads the new row word *asked early*, marked `early` with its `early_line` (*once W closes*, W the first wait in one order — the counted ids in key order (`_key_sort`), `<repo>#TD-n` for another repo's, then the member's own date or event wait — named as its id, or, where it is the member's own gate or a gated blocker whose only wait is its own date or event, as that wait (*once <date>*, *once its event lands*); where more waits stand, *and k more close* follows it, the member's own gate among the k, since the answer acts once the last ends) (never `then_yours`, which withholds answers), in *A row's state*'s order beside *waiting, then yours* (relation words *after TD-n*, *after your TD-n* for a step blocker; teal row and segment titled *asked early*); `_status`, `branch()`, `_seg`, `rail_rows` and the group's `n` read the mark. It keeps its need marked (no *needs you* on the card, no needs-you filter or **Needs you (n)**, out of the repo count and the rail's *Later* count, never the person's own in the card's `waits` test: `queue._own` is false on an `early` member as on one with `open_work`, so one whose only wait is its own `until` or `event` gate waits by its `waiting` mark, as a fact member waits), and draws its question whole: its answers, **Lift the hold** where stamped, and a press written as any.
