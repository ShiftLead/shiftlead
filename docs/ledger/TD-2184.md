## TD-2184: A held question whose entry waits only on other work is read as asked early

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 9bd0dedbbd3d4d7084880258474d15ec
**Part of:** TD-2172

**Why:** Design §4.4 *A question whose entry only waits is asked early* (TD-2172) splits a `decision (paul)` member's counted open work into open parts and waits, and counts a gated blocker that itself waits. The reading must stamp the split so every surface reads one fact.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/queue.py (open_work); design §4.4 A question whose entry only waits is asked early

**Fix:** In `src/sessionorc/queue.py`, one helper `counted_work(entries, xref, held)` runs `open_work` once (unchanged) and then repeats `with_held` and a gated-wait step — adding to each member each `Blocked by:` id that carries `decision (paul)` whose merged work, or date or event wait, is non-empty — until nothing is added (a visited set guarding a cycle, whose gated ids count for nothing; unresolved ids still count for nothing). It returns the work (stamped `open_work`), `open_parts` (the counted ids naming the member in `Part of:`, a held build's own open parts among its design's) and `early_gates` (the member's own unreached `until` or `event (<name>)` gate words; a gated blocker carrying one counts as a wait id). Every site that builds the set calls it in place of `with_held(open_work(…))`: `queue.py`'s `lay_held` and `reading`, the Inbox's and the Repo page's rows (`ui/inbox.py`) and `agent_inbox._open_work` (which hands `open_parts` beside what it returns; `agent_mail`, which indexes its result, reads it). The fact (TD-1926) becomes `open_parts` non-empty, or `open_work` non-empty on a gated `Owner: paul` entry; every reader of the fact reads that one test, never `open_work` alone — the row word *waiting, then yours*, `lift_here`/`lift_item`, the home's stale-press 409 (`agent_inbox._refuse_waiting` on each road that calls it), `ui/queue.line_then_yours`, `ui/queue.mail_then_yours` (an `ask --lift` mail grey with answers withheld only where the entry has the fact, drawn whole with the early line where asked early), the Inbox's `then_yours` reads (`ui/inbox.py`) and `ledger_for_you`. A member with a wait (`open_work` or `early_gates`), no fact, and no design with a question (a design with held builds: with no open part it is asked as any, a need, since its One more round and Hold act at once) reads the new row word *asked early*, marked `early` with its `early_line` (never `then_yours`, which withholds answers), in *A row's state*'s order beside *waiting, then yours* (relation words *after TD-n*, *after your TD-n* for a step blocker; teal row and segment titled *asked early*); `_status`, `branch()`, `_seg`, `rail_rows` and the group's `n` read the mark. It keeps its need marked (no *needs you* on the card, no needs-you filter or **Needs you (n)**, out of the repo count and the rail's *Later* count, set aside in the card's `waits` test as a fact member is), and draws its question whole: its answers, **Lift the hold** where stamped, and a press written as any. An undecided line all of whose covered members are early reads early too and `inbox_sections` leaves it out of the top bar's and rail's counts. Design §4.4 *The card's status* (*A question whose entry only waits is asked early*, *A design's question is asked once*).

**Done when:** Tests show: an entry gated by `decision (paul)` and blocked by two gated entries, A with no counted work and B blocked by an open entry C that waits `until` a later date, reads *asked early* after B, its card Waiting with no *needs you* and its line out of every count; the same entry blocked by A alone is asked as any; a gated entry whose own `until` day has not come reads *asked early*, *once <date>*; a gated blocker whose only work is its own held build's blocker counts as a wait, and a held build whose only blocker is a gated entry that waits passes the wait to its design; a design whose round entry (`Part of` it) or a held build's part is open reads *waiting, then yours* and its stale press is refused 409, while a design whose only counted work is a held build's blocker is asked as any, a need, its **Go now** written; a gated entry blocked by a person's step (`Owner: paul`, ungated) reads *asked early* with *after your TD-n*; a gated `Owner: paul` entry with an open blocker keeps *waiting, then yours*; an `ask --lift` mail on an early entry draws its answers; a line covering an early member and a need is a need; a cycle of gated blockers counts for nothing.
