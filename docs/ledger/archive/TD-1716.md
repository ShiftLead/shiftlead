## TD-1716: ao feedback digest: a seat's transcripts over a window, condensed through the adapters (TD-1491's extractor)

**Type:** feature

**Kind:** build

**Why:** TD-1491's design (§4.7 *Feedback*) gives the `/shiftlead-feedback` procedure a deterministic extractor that condenses a seat's transcripts over a window into text an agent reads. It reads through each tool's adapter, never a tool's files from core. The 2026-11-06 review the procedure replaces read Claude Code's and Gemini's files by hand.

**Location:** design §4.7 *Feedback*, §4.3 `conversations`, `marks_errors`, `read_transcript`, §4.4a NODE_READS, §4.8a; src/sessionorc/adapters.py (TranscriptEntry, Conversation); src/shiftlead/adapters/claude_code, gemini; src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (NODE_READS, ACT_TIMEOUT); src/sessionorc/agent_remote.py (_route_read); src/sessionorc/identity.py (READS); the RPC handlers; src/shiftlead/cli.py; tests/test_transcript.py, tests/test_identity.py

**Done when:** `ao feedback digest` over a scratch home's record writes the parts and the index §4.7 gives. Tests cover:

- each digest line form and its kind mark;
- another RPC answered while a digest runs;
- the day lines in the given zone, across a change of clocks;
- the window's edges;
- an undated entry;
- the part split and *(continued)*, and the split with no prompt before the bound;
- any entry past 64 KiB written with *(cut)*;
- a conversation whose file has no timestamped entry left out;
- a conversation an earlier record listed appearing only under that one;
- the CLI's wait of `DIGEST_TIMEOUT + CALL_TIMEOUT`, the handler's `DIGEST_TIMEOUT` on a local record, and the home's forwarded read at `DIGEST_TIMEOUT`;
- the record's own profile answering None refused in those words, another profile answering None read as none, the own profile asked when `profiles()` omits it, an adapter with no `profiles()`, and `--part-bytes` under 65536 refused;
- the day line after every header, and a part at the floor holding one entry;
- a node's CLI answered *no session* for another host's record;
- the zone's fallback to the offset on a host that cannot resolve the name;
- the two `· n calls` lines at a seam, and the CLI dropping a repeated day line;
- a lone `sidechain` entry, the failed-subagent form, and an agent adapter without `conversations` refused *lists no conversations*;
- repeated `--skip`;
- `marks_errors` null for a record with an error;
- `identity.READS` and `NODE_READS` holding both reads;
- `next` and the CLI's second call, with no entry repeated or lost at a shared instant, and the cut with no prompt before the point;
- a cut between two entries in one second with different fractions (`next.at` carrying the fraction, nothing repeated or lost), and a cut just before an entry dated earlier than the one before it (nothing repeated or lost);
- a conversation whose span meets the window with no entry in it, and one with thoughts only there, absent from the index and the file while `--list` shows them, and a reply of `{text: "", lines: 0, next: null}`;
- the index's `lines` for a conversation continued across parts;
- the profile dedupe, with a conversation held under a profile other than the record's;
- `--list` and `--skip`, and the caller's own conversation left out;
- the `--json` index shape, with the run's resolved `since` and `until`;
- an unreachable record listed with its error, and the exit 1;
- a record whose later conversation fails after an earlier one was written: its parts removed, its read ended there, the index with its `error`, `failed` that conversation, `conversations: []`, `parts: []`, `bytes` 0 and `marks_errors` null, and a later record sharing its conversations listing all but the failed one;
- a stale `<record id>.txt` in `<dir>` removed before the record is read, and every record with no file at `parts: []` and `bytes` 0;
- `--list` with a record whose `conversations` call fails: its `error`, a later record listing what it shares, and the exit 1;
- a shell's refusal.

Claude Code's `conversations`, `error` and `marks_errors` are tested on fixture files, and Gemini's `conversations` likewise. §4.3, §4.4a and §4.7 say what the code does.
