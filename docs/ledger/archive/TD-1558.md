**Why:** Paul's direction, 2026-11-07, sent through his session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-0368c6bccbc6) after TD-1555. The Queue page changes under its reader. Its re-read on the `queue` and `inbox` events (design §4.5 screen 12; the live UI's log showed it every ~10 s on 2026-11-07) re-orders cards, moves items between groups and reopens menus, so things move while Paul reads and possibly while he presses. TD-1555 was one symptom: an Answer… pressed on a board item sent its words as a reply to another card's ask. TD-1555 (#3241) binds a box's target at the press. That cures the misrouting, not the movement.

A page that moves under a reading hand costs the person the thing the Queue exists for: reading their org's work in their own order and acting on it with confidence that the press lands where they looked.
