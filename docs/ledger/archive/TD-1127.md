## TD-1127: A person's session holds, releases and decides (Done / Back to design) an entry: `td_hold` and `ledger_decide`'s roads pass it, and `ao td done` / `ao td design`

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-27 (the designer, from TD-1117)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved
**Part of:** TD-1117
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/mail.py` (`PERSON_SESSION` beside `PERSON_ONLY`; the predicate *an own end with no `controllers`*, inline in `from_role` since TD-1125 (#2493): this build factors it into one `mail` helper that `from_role` and `person_only` both call); `src/sessionorc/agent_common.py` (`person_only`: the RPC's name, the subset, its refusal); `src/sessionorc/agent_ids.py` (`rpc_td_hold`: `held.by`, the holder's note naming the session, `_clear_hold`); `src/sessionorc/settle.py` (`hold`: the protect check under the lock and the reword's `by`; `_marks` carrying `by`; `claim_refusal` taking the presser's words); `src/sessionorc/agent.py` (the claim path passing those words); `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (the mark's `title`); `src/sessionorc/board.py` (`add_decided` and its commit `message` taking `session`, passed to `ledgerboard.subject`'s existing `session=`); `src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py` (`rpc_ledger_decide`: the roads pass, the road-less answer does not); `src/shiftlead/cli.py` (`ao td done`, `ao td design`); the person-only list test

**Why:** TD-1117's design: a direction the person gives through a session they started (*hold this design*, *leave that alone*) lives only in mail, which outlives neither the sender's context nor the receiver's; a hold is the home's record and outlives both, but its RPC refuses every session.

**Fix:** as design §4.8 *A person's session passes two of them*, §4.4 *Hold* and §4.7 say:
1. **The subset.** `PERSON_SESSION = {"td_hold", "ledger_decide"}` beside `PERSON_ONLY`, read by the test. `person_only` stays each RPC's first statement and takes `session_ok: bool` — `True` in `td_hold`, `bool(str(road or ""))` in `ledger_decide`, the coercion inside the call so the check stays the first statement (`tests/test_person_only.py`'s `_first_call`) — and, only when it is set, passes a caller whose record in the one graph is an `own_end` and has no `controllers` (`record.controllers`, as `person_shell` reads it; a role does not end it, as it does not end TD-1116's edge). `ledger_decide`'s next statement is `road = str(road or "")`; it passes such a caller only when `road` is set; with no road it refuses it as now, and its `done` road on an entry `ledger_mod.decided_by(e, ledger_mod.PERSON_OWNER)` holds (`Blocked by: decision (paul)`, `Ask:` or not) is refused inside `_ledger_road`, after the *yours to do* check, when `not mail.as_person(caller, graph)`: *that entry waits on the person's decision*.
2. **Who pressed.** `held.by` is the caller's id (`settle.py` writes `by` already). `_hold_tell` takes the presser (`presser=`, for a hold's own note; the `tell` path below passes none, its words naming the presser): the claimed-entry note reads *<id> is held by the person's session <name>: …* and its `--why` *held by the person's session <name>* (still sent from the person, not through `from_role`); the claim refusal (`settle.py`) and the road's drop notice (*closed by the person's session <name>*, `agent_inbox.py`) name it the same way; the Queue and Repo mark stays *held (person)*, `held.by`'s session in its `title`. The decided line (`ledgerboard.decided_line`) is unchanged, `(person)` in its head; `board.add_decided` and the commit's `message` take a `session` argument, so the subject reads *session <name>* for a person's session's road and *n/a* from the page.
2b. **What it releases.** A person's session's `td_hold --release` passes on a hold whose `held.by` is a record id (a session's or the person's shell's) and is refused on one the person pressed (`held.by` `person`, or no `by`) — the check made in `settle.hold` under `_STORE`'s lock on the stored record, `rpc_td_hold` passing `presser: Literal["person", "shell", "session"]` (`settle.hold`'s signature gains it; `by` is still derived from the caller, never from the class), so a page press between the reading and the write is seen: *that hold is the person's own: they release it on the Repo page*; a new `--why` or a plain re-hold from such a caller on the person's hold is refused the same way (*… they reword it on the Repo page*). The check runs only for a caller that is not `as_person`, so the person's shell releases and rewords the person's hold, and its road reads as the person's: *n/a* in the commit subject, *the person* in the notice. The roads' own clear is not this check: a road clears any hold through `_clear_hold`, one helper `rpc_td_hold --release` calls too (the store's `settle.hold(release=True)` and the reading's `settle.mark`, as the RPC does today), `_clear_hold(root, entry, presser)` passing the presser to `settle.hold`: `rpc_td_hold` passes its caller's, a road passes `person`, since the press is the decision. A hold with no `by` reads as `person`. A session's new `--why` on a session's hold writes its own id as `by` (`at` kept); the person's reword (a `person` caller) writes `person`, and an `as_person` caller's reword of a `person` hold keeps `person`; words equal to the hold's change nothing, `by` included (`settle.hold`'s early return stands, with the protect check run before it). `_clear_hold` runs on every road, whatever the reading says, a record not held being nothing to do, so a page hold pressed between the reading and the road is cleared too.
2c. **The reading carries `by`.** `settle._marks` copies `held.by` beside `at` and `why`; the home, which holds the graph, makes the presser's words — the `queue`/`repos` readings carry them beside `by` for the mark's `title`, and the claim path (`agent.py`, where `claim_refusal` is called) passes them in, since `settle` reads no records. The presser's words: *the person's session <name>* where `by`'s record is an own end with no `controllers` at the reading, *the person* where it is the person's shell (`as_person`), the record's name for any other record, its id for one gone, *the person* for `person`. A `td_hold {tell}` from a session caller appends *(told by <that>)* to its note, but for a road's own call, which goes through a private helper `_hold_notice(tid, tell)`, new, a thin wrapper over `_hold_tell(tid, tell=tell)` that the road calls and `rpc_td_hold` calls too (no marker in the RPC's signature, no presser on the helper): `rpc_td_hold` appends the clause to `tell` itself before calling it, and a road's words, which already name the presser, are the whole note; the holder's `ao progress drop --why` carries the notice's words, not a fixed *told by the person*.
3. **The refusal** names the carve-out only where it applies: with `session_ok` set (the two RPCs), *… a person's own act, refused to every session but the person's own shell and a session the person started by hand (design §4.8)*; every other name keeps today's sentence, which is true of it.
4. **The CLI.** `ao td done <id> [--repo] ["<words>"]` and `ao td design <id> [--repo] ["<words>"]` resolve the repo to its root path as `cmd_td_hold` does (`ledger_decide` matches paths) and call `ledger_decide {repo, id, road, words}` (`road` `done` / `design`), words optional as the row's are; each prints the done / back-to-design line and a line per name in the reply's `told` and `readers` (the road's reply gains `readers`, as `td_hold`'s has, worded as `ao td hold` prints them) (the road returns no `untold`: a holder it could not tell refuses the press). `ao td hold`'s help names the carve-out.
5. **The test.** `tests/test_person_only.py`'s count stays (the list gains no name), and its refusal match stays today's sentence for the names outside `PERSON_SESSION` and is the new one whole for the two inside it (the old is its prefix); `NOT_THE_PERSONS`' *an agent session* (`claude-code`, interactive, no controllers) and its role'd shells with no controllers are person's sessions and move to the passing class for the two names, an unattended agent session taking the agent session's place among those refused every name; the harness supplies `road="done"` for `ledger_decide`, since its all-None call leaves `session_ok` false, and it gains its third class (`session_ok` set exactly in `PERSON_SESSION`'s RPCs): a person's session passes each name in `PERSON_SESSION` and fails every other name in `PERSON_ONLY`; an unattended, controlled or seat-holding interactive session fails all.

**Done when:** tests show a person's session's `ao td hold`, `--release`, a drop notice, `ao td done` and `ao td design` succeed, `held.by` and the note naming it; its road-less `ledger_decide` is refused, its `ao td done` on a `decision (paul)` entry with no `Ask:` is refused while the shell's passes, and a non-string `road` is refused, not raised; its `--release` of a hold the person pressed is refused, and of one a session wrote passes; its `ao td done` on an entry the person held passes and clears the hold, and the holder's note names the presser once; the person's shell releases and rewords the person's hold, and its `ao td done` commits *n/a* and notifies as *the person*; session B's new `--why` on session A's hold makes B its `by`; a hold with no `by` is refused a session's release; a person's session on a node (`id@node` in the home's graph) passes the same calls; the claim refusal and the mark's `title` name a session's hold's presser; the same calls from a team member, an unattended session, an own end with a controller and one holding a seat are refused with the new words; the list test's third class passes. `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass.
