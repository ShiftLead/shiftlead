## TD-1215: Through the forge, a pin's age stops at "older than the oldest point" without trying agent-cadence's seed, which the local read does try

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-29 (grinder-ao-1, from the techlead's note on #2642)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Done 2026-10-29 — found reading #2642 (TD-1208 slice 2), fixed in #2643.
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/seams.py` (`descends`, its forge branch after `_forge_repo`); `tests/` for `seams.descends`

**Why:** `seams.descends` asks whether a pin descends from `oldest` (`cadence.OLDEST` or `OLDEST_V2`) or from `cadence.SEED`, agent-cadence's first commit (TD-1208, design §5 *Which dev-cadence*). In the local checkouts a *False* against `oldest` goes on to try the seed, and *True* there wins. Through the forge it does not: `_compare(repo, oldest, commit)` answering *False* (`behind` or `diverged`) is returned at once, and the seed is compared only when the first compare could not be read. So the two roads answer differently for a pin that is after the seed but not after `oldest`. Today that cannot happen on one forge repo: `ShiftLead/dev-cadence` holds no seed and `ShiftLead/agent-cadence` holds no `OLDEST`, so one of the two compares is always unreadable and the seed is tried. It would matter only if one forge repo ever held both histories (agent-cadence's history grafted onto dev-cadence's, or the reverse). Pointed out by the techlead on merging #2642 (mail m-b89bce626838).

**Resolved:** 2026-10-29 (PR #2643) — the forge branch of `seams.descends` tries the seed whenever the compare against `oldest` is not *True*, as the local road does. Lasting content: design §5 *Which dev-cadence* (unchanged: it already said "each pair in turn"); test `test_a_pin_after_agent_cadences_seed_descends_on_either_history`.

**Done when:** `descends` answers *True* through the forge for a pin that descends from the seed but not from `oldest`, a test shows it, and `pdm run test` passes.
