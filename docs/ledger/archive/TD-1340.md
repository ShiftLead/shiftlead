**Resolved:** 2026-11-03 (grinder-ao-2) — built in #2858: `queue.items_n` read by the header and the road, the header's Start session where two or more count with its own help entry `card-start-all`, `WORK_ALL` on a header press the road counts two or more for, *waits on me* then leaving out an answered entry (`walk_mine`); tests in `tests/test_queue_start_all.py`; seen on a scratch home (`docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-03-td1340-header-start-all.png`).

**Why:** TD-1338 (Paul, 2026-11-03: *there are times that there are multiple questions on a single card that it would make sense to handle in the same session*; he chose *keep both*): a card with several Decide and Do items has no button that starts one session on all of them, so the person starts from one item and pastes the others' ids in by hand.

**Fix:** as designed in §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session* (*Started from the header on a card with items*) and §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*:
- **The count:** the card gains `items_n`, beside the boolean `items_start`, which stays (three tests assert it `is True`/`is False`): the number of items in `wants['decide']` and `wants['do']`, each less red and fork items, plus `len(wants['with_you'])` — an answered item is already outside these, in `wants['answered']` (TD-1331), and an item whose entry another session holds is inside them though it draws no button. The header draws its Start session, or its Open session by the holder rule, where `items_n != 1`.
- **The header's title** on a card with two or more is a new help entry, `card-start-all` — name *Start session*, where *Queue card, all items*, placed beside `card-start` in the same `SCREENS` group — drawn by `help_title`: it starts one session for every item on this card that waits on you, one at a time, and reads Open session while yours is running. On a card with none it keeps `card-start`.
- **The road** is unchanged on the wire: `{repo, id, card}` with no `item`, on the card's `start`. Only with `card` and no `item`, `api_ledger_start` counts the card's items afresh from the `queue` reading by the same rule (the card's `wants` it already builds, app.py ~3281–3284, counted as `items_n` is (at the press `card_mail` has no `cards`, so `with_of` is None and a *with you* item stays in `decide`/`do`: the total is the same) — its `card_wants(…, "", False)` call changes `on`, not the count; never the length of *waits on me*, which also lists answered entries; where the reading or `card_mail` fails, the `why_key` sentence), and where two or more count, `work_text` takes a new first sentence in place of the `why_key` one — a constant beside `WORK_ITEM` (say `WORK_ALL`): *I opened this session from the Queue to work through every item that waits on me on the card around {id} of {repo}'s ledger ({ledger}), one at a time: for each, read it, tell me its goal and what each answer leads to, and wait for my answer before the next.* `work.md` is unchanged; the id list and the closing sentences stand, but on this press *waits on me* (`waits_on_me`) leaves out an entry *answered — waiting on <asker>*. With fewer at the press, the `why_key` sentence, whatever the page drew. An item's press (`item`) keeps `WORK_ITEM`, and an Inbox row's press (no `card`) its own sentence.
- **The help:** `card-start` drops *It is drawn only where no item on the card asks you anything: each such item has its own.* and says it is drawn where no item asks anything; `card-start-all` is new in `HELP` and `SCREENS`, and §4.5a's *The help text (TD-157)* list gains its bullet in this build, word for word with `HELP` (its wording is the build's, never a design PR's).

**Done when** a card with two Decide or Do items draws the header's Start session beside both items' buttons, with `card-start-all`'s title; a card with two items whose entries another session holds counts 2 and follows the holder rule on `start` (no header button where it is held, its row *held by <name>*); a card with two *answered* items and nothing else draws the header's plain Start session, and its press opens on the `why_key` sentence; a card with one draws none in the header; a card with a red PR and one ledger item draws none; a header press on a two-item card opens a session whose prompt starts with the walk sentence and whose *waits on me* names both, a press when only one is left takes the `why_key` sentence, a press from a page drawn with one when two now count takes the walk sentence, and an Inbox row's press on such a card keeps its own; once the session runs every button on the card reads Open session; the help's doc-bound tests pass with the new `card-start`; and `pdm run test` passes.
