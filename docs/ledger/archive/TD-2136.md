**Done when:** With `/proc/meminfo`, the cgroup files and `/proc/<pid>` stubbed: two readings under 10% raise one row and one message naming the three largest holders, one reading does not, a restart neither repeats nor ends it, and the first reading over 20% clears it. A full swap with free RAM raises a `swap` row alone, and RAM turning low under it, snoozed or not, raises and tells its own row. A host with no swap reads RAM alone; a limit on the cgroup or a parent is the total. Holders sum other processes by name, group a live run under its record, fall back to `status` where `smaps_rollup` is unreadable, drop a pid whose start time changed, and mark a cut walk. A node that does not answer keeps its spell. No process is signalled.
A process started 5 s before a live record's `created` is grouped under it. `swap` ranks by `SwapPss` and `ram` by `Pss`, and no row is told before its turning walk ends or is cut. `ao disk` prints each half and *no memory reading*; a snoozed row is not told; an answer lacking `memory` keeps the spell. Recovery before the walk returns writes no *low*, a half that turned during a walk waits for the next, a failed or hung walk is `cut` with no holders and no second walk overlaps it, a half turning while a walk is stuck is written *low* with no holders, a reading between the lines keeps a *low* half and breaks a run of two, a restart forgets an unfinished count, a limit at or above `MemTotal` is no limit, so the machine and its swap are read, and a `memory` field without a half ends that half's spell.
