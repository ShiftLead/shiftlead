## TD-1568: A seat taken over and not handed back is easy to miss: colour Hand back and the session's card

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-10
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-12
**Id take:** 869af4072b3240afb70aac8fb8338b16

**Why:** A seat the person took over and never handed back stops the team's work, and nothing on the page says so. Paul took over ao-grind's fact-check seat (Gemini) to allow its permission prompts; afterwards it stayed interactive (`unattended: false`) with no mark calling it out, so the team's PR fact-checks did not run on their own (Paul, through `ao-led-tds-ledger`, m-1f43099fd62f, 2026-11-10).

What the design draws today is not enough to see it. §4.5 *The card's anatomy* item 2 marks every interactive record the same way: the *interactive* word with the `person` icon at full strength, which a worker taken over shares with the person's own sessions, and those are common on a team card (ao-grind's carries four). §4.5a *Hand back* is a plain bordered `btn sm` on the Focus acts line. The Inbox's *held · mail waiting* row (§4.5a) appears only once unread mail has waited half an hour. A seat that is answering nothing, or a fact-check seat whose work comes as PRs rather than mail, never shows it. The cost is a team that looks healthy while one of its stages has stopped.

Paul's ask: colour the **Hand back** button orange, and colour the session's card, and its tile on the team page, while a seat or member is taken over and not handed back.

**Location:** design §4.5 *The card's anatomy* item 2; §4.5a *Hand back*, *Take over*, *card: compact*, *held · mail waiting*; src/shiftlead/ui/templates (card, compact card, team card tiles, Focus acts line)
