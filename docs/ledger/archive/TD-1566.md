## TD-1566: An answer template like 'Hold: <note>' is recorded literally: the page asks for no note

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-10 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger, a person-session in ledgerboard; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Superseded 2026-11-12 by TD-1557
**Id take:** 54cc590ced6b4e3199f1dcf2c799ab6b
**Location:** src/sessionorc/ledgerboard/__init__.py (is_not_right_form, fill_not_right); src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py (the decide's answer check); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py and its templates (answer buttons); docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md (The answered item); design §4.4 Decide, §4.5a

**Why:** Asked by Paul through the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-d12867a61860). On the end-of-rounds item for TD-1470, whose `Answers:` were `One more round (default) | Go | Hold: <note>`, Paul pressed **Hold** meaning to give a note. The page asked for none: it recorded the answer template itself. #3256 (585ae65d5) wrote `Decided: Hold: <note> (by paul)` on the board line and a `kept waiting: decision (paul) — by paul; answer "Hold: <note>"` log line to TD-1470, TD-1496, TD-1497 and TD-1498, and the card asked the same question again. Paul then gave the note through **Answer**, which went by the reply road (#3257, TD-1555's path) and landed as a reply tail on the line (`— Paul, 2026-11-10: Hold: drop PRs from this version. …`), not as the decision.

The cause: the only answer the code reads as a form with a slot for the person's words is the live look's `Not right: <what>` (`ledgerboard.is_not_right_form`, `fill_not_right`; `agent_inbox`'s decide check; `ui/inbox.py`'s look buttons). Any other `<…>` in an offered answer is a plain button: pressed, it records the text as written. Yet the designer brief (`docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md`, *The end-of-rounds item*) offers `Hold: <note>` on every end-of-rounds item and tells the claimant to write *a Hold's note* into the design entry's `**Ask:**` — a note the decided line never holds. The harm: a decision recorded with no content, the person's actual words stranded in a reply tail a claimant may not read, and every held build logged with a placeholder as its answer.

**Fix:** - **An offered answer with a `<…>` slot is a form, not a button**, generalising `Not right: <what>`: pressed, it opens a text field for the slot (the slot's word as its label), refuses an empty one, and writes `Decided: Hold: <the person's words>`. Design §4.4 *Decide* and §4.5a's answer-button row say so; the vendored ledgerboard's form reading (`is_not_right_form`/`fill_not_right`) generalises to any `<label>: <slot>` answer — a change to ledgerboard's spec, filed upstream on its ledger if the rule belongs there.
- **The writer refuses a placeholder**: `agent_inbox`'s decide check and `board_edit.py`'s decide refuse a `Decided:` (and the ledger's `kept waiting … answer "…"` line) that still holds the literal `<…>`, so no front end can record the template.
- **A claimant reads the reply tail**: the designer brief's *The answered item* says that where a `Hold` line's decision carries no note, the note is the person's reply tail on the line. (TD-1470's own line is handled in this PR: its Log carries Paul's note.)
- Optional, decide in the round: the day on a ledger Log line is UTC while the board's is local (Paul saw 2026-11-11 against 2026-11-10); design §4.4 already accepts the slack for the hold's reading — say whether the Log should write the local day.

**Done when:** Done when pressing an answer whose text holds a `<…>` slot (e.g. `Hold: <note>`) opens a text field, an empty one is refused, and the line is written `Decided: Hold: <the words>` (a test pins it); a decide whose answer still holds the literal `<…>` is refused by the writer (a test pins it); and the designer brief says where a Hold's note is read.
