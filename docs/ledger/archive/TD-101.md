## TD-101: A one-day override of the usage reserves, gone at the reset

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-09-22 (the anchor session; split from TD-100 on Paul's word the same day)
**Owner:** paul
**Kind:** decision
**Status:** Resolved 2026-10-07. Open — **not wanted yet.** Paul, 2026-09-22: a mechanism for temporary reserve settings *seems brittle, and probably not the current priority; the user can handle it manually for now* — a separate entry *if we think the temporary change mechanic is worth doing*. What it would be, if it is: TD-100's standing reserves (a percent per session, a percent per day of the weekly window) left untouched while a person says *reserve 5 today*, and the override expires at the window's reset on its own — TD-026's *window overrides with an expiry* shape, one more row on TD-100's settings page. Until then a one-day change is two edits by hand: the reserve down, and back up after the reset. **Next:** not wanted yet (Paul, 2026-09-22).
**Blocked by:** decision (Paul) — whether the override is wanted at all (not wanted yet, 2026-09-22)
**Location:** design §6 (*Usage gate*), TD-100's settings page, TD-026's overrides

**Why:** on 2026-09-22 Paul wanted one day at 95% and the team back the next week at the standing line; with reserves that is a reserve of 5 for the last day, but 5 a day for the whole week moves every day's line up (65% on the reset day, not 30%). The honest form of a one-day change is an override that expires, not a new standing number — and building it is not worth its complexity until the standing gate (TD-100) exists and the by-hand way has hurt.

**Related:** TD-100 (the standing reserves and the settings page), TD-026 (overrides with an expiry).

**Resolved:** 2026-10-07. Paul: not wanted. The reserves are changed by hand on the Settings page (zeroed for the last hours of the week on 2026-10-06 and set back after the reset), and no override is needed.
