**Why:** TD-230's and TD-231's: a six-hour-old reading ran the account to within two points of its line unseen, because the one source was an endpoint that refuses for hours.

**Fix, in slices a PR each:**
1. **The age, first, since it needs no new source**: every reading's `at` reaches the chip and `ao gate`; the chip prints the age past five minutes, dims past `USAGE_FRESH`, reads *unknown since … (was n%)* past `USAGE_UNKNOWN`; the word *stale* goes from the chip (`ui/help.py` does not name it); the Settings page's Usage card shows age and source.
2. **The report**: `hooks_settings` sets `statusLine` to `agentorc-hook --statusline` with a refresh of sixty seconds; the command reads stdin, takes `rate_limits` through the adapter's `usage_report` (the percentage a float, the reset epoch seconds; `fresh` from the payload's running totals of API work, which the build names after reading a real payload), sends it as the `usage_report` RPC when a number or a reset changed or sixty seconds passed (a small file per session under the home holds the last sent), with a timeout of a second and no queue, then runs the status line it displaced (local, project, then profile settings; its padding kept) with the same stdin and prints its output, nothing when there is none; it never fails the status line: any error is swallowed and the chained command still runs. The host agent merges per account and window (the highest inside one `resets`, a later `resets` outright, `at` moved only by a fresh report or an answer) and keeps the newest reading and one per ten minutes for three hours; a node sends reports on to the home by a link method of its own, dropped when the link is down. A payload with no `rate_limits` (an old client, a metered profile, before the first response) reports nothing.
3. **The endpoint as the fallback**: `_refresh_usage` asks only for an account with a live session and no reading younger than `USAGE_FRESH`, at most once per `USAGE_FRESH`, and not for `USAGE_COOL` after `rate_limited`; the doubling back-off goes; it is asked by the host holding the profile's credentials; a window only the endpoint gives is asked for once an hour while a reserve names it or it stood within ten points of its cap; a window past its `resets` reads unknown and is never a cap for `limited`.
4. **The projection**: `usage.max_age` (`set_settings`, `ao gate --max-age`, the Settings field; `off` allowed); **one reader** gives a reading or a projection to the gate's pass and to `_profile_gated` alike; past `max_age`, for a profile with unattended sessions live and a window whose `resets` is ahead, it reads held + rate × age, at most 100, the rate between the oldest and newest kept readings inside two hours, never below zero and none under a span of twenty minutes; the gate pauses on a projection that reaches the line (a team's reserve priority counted), the mark and one `system` note saying so; an unknown window or no rate pauses nothing and notes *usage unknown*, once per account per day, never under `off`; a pause on a projection ends on a reading under the line, on the window's reset, or on `max_age` or a reserve moved.
5. **Tests**: a reading of 88% taken six hours ago with a rate of one point an hour and a line of 95% does not pause, and with two points an hour does; no history, or one spanning ten minutes, pauses nothing and files one note; a fresh reading under the line resumes and a projection never does; a pause on a projection lifts when the window's reset passes; a profile paused on a projection is not restarted by the keep-running pass; a report that is not fresh moves no age; two sessions reporting 61.5 and 60.0 inside one reset read 61.5; a project's own status line is the one chained when the profile names another; the status-line command with a person's own command prints that command's output and reports once for ten redraws inside a minute; a payload without `rate_limits` reports nothing and breaks nothing; after a 429 the endpoint is not asked for an hour; a report from a node reaches the home's chip; a window past its reset reads unknown.

**Done when:** through a busy evening the chip's reading is under fifteen minutes old with the endpoint asked at most a handful of times, a reading that does go old says its age on the chip and in `ao gate`, and the gate pauses the team on a projection rather than run the account past its line.
