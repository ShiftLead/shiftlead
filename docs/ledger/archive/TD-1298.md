## TD-1298: `stop_note`'s test adds two hours to a local time, so it fails whenever the two hours cross a DST change

**Priority:** high
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-01 (grinder-ao-2, from pr:2773's red precheck)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-01: the test adds its hours in UTC
**Location:** `tests/test_cli.py` `test_stop_note_reads_in_the_local_clock`
**Resolved:** 2026-11-01 (PR #2774) — `tests/test_cli.py` `test_stop_note_reads_in_the_local_clock`

**Why:** The test built its stop as `datetime.now().astimezone() + timedelta(hours=2)`, which keeps today's fixed UTC offset. At 01:36 MDT on 2026-11-01 (America/Denver, DST ending 02:00), it expected `03:36`, and `stop_note` rightly said `stops 02:36` (MST, the reader's clock two hours on). So every precheck run in the two hours before a DST change failed, pr:2773's among them. `stop_note` itself is right. The sibling of TD-1290, in a test this time.

**Fix:** add the two hours to `datetime.now(UTC)`, then `.astimezone()`.

**Done when** the test passes across a DST change: `TZ=America/Denver` at 01:4x MDT on 2026-11-01, where it had failed.

**Related:** TD-1290.
