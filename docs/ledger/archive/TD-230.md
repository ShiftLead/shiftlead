## TD-230: The usage chip shows a stale reading with no age, and the gate acts on it as if fresh

**Priority:** High
**Added:** 2026-09-28 (Paul: *our percentage used for the claude sub seems to be stale — our page reads 88%/95% but actual is 93% used*; then: *add how long ago the reading was made in the display*)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Blocked by:** TD-233
**Status:** Designed 2026-09-28 (the designer, PR #734; the steer to Paul is `m-3cb2968e75c3`, bound 2026-09-29 11:24 MDT): design §4.5a **usage** chip (*The age*), §6 *Usage gate* (*A reading the gate can no longer trust*), §5 `usage_gate.max_age`, §4.7 `ao gate`, the Settings page's Usage row. The Fix's three: (1) the age on the chip past five minutes, *unknown since* past three hours; (2) the gate projects from the rate it last saw and pauses on the projection, resuming only on a reading; (3) the Settings card says the age. Closes with TD-233. **Next:** designed; the build is TD-233.
**Location:** design §4.5a **usage** chip, §4.2 (*usage*: keeps the last good reading), §6 *Usage gate*; `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_refresh_usage`), `~/.agentorc/usage.json` (`fetched`), the gate (`ao gate`, `settings.lines`)

**Why:** on 2026-09-28 the chip read *week 88% · stale* while the account was at 93% (Paul, from the account's own page). The held reading's `fetched` was 22:21Z, and every poll since 22:26Z had been refused (TD-231), so by 04:27Z it was six hours old. The chip says *stale* but not how stale, so a person can't tell five minutes from six hours. The gate reads the same reading: with a 5/day week reserve and one day left its line was 95%, so it saw 88% and paused nobody. A stale reading can't show the gate a crossing, and the real account was two points from the line with four agents working and 19 hours to the reset. Paul accepted the risk for the night.

**Fix:** design (1) **the age on the chip**: *week 88% · 6 h ago* (and in `ao gate`'s line), with a threshold past which it reads plainly *unknown since 22:21* (the hover already gives when the reading was taken and why the poll failed, §4.5a; the new part is the age on the chip itself and in `ao gate`); (2) **the gate on a stale reading**: past a set age (e.g. an hour, a setting), the gate no longer treats the held reading as the truth. Either it projects it forward by the rate the account was rising at while readings were fresh (`usage.json` holds only the latest reading per profile, so a short history of readings would need keeping), or it pauses unattended work once the held reading plus a margin reaches the line, or it asks the person with an Inbox row, *usage unknown for 3 h; keep going?*. The round picks, maybe per window. (3) The Settings page's Usage card says the same age. Then the build. Done when a six-hour-old reading says so on the chip, and the gate does not run an account past its line on a reading it can't trust.

**Related:** TD-231 (why the readings stopped), TD-122 (one poll per account; its live check failed on 2026-09-28), TD-087 (the reason on a refused poll), TD-100 (the gate), TD-189 (context bounds; the same *measure, then act* shape).

**Resolved:** 2026-10-08 (designed; built as TD-233, archived the same day) — see TD-233.
