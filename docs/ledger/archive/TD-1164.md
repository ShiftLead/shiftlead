## TD-1164: A team's size: `teams.<team>.size` per member line over its `count:`, names that never move, read by `teams.plan` and the followable check, and `ao team size`

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-27 (the designer, from TD-1149's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved
**Part of:** TD-1149
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/org.py` (`MemberDef.names` 154); `src/shiftlead/teams.py` (`plan` 1028); `src/sessionorc/models.py` (`run_only` on the record, `HOME_OWNED`), `src/sessionorc/agent.py` (the `keep` act) and `src/sessionorc/settings.py` (`TEAM_KEYS` 359 and the teams validation ~493 — **held**: `src/sessionorc/**`, read by the techlead); `src/shiftlead/flowdefs.py` (`unfollowable` 338, the followable check, called from `teams.py` 415 and 646); `src/shiftlead/cli.py` (`ao team on-work` 2205 as the pattern; `ao team list`; `ao org check`); design §4.9 *A team's size*, §4.9c *Every listed flow must be followable*, §5 `teams.<team>.size`, §4.7 `ao team size`

**Why:** TD-1149 part 2: a team's member count is only its definition's `count:`, so a second grinder for a repo-defined team is a PR.

**Fix:** as design §4.9 *A team's size* says. (1) `teams.<team>.size: {<line name>: <int ≥ 0>}` accepted and validated by `set_settings` (a non-integer or negative refused); a key no member line names is ignored and `ao org check` names it. (2) `org.with_settings` (`org.py` 556) merges `size` onto `TeamDef.size` for every team, whatever flow is picked, and every reader of a team's names reads the merged definition (`session_names`, `next_name` 211, the twice-named check, `edit_members`, `teams_for_form`, the Members route, the card). `MemberDef.names(n=None)`: the definition's names first, then the next numbers — a `count: 1` line `[name, name-2, …]`, a counted line `name-1 … name-n` — and fewer than the count keeps the lowest-numbered. (3) `teams.plan` names each line by its size (absent: its count), `Plan.sit_out` and the `seen` duplicate check (`teams.py` 1142, 1144) taking the sized names; the followable check counts by the sizes — `staffed` (`teams.py` 401, 633) becomes a role staffed when its lines' sizes sum above 0, and `flow_needs` (752) refuses − on a line only where the role's other lines sum to 0. (4) `run_only: bool` on the record (`src/sessionorc/models.py`, default false), carried by launch records and replays; nothing else reads it until TD-1166. `TeamDef.next_free(line, records, from_=None)`: the first of the line's names at or past `from_` (default: past the size) that no current record of the team holds, a record closed `sit_out` or `run_ended` holding none (the create supersedes it); Add member's role-keyed `next_name` stays. `teamrun.start_member(call, org, team, line, *, run_only=False)` (a distinct name: `teamrun.add_member`, 1824, is the dialog's Add member and stays; the line's `next_free`, created as `ao team start` creates a member, `run_only` on the create's record and its launch record) and `teamrun.resize(call, org, team, line, n)` (the setting first, then one session at a time; walking the sized names, a step up creates the first sized name no current record holds, or, where a live `run_only` record holds it, **adopts** it — `run_only` cleared on the record and its launch record through a new host-agent act, `keep`, a member of `HOME_EDITS` (`agent_common.py` and `modes.py`) as `set_mode` is (`rpc_set_mode` in `agent.py` the pattern; `run_only` added to `HOME_OWNED`, `models.py`; on a node applied to the home's copy once the replica has taken it, refused while the link is down, §4.4a's table row) — and creates nothing; a failed step stops and says what was done, the setting stays); **+** on a line whose next name another line holds is refused (*grinder-4 is another line's: rename one of them in the definition*), nothing written. `ao team size <team> <line> <n>`, through `resize`: person-only (refused to a session, as `on-work`), refused for a team the org does not define, a line the definition does not name, below 0, and below what a listed flow's stages need; writes the setting and, on a live team, creates (as `ao team start` creates a member, under the manager, the current flow's lane, reader and stage brief; a line the flow does not use is counted, not started) or winds down by the sit-out form and marks sat out (highest-numbered first) one session at a time to match; prints *<line>: <size> (file says <count>)*. (5) `ao team list` and the team's settings line print a differing size the same way. (6) A lowered size on a team with nothing live: `teamrun.renames` reads the sized names, so the line's records above its size are removals the card's **definition changed — Apply** offers, and Apply or Start marks them sat out.

**Done when** tests pin: `names` for count 1 and 3 at sizes 0–5 (no name of the lower size changes at a higher one); `plan` at a size; the followable check refusing a size of 0 on a role a listed flow needs; `ao team size` on a stopped team (setting only) and on a live one (one created, one wound down and marked sat out), each refusal's words; `set_settings` refusing a non-integer and a negative size; `ao org check` on an unknown key; `ao team list` and the settings line printing *2 (file says 1)*; `next_free` past the size and from a given position, skipping a live record's name and reusing a `run_ended` one's (the create superseding it), and the twice-named check seeing a size; `resize` 1→3 on a live team creating the two sized names `-2` and `-3`; **+** refused where another line holds its next name; `resize` adopting a live run-only record that holds the next name (cleared on the record and its launch record through `keep`, nothing created), the `keep` act on a node member landing on the home's copy and refused with the link down, and a resize of more than one passing it; a size lowered on a stopped team read as a removal and its surplus record marked sat out by Apply and by Start; `run_only` carried by a launch record. `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass.

**Resolved:** 2026-10-28 (grinder-ao-2, #2568, #2569, #2571). Slice 2 (#2569) added `TeamDef.next_free`, `teamrun.resize` and `ao team size`, and made a lowered size read as a removal. Slice 3 (#2571) added `run_only` on the record and its launch record, `teamrun.start_member`, the home-owned `keep` act (`rpc_keep`), and resize adopting a live run-only member. Design §4.9 *A team's size* holds the rules.
