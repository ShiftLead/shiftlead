## TD-1395: An entry that waits on an until or event gate reads as waiting, not blocked: ShiftLead takes its waiting mark from the gates

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-05
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-05
**Id take:** c99fc6df05694769b9dbc809a1add617
**Part of:** TD-1392

**Why:** TD-1392's design: a wait is a gate in `Blocked by:` (`until YYYY-MM-DD`, `event (<name>)`), the form the pinned cadence writes (cadence §2.14), and the `**Waits:**` line is retired. ShiftLead's reader takes its `waiting` mark from the `Waits:` line alone (`ledger.waits_of`), and `kind_of` sorts any non-decision `Blocked by` item as *blocked*, so an entry written with a gate today reads *blocked* rather than *waiting*: its lane's member reads as out of work with nothing, the Queue card's leaf finds no wait, and `ao progress claim` refuses it with no *waits* word.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/ledger.py (`waits_of`, the entry reader, `kind_of`, `in_lanes`, `_unwaited`); src/sessionorc/settle.py (`mark_waits`, `wait_refusal`); src/sessionorc/queue.py (the `unreachable` pass, `_status`, `_wait`); src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (`wait_words`, `wait_line`); src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py (the `wait` and `wait_line` row keys, *blocked by …*), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html (the hover); tests/test_waits.py, tests/test_waits_words.py, tests/test_queue_waiting.py, tests/test_settle.py; design §4.4 *An entry says what it waits for*
