## TD-1281: A Queue card reads *needs you* while the person's own interactive session already works its entry

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Designed 2026-10-30 (the designer) — §4.4 *A row's state*, *The card's status*; §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants*: an `Owner: paul` or `decision (paul)` entry claimed by the person's own session reads *with you in <name>* and is no want — grey, counted nowhere, out of **Needs you (n)** — while mail and board lines about it still count. Built by TD-1284.
**Resolved:** 2026-10-30 — designed in #2743 (§4.4 *A row's state*, *The card's status*; §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants*); built by TD-1284 (PR #2744).
**Location:** design §4.4 *The card's status* (`docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md`), §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* and *Three verbs* (`docs/design/4.5-ui.md`), §4.5a rows *Queue page: card* and *Queue card: Start session / Open session*; `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_status`) and `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (the mail's *needs you*, the **Needs you (n)** filter, the page header's *Decide n · Do n*)

**Why:** Paul, 2026-10-30, with a screenshot of agent-cadence#TD-204's card: *I am currently working TD-204 in an interactive session, but it shows as needs-you in the Queue. It seems like we should indicate that this item is already being worked in an interactive session and drop the "Start Session" button (or change it to a Focus button).* The card sat In progress, held by his session `td204-design`, yet amber, with the *Decide 1* pill, and counted in the **Needs you (160)** filter and the header's *160 need you*. The design makes it so: each Decide or Do item makes the card's status *needs you* (§4.5 screen 12 *Three wants*), and a member that waits on the person (`decision (paul)`, `Owner: paul`) is *needs you* (§4.4 *The card's status*), whoever holds it. The harm: a card the person is already working competes for attention with the ones that truly wait on them, the **Needs you** number overstates what is owed, and the card does not say where the work is happening.

**Fix:** Design first, then build.
- **While the person's own session holds the entry an item asks about** (a record with `unattended: false` and no caller, the test §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs* already uses for Open session), the item is no longer owed. The card leaves *needs you* for it, is not amber for it, is out of the **Needs you (n)** filter, and the item leaves the header's *Decide · Do* counts. The designer settles what the item and the card read instead, for example *with you in td204-design* on the item and a status word that names the session.
- **Say where the work is**: the card, or the item, names the person's session, and **Open session** (already built) stays the way to it.
- **What else stays owed**: an item whose entry no session of the person's holds, and an attached `ask` or `steer` the person has not answered, still count. The designer says whether a mail item about a held entry counts too.
- **Done when** §4.4 and §4.5 screen 12 say it, with §4.5a's rows if a word changes, and a build lands it with a test: a card whose `decision (paul)` entry is held by the person's own session reads neither *needs you* nor amber, and is out of the filter's count.

**Related:** TD-1066 (Start session on every Decide and Do item; Open session with the person's session on the card), TD-912 (Start session on a card), TD-909 (the Queue card's three wants and three verbs).
