## TD-1565: An entry request to a repo lands on its fact-check seat when the techlead seat is unable, and waits there forever

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-10 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger, a person-session in ledgerboard; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-11
**Id take:** 95321e20b5f34195af55674c7a305ce5
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py (_question_seat, _one_seat, _reroute_waiting); src/shiftlead/briefs/fact-checker.md; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_idle_nudge); design §4.9b *A seat that cannot answer is routed around*, *A question already waiting*, §4.10 *A team or a repo as the address*

**Why:** Two `entry` requests from other repos' sessions sat on `ao-grind`'s fact-check seat (`ao-shiftlead-fact-check-ao-1`, Gemini) for days, unfiled and unanswered. One was m-e5f321db6cd6 (from `ao-led-tds-ledger`, 2026-11-05). The other was m-fef6a678f5b0 (from `ao-agent-cadence-ac-grinder`, 2026-11-07). The design says an entry is filed by a techlead alone (§4.9b *A seat that cannot answer is routed around*, *A handed entry*). But `_one_seat` in `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` ranks an *able* record first, and only then the techlead seat (`tags: true`). So when the techlead's profile was capped, the able fact-check seat won and the `entry` envelope landed there. Once there, an `entry` envelope is never moved: §4.9b *A question already waiting* keeps a session's own ledger request on its seat until that seat is able, and `_reroute_waiting` follows that. The fact-check seat is able, but it cannot file. Its brief answers only fact-check asks and reads only `--unread`. The seat read both asks on 2026-11-08 and has answered *no asks waiting* to every doorbell since.

The cost: the seat stays `seat_due` (`by: asks`) forever. It is filled again on every tick that allows it, and that defeated a Stop (TD-1564). Since 2026-11-09 16:14 rule 4 has sent it *idle with work open — nudged* every 20 minutes, a turn each time, while the account was past its weekly limit. Meanwhile the two entries were never filed.

**Resolved:** 2026-11-11 (PR #3263)

**Done when:** Done when `ao td add --repo shiftlead`, sent while `ao-grind`'s techlead seat is unable and its fact-check seat is able, lands on the techlead seat (a test pins it). An `entry` envelope found on a fact-check seat is moved to the techlead seat on the next tick. A fact-check seat given a non-fact-check ask replies and closes it rather than holding it.
