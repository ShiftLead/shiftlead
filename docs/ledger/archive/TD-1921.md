## TD-1921: Sessions leave scratch data, worktrees and caches nothing cleans up, and they filled the machine's disk

**Why:** Agent sessions leave scratch data nothing reaps, and on 2026-11-17 it helped fill kmaster's root disk (118G, 83%) until the kubelet evicted pods under DiskPressure (samscrape TD-320). Measured that day by Paul's samscrape session: about 24 GB of session leftovers, growing about 10 GB a month — `/tmp/claude-1000` 7.5G (1,466 per-session scratch dirs; grinder-ao-1 and -2 alone 4.7G), worktrees about 6G (shiftlead 51 = 2.2G, contractmatch 8 = 2.5G, samscrape 23 = 1.2G), `~/.shiftlead/runs` 2.1G (1,688 runs, 0.6G older than 14 days), and `~/.cache/samscrape-precheck` with `~/.cache/agent-cadence` 1.2G. What is pruned today is narrow: run logs and attachments of ended sessions after `runs_keep_days` (30, design §4.6 *Run-log retention*), and a worktree only when the closing record's own start made it (§4.1 *A close reaps the worktree it made*, TD-978). The tool's scratch dirs, worktrees a session made itself, and precheck caches have no owner and no schedule.

**Ask:** The disk clean-up design ran a fifth review round, which closed two gaps; should a session run one more round before the build?

**Context:** Round 5, the round your One more round bought, read the three fixes left unread after round 4 and found them sound. It found two new gaps that change the build: a live session's scratch skipped its git folders when refreshing them, so the system's ten-day temp clean-up could delete a long-running seat's clone, and *landed* left two of the reap script's answers unplaced. Both are fixed, and no review has read those fixes.
