**Why:** TD-1303's design: §4.10 *A look* (*A look's shots are the pushed commit's*), §4.7 `--shot`, §4.4a *A look's screenshots on another host*, §4.5a rows *Inbox row: a look* and *Send to reviewer*, §4.9b *Send to reviewer*.

**Fix:** as the design says:
- `ao msg --shot`: read `HEAD` and its origin branch (the upstream's name where it is an `origin/*` ref that contains `HEAD`, else the local branch's) in the shots' repo's checkout; refuse *<name> is not committed at HEAD: commit it and push, then send* when `HEAD:docs/mockups/reviews/<name>` does not exist, *<name> differs from HEAD: commit it and push, then send* when `git diff --quiet HEAD -- <path>` fails, and *HEAD is not pushed: push <branch>, or fetch if it was merged, then send* (on a detached `HEAD`, *HEAD is not pushed: check out a branch and push it, then send*) when no `refs/remotes/origin/*` contains `HEAD`; else send `shots` with `shots_at` and `shots_ref`. A detached `HEAD` an `origin` ref contains is sent with an empty `shots_ref`. A `HEAD` no `origin` ref contains, whose every shot `origin/<default>` holds as the same blob (a look after the squash), is sent with `origin/<default>`'s sha and an empty `shots_ref`, not refused.
- The envelope keeps `shots_at` (dropped unless 40 hex) and `shots_ref` (dropped unless it matches `[A-Za-z0-9._/-]{1,200}` with no `..`, no leading `-` and never `HEAD`); every hop re-matches both.
- Every reader (the page's route, `host_shot`, the link's `shot`) takes `at` and `ref` and draws the first that holds the file: `<at>:…` while an `origin` ref contains `at` (an object the checkout lacks reads as not contained), else `origin/<ref>:…`, else `origin/<default>:…`, else absent. A miss on the first step with a `shots_ref` set, in the row's presence test only, before the second step is read (`look_shots`, from the stored entry), fetches `+refs/heads/<ref>:refs/remotes/origin/<ref>`, `--no-prune`, as the background-fetch bullet below says; the image route never fetches. The name and size rules, the four-shot cap and *never the working tree* unchanged.
- The presence cache keys on `at` and `ref` too; after a fetch the three are read again and containment asked afresh. With no `shots_ref` the branch step and the fetch are skipped (and `--shot` on a detached `HEAD` says *check out a branch and push it*).
- A node fetches only on a `head` read. Every reading returns `from` (`commit|ref|default`), and the route sets `max-age=300` or `no-cache` from it, `?host=` too. A node's `shot` reply echoes the request's `at` verbatim; where the request carried `at`, a reply without it reads as a node that cannot be asked (the name alone), in the presence test and the route.
- `rpc_host_shot(host, repo, name, at, ref, head)` and `shots.reading(repo, name, at, ref, head)`; the reply is `{host, repo, name, at, from, exists, png}`. `ref` is read only with `at`: a lone `ref` is ignored.
- `src/shiftlead/briefs/techlead.md` *A look handed to you*: its lead names a designer's look before the merge beside a builder's after it, and the read order (§4.9b).
- The branch fetch runs in the background, at most 30 s, one at a time per `(repo, ref)`. The reader keeps `fetched` per `(repo, ref, at)` (set, when a fetch of the branch ends, good or failed, for every `at` read with that `ref`, hit or miss, before the fetch began; every presence read records its `at`, hit or miss; an `at` not read for two `SHOT_TTL`s leaves the set, on a node as at home; empty after a restart) and `last_end` per `(repo, ref)` (bars another fetch for `SHOT_TTL`). A first-step miss with `fetched` unset starts the fetch if none runs and `last_end` allows, and every such read until one ends answers a miss at once; with `fetched` set it reads the branch and default steps as held and starts a re-fetch in the background once `last_end` is older than `SHOT_TTL`. A fetch's end, good or failed, drops at once every presence answer `(repo, name, at, ref)` and containment answer `(repo, at, ref)` of its `(repo, ref)`. A node's `head` reply never waits on it, so `SHOT_WAIT` and `_shot_host_down` (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`) are unchanged in meaning: a slow fetch is never a host down.
- `rpc_host_shot` (`agent_remote.py` ~317) passes the node's `at` and `from` through, never filling `at` from its own request; a reply without `from` reads as `default`.
- `--shot`'s three checks run only on a file in a checkout; a non-file path is refused by the host agent as before.
- The host agent's `msg` cleans `shots_at` and `shots_ref` and checks no containment.
- **Send to reviewer** hands `shots_at` and `shots_ref` with `shots`; the techlead brief's *A look handed to you* reads each image in the same order.
- The *gone* chip's title reads *not available from the sender's repo*.

**Done when** a look sent from an unmerged, pushed branch draws its images in the Inbox row, a node's too; a look whose branch was rebased after the send draws the branch's file once the branch is fetched again; a look with no `shots_at`, and one whose branch was deleted and pruned after a squash merge, draw as today; a look pushed after the reading checkout last fetched draws after its one-branch fetch; a builder's look sent from its branch after the squash merge is accepted and draws the merged file; no fetch of a branch, failed or good, is repeated within `SHOT_TTL`; the Inbox render never waits on a branch fetch (a look pushed since the last fetch draws its name on the first poll and its image on the next), two looks on one branch both redraw on the home's own checkout when its one fetch ends (a node's within two `SHOT_TTL`s), a rebased look's branch is fetched at most once a `SHOT_TTL` and its image never blinks to its name on a re-fetch, at home or on a node, a failed fetch falls to the branch's kept ref then the default's file, and a node fetching is never read as down; a lone `ref` with no `at` reads `origin/<default>` alone; an uncommitted shot, one that differs from `HEAD` and an unpushed `HEAD` are each refused with their line; the image route makes no fetch; an old node's reply without `at` draws the name alone; a malformed non-empty `at` or `ref` on the route is a 404 and an empty `ref` is no branch step; a re-sent look whose same-named shot changed draws the new file though the reading checkout's branch ref was stale; a node's look reads its commit over the link; and `pdm run test` passes.
