## TD-1751: The gate sweep reads an entry's earlier closed until lift as this gate's, and files a false act line instead of lifting

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-13
**Id take:** 1c332258755142b580b7d322abb32d83

**Why:** `gatesweep.lift_branch` gives every `until` gate the same lift branch, `lift/TD-n/until`, while each event gate gets a name of its own. `_lift` reads any PR ever opened on that branch. So once one lift of an entry's `until` is closed unmerged, every later `until` gate on that entry finds the closed PR. The sweep then files an act line, *Lift of until <the new date> on TD-n was closed*, instead of lifting the gate. The line names a gate no lift was ever opened for.

Seen on 2026-11-13: sweep PR #3624 filed this line for TD-1648's `until 2026-11-13`. The only closed lift of TD-1648 was #3507, a lift of `until 2026-11-12`. #3624 was closed unmerged as a false alarm. Every entry a grinder re-gates `until` a later day (a live check's or a re-check's look) is open to this.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/gatesweep.py (lift_branch, _lift); agent-cadence scripts/gate_sweep.py

**Resolved:** 2026-11-13 (PR #3648)

**Done when:** A reached `until` gate on an entry whose earlier `until` lift was closed unmerged gets a lift PR, not an act line. A test holds it.
