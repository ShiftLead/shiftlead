**Why:** Part of TD-2080: every session on a host shares one temporary space, so an unattended seat's test runs can stop the person's sessions and the machine (design §4.6 *Temporary space is a pool of its own*).

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that gives unattended seats their own temporary space and clears it before it fills?

**Context:** Temporary files have repeatedly filled the disk every session shares. This design moves unattended seats to a pool of their own, clears what can be rebuilt when it runs low, and warns you before it is full. Its review rounds ended with nothing left that changes the builds.

**Location:** design §4.6 *Temporary space is a pool of its own*; `src/sessionorc/agent.py`, `agent_tick.py`, `scratch.py`, `hosts.py`, `diskbook.py`, `src/shiftlead/cli.py`, `src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py`

**Resolved:** 2026-11-23 (PR #4397)
