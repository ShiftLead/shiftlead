**Why:** Audit of #4723 (TD-2247). That PR holds the home's late **Go** press to three cases, all on the entry's own repo's board. `_gate_pair` (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`) also hands `queue.gate_pair` `refs_open=linked`, the index through which a line's qualified ref (`owner/repo#TD-n`) reaches an entry of another repo, so a line on another repo's board covers it (design §4.4 *The card's status*; §4.5 screen 6 *The ledger's entries that wait on you* offers the pair only where no open line covers the entry).

With `refs_open=linked,` removed alone from that call (`boards=boards` kept), the 14 test modules that mention `ledger_decide` or `gate_pair` passed at b6f5c8850 (291 passed), and tests/test_gate_pair.py passed at 28715c324 (15 passed). TD-2247's *Done when* named `boards=` with `refs_open=` together, and removing both does fail its `covered` case.

Probe (a temporary test: the home's readings set to repo `repo` with remote `git@github.com:own/repo.git` and entry TD-190 gated on the person with an Ask and no Answers, and repo `other` whose board holds an open `decide` line with `refs: ["own/repo#TD-190"]`): the unchanged `_gate_pair` says TD-190 is not offered the pair; with the argument removed it says it is, so a **Go** from a page drawn before the line would be taken and would lift the gate while the question is asked on the other board.

With it removed together with the other changes that TD-2251 to TD-2255 name, the whole suite at b6f5c8850 (`pytest -n 4`) passed (10259 passed, 24 skipped).
