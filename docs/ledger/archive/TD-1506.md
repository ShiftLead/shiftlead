## TD-1506: Two attachment sweep tests fail under the full suite: a parked tick's to_thread sweep keeps running

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-07
**Id take:** ec163eff3a7f4d95b4c378b6e2d8e80f
**Location:** tests/test_attach.py, tests/test_attachment_life.py, tests/conftest.py (park_ticks), src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py

**Why:** Two attachment tests failed together on one xdist worker (gw1) in #3128's precheck (2026-11-06, f02facd, a diff that touches only `tests/test_push_send.py`), and both pass alone:
- `tests/test_attach.py::test_the_sweep_takes_an_hour_idle_part_whatever_runs_keep_days` failed with `FileNotFoundError` on its idle `.part`.
- `tests/test_attachment_life.py::test_runs_keep_days_0_deletes_no_attachment` failed because `ao-gone-4/old.png` was gone after a sweep with `runs_keep_days: 0`.

The likely cause is that the tick runs the sweep as `await asyncio.to_thread(self._prune_runs, …)` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py:320`). Cancelling the tick does not stop that thread: `park_ticks` cancels the ticker and drains the `*_task` passes, but a `to_thread` sweep the tick had started keeps running. If its directory walk lands after the test writes its files, it deletes them under the default retention and an empty live set. `test_the_sweep_takes_an_hour_idle_part_whatever_runs_keep_days` does not park the ticks at all, so its own tick's sweep races it directly.

**Resolved:** 2026-11-07 (PR #3147)

**Done when:** Both tests hold under the full suite: once ticks are parked, no sweep runs that the test did not start.

- 2026-11-07: archived: Resolved — the sweep's thread is the _prune_task pass that park_ticks drains; the idle-part test parks its ticks
