**Why:** The person cannot see what the disk sweep frees or keeps, so a disk that fills again looks like the sweep's failure or nobody's (TD-1921). Design §4.6 *The sweep's reading* and §4.7 `ao disk` show it per host, before a pass and after it.

**Ask:** The disk clean-up design ran a fifth review round, which closed two gaps; should a session run one more round before the build?

**Location:** design §4.6 *The sweep's reading*, §4.7, §4.4a (the `act` link method); src/sessionorc/, src/shiftlead/cli.py

**Resolved:** 2026-11-19 (PR #4194) — src/sessionorc/diskbook.py, agent_tick.rpc_disk / rpc_disk_dry_run, tests/test_disk_reading.py

**Done when:** In a scratch home, after a sweep `ao disk --json` prints each kind's bytes held, removed in the pass and in 30 days (a pass dated 31 days back left out), each kept item's why and `scratch_root`'s free space; with that free space under a tenth, of bytes or (a separate case) of inodes, by a test's stubbed reading, `ao disk` prints the warning, and a stubbed `f_files` of 0 draws none; `ao disk --host <node> --json`, and a node's own `ao disk --json`, print the same `{host: entry}` shape, one entry; after a host-agent restart the bytes held read *partial* until each item is measured; a node's `ao disk --host` naming another host is refused; `ao disk --dry-run` lists what a pass would remove, is refused while a pass runs, and leaves `disk.json` and the worktree pass's place unchanged; with two nodes each stubbed to take 200 s, `ao disk --json` prints one entry per host in under 400 s; with one stopped, its entry is an error reading *unreachable* while the other answers; a node's dry run refused as *a pass runs* is that host's entry while the others answer; a node's `ao disk --dry-run` whose pass is stubbed to take 200 s answers at the home (past `ACT_TIMEOUT`), and one stubbed past `SWEEP_DRY_S` answers *partial* inside it; a session's `ao disk` on a node whose home cannot be reached is not refused by the offline gate (`modes.offline_refusal`).
