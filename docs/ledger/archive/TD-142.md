**Why:** every other row's act goes back to a session with a press; a board line's goes back through a text editor and a wait for the next session, and Paul asked for the press (TD-126).

**Fix:** two slices; both touch `src/sessionorc/**`, so the techlead reads them (§4.9b).
1. **The file half and the row**, landable now: the `reply` edit and `board_reply` writing on the board and committing; the row's **Reply** and its dialog; the result and trail *written on the board*; no mail, no standing yet. `board_reply` refuses as `board_edit` does (the line must still hold the text; the checkout must take the commit).
2. **The mail half and the standing**, after dev-cadence's reader carries `session`, `host` and `refs` per item and prints a line's ` — Paul, <date>:` tails whole after the head it clips at 200 characters (`MAX_ITEM_CHARS`) in the SessionStart line — both asked on the board 2026-09-24; until the second, the reply reaches the next session through the Inbox row only: the `handed` note to each live lease holder; the standing word from the records — *still on <ref> — <session> holds it* when a live record holds an unexpired declared lease on a ref, *moved on* when a live record carries the item's `session` name and none holds a lease, *gone* otherwise; the result and trail *written on the board · sent to <session> (holds <ref>)*. Until then the row draws no standing and Reply's result says *written on the board — no session standing is known: the board reader carries no session fields yet*.
