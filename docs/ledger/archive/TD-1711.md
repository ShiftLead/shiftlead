**Why:** A design's cost is the author's own turns, above all the turns that apply each review round's fixes on Fable at 140k to 300k tokens of context; the reviewers are a few percent of it. The analysis of 3,120 findings from 41 review records (docs/decisions/2026-11-11-design-review-findings.md) says where the rounds' work goes: 16% of findings need no model, 13% are a false claim about what exists (two thirds of them found after round 1, and at least 177 of the 394 in text a fix had changed, since a later round's fact-check reads only what changed), 37% are detail a builder would meet anyway, and 25% are the hard situations (two actors at once, interaction with another rule, restarts, endings, timing, actors, error paths) a third of which only a design review or live use would catch; a quarter of all findings follow from an earlier fix. Paul holds two Fable limit resets that lapse in March and wants the Fable allotment spent on what needs Fable (Paul, 2026-11-11, session fabplan: *I think this is the right shape*).
