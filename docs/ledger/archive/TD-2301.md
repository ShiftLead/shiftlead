**Why:** Test audit of #4790 (TD-2069 slice 2). `_login_moves` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`) reads each login with its team's extra reserve and projected usage, passes over a login that asks for the director and for a prompts seat, and runs in the tick before the usage gate, so that a session at its line is moved first and paused only where nothing is below it (design §6 *Usage gate*, *After the cut-over*).

Each change below, made alone, left tests/test_login_move.py green (27 passed at ff1c0ef8d; the two halves of the third at 22f1e01c9, 25 passed):

- `accounts = self._projected(gate["accounts"], doc, now)` made `gate["accounts"]`.
- `extra = settings_mod.team_extra(doc, team) if team else 0` made `0`.
- `unasked = _director(s) or s.builtin == "prompts"` made `False`, and each half removed alone.
- `await self._login_moves(snapshot_at)` moved below `_enforce_usage_gate` in the tick.

The tests replace `teamstore.login_rows` with a fixed table, so nothing the move hands it is read, and none runs the tick. `next_login`'s own `unasked` is tested; the tick's choice of it is not. Probe (a temporary test, removed after): unchanged, `login_rows` is handed the projected accounts and the team's extra, and a prompts seat passes over an asking second login to the third; with the first three changed, it is handed the raw accounts and 0, and the seat moves to the asking login. The PR's fact-check ran one tick at c225ebf6b with the two calls swapped and the gate's readings patched: the gate typed its pause line into the old session, and the move followed in the same tick.

With these (the third as `False`), the changes of the entries filed beside this one and a few more this audit filed nothing on, made together at ff1c0ef8d, the whole suite passed (10475 passed, 26 skipped).

A break would move a session late or not at all under a team's reserve, put the director or a prompts seat on a login that stops at its prompts, or type the pause line into a session just before moving it.
