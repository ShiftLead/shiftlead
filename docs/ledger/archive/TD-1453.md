**Why:** Asked by Paul through the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-6b440cdfdbb0): review what reaches the person where and when, across the Inbox, the Queue and the board.

Seen 2026-11-06. agent-cadence's grinder asked *TD-221: merge #164?* (m-ab094b32f47e, about TD-221), and the ask showed only in the Inbox, with no Queue card. Earlier the same day, shiftlead's *merge #2996?* ask on TD-1388 slice 1 did show on its Queue card. So the person sees merge asks in two places, depending on the entry's state. Paul expected the Queue.

The rule as built: `item_home` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py:46`; §4.5 screen 6 *Where a row is drawn*, screen 12) puts a question, steer or note on the Queue only when its `about` (or the first `TD-n` in its text) names an entry that an open card holds (`card_index`, `src/sessionorc/queue.py:81`). Everything else goes to the Inbox: *an entry on a completed card or found nowhere*. Cards come from every open entry of every registered repo, ranked or not (`reading()`, `src/sessionorc/queue.py`). `rank.yml` only orders them, so an unranked entry in flight does get a card. A merge ask is about a PR, and that PR often archives the very entry it names. #164 archived agent-cadence's TD-221 (`docs/ledger/archive/TD-221.md`, b4c5f87). So the ask is about an entry the reading sees as closed once the archive is read, or on a card the PR is about to complete, and design §4.4 *The card's status* makes *needs you* an attached question.

Corrections to the first report: `ao rank --list` lists cards, not entries (each line ends *· N entries*), and shows every repo's cards unless `--repo` is given (`src/shiftlead/cli.py:3664`). The *6* was one card's count. Ledgerboard's TD-053 and TD-046 are archived on its main, so its *no open cards* is right.

A second finding, from the same session (m-f38709d10cfa, 2026-11-06): most of Paul's 14 open asks were stale (the session counted 13; the asks it named come to 14). Eleven were merge asks whose PR had since merged:
- dev-cadence #429, #451, #452, #455, #457;

Two were contractmatch TD-062's private-email asks, resolved since #250 merged, and one was TD-959's token ask (TD-959 dropped 2026-11-04). Several of the asking sessions are forgotten. `_asker_gone` (`src/sessionorc/agent_attention.py`) closes such a session's asks to the person as `asker_gone`, but not an ask whose `about` is a PR or a `TD-n` (`reference_of`, `models.py`). That one is kept open as an orphan that outlives its asker (TD-213), so it reads *its asker is gone — it waits until you answer it* and nothing ever closes it. No `closed_reason` is *merged*, and no pass closes an ask when the PR it carries merges. They sat in *Needs you* for up to 9 days. A mail entry already carries the PR it is about (`pr`, `src/sessionorc/models.py`).
