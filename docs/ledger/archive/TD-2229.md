**Why:** Audit of #4672 (round 5 of TD-2182's design). `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` holds a rule written as temporary: "**A fix no review has read recommends One more round** (agent-cadence#TD-243, until a pinned §4.8 says it, when this line goes): while any build-changing fix is unread by a review … the end-of-rounds item recommends `One more round`, never `Go`". TD-1490 added it (#3124, merged 2026-11-06 local).

The pinned cadence says it now. agent-cadence#TD-243 is archived `Resolved 2026-11-18`: "no build-changing fix ends the rounds unread — a closing read reads it — and the item recommends the counted verdict; written into cadence.md §4.8 by TD-287". This repo's pin first carried that commit with #4106 (c7fb0c1ac, 2026-11-18), and the line stayed. §4.8 as pinned says the item's recommendation is "the counted verdict, never the author's (after a bought round: `Go` on a counted `Go`, `Hold` on a counted `Hold`, `One more round` on a counted `Closing read`)", reached through `review_stats.py --verdict`, a closing read and a judge, and it has the record carry `Reach:`, `Go by:`, `Holds:` and `Bought:` lines. The brief names none of the verdict, the closing read or the judge, and its closing-fact-check bullet repeats *recommend One more round*.

Where #4672 shows it: `agent-cadence run review_stats.py --verdict TD-2182` on that PR's record prints `build-changing per round (design reviews / fact-checks): 1: 10/1, 2: 4/0, 3: 1/0, 4: 1/0, 5: 8/0`, `last 3 rounds not falling: yes`, `verdict: Hold: split or narrow` (the same with a `Bought: 4` line added to a scratch copy). The board item that PR raised, `docs/user_attention/2026-11-25-8805257a.md`, answers `One more round (default)`, where after a bought round §4.8 recommends the counted `Hold`; after a bought round it runs neither a closing read nor a judge. The same PR departs in ways the line does not by itself cause: the item shows the person neither the counted verdict nor the per-round counts (its context says only that round 5 found more than round 4) and names its five unread fixes in words, not by finding id (F54 to F58), which the brief asks for; `docs/reviews/TD-2182.md` has no `Reach:`, `Go by:`, `Holds:` or `Bought:` line (the verdict warns `no Reach: line — read as far-reaching`).

It began a round earlier: on #4597's record (672404e73, round 4 ended) the same command prints `unread build-changing: F43`, `verdict: Closing read`, `next: closing-read`; an item recommending `One more round` was raised in its place, and the person bought round 5 from it.

The harm: the person is asked to buy a whole round where a closing read was the next step, and is later recommended another on a design the shared count says to split or narrow, without being told that the two differ.
