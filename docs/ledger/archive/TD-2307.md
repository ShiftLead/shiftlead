## TD-2307: Two grinders' claims on one entry were both granted, so the same work was built twice

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Dropped 2026-11-26
**Id take:** 48d065e09eee4f8bb8438a4a774f25e5

**Why:** Two grinders of ao-grind each claimed TD-2300 at about 11:33Z on 2026-11-26 and both claims were granted: ao-shiftlead-grinder-ao-2's (merged as #4816) and ao-shiftlead-grinder-ao-1's (#4815, closed unmerged once #4816 merged, a full build and review spent twice). Design §4.8 says a claim is a lease, refused while a live session already holds the reference, and `_lease_holder` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py`) says two claims a moment apart get one grant and one refusal.

At 11:45Z `ao status --json` showed grinder-ao-1's TD-2300 entry with `source: "derived"` (`at` 11:45:12Z, pr 4815), while `_lease_holder` counts only `declared` claims. Two readings fit and neither was checked: the two RPCs interleaved at an await between `_lease_holder` and the write of the claim, or a declared claim was later rewritten as derived (by the branch or PR derivation), which design §9 invariant 10 says never happens and which would take the lease away from a live holder.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent.py rpc_progress, _lease_holder

**Fix:** Reproduce first: in a test, two members claim one ref through `rpc_progress` concurrently (`asyncio.gather`), and separately a member claims, then the derivation that reads its branch and PR runs, and a second member claims. Whichever lets the second claim through is the bug: make the lease's read and the claim's write one step with no await between them, or keep a declared claim's `source` as `declared` when derivation touches the entry. Say in design §4.8 what holds if neither is the cause.

**Done when:** A test shows a second member's claim on a ref a live member has claimed is refused, both when the claims are concurrent and after derivation has run on the first claimer's branch or PR.
