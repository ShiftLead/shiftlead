## TD-1362: The Repo page's *For you* and the Inbox's *Later* draw a held entry's own row beside the open board line that asks its question

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-04
**Resolved:** 2026-11-04 — #2894 (grinder-ao-2): `queue.split_later` leaves out a *Later* row that an open Decide or Do line the Inbox draws (due or not due yet) names in its `Context:` (`queue.asked_entries`, a bare `TD-n` read as the line's own repo's). The Repo page's *For you* now also draws a line not yet due in the row's place, with its due words, and takes it off *Not due yet*; it already drew a due one there. The tests are `tests/test_ui_held_board_line.py`; the UI check on a scratch home is in the PR.

**Why:** #2892 (TD-1355) made the Queue card draw an open Decide or Do board line in place of the held entry its `Context:` names (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs*), and qualified the line's bare `TD-n` with its own repo. Two other places still draw the entry's own row with **Lift the hold** / **Answer…** beside the line, so the person can answer the wrong one. One is the Repo page's *For you*, which design §4.5 says draws the line "in the row's place, as the Queue does". The other is the Inbox's *Later*, for an entry on no open card. This is how Paul's answer to ledgerboard#TD-023 landed as a new board item (ledgerboard#116).

**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`ledger_for_you`: its *linked* set leaves out only an entry an open **decided** line names; the Inbox's *Later* rows that `split_later` keeps when no open card holds the entry); the Repo page's *For you* rows (`repo_page` in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py`).

**Fix:** in both places, leave out a `for-you` entry's row where an open Decide or Do board line of the same repo names it in its `Context:` (`sessionorc.queue.refs`, a bare `TD-n` read as the board's own repo, as `queue.item_home` now does), and draw the line in its place. Done: a test for each page.
