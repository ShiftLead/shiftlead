**Why:** Docs audit of #4803 (TD-2070 slice 3). #4803 added the `login` to a node's `spend` link method and wrote it into §4.4a and the module docstring of `src/sessionorc/agent_spend.py`, but not into §4.4, the section §4.4a sends the reader to for this method.

Design §4.4 *A metered account's reading is a sum, not a poll*: "the node reads its own transcripts and sends them home as `spend {account, profile, prices, turns, cursors}`, a link method with a reply".

Design §4.4a: "the node's turns travel the other way as `spend {account, profile, login, prices, turns, cursors}`", the `login` the `<tool>:<name>` the profile is in a switched node's `logins.yml`, "kept at the home beside the profile under that host". The code agrees with §4.4a: `_spend_send` adds `head["login"]` where the node has one, and `_take_spend` keeps it in `spend.json` as `hosts.<host>.logins.<profile>`. §4.4's account of what `spend.json` holds ("one row per account per day holding tokens by kind and cost, and the cursors") does not mention it either.

The docstring of `_spend_to_home` in the same module still lists the old fields too (read at 7ff76f1ae).
