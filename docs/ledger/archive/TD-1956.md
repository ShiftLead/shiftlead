## TD-1956: With no question seat, a member's questions land on the person: the director answers them under an answer delegation

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-18
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Dropped 2026-11-25
**Id take:** ba55420f962a46c8906397992f2b5c22
**Blocked by:** TD-1954, decision (paul)

**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-18, in TD-1953's frame (docs/decisions/2026-11-18-tools-flows-teams.md): when a team's flow names no question seat, a member's questions land on the person, which *seems contrary to the use of a director (to take load off of the user)*; then, to keep the frame simple, *drop the director from this discussion for now and pick it up in another TD*.

Today a member with no techlead asks the person directly (`{techlead}` reads `none`, design §4.9b). Under `director.work` the director already reads the person's open questions and recommends on each (TD-1910, §4.9b *The director works for the person*), but the person still answers.

**Location:** design §4.9b (the director), §4.10 (reply edges), §5 director.delegate, §6 rule 3

**Ask:** Will you design with a session how the director answers a member's question when its team's flow has no question seat?

**Fix:** A new `answer` delegation in `director.delegate` (§5, §4.7, under §4.8's `work` grant) lets the director answer such a question itself, from the person's preferences, instead of only recommending. What the frame's review rounds 3 and 4 found (docs/reviews/TD-1953.md, F23–F26 and F33–F37) is this entry's starting list:

- **No new mail road into the director.** It holds no team badge and no `asks` trigger (§4.10), a direct send would be bounded by nobody's count, and a `{techlead}` naming it would go stale.
- **A new reply edge** (§4.10): a session replies today only to an ask in its own inbox. The director's reply to a person-inbox question goes to the asker and closes it with a reason of its own.
- **Bounds**, checked by the host agent at the reply: the asker's team has no question-marked seat; the question carries no `--lift` and no `passes`; the sender is not the director.
- **The floor carve-out.** §4.9b's floor sends *a question addressed to the person by name* to the person at every level. It is carved out only for a question that reached the person for want of a question seat; the rest of the floor holds.
- **Timing.** A watch fill comes hourly at best, and a `steer` lapses first. Recommended: such a question makes the director due at once (§6 rule 3).
- **Off by default.** `answer`, like `work`, is off until the person turns it on (§5 `director.delegate` is `[]`).
- **Telling.** Each answer is an `act` record under `director.review` and the digest.
- **Answer quality.** The director answers from preferences, not from the design and the ledger, so a flow that wants real work answers names a question seat.

Its build entries carry `Part of: TD-1956` (cadence §2.4).

**Done when:** the design round with Paul has written the director's answer delegation and filed its builds.
