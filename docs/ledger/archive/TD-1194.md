**Why:** TD-1189's design, agreed with Paul on 2026-10-28 from the mockups (shape A and the completed face): a team's debt and PRs sit on its own card beside what it holds, and what it just finished stays on the card after the claim closes.

**Fix:** as the §4.5a rows say.
1. **The facet on the card.** On the Sessions page every team card that draws a summary draws the Repo facet first: Technical debt and Pull requests, no name row (as `on_line`), its lanes *i* the team's (`team_lanes`, which `team_summary` already computes). `team_groups` stops dropping `repo` from `drawn` on the Sessions page and keeps dropping it on the Repo page (a parameter of the shared builder). `.tsum`'s tracks give each drawn facet its own share (Repo 1, TDs in motion 1.15, Doing 1.05), so two split the row by theirs, replacing today's `:not(:has(.frepo))` pair; one column below 1100 px in the same order.
2. **The repo's line.** It keeps its name and *open →*. It draws the facet, with the repo's lanes, only while no card of the repo shows one: a repo with no team, or every team card of the repo folded or drawing no summary. The fold is the browser's, so the server renders the line's facet for every repo and the client shows it only while that holds: in `syncTeams`, which runs on each fold and swap of the groups, from `isFolded`, the filter's hidden cards and whether a card has a `.frepo`. The line's selector keeps its `repo:<name>` key. On the Repo page the line keeps the facet always and the team cards draw none.
3. **The completed face.** `team_summary` gains, per window, the team's closes: `completed_in`'s rows whose id, or closing PR, a record with the team's badge names in its `progress` (a claim's `ref` or `pr`; `review_pr` is cleared at the merge, so it never matches) or in a `restarts` entry's `done`. Each row carries id, title, PR and URL, the closed time and its short age. Eight are sent per window, plus the window's count. The TDs in motion head gains the *in motion · completed* toggle (`data-pick="motion"`). The completed face's head reads *Completed (n)* with the page's window picker; the rows sit on the facet's grid with the age in the phase column; then *+n more*, linking to `/repo/<name>?win=<w>#completed`; and *nothing completed this <window>* when empty. The face is stored per team (`store`, a key such as `motion:<team>`, a branch in `pickSummary` and `summaryState`), not held per state as `faceFlip` is: a stored pick wins whatever the team then holds. The matcher is its own reader of `completed_in`'s rows against the team's records, not part of `motion_rows` (which reads only `claimed`); it reads `progress` entries of any status and the members passed in include exited records with their `restarts`. Until it is picked, the facet opens on *in motion* while the team holds a claim, else on *completed*.
4. **`drawn_facets`.** It draws the Repo facet when its reading exists, and the motion facet when the team holds a claim or has a close in any window — a new summary key (`completed`, per window) beside `motion`, which `holds["motion"]` reads with it.
