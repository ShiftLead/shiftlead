**Why:** Paul's ask (m-417b7f8b20c7, 2026-11-03): choosing an answer on a Queue *needs you* item takes about 10 s before the page responds. A click should get a near-immediate response, beyond the notification.

The cause, read from the code: the press (`app.js` click handler → POST `/api/person/ledger|board`, `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`) awaits the whole write-back before replying. A ledger decide (`rpc_ledger_decide`, `src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`) runs `board_mod.add_decided`, and a board decide (`rpc_board_edit`) runs `board_mod.write_back`. Both land through `src/sessionorc/board.py`'s `_tree` and `_land`, which run these one after another: a `git fetch`, a worktree reset, a commit, a branch push, `gh pr create`, `gh pr merge --squash`, a fetch, a remote branch delete and another fetch. Each step has a 20 s timeout (`GIT_TIMEOUT`), the whole press is bounded at 40 s (`BOUND`), and presses are serialised by the `_EDIT` lock. Only then does `_reread_board` broadcast the new `repos`.

The page is already partly optimistic (TD-1316, TD-1321 *rule 4*): `pressed()` marks a board decide *decided: X — landing on the board…*, and a ledger decide removes the row and shows a *deciding — writing on the board…* toast, with rollback on refusal and `AO.inflight` carried across rereads. But the card's own state is computed on the server: its *needs you* pill, its Decide count and its place in the Queue. So none of it changes until the push arrives, and on a ledger decide the item vanishes while the card's state stays unchanged until the push.

**Resolved:** 2026-11-07 (PR #3078) — parts TD-1461 (#3052, live check #3078); diagnosed in #3012, designed in #3043; design §4.5 rule 4 A Queue card's own state follows the press

**Done when:** A press of an answer on a Queue card changes that card within a frame: its *needs you* goes and its counts drop, and a refusal puts them back. Either the reply returns before the board lands, or the design records why it waits. A test pins the card's local state after a decide press. The live check: a press on the Queue reads *answered* at once, recorded here before archiving.
