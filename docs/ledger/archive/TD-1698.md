**Why:** TD-1557's design: §4.4 *Decide* (*A form's press is the decide*, *A Reply that fills a form decides*) and §4.10 *A form is filled, never pressed*. On 2026-11-07 Paul typed his `Hold: <note>` into **Answer…** on a board end-of-rounds item. It took the Reply road and left the line due, because `_board_answer` fills only ledgerboard's `Not right:` form. Today a `Hold: <note>` button pressed as written writes the placeholder as `Decided:`. A ledger entry's answer index or a mail `--pick` naming such an answer would send it too. This build is the agent's half. It reads forms in general through the ledgerboard release that carries `is_form` and `fill_form`.
