## TD-1797: A usage allowance reset inside its window leaves every session limited

**Priority:** high
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-13
**Owner:** paul
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-13
**Id take:** 278bafcccbbf47b295ef002ca2184ee2

**Why:** On 2026-11-13 the Claude account's week window reached 100% at 18:56Z and the allowance was then reset by Anthropic inside the window: the percentage fell to 2% and `resets` stayed 2026-11-18T00:00Z. Every Claude session on kmaster stayed `limited` (8 of them) and the unattended members stayed paused by the usage gate, with nothing to clear it before the window's own reset five days on. `usage.merge` kept the highest percentage inside one `resets` (design §4.4 *The reading*: "inside one `resets` a window's use never falls"), so a session's fresh report of 2% was merged as 100%. Each capped session's status line went on handing the 100% it was last given, about once a minute, and a report that was not fresh could still raise a number. The fresh reports kept the reading young, so the endpoint, the one source allowed to lower a number, was never asked, and an answer would have been raised again by the next stale report.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/usage.py (merge); design §4.4 The reading; tests/test_usage_report.py

**Resolved:** 2026-11-13 (PR #3735) — design §4.4 The reading; tests/test_usage_report.py

**Done when:** Done when a fresh report lower than the held number inside one `resets` lowers the reading and lifts `limited` on every session of the account, a report that is not fresh never raises a held number, the design's sentence and tests say so, and once it is promoted the Claude sessions on kmaster no longer read `limited` from the week window reset on 2026-11-13.
