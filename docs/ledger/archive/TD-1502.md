## TD-1502: A Focus tab's icon cannot tell a running turn from a session working on its own

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-active-vs-bg)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-07
**Id take:** cd5bf71edea8483a8d18ff39ee67b8c6
**Location:** design §4.5 (A tab's icon), §4.2 (Working on its own, A background shell task); src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py (the own-work branch that sets state_class to working); src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (AO.focusIcon); src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html (data-icons)

**Why:** A Focus tab's icon draws one tile for *working* (green, ∿) whether the session's own turn is running or its turn has ended and only its own background work goes on: a live subagent or a live background shell (`run_in_background`, `Monitor`). Paul asked, 2026-11-06, whether the two can be told apart, since a separate icon for each might help.

They can. The record says which: a turn in progress is `state: working`, while work on its own is `state: idle` with the view's `subagents_live` or `shells_live` true (§4.2 *Working on its own*, *A background shell task*), and `cards.py` already sets `own_work` (*working · 1 shell*) only in the second case. But the second case also sets `state_class` to `working`, and `AO.focusIcon` picks the icon from `state_class`, so the tab strip loses the difference the card's slot still shows.

The difference matters at a glance across tabs: a session in its turn may need the person in a moment (a permission, a question, its reply), while one whose turn has ended on a CI watch, a deploy or a reviewer subagent will not speak until that ends, often minutes later. Today the person has to open the tab, or read the card's ending, to know which. The pill's merge is deliberate (*One order*: *working* means *leave it alone, it goes on by itself*) and is not in question here; the tab icon is the cheaper place to say more.

**Fix:** Design first, in §4.5 *A tab's icon*: a twelfth Focus icon for working on its own, drawn when `own_work` is set (an `idle` record with a live subagent or shell), and the plain *working* tile kept for a turn in progress. The round settles:
- the glyph and colour: the same green with a different glyph (a ∿ with a small ⋯ or clock, say) keeps it read as *working*; a separate colour makes it a new state class in all but name;
- how the icon is keyed: a view field the page already has (`own_work`), never a new `State` and never a change to the pill, the ranking, the filters or any rule, since `STATE_ICON_CLASSES` is derived from `State` and the pill's *One order* stands;
- whether the Focus title, which `AO.focusTitle` sets beside the icon, says so too;
- whether subagent and shell need two icons, or one for both (one is the likely answer: both mean *its turn has ended and its own work goes on*).

Render the choices beside today's tile for Paul before any review round (a UI change reviewed with him comes with something to look at). The build then adds `static/icons/state-own.svg` (or the chosen name), the class to `data-icons`, and `AO.focusIcon`'s choice, with a test that an idle record with a live shell gets it and a working one does not.
