## TD-1283: Six defects here escaped their designs, and nothing records it: backfill their review records and `Escaped from:` fields

**Priority:** low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (Paul, session `td204-design`: "Go" on agent-cadence TD-204's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-10-31: six backfilled review records and six `Escaped from:` fields; `review-stats --repo .` lists six resolved escapes
**Location:** `docs/reviews/` (new: TD-947, TD-995, TD-1051, TD-909, TD-804, TD-820); the archived entries TD-960, TD-1128, TD-1095, TD-1129, TD-1031, TD-854
**Resolved:** 2026-10-31 (PR #2757) — `docs/reviews/TD-{947,995,1051,909,804,820}.md`; `Escaped from:` on TD-960, TD-1128, TD-1095, TD-1129, TD-1031, TD-854; mined by hand, no agent

**Why:** agent-cadence's hand-mined dataset of 97 designs (`docs/review-data/` there) found six designs here whose defects a design review should have caught: TD-947 → TD-960, TD-995 → TD-1128, TD-1051 → TD-1095, TD-909 → TD-1129, TD-804 → TD-1031 and TD-820 → TD-854. `review-stats` reads only `docs/reviews/TD-NNN.md` records and the ledgers' `Escaped from:` fields. Until these exist, those escapes count for nothing. Two pairs are left out on purpose: TD-931 → TD-935 is a doc leftover after the build (a build miss), and the five "(questionable)" designs are unconfirmed.

**Fix:** for each of the six designs, write `docs/reviews/TD-NNN.md` in agent-cadence's `adr-template.md` record form. Its header gets `Design:` (the design doc and section), `Class:`, `Decided:` (the dataset's `date`) and `Source: backfill`. Its tables come from agent-cadence's `docs/review-data/design-rounds.json` and this repo's `docs/design-history.md`: reviews and counts it cannot find are written `?`, and findings known only by count are `unlabelled` rows. Then add `**Escaped from:** TD-NNN` to each of the six defect entries, all archived. One PR. Mining the history can be done by hand; **an agent may do it only if the person, asked first, agrees.**

**Done when** the six record files exist, the six defects carry `Escaped from:`, and agent-cadence's `review-stats` run over this repo lists six resolved escapes.

**Related:** agent-cadence#TD-204 and its ADR; TD-1282.
