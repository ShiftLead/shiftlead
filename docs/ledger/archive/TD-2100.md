## TD-2100: Remove precheck directories that a killed run leaves on disk

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-23
**Id take:** d479a6810d64484fbb956ab18ca5b62a
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: a precheck's `/var/tmp/pc.*` directory is removed by its shell's `trap`, which does not run when the shell is killed outright, as an outside kill or an out-of-memory kill does, and `precheck.py` sends SIGKILL to the group on a stop or exception. What is left waits for `systemd-tmpfiles`, which ages `/var/tmp` at 30 days (§4.6), and agent-cadence's `check_tmp.py` does not reap `pc.*`. Read on 2026-11-23: 226 directories, about 211 over a day old, at least 494,553 inodes on the root disk. This build shortens that bound from 30 days to one.

**Location:** docs/cadence.toml, src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (the hourly sweep)

**Done when:** A precheck removes a `pc.*` of its user made over a day ago, keeps one made within the day and a concurrent run's, follows no symlink named `pc.*`, and still runs when the removal fails. §4.6 states the one-day rule. After the build is live on kmaster, no `pc.*` over a day old remains once a precheck has run.
