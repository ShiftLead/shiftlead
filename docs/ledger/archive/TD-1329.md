## TD-1329: #2820's `/api/settings/push_subscribe` forwards `replaces`, and no test would catch it going

**Priority:** low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (test-audit-ao-1, auditing #2820, which closed TD-1305)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-03: a route test now posts a re-subscribe with replaces and holds the device's label and since
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`settings_push_subscribe`); `tests/test_ui_push_card.py`

**Why:** the route builds its params with `for k in ("endpoint", "keys", "label", "key", "replaces") if body.get(k) is not None`, and `sw.js`'s `pushsubscriptionchange` handler posts `replaces` (the old device's id) through it so the re-subscribed device keeps its label and *since*. Deleting `"replaces"` from that tuple in a scratch worktree (restored after) leaves `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pytest -q tests/test_ui_push_card.py` at *9 passed*: the node probe of the worker only checks what the worker posts, and the route test posts no `replaces`. `tests/test_push_store.py` covers `push.subscribe(replaces=…)`, one layer down, so a route that drops the field leaves every test green while a browser-replaced subscription comes back as a second device with the label *a device* and a new *since*.

**Resolved:** 2026-11-03 (PR #2838) — tests/test_ui_push_card.py (test_the_card_saves_and_its_switch_lands_through_the_home)
