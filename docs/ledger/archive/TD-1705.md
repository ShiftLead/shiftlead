**Why:** A decided board line is a work order in every `free-pick` lane, so the person's answer to a design's end-of-rounds item goes to the grinders and never to the designer whose round it is. In ledgerboard on 2026-11-10, a grinder claimed *One more round* on TD-058 and dropped it as the designer's work, while `lb-designer` sat idle waiting on that very answer until the grinder mailed it by hand (TD-1590). TD-1590's design (§6 rule 6 *save a design's answer*, §4.9a *A design's answer*) routes such a line to the `design-first` lanes, the design's own designer's alone (`for`) where one is, and to `free-pick` only where the repo has no designer to reach.
