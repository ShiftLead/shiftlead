## TD-572: Typing into a Focus terminal lags — every page's events stream re-reads the whole fleet's groups on every delta

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-12 (ao-paul, with Paul)
**Owner:** anchor
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved
**Location:** design §4.5 *Live state*, §4.5a *card: compact*, *team card: wound down* note; `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`events`), `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py` (`HEADS_EVERY`, `SHAPE_KEYS`), `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`connectEvents`, the Focus and Inbox pages), `tests/test_ui_events_light.py`

**Why:** Paul, 2026-10-12: *there is lag per keystroke in this (and the other focus) terminals … no lag when typing into the composer text box*. Measured on the live UI with three Focus tabs open: `shiftlead-ui` at a full core (888 s of CPU in 20 min, all on the event loop's thread), a static file's latency p50 3 ms, p90 770 ms, p99 2.4 s, max 3.2 s, and each `/events` stream ~0.9 MB/s — 72 messages in 15 s, ~190 KB each, because every session delta (a working pane's tail moves every second or two) carried `heads`: every team header and the rollup, read over the whole fleet, once per open page. A Focus tab held the same full stream as the Org page and used one record of it. The pty bridge's keystrokes share that loop, so each waited behind the renders; the composer is a plain text box and does not.

**Fix:** the groups ride on a delta only when it changes one of `SHAPE_KEYS` (state, team, controllers, name, seat, unattended), names a record the stream has not seen, or is the stream's first; any other delta marks them for one `groups` event at most every `HEADS_EVERY` (1 s), and a reading equal to the last one sent is not sent. `/events?light=1` (the Inbox) and `?light=1&focus=<id>` (Focus) send no card markup and no groups: the focus record's delta whole, another's as `{event, id, state}` only when a field of `SHAPE_KEYS` or `LIGHT_KEYS` (lane, report, claim, unseen, open work, declaration) moved, which is all those pages re-read on. One reading of the groups is read and sent at a time (`sending`), `dirty` cleared before the read; a reshaping delta always carries its groups; a flush whose read fails retries.

**Done when** a Focus tab's stream carries no groups and only its own session in full, the Org's carries the groups only on a shape change or once a second, and the tests in `tests/test_ui_events_light.py` pass.

**Related:** TD-176 (the groups on every delta), TD-058 (the `/events` handler's end).

**Resolved:** 2026-10-12 (PR #1544) — the groups ride on a delta only on a `SHAPE_KEYS` change, a new record or the stream's first, else one `groups` event per `HEADS_EVERY`, never re-sent unchanged; the Focus and Inbox pages take `/events?light=1` (Focus with `focus=<id>`): no markup, no groups, another record only when it moves.
