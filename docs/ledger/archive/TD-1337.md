## TD-1337: A pop-up menu stays open until its own button is pressed again: a press anywhere outside it should close it, on every page

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** live-check #2851
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-03
**Resolved:** 2026-11-03 (grinder-ao-2) — built in #2851, live in 9caeed6 (`ao promote status`). Read on the live UI, never pressed: `/` and `/inbox` answer 200, the `app.js` they serve (`?v=132a3658eeeb`) carries `AO.menuPress`, `AO.menuEsc`, the `aoMenuClosed` mark and the keys table's skip of it, both pages load in headless Chromium with no script error and `window.AO.menuPress` / `menuEsc` defined, and `/` draws `#newmenu` as a `details.more`. The presses (an outside press closes **+ New ▾**, a card's **more ▾** and a **Snooze**; `Esc` closes with focus back on the button; a fold stays open) were checked on the scratch home, in #2851's UI check.

**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-03, through Add entry: *Our pop-up menus (e.g. when you press the "New" button on the top menu) do not go away unless the originating button is pressed again. They should disappear if an area outside of them on the page is clicked. Lets make sure all of our menus work this way unless there is a compelling reason not to.* Every pop-up menu but the rollup's is a native `<details class="more">`, which only its own summary (or a choice inside it, or opening another menu) closes; one left open sits over the page, fixed to the viewport (TD-121), and hides what is under it until the person finds its button again. The Sessions rollup's two menus are already built the way Paul asks (§4.5a *Sessions: rollup: press and menus*: *a press outside it, `Tab` out of it or opening another closes it*), so the pages disagree with each other as well as with the person.

**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the `toggle` capture listener that folds the other `details.more` and places the open one, ~846–852; `AO.qDots`, the Queue ⋯'s keys, ~858–873; the choice that folds a menu, ~1355–1359; the + New ▾ fold on **Shell**, ~2048; the rollup's own outside-press close, ~4339–4347, the pattern to follow); the menus themselves, each a `<details class="more">`: `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/base.html` (~85, `#newmenu`, **+ New ▾**), `card.html` (~131, the card's **more ▾**), `focus.html` (~145, the Focus header's **more ▾**; ~336, `#reportsmore`), `inbox_row.html` (the eight **Snooze** menus, ~237–1149), `queue_row.html` (~30, the Queue card's **⋯**); `docs/design/4.5a-controls.md` (row *Sessions: rollup: press and menus*, which already says it for the rollup's two menus); `docs/design/4.5-ui.md` (*Browser mechanics*); `tests/test_ui_menu.py`

**Fix:**
- **The design first, in the same PR.** One sentence in §4.5 *Browser mechanics* (or a §4.5a *every page* row, whichever the builder finds the design keeps such rules in) that every pop-up menu — **+ New ▾**, a card's and the Focus header's **more ▾**, `#reportsmore`, every **Snooze** menu, the Queue card's **⋯**, the rollup's two — closes on a press outside it and on `Esc`, focus going back to its button on `Esc` alone, as the rollup row already words it; and the rows of those controls point at it rather than each repeating it.
- **One page-wide listener** for the `details.more` menus, beside the existing `toggle` capture: a `pointerdown` (capture, so a press in the Focus terminal, which keeps its own mouse events, counts too) whose target is not inside an open `details.more` closes it. The press still does what it pressed — it is not swallowed — as the rollup's outside press is not. `Esc` closes an open one wherever focus is (new work: the Queue ⋯'s `AO.qDots` does it only while focus is inside the ⋯).
- **What it must not break:** a menu that a re-read reopens (TD-1315, `AO.reopenMenus`) and a card's delta that keeps its menu open (TD-121) are not presses and stay as they are; a press on a menu's own summary still toggles it (no close-then-reopen); the rollup's own handler is left alone or folded into the one listener, not doubled.
- **Not menus, so not changed:** the folds — `details.fold`, an Inbox row's *details*, a Queue card's Read fold, the Focus side cards, **More ▸** on the New session form — are disclosures the person opened to read or fill, and a press elsewhere on the page must not shut them (TD-1315 is the bug of exactly that). The `<dialog>`s (`#mailbox`, `#startdlg`, `#membersdlg`, `#entrydlg`, `#keyhelp`, `#boardadd`, `#railsheet`) and the narrow-screen rail with its scrim are modal, have their own close, and are out of scope too. If the builder finds another pop-up that is not a `details.more`, it joins the rule.
- **Tests:** a node probe (as `test_ui_menu.py`'s) that a press outside an open `details.more` closes it, a press inside it or on its summary does not close it from outside, `Esc` closes it, and a fold stays open on an outside press.

**Done when** on the live UI a press anywhere outside an open **+ New ▾**, **more ▾**, **Snooze** or **⋯** closes it and still does what it pressed, `Esc` closes it, the folds are untouched, the design says so in one place, and `pdm run test` passes.

**Related:** TD-121 (menus drawn fixed to the viewport, kept open across a delta); TD-1315 (a re-read reopens an open menu and keeps a Read fold open); TD-1204 (the rollup's menus, which already close on an outside press).
