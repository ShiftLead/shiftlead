## TD-2257: Nothing tests a two-word skip setting when checking whether a login leaves its prompts on

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-26
**Id take:** 80295c19c9844087b330b7c79d24d489

**Why:** Audit of #4724 (TD-2068 slice 5). Design §4.9d says "Neither the director nor a prompts seat starts on a login whose `unattended_args` leave its prompts on". `teamstore.asks` (`src/sessionorc/teamstore.py`) decides it: `bool(given) and not all(a in given for a in skip)`, so a login asks where its arguments lack any one of the tool's `skip_args`. Gemini's are two words, `["--approval-mode", "yolo"]`; Claude Code's and Grok's are one.

No test reads a two-word list against arguments that hold one of its words: the tests use a one-word list (`["--skip"]`) or an adapter's own list against none of it, all of it or `["--other"]`, where *all of them* and *any of them* read alike. With `all` changed to `any`, the 29 test modules naming the stores, stage starts or built-in seats passed at 368b1fefa (1162 passed). The same condition in `switchover._asks` (`src/shiftlead/switchover.py`) has no such test either. With both changed, and the changes TD-2258 and TD-2259 name, the whole suite passed at f6882e354 (`pytest -n 8`: 10283 passed, 24 skipped).

Probe at 368b1fefa, the real Gemini adapter registered, a login with `unattended_args: ["--approval-mode", "default"]`: unchanged, `teamstore.asks` reads True and `pick(..., unasked=True)` passes over it to the next login; with the change it reads False and the pick returns that login.

A break would start the prompts seat, which answers permission prompts for the person, on a Gemini login that stops at its own prompts. A break in `switchover._asks` would let `ao switch-over check` pass a director list whose every Gemini login leaves its prompts on.
