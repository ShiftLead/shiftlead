## TD-1349: Build a hidden board line in the Inbox's Snoozed fold, with *now*, and count a board Snooze from today

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (the designer, from TD-1345's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-03
**Part of:** TD-1345
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`board_rows`, which drops an item whose `needs` is None; `board_horizon`, `horizon_of` and `with_standings`' keys, where a hidden row gets its own `snoozed` key; `inbox_sections`, a new `snoozed=` argument and the snoozed list's order); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (each `inbox_sections` call that passes `boards=hz["due"] + hz["waiting"]`, which now passes `snoozed=hz["snoozed"]` too; the agent-down horizon built by hand (`{"due": [], "ahead": [], "hidden": [], "waiting": [], "line": None}`), which gains `"snoozed": []`; the board route's 400 *a snooze names the new date*); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the `board` macro's **Snooze ▾** menu and its title *move its Due: date on the board*, the macro's comment; the fold's rows); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`boardDue`, its base date and comment); `src/sessionorc/board.py` (`_write_back`'s compare of an unchanged line; `write_back`'s `snooze` with no date: `_day('')`, once, passed to `edit_line`, `plan_edit` and `message`); the board route in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`; tests under `tests/`.
**Resolved:** 2026-11-03 — built in #2886 (grinder-ao-2): `board_rows` keeps a line hidden by its `Hide until:` as a `hidden` row, `board_horizon` sets it apart as `snoozed` (read before *Waiting on them*), and `inbox_sections(snoozed=…)` draws it in the **Snoozed (n)** fold alone, uncounted; the board row's Snooze ▾ counts from today and offers *now* first in the fold; `board.write_back` fills a dateless snooze with the host agent's today and lands nothing when the line already says so. UI check on a scratch home, shot `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-03-td1349-snoozed-board-row.png`.

**Why:** since TD-1330 a board Snooze writes `Hide until:`, and two choices made when it moved `Due:` read wrongly: +1 week counts from the later of today and the item's `Due:`, so a line due in 3 days is hidden until it has been overdue 7 days; and a hidden line is drawn nowhere until its date, where a snoozed mail entry sits in the **Snoozed (n)** fold (TD-1345).

**Fix:** as design §4.5a rows *Due strip / Inbox board row: Snooze ▾* and *Inbox: **Snoozed (n)** fold*, §4.4 *Board write-back*, §4.10 *Snooze*:
1. `boardDue` counts +1 day, +1 week and *a date…*'s offered tomorrow from today, never from the item's `Due:`; the menu's title, the macro's comment and the board row's footer sentence (*Snooze moves its `Due:` date*) say it hides the line until the date.
2. `board_rows` keeps an open item whose `needs` is None and whose record's `hidden` is true (its `hide_until` the row's *snoozed until*); `board_horizon` puts such a row under its own key `snoozed` and in none of `due`, `ahead`, its mode's `hidden` fold or `waiting`, read before `board_waits`, so the Repo page, the top bar's counts (`app.py`'s counts read) and the Queue, which read the other keys, never see it; `horizon_of`'s per-repo cut filters `snoozed` as it filters the rest, and `with_standings` gives its rows standings as it does `due`, `ahead` and `hidden`; `board_rows`' skip comment and `inbox_sections`' docstring (*a hidden item is no row*) are reworded with the code; `inbox_sections` takes it as `snoozed=` beside `boards=` and draws it in the **Snoozed (n)** fold, soonest first among the mail entries and state rows, its *snoozed until* the start of its `Hide until:` day on the page's host's local calendar (`datetime.combine(d, time.min).astimezone(UTC)`, as `overdue` reads a civil date), written `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ` as `snoozeUntil`'s mail instants are (the fold sorts on that string), with `until_words` set, so `snoozed_says` reads it as it reads a mail row; `snoozed_n` and the fold's label count it, `overdue` and *Needs you* never; uncounted, in no section, no rail group, no Queue card, no *Not due yet* or *not shown* fold; the find unfolds it.
3. The fold's board row is the `board` macro's row with its id, due words (its `ahead` from `_ahead_words(due, today, tag)`, the call that sets it for the other horizon rows: the reader's tag once its `Due:` has come, *3d overdue*; *due in 6 d · Nov 10* before; *no due date* for an undated line — the macro's section test gains `'snoozed'`, so it draws `ahead`), **Open board**, **Reply**, its answers and **Go with it**, **Done**, and **Snooze ▾** with *now* first (`data-when="now"`), drawn whatever Queue card names its entry; `app.js`'s board-act handler sends *now* with no `due`, and `boardDue` drops its `from` argument, the menu's buttons their `data-due`. On the ring, `x` is **Done** and `s` opens the menu.
4. *now* sends the `snooze` write-back with no date (the board route no longer answers 400 for a snooze without one); `board.write_back` computes the missing date once (`_day('')`, the host agent's `date.today()`) and passes it to `edit_line`, `plan_edit` and `message(…)`, so the line reads `Hide until: <today>` (ledgerboard reads it as shown, `hidden` being `today < hide_until`) and the commit subject is *snooze <head> to <today>*. `_write_back` returns the date it wrote as `due`, and `rpc_board_edit` answers that one, not the request's, as its `**done` spread already does, so a *now* reply names the date written. The page's toast after a board *now* says *back in its section — landed on the board on origin*, and after the no-op answer (no `pr`) *back in its section — the board already said so*, as a mail or state row's *now* says *back in its section* (`app.js`'s board toast, which today reads *snoozed to ${body.due}*). `ao board edit snooze` keeps `--until` required: *now* is the page's alone.
5. Tests: a line hidden past its `Due:` is in the fold, counted by `snoozed_n` and not by `overdue` or *Needs you*, its due words overdue; +1 week from a line due in 3 days is today + 7; *now* writes today and the next reading draws the line in its section; *now* on a line whose `Hide until:` is past writes today and commits; *now* on a line already at `Hide until: <today>` is answered as done with origin's head and no commit (`_write_back` compares the edited line with the old after `_apply` and, unchanged, lands nothing and answers `{commit: <origin's head>, message, line, text, due}` (`message` the subject it computed, which `rpc_board_edit` logs) with no `pr`; the test reads the RPC's reply as a success with no PR opened, and that the page's toast keys on the reply's `pr`, never on the request's `due`); a Reply on a hidden line leaves it in the fold, and once the hide ends it is under *Waiting on them*; a fold board row and a snoozed mail entry sort together by instant.

**Done when:** a board line snoozed from the Inbox sits in the **Snoozed (n)** fold until its date with its controls, *now* brings it back, and +1 day / +1 week count from today.
