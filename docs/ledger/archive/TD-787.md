It sat green, unreviewed and unasked, for 17 hours, until Paul asked whether it was stranded.

**Fix (Paul, 2026-10-16: visible only, drawn whoever wrote the PR, with an age, and named by `ao pr held`).** *As filed; the design's Fable and Sonnet rounds amended items 1–5 — the key's scope and plumbing, `_red_prs` owning `pr_orders.unasked`, `asked` reading the standing's seats by the sender's remote, the reader as the first matching link, `ao pr held` reading the home's reading — and §4.4 *A held PR nobody asked is marked, never ordered*, TD-789 and TD-790 carry the amended form.* The home tells nobody and orders nothing; the reading marks, and the Repo page, `ao repo` and `ao pr held` show.
1. **The key, at the tick.** The tick computes each repo's held key and passes it to `pr_reading` as a new `held=` (a sorted list of globs): the **repo's `held:`**, read from `.shiftlead.yml` directly as `ledger.ledger_path` reads `ledger:` (named sets: their union), since `sessionorc` never imports `shiftlead.repoconfig`; else the union of the `review` globs on the repo's live records (`review_links`, records whose `repo` is the root — what §4.9 *A person in the team* fills from the roles). A repo with neither has an empty key: nothing is held and nothing drawn.
2. **Held, by the reading.** `pr_reading` reads the changed files (`reports.pr_files`, paginated, `GH_TIMEOUT`) of each open PR that is not a sync PR — a draft too, since marking it ready changes no head — **once per head and per key**: the row carries `held` (the matched paths, `[]` for none), `held_head` and `held_key` (the list read), carried from `old` as `sync_head` is; a changed head or key re-reads it. The match is a new `held.match(files, globs)`, which `held_paths` itself calls (`held_paths(files, {"held": key})` would match nothing: `review_links` returns no link for a mapping with no `reader`). A new constant `HELD_READS = 5` caps the reads a pass (separate from `held.READS` and from the sync read), rows with no `held_head` or a stale one first, oldest first; until a PR is read, or while its read fails, it is *not judged* and draws nothing, and a failed read keeps the last word, as `_sync_flag` does. The first fill after the build takes as many passes as it needs.
3. **Asked, remembered.** The tick (`_red_prs`, which owns `pr_orders`) reads the asks in the mail the home holds — each review seat's record mail (`techlead` seats and a flow's stage readers by `held.addressed`, never an auditor seat) and `person_inbox` — and keeps per held PR the first `ask` carrying its `pr` (`pr_orders.asked: {n: {at, to}}`), pruned when the PR closes, joining `_red_prs`' save comparison. The reading is written whole (`RepoStore`), so a restart keeps it, and mail's 12-hour retention or a pass-up past the bound never un-asks a PR. *findings*, *passed*, *the person's* and *merged* are all asked. **Only mail held at and after the build counts:** an open held PR whose ask was pruned before it reads *unasked* — ask again, or let it stand; nothing is seeded from rule 10 or elsewhere.
4. **The tag.** A PR whose `held` is non-empty, never asked, not a draft, not a sync PR, not conflicting, its `checks` *green* or *none* (TD-664's send set; *pending* gets nothing, *not run* keeps *CI never ran*, red keeps the red tags) reads **`unasked · <age>`**, the age from the PR's `created`, in the standing column's place, drawn `wait` (amber). Its title: *touches held paths (`<path>`): post its review, then `ao msg --kind ask --pr <n> <reader>` — where your own `ao pr held` says held*. `<reader>` is the reader of the **first servicing team in the Org's order whose review holds a touched path** — its techlead seat by name, under a flow the chain's first reader, else `person` (§4.9b, a team with no seat); the page gets it from the members it already reads for the standing (app.py ~L1455–L1468). Where no servicing team has any reader, nothing is drawn, as today. The Open PRs *i* panel (`repo_part.html` ~L7) gains *unasked*. **`ao repo`** prints it on the PR's standing line (`unasked 17h — post its review, then ao msg --kind ask --pr 1842 techlead-ao-1`), joining `pr_orders.asked`, and `--json` carries per open PR `held` (paths), `asked` (`{at, to}` or null) and `unasked` (bool, the precedence applied). Because the tag is the reading's, the author's own `ao repo` shows it — §4.7's *a session's `ao repo` shows none* is amended to the mail standing alone.
5. **Whoever wrote it** (Paul). The tag keys on the PR's files, never on its author, as §4.9c ~L526 already says of a held path (*whoever wrote the PR*): a PR from a session whose record carries no `review` still reads *unasked* on a held path, since the home cannot find every author (#1842's was in a scratch worktree). §4.9b gains the sentence that reconciles the two reads: the home's repo-level mark reads the PR's files against the repo's `held:`, the author's `ao pr held` reads its record's `review`; where they differ, an author with a `review` asks the reader the tag names, and one with no `review` merges on the cadence and lets the tag stand. **`ao pr held`** (Paul), on a *not held* answer (either branch: no `review` on the record, or no path its `review` holds) where the repo's key matches, adds *the repo holds `<path>`: the Repo page tags it unasked until a reader is asked*.
6. **AGENTS.md** says a held PR's author posts the review and sends the first ask once the PR is green, and how the tag and a *not held* answer meet — in the PR that filed this entry.
7. **Tests:** the key from the repo's `held:` (and named sets) and its fallback to the live records' `review`; `held`, `held_head` and `held_key` carried through `old`, a changed key re-reading at the same head, a draft read, a failed read keeping the last word, the `HELD_READS` cap and order, a repo-level key with no reader matching; `asked` from a seat's mail and from `person_inbox`, kept past the mail's prune and across a `RepoStore` reload, pruned on close; the tag's precedence (draft, sync, conflicting, red, pending, *not run*, a reader-less repo) and the two-team reader on the Repo page and in `cmd_repo`'s prose and `--json`; `ao pr held`'s added line. §4.5a is untouched (the standing column is no control); the *i* panel's text is pinned by no test today (`grep -ln 4.5a tests` to check at the build).

**No notes, no orders, no Inbox row.** If a held PR is ever stranded again despite the tag, the safety net is a second entry, and Paul has chosen its owner: a held PR unasked past `ORPHAN_AFTER` whose author the home cannot find is **the person's** (an Inbox row), never a grinders' `pr:<n>` order, since a grinder's ask would make the reader merge a PR an interactive author meant to merge (§4.9b, the asker's mode decides the merge).
