## TD-1282: The bump that brings ledgerboard v2.2 and agent-cadence's review records here, with `ao td reserve` asking the escaped-from question

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (Paul, session `td204-design`: "Go" on agent-cadence TD-204's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-10-31: the pin carries agent-cadence TD-204 (3739b0c), ledgerboard v2.2 is vendored, and ao td reserve reprints the filing question
**Blocked by:** agent-cadence#TD-204
**Location:** `docs/cadence.toml` (the `[agent-cadence]` pin); `scripts/vendor_ledgerboard.sh` and `src/sessionorc/ledgerboard.py` (the vendored v2.2), `tests/ledgerboard/MANIFEST`; `src/sessionorc/agent_ids.py` (`ao td reserve`)

**Why:** agent-cadence TD-204 adds `**Escaped from:** <design TD>` to a defect a design missed, so a design's review record can be checked against the defects it let through. The field joins ledgerboard's `CADENCE_FIELDS` in v2.2 (ledgerboard#TD-006). This ledger declares a `Fields:` line, so until the bump an `Escaped from:` on a live entry is an `undeclared-field` error under `check --since`. With the key `review-outcomes` on, agent-cadence's `ledger_next_id.py` prints the filing question last on stderr. `ao td reserve` runs it with `--json` and its stderr piped, so here nothing shows the question unless the wrapper reprints it.

**Resolved:** 2026-10-31 (PR #2756) — design §4.4 *Its answer* (*The filing question*); src/sessionorc/agent_ids.py, src/shiftlead/cli.py; tests/test_td_next.py

**Done when** the pin carries TD-204, the vendored ledgerboard is v2.2 with its fixture test green, and `ao td reserve` prints the question when the key is on and stays silent when it is off.

**Related:** agent-cadence#TD-204 and its ADR; ledgerboard#TD-006, ledgerboard#TD-005 (v2.2); TD-1283 (the backfill, after this).
