## TD-1192: The top bar is held: sticky on a wide screen, away on a phone's scroll down and back on its scroll up, `--barh` for the scroll padding and the rail

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-28 (the designer, from TD-1187's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved
**Part of:** TD-1187
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.topbar` 80, `.rail` 215, the first `@media (max-width: 720px)` block 600); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`AO.holdBar` and `AO.barDecide`, started beside the bar's fit, the other every-page setup (`app.js` 6690); the `toggle` placement listener 612); `tests/` (a UI test of the held bar)

**Why:** TD-1187: on a long page the top bar leaves the screen, and with it the tabs, their numbers, **+ New ▾** and the tools.

**Fix:** as design §4.5 *Browser mechanics* *The top bar is held* says. (1) `app.css`: `:root { --barh: 48px }`; `html { scroll-padding-top: calc(var(--barh) + 8px) }`; `html:has(.topbar.away) { scroll-padding-top: 8px }`; `html:has(body.popped) { --barh: 0px }` (on `html`, where the padding reads it); `.topbar { position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 30 }`; `.rail { top: calc(var(--barh) + 12px) }`; in the ≤720px block, `.topbar.away { transform: translateY(-100%); transition: transform .15s ease-out }` (the transition on `away` only, (2)). (2) `app.js`: `AO.holdBar()`, run at load on a page with a `.topbar` (never on a popped-out Focus, which has none): a `ResizeObserver` on `.topbar` writes its `offsetHeight` as `--barh` px on `document.documentElement`; and one `decide()`, its listeners attached once on every page with a bar, which returns at once, toggling nothing, while `matchMedia("(max-width: 720px)")` does not match (so `AO.barDecide` on a wide screen is a no-op, and a window narrowed into a phone's width mid-session is already wired), and otherwise reads `y = clamp(scrollY, 0, scrollHeight − innerHeight)` (the clamp is the listener's, so the pure function takes clamped `y`), `held` = `.topbar:has(:focus-visible)` (keyboard focus; a tap's focus, which Chrome on Android leaves on a tapped button, does not count) or `#newmenu` open (a `<details>`, its `open` property), and applies `AO.barAway(anchorY, y, barH, away, held)` → `{away, anchorY}`, toggling `.topbar.away`. `decide()` runs on `window`'s passive `scroll` (the document's scroller; no page scrolls an inner box), on `focusin` on the bar, and on `focusout` on the bar a frame later (`requestAnimationFrame`), since during `focusout` the leaving element still matches `:focus-visible`, and on `#newmenu`'s `toggle` — through the existing document capture `toggle` listener (`app.js` 612), whose first act, before its `details.more[open]` filter and `placeMenu`, is `AO.barDecide()` (so a shut re-reads too; on any other `<details>` it changes nothing) (the `decide()` that `AO.holdBar` exposes, a no-op on a page without a bar), so the bar is back in place before the menu is placed from it; no second toggle listener is added. `AO.barAway`'s rules, first match wins, thresholds strict: (a) `held` → `{away: false, anchorY: y}`; (b) `y <= barH + 4` → `{away: false, anchorY: y}` (the 4 px keeps a jittery scroll at the bar's height from popping it out and back); (c) shown (`!away`): `y − anchorY > 4` → `{away: true, anchorY: y}`, else `{away: false, anchorY: min(anchorY, y)}`; (d) away: `anchorY − y > 4` → `{away: false, anchorY: y}`, else `{away: true, anchorY: max(anchorY, y)}`. A `change` listener on the same `matchMedia` removes `away` and sets `anchorY` to the clamped `y`, either way. The ≤720px CSS puts the transition on `.topbar.away` only, so the bar slides out and returns at once (no transform left for **+ New ▾**'s fixed menu to be placed against); `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { .topbar.away { transition: none } }` is a new block. `.rail`'s new `top` is every rail's: the Inbox rail (`inbox_rail.html`) and Focus's put-away rail (`.side.rail`); the Sessions page has none. (3) Nothing else moves: `AO.termFill` and the popped-out Focus's height are unchanged, and so are the keys.

**Done when** on a wide screen every page's bar stays at the top while scrolled, above the cards and below a card's open menu, a toast and a dialog, with **+ New ▾**'s menu opening over the page; the Inbox rail and Focus's put-away rail ride under it; a `scrollIntoView` target (an Inbox jump, a rollup jump) lands below it; on a 390 px phone the bar slides away scrolling down and back scrolling up, stays while its **+ New ▾** menu is open, does not come back on a bounce at the page's foot (iOS Safari, the address bar collapsed), is not held by a tapped ◐ or **?**, and a jump with the bar away lands 8 px from the top; a Tab out of the bar to a control already in view, with the page scrolled down, lets it go on the next scroll down of more than 4 px, not before; resizing a window across 720 px, at the top or mid-page with the bar away, shows the bar; a Tab into the bar while it is away brings it back; Focus's terminal height at the page's top (its `--termh`) reads the same before and after; `AO.barAway` has a unit test of each rule (a)–(d), with a slow run of 2 px steps, a reversal and the clamp's ceiling (the gate for the iOS bounce, which is otherwise checked by hand on a device), in the node harness the UI's pure functions use (as `tests/test_ui_composer_bar.py` does); and a shot of a scrolled page served by `scripts/look_home.py`, wide and phone, is in the PR.

**Resolved:** 2026-10-28 (grinder-ao-1, #2606) — `app.css` holds the bar (`--barh`, sticky at z 30, the scroll padding, every rail's `top`, `.topbar.away` on a phone with its 150 ms slide and none under reduced motion); `app.js` `AO.barAway` (rules (a)–(d)) and `AO.holdBar` (the `ResizeObserver`, one `decide()` on scroll, focus and the width's change, the clamp), and the toggle listener's first act `AO.barDecide()`; `tests/test_ui_held_bar.py` tests each rule, a wired slow run, the reversal and the clamp's ceiling. The UI check on `scripts/look_home.py`, wide and phone, is in the PR with `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-10-28-td1192-*.png`.
