## TD-2252: Nothing tests filling a built-in seat that last ran on another tool or an older profile

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-26
**Id take:** c663f9799fd142318e38c20acfb055d5

**Why:** Audit of #4717 (TD-2068 slice 4). A fill replays the record's last launch, so `_builtin_login` hands `create` two arguments that override it: `"adapter": logins.split(login)[0]` and `"profile": ""`. Both are needed: `_on_login` (`src/sessionorc/agent_common.py`) refuses a launch on a login whose `adapter` is not the login's tool, and one that names a profile other than the login's (design §4.9e *The launch on a login*).

Every record in `tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py` is written with adapter `hookstub`, the tool of both logins, and no profile, so neither argument changes anything there. TD-2068's Log (2026-11-23, round 8) also lists, among the build's tests, that a record the home wrote is filled from the launch written with it, on the set's model and first usable login.

Each of these removed alone from the returned mapping left the 47 test modules that mention `cut_over`, `_first_shape_off`, `builtin` or `unfillable` green at b6f5c8850 (1239 passed), and tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py, test_builtinstore.py, test_teamstore.py and test_team_show.py green at 28715c324 (122 passed):

- `"adapter": logins.split(login)[0],`
- `"profile": "",`

Probe (a temporary test over the module's fixtures: a tech lead's record written held before the cut-over, then due, with the set's first login `hookstub:first` usable). The unchanged code fills both on that login. With its argument removed the fill fails and counts as a failed restart:

- record on adapter `shell`, without `adapter`: *login hookstub:first runs hookstub, not shell: a launch on a login runs its tool*;
- record on profile `oldprof` of a scratch `profiles.yml`, without `profile`: *a launch on login hookstub:first names no profile of its own, not 'oldprof'*.

The profile case is any fill after one on another login, since a fill on a login writes that login's name as the record's and the launch record's profile (`rpc_create`): without the argument, a seat whose first login reaches its reserve could not be filled on the next. The adapter case is a fill whose pick is on another tool than the record's last run. A break would fail each such fill, counted towards the seat's fill ceiling (design §6 rule 3).

With both removed together with the other changes that TD-2251 to TD-2255 name, the whole suite at b6f5c8850 (`pytest -n 4`) passed (10259 passed, 24 skipped).
