## TD-1315: The Queue page's re-read shuts a card's Read fold the person opened (and a ✓ n done fold, and an open ⋯ menu)

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-02: built in #2812
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the Queue's `reread()` in `AO.queue`, ~4703–4726: `lists.innerHTML = …` then `AO.reopenFolds(lists)`; `AO.reopenFolds` and its `foldsOpen`/`foldsShut` press capture, ~1604–1634, which match only `details.fold, details.cardline` with a `data-fold`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html` (`read()` ~80, `<details class="qread">` with no key; `fold()` ~124, `<details class="qfold">` with no key; `dots()` ~30, `details.more.qdots`); the Repo page's board, which draws the same partials and re-reads them (`AO.qcards` ~4852, the re-reads ~3534–3560); `docs/design/4.5a-controls.md` (row *Queue page* — **card**, ~128: *Read under the rows, folded with its count*); `docs/design/4.5-ui.md` (screen 12, ~1436: *The page re-reads on the `queue` and `inbox` events*)

**Why:** Paul (2026-11-02, with two screenshots of TD-1310's card): "There seems to be a page redraw that happens automatically on the Queue page, for example when the read section is opened … it automatically closes … items should not open/close on a page without user action." He opened the card's **Read (5)** and, with no press, it was shut again. The page re-reads its cards on every `queue` and `inbox` event (`reread()` replaces `#queuelists`' HTML), and then puts back only what it knows the person opened: a Focus line's body (`.qdline`, by key), each card's own fold (`qfold` in the browser's store), and the *details* folds `AO.reopenFolds` tracks. A card's Read fold is `details.qread` with no `data-fold`, and the press capture listens only on `details.fold > summary` and `details.cardline > summary`, so its open state is never recorded and every re-read draws it closed — although `reopenFolds`' own comment says *a card's Read line too (TD-911)*. The same holds for a parent's or a card's **✓ n done** fold (`details.qfold`, §4.4 *Rows*; `queue_row.html`'s `fold()`: *a press opens it in place*) and for a card's open **⋯** menu (`details.qdots`). On a busy org the events come every few seconds, so a Read the person is reading closes under them; a control that moves without a press is the harm, and the person cannot tell a re-read from a bug. The Inbox already keeps this rule for its rows (§4.5a *Inbox board row* — **detail block**: *the person's press either way holds while the page lives … a fold drawn open is not reopened by the next poll*), and the Queue's design states no such rule for its own folds.

**Fix:** keep every fold and menu on a Queue card as the person left it across a re-read, while the page lives (never stored, as the Inbox's folds are not):
- Give `details.qread` and `details.qfold` a stable `data-fold` key (the card's id plus `read`; the fold's parent id, or the card's id plus `done` for a loose fold) and have the press capture and `AO.reopenFolds` cover them; or keep their open keys in `reread()` as it keeps `.qdline`s. An open **⋯** menu survives the swap (or the re-read waits while one is open, as it waits while a card is dragged).
- Audit the Queue part and the Repo page's board for any other `<details>` a re-read draws, so none opens or shuts without a press.
- The design says it in the same PR: §4.5a row *Queue page* — **card**, a sentence that the Read fold, a **✓ n done** fold and the **⋯** menu hold the person's press while the page lives and a re-read never opens or shuts them (§4.5 screen 12 beside *The page re-reads*), with a `design-history.md` line.
- Done when: a test drives `reread()` (node, as `tests/test_ui_org_plus.py` drives `AO` functions) with a Read and a ✓ n done fold opened by a press and one shut, and both survive a swap; and on a scratch home, an opened Read stays open across an `inbox` event.

**Related:** TD-911 (the Read fold, built in #2181), TD-891 (folded details filled from what was kept), TD-138 and TD-312 (the Inbox's fold memory), TD-1131 (✓ n done), TD-1310 (the card in Paul's screenshots).

**Resolved:** 2026-11-02 — #2812: Read and ✓ n done folds keyed (`data-fold`) and kept by `AO.reopenFolds` across a re-read; the re-read waits while a menu is open; §4.5 screen 12 and §4.5a *Queue page: card* say so; checked on a scratch home (grinder-ao-2).
