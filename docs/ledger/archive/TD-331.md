**Resolved:** 2026-10-05 (PR #1097) — `src/agentorc/team_skill.md`: seven roles with `designer`'s row (`repoconfig.PRESETS`); the member keys as `org.MEMBER_KEYS` has them; `on_call` and *(on call)* explained (`org.ON_CALL_DEFAULT`, design §6 *A manager on call*); the login `ao team start` does not check, and how to check it; commit and push before the start, since a worktree is cut from `origin/HEAD` (`gitinfo` worktree add); `--anyway`; this machine's name in `hosts.yml` or `hostname -s`.
