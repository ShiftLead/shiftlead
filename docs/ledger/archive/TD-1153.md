## TD-1153: A team's flow, logins, session counts and settings are scattered or hand edits: a team page picks its flow, orders its logins, sets how many sessions each stage may run and the team's settings

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-27
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-25

**Why:** Paul, 2026-10-27: "soon we will want users to be able to control team defs and flows from the UI (maybe a separate page per team)". Today the Members dialog edits only an `org.yml` team, and only its members; a repo's `.shiftlead.yml` team is read-only on the page, so changing its roles, briefs, lanes or flows is a hand edit, a PR, a merge and the checkout's pull. Agreed in TD-1149's conversation: the definition stays in its file — versioned and reviewed with the briefs it names — and the page becomes its editor, beside the team's settings (TD-1149 part 2's size first).

**Location:** `src/shiftlead/org.py` (the definitions), `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`/api/teams/{name}/members`, `/api/teams/{name}/flow/apply`), `src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py` (the Teams card); design §4.9 *Add or remove a member from the team card* (TD-163), §4.9c *Every listed flow must be followable*, §4.5a *Members dialog* and *Settings page* **Teams**, §5 *Configuration*, [ADR 2026-09-25](../../decisions/2026-09-25-settings-audit.md)

**Ask:** Should the builders build the team page design, starting with the settings rail and each team's running settings?

**Fix (to design):** Designed as §4.9d, with Paul (2026-11-21). A team is settings only: a fixed name with a label, one repo, a host, the flow it runs, its logins in the order they are tried, one number per stage for sessions at once with an optional limit for the whole team, and its running settings; it has no members and nothing of it is in a repo's file. The page sits behind the gear with Tools, Flows, Briefs and Built-in seats, draws the flow as lines of stops with the entries in each, and switches the flow at once behind a preview of what happens to each entry. What the round reversed (the definition edited by pull request, the change tray, seat count ranges) is in docs/history/TD-1153.md. The five built-in seats (tech lead, manager, board answers, pull requests nobody holds, sweeper) leave the flows: a built-in set gives their models and logins, a repo uses one set, `default` unless it names another, and a Built-in seats page sets them. Its builds are TD-2059 to TD-2066, in the order docs/reviews/TD-1153.md gives.

**Done when:** The eight builds have merged.
