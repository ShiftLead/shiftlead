**Resolved:** 2026-11-12 (PR #3534) — tests/test_taken_over.py

**Done when:** Tests pin each of these:
- `mail.taken_over` is true for an idle interactive member (team, not `by_person`), and for one also suspended. It is false for a person's own session, a `by_person` record with `controllers`, an unattended member, an ended record and a shell.
- `unanswered`'s `taken_over` state and `_held_mail` read it. `_held_mail` draws no row for a `scheduled` member, and `unanswered` reads a record from before `by_person` as `mail.by_person` does.
- `_view` carries `taken_over` with `taken_lead`, `taken_at` and `taken_tail`, for a flip's stamp, a start's stamp and no stamp. It carries none on a node, with the host link down, or on a record whose own state is `unreachable`.
- The rendered full card of a taken-over member carries `tko`, *taken over* and the composed hover. The compact card carries `tko` and its last line in each of the three forms. The person's own interactive session carries none of them.
- A taken-over `needs-you` card keeps the tint under its amber border.
- A taken-over member's card, full and compact, leads its foot with Hand back (`tko`), never Close session.
  - A `needs-you` one keeps `next_act()`'s lead (Allow / Deny where answerable, else Focus or Details; Focus on the compact card), with Hand back after.
  - It reads no *ready to close ✓*, and the header's *ready to close* does not count it.
  - Its more ⋯ has no *Switch to unattended*.
  - Focus still offers Close session on it.
- The card's Hand back calls `set_mode` to unattended with `via: card` and the usage gate's texts, and clears a stop time that fell due, as Focus's does.
- `app.css` defines `--taken` and `--taken-fg` in the light and dark blocks.
- A taken-over card keeps `data-mine`. Its hover elements carry `data-taken-at`, and the compact line's age is an `.age[data-since]` span.
- The page's live behaviour (the ticker's titles, `fill()`'s copy of `tko` onto the sheet's button) is checked in the build's Playwright render on the scratch home, as the shot is.
- The full card's first render carries the hover's title with a server-drawn age.
- The team header reads *1 taken over* with the member's name in its hover, nothing at 0, and nothing on a non-team header. The rollup draws nothing of it.
- Focus's Hand back, in the acts slot and in the sheet's `fmodemenu` entry, carries `tko` on a taken-over member. It does not on a `by_person` record with `controllers`. The class comes off both after a live Hand back (`app.js`'s `mode_act` update and the sheet's `fill()`).

The build also renders a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`) with a taken-over member, light and dark. Its shot is committed and matches the design's mockups.
