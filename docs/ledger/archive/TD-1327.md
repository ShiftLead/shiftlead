## TD-1327: `/sw.js` draws a push whose `path` is not an in-app path as `/inbox`, and §4.10 *The service worker* says only that a missing field does

**Priority:** low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-14
**Id take:** 82f0b3a53da54da8a8de0be2bf5d765e

**Why:** §4.10 *The service worker* says a payload "that is absent, not JSON, or missing a field is drawn as *something needs you*, `path` `/inbox`, `tag` `more`", and that a tap navigates "to `path`". The built worker does more: `inApp(path)` keeps a `path` only when it starts with `/` and resolves to this origin, and `payloadOf` otherwise draws the push with the received `body` and `tag` but `path` `/inbox`; `notificationclick` re-checks it. So a push with a well-formed `body` and `tag` and a `path` such as `https://other.example/` or `//evil.example/x` is drawn with its own text and opens `/inbox`, which is neither of the design's two cases (all-fields-good, or fallback with *something needs you*). The rule is sound (a tap never leaves the app) but no line of §4.10 or §4.5a states it, and `tests/test_ui_push_card.py` is the only place it is held.
