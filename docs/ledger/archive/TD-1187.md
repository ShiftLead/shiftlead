## TD-1187: The top bar scrolls away with the page: once scrolled down, the tabs, + New and the counts are out of reach

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-28 (techlead-ao-1, handed by Paul, m-96f8956195a1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Resolved
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/base.html` (`.topbar`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.topbar` 80, `.rail { position: sticky; top: 12px }` 215, the `@media (max-width: 720px)` blocks, `body.popped .termbox`'s `calc(100vh - 150px …)` 511); design §4.5a *Sessions top bar* rows (*the bar's three groups* 119), §4.5 *Browser mechanics*

**Why:** Paul, 2026-10-28: *Our menu at the top of the page scrolls with the page. Lets change it to be static, so that after I scroll down I still have access to the menu. Open to other approaches if there is a better solution.* The top bar is an ordinary block at the top of `<body>`, so on a long Sessions, Inbox, Queue or Repo page it leaves the screen, and with it the place tabs (1 Sessions, 2 Inbox, 3 Queue), their needs-you numbers, **+ New ▾**, Settings and the usage and build chips. The keys (`1`–`3`, `n`) still reach them, but the mouse and a phone have to scroll back up. No design line says whether the bar stays.

**Fix:** design it first, then a build. It is a layout choice with knock-ons the design should settle, not one CSS line: (1) the bar held at the top of the viewport (`position: sticky; top: 0` on `.topbar`, with a z-index above cards and below menus, dialogs and toasts), or another approach Paul left open: a slim bar that keeps the tabs and **+ New** once scrolled, or a back-to-top control; (2) the Sessions and Inbox rail, sticky at `top: 12px` today, would slide under a held bar, so its offset follows the bar's height; (3) on a phone (≤720px) the bar wraps to two rows, and holding both eats the screen (TD-003's narrow Focus), so the design says whether a phone holds it, holds the tabs row only, or lets it scroll; (4) Focus sizes its terminal from the viewport, so a held bar must not shrink it twice or overlap it, and a popped-out Focus has no bar; (5) an anchor or `scrollIntoView` target (a row the Inbox jumps to) needs `scroll-margin-top` so a held bar does not cover it. Write the rule in §4.5 *Browser mechanics* and §4.5a's top-bar row, with a shot of each page scrolled, wide and phone, then file the build as `Part of: TD-1187`.

**Done when** the design says what the top bar does when a page is scrolled, on a wide screen and on a phone, and a build entry that implements it is filed as its part.

**Resolved:** 2026-10-28 (grinder-ao-1, #2606) — its one part, TD-1192 (#2606), built the held bar as design §4.5 *Browser mechanics* *The top bar is held* says.
