**Resolved:** 2026-11-06 (PR #3047)

**Done when:** Tests show that:
- **`item_home`** gives `tied` for each case:
  - a board line's `context`;
  - mail's `about` and `words`;
  - a red PR's `pr`;
  - a fork's PR's `title`;
  - a bare ref on another repo's board, its `ref` qualified with the line's own repo;
  - `inbox`, with None.
- **`ledger_home`** gives `entry`.
- **`attach(raw=True)`** gives each kind's `row`, `tied` and `repo`, and `mentioned_items`, while `mentioned` keeps its shape and the page's `queue_view` runs on it unchanged.
- **`card_view`** finds a card by its top entry, a row, an archived row, an unnormalised `TD-5`, a bare `TD-n` and an origin path. It answers None, `ambiguous` and `completed` as above.
- **Under `words=False`** a fixture whose message has text and whose answered rows carry `words` and `outcome.text` (a ledger row's `answered`, `card.top.answered`, `card.rows[*].answered`) shows none of those words anywhere in the JSON, and the in-process road shows them; no mail item or `mentioned` entry carries a mail's words, while a board line keeps its text, detail and answers. `mail` reads `ids` against `full`.
- **`Readings.card`** gives the card the Queue page draws for the same readings, and raises each of the three `CardRefused` kinds from a stubbed call.
- **The endpoint** answers its five statuses.
- **The Queue page's and the Inbox's existing tests** pass with `ledger_view` moved, those that compare an `item_home` result or an Inbox row exactly updated for `tied`; the Inbox's payload carries `tied` on each row.
- **The result is JSON-serialisable** (the endpoint's answer for a fixture card with dates in its rows).
