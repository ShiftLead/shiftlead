Until (1)–(2) are built the briefs' sentence is the rule, and the figure is changed by editing them and restarting the team. **Paul, 2026-09-22, on (3) and (4):** a start at the reset is *a nice idea* but **configurable and off by default** (*less surprising to a user*), one more setting on the page beside the wind-down figures for the session and the weekly window; *decent, not vital* — written down here and in TD-026, not scheduled. The page's first settings are therefore three: the 5-hour figure, the weekly figure, and *start the team at the weekly reset* (off). **Paul, later the same morning — the shape of the figures:** what he actually keeps back is *about 30% of each session and 10% of the weekly in reserve for each day's interactive work*, so the settings are **a reserve per session** (the 5-hour window's threshold is steady: 100 − 30 = 70%) and **a reserve per day** for the weekly window, and the gate computes the weekly threshold from the days left before the reset: `100 − reserve_per_day × days_left`, today counted whole — 10% a day gives 30% on the reset day itself, 60% with four days left, 90% on the last day; the reset time is the adapter's (§4.3). A consequence: the weekly threshold **rises** as the week goes, so §6's *pause … resume* is right for it after all — a team paused at 60% on a Wednesday resumes on Thursday when the line moves to 70% — and the wind-down-with-declaration is what happens when the window is out for the *last* day. The board item's *figure to write back after the reset* is now the pair (30, 10), which the briefs can say until the gate is built. **Paul agreed (30, 10) the same day**, and asked whether today's 95% would have been (30, 5): yes for the last day, but a reserve of 5 a day moves the whole week's line (65% on the reset day, not 30%), so a one-day change is an **override with an expiry** — *reserve 5 today*, gone at the reset, the standing pair untouched — which is TD-026's override shape — **and not this entry's** (Paul, 2026-09-22: *I do not think we need a mechanism built for temporary percentage settings immediately, that seems brittle and probably not the current priority; the user can handle it manually for now*): a one-day change is done by hand, and the mechanism is TD-101, if ever.
