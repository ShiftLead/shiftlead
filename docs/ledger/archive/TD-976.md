## TD-976: The record's `prompts`: the adapter's `prompts` read from the transcript, who sent each, the reply's first line (TD-975's first build)

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-22 (the designer, from TD-975)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** live-check #2299
**Status:** Built 2026-10-22 (grinder-ao-1, #2299): the read, the neutral `Prompt`, the captured fixture and its conformance test, `merge_prompts`, the tick pass with its hook-due mark, the node-owned field and its trimmed view; every *Done when* test but the live one. **Left, read on a scratch home once #2299 is merged** (the merged code runs there whether or not it is live): a session of `ao new` (Claude Code) lists the prompt typed into its terminal in `ao status --json`'s `prompts` as `from: person` within a tick of its `UserPromptSubmit`, and an `ao send`'s prompt under the sender's id.
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/adapters.py` (`OPTIONAL_METHODS`, a neutral `Prompt`), `src/shiftlead/adapters/claude_code/transcript.py` and `__init__.py` (`prompts`, `implements`), `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_refresh_links`'s pass; `_apply_event`, the due mark), `src/sessionorc/models.py` (`RECENT_PROMPTS` beside `RECENT_LINKS`, the `prompts` field, `NODE_OWNED`, `Session.view`, `to_dict`, `from_dict`), `tests/conformance/`; design §4.2 *The record's `prompts`*, §4.3 `prompts`

**Why:** the Focus Prompts card (TD-975, TD-977) reads its rows from the record, which holds no prompts today. The Transcript page's read is on demand and goes backwards by turns; the card wants the newest twenty on every Focus read without opening the file.

**Fix:** as design §4.2 *The record's `prompts`* and §4.3 `prompts` say:
- A neutral `Prompt(at, text, drawn, whole, reply)` and `prompts` in `OPTIONAL_METHODS`. `whole` is set when `drawn` holds the first line uncut (the line ≤ forty characters); the record keeps it beside `drawn`.
- Claude Code's `prompts`: the top-level `user` entries `_prompt_text` reads as prompts, past the cursor. It leaves out the lines no person typed: entries marked `isMeta`, a local command's output (`<local-command-stdout>`), the compaction summary, which `_prompt_text` already drops, the interrupt lines (`[Request interrupted by user]` and `… for tool use]`), and an entry whose text is only a `<task-notification>` or a `<system-reminder>`. The list may grow as the fixture shows more. A slash command the person typed stays a prompt, rendered as the line typed (`/x args`, from its `<command-name>` and `<command-args>`) for `text` and `drawn`, never as its tags. `text` holds the first 300 characters. `drawn` is the tool's glyph and the first non-empty line's first forty characters, cut at forty and not at a word, then trailing spaces trimmed, since `capture-pane` trims a row's. Its exact form (the glyph's indent, a non-breaking space after it, as `composer()` reads the composer's row) is taken from the captured pane, not assumed. `reply` is the first non-empty line of the first top-level text after the prompt, capped at 200; the leading reply is the first such line past the cursor before any prompt.
- The conformance fixture is captured, never written: one conversation of a few prompts on the walk's `conformance` profile, with its transcript and the pane's capture (`capture-pane -p -S - -E -`), including a prompt typed twice, a short one (*yes*), a slash command with arguments and its output, a meta line, and an interrupt. The current transcript fixtures hold none of the last three. The suite asserts the neutral shape, and that each `drawn` opens a row of the pane, both compared as the walk compares them (leading whitespace stripped, trailing spaces trimmed, `\xa0` read as a space).
- The tick pass beside `links`, on its own cursor (an in-memory dict like `_links_cursor`, dropped with the record's other per-record state in the tick's cleanup), once per `LINKS_EVERY`. A record whose hook reported, since its last read, a neutral event with `prompt`, or a main-thread `idle` (no `subagent`; never a `SubagentStop`), is due on the next tick, a mark set in `_apply_event` (`agent_tick.py`, which `rpc_hook` calls). The core never matches a hook's own name (`UserPromptSubmit`, `Stop`).
- Each entry is merged by `at` and `text`. A new entry's `from` comes from the record's `sends` at that moment, each entry matched once, as §4.2 says. A prompt whose text begins with `mail.BRIEF_LINE` (typed as keys before the paste, `agent.py` `_type`) is *the brief*, entry or no entry, consuming the *(the brief)* entry when there is one; the entry is stamped after the composer took the brief (`agent_wake.py`), possibly after the hook-due read. An unconsumed *(the brief)* entry names the earliest prompt within a minute either side of its time, also `from: brief` (never the entry's own `system`). Any other entry: the oldest unmatched one with the same stripped first line, both cut to 300 characters, made before the prompt (the entry is written at the gate, before the paste, `_record_send`), whatever its verdict. An entry already held keeps its `from` and takes a `reply` while it has none. Else the person. A prompt beginning with `BRIEF_LINE` is stored `from: brief`, and the card draws `brief` as *the brief* and `system` as *the policy*. `sends` grows on both sides and is merged by id (`models.GROWS`); the read uses the copy on the node that holds the pane. The leading reply fills the newest entry's `reply` while it has none, and only on a read from a cursor past 0.
- The home's doorbell is left out by the core, never by the adapter: a prompt whose first line has `mail.unread_line`'s form. It is typed with no `sends` entry, so it would otherwise read as the person's. The newest twenty are kept (`RECENT_PROMPTS = 20`). A new tool session id starts the list and the cursor again.
- The field is a `Session` field with its `to_dict`/`from_dict`, node-owned (`models.NODE_OWNED`, beside `links`, with `test_models`' ownership pin), and rides every view of the record, deltas included, as `links` does: Focus redraws its side cards from each delta (`app.js` `render(ev.session)`). `Session.view()` trims each entry, `text` to 120 characters and `reply` to 100; `agent_serve._view` needs no change of its own. A `keys` entry (recorded by `rpc_keys` through `_record_send`, with no kind of its own) needs no exclusion: its text is key names, which no prompt's first line is.
- Gemini CLI does not declare `prompts` in this entry: its card is absent until a captured pane of its prompts gives it a `drawn`.

**Done when** the conformance suite passes on the captured fixture. A tick test on a scratch home shows a prompt, its `from`, and a reply filled by the next read; another shows a record read on the tick after its `prompt` hook, not a minute later; another shows two queued sends with one first line matched oldest first; another an `ao send` longer than 300 characters matched; another the brief read before its *(the brief)* entry is written, still *the brief*. A view test shows `prompts` in a stream delta, trimmed. A full record's view grows by no more than 8 KB, and a `list` of 30 full records stays under 1 MB. Live: on a session of `ao new` on a scratch home, `ao status --json` lists the prompt typed into the terminal as the person's within a tick of its `UserPromptSubmit`, and an `ao send`'s prompt under the sender's id.
