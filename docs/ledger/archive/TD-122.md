## TD-122: The usage chip polls once per profile, so four profiles on one account rate-limit the endpoint and every chip holds a stale reading; the chip names the profile, never the tool or the account

**Priority:** High
**Added:** 2026-09-23 (Paul, comparing the chip with the account's usage page; the anchor session)
**Status:** Open — **the live check failed 2026-09-28**: with one poll per account, the endpoint still refused every poll from 22:26Z for six hours or more (TD-231 investigates, TD-230 shows the age and bounds the gate). **built 2026-09-23 (grinder-ao-1, PR #517): the poll, its back-off and its cache keyed on the account through the adapter's `account_for`, `label` on the contract, per-model weekly windows, one chip per account; the hover's per-profile reserve, days left and next move (the techlead's read of #517) in PR #520; what is left is the live look on `docs/user_attention.md`.** **Designed 2026-09-23 (the designer): §4.2a *The usage reading is the account's*, §4.3 (`label`, `usage()` once per account, every window carried), §4.4 *Usage* (the poll, the cache, the back-off and the drop keyed on the account) and §4.5a **usage** chip (one per account, `<tool> · <account> · <label> n%`) are the spec; §6's gate reads the account's reading against the profile's lines. A grinder builds it; `src/sessionorc`, so the reader merges.** *Done when*: with two profiles on one account live, `_refresh_usage` calls the adapter once per tick of the cadence and both profiles' `usage` answers carry the same `fetched`; a `rate_limited` reply backs off both; the chip on the Org reads *Claude · paul · week n%* once, with both profiles and their sessions on hover; a profile's reserve line still draws from `gate` per profile; the adapter carries a per-model weekly window as `week · <model>` when the endpoint reports one and the worst-window rule takes it in; the shell adapter's `label` exists and its chip still does not; tests cover the once-per-account poll, the shared back-off and the per-model label. Found 2026-09-23. Since the profiles were split by role (`grind`, `grind-sonnet`, `grind-fable`, and `default`, all `account: paul`), the host agent's usage poll (`_refresh_usage`, `USAGE_EVERY` five minutes) asks the endpoint once **per profile**, four calls where one would do; the endpoint answered `rate_limited` to every call from 16:10Z on and asked for an hour (`retry_after: 3600`), so the chip holds the 16:10Z reading dimmed with *· stale* — `5h 12% · week 21%` when the account's page read 7% and 24% — and prints it four times, once per profile, as *week 21% · stale grind · week 21% / 40% · stale +1*. Two faults: the poll and the chip are keyed on the profile when the quota is the **account's** (§4.2a: one login, one usage window, however many profiles share it); and the chip never names the tool or the account, so *grind · week 21%* reads as nothing to the person who knows their account as *Claude, paul* (Paul 2026-09-23; TD-071 item 8 said the same of the older chip). A third: the endpoint reports a per-model weekly window beside *All models* (the account page shows *Fable 17%*) that the reading does not carry. **Next:** built (PR #517, the reader merges); what is left is the live look after a promote.
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/agent.py` (`_refresh_usage`, `_usage`, `_usage_wait`, `USAGE_EVERY`; the `usage` and `gate` RPCs), `src/agentorc/adapters/claude_code/__init__.py` (`usage`, `UsageRefused`, `_retry_after`), design §4.2 (*usage*), §4.2a (accounts), §4.5a **usage** chip, §6 *Usage gate* (its reserves are per profile today), `src/agentorc/ui/static/app.js` (`#usagechip`)

**Why:** a stale number at the moment it is looked at is the failure the chip exists to prevent, and this one is self-inflicted: the fourth profile turned a working poll into a refused one. The gate (§6) reads the same readings, so a stale reading also holds every unattended session against a line that moved an hour ago.

**Resolved:** 2026-10-04 (PR #517; the live check by Paul and the anchor) — one chip per account: the Org reads *Claude · paul · week 68% / 95%*, which Paul confirmed against the account's usage page on 2026-10-04, and `ao gate` shows `grind` and `grind-fable` on one reading (*read 5s ago (reported)*). The reading itself is TD-233's (design §4.4 *Usage, reported first and asked for last*).
