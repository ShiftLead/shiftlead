**Why:** A session whose login reaches its reserve or a limit is to carry on, on the next login of its list, from where it stopped (the frame, A stopped seat moves; design 4.9e, A session moves down the list). Nothing moves a session today, and no adapter reads a transcript into a handoff.

**Ask:** Should the builders build a session's move to the next login and the handoff from its transcript?

**Resolved:** 2026-11-26 (PR #4790) — design 4.9e A session moves down the list; tests/test_login_move.py

**Done when:** On a fixture: a stage's session whose login is marked limited is relaunched on the next login under the same record with the handoff text in what it is told, its stage record's holder, offers and counts unchanged; while its record is marked moving a pass of the home's tick takes no act on it, and no crash restart starts it; a relaunch that fails leaves the holder lost by the Flows rule; a host agent restarted mid-move clears the mark and the holder is lost; with no later login it waits; an interactive session stays. Each of the three adapters returns a fixture transcript's turns as text, newest last, and an adapter without the method hands over nothing.
