## TD-2117: The Queue's Refresh stays in reach while the page is scrolled down

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-23
**Id take:** 6c29517225fd4567a261b916ac0f465d

**Why:** When the Queue changes, its bar says what is waiting (*1 card moved*) and offers **Refresh**, but the bar sits in the page above the first group. A person scrolled down cannot see that anything changed, or press Refresh, without scrolling back to the top. The Queue deliberately holds still while it is read (design 4.5 screen 12, *The page holds still*), so this bar is the only sign that what is on screen is out of date: out of sight, a person can go on reading and acting on an old picture. The person reported it on 2026-11-23 with a screenshot of the bar. A card's page draws the same bar in the same way.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css (.qbar), src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Queue's and card page's bars), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue.html, src/shiftlead/ui/templates/card_page.html, design 4.5 screen 12 The page holds still, design 4.5a Queue page: the bar, Refresh

**Resolved:** 2026-11-23 (PR #4432) — design 4.5 screen 12 The page holds still

**Done when:** On the Queue and on a card's page, scrolled down with a change waiting, the bar's words and **Refresh** are on screen under the top bar at wide and phone widths, and pressing Refresh keeps the first visible card in place. With nothing waiting the bar scrolls away with the page. No card's menu, toast or dialog is drawn under the bar, and a jump to a card lands below it.
