A held seat fills for no cause (`asks`, `prs`, `every`, `swept`), takes no `unfillable` word on a gated profile or at the ceiling, keeps `seat_due`, and carries `stop_held` from the first pass whether due or not; a seat with no `team` is never held; an `ask` sent by `ao msg --team` and `ao td add --repo` lands in its mailbox, is not routed around, and its reply line is the held sentence with the bound's tail — also for the first send after a Stop, before any pass; the ask lapses at its bound as on any closed seat. A seat the person killed (`exited`, held) keeps its questions through the Start; the held sentence is the reply to `ao td add --repo` (`_entry_read_when`), the lapse stamp's tail and the composer sentence under the seat's card; a note to a suspended seat of a stopped team reads the suspended row, not the held one, and a closed member that is not a seat reads its own row; a record with an `ended` mark and a seat closer is read by its `ended`. After `ao team start` (with and without a start time) the record `create` writes carries no `stop_held` and the seat is filled on its standing cause on the next pass with the mail intact, for a team with members and for a team of seats alone. `work_waiting` carries `seats_held` (`asks_waiting` per seat), written with no lane news under `ask` and `start` (`members` empty), survives the `held` strip, leaves `held` (the start bound) as it was, survives Dismiss, and is not written under `off`. A lapsed ask to a held seat is stamped `{state: held, since, team, stopped_by}`, not `not_filled`, `gated` or `fills_exhausted` (a held seat at its ceiling reads `held`), its `by` the asked record's; `stopped_by` reads `person` for a Wind down left undeclared and a seat close twenty minutes later, as `seats_held_by` does; `seats_held` is attached on every `_work_mark` return and keys no seat with nothing waiting; `rpc_work_start` on a held-only mark (a stopped team's or a wound-down one's) answers `started: false` and keeps the mark, `rpc_clear_work` `cleared: false`; the send after a person's Restart of one record reads no hold on the other seats; the reply line to a person sending ends *Start the team to have it read* and reads *wound down* for a wound-down team; a PR whose reader seat is held reads not acting (`held`) in rule 13, where TD-1496 has landed; a record loaded with the old `seat_held` `{by: checkout}` mark draws and holds nothing. Telegram tells a held-only `until` mark once and a `person` one never, on its own row `held:<team>` from `seats_held_at`, also for a mark with lane ids and under `on_work: start` with a `held` bound, also on a wound-down mark, which carries no `stopped`; a handed entry with a blank Team pick goes to team B's live techlead, not team A's held seat, and `_move_handed` skips a held seat; a held-only mark on a wound-down team under `on_work: start` starts nothing; a Start of a team of seats alone (its seat replayed held, `closer` `{by: start, why: held}`) does not hold again; a person's Restart of one record lifts the hold on the team's other seats; a person's Close of one member within `FINISHED_SETTLE` of the rest finishing holds. Live: after a Stop of a team with a question seat, a question to it fills nothing and the journal logs no *the seat is due (asks) — filling it* until the Start.
