**Why:** §6 rule 13 *A reason that names its own end waits* lists a team held `until` (its instant) among the reasons that wait. But rule 8 (`agent_tick` `_work_hold`) writes `held: {why: "until"}` only once the team's `until` is at or before now, so the instant `stuck.comes_back` reads is always past and `stuck.waiting_until` never draws such a team at *waiting*. Either the design means another instant (the next start the team may make), or `until` belongs with the reasons that are a need. Found in the review of TD-1496 slice 3 (#3435).
