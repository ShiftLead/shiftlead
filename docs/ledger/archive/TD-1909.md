**Why:** TD-1904 (#4031) bounds what the usage poll writes to the journal: `_shape` keeps at most 12 top-level keys (`sorted(...)[:12]`) and cuts the whole cause at 200 characters, and its docstring says it is "bounded for the journal". `test_a_body_with_any_window_is_a_reading_and_one_with_none_names_its_shape` (tests/test_claude_adapter.py) feeds only bodies of at most three keys and a short cause, so neither bound is reached. Reverted on origin/main 9ed79347a, with `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pdm run pytest -q tests/test_claude_adapter.py -k "usage or window or body"`: dropping `[:200]` leaves 6 passed; dropping `[:12]` leaves 6 passed. A body with hundreds of keys, or one long key, would write an unbounded line to the host agent's log with no test failing; the bound the test does hold (`math.isfinite`) fails when reverted.
