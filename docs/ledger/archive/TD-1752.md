## TD-1752: A hook's limited ended by the next prompt leaves limited_hook_at on the record

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-13
**Id take:** 34132c96a15944abb63924ab71dd3002

**Why:** TD-1750 (#3644) moved a hook's `limited` mark onto the record as `Session.limited_hook_at`. It is cleared only by the poll's lift or by the poll's own `limited`. A `UserPromptSubmit` that ends a hook's `limited` leaves the mark on the record, and the field's comment says it is None once the lift ends it. The old in-memory map had the same leftover; now it also persists across restarts. A later poll or screen `limited` on the session then waits for a reading fetched after the stale time, at most `USAGE_FRESH`, before it lifts. That is a delay, not a wrong state. The techlead's read of #3644 noted it (m-ebe3675d5eb1).

**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_apply_event), src/sessionorc/models.py (limited_hook_at)

**Resolved:** 2026-11-13 (PR #3649)

**Done when:** A hook's `limited` ended by the next prompt leaves no `limited_hook_at` on the record, and a permission prompt during it keeps the mark. Tests hold both.
