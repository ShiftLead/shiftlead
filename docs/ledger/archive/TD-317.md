## TD-317: Build the person-only gate, and move `agent_wake.py`'s passengers

**Priority:** Low
**Added:** 2026-10-04 (the designer, TD-108's build)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/mail.py` (`PERSON_ONLY`, beside `is_person` and `ACTING_RPCS`), `src/sessionorc/agent_common.py` (the check), `agent_identity.py`, `agent_inbox.py` (`_person_entry`), `agent_mail.py`, `agent_promote.py` (`_promote_person`), `agent_link.py`, `agent_wake.py`, `agent.py`, `tests/`; design §4.8 *A person's own act is a named RPC, and the gate is one list*. Held path (`src/sessionorc/**`): the techlead reads each slice.

**Resolved:** 2026-10-05 (PRs #1049, #1057) — slice 1 (#1049): `mail.PERSON_ONLY`, `agent_common.person_only` first in each of the 25, `tests/test_person_only.py`. Slice 2 (#1057): `agent_wake.py` holds waking and the doorbell alone; the hook's entry in `agent_hook.py` (`HookMixin`), settings and usage in `agent_settings.py` (`SettingsMixin`), the Inbox rows' Dismiss in `agent_inbox.py`, the Restart and the small reads in `agent.py`. Moved as written, the suite's count unchanged.

**Why:** TD-108's design. The 25 person-only RPCs are gated by 18 `is_person` checks written into the RPCs and two helpers (`_person_entry` for five, `_promote_person` for two), and nothing says which RPCs they are: a new one that forgets its check is found by a review or not at all. And `agent_wake.py` (999 lines) still holds what followed the wake section in the old file — the hook's entry, the settings RPCs, the small reads — so its name says a quarter of what is in it.

**Fix, in two slices, a PR each; no behaviour changes beyond the refusals' wording:**
1. **The gate.** `mail.PERSON_ONLY`, a frozenset of the 25 names in design §4.8's paragraph (`inbox_delete`, `inbox_snooze`, `inbox_hand`, `inbox_dismiss`, `attention_snooze`, `inbox_pause`, `inbox_resume`, `inbox_go_with_it`, `thread`, `identity_ack`, `identity_log`, `suspend`, `board_edit`, `board_reply`, `board_reply_hand`, `entry_add`, `settings`, `set_settings`, `commit_defs`, `promote`, `clear_promote`, `clear_work`, `clear_mark`, `restart`, `forget_host`). One check (a function of `caller`, what the act is, and the design section) that raises `RpcError` in one shape — who called, what it may not do, the section — called as the first statement of each of the 25, directly or through `_person_entry` and `_promote_person`, which call it and keep what else each does (`_person_entry` its entry lookup, `_promote_person` its *runs at the home*). The check stays in the RPC and is not moved to `_dispatch_inner`: the link's `act` calls `rpc_<name>` directly (`agent_link.py`). Tests that assert a refusal's old words move with it. **The test** (`tests/test_person_only.py`): every name in `PERSON_ONLY` is an `rpc_` method of `HostAgent` and refuses a session caller before it reads its other parameters; and, read from the source, every `rpc_` method whose first statement is the check (or one of the two helpers) is in `PERSON_ONLY`. `host_files`, the acting RPCs and the RPCs with one person's branch (`inbox`, `msg`, `decide`, `wait`) are not in the list and keep their own checks. `identity_ack` and `suspend` are in it while they refuse every session, as they do today; the techlead's exception (design §4.8a *Who answers first*, not built) takes them out when it lands.
2. **The passengers** (a move as TD-108's step 1 was: methods as written, the suite's count unchanged, constants read as `agent_common.X`). `agent_wake.py` keeps waking and the doorbell (`rpc_wait` to `_refill`). The hook's entry and the permission it waits on (`rpc_hook`, `_await_permission`, `rpc_decide`, `_answered_by`) → `agent_hook.py`, `HookMixin`. Settings and usage (`rpc_usage_report`, `_usage_merge_report`, `rpc_usage`, `rpc_gate`, `rpc_settings`, `rpc_set_settings`, `rpc_commit_defs`, `_commit_defs`, `_reserves_change`, `_amounts_change`, `_amount_rows`, `_lay_gate`, `_person_change`) → `agent_settings.py`, `SettingsMixin`. The person's Dismiss on two Inbox rows (`rpc_clear_work`, `rpc_clear_mark`) → `agent_inbox.py`. `rpc_restart` and `_restart_check`, and the small reads (`rpc_recent_dirs`, `rpc_repos`, `rpc_doing_log`, `rpc_adapters`, `rpc_ping`, `rpc_host`) → `agent.py`, beside `rpc_close` and `rpc_list`. Each module's docstring says what it holds now. A method the builder finds tied to a neighbour by a private helper moves with that neighbour, and the PR says so.

**Done when** `PERSON_ONLY` exists and the test above passes in both directions; no `rpc_` method in `src/sessionorc/agent*.py` carries a hand-written `if not mail.is_person(caller): raise` as its whole gate; `agent_wake.py` holds only waking and the doorbell; `pdm run test` passes with the same count plus the new test's; TD-108 archives with this entry.
