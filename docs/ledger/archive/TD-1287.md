## TD-1287: The Queue's filter row mixes four kinds of pick that behave differently, and nothing says which is which: narrow it to the teams and the find box

**Type:** debt

**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Designed 2026-10-30 (the designer) — §4.5 screen 12 *Filters*, §4.5a *Queue page: filters, find*: the row is the team chips (with a chip per repo no team names), the **Needs you (n)** switch drawn apart with its check mark, and the find box; Steering, the repo pick and the kind pick are dropped, and their old links ignored. **Location:** `docs/design/4.5a-controls.md` (row *Queue page: filters, find*); `docs/design/4.5-ui.md` (screen 12 *Filters*); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_filters.html`; `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`FILTERS`, the picks applied around `by_repo_find`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the in-browser pick, TD-816/TD-829); `docs/mockups/Queue.dc.html`

**Why:** Paul (2026-10-30): "On the Queue pages, the top filters do multiple things and its not clear what/why they behave differently. I believe we could simplify this to just have the team names for filtering, but I am open to one other category if you think it would be really useful and we can display it in a way for it to be clear to the user what it is doing. The text box should stay available." The one row above the groups holds **All**, **Needs you (n)** and **Steering** (one exclusive status pick, `filter=`), a chip per team (OR'd, `team=`), a repo pick where the home reads more than one (`repo=`, which a team pick narrows), and a **kind** pick, **Decide** · **Do** · *held (person)* (`kind=`), and the find box. The four groups combine differently: a status replaces the last status, teams add up among themselves and repos among themselves while a repo pick is ANDed with a team pick, kinds add up, and a status or kind pick hides the Recently completed and *other work* lines while a team or repo pick narrows them. They look the same, as plain chips in one row, so the person cannot tell what a press will do or why two presses behave unlike each other. Since a team is its one repo (§4.9), the repo pick says again what the team chips say, and **Needs you**, **Steering** and the **kind** pick all ask *what needs me* from three angles.

**Fix:** design first: rewrite §4.5a's *Queue page: filters, find* row and screen 12's *Filters* so the row holds **All**, a chip per team and the **find** box, unchanged in its matching and its archive fall-through. The round decides on at most one other pick, and only if it earns its place. My lean (the drafting techlead's): keep **Needs you (n)** alone, drawn apart from the team chips and labelled as what it is, a status, so its press reads differently from a team's; drop **Steering**, the repo pick and the **kind** pick. Then the build: the template, `queue.py`'s picks, `app.js`'s in-browser pick, the persisted picks (an old URL or a remembered `kind=`/`repo=`/`filter=steering` pick is ignored, never an error), the tests that hold the row, and the mockup (`Queue.dc.html` still draws the row before TD-911, with its old kind set, so the round draws from the live page, not from it). Done when the Queue's filter row is the teams, at most that one other pick drawn so its meaning is plain, and the find box, and the design says so.
