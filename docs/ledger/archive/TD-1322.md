## TD-1322: The push child can outrun its bound on a full burst, and an unparseable resolved address ends it

**Priority:** low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (the reader of #2789, for Paul)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-03: the push child keeps inside its bound, and an unreadable address ends only its own post
**Part of:** TD-1302
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/notify.py` (`_global`, `_post`, `push_main`); `tests/test_push_send.py`
**Resolved:** 2026-11-03 (PR #2822) — `push_main` posts every pair at once (up to `PUSH_THREADS`) under a deadline, `PUSH_SECONDS` (`CHILD_SECONDS + REQUEST_SECONDS` less 10 s for doppler's start); a post still running then is printed *no answer* and the child ends by `_abandon` without joining it. `_global` reads an unparseable address as that device's status. Design §4.10 says so; tests in `tests/test_push_send.py`.

**Why:** two gaps found in reading #2789 (its PR comment, *Reader's read*). Both degrade safely today: the posts that never answered read *no answer*, and under `fallback` Telegram carries their rows. Under `both` Telegram sends them anyway. With Telegram off, those rows go untold on any channel.
1. **The bound.** `push_main` posts every device × payload pair on at most 8 threads (`max_workers=min(8, …)`), each given `REQUEST_SECONDS` (20 s). The home kills the child at `CHILD_SECONDS + REQUEST_SECONDS` (50 s, `agent_notify.py` `_push_run`; the watch the same). A full burst is 7 payloads (`BURST` 6 plus *and more*). With 3 devices that is 21 posts, 3 waves of 8: past 50 s when the push services hang. `_global`'s `getaddrinfo` has no bound of its own either.
2. **The crash.** In `_post`, `_global` is called outside the `try`. Its `ipaddress.ip_address(...)` raises `ValueError` on an address it cannot parse, which `f.result()` re-raises in `push_main`, ending the child partway: every post not yet printed takes the exit's reason.

**Fix:**
1. Bound the run as a whole, so the child finishes inside `CHILD_SECONDS + REQUEST_SECONDS` however the services answer. For example, run every pair in one wave (the pairs are few: at most 7 × the devices listed), or give the child a deadline after which it starts no post and prints the rest as unanswered. Say in `notify.py` which bound holds.
2. Catch `ValueError` inside `_global` and treat it as not global (*the push service's name resolves to an address that does not read*).

**Done when** a test with a fake `_post` that sleeps shows a full burst to 3 devices finishing inside the bound with every status printed; a test where `getaddrinfo` returns an unparseable address prints that device's status and the others'; and `pdm run test` passes.
