**Why:** TD-1863 (#4047) changed `stuck_onto_holders` (src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py) so a session's stuck lines go under its pending row first, then its restart row, then any other, by `_holder_rank`; before, a non-pending row was first-wins (`holders.setdefault`). `test_a_stuck_line_goes_under_the_pending_row_first_then_the_restart_row` (tests/test_queue_restart_row.py) calls `_holder_rank` alone on four dicts and never reaches `stuck_onto_holders`. Reverted on origin/main e79c47d55, replacing `if sid not in holders or _holder_rank(r) < _holder_rank(holders[sid]):` with `if sid not in holders:`: `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pdm run pytest -q tests/test_queue_restart_row.py tests/test_notify.py tests/test_cli.py` gives 152 passed, and tests/test_stuck*.py, tests/test_queue*.py and tests/test_inbox*.py give 612 passed. A session with a stalled row listed before its restart row would keep its stuck lines on the stalled row, and no test fails; the ranking the PR added is held by no test of its use.

**Done when:** Done when a test passes a session's stalled row and restart row, in that order, through `stuck_onto_holders` and fails with the rank test in the loop removed.
