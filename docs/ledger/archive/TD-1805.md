**Why:** Found by the test-audit of #3761 (TD-1637 slice 7). `GrokAdapter.launch` (src/shiftlead/adapters/grok/__init__.py) builds its unattended argv from `prof.unattended_args or ALWAYS_APPROVE` and appends `prof.extra_args`, and design §4.3 (the unattended arguments, `permission_prompts: skip`) says a profile's own `unattended_args` pass through. tests/test_grok_adapter.py has no profile that sets either. Probe: in the worktree, `args = prof.unattended_args or ALWAYS_APPROVE` changed to `args = ALWAYS_APPROVE`, and separately `argv += prof.extra_args` changed to `pass`, each left `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pdm run pytest -q tests/test_grok_adapter.py` at 5 passed. The adapter is unregistered, so the conformance suite does not cover it either. Dropping a profile's extra or unattended arguments on a Grok launch would go unseen.
