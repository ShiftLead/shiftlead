## TD-1386: The home's witness log: `witnessed/<month>.jsonl`, its kinds, and the three writers (precheck-fail, press, msg)

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1295's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1295
**Location:** new `src/sessionorc/witness.py` (`KINDS`, the press table, `append`, `prune`, `WITNESS_KEEP_MONTHS`); `src/sessionorc/paths.py` (the `witnessed/` dir); `src/sessionorc/promote.py` (`precheck_on_main`'s red conclusion) and `src/sessionorc/agent_sync.py` (`_sync_precheck`'s); the PR reading that reads `cadence/precheck` (`promote.read_checks`' callers); `src/sessionorc/agent_serve.py` (`_dispatch`, caller `None`); `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` (`_msg`, where an entry lands); the tick (`agent_tick.py`, the monthly prune); the nightly backup's list (§4.4a); design §4.4 *The home keeps a log of what it witnesses*

**Why:** TD-1295's design: a wait on an event the home sees is answered by a count of what the home witnessed, and the home keeps no such record today — mail is pruned 12 h after it is read, a press leaves only a `log.info` line, and a precheck's failure only a forge status.

**Fix:** as design §4.4 *The home keeps a log of what it witnesses* says: the log (one line a record, `{at, kind, repo, text}`, one append-only file per UTC month, written by the agent process alone; an unreadable line skipped), `KINDS` with each kind's text shape, and the three writers — `precheck-fail` (a home-run precheck exiting non-zero, with the `FAILED <id>` pytest node ids its log names; a PR head's `cadence/precheck` first read `failure`, its description in their place; once per repo, PR or `main`, sha), `press` (the press table's RPCs, caller the person, after success only), `msg` (every landed entry's header, never its body). The prune on the first tick of each UTC month, `WITNESS_KEEP_MONTHS` 13; `witnessed/` in the nightly backup. A writer's failure is a log line and never fails the act it records.

**Done when:** tests that each writer appends its record with the text shape the design gives (a `FAILED` id carried, a body never carried, a refused press writing nothing, one `precheck-fail` per sha however often the reading sees it), that a record of a kind not in `KINDS` is refused, that the prune keeps thirteen months, and that a writer's failure leaves its act standing.

**Related:** TD-1295 (the design); TD-1387 (the query); TD-1388 (the check).
