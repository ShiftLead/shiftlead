**Why:** Part of TD-2129 (design §4.6 *Where a session's working files go*). Earlier sessions left files in `~/.cache`, `/var/tmp` and `~/ao-shots`. ShiftLead did not make those paths its own, so nothing removes them by rule: the person sees one list and answers.

**Location:** docs/leftovers, design §4.6

**Fix:** On kmaster, read-only, list the direct children of `~/.cache`, `/var/tmp` and `~/ao-shots` owned by this uid. For each: its size; its newest write, the newest modification time under it by a walk bounded at 60 seconds an entry (a cut walk says so); whether it is a symlink (listed with its target, never followed); and, for a directory that is a git worktree or clone, its repo, its branch, and its counts of uncommitted and unpushed commits. Sort each into one group:
- **Kept, by name:** a tool's own cache in `~/.cache`: `agent-cadence`, `claude`, `claude-cli-nodejs`, `copilot`, `dconf`, `evolution`, `event-sound-cache*`, `fontconfig`, `google-chrome-for-testing-headless`, `gstreamer-1.0`, `helm`, `huggingface`, `ibus`, `ibus-table`, `mesa_shader_cache`, `mesa_shader_cache_db`, `Microsoft`, `motd.legal-displayed`, `mozilla`, `ms-playwright`, `node-compile-cache`, `node-gyp`, `pdm`, `pip`, `pnpm`, `pyright-python`, `qdtools`, `samscrape-precheck`, `tracker3`, `ubuntu-report`, `update-manager-core`, `virtualenv`; `~/ao-shots/pwlib`; `/var/tmp/pc.*`.
- **Kept, by state:** anything written in the last 3 days, held by a process (`/proc/<pid>/` cwd, root, exe, maps or fds), named by a current brief or memory, a git tree with uncommitted or unpushed work, or a symlink whose target lies in a live record's scratch.
- **Proposed for removal:** every other entry of a session's shape: in `~/.cache`, a loose file or a directory whose name starts `ao`, `aw`, `rv`, `rev`, `td`, `tl`, `inb`, `sent`, `tmp.`, `pct`, `dpr`, `pr`, `log`, or is a letter group and digits (`g1`, `r10s`); in `/var/tmp`, anything not kept; in `~/ao-shots`, anything not kept, `scripts/` included, since the scripts there are one-off.
- **Unsure:** a directory in `~/.cache` in none of the above, answered one by one.

The repository is public, so commit `docs/leftovers/TD-2146.md` with the proposed and unsure groups by name (each a session's own name, with its size, newest write, git state and link target where it has one) and the two kept groups as counts alone; the full list stays in this run's scratch.

In the same PR, gate TD-2147 on the person: `ledger.py set TD-2147 --blocked-by "decision (paul)" --ask <file> --answers '…' --lifts '…'`, which replaces its `TD-2146` block, the Ask file one plain sentence of at most 25 words ending in `?`. Add its `decide` item on the board with Context `TD-2147 decision (paul)`, a `--detail` carrying `context`, `question` and `recommended` and naming the list's file, its counts and total size, and the answers *Remove the proposed — a session removes the proposed group and keeps the unsure*, *Remove with changes: <note> — a session removes the proposed group, less what your note keeps and plus the unsure your note names*, *Keep all — a session removes nothing and closes the entry*, all three in `--lift`, since each ends the wait.

**Done when:** `docs/leftovers/TD-2146.md` names every proposed and unsure entry and counts the two kept groups; the four worktrees under `~/.cache` show their repo, branch and git state; TD-2147 carries `decision (paul)` with its Ask and answers, and the board holds its `decide` item.
