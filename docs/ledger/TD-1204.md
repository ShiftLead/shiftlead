**Why:** TD-1203's design, agreed with Paul on 2026-10-28 from the mockups (shape A5): the rollup becomes the page's table of contents — each team, and each repo with no team, one line saying its debt, who is working, its last work and what waits on the person — and a press goes there.

**Fix:** as §4.5 screen 1 *The rollup* and §4.5a *Sessions: rollup* and *rollup: press and menus* say.
1. **The data.** `build()` makes `org_layout` once and passes its items to the groups and to `rollup`. `rollup_counted` skips the layout's `heading` and `repo` items (it reads `g["members"]` today). `rollup` walks them in order and returns the rows and the totals. A team row reads its group: the repo (plain when the layout has no reading for it), the kind segments from the summary's `repo` (the Repo facet's own reading, not recomputed), the open count, the counted member cards' states in urgency order (`rollup_counted`'s set, the one set every count uses), a stopped team's (the group's `stopped`: nothing live or concluded) stubs and its word (`concluded_age`, `wound_down_age`, else *stopped*) — the group carries only the definition's member *count* (`teamrun`'s `members`), so the seats are named from the definition: its roles' names (`defs[team]["roles"]`, as *who for what* reads them), `def_manager`, `def_techlead` and its `seats` — the union of those names, once each, never `person` (a person-held manager) — each matched to a counted card by the card's `name` and stubbed when none is — `summary["doing"][0]`'s age, doer and words, its counted *answer needed* (`answer_blocks` over the counted cards) and its *asked you*. **`asked_line` gains `by`**: one item per member with an open ask, `{member, name, n, id}` — `id` that member's oldest ask's Inbox row, from `ask_blocks`, which has it — so the menu's member lines need no second read. A repo with no team gives its kind segments and count from the repo item's own `facet` (`org_layout`), *could not look* when its ledger reading failed (`ledger.error`); a strip its counted cards and `answer_blocks` over them (no *asked you*); *No repo* the same. The flat page (no layout) gives no rows; the totals keep today's keys (`answer_needed` among them) over `vs`.
2. **The template**: the grid of cells, blanks never dashes; the blocks' heights and colours from the state tokens; the needs-you button and its menu; the ⋯ button and its menu, each act the card's own control and confirm (*Add entry…* with the repo, only where the repo has a ledger reading, *New session in <team>* → `/new?team=`, *New session here* → `/new?dir=<root>`, the root from the host's repo registry, Start, Wind down, Stop now); one legend under the debt column; the totals line (Agents' pills as today, PRs with `COMPLETED_WORD`'s word for the page's window, *n from teams*, *q on the Queue*, *i in the Inbox*, *m overdue*).
3. **The client**: the table's head line in `th` cells; each row's first cell the link (named *<repo> · <team>* and so on), the needs-you and ⋯ buttons in cells of their own, the rows outside `RINGS.org`; a row press (`Enter` on the link, or a click on the row outside the buttons) clears the filter when it hides the target, opens a folded team and stores it (`AO.rollupGo`), scrolls the heading into view, marks it `landed` for 1.2 s (not under reduced motion) and writes `#team=`, `#repo=`, `#strip=` or `#norepo` with `replaceState`; a load with the hash lands there once the groups are drawn, and a hash naming nothing drawn is dropped. The two menus are disclosure buttons: `Enter` / `Space` / press opens, focus on the first item, `↑` / `↓`, `Esc` back to the button, outside press or `Tab` out closes, one open at a time, the press never reaching the row. **While a menu is open, a delta's `#rollup` swap is held** and applied on close, focus then going back to the same row's button by the row's key, or the first row's link when the row is gone. Choosing a menu item closes the menu first, then runs the act (its control's confirm) or follows the link. The `repos` and `doing` events re-render the rollup with the groups, as today.
4. **Gone**: the TDs in motion bar, the PR window picker and the TD-406 / TD-418 shapes (`quiet`, `lone`). *w waiting on review* stays, in the totals' PRs.
5. **Words**: `help.py`'s Agents ***i*** stays as it is.

**Done when** tests pin: a row per team in page order, a repo with no team's row, a strip's sub-row (drawn only with a counted card), *No repo* last; a second team on one repo with blank repo cells; a team on an unread repo with a plain repo and no bar; a stopped team's stubs, its word, and a person's live session in it drawn as a block; the blocks' order and state classes; *w of n*; the last-work cell; the needs-you sum, its solid state, the strip's *answer needed* alone, the menu's lines and URLs (`/inbox?team=<team>&sec=needs&kind=questions`, `/inbox?row=<id>` per member, from `asked_line`'s `by`); each kind of row's ⋯ menu; **the rows' needs-you numbers adding up to *n from teams*, and their blocks to Agents' total**, under TD-924's rules (the existing counting tests kept); the PRs' window word; the flat page's totals line alone; the row press, the filter cleared, the stored fold and each hash on load, a stale hash dropped; the menus' keys, choosing an item, and the held swap (a delta while a menu is open leaves it open, applied on close, focus back by the row's key, or to the first row's link when the row went); the link's accessible name and the tab order, the rows outside the `j` / `k` ring; a failed ledger reading's *could not look*; the stubs named from the definition by the card's `name`; a concluded team drawn as stopped (its word, Start in the menu, no Wind down); no Add entry… without a ledger reading; the three-line row below 720 px.
