**Fix:** as §4.5 screen 1 *The rollup* and §4.5a *Sessions: rollup* say.
1. **The rows** (`rollup`, from the groups the page draws, in `org_layout`'s order): per team, its repo, name, the repo's kind segments and open count (blank on a second team of the same repo), its member cards' states in the grid's urgency order (a stopped team: its definition's seats as stubs and the word), the newest kept doing row's age, doer and words, and its *answer needed*, *asked you*, oldest ask's age and asks by member (each member's oldest row id); per repo with no team, its kind segments and count; per repo's no-team strip holding a session, its cards' states and *answer needed*; *No repo* last.

3. **The client**: a row press scrolls to the team's card, the repo's line, the strip or *No repo*, opens a folded team and stores it (`AO.rollupGo`), flashes the header (not under reduced motion) and writes `#team=`/`#repo=` with `replaceState`; a load with the hash lands there. Rows are `role="link"` tab stops, `Enter` the press; the menus open on press and close on `Esc` and outside presses. *n from teams* and a menu's *answer needed* clear the filter first, as today's *answer needed* does.
4. **Gone**: the TDs in motion bar, the PR window picker and the TD-406 / TD-418 shapes (`quiet`, `lone`); with no row the totals line alone.
