## TD-1321: A decide on a board line or a ledger entry shows its result the moment it is pressed, not ten seconds later when it lands on origin

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (the designer, from TD-1316's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1316
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the `[data-act]` click handler ~1172: `keeps` ~1189, `AO.markPending` ~1152, the board decide ~1346 and its answer ~1552, the `ledger` presses ~1428–1458, the catch ~1585–1597, `AO.inflight`, `AO.restoreRowErrs` ~3478 and the Inbox's swap that calls it ~3556–3585, the Queue's `reread` ~4735–4756, the Repo page's ~4838–4860); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the board row's `.st.decided` ~893 and answers group ~901, `.st.boardreply` ~878, `.rowerr` ~1255 and ~1260, the comment ~1174); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html` (the `.qrow` state cell and its ⋯ `menu`, ~20–30, ~111–114); `tests/test_ui_inbox.py`, `tests/test_ui_queue.py`

**Why:** TD-1316's design: §4.5 screen 6 *A control answers the press*, rule 4 (*A press that writes origin draws its result on the press*), and §4.5a rows *Inbox row* (the **pending** mark) and *Inbox row: ledger entry*. Paul (2026-11-02): a decide on a Queue card's needs-you item takes about ten seconds to show. Measured on kmaster that evening: the board-landing PRs took 6–8 s from the commit to the merge (#2791, #2806, #2809, #2813), the host agent's round trip some 8–10 s with its fetch and check (estimated), and the Queue's re-read 0.7–1.5 s more (`/queue?part=1`, ~2 MB; its `Server-Timing` put 350 ms in the render). Today a board decide keeps its row dimmed, reading *deciding…*, through all of it, and a ledger decide's row stays pending until the re-read drops it.

**Fix:** In the one `[data-act]` click handler (`app.js`), so every page that draws these rows (Inbox, Queue, Repo) gets it — §4.5 screen 6 rule 4 is the spec; this is where it lands:
- **A ledger entry's decide leaves on the press**: `ledger` and `ledger_reply` (an answer, Go with it via `data-go`, Reply once the composer sends, Lift the hold and Done's fixed words) join rule 3's presses — out of the `keeps` list, the row taken out as the request leaves, the ring handed on, a toast that waits (*deciding — writing on the board…*) rewritten by the answer; on a refusal the row comes back on the next refresh with *not decided: <the server's words>* on its `.rowerr` (`AO.pressErrs`).
- **Done… and Back to design…** (`ledger` with `data-road`) come in two shapes, so `leaves` is computed from where the button sits, not from `data-road`: inline in a `.mailrow.ledgerrow`'s `mfoot` (the Inbox's and the Repo page's *for you* rows, `inbox_row.html` ~1236, `repo_part.html` ~25) the row leaves as the bullet above, with *not done: …* / *not sent back to design: …* on its `.rowerr` on refusal; from a `.qrow`'s ⋯ or the card header's ⋯ (`queue_row.html` `menu`, `queue_card.html` ~48 — no `.mailrow`, so `row` is null today and nothing changes for the whole round trip) the press finds its target — `.qrow[data-qid]` for a row's ⋯, `.qhead[data-qid]` for the card header's (never a bare `[data-qid]`, which `.brow` carries too), the qid being `<data-repo>#<data-ref>` — writes its `.qstate` as *done — writing on the board…* / *back to design — writing on the board…*, inserting one in `.qright` before `afters` (the macro order is extras, meter, state, afters, holder, menu) where the target drew none, the header's included where `state(t)` suppresses it; turns its ⋯ off — the `<summary>` gets `aria-disabled="true"` and `pointer-events: none`, `AO.qDots` ignores keys on it, the menu's other items with it; the answer rewrites the state *done — written on the board* / *back to design — written on the board* and turns the ⋯ back on, the re-read only confirming; the toast's waiting and refused words come from the road (`data-road`), not from `PRESS_VERBS`/`PRESS_NOT`'s `ledger` key; the card's *ledger · your decision* `.ledgerrow` for the same entry, found by `data-msg="ledger:<data-repo>:<data-ref>"`, leaves as above where drawn (a Back to design… often has none); a refusal restores the state cell and writes *not done: …* / *not sent back to design: …* on the toast and, where the card's ledger row came back, its `.rowerr`. Key it in `AO.inflight` as `qrow:<qid>` (a `.qrow` has no `data-msg`), deleted in the `finally` as `row.dataset.msg`'s is.
- **A board line's decide stays, drawn decided**: `board` with `action: decide` (an answer, Go with it — the line's default, not the steer's `gowithit`, which stays in `PRESS_LEAVES` — Works, and Not right… once its composer sends) does not mark the row pending: in the press's frame it hides the row's answers group (`.sugg`, the Queue card's `.sugg.qanswers`) and its Go with it (in `mfoot` on the Inbox, in the card's `answer_row` on a Queue card), inserts `<div class="st decided">decided: <answer> — landing on the board…</div>` where the template draws `.st.decided`, disables the row's other controls (`data-pressoff`, no `.pending`), and focuses the row; a toast that waits, *deciding — landing on the board…* (`waiting` set on this path too, not only on a leaving row's). The answer rewrites the line *decided: <answer> · <date>* (the date from the response where it carries one, else today's local date). **Not right…**: the toast settles after both the decide and `/api/entry/hand`; a failed hand-off leaves the row decided and turns the toast red (*no entry was handed on: …*); a failed decide is a refusal; the decided line is rewritten on the decide's answer, the toast after both.
- **A board Reply** (`board` with `action: reply` — the row's Reply, or a Queue card's Answer… on a board item — once the composer sends) appends `<div class="st boardreply">` with the words as text (not through `md()`) and *— landing on the board…* after the row's reply lines at once, disables the row's other controls without dimming, and waits its toast (*replying — landing on the board…*); the answer drops the suffix; the item keeps its answers.
- **Refusals**: the catch block must recognise the drawn paths — today `AO.pressErrs` is set only when `left || writes` and `writes` holds no `board` press, so a refused board decide or Reply would write no error line: set it for them (*not decided: …* — a ledger Reply too, being a decide —, *not replied: …* for a board Reply alone, not `PRESS_NOT`'s fallback *not done*), add `board` decide and reply to the `named` map (no *board failed: …*), and undo the drawn result (remove the inserted line, show the answers, re-enable the controls).
- **Re-reads keep the drawn result while the press is out**: carry each rule 4 press in `AO.inflight` as `{kind: "decided" | "reply" | "left" | "qrow", text}`; after any swap — the Inbox's (`AO.restoreRowErrs` ~3478), and the Queue's `reread` and the Repo page's, which today consult neither `AO.inflight` nor `AO.pressErrs` (so a refused ledger press on a Queue card loses its error line at the re-read) and must call `AO.restoreRowErrs` or its equivalent — re-apply it idempotently: only to a fresh row that still draws its answers (a row the re-read already draws decided from origin is left as the board says, with no second `.st.decided`; a drawn reply only where the fresh row lacks a `.st.boardreply` with the same words), keep a left row out, re-find the ring on the Queue as the Inbox does. Capture `AO.rowErrs(root)` before the Queue's and the Repo page's swaps, as the Inbox does, since `AO.restoreRowErrs(root, kept)` throws on an undefined `kept`. **After the answer** keep a drawn decided line or reply (not a left row) across re-reads until one draws it from the board, for at most 60 s, so a board reading that lags the merge does not draw the answers back. A re-read skipped while `busy` is not retried by this build (the 30 s poll catches it).
- **The Repo page gets a press re-read**: it sets `AO.refreshInboxPage = reread`, as the Queue does (app.js ~4781); today only Sessions, the Inbox and the Queue set it, so the handler's `typeof AO.refreshInboxPage` guards skip there and a refused row that left stays gone until the poll.
- A press that writes the home alone (an `ask`'s answer, a mail Reply, a steer's Go with it) and **Put on the board** (`board_add`) keep their present answer; a rank move is untouched.
- Templates: name the hooks the script needs (a class on a board row's answers group and its Go with it, if they carry none), and fix the comments that say a board decide or a ledger decide's row stays pending (`inbox_row.html` ~1174, the decide paragraph ~883–888).
- The help text of any control whose `title` says the row waits pending (§4.5a's help list, bound to `help.py`) is reworded to rule 4 here, not in the design.

**Done when** A Playwright test in a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`), with `page.route` holding each `/api/person/*` (and `/api/entry/hand`) request open, presses each kind — a board line's answer, Go with it, Works, Not right…, a board Reply and a Queue card's Answer… on a board item, a ledger entry's answer, Go with it and Lift the hold, Done… and Back to design… from a Queue card row's ⋯ (a card with a second entry) and from a card header's ⋯ (a card with its top entry only), and inline on the Inbox's and the Repo page's *for you* ledger row (a `fy_rows` fixture, not a board row) — on the Inbox, on a Queue card and on the Repo page, and asserts the drawn result (the decided line, the reply line, the row gone, the state cell) is on the page while the request is still held; then, still held, forces the page's re-read (the Queue's `reread`, the Repo's, the Inbox's swap) and asserts the drawn result survives it; then releases each request with a refusal for one board decide, one board Reply and one ledger decide and asserts the row is back with its error line, and with a success for the rest; and, after a success, a re-read whose board reading still lags (the stub reader returning the undecided line) keeps the decided line drawn. The press-to-change time, read from the test (not asserted), and screenshots of a board row drawn decided while it lands (`docs/mockups/reviews/<date>-td1321-*.png`) go in the PR body. `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass.
