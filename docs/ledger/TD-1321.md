## TD-1321: A decide on a board line or a ledger entry shows its result the moment it is pressed, not ten seconds later when it lands on origin

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (the designer, from TD-1316's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1316
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the `[data-act]` click handler ~1172: `keeps` ~1189, `AO.markPending` ~1152, the board decide ~1346 and its answer ~1552, the `ledger` presses ~1428–1458, the catch ~1585–1597, `AO.inflight`, `AO.restoreRowErrs` ~3478 and the Inbox's swap that calls it ~3556–3585, the Queue's `reread` ~4735–4756, the Repo page's ~4838–4860); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the board row's `.st.decided` ~893 and answers group ~901, `.st.boardreply` ~878, `.rowerr` ~1255 and ~1260, the comment ~1174); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html` (the `.qrow` state cell and its ⋯ `menu`, ~20–30, ~111–114); `tests/test_ui_inbox.py`, `tests/test_ui_queue.py`

**Why:** TD-1316's design: §4.5 screen 6 *A control answers the press*, rule 4 (*A press that writes origin draws its result on the press*), and §4.5a rows *Inbox row* (the **pending** mark) and *Inbox row: ledger entry*. Paul (2026-11-02): a decide on a Queue card's needs-you item takes about ten seconds to show. Measured on kmaster that evening: the board-landing PRs took 6–8 s from the commit to the merge (#2791, #2806, #2809, #2813), the host agent's round trip some 8–10 s with its fetch and check (estimated), and the Queue's re-read 0.7–1.5 s more (`/queue?part=1`, ~2 MB; its `Server-Timing` put 350 ms in the render). Today a board decide keeps its row dimmed, reading *deciding…*, through all of it, and a ledger decide's row stays pending until the re-read drops it.

**Fix:** In the one `[data-act]` click handler (`app.js`), so every page that draws these rows (Inbox, Queue, Repo) gets it — §4.5 screen 6 rule 4 is the spec; this is where it lands:
- **A ledger entry's decide leaves on the press**: `ledger` and `ledger_reply` (an answer, Go with it via `data-go` (on the Inbox's and the Repo page's rows; a Queue card draws none), Reply once the composer sends, Lift the hold and Done's fixed words) join rule 3's presses — out of the `keeps` list, the row taken out as the request leaves, the ring handed on, a toast that waits (*deciding — writing on the board…*) rewritten by the answer; on a refusal the row comes back on the next refresh with *not decided: <the server's words>* on its `.rowerr` (`AO.pressErrs`).
- **Done… and Back to design…** (`ledger` with `data-road`) come in two shapes, so `leaves` is computed from where the button sits, not from `data-road`: inline in a `.mailrow.ledgerrow`'s `mfoot` (the Inbox's and the Repo page's *for you* rows, `inbox_row.html` ~1236, `repo_part.html` ~25) the row leaves as the bullet above, with *not done: …* / *not sent back to design: …* on its `.rowerr` on refusal; from a `.qrow`'s ⋯ or the card header's ⋯ (`queue_row.html` `menu`, `queue_card.html` ~48 — no `.mailrow`, so `row` is null today and nothing changes for the whole round trip) the press finds its target — `.qrow[data-qid]` for a row's ⋯, `.qhead[data-qid]` for the card header's (never a bare `[data-qid]`, which `.brow` carries too), the qid being `<data-repo>#<data-ref>` — writes its `.qstate` as *done — writing on the board…* / *back to design — writing on the board…*, inserting one (`<span class="qstate s-pending">`, styled in `app.css` as a state word, not dimmed) in `.qright` just before its `details.qdots` (the row's and the header's `.qright` differ in order, and the header has no `afters`) where the target drew none, the header's included where `state(t)` suppresses it; turns its ⋯ off — the `<summary>` gets `aria-disabled="true"` and `pointer-events: none`, `tabindex="-1"` while off, and the `toggle` listener (app.js ~871) closes a `details.qdots` whose summary is `aria-disabled`, since Enter and Space toggle a `<summary>` natively, and `AO.qDots` returns at once on an `aria-disabled` summary, since its ArrowDown/ArrowUp open the menu and focus into it — the menu's other items off with it; the answer rewrites the state *done — written on the board* / *back to design — written on the board* and turns the ⋯ back on, the re-read only confirming; the toast's waiting and refused words come from the road (`data-road`), not from `PRESS_VERBS`/`PRESS_NOT`'s `ledger` key; the card's *ledger · your decision* `.ledgerrow` for the same entry, found by `data-msg="ledger:<repo name>:<data-ref>"` (a ⋯'s `data-repo` is the row's repo name; the inline controls' is the checkout path, `e.root`, so this lookup is the ⋯ shape's alone), leaves as above where drawn (a Back to design… often has none), with its own `AO.inflight["ledger:<name>:<ref>"]` entry as `left`, carrying the same `until` and refusal handling as the `qrow:` one; a refusal restores the state cell and writes *not done: …* / *not sent back to design: …* on the toast and, where the card's ledger row came back, its `.rowerr`. Key it in `AO.inflight` as `qrow:<qid>` for a row's ⋯ and `qhead:<qid>` for the header's, so a state kept for one is never drawn on the other, (a `.qrow` has no `data-msg`), deleted in the `finally` as `row.dataset.msg`'s is.
- **A board line's decide stays, drawn decided**: `board_decide` (an answer, Go with it — the line's default, not a mail row's `gowithit` (an `ask`'s or a steer's), which stays in `PRESS_LEAVES` — and Works) and `board_notright` (once its composer sends; its body's wire `action` is `decide` too), by `AO.pressKey`, stay in `keeps` and branch on their own, so `leaves` stays false, and do not mark the row pending: in the press's frame it hides the row's answers group (`.sugg`, the Queue card's `.sugg.qanswers`) and its Go with it (in `mfoot`; a Queue card's `answer_row` draws none), inserts `<div class="st decided">decided: <answer> — landing on the board…</div>` in place of the answers group, after the reply lines (the template's slot, `inbox_row.html` ~893), disables the row's other controls (`data-pressoff`, no `.pending`), and focuses the row; a toast that waits, *deciding — landing on the board…* (`waiting` set on this path too, not only on a leaving row's). The answer rewrites the line *decided: <answer> · <date>* (today, `YYYY-MM-DD` in the browser's local time; a re-read replaces it with the board's). **Not right…**: the toast settles after both the decide and `/api/entry/hand`; a failed hand-off leaves the row decided and turns the toast red (*no entry was handed on: …*); a failed decide is a refusal; the decided line is rewritten on the decide's answer, the toast after both.
- **A board Reply** (`board_reply`, its body's wire `action` `reply`, staying in `keeps` with `leaves` false — the row's Reply, or a Queue card's Answer… on a board item — once the composer sends) appends `<div class="st boardreply">` with the words as text (not through `md()`) and *— landing on the board…* after the row's reply lines at once, disables the row's other controls without dimming, and waits its toast (*replying — landing on the board…*); the answer drops the suffix; the item keeps its answers.
- **Refusals**: the catch block must recognise the drawn paths — today `AO.pressErrs` is set only when `left || writes` and `writes` holds no `board` press, so a refused board decide or Reply would write no error line: set it for them (*not decided: …* — a ledger Reply too, being a decide —, *not replied: …* for a board Reply alone, *not done: …* / *not sent back to design: …* by `data-road`, not `PRESS_NOT`'s fallback *not done*), and take `ledger`, `ledger_reply` and the board decide and reply out of `writes`' *not written: …* and `named[action]`'s *<control> failed: …*: their toast and error line carry the same rule-4 words, looked up by `key` (and `data-road`), since `named[action]` cannot tell a board decide from a board Done or Snooze (their `action` is `board` alike, told apart by `AO.pressKey`; a board Reply's and Not right…'s are their own, `board_reply` and `board_notright`); a ⋯ press, whose `row` is null, sets `AO.pressErrs["ledger:<name>:<ref>"]` itself for the card's ledger item, and undo the drawn result (remove the inserted line, show the answers, re-enable the controls).
- **Re-reads keep the drawn result while the press is out**: carry each rule 4 press in `AO.inflight` with `kind: "decided" | "reply" | "left" | "qrow"` and `text` added beside today's `{leaves, verb}` (app.js ~1203, ~3481), which every other press keeps as it is — `left` being `leaves: true`; after any swap — the Inbox's (`AO.restoreRowErrs` ~3478), and the Queue's `reread` and the Repo page's, which today consult neither `AO.inflight` nor `AO.pressErrs` (so a refused ledger press on a Queue card loses its error line at the re-read) and must call `AO.restoreRowErrs` or its equivalent — re-apply it idempotently: only to a fresh row that still draws its answers (a row the re-read already draws decided from origin is left as the board says, with no second `.st.decided`; a drawn reply only where the fresh row draws no more `.st.boardreply` lines than the row drew before the press appended its own (recorded on the press, before the append), since the server draws `by, date:` and renders the words through `md()`, so no text match is safe; a new board line is a new `data-msg` the keep never matches, and a ledger entry asked again within the minute keeps its `row_id` and so waits out the keep — accepted, the bound being 60 s; `data-at` is an entry's `Added:` or a line's `due`, never freshness, so nothing reads it here), keep a left row out, re-find the ring on the Queue as the Inbox does. The `qrow` kind needs a pass of its own, new: `AO.restoreRowErrs` walks `.mailrow[data-msg]` alone, so the swap also walks `.qrow[data-qid]` and `.qhead[data-qid]` to re-apply a kept state word, the ⋯ off and its text. **The answer never trusts the captured node**: a re-read while the request was out has replaced it, so on the answer the handler sets the `AO.inflight` entry's `text` to the answered words (*decided: <answer> · <date>*, the reply without its suffix, *done — written on the board*) and looks the row up again (`[data-msg="…"]`, `.qrow[data-qid]`, `.qhead[data-qid]`) to rewrite it, so the next re-apply draws the answered words too — for the `decided`, `reply` and `left` kinds; a `qrow:`/`qhead:` entry is deleted in the `finally` as today's are, and the answer's lookup alone writes *done — written on the board*. Capture `AO.rowErrs(root)` before the Queue's and the Repo page's swaps, as the Inbox does, since `AO.restoreRowErrs(root, kept)` throws on an undefined `kept`. **After the answer** keep a drawn decided line or reply, and a left row out, across re-reads until one draws it from the board (or no longer draws the row), for at most 60 s — the `AO.inflight` entry of a successful press gains `until: Date.now() + 60000` and is not deleted in the `finally` (a refusal's still is), and a re-read drops an expired one — but not a `.qrow`'s state cell, which the re-read replaces, so a board reading that lags the merge does not draw the answers back. A re-read skipped while `busy` is not retried by this build (the page's poll catches it).
- **The Repo page gets a press re-read**: it sets `AO.refreshInboxPage = () => { reread(); AO.refreshInboxCount(); }` — wrapped, since the 20 s tick (app.js ~1864) calls the hook in place of `AO.refreshInboxCount()` and the top bar's count would otherwise stop refreshing on the Repo page while the stream is down (`streamsUp` is the tick's own, so the wrapper refreshes the count every 20 s whatever the stream's state — one small GET, accepted); the extra 20 s re-read of the lists is accepted, as the Queue's is; today only the Inbox (~3143), the message page (~3906) and the Queue set it, so the handler's `typeof AO.refreshInboxPage` guards skip there and a refused row that left stays gone until the poll.
- A press that writes the home alone (an `ask`'s answer, a mail Reply, a mail row's Go with it) and **Put on the board** (`board_add`) keep their present answer; a rank move is untouched.
- Templates: name the hooks the script needs (a class on a board row's answers group and its Go with it, if they carry none), and fix the comments that say a board decide or a ledger decide's row stays pending (`inbox_row.html` ~1174, the decide paragraph ~883–888).
- The help text of any control whose `title` says the row waits pending (§4.5a's help list, bound to `help.py`) is reworded to rule 4 here, not in the design.

**Done when** A Playwright test in a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`), with `page.route` holding each `/api/person/*` (and `/api/entry/hand`) request open, presses each kind — a board line's answer, Go with it (Inbox and Repo page only), Works, Not right…, a board Reply and a Queue card's Answer… on a board item, a ledger entry's answer, Go with it (Inbox and Repo page only) and Lift the hold, Done… and Back to design… from a Queue card row's ⋯ (a card with a second entry) and from a card header's ⋯ (a card with its top entry only), and inline on the Inbox's and the Repo page's *for you* ledger row (an Inbox *Later* ledger row; on the Repo page a `fy_rows` fixture, not a board row) — on the Inbox, on a Queue card and on the Repo page, and asserts the drawn result (the decided line, the reply line, the row gone, the state cell) is on the page while the request is still held; then, still held, forces the page's re-read (the Queue's `reread`, the Repo's, the Inbox's swap) and asserts the drawn result survives it; then releases each request with a refusal for one board decide, one board Reply and one ledger decide and asserts the row is back with its error line, and with a success for the rest; and, after a success, a re-read whose board reading still lags (the stub reader returning the undecided line) keeps the decided line drawn; a re-read while the request is held, then a success, leaves the line reading *decided: <answer> · <date>* (the test asserts the `· YYYY-MM-DD` shape, not a date) (and a card row's state *— written on the board*), not *landing…*; a re-read whose fresh row already draws the reply shows it once, not twice; a Not right… whose decide succeeds and whose `/api/entry/hand` is refused leaves the row decided with the toast red; and a refused ⋯ press restores the card row's state cell and turns its ⋯ back on, the card's ledger item back with its error line.
