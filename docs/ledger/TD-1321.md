## TD-1321: A decide on a board line or a ledger entry shows its result the moment it is pressed, not ten seconds later when it lands on origin

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (the designer, from TD-1316's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1316
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the `[data-act]` click handler ~1172: `keeps` ~1189, `AO.markPending` ~1152, the board decide ~1346 and its answer ~1552, the `ledger` presses ~1428–1458, the catch ~1585–1597, `AO.inflight`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the board row's `.st.decided` ~893 and answers group ~901, `.st.boardreply` ~878, `.rowerr` ~1255 and ~1260, the comment ~1174); `tests/test_ui_inbox.py`

**Why:** TD-1316's design: §4.5 screen 6 *A control answers the press*, rule 4 (*A press that writes origin draws its result on the press*), and §4.5a rows *Inbox row* (the **pending** mark) and *Inbox row: ledger entry*. Paul (2026-11-02): a decide on a Queue card's needs-you item takes about ten seconds to show. Measured on kmaster that evening: the board-landing PRs took 5–7 s from the commit to the merge (#2791, #2806, #2809, #2813), the host agent's round trip 7–9 s with its fetch and check, and the Queue's re-read 0.7–1.5 s more (`/queue?part=1`, ~2 MB; its `Server-Timing` put 350 ms in the render). Today a board decide keeps its row dimmed, reading *deciding…*, through all of it, and a ledger decide's row stays pending until the re-read drops it.

**Fix:** In the one `[data-act]` click handler (`app.js`), so every page that draws these rows (Inbox, Queue, Repo, a message's page) gets it:
- **A ledger entry's decide leaves on the press**: `ledger` and `ledger_reply` (an answer, Go with it, Reply once the composer sends, Lift the hold and Done's fixed words, Done… and Back to design… once their box sends) join rule 3's presses — out of the `keeps` list, the row taken out as the request leaves, the ring handed on, a toast that waits (*deciding — writing on the board…*) rewritten by the answer; on a refusal the row comes back on the next refresh with *not decided: <the server's words>* on its `.rowerr` (`AO.pressErrs`, as `writes` does today).
- **A board line's decide stays, drawn decided**: `board` with `action: decide` (an answer, Go with it's answer, Works, and Not right… once its composer sends) does not mark the row pending: in the press's frame it hides the row's answers group (`.sugg`, or the Queue card's answer row) and its Go with it, inserts `<div class="st decided">decided: <answer> — landing on the board…</div>` where the template draws `.st.decided`, and disables the row's other controls; the answer rewrites the line *decided: <answer> · <date>* (today's local date, the form the template draws) and re-enables nothing that a decided row does not draw; a refusal removes the line, shows the answers again, re-enables the controls, writes *not decided: <the server's words>* on the row's `.rowerr` and turns the toast red.
- **A board Reply** (`board` with `action: reply`, once the composer sends) appends `<div class="st boardreply"><words> — landing on the board…</div>` after the row's reply lines at once; the answer drops *— landing on the board…*; a refusal removes it and writes *not replied: <the server's words>* on `.rowerr`.
- A press that writes the home alone (an `ask`'s answer, a mail Reply) keeps rule 2's pending mark, unchanged.
- The re-read after the answer is unchanged (TD-1315's fold keeping applies to it); a poll that lands while the press is out must not redraw the row over its drawn result — extend `AO.inflight` to the rows rule 4 draws.
- Templates: name the hooks the script needs (a class on the answers group of a board row and of a Queue card's answer row, if they do not already carry one) and fix the comments that say a board decide stays pending (`inbox_row.html` ~1174 and the decide paragraph).
- The help text of any control whose `title` says the row waits pending (§4.5a's help list, bound to `help.py`) is reworded to rule 4 here, not in the design.

**Done when** In a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`) with a board landing slowed to several seconds (a stub `gh`, or a fake remote that delays the push), a Playwright test presses each kind — a board line's answer, Works, Not right…, a board Reply, a ledger entry's answer, Go with it and Done… — on the Inbox and on a Queue card, and finds the drawn result (the decided line, the reply line, the row gone) within 100 ms of the click, before the request answers; a refused press (a line moved on origin) puts the row back with its error line. The figures go in the PR body, with screenshots of a board row drawn decided while it lands, at `docs/mockups/reviews/<date>-td1321-*.png`. `pdm run test` and `pdm run lint` pass.
