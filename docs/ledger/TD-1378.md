## TD-1378: One board decide item that holds a parent's parts is drawn once per held part, so the person sees the same question several times

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (ao-led-tds-ledger, a person-session in ledgerboard, through `ao td add --repo shiftlead`; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Location:** design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs* (*A board line that names the entry is the question itself and is drawn instead of the entry's own block*), §4.5 Repo page *For you* (*A board line whose `Context:` names the entry is drawn in the row's place*), §4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*, *Inbox row: ledger entry*; the cadence's end-of-rounds item (agent-cadence `cadence.md` §4.8 *The end-of-rounds item*, one board `decide` with `Answers: Go | One more round | Hold: <note>`), which names the plan's entry and carries no `Parts:`; the code that links a line to its entries: `item_home`, `asked_entries` and `attach` in `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (TD-1355, TD-1362), which read only a line's `Context:` refs; the vendored ledgerboard v2.3's `parse_parts()` and `covers()` (`src/sessionorc/ledgerboard/__init__.py`, SPEC *`Parts:`*), which already read a parent's `decide` with its `Parts:` as covering each listed part.

**Why:** a design's end-of-rounds question holds its builds with it (the person's layer §2.4 rule), so one board `decide` item gates several entries, and the person should answer it once. On 2026-11-04 the line *Should TD-1286's builds … go ahead as written* (`Context: TD-1286`, `Answers: Go (default) | One more round | Hold: <note>`) gated TD-1286 and its builds TD-1356, TD-1357, TD-1358, TD-1360 and TD-1363, each `Blocked by: decision (paul) — docs/user_attention.md, 2026-11-04 item`. The Queue showed six Decide items with the same question, only TD-1286's offering the line's answers (Go); the builds' blocks offered **Lift the hold** and **Answer…**, each of which would have answered a different question than the one asked (the TD-1355 harm, five times over). The cause, read from the code: TD-1355 draws a line in the place of the entry its `Context:` names, and this line named only the parent; the builds point at the item by its date alone, which no reader resolves, and the line carried no `Parts:` (ledgerboard SPEC, v2.3), the field that lists the parts a parent's `decide` asks about.

**Fix:** design, then build: a `decide` line drawn in the place of every entry it covers — its `Context:` entry and each `Parts:` id, as `covers()` reads it — once, on the card that holds the parent (the parts ride its card, ADR 2026-10-27), listing the parts it holds, with the line's answers, its default and **Answer…**; and none of the covered parts drawing its own **Lift the hold** / **Answer…** block. The round settles: whether the end-of-rounds item of a design with builds should carry `Parts:` (an agent-cadence change to its §4.8, asked for with `ao td add --repo agent-cadence`), or the reading also links a `decision (paul)` part whose parent's open `decide` line names the parent with no `Parts:` (today's lines); how the card lists the held parts (one line under the item, *holds TD-1356, TD-1357, …*); and where a covered part sits on another card or in *Later*. Answering once lifts every listed part through ledgerboard v2.4's parent go (target design §7.4 *Parent go*, `board.py decide`), which is TD-1375's road; until it lands, the decide road stands as it is and the session acting on the answer unblocks the parts, as #2903 did. Done: the design says it; a test that a parent's open `decide` with `Parts:` draws one Decide item listing the parts and no part's own block; and a live read on the next design whose builds are held with it.
