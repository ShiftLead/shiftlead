## TD-2158: Each stage a session ends records its model, tool, login, spend and the flow's version, and a lost offer leaves a row

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e4f0ddc027854ca48fea4addbc94bcbc
**Part of:** TD-2077

**Why:** Part of TD-2077 (design §4.9c *What a run records, for comparing flows*). A comparison of flows needs each stage's model, tool, version, login and spend, and a flow's version, kept on the entry's stage record; today the session record that holds them is forgotten a day after it closes, the record has no flow version, and a lost offer leaves no row.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/stage_end.py, src/shiftlead/stage_make.py, design §4.9c

**Fix:** In `src/shiftlead/stage_end.py` (an accepted `ao stage end`'s history row) and `src/shiftlead/stage_make.py` (`_offer_again`), write `run` on the row as §4.9c's first bullet lists it: `session`, `model`, `tool`, `tool_version`, `login`, `login_moves`, `started`, `ended`, `tokens` by kind, `turns`, `figure`, `asks`, `flow_hash`, copied from the holder's session record and summed from `seat_spend.json`'s rows for that record over every day (the figure as `sessionorc.seats` weighs it), `null` for a field the record does not hold. `_offer_again` writes a `lost` row (`{from, outcome: "lost", by: "home", reason: lost | silent, run, at}`) before it clears the holder, on both its cases. Count `person_asks` on a session's record as each ask or steer it sends the person lands. Compute `flow_hash` as §4.9c's bullet says (the bytes of `flows/<team>.yml`, then each stage's resolved brief in stage order, a package brief followed by its package's version), once per offer. No act reads any of it.

**Done when:** Tests show an accepted stage end and each of the offer-again act's cases writing a row with every `run` field (a missing one `null`), the figure equal to the session's `seat_spend.json` sum, a lost row before the holder clears, `person_asks` counted on a send to the person and not on any other, two flow copies differing by one brief hashing apart, and no act's choice changing with `run` present or absent.
