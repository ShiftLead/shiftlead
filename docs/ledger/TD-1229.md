## TD-1229: The forget-after-keep test failed once under the full suite and passes alone

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-29 (grinder-ao-2, seen in a local full run for #2663)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Waits:** event — the test fails again in a precheck or a full run
**Status:** Open — seen once; not reproduced. Read 2026-10-29 (grinder-ao-2): three runs alone passed in 0.5–2.5 s, and #2663's precheck, which runs the whole suite in a clean worktree, passed on the next head.
**Location:** `tests/test_agent_paths.py` (`test_closed_sessions_are_forgotten_after_keep`, the `wait_for(gone)` at :88); the tick's forget of a closed record past `agent_common.CLOSED_KEEP` (patched to 0 s)

**Why:** a full `pdm run test` on #2663's branch failed this one test with `assert await wait_for(gone)`. That PR changed only `docs/briefs/` and a ledger file. Within `wait_for`'s default 6 s, the closed record never left `list`. The keep is 0 s, so only a tick that did not reach the forget for 6 s explains it, which is the same shape as TD-1218 (a tick slowed under the full suite's load). A precheck that goes red for that reason costs a rerun of the whole suite and reads as the PR's fault.

**Fix:** on the next failure, read what the tick was doing. If the forget waits on a tick that a slow tmux call holds, give the test a longer `wait_for` timeout, or drive the forget pass directly, as `test_first_prompt.py`'s `_unmarked` now names its own wait's state. Until then, nothing to change.

**Done when** the cause is named from a failure and the test no longer waits on an unbounded tick, or a month of full runs passes without it.
