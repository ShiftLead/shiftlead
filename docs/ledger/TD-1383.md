## TD-1383: A second open question to the person from the same sender on the same matter is refused, naming the first

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1379's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1382
**Part of:** TD-1379
**Location:** design §4.10 *One open copy of a question*, *A session reaches a person through the org's person inbox*; §4.7 `ao msg`; the code: `rpc_msg` and `_check_person_depth` in `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`, the seat edge's chain walk in `message_gate` (`src/sessionorc/mail.py`), `src/shiftlead/skill.md` (`ao --skill`).

**Why:** the person inbox's depths count and never compare, so on 2026-11-04 ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead sent the person `m-0d747c0df9e3` and `m-26316e2c8d13` eight seconds apart — the same question, subject and answers — and both stood on the Queue until Paul answered both (TD-1379).

**Fix:** as §4.10 *One open copy of a question* says: in `rpc_msg`, beside `_check_person_depth` and before the entry lands, an `ask` or `steer` to the person is refused while the person inbox holds an open `ask` or `steer` whose `from_` is a record of the sender's `superseded_by` chain with the same `about` and `subject` (as stored: `about` after its default, `subject` after derivation and clipping; case and runs of white space folded) — and, where the new question's `about` is empty, an empty `about` and the same folded text; the refusal *you have m-… open to the person on <about>, "<subject>": wait for its answer, or withdraw it (ao msg --withdraw m-…) and ask anew*. Not read: a `note`, a `reply`, an `--outcome` note, a pass-up, another sender's question. Blocked by TD-1382 because the refusal names its verb.

**Done when:** tests that a same-`about`, same-`subject` second `ask` (and `steer`) from the same sender — and from its resumed successor — is refused naming the first's id; that one from another sender, one on another `about`, one sent after the first closed, and a pass-up land; that with `about` empty a same-subject different-text question lands and a same-text one is refused; `ao --skill` names the rule beside the verb; and the doc-bound tests pass.

**Related:** TD-1379 (the design), TD-1382 (withdraw), TD-995 (the seat edge's one open question per sender), TD-324 (the person inbox's depths), TD-561 (`subject`).
