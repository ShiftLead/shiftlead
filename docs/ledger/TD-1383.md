## TD-1383: A second open question to the person from the same sender on the same matter is refused, naming the first

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1379's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1382
**Part of:** TD-1379
**Location:** design §4.10 *One open copy of a question*, *A session reaches a person through the org's person inbox*; §4.7 `ao msg`; the code: the send path `_msg` (called by `rpc_msg`) and `_check_person_depth` in `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` — `about`'s default and the derived `subject` are settled there after the depth check, so the new check reads them where they are settled, the seat edge's chain walk in `message_gate` (`src/sessionorc/mail.py`), `src/shiftlead/skill.md` (`ao --skill`).

**Why:** the person inbox's depths count and never compare, so on 2026-11-04 ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead sent the person `m-0d747c0df9e3` and `m-26316e2c8d13` eight seconds apart — the same question, subject and answers — and both stood on the Queue until Paul answered both (TD-1379).

**Fix:** as §4.10 *One open copy of a question* says: in `_msg`, once `about` and `subject` are settled and before the entry lands, an `ask` or `steer` to the person is refused while the person inbox holds an open `ask` or `steer` (either kind against either) whose `from_` is a record whose `superseded_by` chain reaches the sender (`message_gate`'s `mine`, read with `mail.chain` and `_successor`) with the same `about` and `subject` (as stored: `about` after its default, `subject` after derivation and clipping, and a copy storing none — a passed-up question — by `mail.subject_of(text)` at the compare; case and runs of white space folded; the chain walk agrees with `from_ == sender` on the home, since a resume moves `from_`) — and, where the new question's `about` is empty, an empty `about` and the same folded text; the refusal *you have m-… open to the person on <about>, "<subject>": wait for its answer, or withdraw it (ao msg --withdraw m-…) and ask anew*, with *on <about>* left out when `about` is empty. Not read: a `note`, a `reply`, an `--outcome` note, a pass-up's send, another sender's question; a passed-up copy in the person inbox is its asker's open question and is matched as one. No `await` falls between the check and the landing, so two concurrent sends cannot both pass; a refused send lands nothing, counts in no tally and takes nothing up (`taken` untouched). Blocked by TD-1382 because the refusal names its verb.

**Done when:** tests that a same-`about`, same-`subject` second `ask` (and `steer`) from the same sender — and from its resumed successor — is refused naming the first's id; that one from another sender, one on another `about`, one sent after the first closed, and a pass-up land, while the asker's own later send matching a passed-up copy is refused — that copy sent to the seat with no `--subject`, so its subject is read from its text; that with `about` empty a same-subject different-text question lands and a same-text one is refused, in the no-`about` words; that an open `ask` refuses a `steer` on the same matter; that case and white-space differences fold, and a clipped subject compares as stored; that a `note`, a `reply` and an `--outcome` note on the same matter land; that a refused send leaves no outbox copy, no tally and no taken-up question; that a `--thread` send is refused only where another open question to the person on the same matter stands; `ao --skill` names the rule beside the verb; and the doc-bound tests pass.

**Related:** TD-1379 (the design), TD-1382 (withdraw), TD-995 (the seat edge's one open question per sender), TD-324 (the person inbox's depths), TD-561 (`subject`).
