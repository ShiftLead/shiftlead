## TD-1558: The Queue moves under its reader: previews on the Queue, and a card page that holds still where the actions are

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** bb936d03a9ab4b9abb878bf26c54b373
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** Paul's direction, 2026-11-07, sent through his session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-0368c6bccbc6) after TD-1555. The Queue page changes under its reader. Its re-read on the `queue` and `inbox` events (design §4.5 screen 12; the live UI's log showed it every ~10 s on 2026-11-07) re-orders cards, moves items between groups and reopens menus, so things move while Paul reads and possibly while he presses. TD-1555 was one symptom: an Answer… pressed on a board item sent its words as a reply to another card's ask. TD-1555 (#3241) binds a box's target at the press. That cures the misrouting, not the movement.

A page that moves under a reading hand costs the person the thing the Queue exists for: reading their org's work in their own order and acting on it with confidence that the press lands where they looked.

**Ask:** Will you give TD-1558's design and its three builds (TD-1761 the Queue holds still, TD-1762 the card's page, TD-1763 the previews) your go? The review rounds converged in round 9; the decide item on the board has the trend and the recommendation.

**Location:** design §4.5 screen 12 (Queue: the re-read paragraph, A card, Three verbs, Addresses), §4.5a Queue rows; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Queue's re-read, AO.reopenMenus); src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py; src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue*.html; docs/decisions/2026-10-19-queue-card.md

**Fix:** Design first (designer). Paul's direction: the Queue shows **previews** (a card's head, its status, its items one line each). Pressing a card or an item opens **that card's own page**, where answering, Lift the hold, Start session and the other actions happen. That page **holds still**: a re-read updates its values in place, and never re-orders or rebinds while a box is open.

The round settles, with Paul (agree the shape and mockups with him before model review rounds, his cadence layer §4.8):
- (a) Does the Queue keep any one-press action (Go on a default, Done), or is every action on the card page?
- (b) On the Queue, do re-reads apply at once, or wait behind an *n changes — refresh* bar while the person is on the page, with no movement until pressed?
- (c) How the card page relates to the `/queue?card=` / `/queue#TD-n` addresses (screen 12 *Addresses*) and to Focus.

The design changes §4.5 screen 12 (*A card*, *Three verbs*, *Addresses*, the re-read paragraph that TD-1315 wrote) and §4.5a's Queue rows, and files the builds as `Part of: TD-1558`.

**Done when:** The design for previews on the Queue and a card page that holds still has merged, answering (a), (b) and (c) with Paul's agreement and naming the §4.5a rows it adds or changes. Its builds are filed as `Part of: TD-1558`.
