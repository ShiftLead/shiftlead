## TD-1581: The week reserve is set per profile, so raising it on one Claude profile leaves the other profiles on the same account running to their old line

**Priority:** high
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-10 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-reserve)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 06716a1ee0cb436f8301ba5f7b779841
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Location:** ~/.shiftlead/settings.yml usage_gate:; src/sessionorc/settings.py; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_gate_reserves, the gate pass); src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py; src/shiftlead/cli.py (ao gate); design §5 usage_gate:, §6 Usage gate, §4.5a Settings page Usage, §4.7 ao gate

**Why:** A subscription's quota is one number, but its reserve is set once per profile. On kmaster, four unattended profiles share the one Claude account `claude-code:paul` and its one `week` reading: `grind`, `grind-sonnet`, `grind-fable` and `grind-ask`. Each has its own `usage_gate:` entry in `~/.shiftlead/settings.yml` and its own card on the Settings page (design §5 `usage_gate:`, §6 *Usage gate*, §4.5a Settings page **Usage**). To keep back *some of the week*, the person has to raise every profile on the account. A profile they miss keeps running down to its own line.

That is what happened at the end of the week of 2026-11-04 (read from the home's `settings.yml` commits and the host agent's journal):
- **07:58, 2026-11-07:** Paul raised `grind-fable` week 0 → 3 and `grind-ask` none → 3.
- **15:01:** `grinder-ao-2` (`grind-ask`) paused at 97% ≥ 97%, as set.
- **17:13:** `designer-ao-1` and `grinder-ao-1` (`grind`) paused at a projected 99.1% ≥ 99%. `grind` was still at week 1, and it carries the org's grinders, techlead and designer (`org.yml` roles). `test-audit-ao-1` (`grind-sonnet`, also at 1) paused with them.
- **17:23:** all three resumed, `RESUME_MIN` after the pause. The 99.1% was a projection: the usage endpoint had been `rate_limited` since 16:28 and `error` from 17:40 until 2026-11-10. Most likely a fresh reading under 99% ended the pause.
- **18:03–18:09:** Paul raised `grind-ask` 3 → 5 and `grind-sonnet` 1 → 5, and stopped `ao-grind` by hand, five presses. `grind` was never raised. Its line stayed at 99% for the rest of the week, so the gate kept back almost nothing.

The raise did take effect on every profile it touched. It missed the profile doing most of the work, and nothing on the page showed that one account's reserve was split four ways. The harm is a spent subscription week with nothing left for the person's own sessions, while it looked as though a reserve had been set. Each new profile on an account (TD-641's `grind-ask` was one) is one more field that has to be kept in step.

**Ask:** Paul's word on the shape before the design round: should the reserve be set **per account** (`claude-code:paul`, `gemini:paul-google`, …)? Each Gemini key is its own account with its own quota, so "per subscription" would be per account. And does a profile keep a reserve of its own as an override, or is the account's the only one? His answer lifts the decision and the designer takes the round.

**Fix:** Design round (designer) on a reserve set **per account**: the usage key the reading already uses, `<adapter>:<account>` (§4.2a). On a subscription it would be one flat or per-day percent per window label. On a metered account it would be one amount. Every profile on the account would read that one line. The team's reserve priority (`teams.<team>.reserve`) would stay as it is, lowering the line for that team's sessions.

The round settles what Paul's answer to the **Ask** leaves open:
- Does a profile's own reserve go away, or stay as an override?
- What are the shapes of `settings.yml` and `ao gate`?
- How does the Settings page group cards, one per account?
- How does today's per-profile file migrate? The default would be the largest reserve among an account's profiles, per window, so no line moves up.

It amends §5 `usage_gate:`, §6 *Usage gate*, §4.5a (Settings page **Usage**, the usage chip's *lowest line among those profiles*), §4.7 `ao gate`, and the code that reads them: `sessionorc/settings.py` (`lines`, the reserve read), `sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_gate_reserves`, the gate pass), `shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py` and `ao gate` in `shiftlead/cli.py`. The builds are filed as parts of this entry.

A separate finding from the same look: for about three days (2026-11-07 16:28 to 2026-11-10) the endpoint poll for `claude-code:paul` was failing, so the gate was reading old readings and projections. Earlier that week, sessions' `usage_report`s were also refused by the identity check (12–47 a day, 2026-11-05 to 07). This entry does not fix either; the round decides whether they become an entry of their own.

**Done when:** Done when one reserve per account is designed and built: one change on the Settings page or through `ao gate` moves the line for every unattended session on that account, and kmaster's four Claude profiles' reserves have migrated into it.
