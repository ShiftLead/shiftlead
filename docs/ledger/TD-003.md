## TD-003: Phone layout: Focus as one screen — the terminal, the strip, the key bar and the input bar, everything else in sheets

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-09-06
**Owner:** paul
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Designed 2026-11-01 (phone-view, with Paul; Paul picked option A of the mockup and kept questions in the terminal); in review (round 1 Sonnet NOT READY, adopted). Built by TD-1299, TD-1300 and TD-1301.
**Location:** design §4.5 *Phone layout* (and *The panel put away*), §4.5a rows *soft-key row* (the key bar), *the strip*, *the tray*, *the input bar · ⌨*, *the sheet (⋯)*, *the switcher*, *a pinch*, *» put away* and *the bar's three groups*; §4.6 *A URL is a link*; §4.4 *Attachment drop*; §10 *Phone answers for questions*; mockup `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-01-phone-focus.html`

**Ask:** Once the review rounds converge and the three parts are built, will you use Focus on your phone for a real session and say whether it is the phone view you want?

**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-01: *Our phone view of the focus terminal is very difficult to use on a small phone … lets come up with a design that makes users want to use shiftlead on their phone, instead of the current state of last resort.* Below 720 px Focus was the desktop page stacked into one column: two rows of top bar, the identity and acts lines, a terminal at 50vh, sixteen soft keys scrolling sideways, an open composer and every side-panel card, so the terminal had a third of a small phone and none once the keyboard came up.

**Fix:** designed in this entry's PR from three research passes (native terminal apps: Termius, Blink, Termux, Moshi; browser terminals: xterm.js on phones, `visualViewport`, `interactive-widget`, a web app manifest; agent apps on phones: Claude, Codex, Happy, Omnara) and the mockup's seven frames. Paul's picks: option A (the strip, the key bar and the input bar, never covering the terminal) over B (a floating dock over the terminal's last rows); a question stays in the terminal and is answered with the key bar (*reproducing questions from the terminal has proved problematic*). The build is three parts:
1. TD-1299 — the narrow Focus screen: strip, tray, input bar and composer sheet, the ⋯ sheet and its card sheets, the switcher, the grid rules, the home-screen manifest and reconnects.
2. TD-1300 — the key bar: the soft-key row reshaped.
3. TD-1301 — a pinch for the font size, and the strip's swipe between records.

**Done when** the three parts are archived and Paul has used Focus on his phone over the route for a real session.
