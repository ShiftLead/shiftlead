## TD-1353: Should a passed-up `steer` be counted in *Needs you* and drawn on the Queue, rather than run uncounted under *Steering*?

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-04
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** decision (paul) — the Ask below

**Ask:** Should a `steer` a techlead passed up to you count in the Inbox's *Needs you* number (and as a Decide item on the Queue), instead of sitting uncounted under *Steering* until its default applies?

**Why:** TD-1352: on 2026-11-03 two steers from ledgerboard's lb-grind (`m-5d77cc3dec15` about TD-020, `m-b90d95278fe0` about TD-023) were passed up to Paul by `ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead`. A read of the live page on 2026-11-04 (grinder-ao-2, `GET /api/person/inbox`) showed both in the person inbox, open, and drawn in the Inbox's *Steering* section, as §4.5a *Inbox row: passed up* says. But *Steering* is outside the top bar's number, and neither reached the Queue, because no open Queue card held the entries their `about` names. So Paul saw them only through a person-session reading the store. A passed-up steer is a question the techlead could not answer and chose to put to the person. Today it looks exactly like a steer the asker sent the person directly, which the design treats as safe to ignore ("doing nothing is a valid answer"). The passer's judgement that it needs the person is not counted anywhere.

**Location:** design §4.5a *Inbox row: passed up* and §4.9b *Passing up keeps the thread and the asker*; `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`inbox_sections`: a `steer` goes to *Needs you* only when `paused_at` is set); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`item_home`, `want_of`).

**Fix:** design first, once answered. *Yes*: §4.5a's row puts a passed-up `steer` in *Needs you* with its time left, counted (as a paused `steer` is), and a Queue card's Decide item counts it. `inbox_sections`, the rail's counts and `--counts` follow, and the help text says why the row is there. *No*: drop the entry. The *Steering* placement stands, and TD-1352's rail fix is the whole of it.
