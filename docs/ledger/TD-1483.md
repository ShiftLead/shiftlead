## TD-1483: A doc-only or ledger-only PR runs the whole suite, which flakes under load and costs minutes per rebase

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger and the techlead seat; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 555b17c25f5240e38687f6ae9aed41a6
**Location:** docs/cadence.toml (`precheck`); the precheck runner (agent-cadence `precheck.py`, which runs the repo's command); tests/

**Why:** Asked by Paul through the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-01263e0bba7e), from a trial review of three seats' transcripts (designer-ao-1, grinder-ao-2, techlead-ao-1), 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-06, deduped against the ledger; the tooling and rule parts went to agent-cadence.

A PR that changes only `docs/` or `docs/ledger/` runs the full suite (~5,000 tests, 3–4 min) at its precheck, and flakes under load: #3043 needed three runs at a load average near 21. On 2026-11-06 the techlead seat's ledger-only PRs #3011 and #3067 each failed once on an unrelated flake (TD-1442, TD-1254) and passed on the re-run.

**Fix:** Design first: a lighter precheck for a PR whose diff touches only docs. For example, the doc-bound tests (those reading `docs/design/`, `docs/ledger/` and the help text: `tests/test_design_doc*.py`, `tests/test_help*.py`, `tests/test_ledger*.py` and every file that names `4.5a`), plus `ledger.py --check` and lint. Say who decides a diff is docs-only (a path list in `docs/cadence.toml`) and what the forge's `cadence/precheck` status records, so the gate still reads one status. The mechanism may be agent-cadence's (`precheck.py` choosing the command by the diff). If so, ask it there and keep the shiftlead half (the path list and the light command) here.

**Done when:** A ledger-only PR's precheck runs the light command and records `cadence/precheck`; a PR touching `src/` still runs the full suite. Builds carry `Part of: TD-1483`.
