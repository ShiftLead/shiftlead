## TD-1250: A `--source` reply is exempt from the one-sentence warning, and no test says so

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (test-audit-ao-1, auditing the last ten merged PRs)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/cli.py` (`cmd_msg`: `question=params["kind"] in ("ask", "steer") and not args.source`); `tests/test_cli.py` (`test_msg_to_the_person_warns_on_an_unshaped_message_and_sends_it`), `tests/test_one_sentence.py`

**Why:** #2696 (TD-916) held an `ask` or `steer` to the person to one sentence and carved out a `--source` reply, whose docstring says "never a `--source` reply". Both new tests send only without `--source`. Probe from a clean worktree at `origin/main` b2d1a3724: deleting ` and not args.source` from that line and running `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pdm run pytest -q tests/test_one_sentence.py tests/test_cli.py -k "shape or person or sentence"` gives `16 passed`, the same as unmutated. So a reply answered from the record, `--kind ask --source …` with a two-sentence first paragraph, could start printing *the person reads one sentence first* and nothing would fail.

**Fix:** add a `--source` case to the CLI test: an `ask` or `steer` with `--reply-to … --source …` and a first paragraph of two sentences prints no *the person reads one sentence first* line.

**Done when:** deleting ` and not args.source` fails a test.

**Related:** TD-916, #2696
