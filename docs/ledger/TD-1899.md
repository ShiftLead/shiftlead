## TD-1899: An opened Recently completed line shows who built, reviewed and merged each entry, from its closing commit's trailers

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-15 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-compl)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 307ac49acc7043f6867676058555de54

**Why:** On 2026-11-14 Paul asked for an opened Recently completed line on the Queue to show more than the `Resolved:` line: which sessions worked on the entry, so he can see at a glance that a flow ran as it should. The first idea was to list the `ao doing` reports. That source does not hold up: a report names an entry only when its writer happens to type the id, and the doing log keeps each team's last 50 reports and each doer's newest 5 (`DoingLogStore`, design §4.8 *the doing log*), so a busy team's reports on an entry closed a few hours ago are gone. A session's `progress` is no record either: it holds the current claim alone and is overwritten by the next.

The record already exists. Each completed entry in the `queue` reading carries the `sha` of its closing squash merge, and that commit's message is written by the merge step, not by hand. #3920's (`1f8408f10`, TD-1848) ends with `Co-Authored-By: grinder-ao-2`, `Agent-Model: claude-code/claude-opus-5-5`, `Review: sonnet (SHIP)`, `Precheck: d7b3ec5…` and `Merged-by: techlead-ao-1 (…)`. Drawn on the line, those say who built the entry, who reviewed it and with what verdict, that the precheck ran, and who merged. A missing review or a merge by the builder is then visible where the person already looks. The value is a quick check of the flow, so Low.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/queue.py (the queue reading's completed entries: sha); src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (queue_view: completed lines' entries); src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_part.html (qdbody); design §4.5 screen 12 Recently completed, §4.5a Queue page, Recently completed

**Fix:** A design round first: a new display on §4.5 screen 12's Recently completed lines and on §4.5a's row *Queue page, Recently completed*.

- **Where it is read.** The home reads the trailers of each completed entry's closing commit (the entry's `sha`, in its own repo's checkout) and carries them on the entry in the `queue` reading, cached by sha, since a merged commit never changes. The page reads no git.
- **What is drawn.** When a line opens, each entry, under its `Resolved:` text and PR, gets one quiet line, e.g. *built by grinder-ao-2 · review sonnet SHIP · merged by techlead-ao-1*. The design settles which trailers count (the `Co-Authored-By:` sessions other than the model and the person, `Review:`, `Merged-by:`, perhaps `Precheck:`), how a missing one reads, and whether an oddity (no `Review:`, merger is the builder) is marked.
- **Limits to state.** A design entry's rounds land through several PRs, and only the closing one is read. An entry closed with no sha, or by a commit with no trailers (a by-hand close, another repo's older cadence), draws nothing.
- Titles stay as they are: another entry improves entry titles.

**Done when:** An opened Recently completed line on the Queue shows, for each entry with a closing sha, the sessions and verdicts its closing commit's trailers name, as the design says, with tests over a commit with trailers, one without, and an entry with no sha.
