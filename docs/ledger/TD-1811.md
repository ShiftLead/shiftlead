- `workorders.is_filing_order(pr, now, after, key, reads)` (the one test, at every call site; `reads` the root's `fresh` map, looked up by `str(number)`) beside `is_sync_order`, `key` the held key the reading was read at (the tick's `_held_keys` union over one remote's roots, which `_red_prs` computes as the read pass does): `filing` true with `filing_head` the row's head; with a key, `held` read at that head and key (`held_head`, `held_key`) and empty (unread: not yet); with an empty key, no fresh `held` needed, but a row carrying a non-empty `held` (kept from an earlier key) is never one; not a draft or fork; checks not red or `not_run`, not conflicting; `created` at least `after` ago; its base the default branch; no entry of the PR's reads (`held.reads`, the pass's `fresh`) with `state` *open*, any addressee's, the person's included.
