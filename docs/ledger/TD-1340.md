## TD-1340: A Queue card with two or more Decide or Do items draws the header's Start session beside the items', and its session walks every item in turn

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (the designer, from TD-1338's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1338
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`items_start` ~608, which becomes a count); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_card.html` (the header's Start session ~36–43, `{% if c.items_start %}`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`api_ledger_start` ~3207, its `why_key` and the `queue` reading's *waits on me*); `src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py` (`WORK_OPENING` ~130, `WORK_ITEM` ~139, `work_text` ~281); `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` (`card-start` ~689); `tests/test_ui_queue.py`, `tests/test_repoconfig.py`, the help's doc-bound tests

**Why:** TD-1338 (Paul, 2026-11-03: *there are times that there are multiple questions on a single card that it would make sense to handle in the same session*; he chose *keep both*): a card with several Decide and Do items has no button that starts one session on all of them, so the person starts from one item and pastes the others' ids in by hand.

**Fix:** as designed in §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session* (*Started from the header on a card with items*) and §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*:
- **The count:** the card carries how many of its items count, in place of the boolean `items_start` (ui/queue.py ~608): every Decide and Do item but a red PR, a fork's PR and an item *answered — waiting on <asker>* (TD-1331's want), an item *with you in <name>* included, held by another session or not — today's `items_start` set less the answered. The header draws its Start session, or its Open session by the holder rule, where that count is 0 or 2 or more, and nothing where it is 1.
- **The header's title** on a card with two or more is a new help entry beside `card-start` (say `card-start-all`), drawn by `help_title`: it starts one session for every item on this card that waits on you, one at a time, and reads Open session while yours is running. On a card with none it keeps `card-start`.
- **The road** is unchanged on the wire: `{repo, id, card}` with no `item`, on the card's `start`. Only with `card` and no `item`, `api_ledger_start` counts the card's items afresh from the `queue` reading by the same rule (the card's `wants` it already builds, app.py ~3281–3284 — never the length of *waits on me*, which also lists red PRs and answered entries), and where two or more count, `work_text` takes a new first sentence in place of the `why_key` one — a constant beside `WORK_ITEM` (say `WORK_ALL`): *I opened this session from the Queue to work through every item that waits on me on the card around {id}, one at a time: for each, read it, tell me its goal and what each answer leads to, and wait for my answer before the next.* `work.md` is unchanged; the id list and the closing sentences stand. With fewer at the press, the `why_key` sentence, whatever the page drew. An item's press (`item`) keeps `WORK_ITEM`, and an Inbox row's press (no `card`) its own sentence.
- **The help:** `card-start` drops *It is drawn only where no item on the card asks you anything: each such item has its own.* and says it is drawn where no item asks anything; `card-start-all` is new, and the help list in §4.5a gains it in this build (its wording is the build's, never a design PR's).

**Done when** a card with two Decide or Do items draws the header's Start session beside both items' buttons, with `card-start-all`'s title; a card with two *answered* items and nothing else draws the header's plain Start session, and its press opens on the `why_key` sentence; a card with one draws none in the header; a card with a red PR and one ledger item draws none; a header press on a two-item card opens a session whose prompt starts with the walk sentence and whose *waits on me* names both, a press when only one is left takes the `why_key` sentence, a press from a page drawn with one when two now count takes the walk sentence, and an Inbox row's press on such a card keeps its own; once the session runs every button on the card reads Open session; the help's doc-bound tests pass with the new `card-start`; and `pdm run test` passes.
