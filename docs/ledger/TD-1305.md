**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (the designer, from TD-1302's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1304, TD-1299
**Part of:** TD-1302
**Location:** a new `src/shiftlead/ui/static/sw.js` served at `/sw.js` (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, `Cache-Control: no-cache`), `/api/settings/push_*` routes in `app.py`, `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/settings.html` (the card after Telegram's), `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, `app.css`, `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` if the help list names the card, `tests/`; the mockup `docs/mockups/Settings.dc.html` (the **Push** card, from `gen.py`) and `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-02-td1302-push-card.png`, `…-td1302-phone.png`

**Why:** TD-1302's design: §4.10 *Told on the home-screen app too* (*The service worker*, *Where it works*), §4.5a row *Settings page — **You**: **Push***, §4.5 *On the home screen*.

**Fix:** as the design says, drawing from the `host` read's `push` view (TD-1304):
- `/sw.js`, registered as `/sw.js?k=<public key>`: on `push`, always show the notification — a payload absent, not JSON or missing a field is drawn as *something needs you*, `/inbox`, `tag` `more` — (title *shiftlead*, `body`, `tag`, the app's 192 px icon, `path` in `data`); on `notificationclick`, focus an open window and navigate it to `path`, or open one; on `pushsubscriptionchange`, subscribe again with its key and post `push_subscribe {…, key, replaces: <old id>}`, the old id from `event.oldSubscription`'s endpoint, or a plain `push_subscribe` when that is null. No fetch handler, no cache.
- The card as §4.5a's row and the mockup draw it: the switch reads on when this browser's subscription id (the first 16 hex digits of SHA-256 of its endpoint, by `crypto.subtle`) is among the devices; its press asks permission, unsubscribes a subscription the home does not list, registers the worker, subscribes with the public key and calls `push_subscribe` with that `key`; off, unsubscribes and calls `push_unsubscribe`; the devices' lines (the date when older than a day) with **Remove** behind a confirm naming the label and its since date; *push is paused: …* while it stands; **on all devices**, **with Telegram** (with *Telegram is off: nothing is sent when push fails* under `fallback` with Telegram off), **secrets** saved by the card's **Save**; **Make key** beside the disabled switch under *no key*, disabled while the card has unsaved changes; **Send a test to this device**; `last_line`.
- The disabled switch and its one line in each case §4.5a lists, the node check first, then the secure-context check, before any `crypto.subtle` or `serviceWorker` call: not a secure context; an iPhone's Safari tab (not standalone); iOS below 16.4; permission denied; *no key*; *unreadable*; *reading the key…*; *set secrets first*; a node.

**Done when** the card is drawn as the mockup; on an `https` origin a desktop browser turns the switch on and receives **Send a test to this device**, and a tap on it opens `/inbox` in the app; each disabled case shows its line, and a malformed push still draws a notification (tested by stubbing the browser's APIs); the screenshots are taken under Playwright and named in the PR; `pdm run test` passes; and the PR adds a board line for Paul, due a day after the merge, to turn it on from his iPhone once it is promoted and confirm a delivery through Apple's push service (the person's check, not the PR's gate).
