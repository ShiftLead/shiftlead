## TD-1364: An entry held on decision (paul) still reads Needs you after the person answers its board decide item with a work order

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (ao-led-tds-ledger, a person-session in ledgerboard, through `ao td add --repo shiftlead`; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1375
**Location:** design §4.4 *The card's status* (the paragraph *A `decision (paul)` member whose question the person has answered is no need while its asker acts on the answer*, TD-1316, whose link is mail alone: *A hold no answered question names reads as before*), §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants*, §4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*; the code that reads it: `answered_hold` in `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (TD-1331), `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (the card's status and the member's word; `item_home`, `attach` and `CONTEXT`, which link a board decide line to its entry).

**Why:** a `decision (paul)` entry whose question is a board `decide` item (as TD-1355 draws it, in the entry's place) gives its card *needs you* even after the person has answered that item with a work order and a session is acting on it. On 2026-11-04 ledgerboard#TD-023's hold was asked by ledgerboard's board item (session `lb-designer`); Paul answered *One more round* (ledgerboard #117, #118; the item was closed later, #119), the designer held TD-023 for the round, and the Queue card still said *Needs you* while nothing was asked of him. Under ledgerboard v2.4 (SPEC *The decide lift*, *Closing on the answer*) a work order on a decision's `decide` item keeps the gate, logs `kept waiting: <gate> — by <who>; answer "<label>"` on the entry, and leaves the item open, decided, until whoever acts on it closes it. The ledger alone cannot tell *a question waits on the person* from *the person answered and a session works*; the board can. TD-1316 made exactly this distinction for an `ask` or `steer` in mail (*answered — waiting on <asker>*), and the design says a hold that no answered mail question names *reads as before*, so a board-asked hold has no such word: that is the gap, and why this is a design change before a build.

**Fix:** extend §4.4 *The card's status*'s answered-hold rule to a board `decide` item that names the member (its `Context:`, read as TD-1355 reads it): decided with an answer that does not lift the gate (a work order, ledgerboard's `closes()` false), the member reads a waiting word and gives the card no *needs you*, and it needs the person again when the next undecided `decide` item naming it lands, or the item closes without a lift. The round settles: whose name the word carries (the decided item's `session`, the entry's holder from the reading's `held`, as the asker expects — *waiting on <holder>* — or both in an order); how long it holds (the item open and decided; or the entry's `kept waiting` Log line, ledgerboard v2.4, against a bound such as TD-1316's day); whether it reuses *answered — waiting on <asker>* or needs a word of its own, and that **Lift the hold** stays in the ⋯ as TD-1316 keeps it. Design and build it after TD-1375 (TD-1369's decide-lift part), which brings `closes()` and the `kept waiting` Log line into ShiftLead's decide road. Done: the design says it; a test that a member whose board item is decided with a work order reads the waiting word and gives no *needs you*, and reads *needs you* again when a new undecided item names it; and a live read on the next such hold.
