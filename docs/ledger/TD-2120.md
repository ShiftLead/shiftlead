## TD-2120: A live check under a flow is told to read and never press, and can wait behind a fix or a gate

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 744c3b5fc72d4a10b64e8c1d56f68dac
**Blocked by:** TD-2051, TD-2052
**Part of:** TD-2114

**Why:** Once flows run, a pickable live check reaches a build stage, whose brief tells a session to build. Three of a live check's four endings leave the entry open behind a new blocker or gate, and no outcome a build stage has today can carry that: `work-pushed` is refused without the archive, and a merged pull request that leaves its entry open sends it to the person.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/stage_start.py, stage_end.py, stagerecord.py, stage_acts.py

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *A live check under a flow*, with the `check-waits` row of *An outcome is the session's word, checked* and reconcile's cases for it.

- **The mechanics** (`stage_start.told`): for an entry whose `Kind:` is `live-check`, whatever brief the stage picks, say that this replaces any building the brief asks for, that the live copy is read and never pressed, the four endings and each one's outcome, and that the cadence's fact-check comment is posted before `check-waits`.
- **The outcome** (`stage_end`): `check-waits`, taken on a build stage for a live check alone, with the facts of its row. It is written by no flow, so `flow3.OUTCOMES` and the flow check do not list it. It moves to `merge`, with a refused merge back to this stage at most three times.
- **The record** (`stagerecord.WAITS`, `stage_acts`): `waits: check` once merged, including after a crash between the merge and the write; holder, branch and pull request cleared; released when the entry is pickable, as one move counted against `max-moves`, re-entering by the way in where its stage has gone.

**Done when:** Tests on fixtures show: a live check's session is told the check's rules under a standard brief and under a person's own, and a build entry's is not; `check-waits` is refused on a build entry, on a pull request that archives the entry or changes a file outside the ledger and board, and where the head adds no blocker or only a reached `until`; accepted, it merges past a review stage; after the merge the record waits and no act offers it, including after a crash before the wait is written; the entry going pickable releases it to a fresh session in the same stage, counted as one move, and at `max-moves` it goes to the person; a live check that holds ends by `work-pushed` on the flow's own route.
