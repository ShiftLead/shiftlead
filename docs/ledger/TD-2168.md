## TD-2168: A job session's launch is tested for its name and prompt, not for the grants, host, ledger and project it starts with

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 8f590faeb7dc48efa217cf62f67e9cb2

**Why:** A job session's launch (`stage_job_start.launch`, #4516, TD-2053) builds a `teams.Launch` from the role and the repo's config, and `tests/test_stage_job_start.py` asserts only `role`, `lane`, `name`, `team`, the model and the prompt's shape. Evidence: each of these edits leaves all 11 tests passing: `host=host if host != here else ""` to `host=host`; `cfg.venv = teams.node_venv(host, here)` removed; `grants=list(role.grants)` to `grants=[]`; `project=org.project_of(team.repo)` to `None`; `context_bound=role.context_bound` to `0`; `unlisted=unlisted` to `False`; `ledger=cfg.ledger` to `None`. A job session started with no grants, on the wrong host or without its repo's ledger would be unseen.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/stage_job_start.py; tests/test_stage_job_start.py

**Fix:** Extend `test_a_job_starts_in_the_build_role_on_the_seats_model_its_lane_the_work_order`, or add one, to assert the `Launch` fields the job takes from the role and config (host on a node versus here, grants, project, context bound, ledger, venv, unlisted), the way the entry-stage launch tests do. Check each fails with its line reverted.

**Done when:** Reverting any one of the seven lines named in the Why makes a test in `tests/test_stage_job_start.py` fail.
