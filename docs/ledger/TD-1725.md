**Why:** A design conversation's derived claim outlives the design: TD-1529's builds were filed and Paul's Go given, yet the interactive session that ran the design still held the design entry by its branch `td-1529`, so the card read as if that session owned the builders' work. TD-1567 designs the release: design §4.8 *Reports*, *A finished design lets go of its derived claim*.

**Fix:** "Done" is read from the facts of the record's repo as the Queue maps a record to one (`queue._Repos.of_record`, a resolved-path compare of `rec.repo` with the readings' roots — or a helper shared with it; a node's record whose path matches no root is left as it is) — the ledger read against origin; a record it cannot map is left as it is: the entry open, `kind` `design-first`, `parts_open` or `parts_archived` non-empty (an archived part keeps it done after its last build archives while the entry waits on something else, §4.9c), not `ledger.decided_by(e, PERSON)` (the one predicate, never a second string test); a ref the facts do not hold is left alone.
1. The write: where the home writes derived progress — the tick's `reports.derive` results in `agent_tick.py` (before the `report_progress` upserts, ~line 5134) and a node's derived reports as the home takes them (`agent_remote._take_derived`, applied as the home's own tick does, §4.4a) — drop a non-declared `claimed` entry (derived; a scraped one too) with no PR on such a ref — the same set step 2's `retire_branch_claims` deletes.
2. The retire (at the home alone: `mode == "home"`, where `self.remote` is filled): on `DERIVE_EVERY`, the home's tick retires such an entry already on any record, local or a node's — the node's records (`self.remote`) in a pass of their own with their own clock, since the derive loop walks `self.sessions` alone, saved and pushed as `_take_derived` does (`_save`, then `_push_changes`) — whether or not the record is otherwise due — after the derive loop's existing skips (a record with no `dir`, a `closed` record, one with no agent adapter), which stand (the node skips a record with nothing to derive and `_send_derived` sends nothing, so the home cannot wait for one); the delete is `retire_branch_claims`'s, whatever branch the record is on.
A declared claim, a `done` and an entry with a PR are out of reach. Tests: a derived claim on a designed entry is neither written nor kept, on the checked-out branch too; one whose only part is archived while the entry stays open still lets go; one on a design still gated by `decision (paul)`, or with no part at all, stands; a declared claim on a designed entry stands (the designer's fallback archive of a design left open, §4.9c, among them); a node's sent derived claim is filtered, and one already on a node's record is retired by the home's tick (a node record whose path matches a root; one matching none is left); a Start session on a designed top entry (§4.5 screen 12 *A designed top entry is the exception*) holds no derived claim and Open session still finds it by name.

**Done when:** A derived claim with no PR on a `design-first` entry whose builds are filed and whose `decision (paul)` gate is lifted is neither written nor kept, as design §4.8 *A finished design lets go of its derived claim* says, with tests of each case in the Fix.
