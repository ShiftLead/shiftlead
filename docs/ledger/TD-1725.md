## TD-1725: A derived claim on a design-first entry lets go once the design is done (builds filed, gate lifted)

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 54d0d777e25e4789901f327d92a652f2
**Part of:** TD-1567
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (derived reports' write), src/sessionorc/agent_remote.py (_take_derived), src/sessionorc/reports.py; design §4.8 Reports

**Why:** A design conversation's derived claim outlives the design: TD-1529's builds were filed and Paul's Go given, yet the interactive session that ran the design still held the design entry by its branch `td-1529`, so the card read as if that session owned the builders' work. TD-1567 designs the release: design §4.8 *Reports*, *A finished design lets go of its derived claim*.

**Fix:** Where the home writes derived progress — the tick's `reports.derive` results in `agent_tick.py` (before the `report_progress` upserts, ~line 5134) and a node's derived reports as the home takes them (`agent_remote._take_derived`, which applies them as the home's own tick does, §4.4a) — drop a derived `claimed` entry with no PR whose ref is a `design-first` entry whose design is done, and retire one already on the record (the same delete as a branch-only claim's retire, `retire_branch_claims`, whatever branch the record is on). "Done" is read from the record's repo's facts (`self._repos.get(s.repo)`, the ledger read against origin): the entry open, `kind` `design-first`, `parts_open` non-empty, no `decision (paul)` in `blocked_by`; a ref the facts do not hold is left alone. `derive`'s own `retire` covers only left branches, so compute these refs from `s.progress` (derived, `claimed`, no `pr`), and let a record holding one count as due on `DERIVE_EVERY` even with no branch, pending, left or reviews. A declared claim, a `done` and an entry with a PR are out of reach. Tests: a derived claim on a designed entry is neither written nor kept, on the checked-out branch too; one on a design still gated by `decision (paul)`, or with no open part, stands; a declared claim on a designed entry stands; a node's sent derived claim is filtered the same way.

**Done when:** A derived claim with no PR on a `design-first` entry whose builds are filed and whose `decision (paul)` gate is lifted is neither written nor kept, as design §4.8 *A finished design lets go of its derived claim* says, with tests of each case in the Fix.
