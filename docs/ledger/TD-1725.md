## TD-1725: A derived claim on a design-first entry lets go once the design is done (builds filed, gate lifted)

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 54d0d777e25e4789901f327d92a652f2
**Part of:** TD-1567
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (derived reports' write), src/sessionorc/reports.py; design §4.8 Reports

**Why:** A design conversation's derived claim outlives the design: TD-1529's builds were filed and Paul's Go given, yet the interactive session that ran the design still held the design entry by its branch `td-1529`, so the card read as if that session owned the builders' work. TD-1567 designs the release: design §4.8 *Reports*, *A finished design lets go of its derived claim*.

**Fix:** Where the home writes a derived progress entry — the tick's `reports.derive` results in `agent_tick.py` (the `report_progress` upserts), and a node's derived reports as the home receives them — drop a derived `claimed` entry with no PR whose ref is a `design-first` entry whose design is done (open in the home's repo facts, `parts_open` non-empty, no `decision (paul)` in `blocked_by`), and retire one already on the record (the same delete as a branch-only claim's retire, whatever branch the record is on). A ref the repo facts do not hold is left alone. A declared claim, a `done` and an entry with a PR are out of reach. Tests: a derived claim on a designed entry is neither written nor kept; one on a design still gated by `decision (paul)`, or with no open part, stands; a declared claim on a designed entry stands; a node's sent derived claim is filtered the same way.

**Done when:** A derived claim with no PR on a `design-first` entry whose builds are filed and whose `decision (paul)` gate is lifted is neither written nor kept, as design §4.8 *A finished design lets go of its derived claim* says, with tests of each case in the Fix.
