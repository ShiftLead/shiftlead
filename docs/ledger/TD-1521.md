## TD-1521: Build TD-1476 (2 of 3): an ask to an unable pool member goes to the first able member after it, at the send and on the tick's move

**Type:** feature

**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 32390a816cbe431388569f19b4f6283e

**Part of:** TD-1476
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py (_route_around ~265, _reroute_waiting ~317, _question_seat ~489); design §4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool*

**Why:** TD-1476's design (§4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool*): routing around an unable seat (TD-1443) must try the pool's later members, in order, before the techlead or the person.

**Fix:** Build the routing of §4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool* (*Routing within the pool comes first*):
- `agent_mail.py` `_route_around` (~265): a general question (`kind` ask, `pr` None, no `entry`) to a record whose `seat` carries `pool` and that `seat_unable` reads unable goes to the record of the same team and `seat.pool` with the lowest `rank` above its own that is not superseded, is fillable (`_fillable`), is able (`seat_able`) and is not the sender; `routed` is `{seat, name, why, at}` of the unable member, the sender's reply printing it as today. A member whose counts would refuse the ask (mailbox depth, one open question per sender per seat) is passed over as an unable one is, at the send and on the tick's move: each candidate is tried with `_landing_refusal(records, sender, member, moved)` (~409, the tick's helper; a member is a question seat, so its edge is checked), the real sender given; with every later member unable or refusing, today's road applies with its own refusals. No later member able: today's road (`_route_lead`, else the person), unchanged. A `pr` ask and an `entry` envelope keep today's roads.
- `_reroute_waiting` (~317): a general question waiting on an unable pool member moves to that next member by the same choice, before the techlead or the person, with today's `rerouted` close and landing rules (*waits on <member>* where a count refuses).
- Never to a lower rank: an earlier member able again takes only new asks sent to it.
- The person's own question (`_route_around` returns early for `sender == PERSON`, ~275) lands on the unable member, and `_reroute_waiting` (which today keeps a person's question where no techlead is able, ~349–356) moves it to the next able member; with no later member able the existing rule applies (the team's able techlead, else it stays), never to the person.
- A working member is able (`seat_able`): the pool is a fallback, not a share. An on-call member is able where rule 3 would fill it; one rule 3 would not fill (`unfillable`, the fill ceiling among its causes) is skipped.
- `_question_seat` (~489): a pool member of rank 2 or more is no candidate, so `_one_seat` sees the pool as its first member alone.
- Tests: a member 2 whose mailbox is full is passed over for member 3; a person's question to an unable member 1 moves to member 2 on the tick, and with no member able stays on member 1; a working member 2 takes the ask; member 1 limited → member 2 gets the ask with `routed`; members 1 and 2 unable → member 3; all unable → the techlead, else the person; an ask to member 2 when it is unable goes to member 3, never member 1; member 2 as the sender is skipped; a `pr` ask to an unable member goes to the person; a waiting ask on member 1 moves to member 2 on the tick it turns unable; `--team` with an unable techlead and an able pool resolves to member 1, and with both unable to the techlead.

**Done when:** An ask to an unable pool member lands on the first able member after it, carrying `routed`, at the send and on the tick's move; only with none able does it go to the techlead or the person; the tests pass.
