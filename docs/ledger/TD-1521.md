**Why:** TD-1476's design (§4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool*): routing around an unable seat (TD-1443) must try the pool's later members, in order, before the techlead or the person.

**Ask:** Should TD-1476's builds (TD-1520, TD-1521, TD-1522: an asks seat's pool: of profiles, each member a seat of its own, a question to an unable member going to the next able member before the techlead or you) go ahead as written after four review rounds ended at the cap, every build-changing fix read by a later review?

**Fix:** Build the routing of §4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool* (*Routing within the pool comes first*):
- `agent_mail.py` `_route_around` (~265): a general question (`kind` ask, `pr` None, no `entry`) to a record whose `seat` carries `pool` and that `seat_unable` reads unable goes to the record of the same team and `seat.pool` with the lowest `rank` above its own that is not superseded, is fillable (`_fillable`), is able (`seat_able`) and is not the sender; `routed` is `{seat, name, why, at}` of the unable member, the sender's reply printing it as today. A member whose counts would refuse the ask (mailbox depth, one open question per sender per seat) is passed over as an unable one is, at the send and on the tick's move: each candidate is tried with `_landing_refusal(records, sender, member, moved)` (~448, the tick's helper; a member is a question seat, so its edge is checked), the real sender given — at the send no entry exists yet, so the check builds the `MailEntry` it would land, and runs only on a candidate already found fillable (`records[target]` must exist); with every later member unable or refusing, today's road applies with its own refusals. No later member able: today's road (`_route_lead`, else the person), unchanged. A `pr` ask and an `entry` envelope keep today's roads.
- `_reroute_waiting` (~317): a general question waiting on an unable pool member moves to that next member by the same choice, before the techlead or the person, with today's `rerouted` close and landing rules; a count-refusing member is passed over, and *waits on <the techlead | the person>* is written only where a count refuses the existing road.
- Never to a lower rank: an earlier member able again takes only new asks sent to it.
- The person's own question (`_route_around` returns early for `sender == PERSON`, ~275) lands on the unable member, and `_reroute_waiting` (which today keeps a person's question where no techlead is able, ~349–356) moves it to the next able member; with no later member able the existing rule applies (the team's able techlead, else it stays), never to the person.
- A working member is able (`seat_able`): the pool is a fallback, not a share. An on-call member is able where rule 3 would fill it; one rule 3 would not fill (`unfillable`, the fill ceiling among its causes) is skipped.
- `_question_seat` (~489): a pool member of rank 2 or more is no candidate, and member 1 is read as able when any member of its pool is (the same choice `_route_around` makes), so `_one_seat` sees the pool as one seat; the any-member test reads no sender and no counts (the send's own choice then stands), and the pool is a candidate only while member 1 passes `_fillable` (~513–518).
- Tests: `--team` with an unable techlead and an able member 1 resolves to member 1; with member 1 unable and member 2 able it resolves to member 1 (and the send moves the ask to member 2); with every member unable it resolves to the techlead; member 1 sat out (unfillable) and member 2 able: the pool is no candidate; a `pr` ask via `--team` with member 1 unable, member 2 able and the techlead unable resolves to member 1 and goes to the person (a `pr` ask never moves within a pool); a member 2 whose mailbox is full is passed over for member 3; a person's question to an unable member 1 moves to member 2 on the tick, and with no member able stays on member 1; a working member 2 takes the ask; member 1 limited → member 2 gets the ask with `routed`; members 1 and 2 unable → member 3; all unable → the techlead, else the person; an ask to member 2 when it is unable goes to member 3, never member 1; member 2 as the sender is skipped; a `pr` ask to an unable member goes to the person; a waiting ask on member 1 moves to member 2 on the tick it turns unable.

**Done when:** An ask to an unable pool member lands on the first able member after it, carrying `routed`, at the send and on the tick's move; only with none able does it go to the techlead or the person; the tests pass.
