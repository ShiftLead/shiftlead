**Why:** TD-1476's design (§4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool*): routing around an unable seat (TD-1443) must try the pool's later members, in order, before the techlead or the person.

**Fix:** Build the routing of §4.9b *A seat as an ordered pool* (*Routing within the pool comes first*):
- `agent_mail.py` `_route_around`: an `ask` to a record whose `seat` carries `pool` and that `seat_unable` reads unable goes to the first record of the same team and `seat.pool` with a higher `rank` that is not superseded, is fillable (`_fillable`) and is able (`seat_able`), lowest rank first; `routed` is `{seat, name, why, at}` of the unable member, the sender's reply printing it as today. None able: today's road (`_route_lead`, else the person), unchanged.
- `_reroute_waiting`: a general question waiting on an unable pool member moves to that next able member by the same choice, before the techlead or the person, with today's `rerouted` close and landing rules.
- Never to a lower rank: an earlier member able again takes only new asks sent to it.
- `_question_seat` / `_one_seat`: unchanged (no member carries `tags`); test that a team with a techlead and a pool still resolves `--team` to the techlead.
- Tests: member 1 limited → member 2 gets the ask with `routed`; members 1 and 2 unable → member 3; all unable → the techlead, else the person; an ask addressed to member 2 when it is unable goes to member 3, never member 1; a waiting ask on member 1 moves to member 2 on the tick it turns unable.

**Done when:** An ask to an unable pool member lands on the first able member after it, carrying `routed`, at the send and on the tick's move; only with none able does it go to the techlead or the person; the tests pass.
