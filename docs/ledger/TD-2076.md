**Why:** After the switch-over's marker is written nothing of the first shape may run, yet its code stays until TD-2056 so the window can be undone (design 4.9e, What decides which shape runs). Unless each first-shape mechanism reads the marker, lanes and flows would both act on one entry after the window.

**Ask:** Should the builders build the switch that turns the old shape off once the switch-over's marker is written?

**Fix:** Wire the one function that reads the cut_over marker into every mechanism of the first shape, as design 4.9e What decides which shape runs gives it: after the marker no first-shape team definition is read, so no session is a member, no lane exists and no team defines a seat, and each mechanism reads the marker and does not rely on an empty list. Policy rules 2, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 14 and the reader chain act on nothing, and rules 1, 4 and 5 act on no member; the reading of which entry is pickable is kept for the Offer act. Rule 3 stays for the director and for a record marked builtin (the tech lead, the sweeper, the prompts seat; design 4.9d How each one runs, whose records TD-2065 writes), and what rules 1, 4 and 5 say of a seat's session they say of those; no team-defined seat is read, no manager is filled and no record is due by team. This build turns the first shape off and fills nothing itself. None of rules 1 to 5 acts on a stage's or an audit's session, and ao progress restart from one is refused with the design's words. After the marker every reading of a seat (rule 3's fill, the address a question resolves to, the tagging seat) takes only a record marked builtin or the director's. Rule 13 no longer reads a decided board line against the lanes (its reading against a job record is TD-2053's), and reports an open ask, except one to a first-shape member or seat read from its record as the design gives it. The page's concluded is drawn for no team; the tick runs no first-shape sweep and launches nothing by a profile; ao team flow, size, max, add and on-work, the Settings team card's flow pick and Apply and the Members dialog are refused with the design's words and the team page named; ao pr held answers that no pull request is held; the first shape's team start is refused in favour of the team page's. Before the marker each is unchanged.

**Done when:** On a fixture home with the marker written and a first-shape team definition still in its files: a tick tells no lane, fills no team-defined seat and runs no first-shape sweep; each of the named verbs and controls is refused with the words; ao pr held answers as the design says; a session whose record carries restart_wanted is not restarted, a wound-down team is not started for work, a team with only its tech lead live for ten minutes is not wound down, an idle stage's session is not nudged, and ao progress restart from a stage's session is refused. A first-shape seat record with no builtin mark is not filled for an open ask, a first-shape manager's record is not filled for a pending permission, and the director's record is still due for a pending prompt; that a built-in seat or the director is filled on a login is TD-2065's and TD-2068's to test. An ask sent with --team to a team whose only seat record is a first-shape one is refused and not delivered to it. The stuck-work pass names no lane for a decided board line, and reports an open ask to a person's own session, and reports no open ask to a first-shape member. On the same fixture without the marker every one behaves as today.
