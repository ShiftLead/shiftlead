## TD-1853: A Not clear answer on every item that waits on the person sends it back to its writer and is counted

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature

**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d4e491f8e6ad438183dfdb01a65e8057

**Why:** TD-1845's design, rule 6 (design §4.10 *Text a person reads is checked at its writer*: **Not clear**; §4.5a row **Not clear**). Paul, 2026-11-14 (m-2996b23628a7): a *Not clear* answer on every person-facing item sends it back to its writer to rewrite and is counted, so plainness, which no check can judge, gets a measured feedback loop instead of relying only on guidance.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the plain-text checks and the Not clear answer as this design now stands?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js; src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (PERSON_ACTS, the board and ledger actions, witness_press); src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py (inbox_not_clear, board_edit, ledger_decide); src/sessionorc/models.py (CLOSED_REASONS, asks_waiting); src/sessionorc/witness.py (PRESS_WORDS); src/shiftlead/ui/help.py; src/sessionorc/mail.py (PERSON_ONLY); src/sessionorc/modes.py (MAILBOX); the board and ledger Reply roads; the host log; src/sessionorc/board.py (NOT_CLEAR, edit_line, write_back); src/shiftlead/feedback.py (digest); src/shiftlead/feedback.md

**Fix:** 1. **The answer**: `inbox_row.html` (card and Inbox modes) draws **Not clear** on an `ask` or `steer` to the person (passed up or not), a board `decide` or `act` line and a ledger entry that waits on the person, wherever their answers are drawn (a *with you in <name>* item among them) — never on a look (an entry carrying `shots`, or a board `look` line), a rerouted or routed-around question, or any other item; never outlined, never a default or a lift. In `card` mode it is the last of the answers, after the answer buttons as they stand (TD-1842, which builds the one answer bar, carries it into slot 1's last place); on the Inbox's row it is among the row's own controls after **Reply**, never in the *suggested by* group. Title *the writer rewrites it*; no confirm. `app.js` posts it as a fixed press (`data-act="not-clear"`): mail to `/api/person/notclear` through `PERSON_ACTS` (a new key `notclear` → `inbox_not_clear`); a board line through the board's decide action with `not_clear: true`, which `app.py` sends to `board_edit` as `action: decide` and `board.NOT_CLEAR`, `answers` the list the row holds (the agent's `_board_answer` is what accepts the fixed answer whatever the line offers); a ledger entry through the ledger's action with `not_clear: true`, which `app.py` sends to `ledger_decide` with the fixed words, skipping the check that a Reply carries words.
2. **Mail**: a new person-only RPC `inbox_not_clear {msg}` in `src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`, beside `rpc_inbox_go_with_it` and shaped as it is — `self._person_entry` first, as in `inbox_go_with_it`, `mail.withdrawn_refusal` next, added to `mail.PERSON_ONLY` (design §4.8; `tests/test_person_only.py`'s count up by one), placed in `modes.MAILBOX` beside `inbox_go_with_it`. On a question its writer asked: closes every copy with `closed_reason` `not_clear` (`not_clear` joins `models.CLOSED_REASONS`, and the reasons `Session.asks_waiting` counts a `system` note against, so a seat asker is due), takes no `steer` default (a paused one too), owes no outcome, and files the asker a `system` note on the question's thread, waking it as a person's reply does: *The person marked this Not clear: rewrite it for them and ask again.* On a passed-up copy it **undoes the pass**: drops the person's copy and clears `passed_up` and `recommend` on every copy (once TD-661's `passes` chain is built: pops the latest entry and sets them back from the one before, clearing them only when none is left), so the question is open again in the passer's (`recommend.by`) inbox; a paused `steer` is resumed as `rpc_inbox_resume` does it (`bound` moved later by the time held, `paused_at` cleared, its asker sent the same *resumed* note as an ordinary `note` wake); and files the passer, followed along `superseded_by` to its live successor, a `system` note, waking it as a reply does: *The person marked your pass-up Not clear: rewrite your --question and pass it up again.* (a pass made before TD-1846, with no `recommend.question`: *The person could not follow the question you passed up: pass it up again with --question.*) An orphaned question is read first: closed with no note, a passer told nothing. A passer gone — no record at the end of its `superseded_by` chain — leaves nobody to rewrite it: the question closes `not_clear` and the asker is noted, as for a question its writer asked. Every other passer, closed, exited or unable among them, gets the question back, and the question's own lifecycle (the sweep's marks, the route-around rule) moves it on. Refused on a closed entry, a note, a reply. Its reply is `{writer, kind, repo}` — the record's `name`, else its id with the host dropped (an orphan's the `orphaned` record's `name`); the entry's kind; its repo — each `-` where it has none. Once TD-1829/TD-1831 build the lifting answer's key, it is refused while that key is in flight, *is already being answered*, as the question's other closes are. The closed row's words — `app.js`'s two closed-reason readers and `inbox_row.html`'s — read *marked Not clear — back to `<writer>`*.
3. **A board line**: `src/sessionorc/board.py` gains `NOT_CLEAR` (*Not clear: rewrite this line — the person could not follow it.*) and `not_clear(text, answer)` beside `HAVE_A_SESSION` and `session_do`; `edit_line` accepts it on a `decide` and an `act` line, checked against the line found on origin's head; `_board_answer` in `agent_inbox.py` takes it whatever `answers` the line offers; and `write_back`'s board-only test becomes `session_do(...) or not_clear(...)`, so no gate in the line's `Context:` is lifted or logged: the decided line is a work order `board:<key>`, and the session that takes it closes the line and adds it again rewritten.
4. **A ledger entry**: `ledger_decide` with the fixed words *Not clear: rewrite it — the person could not follow it.*, as every Reply on an entry: its decided line the work order, the entry grey *answered — waiting on <holder>*, its gate standing.
5. **The count**: `witness.PRESS_WORDS` gains `"not-clear": False` (no `dir`: the record tied to `witness.OWN_REPO` wherever the item's repo is served), and after the road's call succeeds the person route in `app.py` sends it through the `witness_press` RPC by a helper hoisted out of `_new_routes` (`dir` optional, empty here) and shared with the New session form, target `<kind> <repo>:<ref> <writer>` — `<kind>` one of `ask`, `steer`, `decide`, `act`, `entry`; `<repo>` the registry name (`-` for mail sent with none); `<ref>` the mail id, `board:<key>` or `TD-n`; `<kind>`, `<repo>` and `<writer>` from `inbox_not_clear`'s reply for mail; `<writer>` `-` otherwise. Design §4.4's list of press places names it. `ao feedback digest` (`src/shiftlead/feedback.py`) adds `not_clear` to its index, `{read, presses: [{writer, kind, week, n}]}` (`read` whether the home's witness directory is there, `week` the ISO week of `at` in UTC written `2026-W46`, `n` per `(week, writer, kind)`, sorted by them, the window `since <= at < until`; filled in both modes; `feedback.md`'s report-mode steps do not read it): the window's `not-clear` presses read by `witness.records()` in the `ao` process from the home's store (filled on the home machine, empty on a node, which the report says), counted by writer, kind and week from each record's text split on spaces, empty where the log has none (design §4.7 `ao feedback`, step 2); `feedback.md`'s report states them.
6. **The briefs**: every brief TD-1848 changed says, in the same place, that **Not clear** sends an item back to its writer to rewrite, and the techlead brief that a pass-up so marked is back in its inbox, to rewrite `--question` and pass it up again.
7. **Help**: the control's help entry in `help.py` (design §4.5a row **Not clear**), its words the row's; run the doc-bound tests.
8. Screenshot a card and an Inbox row showing **Not clear** (Playwright, a scratch home) and commit the shots under `docs/mockups/reviews/`, named in the PR.
9. Tests: each road; the mail close and its note; a passed-up copy's pass undone, its passer noted and able to pass it up again, the person's copy gone; a paused passed-up `steer` resumed by the undo and lapsing at its moved bound; a gone passer's question closed and its asker noted; a withdrawn question refused; an orphaned one closed with no note; a `steer` so closed, paused or not, takes no default and owes nothing; a seat asker due after its note; a closed techlead passer getting the question back; a passer with no record left closing it and noting the asker; an orphaned passed-up question closed with no note to the passer; a text test that `feedback.md`'s report-mode steps do not read `not_clear`; a paused passed-up `steer`'s asker sent the resume note; a session caller refused; a board `decide` and an `act` line decided with `NOT_CLEAR`, and a gated `decide` line's gate neither lifted nor logged; each press record; the answer drawn on the named kinds alone, and on the Inbox row after **Reply**.

**Done when:** **Not clear** is drawn on every ask, steer, decide and act line and waiting ledger entry, but a look or a rerouted question, — last on the Queue, after **Reply** on the Inbox row — and nowhere else; its press closes a question as `not_clear` and tells its writer, undoes a pass-up and tells its passer, decides a board line or a ledger entry with the fixed words as a work order, and records one `not-clear` press, which the feedback review tallies; the briefs say that Not clear sends an item back to its writer, and the techlead brief what a passer does with a marked pass-up; the help entry is in `help.py`; the shots are in the PR; `pdm run test` green.
