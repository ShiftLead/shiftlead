## TD-2046: Smoke passes: show that a model on a login can do a stage's work before a flow relies on it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3d88984afd7d45faaf0111eb7e3856e6
**Blocked by:** TD-1954, decision (paul)

**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-21, in TD-1954's conversation: smoke passes get an entry of their own. The Tools page design (§4.2b) draws no smoke passes and leaves them to the Flows design: a pass is a tool, a model and a kind of work shown to do its job before a flow relies on them. TD-1954 is already large (stages, outcomes, the stage record, audits, two pages), so it does not design them.

**Ask:** Once the Flows design is built, will you run the design round on smoke passes, the check that a model can do a stage's job?

**Fix:** Design the smoke pass. The questions the round answers: what a pass proves (a model on a login can take a stage of one kind from its brief to an accepted outcome); what it runs against, so it touches no real entry; when it runs and how long a pass holds; where its result is kept; what the Flows page and the flow check show for a stage whose model has no pass; and whether a missing pass refuses the flow or only warns.

**Done when:** A design section says what a smoke pass is, when it runs, where its result lives and what a flow shows without one, with its builds filed.
