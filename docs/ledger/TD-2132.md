## TD-2132: Review copies in a live seat live in one place and age out after a day

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 55ba7464d84b45dcb8fc589d480c796e
**Blocked by:** TD-2130, TD-2131
**Part of:** TD-2126

**Why:** Part of TD-2126, design §4.6 *A live seat's rebuildable copies*, part 4. Review copies hold about 12,000 to 18,000 inodes each and nothing ever releases them in a live seat.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/scratch.py, src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py, src/shiftlead/briefs, design §4.6 *A live seat's rebuildable copies*

**Fix:** Add the `copies/` rule to the hourly pass and as the pressure pass's rule 4, not through `reclaim`'s `take`, which reads modification times alone: the real-directory checks; a copy's age by `_quiet` with its new change-time flag, bounded by `RELEASE_WALK_S` in the hourly pass and `diskbook.WALK_S` in rule 4 (a cut walk keeps the copy, listed as *walk cut short* hourly and logged under pressure); one set of process-held paths built once a pass (working directory, root, executable, maps, open files), a gone process skipped, and an unlistable `/proc` or an unreadable process of this uid keeping every copy; the git-work and transcript holds; finishing any `.gone-*` first with no age test and never taking one as a candidate, the rename to a free `.gone-<name>` (suffix `-<n>`) before removal, and the worktree prune. Add to every `src/shiftlead/briefs/*.md` that carries the `$SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH` line: a copy goes in `$SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH/copies/<name>`, directories only, and may go after two quiet hours under pressure; a failed suite's tree may go ten minutes after its last write under pressure, so read it first. The hourly rule runs under the same guards as TD-2131's (given `named`, no blind adapter, the deadline), and a dry run lists a copy as gone and removes nothing. Test a `cp -a` copy.

**Done when:** A copy past 24 hours is removed, and past 2 hours under pressure. One with a process in it, git work or a transcript store is kept, and so is one whose walk was cut short, and every copy where `/proc` or a process of this uid cannot be read. A leftover `.gone-*` is finished and never blocks a later copy of the same name. A symlinked `copies` or candidate and a dry run remove nothing. Nothing outside `copies/` is removed by this rule, and each role brief that carries the `$SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH` line names the place.
