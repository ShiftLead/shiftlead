## TD-1740: docs/ledger/README.md still describes the Status as free text, which ledgerboard v2.7 retired

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 9e22aee2b07e4f9b86fcc02c0c3f8644

**Why:** The techlead's read of #3602 (m-165bfa057b2b) noted that `docs/ledger/README.md` still says "the Status says what is next". Under ledgerboard v2.7 an open entry's Status is `Open`, and a closed one's is a dated close (`Resolved <date>`, `Dropped <date>`). Its notes go in the Log (`ledger.py note`). The same README also tells a reader to archive by `git mv` and a hand-set Status, and to spell out a partial resolution in the Status. A reader following those lines writes what v2.7's helpers refuse or never write.

**Location:** docs/ledger/README.md

**Fix:** In `docs/ledger/README.md`, say what v2.7 does. An entry is archived with `agent-cadence run ledger.py archive TD-n --why … --pr <n>`, which writes the dated Status and the `**Resolved:**` line and moves the file. A partly resolved entry records what shipped and what remains in its Log (`ledger.py note`). What a grinder cannot build is said by its Kind or Owner, and its Log says what is next.

**Done when:** `docs/ledger/README.md` names no Status other than `Open` and a dated close, and it names the helpers for archiving and notes.
