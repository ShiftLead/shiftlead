**Why:** A person choosing a team's flow and counts needs to see the path and where its work is now.

**Ask:** Should the builders build the team page's route map?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *The route map draws the flow as lines* and band 2 of *The page*: lines derived from the flow by the stated rule, a stop per stage with its kind, model, the login a session would start on now and its number, a pill per record in the stage and a dashed pill per record waiting on the person at the stage that asked, *Beside the path*, and the team limit. An audit's next clock is drawn once TD-2053's run records exist. Under the map, a last group *waiting on you, its stage is gone* for a record that waits on the person in a stage the flow no longer has. Under *Beside the path* the page also draws the line *Built-in seats: set `<set>`*, linking to the set's page; it is drawn by whichever of this build and TD-2066 lands second.

**Done when:** For each built-in flow and a flow with a stage on no line, the map draws the lines the rule gives; pills match the stage records of a fixture exactly; a record the page cannot place is drawn as such, never guessed. Once TD-2066 has landed, the line under *Beside the path* names the repo's built-in set.
