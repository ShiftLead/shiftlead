## TD-1267: Copy's toast in the Focus pane says nothing when a selection stops at the screen's edge or a wrapped line copies as rows

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (the designer, from TD-1263)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1263
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the Focus terminal's `copySel`, the copy-on-select `mouseup` listener, `#tcopy`'s click, `AO.toast`); `tests/test_ui.py` or a new `tests/test_ui_focus_copy.py`; design §4.6 *A selection is one screen, and a copy is its rows*, §4.5a *Copy / Paste* and *copy on select*

**Why:** TD-1263 decided not to scroll a selection past the pane's edge or join a wrapped line at copy time (neither behaves the same every time), and to point the person to the Transcript instead. Today a drag stops at the edge and a wrapped line copies broken with no word from the page, so the person finds the limit by pasting.

**Fix:** as §4.6 *A selection is one screen* writes it:
- Track a selection drag: a primary-button `mousedown` on the terminal starts one; while it is held, a `mousemove` (on the document, since the pointer leaves the pane) whose `clientY` is above the top or below the bottom of xterm.js's `.xterm-screen` marks it *past the edge*; the page's `mouseup` ends it. A Shift+click is no drag.
- One pure function picks the note, e.g. `AO.copyNote({edge, rows, firstMulti, transcript})` → the note's words or `""`: the edge's note first; the first-copy note only when the copied text holds a newline, no earlier copy of the page's load held one, and the record has a Transcript (`v.adapter_id`, the header's own test); the edge's wording without a Transcript is *copy a screen at a time*.
- `copySel` (Copy, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+Shift+C, copy on select) appends the note to *copied* or *clipboard blocked …* after a dash; a drag past the edge with copy on select off, or a release that copied nothing, toasts the note alone, without its dash. A toast with a note stays 7 s (`AO.toast`'s refusal time); one without is unchanged.
- The page-load flag for *first copy of several rows* is set by any copy whose text holds a newline, whether or not its note was drawn.

**Done when:** in a Focus pane, a drag held past the top or bottom of the terminal and released toasts *copied — the selection stops at the screen's edge; Transcript has the whole conversation* (copy on select on) or the note alone (off), every time; the first copy of several rows on a fresh load adds *— a wrapped line copies as rows here; Transcript has it whole* and the second does not; on a `shell` record the edge note reads *— the selection stops at the screen's edge: copy a screen at a time* and the first-copy note is never drawn; a one-row copy inside the screen toasts *copied* as today. A test drives the note function over each case (edge with and without a Transcript, first and second multi-row copy, a one-row copy, edge and multi-row together); a UI check on a scratch home shows the edge toast, its shot named in the PR.
