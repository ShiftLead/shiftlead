**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature

**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 05e729444b164c05853d75d357dca5a8

**Why:** TD-1984's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*, *Beside another holder*): Paul, 2026-11-18, saw the card around TD-1977 draw no Start session while grinder-ao-2 held the entry, and expects every card of open work to offer one. **Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session* (*Beside another holder*, *Started from an item*, the answered item's rule, *Started from the header on a card with items*, *An item on a kept card*) and §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session* and *Inbox row: ledger entry: Start session / Open session*:

- `queue_card.html` (its `ss.start and not ss.other` test), `queue_row.html` and `inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode (the comment near its answered item too): Start session is drawn on a Decide or Do item and in the header whatever other session holds the entry it starts on (the exclusions that stay: a red PR, a fork's PR, a stopped holder's state row, a stuck row); a held item counts for the header's rule as before.
- `/api/ledger/start` (`ui/app.py`): the three 409 *held by <name>* refusals on the card roads go (the kept road's, the open card's, and the holder check after the name). - Tests: rewrite the old 409 *held by* assertions (`tests/test_queue_start.py` near 245 and 338, `tests/test_queue_kept_start.py` near 237) and the old rule's description in `tests/test_queue_start_all.py`; add: a held item and a held header draw the button; the press names `with-td-<n>` (`with-td-<n>-2` where that is taken) and the tick derives no claim from it; a second press, before and after the session's record is listed, opens the first (by its lane); the row keeps the holder's word; the prompt carries the holder sentence with and without a PR and a branch; the Inbox road on a held entry opens the person's own session where one has the lane, else the holder; no road answers 409 *held by*.
