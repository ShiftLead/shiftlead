**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature

**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build

**Why:** TD-1984's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*, *Beside another holder*): Paul, 2026-11-18, saw the card around TD-1977 draw no Start session while grinder-ao-2 held the entry, and expects every card of open work to offer one. **Location:** design §4.5 screen 12 Three verbs: Start session (Beside another holder); §4.5a Queue card: Start session / Open session; src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (card_session, item_start, the answered count), src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (/api/ledger/start), src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py (WORK_HELD, work_text), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_card.html, queue_row.html, inbox_row.html; tests/test_queue_start.py, test_queue_kept_start.py, test_queue_start_all.py

**Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session* (*Beside another holder*, *Started from an item*, the answered item's rule, *Started from the header on a card with items*, *An item on a kept card*) and §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*:

- `/api/ledger/start` (`ui/app.py`): the three 409 *held by <name>* refusals on the card roads go (the kept road's, the open card's, and the holder check after the name); beside another holder the name is the first of the ten names Add entry's test (`entry_name_why`) finds free, else the filled New session form with its reason; the Inbox road (no `card`) keeps its Open session to the holder's Focus.
