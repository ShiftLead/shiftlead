**Why:** TD-1984's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*, *Beside another holder*): Paul, 2026-11-18, saw the card around TD-1977 draw no Start session while grinder-ao-2 held the entry, and expects every card of open work to offer one.
