## TD-1924: The stuck line for a design's answer names a grinder, and an idle designer behind an early restart reads only idle

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 8ae43dbfe6f6425ba3244ed830825dce
**Part of:** TD-1900

**Why:** §6 rule 13's stuck line for a decided board line read the order unjoined, so an order rule 6 routes to the design-first lanes matched the free-pick lanes and named a grinder (2026-11-16, TD-1829's One more round named grinder-ao-1); and a designer idle behind an early restart read only *idle*, not the press that cures it (TD-1900).

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 13 *A decided board line waiting on a session* and its reasons (TD-1900):

1. `agent_tick._stuck_orders` reads each repo's `board:<key>` orders from the repo reading's stored joined `work_orders` (the list `_join_orders` wrote, the one `ao repo` prints) rather than joining `reading["boards"]` again in `stuck.orders`; where that list is absent (a repo whose ledger reading failed this tick), the carried orders are read as `carry` keeps them. So `stuck.lane_records` and `ledger.lane_matches` see the order's `lane`/`for`, and a design's answer is the design-first lane's, or its one designer's, never the free-pick lanes'.
2. `stuck.judge`, for every kind it reads (an ask's addressee, an order's actor, a holder): a not-acting record for which `agent_common.restart_waits_of` gives a mark with `lapses` false reads `{state: "restart", word, since}`, `word` the mark's short word, `since` the record's idle start (`agent_common.idle_from`) as `idle`'s is; tested after `stalled` and before `idle`, and in place of whatever reason `unanswered.read` gives that record. The sentence, made at render: *`<name>` waits on your Restart: <word>*. The `stuck` shape check takes the new state.
3. Tests (`tests/test_stuck.py`, the tick's): a decided line whose order rule 6 routed to the design-first lane names the idle designer, not an idle grinder; one with a grinder holding no lease and the designer idle names the designer; a designer with an early restart reads *waits on your Restart: restart wanted · early*; one at its restart ceiling reads *restarts exhausted*; a lapsing fill mark reads as before; an ask whose addressee waits on a restart reads the same.

**Done when:** A stuck decided line routed to the designer's lane names the designer; any stuck work whose actor's restart waits on the person reads *<name> waits on your Restart: <word>*; tests above pass.
