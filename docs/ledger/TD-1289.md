## TD-1289: The Queue's filter row is the team chips, the Needs you (n) switch and the find box

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (the designer, from TD-1287)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1287
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_filters.html`; `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`FILTERS`, `KINDS`, `_card_kinds`, `queue_picks`, `picks_query`, `apply_picks`, `queue_view`'s card `kinds`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (the Queue page's `ctx`: `kinds`, `teams`, `team_repos`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_card.html` (the card's `data-` attributes); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (the Queue's in-browser apply around line 4266, its pick parse and memory, the row's press handler); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.qkinds`, the switch); `tests/test_ui_queue.py` (slice 4), `tests/test_ui_queue_picks.py`; `docs/mockups/` (`Queue.dc.html`'s source in `gen.py`); design §4.5 screen 12 *Filters*, §4.5a *Queue page: filters, find*

**Why:** TD-1287: the row held four kinds of pick — a status, the team chips, a repo pick and a kind pick — which combine differently but are all drawn as plain chips, so Paul could not tell what a press would do. The design keeps the teams, one other pick drawn as what it is, and the find box.

**Fix:** as §4.5 screen 12 *Filters* and §4.5a write it:
- **The row.** `queue_filters.html` draws, in order: a small *teams* label (the `qsub` style the kind pick used), **All** and the team chips; the chips are the Queue page's `teams` (a team whose repo the view reads, or a picked one, as now) plus, for each repo in `view["repos"]` that no team's repo names, a chip named for the repo, `data-k="team"` alike, whose `team_repos` entry maps the name to itself (so `apply_picks` and the browser read it as a team's chip with no new key); then a gap and the **Needs you (n)** switch — a `<a role="switch" aria-checked>` (or a button) carrying `data-k="filter" data-v="needs-you"` and its check mark ☐ / ☑ before the words, styled apart from `.qf` chips (no chip border; the mark and the words), `title` *only the cards that need you — it narrows the teams picked, it picks none; the completed lines are hidden while it is on*, its count `#qfneedsn` as now; then the find form, whose hidden inputs carry `filter` and `team` alone. **Steering**, the repo chips and the `qkinds` span are removed, and `.qkinds` with them.
- **The picks.** `FILTERS` is `{"needs-you": …}` alone; `queue_picks` drops `repo` and `kind` (an old `repo=`, `kind=` or `filter=steering` reads as not picked, never an error — today's *unknown is dropped* rule, widened); `picks_query` writes `filter` and `team` alone; `apply_picks` loses the repo and kind branches and the steering branch, so a card passes by the chips, the switch and the find; `status_pick` is the switch alone; a team name mapped to itself is a repo with no team. `KINDS`, `_card_kinds` and the card's `kinds` field go (TD-1284's `_card_kinds` clause with them), and the Queue page's `ctx` drops `kinds`. **All** clears the chips only (and the chips' memory); the switch and the find keep their own state.
- **The browser.** `app.js`'s Queue apply drops `picks.kind`, `picks.repo` and the steering test, its parse ignores the dropped keys from the URL and from the remembered picks (a memory holding `kind=` restores as if it held none), the press handler toggles the switch's `aria-checked` as it toggles a chip's `aria-pressed`, and **All** clears `team` alone.
- **The mockup.** `Queue.dc.html`'s filter row is redrawn from the live row (its source still has the row before TD-911), regenerated by `gen.py`.

**Done when:** the row draws *teams*, **All**, a chip per team and per repo no team names, the **Needs you (n)** switch apart with its mark, and the find box, and nothing else; a picked chip set and the switch combine as chips OR'd, ANDed with the switch and the find, the same on the server and in the browser; **All** clears the chips and leaves the switch; the completed and *other work* lines hide while the switch is on and answer the chips and find; `/queue?filter=steering`, `?repo=x` and `?kind=do` draw the page unpicked with no error, and a remembered `kind=` pick restores as none; `/queue?filter=needs-you` draws the switch on; a repo no team services gets its own chip and narrows to its cards. Tests cover each case.
