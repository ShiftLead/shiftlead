## TD-1345: A board Snooze now hides the line: its +1 week can hide it past its own Due:, and a hidden line is nowhere on the Inbox

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (grinder-ao-2, found building TD-1330)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Location:** `docs/design/4.5a-controls.md` (*Due strip / Inbox board row: Snooze ▾*); `docs/design/4.5-ui.md` (screen 6, the **Snoozed (n)** fold); `docs/design/4.10-messages.md` (*A snooze is a date, not a hiding place*); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`board_rows`, `person_sections`); the Snooze menu's date choices in `src/shiftlead/ui/`

**Why:** since TD-1330 (ledgerboard v2.3), a board line's **Snooze** writes `Hide until: <date>` and leaves `Due:` alone, and the Inbox draws only what ledgerboard's `needs_you` returns, so a hidden line is no row until its date. Two choices made when Snooze moved `Due:` now read differently:
- **The base date.** §4.5a's +1 day and +1 week count from the later of today and the item's own date, so a row coming up moves later. A hide counted from a future `Due:` hides the line through its deadline and past it: a line due in 3 days, snoozed +1 week, is out of the Inbox until it has been overdue for 7 days.
- **Where a hidden line is.** A snoozed mail entry or state row sits in the **Snoozed (n)** fold with its controls, and §4.10 says a snooze is *a date, not a hiding place*. A hidden board line is drawn nowhere until its date. TD-1330 followed its own Fix (*a hidden item is not shown*) and says so in §4.4 and §4.10.

**Fix:** a design round settles both. Does +1 day / +1 week count from today for a hide? Is a hidden board line drawn in the Snoozed fold with *hidden until <date>*, its Snooze ▾ offering *now* (which needs a way to clear `Hide until:`, a ledgerboard edit it does not have)? Builds follow.

**Done when:** the design states both, and the build matches it.

**Related:** TD-1330 (the v2.3 vendor); TD-371 (a snooze is a date); ledgerboard target design §6.
