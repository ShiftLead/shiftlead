**Fix:** Build §4.5 screen 12 *The card's page* and *Addresses*, and §4.5a *Card page: ‹ Queue, the page's line, its bar*:
- `/card/<card id>` (percent-encoded `#` and `:`): the one card, drawn by the Queue's card renderer with every control, its folds open, under its line. Its re-read is a one-card fragment (the Queue's own reading cut to the card, with mail's words, unlike `/api/queue/card`). It follows TD-1761's patch: values in place, *gone — refresh*, arrivals and the card's own move behind its bar. While a box is open on the page, the whole page holds.
- A completed card with no item for the person is drawn as its *Recently completed* line opened (each entry's `Resolved:` and PR); a card the view no longer holds stays as drawn (below).
- **‹ Queue**: history back when opened from the Queue, else `/queue#<card id>`; the Queue's side (drawing whole scrolled so the card is where it was) is TD-1763's.
- `?row=` and `item=` scroll and ring on the card's page. `/queue?card=…` redirects to `/card/<card>` with `row=` and `item=`. `/queue#TD-n` stays the Queue's.
- The one producer of a `/queue?card=` address, `queue_path` in `src/sessionorc/notify.py` (the Telegram link `queue_link` and the push `path`, `agent_notify.py`), writes `/card/…`; `showAddress` in `app.js`, which reads `?card=` today, gives way to the redirect; the tests that pin `/queue?card=` move with it.
- A link the card renderer draws to another card (`queue.py`'s holds and `on`/`after` hrefs, `queue_row.html`'s *on TD-n's card*) is `/card/<id>` on the card's page; one to a row of the same card stays an anchor.
- Tests: the page draws one card with its controls; the redirect keeps `row=`/`item=`; the states named below; the address producers write `/card/`.
- Opening: on load and on the redirect, draw the card with that id (the re-read's match); else, at load only, redirect to the card that draws the address's `item=` or `row=` (as `showAddress` finds them; the path's id is never looked up as a row); else open `/queue#<id>`; a failed read is *could not read the Queue — retrying*.
- A refused send (409) keeps the box and its words and draws the values that waited.
- A mail item's sentence opens `/inbox/<id>` with Back to the card's page; `j`/`k` there step this card's items. Today `inbox_entry_page` (`app.py`) keeps `back` only when it is `?…`, `/queue` or `/queue?…` and builds `/inbox{back}`, and `AO.openEntry` (`app.js`) collects `.queuepage` rows: extend the `back` whitelist to `/card/…` (used as is, not under `/inbox`), and give the card's page a hook so `openEntry` collects that one card's items. Test that Back and `j`/`k` stay on the card.

The Queue's side (writing that entry before it opens a card's page, and drawing whole anchored on the card on `pageshow`) is TD-1763's, which draws the Queue's links to the card's page.
- On the card's page the hold is an item's or row's (pointer, keyboard focus or its menu), not the card's; an open box holds the whole page and **Refresh** cannot be pressed while it is open.
- The card's bar has its own help entry (§4.5a's help list, `help.py`): **the bar** (Card page) — *Counts what changed on this card since it was drawn — new items, a move to another group — without moving anything; Refresh draws it.*
- A failed or unserved re-read never replaces its reading (TD-1761's rule).
- The card's page includes `board_add.html` (the `#boardadd` dialog **Put on the board** opens; today only the Inbox pages do), and that dialog counts as a box for the page hold.
- The card's come-back entry uses its own `sess` key, not `ENTRY_ORDER`.

Test `/card/loose%3Aother%23TD-009` and the hand-typed `/card/loose%3Aother%23TD-9` (both `same`), and that `/card/<a row's id>` with no `item=`/`row=` goes to `/queue#<id>` and to the opening fallbacks and that a merged card's page goes *gone*, not to the holding card. The page does not follow a card that changes id: where the view holds no card with its id (merged, re-topped, completed under a new id, a loose card's last ask answered), every item and row is *gone* at once (controls hidden, so nothing stale is pressed) but for an item the person's own press answered (it keeps its result); this overrides item and row holds but waits behind an open box's page hold (the box's item becomes *gone* when the box closes or a refusal draws, unless its own press answered it; a box still open over a *gone* item keeps its words, send disabled, *the card has left the Queue*); the bar reads *done* where the top entry is among the reading's completed ids, else *no longer on the Queue* (a loose page always the latter); **Refresh** opens `/queue#<top entry id>`, the Queue's find, or the plain `/queue` from a loose card's page.
