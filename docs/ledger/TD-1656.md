**Why:** PR #3419 (TD-1547) added `_extra_seat_hold` to `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, whose guard line is `if s.superseded_by or s.scale.get("drop") or self._owes_reading(s) or self._just_restarted(s, now): return`, then `if s.gated or s.suspended or s.restart_ceiling: return`, and a first line returning unless `s.pane` and `s.host == self.host`. Its docstring says a gated, suspended or ceilinged seat is left to the road that holds it, and the module docstring of `tests/test_td_max_seat_restart.py` says "One with work left, gated or at its ceiling is left as it stands". Only dirty git, `restart_ceiling` and an owed ask are exercised in `test_an_exited_extra_seat_with_nothing_waiting_is_closed_held_at_the_tick`.

Evidence (reverts in a scratch worktree on origin/main 0c3fc6629; each run `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src pdm run pytest -q tests/test_td_max_seat_restart.py` gave `3 passed`; restored after each):
- guard reduced to `if self._owes_reading(s) or self._just_restarted(s, now):` (no `superseded_by`, no `scale.drop`): 3 passed.
- guard reduced to `... or self._owes_reading(s):` (no `_just_restarted`): 3 passed.
- `if s.gated or s.restart_ceiling:` (no `suspended`): 3 passed. No test sets `gated` on the hold path at all (the only `gated` in the file goes through `_crashed`).
- first line without `s.host != self.host`: 3 passed; without `not s.pane`: 3 passed.
- all of `superseded_by`, `scale.drop`, `_just_restarted` removed, then `pytest -k "extra or seat" tests`: 402 passed. No other test names `_extra_seat_hold` or `extra_held` (`grep -rln tests/`).

So the tick could close a superseded extra, one a lowered max is dropping, a suspended or gated one, a remote host's, or one just restarted, and no test would notice.

**Fix:** Add cases to `tests/test_td_max_seat_restart.py`: for each of `superseded_by`, `scale["drop"]`, `suspended`, `gated`, `_just_restarted`, `pane=False` and a record of another host, `_extra_seat_hold` leaves the exited extra `exited`; check each by reverting its guard.

**Done when:** Each guard in `_extra_seat_hold` is removed in turn and `tests/test_td_max_seat_restart.py` fails each time.
