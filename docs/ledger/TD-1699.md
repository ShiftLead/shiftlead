**Why:** TD-1557's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Answer*, screen 6 *A board line's Reply*, §4.5a row **<head>…**). The page draws a form answer such as `Hold: <note>` as an ordinary button that sends its placeholder, and Answer… on a board line gives no sign which words decide. This build is the page's half. It rests on TD-1698's agent half: the `form` flag on answers, and `board_reply`'s `decided` answer.

**Fix:** Design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Answer*, screen 6 *A board line's Reply*, and §4.5a rows **<head>…**, **Works** / **Not right…**, *Inbox row: ledger entry* and *Queue card: answers, Answer…* (TD-1557).
- **Draw a form as `<head>…`** wherever an item's answers are drawn: the Inbox board row and Due strip (`inbox_row.html`'s board answers), the mail row (`answer_row`), a ledger entry's row, and the Queue card's answer row. The flag is the reader's `forms` list, parallel to `answers` (TD-1698), never a match in the template or the script. A form is never sent as written. A live look's pair stays **Works** / **Not right…** as drawn today: `Not right…` is the look case of the same control.
- **A board form's press is the decide**, generalising today's `board_notright` (`app.js`). It opens a box begun with `<head>: `: the row's composer on the Inbox, and the item's **Answer…** box on a Queue card. It sends `board_edit` `decide` with the words as typed and `form`, the form's index; the agent puts the head back (TD-1698), and the page matches nothing. An empty fill is refused. The box is bound to its road (TD-1555): a card's **Hold…** box sends the decide, and its **Answer…** box sends `board_reply`. The hand-off to the techlead (`/api/entry/hand`) stays the live-look pair's alone, so a **Hold…** press hands nothing on. A number key on a **<head>…** button opens its box. The row is drawn decided as any answer is. A ledger entry's form opens its **Reply** begun with its head. A mail form opens the Reply composer begun with its head and is sent as a typed reply, with no index.
- **The box's foot**: whenever the row's **Reply** or a card's **Answer…** opens on an open, undecided board line with a form, it reads *words starting <head>: decide this line; anything else is a reply*. Where there are two forms, the heads are joined by *or*. A decided line, or one with no form, draws no foot, and a form's own box carries none.
- **The decided answer of a Reply**: a board Reply or **Answer…** is sent as today, with no answers. `/api/person/board`'s `reply` branch already passes the `board_reply` RPC's answer through, so the script reads `decided` from it. Its `decide` branch passes `form` on (TD-1698). The Reply is drawn as a reply while it lands. On `decided`, the row is redrawn *decided: <answer> · <date>*, with the toast *decided — written on the board*. On a Queue card the item goes to *Waiting on them* and the card's counts are re-read. On a live-look row, the Reply and **Answer…** controls carry the repo and the item's head, as **Not right…** does. A `decided` there is the *Not right* fill, and the page makes **Not right…**'s second call, the hand-off to the repo's techlead. A refused fill puts the row back with the decide's words.
- **Correct the comments** in `answer_row` and the board answers that say every Answer… takes the decided line, or that only `Not right` is a form.
Tests: a board row offering `Go | Hold: <note>` draws **Go** and **Hold…**. **Hold…** opens the box begun `Hold: ` and sends a decide. A Reply `Hold: x` answered `decided` redraws the row decided and moves the card item to *Waiting on them*. The foot is drawn on Reply and Answer… on an open line with a form. It is absent on a decided line and on **Hold…**'s box. A **Hold…** press makes no hand-off. A mail ask's form and a ledger entry's form each draw **Hold…**. A look's pair is unchanged, and a look's Reply `Not right: x` makes the hand-off.
