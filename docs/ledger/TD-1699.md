**Why:** TD-1557's design (§4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Answer*, screen 6 *A board line's Reply*, §4.5a row **<head>…**). The page draws a form answer such as `Hold: <note>` as an ordinary button that sends its placeholder, and Answer… on a board line gives no sign which words decide. This build is the page's half. It rests on TD-1698's agent half: the `form` flag on answers, and `board_reply`'s `decided` answer.

**Fix:** Design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Answer*, screen 6 *A board line's Reply*, §4.5a rows **<head>…**, **Works** / **Not right…** and *Queue card: answers, Answer…* (TD-1557).
- **Draw a form as `<head>…`** wherever an item's answers are drawn: the Inbox board row and Due strip (`inbox_row.html`'s board answers), the mail row (`answer_row`), and the Queue card's answer row. The flag is the reader's `form` (TD-1698), never a match in the template or the script. A press opens the item's word box begun with `<head>: `: the board row's Reply composer, the card's **Answer…** box, or the mail Reply composer. A form is never sent as written. A `(default)` on a form draws no **Go with it**. A live look's pair stays **Works** / **Not right…** as drawn today: `Not right…` is the look case of the same control.
- **The box's foot on a board line with a form**: *words starting <head>: decide this line; anything else is a reply*, naming each form's head.
- **The decided answer of a Reply**: a board Reply or **Answer…** sends the item's `answers` with it, and is drawn as a reply while it lands. On the answer's `decided` the row or card item is redrawn *decided: <answer> · <date>*, with the toast *decided — written on the board*. On a look's filled `Not right`, the page makes **Not right…**'s second call, the hand-off to the repo's techlead, as that button does.
- **Correct the comments** in `answer_row` and the board answers that say every Answer… takes the decided line, or that only `Not right` is a form. Add a help entry for row **<head>…** where `help.py` binds §4.5a rows.
Tests: a board row with `Go | Hold: <note>` draws **Go** and **Hold…**, and Hold… opens the composer begun `Hold: `. A Reply `Hold: x` answered `decided` redraws the row decided. A mail ask's form draws **Hold…**. A look's pair is unchanged.

**Done when:** On a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`), an end-of-rounds-shaped board item draws **Go**, **One more round** and **Hold…** on its Inbox row and its Queue card. **Hold…** opens the box begun `Hold: `, and sending `Hold: drop Dismiss` from the card's **Answer…** redraws the item decided and writes `Decided: Hold: drop Dismiss` on the board.
