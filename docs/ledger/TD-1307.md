## TD-1307: A look's screenshots from the pushed commit — `shots_at` on `ao msg --shot`, the commit-or-push refusal, and the readers serving that commit while origin holds it

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt

**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1303
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/cli.py` (`_shot_path`, `--shot`: `HEAD`, the two checks, `shots_at`); `src/sessionorc/mail.py` (the envelope's `shots_at`, cleaned against `[0-9a-f]{40}`); `src/sessionorc/shots.py` (`_spec`, `read`, `exists` taking `at`: `<at>:docs/mockups/reviews/<name>` while `git for-each-ref --contains <at> refs/remotes/origin` names a ref, else `origin/<default>`); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`look_shots`, `_shot_on_origin`, `_shot_on_host`, the `_shot_seen` cache keyed on repo, name and `at`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`/repo/<name>/shot/<name>.png?at=`); `host_shot` and the link's `shot` method (`at`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` and the Queue card's look (the `?at=` on each image); `tests/`

**Why:** TD-1303's design: §4.10 *A look* (*A look's shots are the pushed commit's*), §4.7 `--shot`, §4.4a *A look's screenshots on another host*, §4.5a row *Inbox row: a look*.

**Fix:** as the design says:
- `ao msg --shot`: read `HEAD`; refuse *<name> is not committed at HEAD: commit it and push, then send* when `HEAD:docs/mockups/reviews/<name>` does not exist, and *HEAD is not pushed: push <branch>, then send* when no `refs/remotes/origin/*` contains `HEAD`; else send `shots` with `shots_at`.
- The envelope keeps `shots_at`, dropped when it is not 40 hex.
- Every reader (the page's route, `host_shot`, the link's `shot`) takes `at`: the commit's file while an `origin` ref of that checkout contains it, else `origin/<default>`'s, else absent; the name and size rules, the four-shot cap and *never the working tree* unchanged.
- The presence cache keys on `at` too.

**Done when** a look sent from an unmerged, pushed branch draws its images in the Inbox row and on the Queue card; a look with no `shots_at`, and one whose branch was deleted after a squash merge, draw as today; an uncommitted shot and an unpushed `HEAD` are each refused with their line; a malformed `at` is a 404; a node's look reads its commit over the link; and `pdm run test` passes.
