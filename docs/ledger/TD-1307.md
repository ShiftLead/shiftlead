**Why:** TD-1303's design: §4.10 *A look* (*A look's shots are the pushed commit's*), §4.7 `--shot`, §4.4a *A look's screenshots on another host*, §4.5a rows *Inbox row: a look* and *Send to reviewer*, §4.9b *Send to reviewer*.

**Fix:** as the design says:
- `ao msg --shot`: read `HEAD` and its branch in the shots' repo's checkout; refuse *<name> is not committed at HEAD: commit it and push, then send* when `HEAD:docs/mockups/reviews/<name>` does not exist, *<name> differs from HEAD: commit it and push, then send* when `git diff --quiet HEAD -- <path>` fails, and *HEAD is not pushed: push <branch>, then send* when no `refs/remotes/origin/*` contains `HEAD`; else send `shots` with `shots_at` and `shots_ref`.
- The envelope keeps `shots_at` (dropped unless 40 hex) and `shots_ref` (dropped unless it matches `[A-Za-z0-9._/-]{1,200}` with no `..` and no leading `-`); every hop re-matches both.
- Every reader (the page's route, `host_shot`, the link's `shot`) takes `at` and `ref` and draws the first that holds the file: `<at>:…` while an `origin` ref contains `at` (an object the checkout lacks reads as not contained), else `origin/<ref>:…`, else `origin/<default>:…`, else absent. A miss on the first two, in the row's presence test only (`look_shots`, from the stored entry), fetches `+refs/heads/<ref>:refs/remotes/origin/<ref>` once, `--no-prune`, bounded; the image route never fetches; the containment answer and the fetch are kept per `(repo, at)` for `SHOT_TTL`. The name and size rules, the four-shot cap and *never the working tree* unchanged.
- The presence cache keys on `at` too; after a fetch the three are read again and containment asked afresh. With no `shots_ref` the branch step and the fetch are skipped (and `--shot` on a detached `HEAD` says *check out a branch and push it*).
- A node fetches only on a `head` read. Every reading returns `from` (`commit|ref|default`), and the route sets `max-age=300` or `no-cache` from it, `?host=` too. A node's `shot` reply echoes the request's `at` verbatim; where the request carried `at`, a reply without it reads as a node that cannot be asked (the name alone), in the presence test and the route.
- The host agent's `msg` cleans `shots_at` and `shots_ref` and checks no containment.
- **Send to reviewer** hands `shots_at` and `shots_ref` with `shots`; the techlead brief's *A look handed to you* reads each image in the same order.
- The *gone* chip's title reads *not available from the sender's repo*.

**Done when** a look sent from an unmerged, pushed branch draws its images in the Inbox row, a node's too; a look whose branch was rebased after the send draws the branch's file; a look with no `shots_at`, and one whose branch was deleted and pruned after a squash merge, draw as today; a look pushed after the reading checkout last fetched draws after its one-branch fetch; an uncommitted shot, one that differs from `HEAD` and an unpushed `HEAD` are each refused with their line; the image route makes no fetch; an old node's reply without `at` draws the name alone; a malformed `at` is a 404; a node's look reads its commit over the link; and `pdm run test` passes.
