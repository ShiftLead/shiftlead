## TD-1948: The Focus terminal draws at the pane's grid and offers Fit to window

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e7d54c8dd4164fefbf11a193a3266980
**Blocked by:** TD-1947, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1919

**Why:** TD-1919's design (design §4.6, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does*, §4.5a **Fit to window**, **terminal mark**, **a pinch**): with the window pinned (TD-1947), the page must draw the pane at the pane's grid instead of fitting its box, and give the person the one press that sizes it.

**Ask:** The terminal size design used its four review rounds and two late fixes are unreviewed; should a session run one more round?

**Location:** design §4.6, §4.5 What the terminal's grid does, §4.5a Fit to window, terminal mark, a pinch; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Focus terminal), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html, tests/test_ui_term_mark.py

**Fix:** As design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present* and *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand*, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does* and *The panel put away*, and §4.5a **Fit to window**, **more ▾**, **the sheet**, **a pinch** and **terminal mark** say, in `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, the Focus template and its CSS:
1. **Draw at the pane's grid.** The terminal is sized by the bridge's `grid` frame (`term.resize`), never by `FitAddon.fit()` on its box: the `fit.fit()` calls in `AO.setTermLook` and `AO.termFont`'s path, the box's `ResizeObserver` and `phone.closed(() => fit.fit())` go, as do `AO.resizeSender`, `sendGrid` and the `term.onResize` wiring; a face or size change changes how many of the grid's columns the box shows, never the grid. The put-away, the phone's keyboard, turn, pinch and sheets send nothing. A box smaller than the grid as §4.6 says: `.termbox` the scroll container (`overflow-x: auto`, `overflow-y: hidden`) holding `.xterm` at the grid's pixel size (today's `height: 100%` on `.xterm` and the viewport's clip go); vertically the lowest box-height run of rows holding the cursor's row (xterm's `term.buffer.active.cursorY`, copy mode's cursor included), set on every cursor move and box change; sideways from the left, moved by a sideways wheel, Shift+wheel or a sideways finger (the TD-986 handler sets `scrollLeft`), the cursor's column kept in view while the terminal has focus, the helper text box focused with `preventScroll`; the wheel and the vertical drag still send `scroll`. A larger box leaves the room empty.
2. **Fit to window** in *more ▾* and the phone's sheet: computes the box's grid by `AO.boxGrid()` (below), at the send; not drawn when that grid is the pane's or on an ended record, drawn on a read-only Focus; on a record with an agent waits for it `idle` with nothing of its own running and no pane byte for two seconds, the terminal mark reading *fitting when idle…*, a second press, a closed socket or a reload cancelling, five minutes ending it with the note *not fitted — the session stayed busy*; on a record with no agent fits at once; measured at the send — held while the page is hidden or the box has no size, nothing sent when the proposal is not finite, one helper, `AO.boxGrid()`, measures for the send, the equal test, the item's drawing, the title and the pinch's note: `.termbox`'s `getBoundingClientRect()` (`.termbox` the `.xterm`'s parent, no wrapper) less its padding and borders, its sideways scrollbar not subtracted, over the fit addon's current cell size, floored; on a phone it divides the box's pixels last seen with the keyboard and sheets down (kept on load and on any box change with the keyboard down; the keyboard is up while the terminal or any editable element has focus) by the current cell size, so a pinch or font changes the grid with no new measurement; a press with no pixels kept puts the keyboard and sheet down (blurring any field) and keeps them once the box's height has held 100 ms (an animation frame after the relayout), and if the keyboard is still up 500 ms after the press sends nothing and notes *put the keyboard down, then press again*; until pixels are kept the item is drawn; a press whose grid equals the pane's sends nothing and closes the sheet noting *already this size*; the item stays drawn and reads **Cancel fit** while it waits, and a box whose grid becomes the pane's ends the wait quietly; the title names the pane's grid and the box's; then sends `{"fit": [cols, rows]}`; the TD-986 handler locks a drag to one axis by its first 10 px; on the wide layout's own Focus (not a phone, not a Pop out) once the bridge's `grid` frame answers the fit (never on a cancelled, timed-out or refused one; a refused write leaves the fit and notes it) also writes `person.terminal.grid` through `/api/settings/person` (TD-1947 extends it). Its `title` is §4.5a's.
3. **The terminal mark**: *resized by another client* and its hover go, *fitting when idle…* takes its place in the precedence with its hover; `tests/test_ui_term_mark.py` follows.
4. **The pinch's note** reads *13 px · 46 of 120 columns*: the first figure `AO.boxGrid()`'s columns at the new size (the box's), the second `term.cols` (the pane's).
5. **Look first** (design §4.9b): shots of a wide Focus whose box is smaller and larger than the grid, a phone Focus with the keyboard up on a 120-column pane, and the menu with Fit to window, in the PR.

**Done when:** A Focus draws the pane at the bridge's grid with no resize sent by any box change; a smaller box scrolls sideways from the left and shows the lowest run of rows holding the cursor's row, a scroll into history passing through it; a phone's fit divides the box's pixels last seen with the keyboard down by the current cell size (a pinch changing the grid with no new measurement; a press with none kept putting the keyboard down and keeping them after the box holds 100 ms, or sending nothing if the keyboard is still up at 500 ms), and a press equal to the pane's grid notes *already this size* and sends nothing; a box with its sideways scrollbar showing measures the same grid as without; Fit to window waits for a quiet pane on a record with an agent and fits at once on one without, reads Cancel fit while it waits, ends after five minutes with its note, is drawn on a read-only Focus, and sends one fit (writing the grid on the wide layout only, once the bridge answers); the *resized by another client* mark is gone and *fitting when idle…* is drawn; each under a test, with the shots in the PR.
