## TD-1948: The Focus terminal draws at the pane's grid and offers Fit to window

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e7d54c8dd4164fefbf11a193a3266980
**Blocked by:** TD-1947
**Part of:** TD-1919

**Why:** TD-1919's design (design §4.6, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does*, §4.5a **Fit to window**, **terminal mark**, **a pinch**): with the window pinned (TD-1947), the page must draw the pane at the pane's grid instead of fitting its box, and give the person the one press that sizes it.

**Location:** design §4.6, §4.5 What the terminal's grid does, §4.5a Fit to window, terminal mark, a pinch; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Focus terminal), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html, tests/test_ui_term_mark.py

**Fix:** As design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present* and *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand*, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does* and *The panel put away*, and §4.5a **Fit to window**, **more ▾**, **the sheet**, **a pinch** and **terminal mark** say, in `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, the Focus template and its CSS:
1. **Draw at the pane's grid.** The terminal is sized by the bridge's `grid` frame (`term.resize`), never by `FitAddon.fit()` on its box; the box's `ResizeObserver`, `AO.setTermLook`'s refit, the put-away, the phone's keyboard, turn, pinch and sheets send nothing. `AO.resizeSender` and the `{resize}` send go. A box smaller than the grid shows the bottom rows and the left columns with a sideways scrollbar (sideways wheel, Shift+wheel, a sideways finger in the TD-986 touch handler); the wheel and the vertical drag still send `scroll`. A larger box leaves the room empty.
2. **Fit to window** in *more ▾* and the phone's sheet: computes the box's grid as the fit addon's `proposeDimensions()` does; not drawn when that grid is the pane's, on an ended record or a read-only Focus; waits for the record not `working` and no pane byte for two seconds, the terminal mark reading *fitting when idle…*, a second press cancelling; then sends `{"fit": [cols, rows]}`; on the wide layout also writes `person.terminal.grid` through `set_settings`. Its `title` is §4.5a's.
3. **The terminal mark**: *resized by another client* and its hover go, *fitting when idle…* takes its place in the precedence with its hover; `tests/test_ui_term_mark.py` follows.
4. **The pinch's note** reads *13 px · 46 of 120 columns*.
5. **Look first** (design §4.9b): shots of a wide Focus whose box is smaller and larger than the grid, a phone Focus with the keyboard up on a 120-column pane, and the menu with Fit to window, in the PR.

**Done when:** A Focus draws the pane at the bridge's grid with no resize sent by any box change, a smaller box scrolls sideways and keeps the bottom rows, Fit to window waits for a quiet pane and sends one fit (writing the grid on the wide layout only), the *resized by another client* mark is gone and *fitting when idle…* is drawn, each under a test, with the shots in the PR.
