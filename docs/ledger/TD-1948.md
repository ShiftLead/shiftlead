## TD-1948: The Focus terminal draws at the pane's grid and offers Fit to window

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e7d54c8dd4164fefbf11a193a3266980
**Blocked by:** TD-1947
**Part of:** TD-1919

**Why:** TD-1919's design (design §4.6, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does*, §4.5a **Fit to window**, **terminal mark**, **a pinch**): with the window pinned (TD-1947), the page must draw the pane at the pane's grid instead of fitting its box, and give the person the one press that sizes it.

**Location:** design §4.6, §4.5 What the terminal's grid does, §4.5a Fit to window, terminal mark, a pinch; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Focus terminal), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html, tests/test_ui_term_mark.py

**Fix:** As design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present* and *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand*, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does* and *The panel put away*, and §4.5a **Fit to window**, **more ▾**, **the sheet**, **a pinch** and **terminal mark** say, in `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, the Focus template and its CSS:
1. **Draw at the pane's grid.** The terminal is sized by the bridge's `grid` frame (`term.resize`), never by `FitAddon.fit()` on its box: the `fit.fit()` calls in `AO.setTermLook` and `AO.termFont`'s path, the box's `ResizeObserver` and `phone.closed(() => fit.fit())` go, as do `AO.resizeSender`, `sendGrid` and the `term.onResize` wiring; a face or size change changes how many of the grid's columns the box shows, never the grid. The put-away, the phone's keyboard, turn, pinch and sheets send nothing. A box smaller than the grid as §4.6 says: `.termbox` the scroll container (`overflow-x: auto`, `overflow-y: hidden`) holding `.xterm` at the grid's pixel size (today's `height: 100%` on `.xterm` and the viewport's clip go); vertically the lowest box-height run of rows holding the cursor's row (xterm's `term.buffer.active.cursorY`, copy mode's cursor included), set on every cursor move and box change; sideways from the left, moved by a sideways wheel, Shift+wheel or a sideways finger (the TD-986 handler sets `scrollLeft`), the cursor's column kept in view while the terminal has focus, the helper text box focused with `preventScroll`; the wheel and the vertical drag still send `scroll`. A larger box leaves the room empty.
2. **Fit to window** in *more ▾* and the phone's sheet: computes the box's grid as the fit addon's `proposeDimensions()` does, at the send; not drawn when that grid is the pane's or on an ended record, drawn on a read-only Focus; on a record with an agent waits for it `idle` with nothing of its own running and no pane byte for two seconds, the terminal mark reading *fitting when idle…*, a second press, a closed socket or a reload cancelling, five minutes ending it with the note *not fitted — the session stayed busy*; on a record with no agent fits at once; measured at the send — held while the page is hidden or the box has no size, nothing sent when the proposal is not finite, on a phone the press puts the sheet and the keyboard down and the grid is measured then from the terminal's box by `proposeDimensions()` once the page settles (the next `visualViewport` resize, else 300 ms), that grid the one sent, measured again on a turn only while the keyboard is down; the item stays drawn and reads **Cancel fit** while it waits, and a box whose grid becomes the pane's ends the wait quietly; the title names the pane's grid and the box's; then sends `{"fit": [cols, rows]}`; the TD-986 handler locks a drag to one axis by its first 10 px; on the wide layout's own Focus (not a phone, not a Pop out) once the bridge's `grid` frame answers the fit (never on a cancelled, timed-out or refused one; a refused write leaves the fit and notes it) also writes `person.terminal.grid` through `/api/settings/person` (TD-1947 extends it). Its `title` is §4.5a's.
3. **The terminal mark**: *resized by another client* and its hover go, *fitting when idle…* takes its place in the precedence with its hover; `tests/test_ui_term_mark.py` follows.
4. **The pinch's note** reads *13 px · 46 of 120 columns*: the first figure the fit addon's `proposeDimensions()` at the new size (the box's columns), the second `term.cols` (the pane's).
5. **Look first** (design §4.9b): shots of a wide Focus whose box is smaller and larger than the grid, a phone Focus with the keyboard up on a 120-column pane, and the menu with Fit to window, in the PR.

**Done when:** A Focus draws the pane at the bridge's grid with no resize sent by any box change; a smaller box scrolls sideways from the left and shows the lowest run of rows holding the cursor's row, a scroll into history passing through it; a phone's fit measures its grid with the sheet and keyboard down; Fit to window waits for a quiet pane on a record with an agent and fits at once on one without, reads Cancel fit while it waits, ends after five minutes with its note, is drawn on a read-only Focus, and sends one fit (writing the grid on the wide layout only, once the bridge answers); the *resized by another client* mark is gone and *fitting when idle…* is drawn; each under a test, with the shots in the PR.
