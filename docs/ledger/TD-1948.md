**Why:** TD-1919's design (design §4.6, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does*, §4.5a **Fit to window**, **terminal mark**, **a pinch**): with the window pinned (TD-1947), the page must draw the pane at the pane's grid instead of fitting its box, and give the person the one press that sizes it.

**Ask:** The terminal size design ran a fifth review round, and five late fixes are unreviewed; should a session run one more round?

**Location:** design §4.6, §4.5 What the terminal's grid does, §4.5a Fit to window, terminal mark, a pinch; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the Focus terminal), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html, tests/test_ui_term_mark.py

**Fix:** As design §4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present* and *Fit to window: the person resizes on demand*, §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does* and *The panel put away*, and §4.5a **Fit to window**, **more ▾**, **the sheet**, **a pinch** and **terminal mark** say, in `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, the Focus template and its CSS:
1. **Draw at the pane's grid.** The terminal is sized by the bridge's `grid` frame (`term.resize`), never by `FitAddon.fit()` on its box: the `fit.fit()` calls in `AO.setTermLook` and `AO.termFont`'s path, the box's `ResizeObserver` and `phone.closed(() => fit.fit())` go, as do `AO.resizeSender`, `sendGrid`, the `term.onResize` wiring and the `cols` and `rows` on the terminal socket's address; the terminal opens at the first `grid` frame's size; a face or size change changes how many of the grid's columns the box shows, never the grid. The put-away, the phone's keyboard, turn, pinch and sheets send nothing. A box smaller than the grid as §4.6 says: `.termbox` the scroll container (`overflow-x: auto`, `overflow-y: hidden`) holding `.xterm` at the grid's pixel size (today's `height: 100%` on `.xterm` and the viewport's clip go); vertically the lowest box-height run of rows holding the cursor's row (xterm's `term.buffer.active.cursorY`, copy mode's cursor included), set on every cursor move and box change; sideways from the left, moved by a sideways wheel, Shift+wheel or a sideways finger (the TD-986 handler sets `scrollLeft`), the cursor's column kept in view while the terminal has focus, the helper text box focused with `preventScroll`; the wheel and the vertical drag still send `scroll`. A larger box leaves the room empty.
2. **Fit to window** in *more ▾* and the phone's sheet: computes the box's grid by `AO.boxGrid()` (below), at the send; not drawn when that grid is the pane's or on an ended record, drawn on a read-only Focus; on a record with an agent waits for it `idle` with nothing of its own running and no pane byte for two seconds, the terminal mark reading *fitting when idle…*, a second press, a closed socket or a reload cancelling, five minutes ending it with the note *not fitted — the session stayed busy*; on a record with no agent fits at once; measured at the send — held while the page is hidden or the box has no size, nothing sent when the proposal is not finite, one helper, `AO.boxGrid()`, measures for the send, the equal test, the item's drawing, the title and the pinch's note: `.termbox`'s `getBoundingClientRect()` (`.termbox` the `.xterm`'s parent, no wrapper) less its padding and borders, its sideways scrollbar not subtracted, over the terminal's current cell size (the renderer's CSS cell, `term._core._renderService.dimensions.css.cell`, private to xterm, which the fit addon's `proposeDimensions()` reads too, less the overview ruler the design drops), floored; on a phone it divides the box's pixels kept with the keyboard and every sheet down (the keyboard is up while the terminal or any editable element has focus) by the current cell size, so a pinch or font changes the grid with no new measurement; pixels are kept on load and on any box change with the keyboard and every sheet down, never a box with no size or before the phone layout applies, and dropped on a turn of the phone (`matchMedia("(orientation: portrait)")` changing) or a crossing of the narrow query (`html.phonefocus` flipping), which also cancels a waiting fit noting *not fitted — the screen changed, press again*; every press measures afresh: it blurs any focused field and closes the sheet, keeps the box's pixels at the first animation frame after the relayout at which the height has not changed for 100 ms with no editable element focused (counted from the press when nothing was focused, else from the first `visualViewport` `resize` after the blur; with no such resize within the limit and nothing focused, the box as it is), then goes on to the equal test, the quiet wait and the send; from the press until its hold ends the layout holds as it does while a field is focused (`AO.narrowNext` reads a running hold as editing, and the narrow query is read again once it ends), so the layout at the press is the fit's; a turn during the hold ends the press with the turn's note, a press while a hold runs is ignored, a page hidden during it ends the press with nothing kept and no note, and a `visualViewport` `resize` whose `scale` differs from the last one's is a zoom, never the keyboard's fall; the limit is one second, counted from the press and bounding only the wait for that first `resize`, and once it comes the hold has a limit of its own, one second from that `resize`; and this build measures the keyboard's fall on an iPhone and an Android phone, records both figures in docs/history/TD-1948.md and raises the limit if either is longer; a wide layout on a coarse pointer (`(pointer: coarse)`) presses the same way, blur and hold, the pixels its hold keeps being this fit's alone (the kept pixels stay the phone's) and, while it waits, kept again on every box change while nothing editable is focused and no sheet is open: the equal test, the send and the setting's write divide them by the current cell size however the keyboard stands by the send, and a turn or a crossing of the narrow query while it waits cancels it with the same note; with no such frame within the hold's limit it uses the pixels kept before, or with none (always so on a coarse-pointer wide page, which keeps none before its hold) keeps nothing, sends nothing and notes *put the keyboard down, then press again* over the terminal where the pinch's note shows; on a phone the item is drawn on every live record and an equal press notes *already this size*; until pixels are kept the helper reads the live box, so the title and the pinch's note show its grid; a grid outside the bridge's bounds (columns 20–500, rows 5–200), checked at the press and again at the send, sends nothing, ends any wait and notes *not fitted — this box is too small* (or *too large*) where *already this size* shows; a press whose grid equals the pane's sends nothing and closes the sheet noting *already this size*; the item stays drawn and reads **Cancel fit** while it waits, and a box whose grid becomes the pane's ends the wait quietly; the title names the pane's grid and the box's; then sends `{"fit": [cols, rows]}`; the TD-986 handler locks a drag to one axis by its first 10 px; on the wide layout's own Focus (not a phone, not a Pop out) once the bridge's `grid` frame answers the fit (the first on that socket equal to the grid sent, within ten seconds; with none nothing is written and a note says *not fitted*) (never on a cancelled, timed-out or refused one; a refused write leaves the fit and notes it) also writes `person.terminal.grid` through `/api/settings/person` (TD-1947 extends it). Its `title` is §4.5a's.
3. **The terminal mark**: *resized by another client* and its hover go, *fitting when idle…* takes its place in the precedence with its hover; `tests/test_ui_term_mark.py` follows.
4. **The pinch's note** reads *13 px · 46 of 120 columns*: the first figure `AO.boxGrid()`'s columns at the new size (the box's), the second `term.cols` (the pane's).
5. **Look first** (design §4.9b): shots of a wide Focus whose box is smaller and larger than the grid, a phone Focus with the keyboard up on a 120-column pane, and the menu with Fit to window, in the PR.
