## TD-1284: A Queue card's entry the person's own session holds reads *with you in <name>* and is no want

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (the designer, from TD-1281)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1281
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_state`, `row`, `_status`); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`want_of`, `attach`, `card_wants`, `_card_kinds`, `branch`, `_seg`, `queue_view`'s `items_start` and `n`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`card_mail`, which builds `attach`'s callables); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_card.html` (the header's items, and the top entry's state at its line 46), `queue_row.html` (`state`, the `needsyou` class, the holder pill), `inbox_row.html` (the `ledger` macro's `qneed` span and card-mode foot); `tests/test_queue.py`, `tests/test_queue_wants.py`, `tests/test_queue_start.py`; design §4.4 *A row's state*, *The card's status*; §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants*, *Three verbs: Start session*; §4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*, *Queue card: Start session / Open session*

**Why:** TD-1281: a card whose `decision (paul)` or `Owner: paul` entry the person is already working in their own interactive session read *needs you*, amber, with the *Decide 1* pill, and counted in **Needs you (n)** and the header's *Decide · Do*, so a card the person is working competed with the ones that truly wait on them.

**Fix:** as §4.4 and §4.5 screen 12 write it:
- **The reading.** `_state` returns a third value, `with` (the name, else `None`), beside its word and `needs`, and the word *with you in <name>*, for an `Owner: paul` or `decision (paul)` entry with a claim (its `claims`, from `held`) whose record has `unattended: false`, `<name>` the first such claim's record's name — not the first claim's, since an unattended worker may also hold it — after the *held (person)* test and before the owner and decision tests, so a held entry keeps *held (person)*, an idle interactive holder never reads *stalled*, and a red PR on the entry is the row's `pr`, not its word (on purpose, §4.4). `row` sets `holder` to that record (not `claim[0][0]`) on such a row, so the header's and the row's Focus link reach the person's session. The row **keeps its `needs`** (`owner` or `decision`) and gains `with`, that name; `card_entries`'s docstring says a `needs` row with `with` is no want though under *waits on me*: the id lists read `needs` and stay as they are — `card_entries` still puts the entry under *waits on me*, so `item_start` and `/api/ledger/start` (`app.py`, which checks `card_entries(card)[0]`) accept a press on it unchanged. Every need test reads a row with `with` as no need: `_status`'s `any(r["needs"] for r in rows)` becomes `any(r["needs"] and not r.get("with") for r in rows)`, so the card's status falls to the next word (*ready* where nothing else waits; the card is In progress by the claim, and draws *d/m done*). Only the claim counts, not the `td-<n>` name (accepted, §4.4). An entry claimed only by unattended records reads as now.
- **The item.** The card's ledger item for that entry (the Inbox's row from `shiftlead.ui.inbox.ledger_for_you`, attached to the card) takes a want `with`: `attach` gains a callable beside `start`, `with_of(card id, ref) -> name`, which `card_mail` builds from the cards it already holds (`by_id[c]`'s `top` and `rows`), and `put` gives `with` where it names one (`ledger_for_you`'s own holder field is not the test); and computes the item's `go` (`start(card, ref)`) for `with` as for `WANTS`, so the item draws **Open session**. `card_wants` gains a `with` list, counted in none of `n` (`n.decide` and `n.do` stay Decide and Do alone, so the pill and `queue_view`'s header `decide` / `do` sums leave it out). `queue_view`'s `items_start` counts a `with` item, so the header draws no second Open session. `_item_kind` gives `with` no kind (`''`). The card draws it in the header after Decide and Do, grey (no amber class); `inbox_row.html`'s `qneed` span reads *with you in <name>* where the item's `want` is `with`, its answers, Open session and ⋯ unchanged.
- **The marks.** `branch` and so `_seg` give a row with `with` `open` (grey), not `needs`, and `_seg`'s title its state word, not *yours to do* / *your decision*; `_card_kinds` adds no `do` or `decide` for a row with `with`, so the kind pick does not list the card for it; `queue_row.html`'s `state` macro draws the row's state word *with you in <name>*, not `needs_word`, where `with` is set, the row's `needsyou` class is `r.needs and not r.with`, and the row's holder pill is not drawn beside the word, as for *held by*; `queue_card.html`'s header draws the top entry's `state(t)` where `t.with` is set (today it draws none while `t.needs`), and no holder pill beside it. `_mail_status` and `card_edge` read only Decide and Do and the status, so neither changes; a test holds both.
- **What stays.** An `ask`, `steer`, look or board line about the entry stays Decide or Do and counted; the Inbox's *Later* row and the Repo page's board are unchanged; the top bar is unchanged (a ledger need enters no count).

**Done when:** a card whose only open member is a `decision (paul)` entry claimed by an `unattended: false` record reads In progress with status *ready*, its edge not amber, out of the **Needs you (n)** filter, the header's `needs_you` count and the kind pick's Decide, its item drawn *with you in <name>* with Open session (and none in the header) and counted in neither the pill nor the page header's `decide` / `do`, its row (or, the entry being the top, its header) *with you in <name>*, grey, with no second holder pill; a press of that item's Open session reaches the start road and goes to the session, never refused; the same with `Owner: paul`; the same entry claimed by an unattended record keeps *needs you*; an entry claimed by an unattended record first and an interactive one reads *with you in <the interactive one>* and its holder is the interactive one; the Inbox's *Later* row for the entry (no card holds it) is unchanged; a held entry the person's session claims reads *held (person)*; an open `ask` about the claimed entry keeps the card *needs you* and on the pill; an idle interactive holder does not make the card *stalled*; with the claim gone (the record exited) the entry is Decide or Do again. Tests cover each case.
