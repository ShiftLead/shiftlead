## TD-1284: A Queue card's entry the person's own session holds reads *with you in <name>* and is no want

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (the designer, from TD-1281)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1281
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_state`, `_status`, `card_entries`); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`want_of`, `attach`, `card_wants`, `_mail_status`, `card_edge`, `branch`, `queue_view`'s `n`); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_card.html`, `queue_row.html`; `tests/test_queue.py`, `tests/test_queue_wants.py`; design §4.4 *A row's state*, *The card's status*; §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants*, *Three verbs: Start session*; §4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*

**Why:** TD-1281: a card whose `decision (paul)` or `Owner: paul` entry the person is already working in their own interactive session read *needs you*, amber, with the *Decide 1* pill, and counted in **Needs you (n)** and the header's *Decide · Do*, so a card the person is working competed with the ones that truly wait on them.

**Fix:** as §4.4 and §4.5 screen 12 write it:
- **The reading.** `_state` returns *with you in <name>* (needs `None`) for an `Owner: paul` or `decision (paul)` entry with a claim whose record has `unattended: false`, `<name>` that record's name (the first such claim's) — after the *held (person)* test and before the owner and decision tests, so a held entry keeps *held (person)* and an idle interactive holder never reads *stalled*. Only the claim counts (`held`, from `rec.progress`), not the `td-<n>` name: until the tick derives the claim the row keeps its need (accepted, §4.4). An entry claimed only by an unattended record keeps its need, as now. `_status` then gives no *needs you* for it by its existing `r["needs"]` test, and the card is In progress by the claim. `card_entries` puts the entry under *the rest*, since its `needs` is gone. `_own` (the Waiting predicate) is unchanged.
- **The item.** The card's ledger item for that entry (the Inbox's row from `shiftlead.ui.inbox.ledger_for_you`, attached to the card) takes a fourth want, `with`, beside `held`: `want_of` (or `attach`, which has the card's row) gives `with` where the card's reading row for the item's entry reads *with you in <name>*; `card_wants` gathers it as `with`, counted in none of `n`. The card draws it in the header after Decide and Do, grey (no amber class), its line led by *with you in <name>*, with its answers, its item **Start session** / **Open session** (`item_start`, which already gives `own`) and its ⋯ unchanged. `_mail_status` and `card_edge` read only Decide and Do and the ledger's status, so neither changes once the item is `with` and the status is no *needs you*; check both. `branch` gives the row grey (`open`), not amber, since its `needs` is gone; the parts meter's segment follows `branch`.
- **What stays.** An `ask`, `steer`, look or board line about the entry stays Decide or Do and counted; the Inbox's *Later* row and the Repo page's board are unchanged; the top bar is unchanged (a ledger need enters no count).

**Done when:** a card whose only open member is a `decision (paul)` entry claimed by an `unattended: false` record reads In progress, its status no *needs you*, its edge not amber, out of the **Needs you (n)** filter and the header's `needs_you` count, its item drawn *with you in <name>* with Open session and counted in neither *Decide* nor *Do*, and its row *with you in <name>*, grey; the same with `Owner: paul`; the same entry claimed by an unattended record keeps *needs you*; a held entry the person's session claims reads *held (person)*; an open `ask` about the claimed entry keeps the card *needs you* and on the pill; an idle interactive holder does not make the card *stalled*; with the claim gone (the record exited) the entry is Decide or Do again. Tests cover each case.
