**Why:** The usage gate (design §6 *Usage gate*) is only as good as its reading of the account. On kmaster that reading has two sources, and both have been failing.

First, the endpoint poll mostly fails for `claude-code:paul`. The host agent logs one line each time the poll's reason changes (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, `_usage_reading`). Between 2026-10-25 and 2026-11-10 it logged:
- 34 changes from `ok` to `rate_limited`, and 9 from `ok` to `error`;
- 19 changes between `error` and `rate_limited`;
- only 7 back to `ok`, every one of them from `rate_limited`.

From 2026-11-07 16:28 until the restart on 2026-11-10 17:57, the poll never answered `ok`: it went from `rate_limited` to `error` at 17:40 and moved only between those two after that. Each 429 holds the poll off for `USAGE_COOL` (an hour, or the endpoint's `Retry-After` when longer).

Second, some sessions' own usage reports are refused. A Claude Code session reports its usage over `usage_report`, and since `identity_mode: enforce` the home refuses a report from a caller it cannot place (`src/sessionorc/agent_identity.py`, `_identify`). The journal shows `identity alarm (enforce): unknown claimed '<session>' on usage_report` 88 times since 2026-11-01:
- Nov 2: 2; Nov 3: 5; Nov 4: 4; Nov 5: 12;
- Nov 6: 47; Nov 7: 16; Nov 10: 2.

The claimed names are real sessions, and their reports were thrown away. Most are this repo's members and seats:
- `designer-ao-1` 22, `grinder-ao-2` 21, `techlead-ao-1` 13, `grinder-ao-1` 6;
- `card-session` 5, `docs-audit-ao-1` 2, `test-audit-ao-1` 1.

The rest are the person's `ao-led-tds-ledger` (7) and other repos' sessions on the same home: agent-cadence's `ac-grinder` (7) and `ac-techlead` (1), and ledgerboard's `lb-designer` (2) and `lb-techlead` (1). So the fault is the home's, not one team's.

With the poll failing and some reports refused, the gate runs on old readings or projections (§6 *A reading the gate can no longer trust*). A pause can then go wrong in both directions. On 2026-11-07 the team paused at 17:13 on a *projected* 99.1% and resumed ten minutes later (TD-1581's timeline). A paused session reports nothing, so ending a pause relies on the endpoint (§6), and the endpoint was the source that kept failing. Whatever reserve the person sets (TD-1581), the gate keeps it only while it has a true number.

**Fix:** Find out why each source fails, then fix what can be fixed.

1. **The poll.** Read the adapter's usage call (`src/shiftlead/adapters/claude_code/__init__.py`, its `usage_for`) and what an `error` answer is. Log the status code or the exception once per change of reason, beside the reason, so the next look has it.
   - Count how often the home asks. It should be at most once per `USAGE_FRESH` per account, and not at all while a reading is young (`_usage_young`).
   - Check whether anything else on the machine polls the same endpoint on the same login, such as a node, a conformance home, or the statusline.
   - If the endpoint simply allows little, say so in §4.4 *Usage*. Then the session reports are the gate's main source, and the projection rules should be written with that in mind.
2. **The refused reports.** Find why a member's `usage_report` comes from a caller the home classifies as `unknown`:
   - a statusline process outside the pane's cgroup;
   - a report sent after the pane changed (`_id_gone` grace);
   - a resumed session whose pane is not yet listed (`_id_note_panes`).

   Fix the classification, or let a `usage_report` whose claim matches a live record be served as `hook` is after its pane ends (`_id_gone_channel`). Never refuse it silently.
3. Live check: with a team working for a day, the journal shows no `unknown … on usage_report`, and the poll answers `ok` at least once whenever no session has reported for `max_age`.

**Done when:** Done when a working team's usage reports are no longer refused as `unknown`, and the gate has a reading younger than `max_age` while unattended sessions work. An `error` from the poll logs its cause, and the endpoint's actual allowance is recorded in §4.4 *Usage*.
