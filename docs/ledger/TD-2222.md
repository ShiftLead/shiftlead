## TD-2222: A design the person has approved keeps a review record that says the decision is still pending

**Priority:** Medium

**Kind:** design-first

**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** Audit of #4668 (the person's *Go* on TD-2172's design) and of the builds of TD-2077 merged after its *Go* (#4659). Seventeen review records still read `**Decided:** pending` and `**Go by:** pending` on origin/main (at d9a508952) although each design entry's last gate line in its Log is `gate lifted: decision (paul) — by paul; answer "Go"`: TD-1153, TD-1954, TD-1962, TD-1968, TD-1976, TD-1984, TD-2021, TD-2077, TD-2080, TD-2103, TD-2104, TD-2114, TD-2126, TD-2127, TD-2129, TD-2164 and TD-2172 (`docs/reviews/TD-<n>.md`).

The rule they break is the cadence's §4.8: after the item "`Go` lifts the block (the decide lift, §2.4), the unreviewed fixes go into the build entries' Fix, and the record gets `Go by: <person>` and `Decided:`" (procedures/cadence.md step 3: "the session acting on the person's answer adds one line to the record"). No local text asks for that line: design §4.9a *A design's answer* says "*Go* drops the blocks and moves the unread fixes into the builds' Fix in one PR", and `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` *The answered item is yours* lists dropping the blocks, moving the fixes and closing the board line, as does `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md` (*A design's answer*).

Why no session writes it: a press on a gated item is the decide lift, which lifts the gate and closes the item in the press's own pull request (design §4.4 *A decide on a gated item is the decide lift*), while a session's work orders are the open decided lines (§6, "every open decided line"). So no session is sent after such a press, the briefs' text on *Go* does not run, and the rule's other step, moving the unread fixes into the builds' Fix, has nobody either. #4668 lifted the four gates and closed the line, and touched ledger and board files alone. Writing the record inside that pull request would send it to ordinary review: ledgerboard's `lift_pr_verdict` answers `lift-files` for a path outside the board and the ledger.

Forty-five more records have no `Go by:` line at all, though the entry's newest decision gate line is an answer's lift (all *Go* but TD-1581's, which answered a question the design put to the person and is owed nothing); thirty-four of them read `**Decided:** pending…`, ten hold prose, a lone `—` or nothing in `Decided:`, and one (TD-1829) has its date: TD-704, TD-1392, TD-1411, TD-1423, TD-1434, TD-1435, TD-1437, TD-1453, TD-1470, TD-1476, TD-1482, TD-1485, TD-1491, TD-1529, TD-1543, TD-1550, TD-1551, TD-1556, TD-1558, TD-1564, TD-1567, TD-1568, TD-1569, TD-1573, TD-1576, TD-1577, TD-1581, TD-1590, TD-1711, TD-1718, TD-1720, TD-1721, TD-1722, TD-1801, TD-1804, TD-1829, TD-1832, TD-1845, TD-1854, TD-1856, TD-1895, TD-1919, TD-1921, TD-1953, TD-1959. Sixty-one records in all, TD-1581 apart, are owed a `Go by:`, a `Decided:` date or both (counted on origin/main of 2026-11-25 from each entry's newest decision gate line).

What it costs: `agent-cadence run review_stats.py --verdict TD-2172` prints `next: item`, as if the question were still to be asked (TD-2129's prints the same). The cadence check's `reach` row holds a far-reaching build "unless the record's `Go by:` names a person"; it is idle here only because `docs/cadence.toml` has no `[far-reaching]` table, which TD-1961 would add, held on a decision of the person's.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that writes your Go onto a design's review record once your answer lifts its gate?

**Context:** The rounds ended at round 4 by the stop rule with a counted Go, which the judge confirmed; it reads the design as far-reaching because its builds change the designer's and grinder's briefs, so the Go is yours.

**Design PR:** #4683

**Location:** docs/reviews/, design §4.9a, docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md

**Fix:** Decide who writes the record, and moves the unread fixes, when the person's press lifts a design's gate: a session sent for the answer, ShiftLead's write-back (a second pull request, or a change to the lift's carve-out in ledgerboard), or the cadence's decide lift, which would be filed in agent-cadence. Say it in design §4.9a, `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` and `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md`, and fill in `Go by:` and `Decided:` on all the records listed in the Why from each entry's `gate lifted` Log line and its closed board item.

**Done when:** No design entry whose gate the person lifted with *Go* has a review record reading `Go by: pending` or `Decided: pending`; design §4.9a and both designer briefs name who writes those lines and moves the unread fixes at the next *Go*.
