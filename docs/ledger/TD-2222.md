## TD-2222: A design the person has approved keeps a review record that says the decision is still pending

**Priority:** Medium
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 102afa74affa440ea5cd23f3bfb7742c

**Why:** Audit of #4668 (the person's *Go* on TD-2172's design) and of the builds of TD-2077 merged after its *Go* (#4659). Seventeen review records still read `**Decided:** pending` and `**Go by:** pending` on origin/main (at d9a508952) although each design entry's last gate line in its Log is `gate lifted: decision (paul) — by paul; answer "Go"`: TD-1153, TD-1954, TD-1962, TD-1968, TD-1976, TD-1984, TD-2021, TD-2077, TD-2080, TD-2103, TD-2104, TD-2114, TD-2126, TD-2127, TD-2129, TD-2164 and TD-2172 (`docs/reviews/TD-<n>.md`).

The rule they break is the cadence's §4.8: after the item "`Go` lifts the block (the decide lift, §2.4), the unreviewed fixes go into the build entries' Fix, and the record gets `Go by: <person>` and `Decided:`" (procedures/cadence.md step 3: "the session acting on the person's answer adds one line to the record"). Neither local text asks for that line: design §4.9a *A design's answer* says "*Go* drops the blocks and moves the unread fixes into the builds' Fix in one PR", and `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` *The answered item is yours* lists dropping the blocks, moving the fixes and closing the board line. #4668, the press's own pull request, lifted the four gates and closed the line, and touched ledger and board files alone.

What it costs: `agent-cadence run review_stats.py --verdict TD-2172` prints `next: item`, as if the question were still to be asked (TD-2129's prints the same). The cadence check's `reach` row holds a far-reaching build "unless the record's `Go by:` names a person"; it is idle here only because `docs/cadence.toml` has no `[far-reaching]` table, which TD-1961 adds.

**Location:** docs/reviews/, design §4.9a, docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md

**Fix:** Decide who writes the record when the person's press lifts a design's gate (the session that takes the answer, ShiftLead's own write-back, or the cadence's decide lift, which would be filed in agent-cadence), say it in design §4.9a and the designer brief, and fill in `Go by:` and `Decided:` on the records listed in the Why from each entry's `gate lifted` Log line and its closed board item.

**Done when:** No design entry whose gate the person lifted with *Go* has a review record reading `Go by: pending`, and design §4.9a and the designer brief name who writes the line at the next *Go*.
