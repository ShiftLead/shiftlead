Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director this widens).

**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person* *Brief me*, *Acting by delegation*, §4.7, §4.10, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/shiftlead/briefs/

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *Brief me* and the `direct` delegation, with §4.7 (`ao director brief`), §4.5a (the Director card's **Brief me**) and §4.10 (`[director]`):
- `ao director brief` and the card's **Brief me**, drawn only while `director.work` is on: a person's own (refused to a session as `set_settings` is); starts the person's own interactive session in the director's directory with the package's new `brief_me.md`, filled with the director's brief and preferences, and opens its Focus. `brief_me.md` says: read what a watch reads, talk it through, put every state change on the cards as the director's items.
- `direct`: with it, the director may send a `steer`, `note` or `ask` to any seat about work the person's items or answers name; such mail, allowed by the `work` grant (§4.8), is marked `[director]`, binding on conduct as a `[person-session]` text does and never on what a seat answers, read live: it reads `[other]` once `direct` is taken away or `director.work` is off. Without `direct` the director mails no seat but as a question seat's asker.

**Done when:** **Brief me** starts the person's own session with `brief_me.md` filled and opens its Focus, and is refused to a session; with `direct`, the director's mail to a seat is marked `[director]` and reads `[other]` once `direct` is removed or `work` is off; without it a director's mail to a seat is refused; tests cover each.
