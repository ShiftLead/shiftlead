**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person* *Brief me*, *Acting by delegation*, §4.7, §4.10, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/shiftlead/briefs/

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *Brief me* and the `direct` delegation, with §4.7 (`ao director brief`, `--for`), §4.8 (`work`), §4.5a (the Director card's **Brief me**) and §4.10 (the `director` edge of *A session may message*, `[director]`):
- `ao director brief` and the card's **Brief me**, drawn only while `director.work` is on: a person's own (refused to a session as `set_settings` is); starts the person's own interactive session in `~/.local/share/shiftlead/director-brief/` (never the seat's directory) with the package's new `brief_me.md`, filled with the director's brief and preferences, and opens its Focus. Its record is `by_person: true` (§4.10 *between the person's own sessions*), its start being the person's press, so its note reaches the director seat by that edge. The session holds no `work` grant; `brief_me.md` says: read what a watch reads, talk it through, and hand what is meant for the cards to the director seat as a `note` (`ao msg director "…"`). A note from a person's session makes the director due by `watch` at once (TD-1934's trigger).
- `direct`: §4.10's new edge `director`, tried after `own`: the director seat, while `direct` is delegated and `director.work` is on, may send a `steer`, `note` or `ask` to any record with an agent but an interactive one, each with `--for <mail id>`: the person's answer to one of its asks or an item of its the person answered or dismissed, at most fourteen days old, naming the same first `--about`; the send path checks it and prints it in the header; every other bound of a send (depths, the sameness rule, a steer's bound) holds. Such mail is marked `[director]`, binding on conduct as a `[person-session]` text does, never on what a seat answers nor on the *Never* list, a merge outside the merge line, a lease or a hold; read live, it reads `[other]` once `direct` is taken away or `director.work` is off. Without `direct` or a good `--for`, the director's mail to a seat is refused but as a question seat's asker.

**Done when:** **Brief me** starts the person's own session in its own directory with `brief_me.md` filled, its record `by_person: true`, and opens its Focus, and is refused to a session; a note from it reaches the director and makes it due at once; with `direct`, the director's mail to a seat with a good `--for` passes the `director` edge and is marked `[director]`, and is refused with a missing, old or mismatched `--for`, to an interactive record, or past a depth; it reads `[other]` once `direct` is removed or `work` is off; the test key of §4.5a's Director card **Brief me** control passes; tests cover each.
