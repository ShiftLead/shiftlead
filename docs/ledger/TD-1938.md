**Why:** The person also wants the director in conversation, and directing the seats about the work they have answered (design §4.9b *Brief me*, *Acting by delegation* `direct`, §4.10 `[director]`, TD-1910).

Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director this widens).

**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person* *Brief me*, *Acting by delegation*, §4.7, §4.10, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/shiftlead/briefs/

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *Brief me* and the `direct` delegation, with §4.7 (`ao director brief`, `--for`), §4.8 (`work`), §4.5a (the Director card's **Brief me**) and §4.10 (`[director]`):
- `ao director brief` and the card's **Brief me**, drawn only while `director.work` is on: a person's own (refused to a session as `set_settings` is); starts the person's own interactive session in `~/.local/share/shiftlead/director-brief/` (never the seat's directory) with the package's new `brief_me.md`, filled with the director's brief and preferences, and opens its Focus. The session holds no `work` grant; `brief_me.md` says: read what a watch reads, talk it through, and hand what is meant for the cards to the director seat as a `note` (`ao msg director "…"`). A note from a person's session makes the director due by `watch` at once (TD-1934's trigger).
- `direct`: with it, the director may send a `steer`, `note` or `ask` to any seat but an interactive record, each with `--for <mail id>`: the person's answer to one of its asks or an item of its the person answered or dismissed, at most fourteen days old, naming the same first `--about`; the host agent checks it and prints it in the header. Such mail is marked `[director]`, binding on conduct as a `[person-session]` text does, never on what a seat answers nor on the *Never* list, a merge outside the merge line, a lease or a hold; read live, it reads `[other]` once `direct` is taken away or `director.work` is off. Without `direct` or a good `--for`, the director's mail to a seat is refused but as a question seat's asker.

**Done when:** **Brief me** starts the person's own session in its own directory with `brief_me.md` filled and opens its Focus, and is refused to a session; a note from it makes the director due at once; with `direct`, the director's mail to a seat with a good `--for` is marked `[director]`, and is refused with a missing, old or mismatched `--for` or to an interactive record; it reads `[other]` once `direct` is removed or `work` is off; tests cover each.
