**Why:** Audit of #4737 (TD-2068 slice 8), which has the home write an ended director record again after the cut-over where it carries another model or login list than `org.yml`'s `director:` gives (design §4.9e *The launch on a login*; `src/shiftlead/stage_seats.py`: "an ended record that carries others ... is written again in place, its mail kept, and a live one when its session has ended"). Three of the guards that decide this can each be removed with the tests still passing. The tests of the rewrite (`tests/test_director_on_login.py`) call `write_director(home, org)` with no `graph`, over a one-record fake, and none calls it before the cut-over.

Each change below, made alone, left tests/test_builtin_fill_login.py, test_director_on_login.py, test_director_seat_start.py, test_org.py, test_stage_tick.py and test_stage_seats.py green at eb5547a84 (169 passed). Each probe (a temporary test, `stage_seats.write_director` over the module's `_org` and `_Home`) answered as said on the unchanged code:

- `and not _stale(s.seat_model, s.login_list, want)` removed from `_director_in`. The home's pass is the one production caller and passes its snapshot (`stage_tick.py`: `write_director(call, org(), graph)`). Probe, a graph holding a director on `sonnet` and a key naming `opus`: unchanged, the record is written again; changed, nothing is asked of the home and nothing is returned. A record written before the cut-over, or before the key changed, would never be rewritten, so its fill keeps the old list, or *cannot run* for want of one.
- `teamrun.is_director(r) and` removed from the rewrite loop in `write_director`. Probe, an unrelated ended session listed before a live director on an old model: unchanged, no create; changed, a director create is sent while the director is live, reported as *worker: written again on the org's model and logins*.
- `or not teamstore.first_shape_off()` removed from `_launch_of`. Probe, before the cut-over with `model:` and `logins:` in the key and an ended record carrying neither: unchanged, nothing is created; changed, the record is written again (and, since a create before the cut-over carries neither, it would be again on every pass that finds it ended).

With all eight changes of TD-2262, TD-2263 and TD-2264 made together at eb5547a84, the 196 test modules that name the touched modules, calls or the director passed (`pytest -n 4`: 4811 passed, 8 skipped); the whole suite was not run to a result.

**Location:** tests/test_director_on_login.py; src/shiftlead/stage_seats.py write_director, _director_in, _launch_of

**Fix:** In `tests/test_director_on_login.py`: hand `write_director` a graph holding an ended director record on another model and assert it is written again (and one on the key's model, asserting nothing is asked of the home); list an unrelated ended session beside a live director on an old model and assert no create; call it before the cut-over with `model:` and `logins:` in the key and an ended record carrying neither, and assert no create.

**Done when:** Each of the three changes fails a test.
