## TD-1503: The design does not say a blank-pick handed entry its seat took up but has not settled moves when the seat turns unable

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d115dda9f033451ea99328ff78cc9ba9
**Location:** docs/design/4.9b-techlead.md (A question already waiting); docs/design/4.10-messages.md (the handed entry)

**Why:** The techlead's pass on #3114 (TD-1480 slice 2, m-084a39c91f2b, note a): a handed entry with a blank pick that its seat took up but has not settled still moves when that seat turns unable. That is right, since an unable seat cannot work it, but design §4.9b *A question already waiting* and §4.10 do not say so plainly, so a team change on an entry already in progress could surprise Paul.

**Fix:** Say it in §4.9b *A question already waiting* (the handed entry's wait) and §4.10's handed-entry text: a blank-pick handed entry moves whether or not its seat has begun it, until the seat reports an outcome (a settled one stays, #3114's review fix); the moved copy's team is the new team's, and the old seat's work on it, if any, is the new seat's to pick up from the thread. Doc-only; a history line under §4.9b.

**Done when:** §4.9b and §4.10 say plainly that a blank-pick handed entry its seat took up but has not settled moves when the seat turns unable, and that a settled one stays.
