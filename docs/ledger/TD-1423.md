## TD-1423: A held-PR read past its bound says the reader did not answer, not why its seat never ran

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-05 (ao-led-tds-ledger, through the techlead seat; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** ef1d56f6999a4e9fb807c4216e7ceb8b
**Location:** design §4.9b *The bound*; src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md and the flow briefs (the author's ask past the bound); src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py (a `--thread` ask to the person)

**Why:** Asked by the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-856168b420e1). On 2026-11-05, grinder-ao-2's asks for #2963 (m-16c08a685c90) and #2970 (m-a8a54b788f90) passed the reader's 2h bound. Each was handed to the person saying the reader *did not answer within the 2h bound and is now closed*. In fact the reader's seat, techlead-ao-1, had hit the fill ceiling at 15:04:38Z, before either ask (15:04Z, 15:19Z), and was never filled again (TD-1422). The person read it as the techlead ignoring the PRs and merged both themselves. Design §4.9b *The bound* has the author ask the person on the same thread past the bound, the number in its brief and untimed by the home. Nothing tells the author, or the person, why the reader did not read: a seat at a ceiling, a gated profile, a run that ended without answering. Nothing names the way back (Resume) either, so the person reviews in the reader's place rather than restarting it.

**Fix:** Design first (§4.9b *The bound*, §4.10). When a held-PR ask goes to the person past the bound, the message names the reader seat's state as the home holds it: at the fill ceiling since <time> (`restart_ceiling`, its `why`), its profile gated (the reset time), never filled for this ask, or filled and ended without a reply. It also names the press that brings it back (Resume on the seat's restart row). Decide whether the author's brief composes this from `ao status`, or the home adds it to the thread when the author's `--thread` ask arrives, so every author says it the same way.
