**Why:** The interactive session TD-1910 is modelled on merged within the person's merge line; the director does it by a delegation the person gives, and the person chooses how they see what it did (design §4.9b *Acting by delegation*, *How the person sees the acts*).

Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director this widens).

**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person* *Acting by delegation*, *How the person sees the acts*, §4.4 *The director's ready fields*, §4.7, §5, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *Acting by delegation* (`merge`) and *How the person sees the acts*, with §4.4 *The director's ready fields*, §6 rule 3 (the ready trigger), §4.7 (`ao pr merge`), §5 (`director.delegate`, `director.merge_paths`, `director.review`, `director.digest_at`), §4.5 screen 6 (the act record's and digest's rows) and §4.5a (the Director card's **delegate**, **merge paths** and **review**; the digest's **Dismiss all**; the act record's **Seen**):
- `director.delegate` (a list of `merge`, `direct`; empty by default) and `director.merge_paths`, one setting for every repo, on the Director card: `docs` by default, read per repo as its `[precheck-paths]` docs globs less `docs/design.md`, `docs/design/**`, `docs/briefs/**`, `docs/decisions/**` and every glob of its `held:`; or a list of globs, less the repo's `held:`.
- The ready reading: an open PR waits on the person's merge when its author's record is interactive or it is a filing PR nobody holds (§4.4); it is ready when its precheck is green and its review evidence `SHIP` or `FIXED` and, where the repo's `held:` globs match a file, the last reader's `--verdict pass` stands at its head. The host agent reads it: while `merge` is delegated each open PR's row of the `repos` reading (§4.4) carries `files`, `evidence` (read from the forge with `files` for each open PR whose head or precheck changed: the verdict word of its newest `cadence-review:` comment, whatever head it was posted at, the comments paged whole with `gh api --paginate`), `read_head` (the head they were read at), `reader_pass` (from the home's mail), `waits_merge` and `ready`, printed by `ao repo --json`; a PR of 100 files or more is never ready; the five absent with `merge` not delegated or on a GitLab repo; the gate at the merge stays the bound.
- `ao pr show <repo>#<n> [--json]` (§4.7): a plain read for any session under no grant, the host agent's `pr_show` RPC reading the forge at the call as `pr_wait` does: the PR's state, title, body, head (full sha), files and diff (cut at 200 KB, `truncated: true`) with its open row's ready fields, with their `read_head`, `null` where the PR is not open or `merge` not delegated and `ready` `null` where `read_head` is not the live head; run with `gh -R`, needing no checkout; refused for an unregistered repo (*unknown repo*) and one not on GitHub (*not a GitHub repo*); the director's brief reads it before a merge and never merges a truncated diff.
- §6 rule 3's ready trigger (on TD-1934's `watch`): while `merge` is in `director.delegate`, the seat is due by `watch` at once when the home reads a PR turn ready with every file it changes in `director.merge_paths`, keeping `seat_due: {at, by: watch, ready}` and `seat_filled` `{by: watch, ready}` (`ready` the `<repo>#<n>` list), so a fill is once per set of PRs.
- `ao pr merge <repo>#<n> --head <sha> --for person --why "<line>"` (`--head` the full sha of the head the director judged in `ao pr show`; *head moved* checked first against the live head, readiness then read again at that head, never from the reading): the director seat's alone, under `merge`; the host agent itself, in a detached worktree of the home's checkout under `~/.local/share/shiftlead/merges/<repo>/` (made at the first merge, moved to the PR's base each time, never entered by the director's session), one merge at a time per repo, a second waiting for the first, runs `check_cadence.py --pr <n> --trailers`, then `forge.py squash-message`, appends `Merged-by: director for <person> (merge)` to the body and merges with `gh pr merge --squash --match-head-commit <the --head sha>`. Refusals, each named: *not the director*, *merge not delegated*, *not waiting on your merge*, *not ready*, *head moved: <sha>*, *gate: FAIL <row>*, *outside merge paths: <file>*, *a fork's PR*, *held by <session>* (a live session other than the author holding it as `pr:<n>` or by its entry; the author's own claim never refuses), *no agent-cadence pin*, *not a GitHub repo*, *no checkout on this host*, *the director's own PR*, and *director.work is off*.
- The act record: on success, a `note --shows act` from the director seat to the person, `--about` the PR, unchecked and outside §4.10's per-sender depths, its text the act and the `--why`; drawn on the card of the entry the PR names, else in the Inbox (the PR's loose card leaves at the merge); closed seven days after, or at Dismiss or Seen.
- `director.review`, read at draw, so a change applies to every open record at once: `none` (the act record under Read), `digest` (also, at the first tick at or after `director.digest_at` each day while an open act record no digest listed stands, one `note --shows digest` the host agent writes from the director seat with no `--about`, in the Inbox under *Needs you*, counted, *The director acted for you n times*, each act linked, records made before the change among them, `last_digest.at` kept on the record; the one due when `director.work` turns off is still written; **Dismiss all** dismisses it and each listed record by `inbox_dismiss`), `each` (the act record under *Needs you*, counted, with **Seen**).
- The director's brief gains the merge line's test, its judgement's words and its bound (`merge_paths` is the host agent's; the judgement only narrows).

**Done when:** In a scratch home with a scratch forge repo, the director is due at once when a PR turns ready with every file in `merge_paths` under `merge`, and not without it or for a ready PR outside the paths; `ao repo --json` carries each open PR's ready fields and `ao pr show --json` its files and diff; merges a doc-only PR at SHIP and green, the squash message carrying the `Merged-by:` trailer, and an act record is written and drawn on the entry's card or in the Inbox; each refusal named in the design is raised by a test (a head moved after `--head`, a repo not on GitHub, a fork's PR, a file outside `merge_paths`, a design, brief, decision or `held:` file under the default, a lane holding a filing PR, no checkout on this host among them); each `director.review` value draws as designed, a change applying to open records at once, the digest written once a day at its time as `--shows digest` and closing with its records on Dismiss all; the test keys of §4.5a's Director card **delegate** and **review** controls, the digest's **Dismiss all** and the act record's **Seen** pass.
