**Why:** The interactive session TD-1910 is modelled on merged within the person's merge line; the director does it by a delegation the person gives, and the person chooses how they see what it did (design §4.9b *Acting by delegation*, *How the person sees the acts*).

Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director this widens).

**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person* *Acting by delegation*, *How the person sees the acts*, §4.7, §5, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *Acting by delegation* (`merge`) and *How the person sees the acts*, with §4.7 (`ao pr merge`), §5 (`director.delegate`, `director.merge_paths`, `director.review`, `director.digest_at`), §4.5 screen 6 (the act record's and digest's rows) and §4.5a (the Director card's **delegate**, **merge paths** and **review**; the digest's **Dismiss all**; the act record's **Seen**):
- `director.delegate` (a list of `merge`, `direct`; empty by default) and `director.merge_paths` (`docs`, the repo's `[precheck-paths]` docs globs, by default, or a list of globs), on the Director card.
- The ready reading: an open PR waits on the person's merge when its author's record is interactive or it is a filing PR nobody holds (§4.4); it is ready when its precheck is green and its review evidence posted and, where the repo's `held:` globs match a file, the last reader's `--verdict pass` stands at its head. TD-1934's `watch` trigger reads it.
- `ao pr merge <repo>#<n> --for person --why "<line>"`: the director seat's alone, under `merge`; the host agent itself, in a detached worktree under `~/.local/share/shiftlead/merges/<repo>/` (made at the first merge, moved to the PR's base each time, never entered by the director's session), runs `check_cadence.py --pr <n> --trailers`, then `forge.py squash-message`, appends `Merged-by: director for <person> (merge)` to the body and merges with `gh pr merge --squash --match-head-commit`. Refusals, each named: *not the director*, *merge not delegated*, *not waiting on your merge*, *not ready*, *gate: FAIL <row>*, *outside merge paths: <file>*, *a fork's PR*, *held by <session>* (a live session other than the author holding it as `pr:<n>` or by its entry; the author's own claim never refuses), *no agent-cadence pin*, *the director's own PR*.
- The act record: on success, a `note --shows act` from the director seat to the person, `--about` the PR, unchecked and outside §4.10's per-sender depths, its text the act and the `--why`; closed seven days after, or at Dismiss or Seen.
- `director.review`, read at draw: `none` (the act record under Read), `digest` (also, at the first tick at or after `director.digest_at` each day while an open act record no digest listed stands, one `note` from the director seat with no `--about`, *The director acted for you n times*, each act linked, `last_digest.at` kept on the record; **Dismiss all** dismisses it and each listed record by `inbox_dismiss`), `each` (the act record under *Needs you*, counted, with **Seen**).
- The director's brief gains the merge line's test, its judgement's words and its bound (`merge_paths` is the host agent's; the judgement only narrows).

**Done when:** In a scratch home with a scratch forge repo, the director merges a doc-only PR at SHIP and green under `merge`, the squash message carrying the `Merged-by:` trailer, and an act record is written; each refusal named in the design is raised by a test (a fork's PR, a file outside `merge_paths`, a lane holding a filing PR among them); each `director.review` value draws as designed, the digest written once a day at its time and closing with its records on Dismiss all.
