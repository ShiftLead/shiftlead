## TD-1966: One design's review record names its findings columns differently, so the review counter will refuse it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-18 (ao-agent-cadence-ac-grinder, through ao td add; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** c0f5d0ba660a409183c9db65784247b6

**Why:** The findings table in `docs/reviews/TD-1920.md` is headed `id | round | review | topic | where | finding | label | status`. Cadence §4.8 (the pinned `cadence.md`) lists a review record's findings columns as id, round, by, topic, mechanism, finding, class and status. The eight newest records under `docs/reviews/` use exactly those, and the older ones the same less `mechanism`. TD-1920 is the only record that names its columns `review`, `where` and `label`. agent-cadence's `review_stats.py` now reads findings by the header names id, round, by, topic, finding, class and status, and refuses a record that lacks one (agent-cadence TD-293, its PR #270; reported by agent-cadence's grinder on 2026-11-18). So once this repo's pin moves past that change, `review_stats.py --verdict TD-1920` exits 2, and the design's counted verdict cannot be read. The pin today reads the seven-column form by position, so it misreads every eight-column record's class, TD-1920's among them, until the bump. TD-1920 also numbers its reviews `R1a`, `R1b`… and its findings `R1a-F1`…, where §4.8 gives `R<n>` in the order run and `F<n>`. The pinned reader takes only `R<n>` in the Reviews table.

**Location:** docs/reviews/TD-1920.md (the findings table's header); cadence §4.8 *Design review rounds* (the record's form)

**Fix:** In `docs/reviews/TD-1920.md`, rename the findings table's header to §4.8's names, keeping the record's column order: `review` becomes `by`, `where` becomes `mechanism` and `label` becomes `class`, so it reads `id | round | by | topic | mechanism | finding | class | status`. The `where` cells name the section or file each finding touches, which serves as the mechanism phrase. Then run `agent-cadence run review_stats.py --verdict TD-1920` on the bumped pin. If it refuses or skips the `R1a`-style review ids, renumber in one pass: the reviews `R1`… in the order run, the findings `F1`… in order, and every `by` cell and `reopens` reference to match. If TD-1920's next review round is running, its designer makes the change on that round's branch instead, so the two do not conflict.

**Done when:** `docs/reviews/TD-1920.md`'s findings header reads `id | round | by | topic | mechanism | finding | class | status`, and `review_stats.py --verdict TD-1920` on the bumped pin reads every review and finding row without refusing or skipping one.
