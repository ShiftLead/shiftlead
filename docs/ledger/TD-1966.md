## TD-1966: One design's review record names its findings columns differently, so the review counter will refuse it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-18 (ao-agent-cadence-ac-grinder, through ao td add; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** c0f5d0ba660a409183c9db65784247b6

**Why:** The findings table in `docs/reviews/TD-1920.md` is headed `id | round | review | topic | where | finding | label | status`, where cadence §4.8 (the pinned `cadence.md`) gives a review record's findings table as `id | round | by | topic | mechanism | finding | class | status`; every other record under `docs/reviews/` follows that form. agent-cadence's `review_stats.py` now reads the findings by their header names and refuses a record that lacks one (agent-cadence TD-293, its PR #270), so once this repo's pin moves past that change, `review_stats.py --verdict TD-1920` exits 2 and the design's counted verdict cannot be read; on the pin today it reads the columns by place and misreads every finding's class. Reported by agent-cadence's grinder on 2026-11-18.

**Location:** docs/reviews/TD-1920.md (the findings table's header); cadence §4.8 *Design review rounds* (the record's form)

**Fix:** Rename the findings table's header in `docs/reviews/TD-1920.md` to §4.8's form: `review` becomes `by`, `where` becomes `mechanism` (its cells already name where the finding touches) and `label` becomes `class`, keeping the column order the record has (`id | round | by | topic | mechanism | finding | class | status`), and leave every row as it is. If TD-1920's next review round is running, its designer makes the change on that round's branch instead, so the two do not conflict. Then run `agent-cadence run review_stats.py --verdict TD-1920` and check it reads the classes (build-changing or minor) the rows carry.

**Done when:** `docs/reviews/TD-1920.md`'s findings header reads `id | round | by | topic | mechanism | finding | class | status`, and `review_stats.py --verdict TD-1920` reads it without refusing.
