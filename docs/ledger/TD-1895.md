## TD-1895: A team's Wind down leaves its idle members open when no manager is live or one member never declared

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** db8e43a440bc4714b7b66ba5edfa3df1
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** On 2026-11-15 at about 13:00 local Paul pressed Wind down on ao-grind. The idle seats (techlead, audits, fact-check-ao-2) closed at once; the page's press closes no session itself, so that was another close (likely rule 3's of an idle seat), not read here. grinder-ao-1 and grinder-ao-2 were at work when the wrap-up reached them, and declared out of work afterwards (about 14:55 and 14:23). designer-ao-1, idle on TD-1580 for 12h, took the wrap-up and declared nothing. None of the three was ever closed: `ao team list` still read *3 live*, and Paul had to press Stop now and Start by hand (reported by the session ao-led-tds-ledger, 2026-11-15). The manager, manager-ao-1, had been closed about 23h before the press.

The cause, read in the code. Three gaps stack up:

1. **The page's Wind down closes no member.** `/api/teams/<t>/stop` (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, the stop route) runs `teamrun.stop_members` and then `teamrun.stop_lead` only when a live lead exists (`st.lead is not None`), and even then without `close=True`. With manager-ao-1 gone, nothing waited on the members and nothing closed them. `ao team stop --close`, the manager's own trigger, does close each member that settled clean and pushed (`teamrun._close_settled`). The page's press, the person's, does not.
2. **A wrapped-up member that does not declare blocks rule 9 forever.** `WRAPUP_PROMPT` (`src/sessionorc/models.py`) says *commit and push … then stop*. It does not ask for `ao progress none`. designer-ao-1 took the wrap-up and went idle undeclared, so `work.finished` reads *designer-ao-1 idle, not declared*. Rule 9's pass (`agent_tick._finished_team`) then closes nobody, not even the two grinders that had declared. Its manager-less branch returns before any close while the reading has a clause, and closes members only once the whole reading holds and has settled for `FINISHED_SETTLE`; the branch that closes members whatever the reading says runs only after a live manager was told.
3. **The design promises an exit the tool never makes.** §4.5a's row *team card: Start / Wind down / Stop now* says Wind down's members *each finish what they hold and exit*. §4.9a already says a Claude Code worker's `/exit` does not leave and a wrapped-up session sits `idle` (the 2026-09-17 note behind `stop_lead`'s `close`). So after a person's Wind down no session's own act and no tick's act ends a member.

The harm: Wind down, the person's ordinary way to end a team, leaves its members live until a person closes them, and the team cannot be restarted (Start refuses a live member that is not concluded). Stop now kills, so it is no cure for a member holding work.

**Ask:** The wind-down's close design ran a tenth review round and closed a gap where a seat could refill early; should a session run one more?

**Design PR:** #4001, #4094, #4138, #4147, #4168, #4227, #4240

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (the team stop route: stop_members, stop_lead without close); src/shiftlead/teamrun.py (stop_members, stop_lead, _close_settled); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_finished_team, _finished_close); src/sessionorc/work.py (finished); src/sessionorc/models.py (WRAPUP_PROMPT); design §4.5a team card: Start / Wind down / Stop now, §4.9a, §6 rule 9

**Fix:** A design round first, since §4.5a's promise and §4.9a's reading disagree and the choice is behavioural. The question put to the techlead was whether a member that finishes under a wind-down should close itself or be closed by the tick. **Recommended: the tick closes it.**

- A person's Wind down is the person's word that the team ends, so a close after it follows that word and infers nothing (§9 invariant 14 is about a run ending by the session's own word; this is the person's).
- A member's own `ao close` depends on every brief and every tool telling it to. The wrap-up prompt names neither, and designer-ao-1 shows that a member stops short of it.
- The tick already has the safe close: `_finished_close`, under the wrap-up's check (idle, git fields known, nothing dirty, nothing unpushed, not suspended, link up).

The direction to design and then build:

1. **The tick's close after a person's wrap-up.** In the tick, a supervised unattended crew member (not a seat, not the manager) with `wrapup_at` set by the person (`wrapup_by: person`) that is `idle`, not mid-turn since the send, and passes `_finished_close`'s check is closed with closer `{by: tick, why: wound down}`, whether or not it declared. One holding work, or waiting on the person or a reader, is left open and named on the team card, as `_close_settled` names it today. A declared member skipped by `stop_members` (*finished, nothing sent*) is closed the same way.
2. **The page's Wind down closes as `ao team stop --close` does** where no lead is live, so the press and the CLI agree. Or it leaves closing to item 1 alone; the design round picks one road, not both.
3. **A wound-down member never blocks the wind-down of the others.** Rule 9's manager-less branch closes each finished member even where another member's clause keeps the reading from holding.
4. **The design.** §4.5a's Wind down row and help text say who ends a member (the tick, after it settles clean and pushed) instead of *exits*. §4.9a says the same beside *A person's Start on a concluded team*, and §6 rule 9 gains the person's wrap-up as a trigger. The manager is left as it is: rule 9 already tells a live manager and closes it after `WRAPUP_GRACE`.
5. Whether the wrap-up prompt should also ask for `ao progress none` is the round's question too. It would let rule 9's reading hold, but a declaration made because a person pressed Wind down is not *I searched and found nothing* (§4.9a).

Tests: ao-grind's case — a team with its manager closed, two declared idle members and one idle undeclared member with `wrapup_at` by the person, all clean and pushed. After one tick, all three are closed. A member with unpushed work stays open and is named.

**Done when:** After a person's Wind down on a team with no live manager, every member that settles idle, clean and pushed is closed by the tick within a tick of settling, declared or not, and the team reads wound down. One holding work is left open and named. The design's Wind down row says who closes it, and the test of ao-grind's case passes.
