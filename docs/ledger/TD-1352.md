## TD-1352: A question passed up to the person is not drawn on the Inbox or the Queue, so it waits unseen until its bound

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (ao-led-tds-ledger, a person-session in ledgerboard, through `ao td add --repo shiftlead`; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — found live 2026-11-04 in ledgerboard's lb-grind team; the cause is not yet found.
**Location:** the pass-up and its reading: `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` (`rpc_pass_up`, which appends the asker's copy to the person inbox), the person inbox read the page and `ao inbox --counts` share (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`, `/api/person/inbox` in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`), the page's sorting (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` `inbox_sections`, `rail_kind`) and the Queue's Decide cards; design §4.9b *Passing up keeps the thread and the asker*, §4.5a row *Inbox row: passed up*, §4.7 `ao inbox --counts`, §4.10 *An orphaned `steer` lapses to its default*.

**Why:** a techlead passes up what it cannot answer so the person decides it before the asker's default applies (design §4.9b). On 2026-11-04 two steers to ledgerboard's techlead seat `ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead` — `m-5d77cc3dec15` (TD-020, from lb-grinder) and `m-b90d95278fe0` (TD-023, from lb-designer) — carried `passed_up` (2026-11-03T18:37:19Z and 17:13:36Z) and no answer, yet neither was drawn in the UI's Decide list or its Inbox. Paul saw them only because a person-session read the store; their defaults were hours from applying. The same techlead's own `ask`s to the person (`m-26316e2c8d13`, PR #99) were drawn. So the person's one press that §4.9b promises never happens: every passed-up `steer` silently becomes its default, and a passed-up `ask` sits until it expires. Both seen were `steer`s, and the design draws a passed-up `steer` under *Steering* (§4.5a *Inbox row: passed up*), not *Needs you*, so where the reporter looked may matter; that is the first thing to settle.

**Fix:** reproduce on a scratch home (a seat passes up a `steer` and an `ask` from a member; a member on another project's repo too, as here), then find where the copy is lost — not written to the person inbox, written to another home's (a node's, §4.4a), filtered out of the page's or the Queue's read, or drawn but in a section the person does not look at — and make it as §4.9b and §4.5a's row say: the asker's question under its own kind's heading (an `ask` in *Needs you*, a `steer` in *Steering* with its time left), with *`<passer>` recommends: …* and the passer's answers, counted, until answered or lapsed. Should the person want a passed-up `steer` counted in *Needs you* rather than *Steering*, that is a design change first. Also confirm, in a test, that `ao inbox --counts` and the page count the same set (§4.7 says `--counts` reads the UI's own counts first). An entry with `adopted_at` and no answer is still open by design: an adopted `steer` lapses to its successor on time (§4.10), so `m-5d77cc3dec15` staying open is right. Done: a test that a passed-up `ask` and `steer` each appear on the Inbox page and in the counts, and a live read that the next pass-up is drawn.
