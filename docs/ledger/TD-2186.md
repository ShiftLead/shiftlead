**Why:** Design §4.5 screen 6 (TD-2172): a gated ledger question with no `Answers:` drew only **Lift the hold**, so a yes-or-no question had no yes to press.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py, src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py (ledger_decide); design §4.5 screen 6

**Fix:** Where an entry carries `decision (paul)`, has an `**Ask:**` and no `**Answers:**`, is no `Owner: paul` step, and no line covers it, the reading (`ui/inbox.ledger_for_you` and the Queue's reading) hands it the gate sweep's pair, answers `Go | Not yet`, no default, `Go` in its lifts; the Queue item, the Inbox row and the Repo page draw **Go** and **Not yet** (no **Go with it**), **Lift the hold** beside them where stamped. The home's `ledger_decide` (`sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`) writes the pressed answer as the sweep's own line, decided: `Context: TD-n decision (paul)`, `Answers: Go | Not yet`, `Go` its lift, so a Go is ledgerboard's decide lift the gate sweep's lift PR lands and a Not yet closes the line `— kept waiting.` as the sweep's does (§4.4 *The line covers*, TD-1375).

**Done when:** Tests show such an entry drawing **Go** and **Not yet** on the Queue, the Inbox and the Repo page, a Go writing a decided line whose Context carries the gate and whose lift is Go, a Not yet writing the waiting-only answer, and an entry with its own `Answers:` or a covering line drawing those instead.
