**Why:** #3280 (TD-1450 slice 2) wires a live check's lift PR as a work order, and three of its lines have no test:

- `src/shiftlead/cli.py` pickable line: `prio = str(e.get("priority") or "High")`. Replaced by `"High"`, 1219 tests of the modules that mention `pickable` still pass.

Found by: test-audit-ao-1, audit of the 10 PRs merged to 2026-11-11.

**Fix:** Add tests that (1) run the tick's `_red_prs` over a ledger entry of priority Medium and a lift PR, and assert the order's priority is Medium; (2) assert `ao repo`'s line counts a lift PR once; (3) assert the pickable line prints the lift order's priority.
