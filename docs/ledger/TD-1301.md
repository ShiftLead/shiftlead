## TD-1301: A pinch sets the Focus terminal's font size on this browser, and a sideways swipe on the strip moves between records

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-01 (phone-view, from TD-003's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — the design's review rounds run first, then Paul's go; after TD-1299, whose strip it swipes.
**Blocked by:** TD-1299, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-003
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`AO.setTermLook`, the terminal's touch handlers ~6084, the strip TD-1299 adds), `app.css`; tests beside `test_ui_soft_keys.py`

**Ask:** Now the phone layout's review rounds have converged, should a grinder build the pinch and the swipe?

**Why:** TD-003's design: §4.5a *a pinch* and *the strip*'s swipe; §4.5 *What the terminal's grid does*.

**Fix:**
1. **A pinch** on the terminal: the four terminal elements `touch-action: none` under the narrow query, in place of §4.6's `pinch-zoom`; a second finger ends a one-finger scroll and starts the pinch (`app.js` ~6084, the TD-986 handler otherwise unchanged); font size 9–16 px, kept in `localStorage` in place of `person.terminal.size` on this browser alone, `AO.setTermLook` reading it first so a Settings broadcast (`AO.termChan`) leaves it; one grid sent when the fingers lift; a passing note *<n> px · <cols> columns*.
2. **The strip's swipe**: 40 px, mostly sideways, opens the next or previous record in the switcher's order; nothing from the screen's edge.
3. **Help**: §4.5a help entries for *a pinch* and the strip's swipe, in `help.py` and the design's list in the same PR.
4. Tests: the stored size wins over the setting and over a broadcast on this browser only and is clamped; the swipe's order is the switcher's.

**Done when** a pinch on a phone changes the columns once, with its note, and survives a reload, and the swipe walks the records in the switcher's order.
