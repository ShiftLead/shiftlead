## TD-1435: A seat that read an ask but did not work it is never rung again, and an ask from a seat it may not write to cannot be closed

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06 (ao-led-tds-ledger relaying Paul, through the techlead seat; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** f75c23e3e80748debe2583cea938ccd2
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_wake.py (`_decide_wake`, the `mail_decided` watermark, `_ring_judged`); src/sessionorc/mail.py (`undecided_mail`, `mail_wakes`, the send rules); src/shiftlead/briefs/techlead.md; design §4.10

**Why:** Asked by the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger`, relaying Paul (m-5fd9b63f50c7). An ask a seat has read but not worked is never rung again. The doorbell (`_decide_wake`, `src/sessionorc/agent_wake.py`) looks only at mail past the `mail_decided` watermark (`mail.undecided_mail`), and the ring that covered an ask moves the watermark past it. A seat that ends its turn with asks still open, for example listing them as *still open, not acted on*, then sleeps until new mail arrives, and its askers stay blocked. Seen 2026-11-06: `ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1` ended a turn listing the open asks for TD-1388 (#2997), TD-1428 (#3003), agent-cadence TD-233 and four UI asks from the person, and nothing woke it to work them. The only nudge it got was the idle reminder for an outcome owed to the person, which counts the person's asks alone.

A second way an ask can stay open for good, from the same session: an ask from a session this seat may not write to cannot be answered on its thread. `ao msg --kind reply --reply-to m-7bdae3194ee7` from the techlead seat to `ao-agent-cadence-ac-grinder` (another repo's grinder) was refused (*cannot message …*, design §4.10), and `ao inbox` has no close. The ask stays *open, no bound*.

**Fix:** Design first (§4.10 *Mail*, the doorbell, and the techlead's brief). Options from the ask:
- A seat whose inbox holds read, unanswered asks is rung again when it goes idle, with the ring counted against the wake budget and stopped by the unread-ring run rule (`_ring_judged`), so a seat that cannot act is not rung forever.
- The techlead's brief (`src/shiftlead/briefs/techlead.md`, a held path) says to keep working until every open ask is answered or handed on. A seat never ends a turn on a list of asks it has not acted on.

Take both: the brief is the cheap half and the ring is the backstop. For the second gap: a reply to an ask is always allowed on that ask's own thread, whoever sent it (or there is an `ao inbox close <id> --why` for an ask the seat cannot answer), so the ask can be closed.

**Done when:** The design says what rings a seat that is idle with read, unanswered asks, and how an ask from a sender the seat may not write to gets closed. Builds carrying `Part of: TD-1435` make it so. A test: a seat idle with one read, open ask is rung once within the budget. Another test: a reply on the thread of an ask from a cross-repo seat is accepted.
