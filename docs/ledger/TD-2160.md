## TD-2160: A closed stage record moves to a kept copy instead of being removed, and can be printed

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-24
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e4f0ddc027854ca48fea4addbc94bcbc
**Blocked by:** TD-2158, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-2077

**Why:** Part of TD-2077 (design §4.9c *A closed record is kept thirty days*, §4.4 *The stage record*, §4.7 `ao stage show`). The tick removes a closed stage record from the state branch's head after thirty days, so a comparison weeks after the switch-over would have to replay the branch's history to read the entries it measures.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that records what each flow's run leaves behind, or run one more review round first?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/stage_make.py, src/shiftlead/stage_acts.py, src/shiftlead/cli.py, src/sessionorc/agent_stage.py, design §4.9c

**Fix:** In the tick's sweep of closed records (`sweep_closed` in `src/shiftlead/stage_make.py`, `closed_expired` in `src/shiftlead/stage_acts.py`), move each expired record to `closed/<id>@<YYYY-MM-DD>.json` (the UTC day of its `closed_at`, `-2` and on after the day for a second copy that day) in the same commit, in place of removing it; and in `offer` (`stage_make.py`), where a reopened entry's closed record is replaced by a fresh one, copy the closed record there in the same commit. A renumber (`_renumbered` in `stage_make.py`) renames the entry's copies in the commit that moves its live record; an entry with copies alone is not read for a renumber and its copies keep their id. Where an offer copies a closed record and is also the first under a new flow hash (TD-2158), the fresh record, the copy and the version's text go in its one commit. The pass never reads `closed/`. Add `ao stage show --closed TD-n`, printing the entry's closed copies in the order of their `closed_at` from the branch's head (`cmd_stage_show` in `src/shiftlead/cli.py`, the stage-show RPC in `src/sessionorc/agent_stage.py`, and a read on `StageStore`).

**Done when:** Tests show an expired record moved in one commit with its bytes unchanged, a reopen inside the thirty days leaving the closed record's copy in the commit that writes the fresh one, a second copy on one day taking `-2`, a renumber of a live record renaming its entry's copies, an offer under a new hash writing record, copy and version text in one commit, the pass's reading ignoring `closed/`, and `ao stage show --closed` printing copies by `closed_at`, a `-2` copy after its day's first.
