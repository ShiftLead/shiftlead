## TD-1526: A design's asks, steers and end-of-rounds item carry no link to the design PR, so its Queue card cannot open the design

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-07 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger and the techlead seat, m-5817cb52379e; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** bd4e2beb8d314cf2adf0798e44298d91
**Location:** design §4.7 *ao msg* (`--pr` rides only on an `ask`), §4.9b *The reader*, §4.5 screen 12 *What is attached to a card*, §4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*; src/sessionorc/ledgerboard/board.py (a board line's fields); src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (an item's `pr`); agent-cadence `cadence.md` §4.8 *The end-of-rounds item*

**Why:** A question about a design reaches the person with no way to open the design from its Queue card. The designer's steer m-7a8af3e5ba2f on TD-1476 (*Ordered pool scope*) names PR #3163 only in its text: its `pr` field is empty, so the card drew no link, and Paul could not open the design from the card (Paul, 2026-11-07, through ao-led-tds-ledger, m-5817cb52379e). The same holds for a board `decide` item with a `Parts:` line — the end-of-rounds item — whose card links no design PR.

Two causes, checked in the repo: `ao msg --pr` rides only on an `ask` (design §4.7 *ao msg*, §4.9b *The reader*), so a `steer` cannot carry its design's PR at all, and an `ask --pr` is the reader's hand-off, which brings a held-PR read with it; and a board line has no PR field (`src/sessionorc/ledgerboard/board.py`: `Context:`, `Parts:`, answers, lift), so a decide item's card has only its entries to go by.

Bears on TD-1378 (the Queue's Parts display), where the end-of-rounds item is drawn. Its sibling TD-1527 (a design's synopsis, linked from the same card) may share the design round.

**Fix:** Design first. Settle:
- how a `steer`, an `ask` or an end-of-rounds item about a design in a PR carries that PR, so its card links it — a `--pr` (or another field) on a `steer` and on an `ask` to the person that does not make the message a held-PR read (§4.9b *The reader*, §4.9c *A review stage any seat may hold*), or the card deriving the link from the `about` entry's open design PR (§4.5 screen 12 *What is attached to a card*: a PR-only item already names its entry from the repo's open PRs);
- how a board `decide` item with a `Parts:` line links the design PR: a field on the line (ledgerboard's SPEC, so asked of ledgerboard as `ledgerboard#TD-n`, and of agent-cadence's `board_edit.py` and the cadence's end-of-rounds item, `cadence.md` §4.8), or derived from its `Context:` entry's open or just-merged design PR;
- where the link is drawn on the card (§4.5a *Queue card: what needs the person*), and on the Inbox row.
Another repo's part is filed on that repo's ledger, never here.

**Done when:** A design round names how a design's asks, steers and end-of-rounds item carry its PR and where the card draws the link, and files its builds with `Part of: TD-1526`; a steer like m-7a8af3e5ba2f then shows a link to its design PR on its Queue card.
