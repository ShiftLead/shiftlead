## TD-1934: The director wakes on a watch and tells the person what needs them as Queue items checked against the live state

**Priority:** High
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 53db97f063744d85a11bc9c7e33232d7
**Blocked by:** TD-642, TD-662
**Part of:** TD-1910

**Why:** TD-1910's design gives the director a second role, working for the person (design §4.9b *The director works for the person*). Its base is the watch that wakes it and the items it sends: without them nothing else of the role has anything to stand on.

Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director this widens).

**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person*, §6 rule 3, §5, §4.7, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/sessionorc/, src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *The director works for the person*, its first three bullets and *What it tells the person is a Queue item*, with §6 rule 3 (`watch`), §5 (`director.work`, `director.watch`), §4.8 (the `work` grant), §4.7 (`--about` on the director's sends, `--shows`, `ao board list`) and §4.5 screen 6 (*Read*):
- `settings.yml`'s `director.work` (`on`/`off`, off by default) and `director.watch` (`1h` by default), read by the home; the Director card's **work** toggle and **watch every** field (§4.5a), saved with the card's Save.
- §4.8's `work` grant: given to the director seat by the home while `director.work` is on, taken back when off, never by `set_grants`; it gates every director-only verb below and in TD-1936 to TD-1938.
- §6 rule 3's `watch` due reason for the director seat: due `director.watch` after its last watch fill concluded (at once with none), and at once when a PR its `merge` delegation could merge turns ready (precheck green, review evidence posted), `seat_due: {at, by: watch, prs}` and `seat_filled` keeping `{by: watch, prs}`, so a fill is once per set of PRs. Off, no `watch` reason is ever raised; turning it off ends the watch at the next tick.
- `ao msg --about` on the director's sends to the person: repeatable and required (`board:<key>`, `<repo>#TD-n`, `<repo>#<n>`, or a repo alone for a gap); the host agent refuses the send when any is closed at that moment, naming each (`stale about: shiftlead#4081 merged 01:50Z`); a `--shows done` note and a pass to the person (TD-661) are not checked; every other sender's `--about` is unchanged (one ref, unchecked). The card it draws on is the first `--about`'s.
- `ao board list [--repo <name>] [--json]` (§4.7): every registered repo's open board lines from the host agent's `boards` reading, key, repo, kind, due, text and context in `--json`; a read for any session.
- `ao msg --shows fyi|watch|done` on a `note`, the director's alone: `fyi` and `watch` drawn under the card's **Read** (the card its refs name, else the Inbox); a `watch` closes by itself when every ref it names closes, or on a `--shows done` note on its thread.
- The director's brief (`src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md`) gains the watch fill: read the org through `ao repo --json`, `ao board list --json`, `ao status --json` and its inbox, never a checkout or `~/.shiftlead`; send decisions as `ask`s with the recommendation first, reports as `fyi`, waits as `watch`.

**Done when:** With `director.work` on in a scratch home, the director seat is woken by `watch` at its interval and on a ready PR; a send of its with a closed ref is refused naming the ref; a `--shows fyi` and a `--shows watch` note draw under Read on the card their ref names, the watch closing when its refs close; with `director.work` off no `watch` is raised; tests cover each, and the §4.5a rows' test keys pass.
