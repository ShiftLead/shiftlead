## TD-1934: The director wakes on a watch and tells the person what needs them as Queue items checked against the live state

**Priority:** High
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 53db97f063744d85a11bc9c7e33232d7
**Blocked by:** TD-642, TD-662
**Part of:** TD-1910

**Why:** TD-1910's design gives the director a second role, working for the person (design §4.9b *The director works for the person*). Its base is the watch that wakes it and the items it sends: without them nothing else of the role has anything to stand on.

Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director this widens).

**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person*, §6 rule 3, §5, §4.7, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/sessionorc/, src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *The director works for the person*: *On by the person's choice*, *Woken by the home*, *Checked against the live state at the write* and *What it tells the person is a Queue item*, with §6 rule 3 (`watch`), §5 (`director.work`, `director.watch`), §4.8 (the `work` grant), §4.7 (`ao msg`'s director options, `ao board list`), §4.5 screen 6 (the director's rows of *One place for each thing that needs the person*, *Read*):
- `settings.yml`'s `director.work` (off by default) and `director.watch` (`1h`); the Director card's **work** toggle and **watch every** field (§4.5a). Off: the running watch fill closed at the tick, open `watch` notes closed, the rest kept.
- §4.8's `work` grant, set and taken back by the home with `director.work`, never by `set_grants`; it gates every director-only option and verb here and in TD-1936 to TD-1938.
- §6 rule 3's `watch`: due `director.watch` after the last watch fill's close (`seat_filled.at`), only when the `repos` or `boards` reading or the seat's inbox changed since (`watch_seen`); at once on a note from a person's session; while `merge` is delegated, at once on a PR turning ready (`seat_due: {at, by: watch, ready}`, `seat_filled` keeping `{by: watch, ready}`); never while the seat has used five of its six `FILL_CEILING` fills in the hour.
- `ao msg` for the director's sends to the person: `--about` repeatable and required (`board:<key>`, `<repo>#TD-n`, `<repo>#<n>`, `<repo>#gap:<slug>`), each read at the send from the forge (a PR) or origin's default branch fetched then (a board line, an entry); refused when any is closed (`stale about: …`) or unknown (`unknown about: …`), and when kind and first `--about` match an open item of the seat (`already open: m-…`); a `--shows done` note and a pass to the person unchecked. `--shows fyi|watch|recommend|done` on a `note`, the director's alone; a `watch` needs a ref that is not a gap key. A non-director's `--shows`, `--for` or second `--about` refused. The usage line of §4.7 updated.
- Drawing (§4.5 screen 6): on the card the first `--about` names (a board key on its line's card), else the Inbox; `recommend` under the question it sits on as *The director recommends: …*; `fyi` and `watch` under **Read**. Closing: the tick closes a `watch` or `fyi` when every ref closed, an `fyi` also seven days after sending; a `recommend` with its question; a closed note leaves Read and keeps its thread.
- `ao board list [--repo] [--json]` (§4.7): open board lines from the `boards` reading.
- The director's brief (`src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md`): the watch fill reads `ao repo --json`, `ao board list --json`, `ao status --json`, its inbox and its own open sends (`ao inbox --sent --json`); answers a prompt or a passed question first and re-reads `ao status` between steps; sends recommendations, decisions (only what it can carry out), reports and waits as designed; edits no checkout, reads a repo only in the home's checkout, read-only.

**Done when:** In a scratch home with `director.work` on: the seat is due by `watch` only after the interval and a reading change, at once on a ready PR under `merge`, never past five fills in the hour; each `ao msg` refusal above (stale, unknown, already open, a non-director's option) is raised by a test; `recommend`, `fyi` and `watch` notes draw where §4.5 screen 6 says and close as designed; `ao board list --json` lists open lines only; turning `work` off closes the running fill and the open `watch` notes; the §4.5a rows' test keys pass.
