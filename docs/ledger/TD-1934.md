## TD-1934: The director wakes on a watch and tells the person what needs them as Queue items checked against the live state

**Priority:** High
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 53db97f063744d85a11bc9c7e33232d7
**Blocked by:** TD-642, TD-662
**Part of:** TD-1910

**Why:** TD-1910's design gives the director a second role, working for the person (design §4.9b *The director works for the person*). Its base is the watch that wakes it and the items it sends: without them nothing else of the role has anything to stand on.

Related: TD-636, TD-657 (the director this widens).

**Location:** design §4.9b *The director works for the person*, §6 rule 3, §5, §4.7, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/, src/sessionorc/, src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md

**Fix:** Build design §4.9b *The director works for the person*: *On by the person's choice*, *Woken by the home*, *Checked against the live state at the write* and *What it tells the person is a Queue item*, with §6 rule 3 (`watch`), §5 (`director.work`, `director.watch`), §4.8 (the `work` grant), §4.7 (`ao msg`'s director options, `ao board list`), §4.5 screen 6 (the director's rows of *One place for each thing that needs the person*, *Read*):
- `settings.yml`'s `director.work` (off by default) and `director.watch` (`1h`); the Director card's **work** toggle and **watch every** field (§4.5a). Off: the running watch fill closed at the tick, open `watch` notes closed, the rest kept; every director-only option and verb but `ao td add --approved` refused, *director.work is off*.
- §4.8's `work` grant, set and taken back by the home with `director.work`, never by `set_grants`; it gates every director-only option and verb here and in TD-1936 and TD-1938 (`ao td add --approved`, TD-1937, is checked by its ask instead).
- §6 rule 3's `watch`: due `director.watch` after the last watch fill's close (`seat_filled.at`), only when `watch_seen` changed: a digest over the named fields alone, never a reading's times (of `repos` each repo's open PRs by number, head and precheck state and its ledger entries by id and Status; of `boards` each open line's key; of the inbox its open mail ids); a fill in which a director send to the person was refused for its depth leaves `watch_seen` as it was; due at once on the person's answer to one of its asks (with `work` off, a *File it* alone, `seat_due: {at, by: watch, filing}`, for a fill that only files and that the turn-off's close, of the fill running at that tick alone, does not reach) and on a note from a person's session; never while the seat has used five of its six `FILL_CEILING` fills in the hour. The ready-PR trigger is TD-1936's.
- `ao msg` for the director's sends to the person: `--about` repeatable and required (`board:<key>`, `<repo>#TD-n`, `<repo>#<n>`, `<repo>#gap:<slug>`), each read at the send from the forge (a PR) or origin's default branch fetched then (a board line, an entry); refused when any is closed (`stale about: …`) or unknown (`unknown about: …`; a gap key checked only for its repo registered and checked out on the home's host), and when kind and first `--about` match an open item of the seat (`already open: m-…`); a `--shows done` note and a pass to the person unchecked. `--shows fyi|watch|recommend|done` on a `note`, the director's alone (`act` and `digest` the host agent's, TD-1936); a `watch` needs a ref that is not a gap key; a `recommend` joined to its question by `--reply-to` (a seat's open ask or steer to the person, from `ao inbox --person-questions`; a join only, the addressee staying the person, the host agent resolving the id in the person inbox) or its first `--about` `board:<key>` (an open `decide` line), refused otherwise or when that question is answered or lapsed (`no open question: …`). A director `ask` to the person must end on a doing-nothing answer (*Not now — nothing is done*). A non-director's `--shows` or second `--about` refused (`--for` and its refusal are TD-1938's). The usage line of §4.7 updated.
- Drawing (§4.5 screen 6): on the card the first `--about` names (a board key on its line's card), else, or where that card has gone (a merged PR's loose card), the Inbox; `recommend` under the question it sits on as *The director recommends: …*; `fyi` and `watch` under **Read**. Closing: the tick closes a `watch` or `fyi` when every ref closed, an `fyi` also seven days after sending (a gap-only one by the seven days alone); a `recommend` with its question; a closed note leaves Read and keeps its thread.
- `ao board list [--repo] [--json]` (§4.7): open board lines from the `boards` reading.
- `ao inbox --person-questions --json` (§4.7, §4.8): the director seat's alone under `work`, the open `ask`s and `steer`s in the person inbox by id, sender, kind, `about` and subject, marking nothing; refused to any other session.
- The director's brief (`src/shiftlead/briefs/director.md`): the watch fill reads `ao repo --json`, `ao board list --json`, `ao status --json`, its inbox, `ao inbox --person-questions --json` and its own open sends (`ao inbox --sent --json`); answers a prompt or a passed question first and re-reads `ao status` between steps; sends recommendations, decisions (only what it can carry out), reports and waits as designed; edits no checkout, reads a repo only in the home's checkout, read-only.

**Done when:** In a scratch home with `director.work` on: the seat is due by `watch` only after the interval and a change in `watch_seen`'s named fields (a reading's new times alone raise none), at once on the person's answer to its ask and on a person's session's note, never past five fills in the hour, and a fill with a depth-refused send leaves `watch_seen` unchanged; each `ao msg` refusal above (stale, unknown, a gap key in an unregistered or elsewhere-checked-out repo, already open, no open question, an ask with no doing-nothing last answer, a non-director's `--shows` or second `--about`, *director.work is off*) is raised by a test; `recommend` (by `--reply-to` and by `board:<key>`), `fyi` and `watch` notes draw where §4.5 screen 6 says, in the Inbox once their card has gone, and close as designed; `ao board list --json` lists open lines only; turning `work` off closes the running fill and the open `watch` notes; `ao inbox --person-questions` lists the person's open questions to the director and is refused to another session; the test key of §4.5a's Director card **work** control passes.
