## TD-1678: test_ledger requires a Resolved line that ledgerboard's superseded and dropped archive forms do not write

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 8890f85e436249eca9fba5e2cf628529
**Location:** tests/test_ledger.py (test_every_entry_has_a_body_and_not_someone_elses)

**Why:** `tests/test_ledger.py::test_every_entry_has_a_body_and_not_someone_elses` asserts `**Resolved:**` in every archived entry. ledgerboard's own writer archives a superseded or dropped entry without one: `agent-cadence run ledger.py archive TD-n --superseded-by TD-m` (or `--dropped`) writes `**Status:** Superseded <date> by TD-m` (or `Dropped`) and puts the reason on an `archived:` Log line, as ledgerboard's SPEC (*Superseded and dropped*, v2.4) and target design (*`Dropped` has no `**Resolved:**`-style body line. Its why is the `archived:` Log line*) define it. So the helper's correct output fails shiftlead's precheck: on 2026-11-11 PR #3447 archived TD-1651 `--superseded-by TD-1664`, its precheck went red on this test, and the entry had to be reopened and archived again with `--pr` and `--pointer` to carry a Resolved line it does not need (older dropped entries such as TD-997 carry one only because they were archived before that form).

**Fix:** In `tests/test_ledger.py`, change the archive half of `test_every_entry_has_a_body_and_not_someone_elses`: an archived entry passes when it holds `**Resolved:**`, or when its Status is `Superseded … by TD-m` or `Dropped` and its Log holds an `archived:` line. Keep the test's purpose (a heading with no body of its own still fails): the Priority and Why checks stay for every entry. Read the Status word through ledgerboard's parser where the test can import it, rather than a second regex. Add a case to the test's fixtures, or a small test beside it, for a superseded and a dropped archive written by `ledger.py archive`, so the helper's form and the test cannot drift again.

**Done when:** An entry archived with `ledger.py archive --superseded-by` or `--dropped` passes `tests/test_ledger.py`, an archived entry with neither a Resolved line nor that form still fails it, and a test holds both.
