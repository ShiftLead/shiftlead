**Why:** The person decides, before the window, each team's new flow, its logins and counts and the built-in set, and needs to see what the move would make and what stands in its way, with nothing changed (design 4.9e, The three commands). There is no page for it.

**Ask:** Should the builders build the switch-over's check and its answers file?

**Fix:** Build ao switch-over check, a client command on the home's files that changes nothing. It prints, per host, the logins the move would make from its profiles (design 4.2b, What the switch-over makes), each team with what the answers give it, the built-in set, the work that would be carried, and every blocker design 4.9e lists, each named with what cures it, and exits non-zero while one stands. With --answers it writes ~/.shiftlead/switch-over/answers.yml where there is none, filled with the suggestions the design gives, and lists beside each team's flow the seats the team runs today that the flow lacks, and each open ask to a first-shape member or seat.

**Done when:** On a fixture home shaped like today's (ten profiles on six folders, six teams, a container node with profiles of its own): check prints the home's six logins and the node's, six teams and the carried entries, and names each blocker of a fixture made to have one of each kind; --answers writes a file the check then accepts once names and flows are filled; --host --answers on a fixture node writes that node's part alone; the home's files are byte-identical before and after.
