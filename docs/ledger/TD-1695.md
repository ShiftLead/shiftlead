**Why:** TD-1556's drawing half. A `blocked` outcome rides on the `ask` or `steer` it settles, so `ui/queue.py`'s `want_of` gives it *decide*. On TD-1529 it showed as a Decide card offering only Reply or Dismiss for a question already answered, and it stayed after the Reply, since nothing moved a replied blocked row out of *Needs you*. Design §4.10 *Outcomes*, §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* and §4.5a *Inbox row: `blocked` outcome* now draw it as a Do item. A replied one (TD-1694's `outcome.replied`) is FYI.

**Fix:** Design §4.10 *Outcomes* (*`blocked` is not a dead end*, and *`--thread` takes a `blocked` outcome's place*), §4.4 *The card's status* (*A `blocked` outcome keeps the word*), §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* (the **Do** bullet) and §4.5a's row *Inbox row: `blocked` outcome* hold the rules. Build them:
- **The want.** In `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`'s `want_of`, a row whose `outcome.state` is `blocked` (a closed question's, or a handed row's) answers `do`, before its `kind in ("ask", "steer")` test. It comes after the `READ_LINES` test, so a replied one in FYI still reads `read`. The docstring already lists the blocked outcome under *do*. The row's head keeps its kind badge (`ask`, `steer`, the handed entry's), and the *outcome · blocked* mark stays `inbox_row.html`'s `outcome_line`, already drawn.
- **The replied row leaves.** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`'s `inbox_sections` puts a `blocked` outcome in `needs` in the closed-question branch. There, one whose outcome carries `replied` goes to `fyi`, drawn under the question as a `done` or `dropped` outcome is (`answer_given`, `outcome_line`). The `handed` loop is unchanged: a handed entry's outcome never carries `replied`. The Inbox count and the card's Do count drop with the Reply.
- **Two lines** in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html`, after `outcome_line`:
  - *you replied · <age>* from `outcome.replied.at`, on a replied blocked outcome;
  - *was blocked: <text>* from `was.text`, with *· you replied <age>* where `was.replied` is set, on an outcome carrying `was` or a handed entry carrying its own `was` (TD-1694).
  No new control.
- **The card's hold** (held path: `src/sessionorc/`). `src/sessionorc/queue.py`'s answered hold (the `outcome.get("state") != "done"` branch near line 704, `tests/test_queue_answered_hold.py`) ends on any outcome but `done`. Make a `blocked` outcome keep it. Without `replied`, the hold stands whatever the asker's state: skip the asker-gone branch, the `written > closed` test and the day bound. With `replied`, read it as no outcome, so the hold ends when the asker is gone. In both cases return the outcome as it is (the blocked dict), as the `done` path does, so the foot's readers (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`'s hold words) draw *<asker>: <line>*. A `dismissed` or newer outcome is read as today. An unreplied `blocked` linked question holds the member even where a newer answered question about the entry is `best`: look for one before choosing `best`. A replied one's hold ends with its person-inbox copy (FYI's window from `replied.at`).
- **Not here: the handed hold.** §4.5 screen 12's *handed to <seat>* mark, which now keeps a `blocked` handed ask, is not built yet; TD-1660 (TD-1543's build) builds it, and its Log carries this change.
- **The replaced note.** `inbox_sections`' `settles` map, built from `outcome.by`, also takes `outcome.was.by`, so the replaced blocked outcome's note stays hidden under its question. The outcome's own row is then the card's one need, Do.
- **Tests,** in `tests/test_ui_inbox*.py`, `tests/test_queue*.py` and `tests/test_queue_answered_hold.py`:
  - a TD-1529-shaped row (a steer answered, its outcome `blocked`) is counted once, and its card's want is *Do 1*, never *Decide*;
  - the same about a `decision (paul)` entry: the entry holds even with its asker exited, so the card has the one Do item; once replied, an exited asker ends the hold;
  - the same row with `replied` is in FYI, uncounted, with *you replied*;
  - an `asked_again` with `was` draws *was blocked*;
  - a handed entry's blocked row is Do;
  - after a newer `done`, the old blocked note is not an FYI row of its own.

**Done when:** A TD-1529-shaped row (a steer answered, its outcome `blocked`, about a `decision (paul)` entry) is the card's one need, a counted Do item on the Inbox and its Queue card, never Decide. It leaves for FYI with *you replied* once the person replies. This is shown on a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`, design §4.9b), with the shots named in the PR, and the tests above pass.
