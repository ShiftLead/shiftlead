## TD-1695: A blocked outcome draws as Do, and one waiting on an open item is a line under it, counted once

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** b9805dbd226d44c0820a06cff27c94f3
**Blocked by:** TD-1694
**Part of:** TD-1556
**Location:** design §4.10 *Outcomes*, §4.5 screens 6 and 12 *Three wants*, §4.5a *Inbox row: `blocked` outcome*; src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (`want_of`), src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (`inbox_sections`), src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html, src/shiftlead/cli.py (`ao card`), tests

**Why:** TD-1556's drawing half. A `blocked` outcome rides on the `ask` or `steer` it settles, so `ui/queue.py`'s `want_of` gives it *decide*, and when it waits on another question already in front of the person the Inbox and the card count that need twice and the outcome never leaves with it (TD-1529: two Decide cards for one answer owed). Design §4.10 *Outcomes*, §4.5 screens 6 and 12 *Three wants* and §4.5a *Inbox row: `blocked` outcome* now draw it as a Do item, and as a line under the open item its `waits_on` (TD-1694) names, counted once.

**Fix:** Design §4.10 *Outcomes* (*A `blocked` outcome that waits on another open item is drawn under it, and counted once*), §4.5 screen 6 (the two rows counted after an answer), screen 12 *Three wants* (the **Do** bullet) and §4.5a's row *Inbox row: `blocked` outcome* hold the rules; build them:
- **The want.** `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`'s `want_of` answers `do` for a row whose `outcome.state` is `blocked` (a closed question's, or a handed row's), before its `kind in ("ask", "steer")` test; its docstring already lists the blocked outcome under *do*.
- **The fold.** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`'s `inbox_sections`, in both places a `blocked` outcome goes to `needs` (the closed question's branch and the `handed` loop): read `outcome.waits_on`; the first id in it whose person-inbox entry is still open (`_entry_open`), snoozed or not, takes the outcome — it is put in no section and is attached to that entry as `blocked_here` (a list of `{id, from, from_name, text}`), so it is counted nowhere and draws wherever that entry's row draws (*Needs you*, *Steering*, the Snoozed fold, a Queue card). Where every id in `waits_on` is closed or gone, the outcome goes to `fyi` with `cleared: {id, reason}` (the last-closed id's `closed_reason`, `gone` where the entry is no longer held). An empty or absent `waits_on` keeps today's branch. The `count` (*Needs you*'s length) and the Queue card's want counts follow from the sections, so nothing else counts it.
- **The line.** `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html`: on any row carrying `blocked_here`, one display line per item under the row's text, *<from_name> is blocked on this: <text>*, a link to `/inbox/<id>` (the outcome's own message page, whose row keeps **Reply** and **Dismiss**); an FYI row with `cleared` draws *cleared — <id> <reason>* after its `outcome_line`. No new control.
- **`ao card`** lists a folded outcome's id under the item it waits on, as the card draws it.
- Tests: `tests/test_ui_inbox*.py` / `tests/test_queue*.py` beside the outcome tests — a TD-1529-shaped pair (a steer answered, its `blocked` outcome naming another sender's open ask in `waits_on`): one item in *Needs you* (the ask), the line under it, the count 1, the card's want *Decide 1* and no *Do*; the ask answered: the outcome in FYI with *cleared*; a `blocked` outcome with `waits_on: []`: counted, want *do*; one with no `waits_on` key (stamped before TD-1694): the same.

**Done when:** On a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`, design §4.9b) holding a TD-1529-shaped pair — a steer answered and its `blocked` outcome whose `waits_on` names another session's open ask — the Inbox's *Needs you* and the Queue card each count one need, the outcome a line under the ask; answering the ask moves the outcome to FYI as *cleared*; a `blocked` outcome naming nothing is a counted Do item labelled *outcome · blocked*, never Decide; the PR names the shots, and the tests above pass.
