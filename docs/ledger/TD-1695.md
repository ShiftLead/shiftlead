**Why:** TD-1556's drawing half. A `blocked` outcome rides on the `ask` or `steer` it settles, so `ui/queue.py`'s `want_of` gives it *decide*. On TD-1529 it showed as a Decide card offering only Reply or Dismiss for a question already answered, and it stayed after the Reply, since nothing moved a replied blocked row out of *Needs you*. Design §4.10 *Outcomes*, §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* and §4.5a *Inbox row: `blocked` outcome* now draw it as a Do item. A replied one (TD-1694's `outcome.replied`) is FYI.

**Fix:** Design §4.10 *Outcomes* (*`blocked` is not a dead end*), §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* (the **Do** bullet) and §4.5a's row *Inbox row: `blocked` outcome* hold the rules. Build them:
- **The want.** `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`'s `want_of` answers `do` for a row whose `outcome.state` is `blocked` (a closed question's, or a handed row's), before its `kind in ("ask", "steer")` test. Its docstring already lists the blocked outcome under *do*. The row's head keeps its kind badge (`ask`, `steer`, the handed entry's), and the *outcome · blocked* mark stays `inbox_row.html`'s `outcome_line`, already drawn. No template change.
- **The replied row leaves.** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`'s `inbox_sections` puts a `blocked` outcome in `needs` in the closed-question branch (and in the `handed` loop, which is unchanged: a handed entry has no Reply, so its outcome never carries `replied`). There, one whose outcome carries `replied` goes to `fyi`, drawn under the question as a `done` or `dropped` outcome is (`answer_given` and `outcome_line`), so the Inbox count and the card's Do count drop with the Reply.
- **Tests,** in `tests/test_ui_inbox*.py` and `tests/test_queue*.py` beside the outcome tests:
  - a TD-1529-shaped row (a steer answered, its outcome `blocked`) is counted once, and its card's want is *Do 1*, never *Decide*;
  - the same row with `replied` is in FYI, uncounted;
  - a handed entry's blocked row is Do.

**Done when:** A TD-1529-shaped row (a steer answered, its outcome `blocked`) is a counted Do item on the Inbox and its Queue card, never Decide, and leaves for FYI once the person replies. This is shown on a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`, design §4.9b), with the shots named in the PR, and the tests above pass.
