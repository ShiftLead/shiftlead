## TD-1919: The Focus terminal holds its pane at one grid, and the person resizes it on demand

**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-16, on Focus panes with lines cut off, missing and repeated; the measurements on a real tmux 3.5a with Claude Code (Haiku), 30 long labelled paragraphs streaming while a client's grid changes, counted in tmux's own scrollback (`tests/test_resize_repaint_live.py`, `tests/test_tmux_resize_history_tmux.py`): with the window following its client, a width change repeats 5 to 13 paragraphs and a height change loses 3 to 4 whole ones, a reattach at another size does both, and a plain printing program in the same pane loses and repeats nothing, so the damage is the tool's repaint over what tmux keeps, not the browser; waiting for the pane to go idle first does not help (a width change at idle still repeats the last two paragraphs, a height change still loses 3 to 4), `attach -f ignore-size` alone does not help (one client leaves nothing to defer to), and fixing only the rows does not help (width alone repeats); with the window pinned (`window-size manual` and one `resize-window`) every paragraph was there once in 8 of 8 runs (two each of steady, width, height and reattach) and in the live test's combined run (resizes, then six reattaches). TD-794's debounce already sends one resize per gesture and the damage stands, which is the case its step 3 names.

**Design PR:** #4093

**Location:** design §4.6 Attach behaviour with another client present, §4.5a; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the terminal's fit and resize); src/shiftlead/ui/pty_bridge.py; src/sessionorc/tmux.py (the session options)

**Fix:** Design first (§4.6 *Attach behaviour with another client present*, §4.5a, TD-794's step 3), then builds under it. The design says: the bridge pins a session's window at one grid (`window-size manual`) and attaches at it, so no reload, reattach or layout change resizes the pane; the terminal draws at the pane's own grid instead of fitting its box, and a box that is smaller scrolls and one that is larger leaves room (the phone layout, the key bar and the keyboard change the box, never the grid); the grid is chosen once, when the session starts, and remembered. The person resizes on demand, for a new monitor or a new window, with one control (a *Fit to window* beside the terminal's other controls, §4.5a) that sets the pane's grid to the box's. What that control does to a tool that is drawing is the open part, and each way is measured on the live test before the design picks one: wait for the pane to be idle; interrupt the turn first; stop the tool's process while the window changes and let it go after; clear tmux's history and ask the tool for a clean repaint afterwards. The design also says what another attached client (a VS Code attach, `ao focus`) sees at a different size (padding or a cropped view) and what becomes of the *resized by another client* mark (TD-474).

**Done when:** Done when the design describes the pinned grid, the on-demand fit and its rule for a tool that is drawing, each with its measurement in the history, and every build it leaves has its own entry, the live test's pinned mode guarding them.
