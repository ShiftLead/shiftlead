## TD-1441: Rule 8's daily start cap counts the person's useful starts as runaway ones, and holds a team his Go just unblocked

**Why:** Asked by Paul through the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-3b1f5eda9c97). Rule 8's daily start cap counts useful starts the same as runaway ones. Rule 8's `on_work: start` starts a team at most `WORK_STARTS_DAY` (3) times in `WORK_DAY` (24 h), never twice within `WORK_EARLY` (30 min) (`src/sessionorc/agent_common.py:90`, read at `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py:2523`; design §6 rule 8). Every start counts, whether or not the team did anything with it.

Seen 2026-11-06: rule 8 started `lb-grind` at 01:44Z, 05:42Z and 14:50Z. Each start came from Paul's **Go** or **One more round** on a design item, and each led to merged work: TD-050's rounds and builds, the v2.6 release and TD-053's rounds. Between them the team wound down while it waited for his next answer (rule 9, `FINISHED_SETTLE`, 10 min). His Go on TD-053 landed at 16:53Z, but the team was held at `{why: day, count: 3}` with TD-053 and TD-046 in its lanes until he pressed **Start** by hand at about 17:50Z. On a day full of decisions, the cap throttles the person's own answers. Paul asks whether the caps serve him or are only runaway guards he could multiply by ten. `WAKE_BUDGET` (30 an hour) and `RESTART_CEILING` (3 in 2 h) look sound. This one bites in normal use.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (`WORK_STARTS_DAY`, `WORK_DAY`, `WORK_EARLY`, `FINISHED_SETTLE`); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_work_start ~2435–2490, _work_held ~2524–2560; rule 9's `_finished_pass`); src/sessionorc/work.py (`finished`); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (the hold's *started N times today*); design §6 rule 8, rule 9, §4.5a

**Fix:** Design first (§6 rule 8, rule 9).
- **The count.** Only futile starts count toward `WORK_STARTS_DAY`: a start after which the team claimed nothing and merged nothing before it wound down. A runaway stays bounded by `WORK_EARLY`, at most two starts an hour. `work_started` would keep each start's outcome, read at the wind-down.
- **The wind-down (related).** A team whose lanes hold only entries gated on the person's decision reads as *waiting on the person* rather than finished. Today `work.finished` (`src/sessionorc/work.py`) reads only the member records, never the lanes. Under the change it does not wind down under rule 9, so it needs no fresh start minutes after the answer lands.
- **Stopgap until the design lands.** Raise `WORK_STARTS_DAY` to 10, a one-constant change. The design's *three* follows it in §6 rule 8 (`6-policies.md`, twice) and §4.5a (*three starts in a day*). No test or help text pins the value. It needs Paul's yes, since it changes behaviour.

**Done when:** The design says which starts count toward the daily cap and whether a team waiting only on the person's decisions winds down. A build with `Part of: TD-1441` makes it so. Its test: three useful starts in a day leave a fourth start open, and three futile ones hold the team at `day`.
