## TD-1921: Sessions leave scratch data, worktrees and caches nothing cleans up, and they filled the machine's disk

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17 (Paul, through his session ao-samscrape-samup; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 5ddd2519cdf54fe0b28af0641f43fcdf
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** Agent sessions leave scratch data nothing reaps, and on 2026-11-17 it helped fill kmaster's root disk (118G, 83%) until the kubelet evicted pods under DiskPressure (samscrape TD-320). Measured that day by Paul's samscrape session: about 24 GB of session leftovers, growing about 10 GB a month — `/tmp/claude-1000` 7.5G (1,466 per-session scratch dirs; grinder-ao-1 and -2 alone 4.7G), worktrees about 6G (shiftlead 51 = 2.2G, contractmatch 8 = 2.5G, samscrape 23 = 1.2G), `~/.shiftlead/runs` 2.1G (1,688 runs, 0.6G older than 14 days), and `~/.cache/samscrape-precheck` with `~/.cache/agent-cadence` 1.2G. What is pruned today is narrow: run logs and attachments of ended sessions after `runs_keep_days` (30, design §4.6 *Run-log retention*), and a worktree only when the closing record's own start made it (§4.1 *A close reaps the worktree it made*, TD-978). The tool's scratch dirs, worktrees a session made itself, and precheck caches have no owner and no schedule.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the disk clean-up design now, or should a session run one more review round first?

**Design PR:** #4091

**Location:** design §4.6 *Run-log retention*, §4.1 *A close reaps the worktree it made*; docs/briefs/** (where a session puts scratch data); the adapters under src/shiftlead/adapters/

**Fix:** Design first:
- one standard scratch location per session, which briefs and the adapters name, so a session's temp data is findable and attributable;
- a periodic cleanup on the home's tick (or a timer it owns) of: scratch dirs of sessions no longer live, worktrees whose record has ended and whose branch is merged or pushed (never one with unpushed work), run logs past a shorter or size-bounded keep, and precheck caches past an age;
- a reading of what it would remove and what it removed, so the person can see the disk it frees;
- **Paul's constraint: transcripts are kept** — `~/.claude/projects` and each tool's equivalent are never touched, since they feed later post-analysis; the design names them as excluded and a test holds it.

**Done when:** the design says where a session's scratch data lives, what the cleanup removes on what schedule, and that transcripts are never removed; its builds are filed with Part of this entry.
