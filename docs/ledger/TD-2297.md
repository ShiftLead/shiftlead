## TD-2297: Two design pages say a session waits only when no listed login is usable, but it never moves back up

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 8d9433fd9e454acf8311d3e7fc0d345f

**Why:** Docs audit of #4790 (TD-2069 slice 2, the tick's move down the login list). Design §4.9e *A session moves down the list* moves a session only to a later login: "a later login of its list is usable and offers its model", "With no later login usable the session waits where it is", and "A session does not move back up while it runs".

Two other passages, which #4790 left as they were, say a session is held only when every listed login is unusable:

- §4.9d *The logins, in order* (`docs/design/4.9d-teams.md`): "**The usage gate holds a session only when no login in the list is usable for its model** (§6 *Usage gate*, *After the cut-over*): then it waits where it is, as a gated session does today."
- §6 *Usage gate* (`docs/design/6-policies.md`): "**a session is held only when no login in its team's list, or for a built-in seat its repo's built-in set's, is usable for its model**: one whose login reaches its line is moved to the next usable login of the list first, and pauses, as above, only where there is none."

They differ for a session on a lower login while an earlier one is usable. Probe at b82ce3f36: list `claude-code:first`, `claude-code:second`, the session on `second`, `first` usable, `second` at its reserve (and, in a second run, `limited`): `teamstore.next_login` answers `None`, so nothing moves. By reading, `_enforce_usage_gate` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`) takes the reserve lines and the reading of the record's own `profile`, its login's name after the cut-over, and nothing of `login_list`, so at its reserve the session is paused although a listed login is usable. The later-only rule is held by the `never-up` and `last` cases of `test_next_login_is_the_first_usable_one_below_that_offers_the_model` (`tests/test_login_move.py`).

**Location:** docs/design/4.9d-teams.md The logins, in order; docs/design/6-policies.md Usage gate; docs/design/4.9e-switch-over.md A session moves down the list

**Fix:** Word §4.9d's sentence and §6's by §4.9e's rule: a session is held only where no **later** login of its list is usable for its model, an earlier one being taken at the next launch. Where holding no session while any listed login is usable is meant instead, that is a change to §4.9e, `teamstore.next_login` and its test first. Add the history lines to this entry's history file under §4.9d and §6.

**Done when:** §4.9d *The logins, in order*, §6 *Usage gate* and §4.9e *A session moves down the list* say the same of a session whose login reaches its line while only an earlier listed login is usable.
