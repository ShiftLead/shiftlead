## TD-1968: Start session on a Queue card's header shows nothing for seconds after the press

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-18 (paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 60b14ba5b75b41cb8178867b60d9d0b8

**Why:** Seen by Paul on 2026-11-18 on the Queue: he pressed the **Start session** in a card's header (TD-794's card, its top line), and for 6 to 8 seconds nothing on the page changed. Then the Focus page of the new session opened. In that time the press looks lost, so the person presses again or leaves. The start itself does real work before it answers (`/api/ledger/start` creates a worktree and branch and starts the session), so seconds are expected. Silence during them is not.

Design §4.5 screen 6 *A control answers the press* says a press answers in the same frame: the pressed control and its row's are disabled, the row is marked pending, `aria-busy`, and its age gives way to the verb under way (*starting…* is already in the page's verb table for `ledger_start`). But rules 2 to 4 there are written for a row. A Queue card's header has no row. In `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` the press handler's `pressed()` returns at once when the button has no enclosing `.mailrow`, so the header's Start session (`queue_card.html`, class `qstart`) draws nothing until the answer comes back. Screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session* does not say what that press shows.

**Location:** design §4.5 screen 6 *A control answers the press*, screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*, §4.5a *Queue card: Start session / Open session*; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the press handler's pressed(), the ledger_start branch); src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_card.html (the header's qstart button)

**Fix:** Design first (§4.5 screen 6 *A control answers the press*, §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session*, §4.5a's *Queue card: Start session / Open session* row). Say what a press with no row shows in its own frame. A likely shape: the pressed control alone is disabled and `aria-busy`, and its label gives way to the verb under way (*starting…*). It stays so until the answer either moves the page to Focus or the form, or is refused. A refusal puts the label back and toasts the server's words. Say whether every other control with no row (a card header's other controls, a ⋯ menu's items) follows the same rule, so no press on the page is ever silent. Then file the build: `pressed()` in `app.js` and the header template. A test should hold that the header's press is marked before its request leaves. While there, note in the build where the start's seconds go (worktree creation, the session's launch), in case one of them can answer sooner. That is not part of this entry's done.

**Done when:** The design says what a Queue card header's Start session, and any other press with no row, shows in the frame of the press, and its build is filed with `Part of:` this entry.
