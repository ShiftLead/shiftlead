**Why:** Part of TD-2080: temporary files keep filling the space every session depends on, and nothing reacts until it is full (design §4.6).

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that gives unattended seats their own temporary space and clears it before it fills?

**Context:** Temporary files have repeatedly filled the disk every session shares. This design moves unattended seats to a pool of their own, clears what can be rebuilt when it runs low, and warns you before it is full. Its review rounds ended with nothing left that changes the builds.

**Location:** tests/conftest.py, scripts/judge.py, scripts/conformance.py

**Fix:** Move the repo's own writers off bare `/tmp` (design §4.6 *Temporary space is a pool of its own*): `tests/conftest.py`'s scratch roots go under `$TMPDIR` where the resulting root fits `scratch.MAX_ROOT`, else `/tmp` as today, each writing its pid to `.shiftlead-test-run` inside it (§4.6 *The pressure pass*, rule 1), and `sweep_stale_scratch_roots` reads `/tmp` and every seat's directory under `shiftlead-<uid>/` by that file, as `_run_parents` does, the `OWNERS` files staying for the roots at the top of `/tmp`; `scripts/judge.py` and `scripts/conformance.py` use `SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH` when set.

**Done when:** A suite run inside a seat leaves no new `aoscr-*` directory at the top of `/tmp`, a killed run's root in another seat's directory is removed by the next suite run, and `judge.py` and `conformance.py` make their homes under `SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH` when it is set.
