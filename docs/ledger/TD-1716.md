## TD-1716: ao feedback digest: a seat's transcripts over a window, condensed through the adapters (TD-1491's extractor)

**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-11
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open

**Part of:** TD-1491
**Location:** design §4.7 *Feedback*, §4.3 `conversations`, `marks_errors`, `read_transcript`, §4.4a NODE_READS; src/sessionorc/adapters.py (TranscriptEntry, Conversation); src/shiftlead/adapters/claude_code, gemini; src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (NODE_READS, ACT_TIMEOUT); src/sessionorc/agent_remote.py (the forwarded read's bound); the RPC handlers; src/shiftlead/cli.py; tests/test_transcript.py

**Why:** TD-1491's design (§4.7 *Feedback*) gives the `/shiftlead-feedback` procedure a deterministic extractor that condenses a seat's transcripts over a window into text an agent reads. It reads through each tool's adapter, never a tool's files from core. The 2026-11-06 review the procedure replaces read Claude Code's and Gemini's files by hand.

**Fix:** Build §4.7 *Feedback*'s extractor and §4.3's additions:

- **§4.3**: `TranscriptEntry.error` on a `tool` entry, which `to_dict` carries only when True. The Claude Code adapter sets it in `read_transcript` from the tool result's `is_error`. The class attribute `marks_errors` is True for Claude Code, and False where unset; Gemini CLI's is TD-1719. Add the optional `conversations(cwd, profile) -> list[Conversation] | None`, where `Conversation` is `(id, first_at, last_at, size)`, oldest first, read from each file's head and tail only. Claude Code reads the profile's `projects/<munged cwd>/*.jsonl`. Gemini CLI reads the top-level chats of the projects whose `.project_root` names `cwd`. The conformance suite (TD-112) names it among the reads an adapter declares.
- **RPCs**: add `conversations {id, since, until}` and `transcript_digest {id, conversation, profile, since, until, zone, skip}`, both added to `NODE_READS`.
  - `conversations` asks every profile the adapter's `profiles()` lists. It keeps one entry per `id`, preferring the record's own profile. It refuses a shell with *a shell has no transcript*, and answers `{marks_errors, conversations}`. The home forwards both reads to a node with the call's 300 s bound, not `ACT_TIMEOUT`.
  - `transcript_digest` takes `profile` as required. It pages `read_transcript` back by `before` until it passes `since`.
  - Each line form is as §4.7 lists them, rendered in the caller's `zone`, with `since` inclusive, `until` exclusive and the first `skip` entries at `since` dropped:
    - day lines;
    - `HH:MM` leads, with continuation lines indented;
    - a prompt cut to three lines;
    - whole text;
    - a `✗` line's `<call>` cut to 120 characters, its result kept to three lines or 300 characters, and `→ (no result)`;
    - collapsed calls;
    - the subagent line, never counted in a run, and a lone `sidechain` entry's;
    - compaction;
    - no thought.
  - An undated entry, of any kind, takes the instant before it. A reply that would pass 4 MiB as JSON ends at the last prompt before that point, else at the last entry before it. It always holds at least one entry, and an entry that alone passes the point is written to 64 KiB with *(cut)*. `next` is then `{at, skip}`; otherwise it is null.
- **CLI**: the `ao feedback` parser, whose bare form prints usage and exits 2, and `ao feedback digest <seat>… --since <when> [--until <when>] --out <dir> [--part-bytes N] [--skip <conversation>…] [--list]`.
  - `<when>` is a local day or an instant. The CLI sends its zone's IANA name, else its UTC offset, and bounds each call at 300 s.
  - The calling session's own conversation, its `SHIFTLEAD_SESSION` record's `adapter_id`, is always skipped where there is one. A conversation an earlier record listed is listed only under that one.
  - The CLI calls again with `next`'s `since` and `skip` until `next` is null.
  - It writes `<dir>/<record id>.txt`, or `<record id>.1.txt` and on, split at a conversation header or a prompt. Each conversation goes under its `## <id> · <profile> · <first_at> → <last_at>` header; a part that starts inside a conversation repeats the header with *(continued)* and its day line.
  - It prints the `--json` index §4.7 gives.
  - A record with none in the window gets no file. A record whose host does not answer, or whose read is refused or passes its bound, is listed with its `error`, and the others go on; the run exits 1.
  - It writes nothing outside `<dir>`.
- Update `tests/test_transcript.py`'s `NODE_READS` set.

**Done when:** `ao feedback digest` over a scratch home's record writes the parts and the index §4.7 gives. Tests cover:

- each digest line form;
- the day lines in the given zone, across a change of clocks;
- the window's edges;
- an undated entry;
- the part split and *(continued)*;
- `next` and the CLI's second call, with no entry repeated or lost at a shared instant, and the cut with no prompt before the point;
- the profile dedupe, with a conversation held under a profile other than the record's;
- `--list` and `--skip`, and the caller's own conversation left out;
- the `--json` index shape;
- an unreachable record listed with its error, and the exit 1;
- a shell's refusal.

Claude Code's `conversations`, `error` and `marks_errors` are tested on fixture files, and Gemini's `conversations` likewise. §4.3, §4.4a and §4.7 say what the code does.
