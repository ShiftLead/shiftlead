**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *The same pass records a design's Go* (in the event runner's paragraph), in `src/sessionorc/gatesweep.py`, `src/sessionorc/reports.py` and the home's daily pass that calls it.

1. **`record_owed(root) -> list[dict]`**: read the swept repo's ledger, its archive included, from the same `origin/<default>` reading the sweep uses. Take every entry with `Kind: design-first` that has a review record (`docs/reviews/TD-<n>.md`, in the repo's own form: three-digit ids as `TD-NNN`). Parse each Log line with ledgerboard's `LOG_SHAPES`, and take the last line in the Log's order (not by date: a gate added and lifted on one day) about the entry's decision gate (`gate added`, `gate lifted`, `gate reassigned` or `kept waiting`, naming `decision (…)`). The entry is owed a record when that line is a Go's lift (`gate lifted: decision (<p>) — by <by>; answer "<a>"` with `<a>`, case-folded and a ` (default)` mark dropped, one of `go` (the end-of-rounds item's decide lift), `go ahead` (**Go now**, **Lift the hold**, a `blocked` outcome's Lift) or `yes` (§4.10's lifting answer on a gate's item; a design asks a yes-or-no question on its gate with answers of its own, as TD-1581 did), the delegate form `by <delegate> for <person> (<scope>)` and the `LOG_LIFT_SUFFIX` tail allowed) and the record's `**Go by:**` reads `pending` or is missing, or its `**Decided:**` has no `YYYY-MM-DD` at its start (`review_stats.py`'s `DATE_RE` reading). A lift by any other answer (TD-1581's `Per account, no override`, `Works`, `Pinned, run it again`), an override's lift, or a newer line of another kind (a gate added again for a Hold, `kept waiting`), means nothing is owed. A record with no `**Decided:**` line, or in no form this reads (another repo's own), is skipped and named in the run log. Each result carries the id, the person who answered (the delegate form's `<person>`, else `<by>`), and the lift line's date as the Log wrote it (never converted to UTC).
2. **The write**, one PR for the repo on `record/<UTC date>`, from a worktree of the sweep's own (never the registered checkout), through `_ship`. It changes each owed record's header lines and nothing else. A `**Go by:**` reading `pending` becomes `**Go by:** <person>`; a missing one is added after `**Source:**`, else after `**Verdict:**`, else after `**Decided:**`; one naming anyone else is left. A `**Decided:**` with no date at its start gets the lift line's date at its start, and keeps what it held after a leading word `pending`, then a `,` or a lone `—` after it, are dropped: `pending` becomes `<date>`, an empty line `<date>`, `pending, 2026-11-14 (the Hold revision …)` (TD-1832's) becomes `<date> — 2026-11-14 (the Hold revision …)`, `pending (the bought round 5 ended …)` becomes `<date> — (the bought round 5 ended …)`, and prose such as `One more round (Paul, 2026-11-12, …)` becomes `<date> — One more round (Paul, 2026-11-12, …)`. A `Decided:` starting with a date is left. The title is `reviews: record pass <UTC date> — <n> records`, naming no entry id (the merge gate's `ledger` row refuses a title naming an id on a PR that touches no ledger file, and an id would draw the PR on that entry's Queue card), and the body has one line `- record: TD-<n> — <the lift line>` per record.
3. **The rules are the sweep's**: the home opens the PR and merges none. Nothing is written while an earlier `record/` PR is open, and one record PR at most is written per repo per UTC day: a pass that finds a `record/<that day>` branch on the remote, open, merged or closed, writes nothing until the next UTC day. A record PR closed unmerged makes the records its body's `- record: TD-<n>` lines name not owed for ledgerboard's `SWEEP_REASK_DAYS` from its close, read from the closed `record/` PRs as `_held_by_notices` reads a closed notices PR's lines. The pass runs on the event runner's schedule (once a UTC day, and on the tick after a live-ref push), after the lifts and the board PR, for every repo it sweeps. The run log says what it wrote or why it wrote nothing.
4. **A work order from its first reading**: `reports.sweep_of` returns `"record"` for a `record/YYYY-MM-DD` head (a calendar date, matched whole by a new `RECORD_HEAD_RE` beside `SWEEP_HEAD_RE` and `NOTICES_HEAD_RE`; a `RECORD_PREFIX` beside `SWEEP_PREFIX`), so `workorders.is_sweep_order`, the filing rule's exclusion and `red_prs`/`red_holders` take it as they take a `sweep/` head (*A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once*, TD-1854), and a grinder reviews and merges it as it does a sweep PR. It gets words of its own wherever a sweep PR's are written, each reader choosing by the `sweep` mark's value: `pr_order` marks it `sweep` with `sweep_of`'s value (`"sweep"`, `"notices"`, `"record"`, all truthy, so a reader testing the mark's truth is unchanged) where it now writes `True`, and `workorders.RECORD_CONTEXT` (*the gate sweep's record of designs' Go*) stands beside `SWEEP_CONTEXT` in `pr_order` (Medium, its Context the URL and that phrase), the nudge (`record PR #n has been open since … — take it or say why`), `teamrun`'s `red_prs` `what`, `ao repo`'s sweep line (`cli.py`, both places it writes `SWEEP_CONTEXT`; an unserved repo's *the person's · record since <t>* line and the first line's *n record PRs to take* count, which a record order's truthy `sweep` mark already brings into `agent_tick`'s `person` list) and the Inbox row (`ui/inbox.py`). Where a reader copies the mark on, it copies the value, never `True`: `ui/inbox.py`'s row (`{"sweep": True}` today), and `ui/queue.py`'s and `cli.py`'s counts read it by truth and are unchanged. The order's key words stay the sweep's: `red_prs`' `word` stays `sweep` and its time key `sweep_since`, so `ui/org.py`'s facet filter and the `*_since` reads are unchanged. The seat's job kinds (`stage_job.PR_KINDS`) gain `record`, which `stage_job`'s kind choice (today `next(k for k in ("sync","lift","sweep","filing") if o.get(k))`) takes where the `sweep` mark reads `record`, its time still `sweep_since`, with its section `src/shiftlead/briefs/job/pr/record.md` saying what design §4.4 *What the taker does* says of *A record PR*: merged on the cadence once its diff touches `docs/reviews/` alone and each record matches its body's line against the entry's newest decision gate line on `origin/main`; a record main already wrote or one gated again taken out, of the diff and of the body (its `- record:` line deleted, the title's count lowered), so a closed PR's body names only what it still held; a conflict re-written on main's copy; a PR left with no record closed unmerged, its body naming none. Any wording of §4.5a's rows that lists the sweep's heads gains `record/` in this build.
5. **Tests**: a scratch repo with a design entry whose Log ends in a lift and whose record reads `pending` gets one PR changing those two lines. A missing `Go by:` is added in place. A delegated lift's record names the person, not the delegate. A lift line with a `; board <repo>` tail is read. Prose in `Decided:` is kept after the date, and `pending, <date> (…)` keeps what follows the comma. A record that names a judge, or already has a date, is untouched. A lift answered `go ahead` or `Yes` is owed a record. A second pass on a UTC day that already has a `record/` branch writes nothing. A taker's take-out leaves a body naming only what remains. A newer gate line, a lift by any other answer, or an override's lift, means nothing is owed. A gate added and lifted on the same day is read in Log order. The title names no id, and a closed record PR's body lines hold their records. A record order's Context, nudge, `ao repo` line, Inbox row and job kind use the record words. A record with no `Decided:` line is skipped. An open `record/` PR blocks the write, and a recently closed one holds its records for the window. An archived design is read. `stage_job_start` on a record order starts kind `record` with its brief section. `sweep_of("record/2026-11-25")` is `"record"` and `sweep_of("record/x")` is None. Running the pass twice gives no second PR.
