## TD-2225: The home writes the person's Go on a design's review record, which today stays pending

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 7aa7ac1c9a524d9fa5b57c4af701997d
**Part of:** TD-2222

**Why:** A press that lifts a design's gate with *Go* lifts the gate and closes the board line, so no session is sent after it, and nothing writes the Go on the design's review record. Sixty-two records lack the Go: seventeen read `Go by: pending`, forty-five have no `Go by:`, and all but one of them have no date in `Decided:`, although each design's newest decision gate line is the person's answer lifting it (TD-2222). Design §4.4 *The same pass records a design's Go* (TD-2222) gives the record to the home's daily gate-sweep pass.

**Location:** src/sessionorc/gatesweep.py; src/sessionorc/reports.py (sweep_of); the home's event runner pass; tests

**Fix:** Build design §4.4 *The same pass records a design's Go* (in the event runner's paragraph), in `src/sessionorc/gatesweep.py`, `src/sessionorc/reports.py` and the home's daily pass that calls it.

1. **`record_owed(root) -> list[dict]`**: read the swept repo's ledger, its archive included, from the same `origin/<default>` reading the sweep uses. Take every entry with `Kind: design-first` that has a review record (`docs/reviews/TD-<n>.md`, in the repo's own form: three-digit ids as `TD-NNN`). Parse each Log line with ledgerboard's `LOG_SHAPES`, and take the newest line about the entry's decision gate (`gate added`, `gate lifted`, `gate reassigned` or `kept waiting`, naming `decision (…)`). The entry is owed a record when that line is an answer's lift (`gate lifted: decision (<p>) — by <by>; answer "<a>"`, any answer, the delegate form `by <delegate> for <person> (<scope>)` and the `LOG_LIFT_SUFFIX` tail allowed) and the record's `**Go by:**` reads `pending` or is missing, or its `**Decided:**` has no `YYYY-MM-DD` at its start (`review_stats.py`'s `DATE_RE` reading). An override's lift, or a newer line of another kind (a gate added again for a Hold, `kept waiting`), means nothing is owed. Each result carries the id, the person who answered (the delegate form's `<person>`, else `<by>`), and the lift line's date.
2. **The write**, one PR for the repo on `record/<UTC date>`, from a worktree of the sweep's own (never the registered checkout), through `_ship`. It changes each owed record's header lines and nothing else. A `**Go by:**` reading `pending` becomes `**Go by:** <person>`; a missing one is added after `**Source:**`, else after `**Verdict:**`, else after `**Decided:**`; one naming anyone else is left. A `**Decided:**` with no date at its start gets the lift line's date at its start, and keeps what it held after a leading word `pending` and a lone `—` are dropped: `pending` becomes `<date>`, an empty line `<date>`, `pending (the bought round 5 ended …)` becomes `<date> — (the bought round 5 ended …)`, and prose such as `One more round (Paul, 2026-11-12, …)` becomes `<date> — One more round (Paul, 2026-11-12, …)`. A `Decided:` starting with a date is left. The subject is `shiftlead: record the Go on TD-a, TD-b (session n/a)`, and the body lists each record with its entry's lift line.
3. **The rules are the sweep's**: the home opens the PR and merges none. Nothing is written while an earlier `record/` PR is open. A record PR closed unmerged makes the records it names not owed for ledgerboard's `SWEEP_REASK_DAYS`, its close date read from the PR as `_held_by_notices` reads a closed notices PR. The pass runs on the event runner's schedule (once a UTC day, and on the tick after a live-ref push), after the lifts and the board PR, for every repo it sweeps. The run log says what it wrote or why it wrote nothing.
4. **A work order from its first reading**: `reports.sweep_of` returns `"record"` for a `record/YYYY-MM-DD` head (a calendar date, matched whole, beside `SWEEP_HEAD_RE` and `NOTICES_HEAD_RE`; a `RECORD_PREFIX` beside `SWEEP_PREFIX`), so `workorders.is_sweep_order`, the filing rule's exclusion and `red_prs`/`red_holders` take it as they take a `sweep/` head (*A PR the gate sweep opens is the team's at once*, TD-1854), and a grinder reviews and merges it as it does a sweep PR. Any wording of §4.5a's rows that lists the sweep's heads gains `record/` in this build.
5. **Tests**: a scratch repo with a design entry whose Log ends in a lift and whose record reads `pending` gets one PR changing those two lines. A missing `Go by:` is added in place. A delegated lift's record names the person, not the delegate. A lift line with a `; board <repo>` tail is read. Prose in `Decided:` is kept after the date. A record that names a judge, or already has a date, is untouched. A newer gate line, or an override's lift, means nothing is owed. An open `record/` PR blocks the write, and a recently closed one holds its records for the window. An archived design is read. `sweep_of("record/2026-11-25")` is `"record"` and `sweep_of("record/x")` is None. Running the pass twice gives no second PR.

The first live pass writes the 62 records owed on shiftlead's origin/main of 2026-11-25 (TD-2222's Why lists them), and any owed since. Check its diff against each entry's lift line before merging it.

**Done when:** The home's daily pass opens one `record/<UTC date>` PR per repo with the Go of every lifted design whose record is owed one, and `sweep_of` makes that PR a work order. The tests above pass. Once the change is live, the first pass's PR is merged and no shiftlead review record of a design lifted by an answer reads `Go by: pending`, lacks `Go by:`, or has a dateless `Decided:`.
