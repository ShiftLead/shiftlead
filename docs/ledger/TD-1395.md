**Why:** TD-1392's design: a wait is a gate in `Blocked by:` (`until YYYY-MM-DD`, `event (<name>)`), the form the pinned cadence writes (cadence §2.14), and the `**Waits:**` line is retired. ShiftLead's reader takes its `waiting` mark from the `Waits:` line alone (`ledger.waits_of`), and `kind_of` sorts any non-decision `Blocked by` item as *blocked*, so an entry written with a gate today reads *blocked* rather than *waiting*: its lane's member reads as out of work with nothing, the Queue card's leaf finds no wait, and `ao progress claim` refuses it with no *waits* word.

**Ask:** Go once TD-1392's review rounds, which ended at the cap, are accepted? Answered on the board's 2026-11-05 decide item for TD-1392, which lists this build among its parts.

**Fix:** As design §4.4 *An entry says what it waits for* now says:
1. **The `waiting` mark from the gates.** The entry reader reads each entry's gates through the vendored `ledgerboard.parse_gates`; the marking pass marks an entry `waiting: {on, until, name, why}` when what keeps it from pickable is only its `until` and `event` gates (no open id, no decision, no open part) — `on` `until` while its one date stands (a UTC day), else `event` (its name, and `why` the first line of its `**Event (<name>):**` section) — or, while one still stands, a `**Waits:**` line read as today (`waits_of`), its `until` date read as a UTC day. An `until` stops waiting on its UTC day, before the sweep removes it. `in_lanes`' *but for its wait* (`_unwaited`) reads such an entry as pickable with its `until` and `event` items removed (ledgerboard gives it `pickable: no` with them in `blocked_by`), so a lane that would take it counts it under `waiting`.
2. **The page kind reads past `until` and `event` gates**: `kind_of` sorts by the entry's Kind as if they were not there (a waiting design-first entry *design*, a build *pickable*, a live check *live check*); an open id, a decision or an unreadable item sorts as today.
3. **The words.** `ao progress claim` on a waiting entry is refused (`settle.wait_refusal`) *TD-n waits until <date>* or *TD-n waits on event (<name>)*, `--force` passing it as today (the forcing session's PR drops an `until` with `set --blocked-by`, since `lift` refuses one before its day, and lifts an event gate with `lift`); `ao repo`, the Repo page's waits mark, the Queue card's leaf (`queue.py`'s `_status` `waits` test and `_wait`'s `of_its_own` and `waited`; its `unreachable` pass reads an `until` or `event` item as a wait, never *unresolved*, so `_status` does not return *blocked* for it) and the lanes line read the mark from the gates — the hover the gate and its Event line, a `Waits:` line's words while one stands (`teamrun.wait_words` and `teamrun.wait_line`, the `wait` and `wait_line` row keys in `ui/repo.py`, `ui/templates/queue_row.html`'s hover); the Repo page row's *blocked by …* names ids and decisions alone, not the gate.

**Done when:** tests that an entry `Blocked by: until <future day>` and one `Blocked by: event (x)` with its section are marked `waiting` (on, date or name, the Event line), sorted by their Kind, out of every lane and counted under `waiting`; that `until` of today's UTC day is not waiting; that an entry with both an `until` still standing and an `event` is `on: until`, and `on: event` from the date's day; that a waiting live check naming a live build is read by `_unwaited` as pickable and counted under its lane's `waiting`; that a `Kind: build` re-check with a future `until` is in no lane and enters `free-pick` on its day; that an entry with an open id or a decision beside its gate is not marked waiting; that a claim is refused with each word and passes with `--force`; that a Queue card whose member waits on an event is Waiting with *waits on <the Event line>* and its gate gives no `unreachable`; that a waiting Repo page row's hover is the gate and its Event line and its *blocked by …* omits the gate; and that a `Waits:` line still standing reads as before, its date a UTC day.
