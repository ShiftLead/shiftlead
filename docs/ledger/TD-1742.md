## TD-1742: The designer brief never names cadence step 5's line, so design PRs merge without Merged-by:

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-13
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 133598d843ba478184a6dee7ca3b9e74
**Part of:** TD-1412

**Why:** The designer's brief never says how to merge, so its design PRs merge around cadence step 5's line and their squashes carry no `Merged-by:`. TD-1421's week read (2026-11-06T20:10Z, the pin bump #3032, to 2026-11-13) lists 14 PRs merged outside step 5 that are not tool merges. Six are designer-ao-1's, each a bare `gh pr merge <n> --squash` read from its transcript: #3043, #3050, #3061, #3107, #3552 and #3569, the last on 2026-11-12T07:45Z.

TD-1418 (#3027) put the `{merge}` slot into every built-in brief that had a merge sentence. `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md` and the td flow's design stage `src/shiftlead/flows/td/design.md` had none: they say only *you merge nothing on a held path*. The designer still merges its own design PRs, which are not held. No designer brief from 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-12T17:56Z carried `squash-message`, while every grinder, techlead and auditor brief has carried it since 2026-11-07T15:36Z. `test_no_built_in_merges_outside_step_5s_line` (tests/test_briefs_layout.py) fails only on a bare `gh pr merge` in a brief, so a brief that merges without naming any line passes it.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/flows/td/design.md; src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md; tests/test_briefs_layout.py

**Fix:** 1. Give the designer's merge the `{merge}` slot. In `src/shiftlead/flows/td/design.md`, after *You merge nothing on a held path*, say that a design PR that is not held merges on the gate through cadence step 5's line, typed whole as one command, followed by `{merge}`. Do the same in `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md` if the designer brief is rendered without that stage. Add `{merge}` to the designer's slot set in `tests/test_briefs_layout.py`.
2. Make the test catch the gap: a built-in brief or flow stage that tells a session to merge its own PR (*merge once the gate passes*, *merge on the gate*) must hold `{merge}`.

**Done when:** The designer's rendered brief carries cadence step 5's line, and a test fails when a brief that has its session merge leaves `{merge}` out.
