## TD-1343: No test holds that the header's walk press leaves an *answered — waiting on <asker>* entry out of *waits on me*: `walk_mine` is tested alone, and the press can drop it unseen

**Priority:** low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (test-audit-ao-1, auditing #2858)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`api_ledger_start`, the `walk, mine = True, walk_mine(mine, wants)` line); `tests/test_queue_start_all.py` (`test_the_walk_sentence_and_waits_on_me_without_an_answered_entry`, `test_a_header_press_on_a_two_item_card_walks_them`)

**Why:** #2858 (TD-1340) says the header's walk press leaves out an entry *answered — waiting on <asker>* from *waits on me*. Probe: in a scratch worktree on `origin/main`, change that line to `walk, mine = True, mine` and run `pytest tests/ -k "queue or fork or answered or with_you or inbox_ledger"`: 322 passed, 1 skipped. Nothing fails. `walk_mine` has a unit test (`wants = {"answered": [...]}` on the function alone), but the two press tests (`_press`) never put an answered item on the card, so the line that calls it in the road is held by no test. Dropping that call, or passing the unfiltered `mine`, would send a walker the answered entry as one that waits on the person. Fix: a press test whose card carries an answered entry (an `answered` want beside two Decide/Do items) and asserts the prompt's *waits on me* leaves it out.
