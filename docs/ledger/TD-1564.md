**Why:** Paul could not stop `ao-grind` (reported 2026-11-10 through `ao-led-tds-ledger`, a person-session in ledgerboard). After each Stop the tick filled the team's fact-check seat again. On 2026-11-07 between 18:03 and 18:08 MDT, while Paul was stopping teams and turning `on_work` off, the journal logged *ao-shiftlead-fact-check-ao-1: the seat is due (asks) — filling it* and *superseded the exited session* six times. It happened again on 2026-11-09 at 16:12 and 16:14.

This follows the design as written. TD-1128 (#2495) made rule 3 fill a closed `asks` seat that a question waits for "whatever its team" (§4.10 *A team or a repo as the address*), so that cross-repo filing does not depend on a running techlead. Two other rules point the other way: §4.9a *Out of work does not mean out of reach* says the home never starts a stopped team (rule 8 *A team that stopped*, TD-781), and §4.4's tagging seat is never filled for a stopped team. So a question's arrival overrides the person's Stop, and each fill spends a turn on an account that may be capped. A Stop that the home undoes is a control that does not hold.

**Design PR:** #3273

**Fix:** A design round settles which rule wins, then a build:
- **A stopped team's seats are held.** Rule 3 does not fill an `asks` seat whose team is stopped or stopping, whatever is due. The mail is kept in the seat's mailbox, and the team's next Start fills the seat for it. This mirrors how a question's end waits for the person's Start (§6 rule 8). Decide whether a wound-down team behaves the same, and how rule 8's *team start* row counts a question that is waiting on a held seat as work.
- **The sender is told.** `--team`/`--repo`, and `ao td add --repo`, resolve to a held seat and land there. The reply line says the team is stopped and the question waits for its Start, or it is routed as for an unable seat (§4.9b *A seat that cannot answer is routed around*). The design picks one.
- **The card says so.** The seat's card and the team card read *held: team stopped — n questions wait for Start*, which is a §4.5a row.
- **The design** changes §6 rule 3, §4.10 *A team or a repo as the address* and TD-1128's sentence together, so the three agree.

**Done when:** Done when an `ask` to a stopped team's seat neither fills it nor starts any session, a test pins this, and the next Start fills the seat with the mail intact. The seat card and the team card say the seat is held, and §6 rule 3, §4.10 and §4.9a agree.
