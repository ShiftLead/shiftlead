**Why:** A design's cost is the author's own turns, above all the turns that apply each review round's fixes on Fable at 140k to 300k tokens of context; the reviewers are a few percent of it. The analysis of 3,120 findings from 41 review records (docs/decisions/2026-11-11-design-review-findings.md) says where the rounds' work goes: 16% of findings need no model, 13% are a false claim about what exists (two thirds of them found after round 1, and at least 177 of the 394 in text a fix had changed, since a later round's fact-check reads only what changed), 37% are detail a builder would meet anyway, and 25% are the hard situations (two actors at once, interaction with another rule, restarts, endings, timing, actors, error paths) where a design review finds what nothing else would; a quarter of all findings follow from an earlier fix. Paul holds two Fable limit resets that lapse in March and wants the Fable allotment spent on what needs Fable (Paul, 2026-11-11, session fabplan: *I think this is the right shape*).

**Design PR:** #3524

**Ask:** Will you let the three designs of the cheaper way to design start: what the author prepares before a review (TD-1720), the design-coord seat (TD-1721) and the trial (TD-1722)?

**Fix:** The plan is docs/decisions/2026-11-11-fable-reset-plan.md, shrunk to its decisions after its own review rounds reopened the trial's protocol twice (docs/reviews/TD-1711.md; Paul, 2026-11-11: shrink it and split the designs). Its mechanisms are three designs, each a part of this entry with its own rounds, in the plan's proposed order:

1. TD-1722's cost model: the share of the designer's spend that is applying fixes, and spend for each design.
2. TD-1720: what the author prepares before a review (the checklist, the claims and repeated-rules blocks, the pre-review script Paul agreed, and its gate).
3. TD-1721: design-coord, the Sonnet seat that carries the rounds and asks Fable only about the hard findings.
4. TD-1722's replays of three designs Paul chooses, critical to how shiftlead works, then the first ten real designs on the new shape.

The review rounds keep what agent-cadence's rule fixes (the fact-checks, the cap, the stop rule, the record, the end-of-rounds item). For the trial the shape is written in this repo alone (briefs, the team's definition, one script); it goes upstream to agent-cadence as its own entry (`ao td add --repo agent-cadence`) only if it passes. The Fable window in the gate's reading, what each role is made to read and the messaging review are in the plan and get entries of their own when Paul starts them.

**Done when:** Done when TD-1720, TD-1721 and TD-1722 are designed and built, the three replays and the first ten real designs on the new shape are read against the baseline as TD-1722 says, and Paul has said whether the new shape stays.
