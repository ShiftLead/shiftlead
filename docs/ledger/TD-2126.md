## TD-2126: Keep a working seat's own test and review copies from filling temporary space

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** cc25636b92cd4dd7b40edf094c001277
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: a live seat's scratch is never aged, so what a seat makes by hand stays until the seat ends. Read on 2026-11-23, seats held about 383,000 of the 522,000 inodes in `/tmp`, and `grinder-ao-1` alone rose from 235,000 to 337,000 within the hour. Its `t.PQjB` test tree holds 187,000 inodes, has no `.shiftlead-test-run` marker, and so is not recognised by the pressure pass's first rule. The techlead's review copies (`src4424`, `merged4420`) hold about 12,000 each, and precheck trees about 19,000 each.

**Design PR:** #4455

**Location:** src/sessionorc/scratch.py, src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py, docs/briefs, design §4.6 *The disk sweep* and *The pressure pass*

**Fix:** Design how a live seat's rebuildable copies are released. Settle: how a hand-run suite with its own `--basetemp` that is killed or fails is marked as a test run (TD-2098 already releases one that exits 0); whether review copies and precheck trees go through one helper that names and removes them; what age makes an unmarked copy removable in a live seat, given that git work is never taken; whether a seat gets a budget and is told when it passes it; and what the seats' briefs say. Measure what is in the largest seats first.

**Done when:** A killed or failed hand-run suite in a live seat is reclaimed without waiting for the seat to end. A review copy or precheck tree past the agreed age is removed, and one in use or holding git work is kept. One seat's scratch stays under the agreed budget on kmaster for a week of normal grinding, or the seat is told.
