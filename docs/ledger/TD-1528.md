**Why:** TD-1470's Queue card reads Decide (4) — one per held entry (TD-1470, TD-1496, TD-1497, TD-1498), each *held until your decision* with round 5's question, **Lift the hold** and **Answer…** — while Paul has already answered **One more round** and the bought sixth round (TD-1525) is running (Paul's screenshot, 2026-11-07, m-87c0a64dfefc). The same shows on TD-1437 and TD-1453 between rounds. The person is asked again a question already answered, and cannot tell work waiting on the designer from work waiting on him.

The one-per-part half is TD-1378 (built as TD-1380). The other half is already designed — design §4.4 *The card's status* (*A question the person answered on the board holds the member the same way*, TD-1364, built as TD-1368): a closed decided line whose `Closed:` reason begins *carried to TD-m* holds each covered member, *answered — waiting on <holder>*, while TD-m is open, with no bound. A line closed in any other words holds only while a live record claims a covered member, within `BOARD_WAIT_DAYS`.

The closes do not use the words. The designer brief (`docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md`) says to close the answered end-of-rounds line with `ao board edit done --why "carried to TD-n"`, but every bought-round close on the board reads *see TD-n: …* instead (TD-1470's 9f8e7f26 *see TD-1525: the bought sixth round, the designer's*; TD-1453's *see TD-1523*; TD-1437's *see TD-1524*; TD-1510's). `workorders.py` reads only a leading *carried to*, so each such line falls to the other-words rule and the hold reads *needs you* again. Nothing checks the words when the line is closed.

**Design PR:** #3211

**Fix:** Choose, and record in design §4.4 *The card's status*:
- the closing words are made hard to get wrong: a helper or a `--carried-to TD-n` option on the close (`ao board edit done`, agent-cadence `board_edit.py`, asked of agent-cadence on its own ledger) writes *carried to TD-n* itself, and the designer brief names it; or
- the reader takes the form sessions actually write too (*see TD-n* leading the reason) — a widening of `workorders.py`'s parse that the design must say, since *see TD-n* is also how other closes point at unrelated entries (the Added-date test already limits it to an entry filed for the answer).
Either way, mend the four standing lines' readings (a line's close is not re-edited by hand: the helper, or the reader's widening, covers them), and check whether **Lift the hold** on an answered, carried hold should leave the ⋯ (the design keeps it there today, *Lift the hold is in the ⋯*).

**Done when:** A bought round's closed end-of-rounds line holds its entries as *answered — waiting on <holder>* (or *on TD-m*) on the Queue card until that round's entry archives, with no *held until your decision* item; TD-1470's card reads so while TD-1525 runs.
