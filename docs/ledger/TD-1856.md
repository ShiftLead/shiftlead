## TD-1856: A restart waiting on the person reads stalled on its entry's Queue card

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-14 (paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 44f0377b786b410fa4dfc09fee1a5e17

**Why:** On 2026-11-14 Paul asked whether grinder-ao-1 was actually stalled (Add entry, m-839271b72f66, with a screenshot of TD-1711's Queue card). The card's rows for TD-1774 and TD-1775 read *stalled*, and so did the card. The session was not stuck. At 2026-11-13 22:16Z it reached its context limit with TD-1775's PR #3747 pushed, and asked for a restart. Because nothing merged in that run, the home marked the restart `early` (design §4.9a *Early is decided from the record*), and neither its controller (its manager) nor the tick acts on an early restart. It had waited a day for Paul's **Restart** or **Resume** on the Inbox's restart row. The card did not say so. Design §4.4 *A row's state* reads an idle unattended holder as *stalled*, the team's word for its techlead that "asks the person for nothing". And §4.5 screen 6 *One place for each thing that needs the person* keeps a restart row in the Inbox, with only the holder's pill as a mirror. Only a stopped holder's five state rows move to the entry's card. So work that is current and waits on one press from the person read on its card as a team's problem. The press that unblocks it sat on another page. This goes against Paul's 2026-11-06 rule (TD-1453): *anything that affects current work goes to the Queue, on its entry's card*.

**Location:** docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md (A row's state; The card's status); docs/design/4.5-ui.md screen 6 (One place for each thing that needs the person); docs/design/4.5a-controls.md (Inbox row: restart; Queue card: holder's state row); docs/design/4.9a-winding-down.md (Early is decided from the record); src/sessionorc/queue.py (row state, card status, stopped holder's rows)

**Fix:** A design round in §4.4 (*A row's state*, *The card's status*) and §4.5 screen 6 (*One place for each thing that needs the person*) and §4.5a (*Queue card: holder's state row*, *Inbox row: restart*). The direction to weigh: a restart row that waits on the person (an `early` or `repeat` `restart_wanted`, and likely `restart_ceiling` and `restart_blocked`) for a seat whose live claim an open card holds moves to that card as a **Do** item, as a stopped holder's state row does (TD-1453), with the row's own **Restart** and **Resume**. The rows that seat holds read what waits instead of *stalled*: *restart wanted · early*, as the chip says. The card's status is then *needs you*, not *stalled*. The Inbox keeps the restart row of a seat holding no open card's entry. The round settles which restart reasons move, which claim a seat holding several entries draws its row on (the stopped holder's rule), and whether *stalled* should also stop being read where any Inbox row waits on the person for that holder.

**Done when:** The design says where a restart that waits on the person is drawn when its session holds an open card's entry, and what that card's rows and status read; its build has landed; and a card like TD-1711's on 2026-11-14 reads that it waits on the person, with the Restart and Resume presses on the card, not *stalled*.
