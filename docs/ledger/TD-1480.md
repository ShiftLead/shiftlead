## TD-1480: Move a question already waiting on a seat that turns unable: rerouted, the landing's holds, --thread follows

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 855ec50042be435aaf16e39b6d78813d
**Blocked by:** TD-1473
**Part of:** TD-1443
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (the re-route pass); src/sessionorc/models.py (CLOSED_REASONS); src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py (_question_to_take_up); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py (waits on lines); design §4.9b, §4.10, §4.5a

**Why:** TD-1443's design, split from TD-1473 (round 1, Sonnet: one build carried too much). Nothing re-routes an ask already waiting on a seat that turns unable; it waits until the worker's `TECHLEAD_WAIT` pass-up, or forever for a person's handed entry.

**Fix:** Design §4.9b *A seat that cannot answer is routed around* (*A question already waiting*, the handed entry's wait, `--thread` follows the move), §4.10 *One way of being closed*, §4.5a *Inbox row: routed around* (its *waits on* lines) (TD-1443). Uses TD-1473's `seat_able` and routing rules.
- A tick pass (src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py) moves only a general question (no `pr`, no `entry`), a reader's `ask` (to the person), and a handed entry with a blank pick waiting on its seat; never an `entry` request with the person's team pick or a session's own ledger request, which stay until their seat is able. Each such open `ask` to a seat that is not able closes on every copy `rerouted` (`CLOSED_REASONS`, src/sessionorc/models.py), and a copy with the same `root`, text, `pr`, `about`, bound (none for a handed one), `handed` mark, `entry` (its `team` rewritten to the new team's on a blank pick's move; `entry.picked`, written by TD-1473, tells a blank pick from the person's) and `draft` lands with the new addressee by TD-1473's rules, carrying `routed`, with no bound when the addressee is the person (a reader's `review.bound` does not follow it) and the seat's own bound where it lands on a seat. A `steer` is not moved: it lapses to its default.
- The move is a landing, not a send: it passes no gate, then counts as any open ask; where a depth, *one open question per sender per seat*, or *One open copy* (TD-1379) would refuse it, the ask stays on its seat, retried each tick, saying *waits on <name>: <the refusal>* — on the person's Inbox row for the person's own question or handed entry, and for a session's on the addressee's Focus Inbox card, the sender's Sent card and its `ao inbox --sent`. A handed entry with a blank pick waiting for an able techlead is moved the first tick any servicing team's is.
- A late reply on a `rerouted` thread closes nothing.
- `--thread` follows the move (`_question_to_take_up`, src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py): on a `rerouted` entry it takes the open copy on the same `root`; one already with the person is refused as *passed up — the person holds it*.

**Done when:** Tests: an ask already waiting on a seat that turns `limited` closes `rerouted` on every copy and its copy lands on the same thread with the techlead or the person, carrying `routed`; a `steer` waiting on it is left to lapse; where the sender already holds an open question to the person on the same `about`, it stays, reads *waits on <name>: …*, and moves once that question closes; a handed entry's moved copy keeps `entry` (its `team` the new team's), `draft` and no bound; a handed entry with a blank pick waiting on a limited techlead moves the first tick another servicing team's techlead is able; one with the person's pick, and a session's own ledger request, are never moved; a reader's `ask` moved to the person carries no bound; the seat's late reply closes nothing; `--thread` on the rerouted original takes the copy, and on one already with the person is refused.
