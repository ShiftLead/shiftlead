## TD-1903: A Start session prompt carries the card as ao card prints it, and drops I drive this one

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-16
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** eff4e53c47804c6a84e26c345b7da2d1

**Why:** A **Start session** on a Queue card or an Inbox ledger row sends the session only two lists of ids (*waits on me*, *the rest*), so the session re-reads every entry to learn what the person already saw on the card: titles, state words, the items' questions and answers, and a decided line's *decided* words. On 2026-11-16 a session started on TD-1829 asked Paul a question his board item had already answered, because the prompt said only *it waits on me (it is blocked by a decision of mine)*. Paul (2026-11-16, in his own session): drop *Then wait for me: I drive this one.*, superfluous in an interactive session; keep the typed first line, which some sessions need to trust the rest; and give the session the card's full information, as `ao card` already prints it.

**Fix:** Design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Start session* (the opening prompt), §4.5a *Inbox row: ledger entry*, §4.7 *`ao card`*, §4.1 *The typed line* (TD-1903).

1. **One renderer** (`src/shiftlead/cardtext.py`, new): move `_card_text`, `_card_item_lines`, `_card_row_line`, `_card_time`, `_answer_lines`, `MAIL_WORDS_CUT` and `CARD_NOT_MAIL` out of `src/shiftlead/cli.py` unchanged (they need only `datetime`, `Mapping` and `Any`), `_card_text` as `card_text(got, cut: bool | None = None)`, importing nothing from `shiftlead.ui`. `cli.py` keeps its names (`from shiftlead.cardtext import card_text as _card_text`, and the rest likewise), so `cmd_card`, `tests/test_cli_card.py` and the `test_stuck_*` files run unchanged and `ao card`'s output is byte-for-byte what it was. Beside them, `CARD_PROMPT_CAP = 12000` and `card_block(got, id_) -> list[str]`: the data sentence, a blank line, `card_text(got, cut=True)` cut where the next item block or row would pass the cap (whole blocks and rows only) with the line *… cut; `ao card <id>` prints the rest*, a blank line, *— end of the card —*.
2. **The template** (`src/shiftlead/briefs/work.md`, `repoconfig.work_text`): the closing sentence loses *Then wait for me: I drive this one.*; a slot `{card}` sits between the id-list sentence and the closing sentence. `work_text` gains the keyword `card: Sequence[str] = ()` (positional callers, `tests/test_queue_start_all.py`, unchanged), and fills `{card}` **last**, after `{opening}`, `{mine}` and `{rest}`, so a card line holding `{mine}` is never rewritten; with no card the slot and its blank lines vanish and the prompt reads as today but for the dropped sentence.
3. **The road** (`src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`, the `/api/ledger/start` handler): the card is read last, after the holder and name checks and the role's resolve, just before `work_text`: `h.card(<the card's id>, words=False)` (the read `/api/queue/card` serves), passed through `json.loads(json.dumps(got, default=str))` as that endpoint does, and used only where the answer is a dict with a `card` key; `None`, `{completed}`, `{ambiguous}` or any exception (`contextlib.suppress(Exception)`) leaves it out, never refusing the press. Not read on a kept card's press. The Inbox row's press reads the card that holds its entry the same way, where one does. **The form road** (`ledger_start_form`) gets the prompt built with `card=()`, since it travels in a GET URL.
4. **Tests**: `tests/test_ui_inbox_ledger.py` (~476-483), `tests/test_queue_start.py` (~166, ~207) and `tests/test_queue_kept_start.py` (~77) expect the closing sentence without *I drive this one*; `test_queue_start.py`'s fake world serves `list` and the inbox for `card_mail`, so a press on a card with a Decide board line and a decided line carries the card block (bracketed by the data sentence and *— end of the card —*, its lines equal to `card_text` over the same `got`, a mail item's words absent), never passing vacuously; an oversize card is cut at a block boundary with the *… cut* line; a card line holding `{mine}` survives; a kept card's press, an Inbox entry on no card, a failing or `{ambiguous}` card read, and the form road's URL carry no card block.
5. History: a line in `docs/history/TD-1903.md` under each section the build changes.

**Done when:** A **Start session** prompt carries the card as `ao card` prints it under a pane, between the id lists and the closing sentence, marked as data, bounded at `CARD_PROMPT_CAP`, with no mail's words and none on the form road, and no longer says *Then wait for me: I drive this one.* `ao card` and the prompt share one renderer. Tests hold the card block, its cut, its absence where there is no card, and the dropped sentence.
