## TD-2298: A session closed to move to another login shows no reason on its card

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 8d9433fd9e454acf8311d3e7fc0d345f

**Why:** Docs audit of #4790 (TD-2069 slice 2). Design §4.5 says a close of the home's reads "*closed by the tick · <why>*" and lists the words: *team finished*, *for a restart*, *brief changed* and *flow changed*, *cache lapsed*, *flow sat it out*, *seat done*, *after <presser>'s Wind down* (`docs/design/4.5-ui.md`, the card's ending).

#4790 added a close of the tick's with closer why `login-move` (`_move_login`, `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`). Neither that list nor `TICK_WHY` (`src/shiftlead/ending.py`) names it. Where the launch on the next login fails, the record stays closed (§4.9e: "A launch that fails clears the mark with the session ended"), and the card's ending says no why.

Probes at b82ce3f36:

- `ending.closer_words` on a record closed by `{by: tick, why: login-move}` answers `closed by the tick`.
- `restarted_view` (`src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`) on the failed entry answers `restarted · login-move · failed`, the why as written, so the only word of a reason is the internal one.
- The same bare `closed by the tick` answers for other whys the code writes and §4.5's list does not name: `lost-brief`, `extra_held`, `host_full`, `retired`, `extra_dropped`, and the three sentences `stage_start.end` is handed (*its stage record no longer names it*, *its audit's run record no longer names it*, *its job record no longer names it*; `src/shiftlead/stage_start.py` and its callers). What each of those records' cards draws besides the ending was not checked.

The **restarted** chip and `ao status -v` print a restart's why as written, which is their documented behaviour; this entry is about the ending's words alone.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ending.py TICK_WHY; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py _move_login; docs/design/4.5-ui.md the card's ending

**Fix:** Give `login-move` words in §4.5's list and in `ending.TICK_WHY` (suggested: *to move to another login*). For each other why the Why names (`lost-brief`, `extra_held`, `host_full`, `retired`, `extra_dropped` and the three record sentences of `stage_start.end`), either give words the same way or have §4.5 say that its close reads bare, and why. Add the history line to this entry's history file under §4.5.

**Done when:** `closer_words` on a record closed by the tick for `login-move` reads *closed by the tick ·* and words §4.5 lists, held by a test, and each other why the Why names either does the same or is named in §4.5 as reading bare.
