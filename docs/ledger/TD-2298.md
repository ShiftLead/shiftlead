## TD-2298: A session closed to move to another login shows no reason on its card

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 8d9433fd9e454acf8311d3e7fc0d345f

**Why:** Docs audit of #4790 (TD-2069 slice 2). Design §4.5 says a close of the home's reads "*closed by the tick · <why>*" and lists the words: *team finished*, *for a restart*, *brief changed* and *flow changed*, *cache lapsed*, *flow sat it out*, *seat done*, *after <presser>'s Wind down* (`docs/design/4.5-ui.md`, the card's ending).

#4790 added a close of the tick's with closer why `login-move` (`_move_login`, `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`). Neither that list nor `TICK_WHY` (`src/shiftlead/ending.py`) names it. Where the launch on the next login fails, the record stays closed (§4.9e: "A launch that fails clears the mark with the session ended"), and the card's ending says no why.

Probes at b82ce3f36:

- `ending.closer_words` on a record closed by `{by: tick, why: login-move}` answers `closed by the tick`.
- `restarted_view` (`src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`) on the failed entry answers `restarted · login-move · failed`, the why as written, so the only word of a reason is the internal one.
- The same bare `closed by the tick` answers for the whys `lost-brief`, `extra_held` and `host_full`, which the code also writes and §4.5's list does not name either. What a held seat's card draws for the last two was not checked.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ending.py TICK_WHY; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py _move_login; docs/design/4.5-ui.md the card's ending

**Fix:** Give `login-move` words in §4.5's list and in `ending.TICK_WHY` (suggested: *to move to another login*). Do the same for `lost-brief` (suggested: *brief lost*), and for `extra_held` and `host_full` either give words or have §4.5 say why their close reads bare. Add the history line to this entry's history file under §4.5.

**Done when:** `closer_words` on a record closed by the tick for `login-move` reads *closed by the tick ·* and words §4.5 lists, held by a test, and each of `lost-brief`, `extra_held` and `host_full` either does the same or is named in §4.5 as reading bare.
