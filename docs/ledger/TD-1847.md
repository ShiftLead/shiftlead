**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open

**Why:** TD-1845's design (design §4.5 screen 12 *An item's shape*, §4.5a *Inbox row: passed up*): an `ask` or `steer` to the person now carries its consequences on its answers and opens its context in its second paragraph, and a question passed up to the person leads with the passer's plain question (`recommend.question`, TD-1846), the asker's text folded — Paul's rule 5: a seat's text is not written for him.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/ (inbox_row.html card mode, the row feeding it); src/sessionorc/queue.py

**Fix:** 1. The card renderer (`inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode and what feeds it): an `ask` or `steer`'s consequences are its answers' (the `consequences` reader already splits them); its second paragraph on is drawn under *context*, no longer as the consequences — mail filed before TD-1846 draws its old consequences paragraph under *context* too, with no migration.
2. A passed-up entry whose `recommend.question` is set: the sentence is that question; the answers are the passer's (as today); *context* holds first *`<passer>` recommends: `<line>`*, drawn as text, then the asker's whole text under a *from `<asker>`* label. A director's and a techlead's passes draw both recommendations, the director's first, as today. With no `recommend.question`, as today.
3. The Inbox row (§4.5a *Inbox row: passed up*) leads with the passer's question where the pass carries one, the asker's text under *details*.
4. Screenshot the card and the Inbox row with a passed-up entry (Playwright, a scratch home) and commit the shot under `docs/mockups/reviews/`, named in the PR.

**Done when:** A passed-up ask carrying `recommend.question` is drawn on its Queue card and Inbox row with the passer's question first and the asker's text folded; an ask with `--answer "Pin it — a session pins it"` draws *a session pins it* beside its button and its second paragraph under *context*; one without `recommend.question` draws as before; the screenshot is in the PR; `pdm run test` green.
