**Why:** TD-1845's design (design §4.5 screen 12 *An item's shape*, §4.5a *Inbox row: passed up*): an `ask` or `steer` to the person now carries its consequences on its answers and opens its context in its second paragraph, and a question passed up to the person leads with the passer's plain question (`recommend.question`, TD-1846), the asker's text folded — Paul's rule 5: a seat's text is not written for him.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the plain-text checks and the Not clear answer as this design now stands?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/common.py (card_shape); src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html (card mode and its comment); src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (the passed-up row)

**Fix:** 1. `card_shape` (`src/shiftlead/ui/common.py`) and `inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode (its comment near the `leads` read too): an `ask` or `steer`'s consequences are its answers' (`mail.read_answers`' `consequences`, as today); its second paragraph on is drawn under *context*, no longer as `leads` — mail filed before draws its old consequences paragraph under *context* too, with no migration.
2. A passed-up entry whose `recommend.question` is set: the sentence is that question's first paragraph, drawn too wherever the row or card would draw the entry's `subject`; the answers are the copy's (the recommendation first, as today); *context* holds the question's own context, then *`<passer>` recommends: `<line>`*, drawn as text, then the asker's whole text under a *from `<asker>`* label, a passed-up `steer`'s inherited `default` drawn there as text. With no `recommend.question`, as today; nothing to draw until TD-1846 stores it, so this step does not wait on it.
3. A question rerouted to the person (§4.9b), or a send `_route_around` turned to the person, its text a seat's: drawn as a non-conforming mail item (its `subject` as the sentence), its text folded under *from `<asker>`*.
4. The Inbox row (§4.5a *Inbox row: passed up*; the passer's name is set in `ui/app.py`) leads with the first paragraph of the passer's question where the pass carries one, and under *details* its context, the *recommends* line, then the asker's text labelled *from `<asker>`*.
5. Screenshot a card and an Inbox row with a passed-up entry (Playwright, a scratch home) and commit the shots under `docs/mockups/reviews/`, named in the PR.

**Done when:** An ask with `--answer "Pin it — a session pins it"` draws *a session pins it* beside its button and its second paragraph under *context*; a passed-up ask carrying `recommend.question` leads with it on its card and row, the asker's text folded; one without draws as before; the shots are in the PR; `pdm run test` green.
