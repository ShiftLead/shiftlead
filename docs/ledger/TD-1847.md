**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-14

**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 18f0b66eed9c4046a9645b23a8656c81
**Blocked by:** TD-1846

**Why:** TD-1845's design (design §4.5 screen 12 *An item's shape*, §4.5a *Inbox row: passed up*): an `ask` or `steer` to the person now carries its consequences on its answers and opens its context in its second paragraph, and a question passed up to the person leads with the passer's plain question (`recommend.question`, TD-1846), the asker's text folded — Paul's rule 5: a seat's text is not written for him.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/common.py (card_shape); src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html (card mode and its comment); src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (the passed-up row)

**Fix:** 1. `card_shape` (`src/shiftlead/ui/common.py`) and `inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode (its comment near the `leads` read too): an `ask` or `steer`'s consequences are its answers' (`mail.read_answers`' `consequences`, as today); its second paragraph on is drawn under *context*, no longer as `leads` — mail filed before TD-1846 draws its old consequences paragraph under *context* too, with no migration.
2. A passed-up entry whose `recommend.question` is set: the sentence is that question's first paragraph, drawn too wherever the row or card would draw the entry's `subject`; the answers are the copy's (the recommendation first, as today); *context* holds the question's own context, then *`<passer>` recommends: `<line>`*, drawn as text, then the asker's whole text under a *from `<asker>`* label, a passed-up `steer`'s inherited `default` drawn there as text. With no `recommend.question`, as today.
3. A question rerouted to the person (§4.9b), its text a seat's: drawn as a non-conforming mail item (its `subject` as the sentence), its text folded under *from `<asker>`*.
4. The Inbox row (§4.5a *Inbox row: passed up*; the passer's name is set in `ui/app.py`) leads with the passer's question where the pass carries one, the asker's text under *details*.
5. Screenshot the card and the Inbox row with a passed-up entry (Playwright, a scratch home) and commit the shot under `docs/mockups/reviews/`, named in the PR.

**Done when:** A passed-up ask carrying `recommend.question` is drawn on its Queue card and Inbox row with the passer's question first and the asker's text folded; an ask with `--answer "Pin it — a session pins it"` draws *a session pins it* beside its button and its second paragraph under *context*; one without `recommend.question` draws as before; the screenshot is in the PR; `pdm run test` green.
