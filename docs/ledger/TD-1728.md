## TD-1728: The pair bound counts over a scaled seat's records

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** c3eb1364ef424a4cb236d5cf2d37e52c
**Part of:** TD-1671
**Location:** design §4.9 Mail to a scaled seat, §4.10 A bounded exchange; src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py _check_bounds

**Why:** TD-1671's design (§4.9 *Mail to a scaled seat*, §4.10 *A bounded exchange*) counts the pair bound between a session and a scaled seat over the seat's records: a sender whose reply-less asks fill extras spends one budget with the seat, not one per extra. Today `agent_mail._check_bounds` reads only the pair tally on the record the send picked (`_pair(me, sid)`, `_pair(records[sid], sender)`), so a sender writing to a seat with extras takes up to the seat's `max` records' worth of `PAIR_BOUND` from it.

**Fix:** In `agent_mail._check_bounds`'s pair branch (`src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`), for each named addressee `sid` other than the person, read the pair count over both seats (`mail.seat_records(records, sender)` and `mail.seat_records(records, sid)`; a record that is no seat is a seat of one):
- the addressee's side: the sum, over every record `b` of `sid`'s seat and every record `a` of the sender's seat, of the entries inside `PAIR_WINDOW` in `b`'s `pair:a` tally, each tally counted once; the sender's side the same with the roles swapped; refuse when either sum is at `PAIR_BOUND`, as one record's tally refuses now.
- where the sender and `sid` are records of one seat (the seat's own mail to itself), read their two tallies alone (`sid`'s `pair:<sender>`, the sender's `pair:<sid>`), as today — the sum over the seat would count each send twice.
- the read creates no tally: read a record's `pair:<a>` with `threads.get`, rolling its window as `_pair` does, and skip a record that has none (`_pair`'s `setdefault` stays for the write after the landing).
- the refusal names the seat by the seat's name (`records[sid].scale["line"]` for an extra, else `records[sid].name`) where `sid` is a seat record; `bound_hit` is written on every existing tally the count read, each of those records saved.
- the tallies are still written where they are now (the sender's `pair:<sid>` and `sid`'s `pair:<sender>`, `agent_mail.py` after the landing); only the read sums.
- the thread branch is unchanged (§4.9: the thread bound needs no sum between a session and a scaled seat, and stays per record otherwise).

Tests beside the seat suite (`tests/test_td_max_*.py`): a sender's reply-less asks spread over a seat's own record and an extra are refused once their sum reaches `PAIR_BOUND`, though neither record's own tally is at it; the refusal names the seat; `bound_hit` lands on each record's existing tally and a record with none gains no `pair:` key; an extra writing to the sender counts in the same budget; mail between two records of one seat counts once; a record that is no seat behaves as today.

**Done when:** `_check_bounds` counts a pair's reply-less entries over both ends' seat records as §4.9 *Mail to a scaled seat* says, and the tests above pass.
