**Why:** Found by docs-audit-ao-1 auditing #2954 (TD-1375 slice 1). The PR made a decide on a gated board item close the line in the same write (`board._write_back` passes the ledger to `plan_edit`; `tests/test_board.py::test_a_go_on_a_gated_decide_lifts_the_gate_in_the_same_write` asserts `line.startswith("- [x] ")`), and rewrote §4.4 *Board write-back* to match ("A decided item that stays open is not done"). Its twin in §4.5 screen 6 was not touched (`docs/design/4.5-ui.md`, the *Waiting on them* paragraph, ~line 823):

> **The line on the board is untouched by all of this**: it stays open and due as its session's work order (cadence §3.5), …

That is said of every decided item, but a `Go` (or `Not yet`) on an item whose `Context:` names one entry and one gate now closes the line, so it never reaches *Waiting on them*. §4.4 limits the rule ("An item whose `Context:` names no gate is answered on the board alone, as before"); §4.5 does not. Also, §4.4's Queue-card passage (~line 1796) still reads "Should TD-1375 make the press a lifting `Go`, the entry leaves on the press and that word goes with it" as a hypothetical, though slice 1 has made it so for a `Go` on a gated item.

**Fix:** Qualify the §4.5 sentence the way §4.4 does (a decided item stays open unless it is a gated decide whose answer lifts or keeps waiting; then the write closes it), and settle the §4.4 ~1796 *Should TD-1375…* sentence against what slice 1 built (the `go ahead` Lift-the-hold line names no gate, so it still stays open — say that outright). Design and its history line only.

**Done when:** §4.5 screen 6 and §4.4's Queue-card paragraph agree with §4.4 *Board write-back* on when a decided line stays open.
