## TD-2091: A switched team's page lacks its When it runs rows and the mark on its tab that it cannot run

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 5d5279d0b42b439699017187666020d8
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** Design §4.9d *The page* gives a switched team four rows under **When it runs** (Starts, Stops, Reserve priority, Holds new work), and §4.5a's *Team page* row and §4.9d *A team that cannot run* give its tab a cannot-run dot. TD-2060's last slice (#4377) draws neither: a team held only in `settings.yml` reads *after the switch-over* in that band, which is false once the cut-over has run, and its tab looks like any other when the team cannot run. Found by the techlead's read of #4377.

**Fix:** On a switched team's page, draw the four rows of §4.9d *When it runs* from the team's `settings.yml` keys, with no *When work appears*, each saved as §4.9d says. Draw the cannot-run dot on each switched team's tab, from the `team_show` reading (`entries=False` is the cheap form `ao org` uses). The switch-over's move is blocked on this entry, so no team reaches the page without them.

**Done when:** On a scratch home, a switched team's page shows the four rows and saves each, *after the switch-over* appears nowhere on it, and a team that cannot run has the dot on its tab.
