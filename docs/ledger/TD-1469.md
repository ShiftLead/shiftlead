**Fix:** Design §4.10 *A question the work settled closes itself*, *One way of being closed* (TD-1453).
- `CLOSED_REASONS` gains `merged` and `settled`; `MailEntry` gains `settled_by: int | None` (`src/sessionorc/models.py`).
- A pass on the home's tick (beside `_asker_gone`'s orphaning, `src/sessionorc/agent_attention.py`) over the person inbox's open `ask`s and `steer`s, orphaned or not: **`merged`** when the entry carries `pr` (an `ask`'s; a `steer` only through an `about` of the PR shape `#n`) and that PR is `merged` in the repo's month of PRs (`recent`, §4.4 *Repo facts*) of the entry's `repo` (TD-1467); an entry with no `repo` (sent before the stamp) takes, in order, its orphan stamp's `repo` (a path, through TD-1467's helper against the stamp's `host`'s registered checkouts), else its sender's live record through the same helper, else nothing — it settles by no merge and is the person's to close; a PR closed unmerged, or in neither list, settles nothing. **`settled`** when its `about` names an entry (bare read as the entry's `repo`'s) archived by a commit later than the question's `at` (*What closed, and by which PR*'s commit time; with none, a `Resolved:` date later than `at`'s day on the home's local calendar — the same day settles nothing without the commit's time), `settled_by` that PR's number; an entry archived before the question's `at` settles nothing.
- A question passed up on a thread (§4.9b *When it cannot answer*, `--thread`) takes the `pr` of the `ask` it closes `asked_person` where its sender gives none: the home copies it at the send, with that `ask`'s `repo` where the new entry's stamp is empty (`--thread` takes only the sender's own question, so the two agree where both exist). The copy also makes the person's reply the PR's read, as §4.9b *The bound* says: `held.read_by`'s older `{reader, held}` form names a thread's readers by the author's asks carrying `pr` (`src/sessionorc/held.py`), so the person is named only once the passed-up question carries one; `held.asks` keeps the author's earlier ask.
- A PR is always the sender's repo's: an ask about another repo's PR is never closed by a merge.
- The pass skips every entry the person sent (a handed entry's `ask` closes by its outcome alone); where both apply, `merged` wins.
- An entry archived before the question's `at` that has no completed card is TD-1468's loose card, not a close.
- The close sets `closed_at` alone, tells nobody, and a `steer` so closed applies no default and writes no lapse note. The Inbox's FYI list words it *settled — #<n> merged* or *settled — TD-n archived (#<n>)*, *(no PR)* where the archive names none; the Focus side panel's **Sent** fold (§4.5a, `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`) and `ao inbox --sent` use the same words.
- The `Resolved:` day is read on the home's local calendar, not through the Queue's `_when` (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`), which reads a date-only value as 23:59:59 UTC (F70).
- Fixed, not reviewed (round 4, F59, F61; the builder checks each): the unstamped order is TD-1467's helper's, called here, not written again; a test sends an ask at 00:05 local on the `Resolved:` day with no closing-commit time, and it settles nothing.

**Done when:** Tests: an open ask to the person with `pr: 164` and `repo: agent-cadence`, whose repo reading has #164 `merged`, closes `merged` on the tick and leaves *Needs you* and its card without the person's press; the same with #164 closed unmerged stays open; an orphaned ask about `TD-221` whose entry is archived by a commit after its `at` closes `settled` with `settled_by: 164`; one asked after the archive stays open; a `steer` closed `merged` applies no default; an asker's going alone still closes nothing that names a reference.

Also: a question passed up on the thread of an `ask` carrying `pr: 164` carries `pr: 164` and closes `merged` with it; an agent-cadence ask carrying `pr: 2996` is not closed by shiftlead's #2996 merging; a second ask about an already-merged PR closes once on the next tick; the FYI, the Sent fold and `ao inbox --sent` read *settled — #164 merged*, *settled — TD-221 archived (#164)* and *(no PR)*; a closed `merged` entry is pruned by retention from its `closed_at`; `settled_by` is written. A question about an entry the work closed closes even when it asked something else (deliberately broad, §4.10), and stays readable in FYI for retention.
On the older `{reader, held}` form, a question passed up with no `pr` of its own, once copied, is read by the person's reply (`held.read_by` returns the person), and `held.asks` keeps the author's earlier ask.
The unstamped order, one test per branch: an orphan stamp's path resolves; a live asker's record resolves; neither, and the ask stays open. A passed-up question whose closed ask carries a `repo` while its own stamp is empty takes that `repo`; a stamped one keeps its own.
An ask sent on the day of an entry's `Resolved:` with no closing-commit time is not settled by that archive; one sent the day before is.
An ask sent at 00:05 local on the `Resolved:` day, with no closing-commit time, settles nothing.
