**Why:** Part of TD-2126, design §4.6 *A live seat's rebuildable copies*, parts 2 and 3. A hand-run suite with its own `--basetemp` that fails or is killed is not recognised, and one seat held 188,046 inodes that way.

**Location:** tests/conftest.py, src/sessionorc/scratch.py, src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py, design §4.6 *A live seat's rebuildable copies*

**Fix:** In `tests/conftest.py`, the controller writes the mark and, for a caller's `--basetemp` under `SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH`, the run-list file, skipping xdist workers and a suite run inside a test. In `scratch.py` and the hourly pass, read the list with its path checks and release runs after `TEST_KEEP_S`, `pytest-current` as the design says; rule 1 reads the list too.

**Done when:** A killed or failed hand-run suite at any depth in a live seat is removed by the hourly pass two hours after its last write and by the pressure pass after ten minutes. A live run, a listed path through a symlink or outside the seat, and a nested suite are not touched.
