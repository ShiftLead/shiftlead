## TD-1349: Build a hidden board line in the Inbox's Snoozed fold, with *now*, and count a board Snooze from today

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (the designer, from TD-1345's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1345
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`board_rows`, which drops an item whose `needs` is None; `person_sections`, the snoozed list and its order); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the `board` macro's **Snooze ▾** menu and its title *move its Due: date on the board*, the macro's comment; the fold's rows); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`boardDue`, its base date and comment); `src/sessionorc/board.py` (`write_back`'s `snooze` with no date: the home's today); the board route in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`; tests under `tests/`.

**Why:** since TD-1330 a board Snooze writes `Hide until:`, and two choices made when it moved `Due:` read wrongly: +1 week counts from the later of today and the item's `Due:`, so a line due in 3 days is hidden until it has been overdue 4 days; and a hidden line is drawn nowhere until its date, where a snoozed mail entry sits in the **Snoozed (n)** fold (TD-1345).

**Fix:** as design §4.5a rows *Due strip / Inbox board row: Snooze ▾* and *Inbox: **Snoozed (n)** fold*, §4.4 *Board write-back*, §4.10 *Snooze*:
1. `boardDue` counts +1 day, +1 week and *a date…*'s offered tomorrow from today, never from the item's `Due:`; the menu's title says it hides the line until the date.
2. `board_rows` keeps an open item whose `needs` is None and whose `hidden` is true as a snoozed row (its `hide_until` the row's *snoozed until*), and the Inbox draws it in the **Snoozed (n)** fold, soonest first among the mail entries and state rows, its date the start of that day on the home's clock; uncounted, in no section, no rail group, no Queue card, no *Not due yet* or *not shown* fold; the find unfolds it.
3. The fold's board row is the `board` macro's row with its id, due words (*overdue n days* once its `Due:` has passed), **Open board**, **Reply**, its answers and **Go with it**, **Done**, and **Snooze ▾** with *now* first, drawn whatever Queue card names its entry.
4. *now* sends the `snooze` write-back with no date; the write-back writes the home's today as `Hide until:` (ledgerboard reads the item as shown, `hidden` being `today < hide_until`), the commit subject ledgerboard's *snooze <head> to <today>*.
5. Tests: a line hidden past its `Due:` is in the fold, uncounted, its due words overdue; +1 week from a line due in 3 days is today + 7; *now* writes today and the next reading draws the line in its section.

**Done when:** a board line snoozed from the Inbox sits in the **Snoozed (n)** fold until its date with its controls, *now* brings it back, and +1 day / +1 week count from today.

**Related:** TD-1330 (ledgerboard v2.3, `Hide until:`); TD-371 (a snooze is a date); TD-220 (the old base date).
