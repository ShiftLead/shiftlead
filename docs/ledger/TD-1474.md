**Location:** src/shiftlead/adapters/gemini/screen_rules.toml; src/sessionorc/screen.py (Manifest.load, Rule, Match); src/sessionorc/models.py (limited_reset, NODE_OWNED, the record's view); src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (the spell's first tick); design §4.3

**Why:** TD-1443's design, second build. Gemini CLI's *Usage limit reached for <model>* is the free tier's per-model daily quota (Paul, m-5bea108c7323): Keep trying spends nothing back, and its screen rule gives no reset, so nothing can tell it from a short rate limit.

**Fix:** Design §4.3 (the Gemini quota box, *An adapter names its volatile rows* is TD-1475's) (TD-1443).
- A screen rule may carry `resets = "daily HH:MM <IANA zone>"` (src/shiftlead/adapters/*/screen_rules.toml, the rule loader); a matching verdict's pending text gains *resets HH:MMZ*, the next such time in UTC, which `seat_able` (TD-1473) reads as the reset.
- The loader (`Manifest.load`, src/sessionorc/screen.py) reads named keys only: `Rule` and `Match` gain `resets`, the pending text is built from it, and `Match.to_dict` and `explain` show it.
- `gemini-usage-limit` (src/shiftlead/adapters/gemini/screen_rules.toml) carries `resets = "daily 00:00 America/Los_Angeles"`.
- A rule with no `resets` keeps giving an unknown reset.
- **`limited_reset`** (on the record, node-owned): set at the first tick of a `limited` spell from the screen rule's `resets` (the first such local time after that tick, `zoneinfo`; a time in a DST gap moves forward, one in a fold takes the first) or from `_cap`'s reset, and kept until the state leaves `limited`, so a daily reset is not recomputed into tomorrow; the tick rewrites the pending's *resets HH:MMZ* from it once set, so the card, the routed lines and TD-1473's `seat_able` read one time.
- `limited_reset` and TD-1475's `stalled_ticks` are in the record's view the home reads from a node (beside `subagent_at`, `NODE_OWNED`, models.py).

**Done when:** Tests: the Gemini quota box at 2026-11-06T20:00Z reads `limited` with *resets 08:00Z* (midnight Pacific, PST); a rule with no `resets` gives none.
A box first seen at 23:50 PT (07:50Z) holds `limited_reset` 08:00Z, and at 00:10 PT with the box still up the record still carries 08:00Z (passed) and the card's pending reads *resets 08:00Z*; 2026-03-07T20:00Z gives 08:00Z and 2026-03-08T20:00Z gives 07:00Z.
