## TD-1546: td-max: the home starts another worker when every worker on a line is busy and work waits, and stops it when the work runs out

**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** cc32a4bacbe7419090add31ca92f3005
**Blocked by:** TD-1545, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1529
**Location:** design §6 rule 14 *Scale with the work*, §4.4 *Team shape*; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py, src/sessionorc/agent_shape.py, src/sessionorc/shape.py

**Why:** td-max's worker half (TD-1529, Paul 2026-11-07: *if the grinders are busy and there is work that can be picked, another grinder would spin up … they would spin down when work was no longer available*): today a line runs its size whether its lane is backed up or empty, so pickable entries wait while every grinder holds a lease. This carries TD-1154's `auto` size for worker lines.

**Ask:** Go once you and the designer agree td-max's shape (floor, ceiling, what triggers scaling, what the card says) and its review rounds end?

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 14 *Scale with the work*: on each shape sample (§4.4 *Team shape*), a worker line with a held extra is **short** when its stage's queue holds work and every sampled member of the stage is `working` or `on its claim` (a member `waiting:review` makes it not short), in three samples running (`SCALE_AFTER`, a count `agent_shape` keeps per stage); short, the tick fills the lowest-placed held extra (`create` from its launch record with `keep_mail`), one at a time per stage (the first line in record order with a held extra; no other fill while one it filled has no claim and is under ten minutes old, `SCALE_STEP`); nothing filled under a balance mark, on a gated profile, with the stage unstaffed, during a wind-down, or past `scale.live` live extras on the host. An extra out of work (`progress none`), idle `SCALE_IDLE_GRACE` (five minutes, from the later of `since`, `subagent_at`, `shells_at`), clean, with no subagent or shell and no claim waiting on its own PR within `SEAT_PR_WAIT`, is closed back to held, not marked sat out. Rule 6 sends an extra no new-work note. Each fill and close writes `scale.last: {at, did, why}` on the record.

**Done when:** With a line backed up for three samples and a held extra, the tick fills exactly one extra and fills no second until the first claims or ten minutes pass; each named guard holds a fill back; an extra that declares out of work is back to held five minutes later while its kept siblings run on; a team whose kept members and live extras are finished winds down; tests hold each.
