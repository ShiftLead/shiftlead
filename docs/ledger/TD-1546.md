## TD-1546: td-max: the home starts another worker when every worker on a line is busy and work waits, and stops it when the work runs out

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-07
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** cc32a4bacbe7419090add31ca92f3005
**Blocked by:** TD-1545, decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1529
**Location:** design §6 rule 14 *Scale with the work*, §4.4 *Team shape*; src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py, src/sessionorc/agent_shape.py, src/sessionorc/shape.py

**Why:** td-max's worker half (TD-1529, Paul 2026-11-07: *if the grinders are busy and there is work that can be picked, another grinder would spin up … they would spin down when work was no longer available*): today a line runs its size whether its lane is backed up or empty, so pickable entries wait while every grinder holds a lease. This carries TD-1154's `auto` size for worker lines.

**Ask:** Go once you and the designer agree td-max's shape (floor, ceiling, what triggers scaling, what the card says) and its review rounds end?

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 14 *Scale with the work*, up on need and down with no idle timer (Paul, 2026-11-07). **Up, at events, never on a timer**: the home asks whether a worker line with a held extra is **short** when an entry becomes pickable in its lane (filed, or its block dropped: a new home-side reading of the team's lanes — `ledger.in_lanes`, §4.4 *In a team's lanes*, read as `agent_shape` reads it — gaining an id it did not hold, kept beside rule 8's `lane_seen`) and when a member of the line claims (`ao progress claim`) while the lane still holds a pickable entry nobody holds, and at the events that clear what held a fill back (a start of the team or a raised `max`, an extra closing, a session closing on the host, the balance mark or the usage gate lifting, the guard's ten minutes passing): while a line is short with a fill held back (newest `scale.last` `stopped`, or a guard standing) each tick reads it again until it fills or is no longer short. Nobody holds: no live record at the home holding a lease on it, declared or derived, not `done` or `dropped`: the lease's test as `ao progress claim` reads it). Short: the lane holds a pickable entry nobody holds and every live member of the line holds a lease (a claim declared or derived, not `done` or `dropped`, a waiting claim included); a member holding no lease is free and picks; a member `limited` or paused by the usage gate with no lease is neither free nor busy, so a line of members busy or so held is short (the gate still holds an extra on the gated profile); a kept member rule 1 is restarting counts as free. Short, the home fills the line's lowest-placed held extra (`create` from its launch record with `keep_mail`). A line's extras' fills count toward a `FILL_CEILING` group of their own, the line's extras together. **One guard**: no second extra on a line until the last one filled has claimed, a closed extra holding no guard; ten minutes with no claim (`SCALE_STEP`) lifts the guard as a stuck start, its `scale.last` `did: unstuck`, *not claimed in 10 min*. Nothing filled under a balance mark, on a gated profile, with the line unstaffed, during a wind-down, or with the host at `scale.live` (every live session on the host, TD-1545's reader), where the home first asks TD-1545's eviction of an idle extra seat for the room and, with none, writes *stopped: host full, <n> live* (`did: stopped`) on the line's lowest-placed held extra. **Down**: an extra that declares out of work (`progress none`) is closed back to held at once once clean (nothing dirty or unpushed, no live subagent or shell, no claim waiting on its own PR within `SEAT_PR_WAIT`), else at the first tick it is; not marked sat out. No `SCALE_AFTER`, no `SCALE_IDLE_GRACE`, no shape-sample count. Rule 6 sends an extra no new-work note. **The shape** (§4.4 *Team shape*): a live worker extra is sampled as a member of its line's stage (`shape.stage_of` reads it by its `lane`, unchanged); a held extra, closed with no pane, is not sampled. Each fill, close and stopped fill writes `scale.last: {at, did: fill | hold | stopped | unstuck, why}` on the record.

**Done when:** With a line whose every live member holds a lease and a held extra, an entry becoming pickable fills exactly one extra at that tick (no wait), and a member's claim with pickable work left does the same; a free member (no lease) means no fill; no second fill until the first claims, ten minutes pass with no claim, or the first closes; a line whose free members are all `limited` is short; each named guard holds a fill back, the host cap writing *stopped: host full*; an extra that declares out of work is back to held at the first clean tick while its kept siblings run on; a team whose kept members and live extras are finished winds down; the shape samples a live extra in its line's stage and never a held one; a fill held back by the host cap fills at the tick after a session on the host closes, with no claim needed; a member awaiting rule 1's restart means no fill; tests hold each.
