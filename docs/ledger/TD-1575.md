**Why:** A held seat (TD-1564, design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held*) keeps the questions sent to a stopped team in its mailbox until the team's Start; the person must see that they wait, on the seat's card, the team card and the team-start row, or the questions wait in silence.

**Ask:** Should TD-1564's builds (TD-1574, TD-1575: a stopped team's seats are held until its Start) go ahead as written after four review rounds ended at the cap, get one more round, or is this the wrong design (Hold, with a note)? The board's TD-1564 decide item asks it; Go lifts this gate.

**Fix:** Build design §4.5a card **held** line, team card **work waiting** note and *Inbox row: team start* (TD-1564), from the home's `stop_held` (on the record view) and `work_waiting`'s `seats_held` (TD-1574; never its `held`, the start bound `work_held()` reads):
1. **The seat's card**: in `src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py` and `templates/card.html` (both the compact card and the full one, as the **routed around** line is drawn), one line under the state: *held: team stopped (by you | at its stop time) — n questions wait for Start*, n the record's `asks_waiting` (as `seats_held` counts it), *held: team stopped — waits for Start* with none, *wound down* in place of *stopped* where the team reads so (the team group's `wound_down`, which the cards already carry); gone once `stop_held` is.
2. **The team card's note** (`inbox.work_note` in `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`, drawn by `templates/group_head.html`, attached in `src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py`'s `team_groups`, whose gate `row.wound_down or _stopped(mark)` must let a mark with `seats_held` through): *· n questions wait on <seat name>* per seat `seats_held` names (keyed by seat id: `work_note` is given the page's records to resolve the name, the id where none is found); for a held-only mark it returns the held clauses alone (today it returns None with no lane ids), the *n entries waiting* clause dropped where `n` is 0.
3. **The team-start row** (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`): a row adds *· n questions wait on <seat name>* after its entries, one per `seats_held` key. A mark with `seats_held` and no lane ids — let through the row's `if not ids and not asked: continue` and `work_ids` — draws a **held-only** row: *`<team>` · stopped <t> · n questions wait on <seat name>* (*wound down* in place of *stopped* where the mark carries no `stopped` stamp), **Start** and **Snooze ▾** alone, no Dismiss (the control set drawn in `templates/inbox_row.html`), under *Later* for the mark's `seats_held_by` `person` and under *Needs you*, counted, for `until`; a row with lane ids sits as it does without `seats_held` (by `stopped.by`, a wound-down one under *Needs you*), ending *· not started: questions alone start no team* under `on_work: start`. On a row with lane ids, **Dismiss** clears the entries and the row keeps its held clause while `seats_held` stands; the Dismiss help paragraph in `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` gains one sentence, *questions waiting on a held seat stay on the row until the Start* (the design leaves the help list's wording to this build).
