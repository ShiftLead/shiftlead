**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py; src/shiftlead/ui/templates/card.html; src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py; src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py; src/shiftlead/ui/help.py (Dismiss, team start); design §4.5a

**Why:** A held seat (TD-1564, design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held*) keeps the questions sent to a stopped team in its mailbox until the team's Start; the person must see that they wait, on the seat's card, the team card and the team-start row, or the questions wait in silence.

**Fix:** Build design §4.5a card **held** line, team card **work waiting** note and *Inbox row: team start* (TD-1564), from the home's `stop_held` (on the record view) and `work_waiting`'s `seats_held` (TD-1574; never its `held`, the start bound `work_held()` reads):
1. **The seat's card**: in `src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py` and `templates/card.html` (both the compact card and the full one, as the **routed around** line is drawn), one line under the state: *held: team stopped (by you | at its stop time) — n questions wait for Start*, n the record's `asks_waiting` (as `seats_held` counts it), *held: team stopped — waits for Start* with none, *wound down* in place of *stopped* where the team reads so (the team group's `wound_down`, which the cards already carry); gone once `stop_held` is.
2. **The team card's note** (`src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py`, the work-waiting note): *· n questions wait on <seat name>* per seat `seats_held` names.
3. **The team-start row** (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`): a row adds *· n questions wait on <seat name>* after its entries, one per `seats_held` key. A mark with `seats_held` and no lane ids — let through the row's `if not ids and not asked: continue` and `work_ids` — draws a **held-only** row: *`<team>` · stopped <t> · n questions wait on <seat name>* (*wound down* in place of *stopped* where the mark carries no `stopped` stamp), **Start** and **Snooze ▾** alone, no Dismiss, under *Later* for the team's word `person` and under *Needs you*, counted, for `until` (the word `stopped.by`, else the seat's `stop_held.by`), ending *· not started: questions alone start no team* under `on_work: start`. On a row with lane ids, **Dismiss** clears the entries and the row keeps its held clause while `seats_held` stands; the Dismiss help paragraph in `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` gains one sentence, *questions waiting on a held seat stay on the row until the Start* (the design leaves the help list's wording to this build).

**Done when:** Tests: a seat view carrying `stop_held` draws the held line with its count (and the no-question form) on both cards and none without it; a `work_waiting` mark with `seats_held` adds the phrase to the team card's note and the team-start row, and a mark with `seats_held` alone draws the row; a mark's `held` (the start bound) draws as before; the row stays under *Later* for `by: person`; a held-only row draws no Dismiss and sits by `stop_held.by`; the card's line reads *wound down* for a wound-down team.
