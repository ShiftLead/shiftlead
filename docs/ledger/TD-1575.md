**Why:** A held seat (TD-1564, design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held*) keeps the questions sent to a stopped team in its mailbox until the team's Start; the person must see that they wait, on the seat's card, the team card and the team-start row, or the questions wait in silence.

**Fix:** Build design §4.5a card **held** line, team card **work waiting** note and *Inbox row: team start* (TD-1564), from the home's `seat_held` (on the record view) and `work_waiting`'s `seats_held` (TD-1574; never its `held`, the start bound `work_held()` reads):
1. **The seat's card**: in `src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py` and `templates/card.html` (both the compact card and the full one, as the **routed around** line is drawn), one line under the state: *held: team stopped (by you | by its stop time) — n questions wait for Start*, n the seat's open asks and steers, *held: team stopped — waits for Start* with none; gone once `seat_held` is.
2. **The team card's note** (`src/shiftlead/ui/repo.py`, the work-waiting note): *· n questions wait on <seat name>* per seat `seats_held` names.
3. **The team-start row** (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`): the same phrase after its entries, and, for a mark with `seats_held` and no ids — the row's `if not ids and not asked: continue` (and `work_ids`) letting it through — the row reads *`<team>` · stopped <t> · n questions wait on <seat name>*; its section unchanged (*Later* for the person's Stop).

**Done when:** Tests: a seat view carrying `seat_held` draws the held line with its count (and the no-question form) on both cards and none without it; a `work_waiting` mark with `seats_held` adds the phrase to the team card's note and the team-start row, and a mark with `seats_held` alone draws the row; a mark's `held` (the start bound) draws as before; the row stays under *Later* for `by: person`.
