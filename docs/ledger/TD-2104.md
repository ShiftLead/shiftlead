## TD-2104: Clear local branches and stale refs that have already landed

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d479a6810d64484fbb956ab18ca5b62a
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: §4.1 *The branches* and `gitinfo.weigh_branches` delete a branch when its session closes and origin has held its tip, but §4.6's sweep deletes none, so a branch that never went through a close stays. Read on 2026-11-23 in shiftlead: 377 local branches whose upstream is gone, and 2,699 refs under `refs/remotes/origin-all/`, dated September and October, which no code or configured remote uses. They may account for much of the 654 MB pack; that is not yet measured. Other repos hold fewer branches (ledgerboard has 153).

**Location:** src/sessionorc/worktreesweep.py, design §4.6 *The disk sweep*

**Fix:** Design this first, as an extension of §4.1's close-time rule to §4.6's sweep, whose text says *nothing here deletes a branch*. A branch is removed only with proof that its work landed: origin has held its tip, or its merged PR's head equals the branch tip. `git cherry` is no proof here, since a squash merge changes the patch id. Never while a worktree has it checked out, and a branch with any unproven commit is kept and listed. The `origin-all` refs and other one-off refs (`refs/recovered`, `refs/scratch`, `refs/techlead`, the two stashes) go to the person as a list to approve, not to a sweep.

**Done when:** A branch with proven landed work and no worktree is removed; one with any unlanded commit is kept and named. The person has approved or declined removing the one-off refs, and the pack size before and after is recorded.
