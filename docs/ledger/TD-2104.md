## TD-2104: Clear local branches and stale refs that have already landed

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d479a6810d64484fbb956ab18ca5b62a
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: nothing removes a local branch after its squash merge, and the design says the sweep deletes none. Read on 2026-11-23 in shiftlead: 377 local branches whose upstream is gone, and 2,699 refs under `refs/remotes/origin-all/`, dated September and October, which no code or configured remote uses. They may account for much of the 654 MB pack; that is not yet measured. Other repos hold fewer branches (ledgerboard has 153).

**Location:** src/sessionorc/worktreesweep.py, design §4.6 *The disk sweep*

**Fix:** Design this first, since it reverses *nothing here deletes a branch*. A branch is removed only with proof that its work landed (`git cherry` against the default branch, or its merged PR's head equal to the branch tip), never while a worktree has it checked out, and a branch with any unproven commit is kept and listed. The `origin-all` refs and other one-off refs (`refs/recovered`, `refs/tmp*`, the two stashes) go to the person as a list to approve, not to a sweep.

**Done when:** A branch with proven landed work and no worktree is removed; one with any unlanded commit is kept and named. The person has approved or declined removing the one-off refs, and the pack size before and after is recorded.
