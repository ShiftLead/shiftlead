## TD-2104: Clear local branches and stale refs that have already landed

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d479a6810d64484fbb956ab18ca5b62a
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: §4.1 *The branches* and `gitinfo.weigh_branches` delete a branch when its session closes and origin has held its tip, but §4.6's sweep deleted none, so a branch that never went through a close stays. Read on 2026-11-23 in shiftlead: 377 local branches whose upstream is gone, and 2,699 refs under `refs/remotes/origin-all/`, dated September and October, which no code or configured remote uses. Other repos hold fewer branches (ledgerboard has 153). Measured on a mirror the same day, the refs are not what fills the 654 MiB pack: `git gc` alone takes it to about 122 MiB, the landed branches free about 6 MiB more and the `origin-all` refs about 15 MiB more.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the rule that deletes landed local branches, or run one more review round first?

**Design PR:** #4422

**Location:** src/sessionorc/worktreesweep.py, design §4.6 *The disk sweep*

**Fix:** Designed in §4.6 *The disk sweep*, **branches**. A branch is removed only with proof that its work landed: its tip is on the default branch, or is exactly the head of a PR merged into the default branch. §4.1's *origin has held its tip* is not enough for a sweep over every branch, and `git cherry` is no proof, since a squash merge changes the patch id. Never while a worktree has it checked out, and a branch with any unproven commit is kept and listed. The `origin-all` refs and other one-off refs (`refs/scratch`, `refs/techlead` and the like) go to the person as a question (TD-2113), with the repack that frees the space; `refs/recovered` and the two stashes are kept whatever the answer. The builds are TD-2112 and TD-2113.

**Done when:** A branch with proven landed work and no worktree is removed; one with any unlanded commit is kept and named. The person has approved or declined removing the one-off refs, and the pack size before and after is recorded.
