## TD-1419: The home's sync merge runs cadence step 5's line, so a pin bump's squash ends in Merged-by: shiftlead-sync

**Location:** src/sessionorc/cadence.py (check's trailers keyword); src/sessionorc/sync.py (judge, merge); src/sessionorc/agent_sync.py (the pass, _sync_merge); design §6 Sync steps 4–5

**Why:** TD-1412's design (§6 *Sync* step 5). The home merges a mechanical sync PR with `gh pr merge <n> --squash --match-head-commit <head>` and no body (`sessionorc/sync.py` `merge`, called from `agent_sync._sync_merge`). So the squash carries no `Merged-by:`, and from the first step-5 merge on, agent-cadence TD-234's weekly read lists every pin bump the home merged as merged outside step 5.

**Fix:** As §6 *Sync* steps 4 and 5 say. The gate runs once. A sync PR's repo is pinned, so both scripts run through `cadence.command` with `cwd` the registry checkout.
- **`cadence.check(root, pr, timeout, trailers=None)`** (`src/sessionorc/cadence.py`) gains a keyword. Given a path, it adds `--trailers <path>` to the gate's argv. An injected `gate=` with the old three-argument shape stays call-compatible: it is called without the keyword when it is `None`.
- **Step 4 (`judge`).** Read `cadence.composes(root)` (TD-1418) before the gate.
  - Unreadable is *no reading* with its words, counted.
  - Where it says yes, `judge` makes `<dir>` (`tempfile.mkdtemp()`) just before the gate, after the `precheck=True` road has returned, and passes `trailers=<dir>/gate`.
  - It removes `<dir>` on every return except one that sets `ready`: a fail, an unknown, no reading, a timeout, a failed BLOCK comment, or an exception. On `ready` it puts `<dir>` on the returned entry as `gate_dir`.
- **Step 5 (`_sync_merge`).** Pop `gate_dir` first thing, before any return, and remove the directory in a `finally` over every exit. Wrap the pass's call from `judge` to `_sync_merge` so a cancellation also removes it. Then, in order:
  1. the sync-off and holder checks, as today;
  2. `forge.py squash-message <n> --gate <dir>/gate --out <dir>` under `GATE_TIMEOUT`;
  3. the head and holders re-read, as today;
  4. `gh pr merge <n> --squash --match-head-commit <sha> --subject <dir>/subject's text --body-file <dir>/body`.
  The merge is made only when `<dir>/sha`, `pr["head"]` and the re-read head are equal. Otherwise it is the moved head, nothing is written, and the function returns None. A `squash-message` refusal is *no reading* with its words.
- **The environment.** Both scripts get `AGENT_NAME=shiftlead-sync`, with `AGENT_MODEL` and `AGENT_MODEL_FILE` removed.
- **The fallback.** Where `composes` says no, the gate runs without `--trailers` and the merge is today's bare one.
- The system note to the person is unchanged.
- **Tests**, with `gh` and the launcher shimmed:
  - `--trailers` only where `composes` says yes, and unreadable counted;
  - `gate_dir` never in the written reading, whether it came back merged, as no reading, or early (sync off, held);
  - the directory removed on each of `judge`'s non-ready roads and each of `_sync_merge`'s exits;
  - the compose and the merge with their flags, in order, with the holders re-read after the compose;
  - the environment;
  - the three-way sha equality;
  - the fallback;
  - a squash commit made in a temporary git repo from the real `forge.py squash-message` output under this environment, read back through `git log -1 --format='%(trailers:key=Merged-by,valueonly)'` as `shiftlead-sync`.

**Done when:** The tests above pass, the temporary-repo round trip among them. The live read of a real sync merge's `Merged-by: shiftlead-sync` is TD-1421's evidence.
