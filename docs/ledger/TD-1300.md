**Ask:** On your phone, does the key bar answer a Claude Code menu by digit and by the drag pad? (the look: m-47ff5ed1cf57, sent 2026-11-02, one look for the three phone checks)

**Fix:**
1. The row's keys: `esc` `tab` `⇧tab` `ctrl` `◂▴▾▸` `↵` `1–9`, sharing the row's width, `touch-action: none`; `AO.softKey` gains `BackTab` (`ESC [ Z`).
2. **ctrl**: latched for the next key — a key of the bar, or the first character of the next `onData` from the phone's keyboard, the rest of that data sent as typed — sent as its control byte (a letter `0x01`–`0x1a`, `[` Esc); a 500 ms press locks it; drawn pressed while latched or locked.
3. **The drag pad**: nothing on a press; 16 px of slide sends the main direction's arrow, repeating every 150 ms while held away, every 60 ms past 64 px, until the finger lifts or returns within 16 px; pointer captured, and the keys' `elementFromPoint` test at `pointerup` not applied to it. Below the narrow query the row sits in the narrow screen's column (TD-1299); above it, on a coarse pointer, it stays where TD-990 put it.
4. **`1–9`** turns the row to `1`–`9` and `‹`; **`⌄`** in its place while the phone's keyboard is up for the terminal, blurring it.
5. `help.py`'s `soft-key-row` wording and the design's help list, in the same PR.
6. Tests in `test_ui_soft_keys.py`: each key's bytes (application cursor mode included), the ctrl latch and lock over a letter, the pad's first arrow, repeat and stop by a scripted pointer, the digit page's flip and back.

**Done when** the key bar answers a Claude Code menu by digit and by the pad on a phone, and the tests hold its bytes.
