On 2026-11-06 a stray **Take over** on the techlead seat, which the person had opened on Focus only to watch, stopped its doorbell. It slept through five messages for about 11h, and only the card's unread chip showed it. This slice makes the press confirm on a member and raises a row when a held member's mail goes unread.

**Ask:** Go with TD-1440, a build of TD-1434's design (the Take over confirm on a team member and the *held · mail waiting* Inbox row), once TD-1434's end-of-rounds item is answered Go? Answered on the board's decide item for TD-1440 (Go | Not yet), beside TD-1434's own.

**Fix:** - **The confirm.** A *member* is a record with `team` set or `controllers` non-empty (the test that draws Hand back, `cards.py`) that is not `by_person` (`mail.by_person`): the person's own session in a team is interactive from its start and is never a member here. On a member, every Take over carries `data-confirm`: Focus's `#fmodeact` (the narrow sheet's entry is a `data-for` proxy of it, so check that the proxy clicks it and the confirm runs once), the Watching line's `#fwatchgo`, and the card's more ▾ *Switch to interactive* (`card.html`). The text is *Take over `<name>`? While you hold it, its mail does not wake it, and asks to it wait for you. Hand back returns it.* The view carries it (`mode_confirm`, empty for a non-member and on an interactive record). app.js's Focus delta handler, which already redraws `#fmodeact`'s words from the view (near app.js:7375), sets or clears `data-confirm` from it too, so a membership change while Focus is open is followed. The generic `b.dataset.confirm` check (app.js:1378) runs before the mode branch. Hand back (`#fmodemenu`) and *Switch to unattended* never confirm, nor do `ao mode` and a session's call (stamped and logged instead). A record with no team and no controllers stays one press.
- **The held row** (`src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`, beside *idle · open work*'s row and its `idle_open_mark`). The mail it reads is what the hold keeps from the doorbell: `mail.undecided_mail(s)` (the entries with no `read_at` past the record's `mail_decided` watermark, the list `agent_wake._ring_typing` rings), and of those the ones with the record in `to` (a copy is not addressed to it, as `asks_waiting` tells them), any kind, any sender, the person included; a closed entry still unread counts, as it does for the doorbell. `mail_decided` moves only in `_decide_wake`, past its `mail_wakes` check, so it stands still for the whole hold: mail landed during the hold, or during a turn under way at the Take over, counts; mail the seat was rung for before the hold and left unread is decided and does not (the doorbell would not ring it again either; a failed decided ring, `doorbell_failed`, is the doorbell's limit). No turn test: a turn the person takes in the pane during the hold clears nothing, and `turn_at` is not read. The view, computed in `agent_serve._view` before its early return for an own-host record, so a home's own records and its copies of node records alike get it (`home=self.host` where a bare address in `to` must be read as the storing host's session, as `asks_waiting` reads it on the remote path; the home holds the inbox and `mail_decided`, §4.4a; none in node mode, which holds no inbox), gains `unread_unrung`, their count, `oldest_unread_at`, the oldest of them, and `held_mail_at`, the later of that and `mode_at` (that alone on a record with no stamp); with no such mail all three are none, and the row needs at least one. They are computed only for a live agent record that is a member and held (`not mail_wakes`), none on every other record, in the same one-pass-per-batch discipline the `_views` comment (PR #318) keeps for `_answers_for`, never a scan of every inbox per record per push. Add the three to `LIGHT_KEYS` (`src/shiftlead/ui/org.py`), so a read or a flip recounts at once rather than on the 60 s tick. The row is derived at the Inbox read from that view, with no stored field. It draws for a live record with an agent (`adapters.has_agent`) that is a member, held (`not mail.mail_wakes(s)`, the doorbell's own predicate) and `idle`, whose `held_mail_at` is at least `HELD_MAIL` = 30 min old. A held member in any other state draws no held row. The row is not told on Telegram. It sits under *Needs you*, counted in the page's and the poll's one count (`ui/inbox.py`'s shared computation, `ui/counts.py`). Its words: *`<name>` · held by `<you | name>` since `<mode_at>` `<via words>` · `<unread_unrung>` unread since `<oldest_unread_at>` — its mail does not wake it while you hold it*. With no stamp: *held since before this was recorded*; with a start's stamp (no `mode_by`): *held since `<mode_at>` from its start*. Its subject joins `STATE_SUBJECTS`. Buttons: **Hand back** (Focus's own two calls: `set_mode` unattended with `via: inbox`, then `set_stop` with no time when the stop time fell due, with no confirm; a refusal is the row's toast and the row stands), **Open** and **Snooze ▾**. Snooze is `attention_snooze` keyed on the record and the kind `held_mail` — not `held`, which is already *merged without its read*'s (its row id `<sid>:held`, inbox_row.html's `e.mark == 'held'` branch, `clear_mark` kind `held`). `held_mail` is the row's id suffix, its `STATE_SUBJECTS` key and its template branch, and joins `rpc_attention_snooze`'s accepted kinds and its error text (`agent_inbox.py`). The row leaves when the mode changes, the mail is read or the record ends; a turn in the pane clears nothing. Put `HELD_MAIL` in `agent_common.py` beside `IDLE_NUDGE`. Add the row's help paragraph to `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py` (a new key, `held-mail`, listed in `SCREENS` beside `idle-open`) and §4.5a's help list together, as `tests/test_help.py` requires, in these words: *- **held · mail waiting** (an Inbox row) — A session with a team or controllers that you took over and left: it is interactive, so its mail does not wake it, and unread mail its doorbell has not rung for has waited half an hour, counted from the take-over at the earliest. Hand back returns it to its team and its doorbell; Open it if you meant to hold it; Snooze sets the row aside and changes nothing on the session. The row leaves by itself when the session reads its mail, changes mode or ends.*
- Tests: the confirm is drawn on a member at every Take over and not on a non-member, never on Hand back, and is set or cleared by the delta when membership changes. The held row draws at 30 min and not before, and never with no mail (a held member stamped long ago with an empty inbox draws none); non-members carry none of the fields. It does not draw for a working record, an unattended record, a non-member, a `by_person` team session, mail already read, a copy, or mail the seat was rung for before the hold and left unread (decided, past `mail_decided`); a turn the person takes during the hold clears nothing (the row stands, and the doorbell rings that mail, when it can, once Hand back returns it); mail landed during a turn under way at the Take over draws it 30 min on; a Take over onto undecided mail already 40 min old, and an attended Resume carrying old undecided mail, draw it 30 min after `mode_at`, not at once, as does mail left undecided before the hold by a spent budget or a held doorbell; `_decide_wake`, a tick and an `rpc_wait` on a held record with mail leave `mail_decided` and `wakes` unchanged (the invariant the row rests on); a node member's row is computed from the home's copy. It is counted on the page and the poll alike. Hand back from the row stamps `via: inbox`, and the row leaves. Snooze with kind `held_mail` is accepted and hides the row, and a record raising both this row and *merged without its read* draws both.

**Done when:** Take over on a member confirms at every place it is drawn. A member held interactive and idle with mail unread for 30 minutes raises the counted *held · mail waiting* row, with Hand back, Open and Snooze. Tests cover each case above.
