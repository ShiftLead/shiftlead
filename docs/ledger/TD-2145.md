**Fix:** 1. TD-2132 adds the `copies/` rule to the role briefs' temporary-files line. 2. Rewrite the memories `scratchpad-too-long-for-unix-sockets` and `headless-screenshots-on-kmaster`, and their `MEMORY.md` lines, to say the same: the scratch fits a socket, shots go in `$SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH/shots/` under Playwright, the Firefox recipe is gone. Change `docs/mockups/shot.sh`'s default output to `$SHIFTLEAD_SCRATCH/shots`, falling back to its present path where the variable is unset, with its usage comment and `docs/mockups/README.md`'s line naming `/tmp/shiftlead-shots`.
