## TD-1843: A press that did not finish keeps the whole answer bar and says what happened in a press line

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-14
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** a5b747c64ced4631a7731568f32710db
**Blocked by:** TD-1842, TD-1831
**Part of:** TD-1832

**Why:** TD-1832's design (#3840): a person's press must never be left stranded. On 2026-11-14 Paul's **Lift it** on grinder-ao-2's `ask` (TD-1796) could not be applied; the Queue then drew the entry *answered — waiting on grinder-ao-2* with no buttons and the `ask` as *outcome · blocked* with **Answer…** alone, and he typed the ledger command himself (#3818). The design keeps the whole answer bar on such an item and adds one sentence above it, the press line, saying what became of the press and what the person can do (design §4.5 screen 12 *A press is never left stranded*, §4.5a *Queue card: press line*).

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html (card mode); src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js, app.css; src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (the header's Start session count); TD-1831's lift on the press (the blocked outcome's lift, then its reply); docs/design/4.5-ui.md (screen 12 A press is never left stranded)

**Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *A press is never left stranded* and §4.5a row *Queue card: press line* (TD-1832), on the Queue and a card's page, on top of TD-1842's bar.

1. **The press line**: one sentence above the bar, in `inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode, the item's only status sentence — *outcome · blocked*, *answered — waiting on <asker>* and a refusal's message drawn in it and nowhere else on the item (the tree row's state word unchanged). Three cases, each a left rule and tint from the theme's tokens: still with a session (`--idle`), could not be done (`--needs`), refused (`--stalled`).
2. **Answered — waiting on <asker>** (the grey item, TD-1316, TD-1364): its foot becomes its press line; the bar draws **Lift the hold** in slot 1 (out of the ⋯, which keeps **Open**), **Answer…** whose words go to the asker on the question's thread (the question row's reply road; on a board answer a Reply on the line), and Start session by *Three verbs*' rules — so the item now counts as one that draws Start session for the header's rule (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py`'s count and the header macro, with their tests). None of the question's answers.
3. **A `blocked` outcome**: the press line *You answered "<answer>". <seat> could not do it: <the outcome's text>.* then what the person can do; the bar draws the question's answers again, the taken one ticked and not pressable, each other one a reply on the thread (the Inbox row's Reply road, recording `replied`), **Answer…** on that road, Start session, **Dismiss**, **Open**, **⋯**. Where the question is about an entry that still carries `decision (paul)` (its `about`, or the entry the outcome rides on), slot 1 first draws that entry's **Lift the hold**: TD-1829's lift on the press, its confirm naming the entry, then, once the lift is written, the fixed reply *I lifted the hold on TD-n myself* on the question's thread, recorded `replied`, so the item leaves to FYI. A refused lift is a refused press (case 4). A handed entry's outcome: no answers, no **Answer…**.
4. **Refused**: a press the home refused (a 409, a permission's `call` check, a changed line) draws its reason, *nothing was sent* and what to do as the press line, the bar redrawn whole from the fresh reading (`app.js`, where today's refusal is drawn on its item); it goes at the next re-read that finds the item unchanged, or at the next press.
5. Tests for each case, the blocked outcome's lift-then-reply road among them (a lift refused sends no reply), and the header's Start session count with an answered item. Shots of the three cases in light and dark, set beside `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-14-td1832-one-set-*.png`.

**Done when:** No item on the Queue or a card's page is left with no answer controls or with **Answer…** alone after a press: each of the three cases draws its press line and the whole bar as §4.5a's *Queue card: press line* row says, a `blocked` outcome on a held entry lifts it from its own bar and tells the asker, the tests pass, and the PR's shots show the three cases in light and dark.
