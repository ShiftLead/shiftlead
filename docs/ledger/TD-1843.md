## TD-1843: A press that did not finish keeps the whole answer bar and says what happened in a press line

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-14
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** a5b747c64ced4631a7731568f32710db
**Blocked by:** TD-1842, TD-1831
**Part of:** TD-1832

**Why:** TD-1832's design (#3840): a person's press must never be left stranded. On 2026-11-14 Paul's **Lift it** on grinder-ao-2's `ask` (TD-1796) could not be applied; the Queue then drew the entry *answered — waiting on grinder-ao-2* with no buttons and the `ask` as *outcome · blocked* with **Answer…** alone, and he typed the ledger command himself (#3818). The design keeps the whole answer bar on such an item and adds one sentence above it, the press line, saying what became of the press and what the person can do (design §4.5 screen 12 *A press is never left stranded*, §4.5a *Queue card: press line*).

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html (card mode); src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js, app.css; src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py (items_n, walk_mine, the item start, the wants); src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py and src/shiftlead/ui/app.py (the lift-then-reply road, on TD-1831's lift); docs/design/4.5-ui.md (screen 12 A press is never left stranded)

**Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *A press is never left stranded* and §4.5a row *Queue card: press line* (TD-1832), on the Queue and a card's page, on top of TD-1842's bar and TD-1831's lift on the press.

1. **The press line**: one sentence above the bar, in `inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode, the item's only status sentence — *outcome · blocked*, *answered — waiting on <asker>*, *answered — applying*, *waits until <date>* and a refusal's message drawn in it and nowhere else on the item (the tree row's state word unchanged). Three cases, each a left rule and tint from the theme's tokens: still with a session (`--idle`), could not be done (`--needs`), refused (`--stalled`). Capital first, plain words.
2. **Answered — waiting on <asker>** (TD-1316, TD-1364): its foot becomes its press line; the bar draws **Lift the hold** in slot 1 (out of the ⋯), **Answer…** and Start session. **Answer…**'s words go to the asker on the question's thread by the road an answered question's *Waiting on them* row's **Reply** takes, for a mail answer, its `done`-outcome form and a steer let go with its default; an exited or forgotten asker refuses it (*no session*), a refused press (step 6); a board answer (a closed or carried decided line) draws no **Answer…**. Start session follows the holder rules: its `start` built (`src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` about line 738, the `want in (*WANTS, "with_you")` test gains the answered want), drawn where no other session holds the entry, its press sending `item` the entry's `<repo>#TD-n` and `why_key` *decision*; where the asker or another session holds the entry no button, the row *held by <name>*. Either way it counts for the header's rule and walk as a held item does: `items_n`, `walk_mine` and `waits_on_me` (`queue.py` 609, 625, ~632), and the start road's own recount, with tests for the held and the free case. *Answered — applying* and *waits until <date>* items: their words as the press line, the bar **Open** and ⋯ alone, outside the header's count.
3. **A `blocked` outcome**: the press line *You answered "<answer>". <seat> could not do it: <the outcome's text>.* then what the person can do; the bar draws the question's answers again as plain replies — the taken one ticked and not pressable, each other one a reply on the thread, none drawn as a lift (TD-1833's `--lift` answers are plain words on an answered ask) — then **Answer…**, Start session, **Dismiss**, **Open**, **⋯**, each reply the Inbox row's Reply road, recording `replied`.
4. **Lift the hold on a `blocked` outcome**: where the first id of the card's repo in the question's `about` (a handed entry: the entry itself) still carries `decision (paul)`, slot 1 first draws that entry's **Lift the hold**, its confirm naming the entry. On a question it is one request, a new host-agent RPC in `src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py` with its route in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py`: TD-1831's lift (`rpc_ledger_decide` with `lift=True`), then, only once that is written, the fixed reply *I lifted the hold on TD-n myself* on the question's thread, recorded as the outcome's `replied`, so the item leaves to FYI. A lift refused by TD-1829's cases sends nothing and is a refused press (step 6); a lift that finds the hold already gone sends no reply and the item is redrawn without the button; a lift written whose reply then fails returns that, and the item draws the press line *The hold on TD-n is lifted; telling <seat> failed: <reason>.* with its bar less the button. On a handed entry's outcome the lift alone, no reply.
5. **One press, one item**: while an entry's question carries an unreplied `blocked` outcome, the entry's own *answered — waiting on <asker>* item is not drawn on the card, its tree row keeping its word; once the outcome records `replied` it is drawn again (step 2). Which entry still carries `decision (paul)` and which items give way is read in `queue.py`'s wants, from the ledger reading, never in the template.
5a. **The lift-then-reply request's keys**: it holds the keys §4.10's lifting answer holds (*An `ask` that lifts the person's gate*): the press's `ledger:<root>:<id>` and the question's id, each refused with *<id> is already being answered* while held, so a double press, a concurrent Delete, withdraw or Reply, and another lift race as they do there; an orphaned question gets the lift alone, its press line saying no one was told.
6. **Refused**: a press the home refused (a 409, a permission's `call` check, a changed line, TD-1829's lift refusals) draws as its press line the home's words, *Nothing was sent.*, and *It now reads as above; press again to answer it.* where the item was redrawn from a fresher reading, the bar redrawn whole from the next re-read (the refusal's reply carries no item) (`app.js`, `AO.pressErrs`); it lasts as today's refusal does, to the item's next press or a reload, on the Queue and through a card's page's own patch alike, replacing the item's other press line meanwhile.
7. Tests for each case — a `--lift` answer on a `blocked` outcome redrawn as plain words (vacuous until TD-1833 lands; write it so it holds once it does) among them — the lift-then-reply RPC (written lift then reply; refused lift sends nothing; hold gone sends nothing; reply failing after the lift), one press one item, the header count with an answered item. Shots of the three cases in light and dark, set beside `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-14-td1832-one-set-*.png`.

**Done when:** No item on the Queue or a card's page is left with no answer controls or with **Answer…** alone after a press: each of the three cases draws its press line and the whole bar as §4.5a's *Queue card: press line* row says, a `blocked` outcome on a held entry lifts it from its own bar and tells the asker, the tests pass, and the PR's shots show the three cases in light and dark.
