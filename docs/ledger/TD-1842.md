## TD-1842: The Queue draws one answer bar, in the same slots, on every item kind

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-14
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** a5b747c64ced4631a7731568f32710db
**Part of:** TD-1832

**Why:** TD-1832's design (#3840; Paul agreed its mockups, m-051aa9f4eff8): every item on the Queue and a card's page ends in one answer bar, its controls in the same slots in the same order whatever the item's kind, so the person reads what is asked and which button answers it the same way on every card (design §4.5 screen 12 *One answer set*, §4.5a *Queue card: the answer bar*). Today the controls change place and name from kind to kind: a mail item's **Open** sits in its heading beside the asker's name, an `act` line's **Answer…** is in its ⋯, and an answered item draws its own set.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html (card mode); queue_row.html; queue_card.html; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css, app.js; docs/design/4.5-ui.md (screen 12 One answer set)

**Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *One answer set* and §4.5a row *Queue card: the answer bar* (TD-1832), on the Queue and a card's page only; the Inbox's own rows, the message page and the Repo page keep their controls.

1. **The bar** in `inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode, for every kind it draws (ledger entry, `ask`, `steer`, board `decide` and `act` line, look, `blocked` outcome, stopped holder's state row, red PR, fork's PR, stuck row, and the grey *with you in* / *answered — waiting on* items): one container, its slots in order — the item's answers in the order written (recommended outlined), **Answer…**, Start session / Open session after a thin rule, a gap, then right-aligned **Dismiss** (mail items), **Open**, **⋯**. A slot the item has no use for is left out. Where today's markup puts a control elsewhere, move it into its slot: a mail item's heading **Open** (the asker's session) to the bar, its *whole entry ›* link dropped (the sentence opens the message page); an `act` line's **Answer…** out of its ⋯ into slot 2 on the card (the Inbox keeps it in the ⋯); a state row's **Allow** / **Deny** and *why?* in slot 1. The ⋯ keeps **Open** last, as *Three verbs: ⋯* says. The heading holds no buttons; the asker's name links its Focus as a holder's word does.
2. **CSS** in `app.css`, from the theme's tokens: the right group right-aligned; on a phone (below 720 px) it wraps to a line of its own, right-aligned. No new pixel sizes (`tests/test_css.py`).
3. **Keys**: the Queue's number keys and `g` press the same answers as before, read from slot 1 in its order (`app.js`).
4. **The header's Start session rule** is unchanged by this build (the *answered* item's slots are TD-1843's).
5. Tests: one per kind that the bar's controls are present in slot order and that the heading holds no `button`; the Inbox's own rows unchanged. Shots of the Queue in light and dark and at 420 px, committed under `docs/mockups/reviews/` and named in the PR, set beside `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-14-td1832-one-set-*.png`.

**Done when:** On the Queue and a card's page every item kind draws the one answer bar in §4.5a's slot order, no item's heading draws a button, the Inbox's own rows are unchanged, the tests above pass, and the PR's shots show it in light, dark and at phone width.
