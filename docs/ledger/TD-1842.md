## TD-1842: The Queue draws one answer bar, in the same slots, on every item kind

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-14
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** a5b747c64ced4631a7731568f32710db
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-1832

**Why:** TD-1832's design (#3840; Paul agreed its mockups, m-051aa9f4eff8): every item on the Queue and a card's page ends in one answer bar, its controls in the same slots in the same order whatever the item's kind, so the person reads what is asked and which button answers it the same way on every card (design §4.5 screen 12 *One answer set*, §4.5a *Queue card: the answer bar*). Today the controls change place and name from kind to kind: a mail item's **Open** sits in its heading beside the asker's name, an `act` line's **Answer…** is in its ⋯, and an answered item draws its own set.

**Ask:** Should TD-1832's design (one answer bar on every Queue item, and no press left stranded) get one more review round, or go to the builders now? Its four rounds ended at the cap, and four fixes from the last round have not been read by a review (F86 and F89, in TD-1843 and TD-1842; F87 and F88, in TD-1843). One more round (recommended) has a review read them first; Go releases TD-1842 and TD-1843 with those fixes marked for their builders to check; Hold: <note> revises the design to your note.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html (card mode); queue_row.html; queue_card.html; src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css, app.js; docs/design/4.5-ui.md (screen 12 One answer set)

**Fix:** Build design §4.5 screen 12 *One answer set* and §4.5a row *Queue card: the answer bar* (TD-1832), on the Queue and a card's page only; the Inbox's own rows, the message page, the Repo page and the Read fold's items keep their controls.

1. **The bar** in `inbox_row.html`'s `card` mode, for every Decide, Do and grey kind it draws (ledger entry, `ask`, `steer`, board `decide` and `act` line, look, `blocked` outcome, an exited asker's owed question, stopped holder's state row, red PR, fork's PR, stuck row; *with you in*, *answered — waiting on*, *answered — applying* or *applying <act>*, *waits until*, *handed to <seat>*): one container, its slots in order — the item's answers in the order written (recommended outlined), **Answer…**, Start session / Open session after a thin rule, a gap, then right-aligned **Dismiss** (only where the Inbox's own row draws Dismiss: a `blocked` outcome or an exited asker's owed question; never an open `ask` or `steer`), a fork's PR's **Copy**, **Open**, **⋯**. A slot the item has no use for is left out.
2. **Moves**: a mail item's heading **Open** (the asker's session; none where the asker has no session) to the bar, its *whole entry ›* link dropped (the sentence opens the message page); an `act` line's **Answer…** out of its ⋯ into slot 2 on the card (the Inbox keeps it in the ⋯); a state row's **Allow** / **Deny** and *why?* in slot 1; a red PR's and a fork's PR's Open into the right group. **Open** is the bar's alone: an item's ⋯ holds no Open (a tree row's ⋯ keeps its own), and an item whose ⋯ would then hold nothing draws none: a ledger item's ⋯ (Open alone today) and a *with you in* item's are gone; **Done…** and **Back to design…** stay in the entry's tree row's ⋯.
3. **The heading** holds no answer or item control: its kind line's words stay as *The Queue's words* has them (TD-1809, built; the mockups' heading words are illustrations), its team badge stays, the asker's name links its Focus as a holder's word does.
4. **In this build**, these items keep today's controls, placed in the bar: an *answered — waiting on <asker>* item draws **Open** and its ⋯ with **Lift the hold** in it, as today (no answers, no **Answer…**, no Start session); a `blocked` outcome and an exited asker's owed question draw **Answer…** (slot 2, where today's Reply is), Start session, **Dismiss** and **Open**, no ⋯, a handed entry's `blocked` outcome the same less **Answer…**; a *handed to <seat>* item (TD-1660's, `answered.kind == "handed"`, once built: whichever of TD-1660 and this build lands second carries it) **Open** and its ⋯ with its entry's answer; a *waits until* item **Open** and its ⋯ with **Lift the hold**; an *answered — applying* item **Open** alone. TD-1843 changes them (it moves **Lift the hold** and the handed item's answer into slot 1 and drops those ⋯s), so tests here must not pin the interim ⋯.
5. **CSS** in `app.css`, from the theme's tokens: the right group right-aligned and kept together when the bar wraps on a phone. No new pixel sizes (`tests/test_css.py`). The Queue has no answer keys, and this build adds none.
6. Tests: one per kind that the bar's controls are present in slot order, that no heading holds a `button` but the team badge, that Dismiss is drawn on a `blocked` outcome and an exited asker's owed question and absent on an open `ask` and `steer`, and that the Inbox's own rows are unchanged. Shots of the Queue in light and dark and at 420 px, committed under `docs/mockups/reviews/` and named in the PR, set beside `docs/mockups/reviews/2026-11-14-td1832-one-set-*.png`.
