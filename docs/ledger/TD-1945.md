**Why:** What sessions leave on a host has no owner and no schedule: the tool's scratch, worktrees a close kept or a session made itself, run logs of 30 days whatever their size, and old precheck and agent-cadence caches (TD-1921: about 24 GB on kmaster, growing about 10 GB a month). Design §4.6 *The disk sweep* adds them to the host agent's hourly sweep and keeps transcripts out of its reach.

**Location:** design §4.6 *The disk sweep*, §4.3, §4.1, §5; src/sessionorc/agent_common.py, src/shiftlead/adapters/

**Fix:** Build design §4.6 *The disk sweep*, with §4.3 (`transcript_roots`), §4.1's pointer and §5 (`scratch_keep_hours`, `worktree_keep_days`, `runs_keep_mb`, `cache_keep_days`), in the host agent's hourly sweep (`PRUNE_EVERY`, `sessionorc.agent_common`):
- scratch of a record ended `scratch_keep_hours` ago, and an unnamed directory under `shiftlead-<uid>/` an hour after its last write;
- worktrees under each registered repo's `.claude/worktrees/` on the host passing `reap_worktrees.sh`'s five tests (run from the pinned tree, its report read), *idle* also read from the live records, older than `worktree_keep_days` by their newest write; never the main checkout, a detached worktree, one with a commit on no remote branch or a modified or untracked file; no branch deleted; a pass weighs worktrees oldest first for at most `SWEEP_WORKTREE_S` (60 s), the next carrying on;
- run logs and attachments of ended sessions past `runs_keep_mb`, oldest ended session first, beside `runs_keep_days`;
- agent-cadence trees that are no registered repo's `origin` default branch's pin and older than `cache_keep_days`, and a repo's `~/.cache/<repo>-precheck/` children older than it, never while a precheck of that repo runs;
- `transcript_roots()` on each adapter (Claude Code `~/.claude/projects` and each profile's `config_dir`'s `projects`; Gemini CLI its chats; Grok Build its sessions directory); the sweep removes only under its named roots, never follows a symlink, and refuses any path inside a transcript root.

**Done when:** In a scratch home with scratch repos and a fake tool home, one sweep removes an ended record's scratch past its keep and leaves a live one's; removes a landed, clean, idle, unlocked worktree past its keep and keeps one with an unpushed commit, one with an untracked file, a live record's, a detached one and the main checkout, each with its why; drops the oldest ended session's run log past `runs_keep_mb` and never a live one's; removes an unpinned agent-cadence tree past its keep and keeps the pinned one; and a test plants a transcript (and a symlink to one) under every adapter's `transcript_roots()` and inside a swept root, runs the sweep, and finds every transcript intact.
