## TD-2182: Check the Queue's live updates, then let the person turn them on or off

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-24 (paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 353cb2a62a1c4634855905d3bab41940
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** Paul saw Queue cards change while the bar said there were 3 updates waiting for **Refresh**, and asked first that the live and Refresh mechanism be checked, then for a setting that turns live updates (no Refresh press needed) on or off, with words saying why a person would want each. What he saw may be the design working: §4.5 screen 12 *The page holds still* patches a card's values (words, pills, counts, ages) in place at once and holds back only places — a card moving, arriving or leaving, a row or item arriving — for **Refresh**. It may also be a bug, where a re-read moves or adds something the design holds. Nobody can tell from the report, so the check comes first. The switch is a change of a decided design: ADR 2026-11-12 (`docs/decisions/2026-11-12-queue-holds-still.md`) lists *a pause toggle* under *Not chosen*, since the bar is the page's one pause under WCAG 2.2.2, so it needs a design round, not a build alone. The value is the person's: a page that keeps itself current for someone watching it rather than pressing on it, while a held page stays the default for someone answering cards, since moving cards make a press land on the wrong one (TD-1555).

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the switch that lets the Queue draw its updates as they come, or run one more review round first?

**Design PR:** #4597

**Location:** docs/design/4.5-ui.md (screen 12 *The page holds still*); docs/design/4.5a-controls.md (Queue page: the bar, Refresh); docs/decisions/2026-11-12-queue-holds-still.md (*Not chosen*); src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js and src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue.html (the Queue re-read and bar)

**Fix:** 1. **The check.** On a scratch home (`scripts/look_home.py`), drive re-reads that change a card's values, move a card, add a card and add an ask, and see what is drawn before **Refresh**. If only values change in place and the bar counts the rest, it is the design working: say so to Paul in this entry's Log, and say which of what he saw were values. If anything the design holds (a place, a new row or item, a card arriving or leaving) is drawn without **Refresh**, that is a bug: file it as a build part of this entry, with what was seen.
2. **The design.** A switch for live updates on the Queue: on, each served re-read is drawn whole as **Refresh** draws it (the card under the pointer, focus or an open menu or box still holding, as today); off, today's held page. The round settles where the switch lives (on the bar beside **Refresh**, the Settings page, or both), whether it is remembered in the browser or in `settings.yml`, whether it reaches a card's page too, its default (held, as today, is the lean), and the help words saying why a person would want each: live for watching the work move, held for answering cards without one moving under the press. It adds a §4.5a row, rewrites §4.5 screen 12 *The page holds still* and the ADR's *Not chosen* line with the person's reason, and checks WCAG 2.2.2 still holds (the switch off is the pause).
3. **The build.** Build entries `Part of:` this one, from the design.

**Done when:** The check's finding is in this entry's Log (and a bug part filed if it found one), and the switch is designed, built and live: a person can turn the Queue's live updates on and off, and the words beside it say why they might want each.
