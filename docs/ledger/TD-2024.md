**Why:** #4264's precheck (a change to the tick's seat causes, nothing near a stop time) failed this one test: at its stop time the tick sent the wrap-up words and then killed the settled shell, but the record's `tail` read `['bash-5.2$']` — the words were not on the pane the tick captured. The full suite's load is the likeliest cause, the shape of TD-1218, TD-1229 and TD-2023: the send was typed but the capture taken before the pane drew it, or after a redraw cleared it. A precheck red for that reason costs a whole-suite rerun and reads as the PR's fault.
