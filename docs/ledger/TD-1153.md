## TD-1153: A team's flow, seat counts and settings are scattered or hand edits: a team page picks its flow, sets each seat's count range and the team's settings, and its edits merge on the person's Save

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-27
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Blocked by:** TD-1954, decision (paul)

**Why:** Paul, 2026-10-27: "soon we will want users to be able to control team defs and flows from the UI (maybe a separate page per team)". Today the Members dialog edits only an `org.yml` team, and only its members; a repo's `.shiftlead.yml` team is read-only on the page, so changing its roles, briefs, lanes or flows is a hand edit, a PR, a merge and the checkout's pull. Agreed in TD-1149's conversation: the definition stays in its file — versioned and reviewed with the briefs it names — and the page becomes its editor, beside the team's settings (TD-1149 part 2's size first).

**Location:** `src/shiftlead/org.py` (the definitions), `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (`/api/teams/{name}/members`, `/api/teams/{name}/flow/apply`), `src/shiftlead/ui/settings_page.py` (the Teams card); design §4.9 *Add or remove a member from the team card* (TD-163), §4.9c *Every listed flow must be followable*, §4.5a *Members dialog* and *Settings page* **Teams**, §5 *Configuration*, [ADR 2026-09-25](../decisions/2026-09-25-settings-audit.md)

**Ask:** Will you design the team page with a session once the Flows page is designed: its flow pick, seat count ranges and settings?

**Fix (to design):** a page per team that picks its flow from the listed ones, sets each seat's count range (Paul, 2026-11-18: "Seat count ranges belong on the team"), and holds the team's settings (until, reserve, balance, on_work), moved from the Settings page's team card. The flow, from the Flows page (TD-1954), is drawn read-only as a strip of its stages and on-call seats with each seat's live state, n8n's node and detail pattern without a canvas. Edits gather in a change tray that shows the diff and what was checked; an `org.yml` team's edit is committed in place, a repo team's goes through a PR that merges itself once its checks pass, the person's Save being the approval, with no agent review. The text editor of the definition is not on the page.

**Done when:** the design round with Paul has written the page and filed its builds.
