**Why:** Audit of #4592, #4594 (TD-2065 slices 4 and 5) and #4671 (TD-2159), and of the other slices of TD-2065 with the same gap. Each changed behaviour and none added a design-history line:

CLAUDE.md: a change in behaviour is a change to the design first, "and the dated fact goes to the history, `docs/history/TD-<n>.md`"; the ledger's Log lines are not it. The same gap was TD-2207's for three earlier slices.

**Location:** docs/history/

**Fix:** Add to `docs/history/TD-2065.md` a dated line for slices 4 to 9 and 11, each under the section it builds (§4.9d for most; §6 for the stop time, the schedule and the fill bound), add to `docs/history/TD-2158.md` a line for each of slices 1 to 6, and make `docs/history/TD-2159.md` with its line under §4.9c, each naming what it did and its PR.

**Done when:** `scripts/history.py`, over the sections they build, prints a line naming each of #4592, #4594, #4599, #4600, #4601, #4604 and #4607, a line naming each of #4660 to #4665, and `scripts/history.py 4.9c` prints a TD-2159 line naming #4671.
