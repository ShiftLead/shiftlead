## TD-2062: Switching a team's flow: the pick, the preview of what happens to each entry, and the switch

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-22
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 7723b7906be7403a9c3b65c73af1f307
**Blocked by:** TD-2061, TD-2048, TD-2051, TD-2052
**Part of:** TD-1153

**Why:** A team runs one flow and the person changes which. A switch with entries in flight must say first what happens to each, and then do exactly that.

**Ask:** Should the builders build the flow pick and the switch?

**Fix:** Build design §4.9d *The flow pick and a switch*: the pick with each flow a team cannot run disabled with its reason; the preview and `team_switch` reading one function for each record's case (stays, session ended and to the person, re-enters, keeps its question, merging and not touched); the copy on the state branch pushed first and the key written after, a refused push refusing the switch, a restart finishing it; the numbers kept for stages of the same key; audits and support seats as stated. `team_switch` is on the person-only list; it records `switching: <flow>` on the home's team record before the push and clears it once the key is written, and a home that restarts finishes a recorded switch. The reconcile cases this design added to §4.9c (a waiting record left until its answer, a kept record rewritten to the new flow, the no-move re-entry keeping its pull request, an entry moved to another team) are built here unless TD-2051 landed with them.

**Done when:** For each case a test shows the preview's line and the record after the switch agree; a switch while a merge is under way leaves that record to its merge; a refused push changes nothing; a switch on a stopped team ends no session.
