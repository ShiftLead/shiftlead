**Why:** TD-704's design (Paul, 2026-11-12, interactive): a *TDs in motion* row's chip is the claim's stage in the team's current flow, named and coloured by `flow.yml`, instead of the fixed `PHASES = ("add", "design", "grind", "review")` in `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py`. *grind* becomes the flow's *build*; *add* is now whatever stage a hunter holds in a flow (*find*); a PR reads a review stage only while that stage's reader has its unanswered ask. Paul: *accuracy is key here, the user needs to be able to trust the ui*.

**Location:** design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage*, §4.4 *The PR's reads*, §4.5a *team card: TDs in motion*; `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py` (`PHASES`, `motion_rows`), `src/shiftlead/review.py` (`standing`), `src/sessionorc/held.py`, `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_red_prs`), `src/shiftlead/flowdefs.py` (`STAGE_KEYS`), `src/shiftlead/ui/repo_board.py` (`team_stages`), `templates/team_summary.html`, `static/app.css`

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage* (rules 1–7) and the stage key `color`, §4.4 *The PR's reads*, and the §4.5a row *team card: TDs in motion*. Slices:

1. **The walk and the reading.** Move the per-seat walk out of `review.standing` (`src/shiftlead/review.py`) into `sessionorc.held`, returning per PR and addressee `{to, at, state, verdict}` (`open` / `answered` / `closed`, `verdict` from the reply `closed_by` names); `review.standing` and `cli._pr_standing` keep their words on top of it. `_red_prs` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, beside `held.asks`) calls it on the mail the home holds (seats' inboxes and `outbox`, `person_inbox`) and saves `pr_orders.reads`, keeping a merged or closed PR's last reads while a live claim names it by `pr` / `review_pr`; part of the save comparison. Tests beside the `asked` and standing ones: a re-ask after findings, a reroute, an expired ask, a person's answer with no verdict, a forgotten asker.
2. **The flow's `color`.** `flowdefs` accepts `color:` on a stage (`STAGE_KEYS`), refuses a word outside the palette (`pink indigo blue amber teal green violet`) naming it, and resolves each stage's colour in §4.9c's three passes. `app.css` gains `--hue-<name>` in both themes; `--ph-*` and the `.ph-*` rules go, the legend's included.
3. **The chip.** `motion_rows` takes the team's stages (`repo_board.team_stages`, read once per page load off the loop and kept beside `cards.FLOWS` for deltas), the team's flow differences (rule 6) and `pr_orders.reads`, and gives each row `stage` and `color` (a palette name, or `''` for grey) by rules 1–7, the chain's last link for rule 3's *you* read from the member's `review`; `PHASES` goes (and its re-export in `app.py`); the sort is the flow's stage order, grey after by word, *you* last; `phases` becomes an ordered list of `(stage, n)`; `phase_w` sizes the chip column. `team_summary.html` draws the chip and the heading from them. `docs/mockups/gen.py` and its outputs are regenerated where they draw `ph-*`.

The mockups Paul approved: `docs/mockups/reviews/td704/` (A the `td` flow, B a repo flow with *fact-check*), from `docs/mockups/reviews/td704-src/td704_shots.py`.
