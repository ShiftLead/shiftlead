**Location:** design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage*, §4.4 *The newest ask, and its answer*, §4.5a *team card: TDs in motion*; `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py` (`PHASES`, `motion_rows`), `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_red_prs`), `src/sessionorc/held.py` (`asks`), flowdefs, `templates/team_summary.html`, `static/app.css`

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage* and the stage key `color`, §4.4 *The newest ask, and its answer*, and the §4.5a row *team card: TDs in motion*. Slices:

1. **The reading.** `_red_prs` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, beside `held.asks`) keeps `pr_orders.newest: {n: {at, to, verdict}}`: each open PR's newest `ask` carrying `pr` and the `verdict` of the newest reply on its thread, read from the asker's record mail; a newer ask clears the verdict; a merged or closed PR keeps its value while a claim names it; part of the save comparison. Tests in `tests/test_agent*.py` beside the `asked` ones.
2. **The flow's `color`.** `flowdefs` accepts `color:` on a stage, refuses a word outside the palette (`pink indigo blue amber teal green violet red`) naming it, and resolves each stage's colour: written, else the name's default (*find* pink, *design* indigo, *build* blue, *review* amber), else the first palette name no other stage of the flow takes. `app.css` gains `--hue-<name>` in both themes; `--ph-*` and the `.ph-*` rules go, the legend's included.
3. **The chip.** `motion_rows` takes the team's current flow and the reading's `newest`, and gives each row `stage`, `color` (a palette name or `''` for grey) and the rules 1–5 of §4.9c; `PHASES` goes (and its re-export in `app.py`); the sort is the flow's stage order, grey after by label, *you* last; `phases` becomes an ordered list of `(stage, n)`; `phase_w` sizes the chip column. `team_summary.html` draws the chip from it and the heading from the ordered counts. `docs/mockups/gen.py` and its outputs are regenerated where they draw `ph-*`.

The mockups Paul approved: `docs/mockups/reviews/td704/` (A the `td` flow, B a repo flow with *fact-check*), from `docs/mockups/reviews/td704-src/td704_shots.py`.
