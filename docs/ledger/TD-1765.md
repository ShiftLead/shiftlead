**Why:** TD-704's design (Paul, 2026-11-12, interactive): a *TDs in motion* row's chip is the claim's stage in the team's current flow, named and coloured by `flow.yml`, instead of the fixed `PHASES = ("add", "design", "grind", "review")` in `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py`. *grind* becomes the flow's *build*; *add* is now whatever stage a hunter holds in a flow (*find*); a PR reads a review stage only while that stage's reader has its unanswered ask. Paul: *accuracy is key here, the user needs to be able to trust the ui*.

**Location:** design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage*, §4.4 *The PR's reads*, §4.5a *team card: TDs in motion*; `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py` (`PHASES`, `motion_rows`), `src/shiftlead/review.py` (`standing`), `src/sessionorc/held.py`, `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_red_prs`), `src/shiftlead/flowdefs.py` (`STAGE_KEYS`), `src/shiftlead/ui/repo_board.py` (`team_stages`), `templates/team_summary.html`, `static/app.css`

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage* (rules 1–6) and the stage key `color`, §4.4 *The PR's reads*, and the §4.5a row *team card: TDs in motion*. Slices:

1. **The reading.** A walk in `sessionorc.held` (the per-seat walk moved out of `review.standing`) gives, per open PR and addressee, `{to, at, state, verdict}` from the mail the home holds — seats' inboxes, the `outbox` of whichever record sent each reply, `person_inbox` — an extra read as its seat, a `passed_up` copy as the person's. `_red_prs` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`) recomputes `pr_orders.reads` from it at every pass, keeps a saved entry only where the mail no longer holds its ask (an `open` one then `closed`, a read verdict never blanked), and keeps a merged or closed PR's last reads while a live claim names it by `pr`; part of the save comparison. The Repo page (`ui/app.py`'s per-seat `inbox` reads), `review.standing` and `cli._pr_standing` then draw their words from `pr_orders.reads`. Tests: a re-ask after findings, a reroute, an expired ask, a seat's pass-up, an extra's reply, a person's answer, a forgotten or renamed seat.
2. **The flow's `color`.** `flowdefs` accepts `color:` on a stage (`STAGE_KEYS`), refuses a word outside the palette (`pink indigo blue amber teal green violet`) naming it, and resolves each stage's colour in §4.9c's three passes. `app.css` gains `--hue-<name>` in both themes; `--ph-*` and the `.ph-*` rules go, the legend's included.
3. **The chip.** `motion_rows` takes the team's stages (`repo_board.team_stages`, read once per page load off the loop and kept beside `cards.FLOWS` for deltas), the team's flow differences (rule 5) and `pr_orders.reads`, and gives each row `stage` and `color` (a palette name, or `''` for grey) by rules 1–6, a reference two holders read differently splitting into a row per chip; `PHASES` goes (and its re-export in `app.py`); the sort is the flow's stage order, grey after by word, *you* last; `phases` becomes an ordered list of `(stage, n)`; a new `phase_w` sizes the chip column. `team_summary.html` draws the chip and the heading from them. `docs/mockups/gen.py` and its outputs are regenerated where they draw `ph-*`.

The mockups Paul approved: `docs/mockups/reviews/td704/` (A the `td` flow, B a repo flow with *fact-check*), from `docs/mockups/reviews/td704-src/td704_shots.py`.

**Done when:** a team card under the `td` flow reads *design*, *build* and *review* chips from the flow; a PR reads *review* only while the techlead's ask on it is open, and its holder's stage before the ask and after `findings`, `pass` or an unanswered close; an ask to the person reads *you* while open and once answered, until a newer ask or the PR's close; a merged PR reads the merging seat's stage or the holder's, marked, never *you*; a repo flow with a fourth stage and `color: teal` draws that chip in teal; a hunter outside the flow draws a grey *hunter* chip; a team waiting on Apply for a flow switch reads grey by role; the Repo page's standing and `ao repo` read `pr_orders.reads`; and the tests cover each of §4.9c's rules 1–6.
