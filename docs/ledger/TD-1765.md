**Why:** TD-704's design (Paul, 2026-11-12, interactive): a *TDs in motion* row's chip is the claim's stage in the team's current flow, named and coloured by `flow.yml`, instead of the fixed `PHASES = ("add", "design", "grind", "review")` in `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py`. *grind* becomes the flow's *build*; *add* is now whatever stage a hunter holds in a flow (*find*); a PR reads a review stage only while that stage's reader has its unanswered ask. Paul: *accuracy is key here, the user needs to be able to trust the ui*.

**Location:** design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage*, §4.4 *The PR's reads*, §4.5a *team card: TDs in motion*; `src/shiftlead/ui/org.py` (`PHASES`, `motion_rows`), `src/shiftlead/review.py` (`standing`), `src/sessionorc/held.py`, `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (`_red_prs`), `src/shiftlead/flowdefs.py` (`STAGE_KEYS`), `src/shiftlead/ui/repo_board.py` (`team_stages`), `templates/team_summary.html`, `static/app.css`

**Fix:** Build design §4.9c *A claim's chip is its stage* (rules 1–5, first that applies, after the stage-name gate) and the stage key `color`, §4.4 *The PR's reads*, §4.9c *What is shown* (the standing's words), and the §4.5a row *team card: TDs in motion*. Slices:

1. **The reading.** A walk in `sessionorc.held` (the per-seat walk moved out of `review.standing`) gives, per open PR and addressee, `{id, to, at, state, verdict, again}` from the mail the home holds — seats' inboxes, the `outbox` of whichever record sent each reply, `person_inbox` — an extra read as its seat, each entry keyed by the mailbox its copy sits in and a pass-up's passer (its `by`) read `closed` from the pass on, `again` true only on a take-up question (`unanswered`, `pr`) answered with index 0, an ask counted for its envelope's `repo` stamp before `held.asks`' fallback. `_red_prs` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`) recomputes `pr_orders.reads` at every pass — outside the `unserved` branch, and in the `new == {…}` save comparison — keeps a saved entry only where the mail no longer holds its ask by `id` (an `open` one then `closed`, a read verdict never blanked), and prunes a PR's entry at its close. The Repo page (drop `ui/app.py`'s per-seat `inbox` reads), `review.standing` / `repo.pr_standing` and `cli._pr_standing` then word the standing from `pr_orders.reads` as §4.9c *What is shown* says, the person's entry included. Tests: a re-ask after findings, a reroute, an expired ask, a seat's pass-up to the person and to a director seat, its recommendation pressed, an extra's reply, the take-up's *Ask the reader again* / *I'll read it* / typed reply, a newer ask to a seat after the person's answer, a forgotten or renamed seat, a node sender with a `repo` stamp.
2. **The flow's `color`.** `flowdefs` accepts `color:` on a stage (`STAGE_KEYS`), refuses a word outside the palette (`pink indigo blue amber teal green violet`) naming it, and resolves each stage's colour in §4.9c's three passes. `app.css` gains `--hue-<name>` in both themes; `--ph-*` and the `.ph-*` rules go, the legend's included.
3. **The chip.** `motion_rows` takes the team's stages (`repo_board.team_stages` against the team's `repo`, read once per page load off the loop and kept beside `cards.FLOWS` so a delta redraws chips without a file read), each member's flow differences with their `what` lines (the stage-name gate — `cards.FLOW_ACTS` keeps only the act today; a node team's flow is not read), the holders' open asks and steers to the person about the reference (rule 2's *or the work*, as §6 rule 5's *waiting* reading finds them), and the `pr_orders.reads` of each claim's own record's repo, and gives each row `stage` and `color` (a palette name, or `''` for grey) by rules 1–5; a reference whose holders read different chips is a row per chip, the facet's count still references, and `ui/repo.py`'s `ledger_lists` (*held by*) and `design_builds`, which key motion rows by reference, join a reference's rows. `PHASES` goes (and its re-export in `app.py`); the sort is the flow's stage order, grey after by word, *you* last; `phases` becomes an ordered list of `(stage, n)`; a new `phase_w`, set as `ref_w` is, sizes the chip column. `team_summary.html` draws the chip and the heading from them. `docs/mockups/gen.py` and its outputs are regenerated where they draw `ph-*`.
