**Why:** TD-1422's design (§6 rule 2 *A person's restart*, rule 3 *The ceiling is a rate, not a stop*; §4.7 `ao restart`; §4.5a *Inbox row: restart*). `ao restart` refuses every seat (*a seat: rule 3 fills it when it is due, and Resume is the way to it*, `src/sessionorc/agent.py` `_restart_check`), and the *fills exhausted* row draws no Restart (`restartable` is false on a seat, `src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`), so a person at a terminal had no command to bring back a seat held at the ceiling, and the Inbox's way was Resume, which brings it back attended.

**Fix:** 1. **The RPC.** In `_restart_check` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py`) the seat test moves below the suspended, superseded and scheduled refusals, so a suspended seat at its fill ceiling gets the suspended refusal, and a superseded one names its successor. A seat whose `restart_ceiling` is `{why: fill}` (plain or `held`) is then not refused and returns from `_restart_check` at once, before the run-ended, state, launch-record, held-name, stop-time and gated-profile refusals (it creates nothing, and `_fill` waits on the gate as any fill does); `_restart_check` returns `{}` for it, and `rpc_restart` branches on the seat's `why: fill` mark straight after `_restart_check`, before `_refill_prompt` and the close; there `rpc_restart`, still a person's own, clears `restart_ceiling` and `fill_lapsed`, writes `restarts = [{at, why: person}]` (the seat's own `fill` entries go with it, as intended: its share of the count resets, and step 2's pass covers the fellows' fills), saves and pushes, creates and closes nothing, and returns the record's view. Every other seat keeps the refusal's words.
2. **The fill past the count.** In `_fill` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`), a seat whose `restarts` hold a `{why: person}` entry with no `fill` entry after it is filled whatever its fellows' count, once, when its cause is due; that fill is written `{why: fill}` as any other and counts from then.
3. **`ao restart`'s reply**: `cmd_restart` tells the cleared seat by the reply's `seat` (the RPC returns the seat's own view; a restart that created a record never returns a seat) and prints *<seat> · fills exhausted cleared — rule 3 fills it when it is due* (§4.7), not the new-record line.
4. **The row, not the card, and the hover.** The held mark's card hover (TD-1428) gains Restart: *it is yours now: Restart it from its Inbox row or `ao restart`, Resume it, or Forget it* (the card itself draws no Restart) (§4.5 screen 10). `restartable` (`src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`) is one flag that both the card's more ▾ (`card.html`) and the Inbox row (`inbox.py`, `inbox_row.html`) read; leave it false on every seat, so more ▾ keeps no Restart for a seat (§6 rule 2, §4.5a), and give the *fills exhausted* row its own test — a seat whose `restart_ceiling` says `why: fill` — so the row alone draws **Restart** beside Resume, calling the same `restart` RPC.
5. **The help.** Add one sentence to the **Restart** entry of §4.5a's help list and the same to `src/shiftlead/ui/help.py`'s `restart` text, in this PR (the test compares them word for word): *On a seat at its fill ceiling it clears the mark and starts nothing: the host agent fills the seat when it is next due.*
6. Tests: `restart` on a seat at the fill ceiling clears the mark and `fill_lapsed`, writes `[{why: person}]` and creates no record; the next tick fills the due seat though its fellows hold six fills in the hour, and the fill after that is counted; `restart` on a seat with no ceiling is refused as before, and on a suspended one with the suspended refusal; the row carries Restart for a fills-exhausted seat and the card's more ▾ does not. Run `grep -ln "4\.5a" tests/*.py` and the help tests.

**Done when:** A person's `ao restart <seat>`, or Restart on the *fills exhausted* row, clears the seat's fill ceiling and the seat is filled on the tick its cause is next due, past the count; `restart` on any other seat is refused as before; the tests in the Fix pass.
