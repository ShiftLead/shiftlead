Answer on the board item whose Context is TD-1470 decision (paul): Go, One more round, or Hold: <note>.

**Fix:** As §4.5 screen 6 and §4.5a *Queue card / Inbox: stuck row* say:
- A stuck row reads *stuck <age>: <the work> — <reason>*, the reason in `unanswered.words`' fixed words, or rule 13's own words for a team's `work_waiting.held`, `stalled` and `idle`. Several stuck rows with the same actor and reason: the first in full, the rest *— <actor>, as on <first work>*.
- Primary place: the card of the entry the work names (the question's first held ref by `item_home`, the board line's `Context:`, the entry the PR's claim names), a Do item; it makes the card's status *needs you* (§4.4 *The card's status*). Where the work already has a row (a decided line under *Waiting on them*) that row moves while stuck — to the Do group, or with no entry to the Inbox's *Needs you* — the stuck words a line under it, and goes back the tick an actor is able; where a stopped holder's state row names the same actor, a line under that row. Naming no entry: a row under the Inbox's *Needs you*, counted there. A decided line's or handed entry's row moved to the Do group or to *Needs you* while stuck is counted there while it is stuck (an answered row's *in no number* yields), so the tab reads the same whether or not the work already had a row (TD-1500 F92, fixed, not reviewed by TD-1470's rounds). Where a stopped holder's state row on the same card names the same actor, that row takes the line alone and the work's own row stays where it was, in no number: one counted item for one cause (TD-1500 F99, fixed, not reviewed).
- A `needs_you` actor's stuck words are a line under its own pending row (holder row or Inbox state row), never a row.
- Stuck work with `waiting_until` draws at *waiting* (under *Waiting on them*, in no number), not a need.
- A handed entry's *Waiting on them* row moves like a decided line. A holder row on another card: both drawn, the stuck row ending *— its holder row is on <TD-n>*.
- `item_home` gains a branch for a stuck row, its refs its work's; drawn as an item of its own, its `tied` is `{by: "stuck", ref: <its work's ref>}` (TD-1500 F96).
- **Open** goes to the actor's Focus; none for a `gone` actor, with no live actor or for a released PR.
- On a card, a stuck row drawn as an item of its own draws its Open alone — no Answer, Start session or ⋯ — and stays out of the header's Start session count, as a stopped holder's state row does; it is counted on the pill and the Queue's tab unless drawn at *waiting*, and a stuck line under another row is that row's, with no count of its own (§4.5 screen 12, §4.5a rows *answers*, *Start session*, *⋯*; TD-1500 F88, F89: the opening fact-check of TD-1453's sixth round, fixed, not reviewed by TD-1470's rounds).
- `ao card` prints its block with its `stuck` key as id and kind *stuck* (§4.7); `--json`'s `wants` carries it; the §4.5a row's help text goes into `help.py` (the doc-bound help list) with its tests.

**Done when:** A stuck reading draws as §4.5 screen 6 and §4.5a say, once. Test (TD-1470's Done when): an open ask whose only able seat is limited for more than `STUCK_ASK_AFTER` makes its entry's card read *needs you* with a stuck row whose reason is *limited*; a stuck decided line is a line under its row in the Do group, not a second row; a stuck ask with no entry is an Inbox *Needs you* row; the row goes the tick an actor is able. Two stuck works with one actor and reason on one card draw the second as *— <actor>, as on <first work>*; a `needs_you` actor's stuck words are a line under its own pending row. Also: stuck work with `waiting_until` draws at *waiting*, in no number; a stuck handed entry's row moves like a decided line's; a holder row on another card leaves both rows, the stuck one ending *— its holder row is on <TD-n>*; `ao card` prints the stuck row's block with its `stuck` key and kind *stuck*.
