**Why:** TD-1470's design (§6 rule 13 *Stuck work*, §4.4 *The card's status*, §4.5 screen 6, §4.5a *Queue card / Inbox: stuck row*): the stuck reading TD-1496 keeps is drawn to the person, once, as a need.

**Ask:** Go with TD-1497, a build of TD-1470's design as TD-1571's Hold revision left it (no PRs)? Answered on the board's end-of-rounds item for TD-1470 (Go | One more round | Hold: <note>).

**Fix:** As §4.5 screen 6 and §4.5a *Queue card / Inbox: stuck row* say:
- A stuck row reads *stuck <age>: <the work> — <reason>*, the reason in `unanswered.words`' fixed words, or rule 13's own words for a team's `work_waiting.held`, `stalled` and `idle`; `unfillable` draws its `why` as is, a line no lane takes *no lane takes it* (§4.5a). Several stuck rows with the same actor and reason: the first in full, the rest *— <actor>, as on <first work>*.
- Primary place: the card of the entry the work names (the question's first held ref by `item_home`, the board line's `Context:` as `item_home` homes it, a `Context:` naming a PR included, TD-1538), a Do item; it makes the card's status *needs you* (§4.4 *The card's status*). Where the work already has a row (a decided line under *Waiting on them*) that row moves while stuck and a need (at *waiting* it stays under *Waiting on them*, the stuck words its line, uncounted) — to the Do group, or homed on no card (`item_home`, TD-1538) to the Inbox's *Needs you* — the stuck words a line under it, and goes back the tick an actor acts (§6 rule 13 *Acting*, never `seat_able`); while the line's item carries `applying` (TD-1508) the row reads as the mark says and the stuck row makes no *needs you*; where a stopped holder's state row names the same actor, a line under that row. Naming no entry: a row under the Inbox's *Needs you*, counted there. A decided line's or handed entry's row moved to the Do group or to *Needs you* while stuck is counted there while it is stuck (an answered row's *in no number* yields), so the tab reads the same whether or not the work already had a row (TD-1500 F92, fixed, not reviewed by TD-1470's rounds). Where a stopped holder's state row on the same surface (one card, or both in the Inbox) names the same actor, that row takes the line alone and the work's own row stays where it was, in no number: one counted item for one cause (TD-1500 F99, fixed, not reviewed).
- A `needs_you` actor's stuck words are a line under its own pending row (holder row or Inbox state row), never a row.
- Stuck work with `waiting_until` draws at *waiting* (under *Waiting on them*, in no number), not a need.
- A handed entry's *Waiting on them* row moves like a decided line. A holder row on another card: both drawn, the stuck row ending *— its holder row is on <TD-n>*.
- `item_home` gains a branch for a stuck row, its refs its work's; drawn as an item of its own, its `tied` is `{by: "stuck", ref: <its work's ref>}` (TD-1500 F96).
- **Open** goes to the actor's Focus; none for a `gone` actor or a work order with no live actor.
- On a card, a stuck row drawn as an item of its own draws its Open alone — no Answer, Start session or ⋯ — and stays out of the header's Start session count, as a stopped holder's state row does; it is counted on the pill and the Queue's tab unless drawn at *waiting*, and a stuck line under another row is that row's, with no count of its own (§4.5 screen 12, §4.5a rows *answers*, *Start session*, *⋯*; TD-1500 F88, F89: the opening fact-check of TD-1453's sixth round, fixed, not reviewed by TD-1470's rounds).
- `ao card` prints its block with its `stuck` key as id and kind *stuck* (§4.7); `--json`'s `wants` carries it; the §4.5a row's help text goes into `help.py` (the doc-bound help list) with its tests.

Which of a seat's claims carries its holder row (so which card a stuck line meets it on) is TD-1468's rule: its declared claims first (§4.5 screen 6, TD-1523).

**Done when:** A stuck reading draws as §4.5 screen 6 and §4.5a say, once. Tests: a stuck decided line at *waiting* stays under *Waiting on them*, uncounted; a stuck line whose item carries `applying` makes no *needs you*. Test (TD-1470's Done when): an open ask whose only able seat is limited for more than `STUCK_ASK_AFTER` makes its entry's card read *needs you* with a stuck row whose reason is *limited*; a stuck decided line is a line under its row in the Do group, not a second row; a stuck ask with no entry is an Inbox *Needs you* row; a stuck line on a card whose actor's holder row is in the Inbox, and a stuck line in the Inbox whose actor's holder row is on a card, each draw both rows, the stuck words ending *— its holder row is in the Inbox* and *— its holder row is on <TD-n>*; a stuck line with no `Context:` and its actor's holder row both in the Inbox draw one counted item, the line under the holder's row; the row goes the tick an actor acts (§6 rule 13 *Acting*, never `seat_able`). Two stuck works with one actor and reason on one card draw the second as *— <actor>, as on <first work>*; a `needs_you` actor's stuck words are a line under its own pending row. Also: stuck work with `waiting_until` draws at *waiting*, in no number; a stuck handed entry's row moves like a decided line's; a holder row on another card leaves both rows, the stuck one ending *— its holder row is on <TD-n>*; `ao card` prints the stuck row's block with its `stuck` key and kind *stuck*.
