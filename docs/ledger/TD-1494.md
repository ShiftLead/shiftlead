**Why:** Build TD-1477 (design §4.5 *Resume on a member confirms too*, §4.5a *Focus (exited / closed)*, *Inbox row: restart*, *Inbox row: state*). A one-press **Resume** of an exited member that ran unattended starts it attended with no word that its mail will no longer wake it; on 2026-11-06 the techlead seat's record was made by such a Resume and slept through its mail for about 11h. TD-1440 made Take over on a member confirm; the same press by another name did not.

**Fix:** - **The test** (`src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`, beside `mode_confirm`): two view fields read from the record `s`, as `mode_confirm` is: **`resume_confirm`**, non-empty where the record is an agent record (`d["agent"]`) holding a tool session id (`adapter_id`), `exited` or `closed`, and a member — `team` set or `controllers` non-empty, and not `by_person` — the test `mode_confirm` uses, **whatever mode the record ended in** (a member held and then exited would come back held again, design §4.5). Its text: *Resume `<name>` attended? While you hold it, its mail does not wake it, and asks to it wait for you. Hand back returns it. To resume it unattended, cancel and use Resume with changes… on its page, with Unattended ticked.* — one string on every site (`<name>` the record's `name`, else its id); a seat gets the same text and nothing more (design §4.5: whether it would be filled instead depends on its marks and run). Empty everywhere else: a person's own session (`by_person`), a non-member, a record with no agent (`d["agent"]` false), a live record (the restart row can stand on one; its Resume lands on the form unasked). And **`member_hold`**, the shared sentences alone where `resume_confirm` is non-empty, else empty. **One constant** in `cards.py` holds the shared sentences *While you hold it, its mail does not wake it, and asks to it wait for you. Hand back returns it.*, read by `mode_confirm`, `resume_confirm` and Reopen and push's append (joined by a space), so the three sites cannot drift.
- **Where it is drawn** — every one-press Resume of a record, as `data-confirm` (the page's click handler already asks it, `app.js` *`b.dataset.confirm && !confirm(…)`*):
  - the ending banner's **Resume** (`src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js`, the exited/closed banner), set from the view on every render so a change of membership while Focus is open is followed, as `mode_confirm` is, the name in it escaped with `esc()` (the banner is built as an `innerHTML` string; the Inbox rows get Jinja's escaping); **Resume with changes…**, **New session here**, **Forget** and **Restart** carry none;
  - the Inbox **restart** row's **Resume** and the **state row for a record exited with unpushed work** (`row == 'unpushed'`): its **Resume** and its **Reopen and push**, in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html`. Both rows are built from the card view by `base(v, …)` in `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`: carry `v["resume_confirm"]` and `v["member_hold"]` on the row there, and nothing else — no second copy of the rule. *Reopen and push* keeps its own confirm and, where the row's `member_hold` is non-empty, appends it after a space (*While you hold it, its mail does not wake it, and asks to it wait for you. Hand back returns it.*); elsewhere its text is unchanged.
  - The confirm comes before the press wherever it lands: a resume that cannot be silent (`resume_blocked`, a refusal) still lands on the form after it, and the form's *Unattended* box decides. An Inbox row's Resume of a record with no `adapter_id` carries no confirm (the field is empty) and lands on the form as today.
- **The Resumable page** (§4.5a *Resumable*) is not built in `src/` today; its Resume is the banner's and carries `resume_confirm` when it is built. Nothing to do here.
- **Unchanged**: `resume_create` (still never `unattended`; the seat carried, TD-1435), the form's Resume with *Unattended*, **Restart**, and `ao new --resume`, which asks nothing.
- **Help** (`src/shiftlead/ui/help.py`, `Resume`, and §4.5a's help list item **Resume**, which the doc-bound tests compare): add one sentence after *a resume is a start, which a manager may act on and which has to be closed again.* — *On a team's member it asks first: while you hold it, its mail does not wake it. Restart, where drawn, puts it back unattended.* Focus's member paragraph (`src/shiftlead/ui/templates/focus.html`, `#fmemberback`: *Resume starts it attended, under you*) gains *, and asks first*. Change them in the same PR and run the doc-bound tests (`grep -ln 4.5a tests`).
- **Tests** (beside `mode_confirm`'s in `tests/test_ui_phone_focus.py`; the repo has no DOM test of app.js, so the app.js checks are source asserts as TD-1440's are — the banner line sets `data-confirm` from `v.resume_confirm` through `esc()`, and the handler's `b.dataset.confirm && !confirm(…)` stands before the `resume` and `reopen-push` branches; the Inbox rows are a Jinja render test whose `data-confirm` equals the row's fields): `resume_confirm` is set on an exited member (team; controllers only) that ended unattended **and** on one that ended interactive, it is empty on a `by_person` record, a non-member, a record with no `adapter_id`, a record with no agent, and a live record; a seat gets the member text and no more; a `closed` member and a controllers-only member get it on the Inbox rows too; a name with a quote in it is escaped in the banner's `data-confirm`; a member resumed unsupervised (now `by_person`) still offers Hand back on its card (`mode_act`); the banner's Resume carries it as `data-confirm` and drops it when the view clears it; the restart row's and the unpushed row's Resume carry it, and Reopen and push carries the appended sentence on a member and its unchanged text on a non-member, the appended text equal to the constant; a press of each row's Resume and Reopen and push goes through the page's `data-confirm` handler, and Cancel sends no create; **Restart** (the banner, the restart row, a card's more ▾) carries no `resume_confirm`; a member resumed unsupervised (`by_person` after) is not woken by mail while interactive (`mail_wakes` false).
