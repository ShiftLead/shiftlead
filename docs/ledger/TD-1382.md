## TD-1382: A sender withdraws its own open question to the person with ao msg --withdraw

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1379's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1379
**Location:** design §4.10 *Withdrawing a question*, *One way of being closed*, *Outcomes*; §4.7 `ao msg`; §4.5 screen 6 FYI; §4.5a *Focus side panel: Sent*, *Inbox row: `note` and the rest of FYI*; the code: `rpc_msg`/`_msg`, `rpc_inbox_delete` and `_close_entry` in `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`, the person's own RPCs (`rpc_inbox_snooze`, `rpc_inbox_pause`, `rpc_inbox_resume`, `rpc_inbox_go_with_it`) in `src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`, `CLOSED_REASONS` in `src/sessionorc/models.py`, `MAILBOX` in `src/sessionorc/modes.py` (the mail RPCs a node forwards to the home; `pass_up` is registered there as `pass_up`, the precedent for `msg_withdraw`), `ao msg`'s parser in the CLI, the FYI words in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (`state_line`) and the Focus Inbox panel's in `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`AO.mailEntry`'s `closed_reason` chain; the Sent card of §4.5a is TD-964's and not built, so its *withdrawn* lands with that card, from the same chain), its `unmail` handler there (the words after a Delete) and the route it calls, `POST /api/person/unmail` in `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (which today returns `declined` alone), `src/shiftlead/skill.md` (`ao --skill`).

**Why:** a sender that put a question to the person by mistake has no way to take it back — Delete (`declined`) is the person's alone, and an `--outcome dropped --for` settles only a question the person answered — so on 2026-11-04 both copies of ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead's duplicate question stayed on the Queue until Paul answered both (TD-1379).

**Fix:** as §4.10 *Withdrawing a question* says: a `msg_withdraw` RPC and `ao msg --withdraw <id>` (the id its only word, as `--pass-up` takes one); the home checks the entry is an `ask` or `steer` in the person inbox, open, and the caller's own (its `from_` a record whose `superseded_by` chain reaches the caller, `message_gate`'s `mine` read with `mail.chain` and `_successor`), then closes it `withdrawn` on every copy and clears `paused_at`; no `system` note, no notification; the section's refusals in its words and in its order (the person, no record, not in the person inbox, not the caller's, a `note` or `reply`, closed), the person and a caller with no record among them (as `pass_up` refuses them); a second withdraw of the caller's own withdrawn entry answers as the first did; Reply, Go with it, Pause, Resume, Snooze and Send to reviewer (`rpc_inbox_hand`, whose open check today says *already answered*) on a withdrawn entry refused with *m-… was withdrawn by its sender at <time>: nothing waits on an answer*, the withdrawn check placed first in each (before `rpc_inbox_pause`'s and `rpc_inbox_go_with_it`'s closed checks, before Snooze's `kind == "steer"` check, and in `_msg`'s reply branch before the reply is addressed, Reply having no closed check today but the orphan's (*its asker is gone*) — that branch refuses any replier's `--reply-to` on a withdrawn entry, a seat's on its passed-up copy included); a notification already sent is followed by none, and nothing cancels one (an `ask` withdrawn inside `NOTIFY_HOLD` is never told); `inbox_delete` takes its closed branch and removes it, returning the entry's `closed_reason` beside `declined: false`, the `/api/person/unmail` route passes `closed_reason` through, and `unmail` reads it to say *withdrawn by its sender — removed* (Dismiss keeps working); on this reason alone — a Reply to a lapsed or declined question stays a late reply, Snooze on other closed entries unrefused; a passed-up copy is the asker's to withdraw, every copy closing; a look with a reviewer withdraws as any question, its handed entry standing and owing, `snoozed_for`/`snoozed_until` left as they are; the RPC returns `{id: person, msg, closed_reason: "withdrawn", closed_at}` and the CLI prints *m-… withdrawn: the person meets it in FYI* with no *when it is read* line; `msg_withdraw` joins `MAILBOX`; `withdrawn` added to `CLOSED_REASONS`; `_owing_question` unchanged — its *closed as <reason>: nothing is owed on a question nobody answered* already meets a withdrawn entry, and a test pins that; the Inbox's FYI row reads *withdrawn by <sender>* (the sender's name as the row already draws it, passed to `state_line`) and `AO.mailEntry` *withdrawn* (the Sent card's word, once TD-964 builds it); `ao --skill`'s Mail section names the verb.

**Done when:** tests that a sender's withdraw closes its own open `ask` and its own paused `steer` as `withdrawn` (on the person's copy and its outbox, `paused_at` cleared, no `system` note written), that it is refused on another sender's question, an orphaned one, a `note`, a closed one and an entry not in the person inbox, that a person's Reply, Go with it, Pause, Resume, Snooze and Send to reviewer on a withdrawn entry are refused naming the withdraw while its Dismiss removes it and a Delete on it removes it returning `closed_reason: withdrawn`, that the person and an unrecorded caller are refused, that a repeat withdraw answers as the first, that the lapse sweep passes a withdrawn `steer`, that a withdrawn entry frees its sender's open-question slot in the depths and counts among its FYIs until retention, that `_owing_question` refuses it with its existing *closed as withdrawn: nothing is owed …* line, that `/api/person/unmail` returns `closed_reason` and the page's toast reads *withdrawn by its sender — removed*, that `ao msg --withdraw` prints its one line with no *when it is read* line and refuses text and every other `msg` option beside it — addressees, `--kind`, `--about`, `--reply-to`, `--subject`, `--answer`, `--default`, `--bound`, `--thread` and the rest, `--json` excepted as on every subcommand (`_pass_up` in the CLI is the precedent, refusing text, `--reply-to`, `--kind` and `--about`), that an `ask` withdrawn inside `NOTIFY_HOLD` is never notified, that the Inbox's FYI row (`state_line`) reads *withdrawn by <sender>* and `AO.mailEntry` *withdrawn*, that withdrawing a passed-up question closes the seat's copy too, that a withdrawn look's handed entry still owes, that a seat's `--reply-to` on a passed-up copy the asker withdrew is refused, and that from a node the withdraw is forwarded while the link is up and refused in words while it is down; `ao --skill` prints the verb; and the doc-bound tests pass.
