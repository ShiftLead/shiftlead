## TD-1382: A sender withdraws its own open question to the person with ao msg --withdraw

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1379's design)
**Owner:** grinder

**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1379
**Location:** design §4.10 *Withdrawing a question*, *One way of being closed*, *Outcomes*; §4.7 `ao msg`; §4.5 screen 6 FYI; §4.5a *Focus side panel: Sent*, *Inbox row: `note` and the rest of FYI`; the code: `rpc_msg`/`_msg`, `rpc_inbox_delete` and `_close_entry` in `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`, the person's own RPCs (`rpc_inbox_snooze`, `rpc_inbox_pause`, `rpc_inbox_resume`, `rpc_inbox_go_with_it`) in `src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`, `CLOSED_REASONS` in `src/sessionorc/models.py`, `MAILBOX` in `src/sessionorc/modes.py` (the mail RPCs a node forwards to the home; `pass_up` is registered there as `pass_up`, the precedent for `msg_withdraw`), `ao msg`'s parser in the CLI, the FYI words in `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (`state_line`) and the Sent card's in `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`AO.mailEntry`'s `closed_reason` chain), `src/shiftlead/skill.md` (`ao --skill`).

**Why:** a sender that put a question to the person by mistake has no way to take it back — Delete (`declined`) is the person's alone, and an `--outcome dropped --for` settles only a question the person answered — so on 2026-11-04 both copies of ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead's duplicate question stayed on the Queue until Paul answered both (TD-1379).

**Fix:** as §4.10 *Withdrawing a question* says: a `msg_withdraw` RPC and `ao msg --withdraw <id>` (the id its only word, as `--pass-up` takes one); the home checks the entry is an `ask` or `steer` in the person inbox, open, and the caller's own along its `superseded_by` chain, then closes it `withdrawn` on every copy and clears `paused_at`; no `system` note, no notification; the four refusals in the section's words; Reply, Go with it, Pause, Resume and Snooze on a withdrawn entry refused with *m-… was withdrawn by its sender at <time>: nothing waits on an answer*, while `inbox_delete` takes its closed branch and removes it (Dismiss keeps working; the page says *withdrawn by its sender — removed* on a Delete pressed on a stale row); on this reason alone — a Reply to a lapsed or declined question stays a late reply, Snooze on other closed entries unrefused; a passed-up copy is the asker's to withdraw, every copy closing; a look with a reviewer withdraws as any question, its handed entry standing and owing, `snoozed_for`/`snoozed_until` left as they are; the RPC returns `{id: person, msg, closed_reason: "withdrawn", closed_at}` and the CLI prints *m-… withdrawn: the person meets it in FYI* with no *when it is read* line; `msg_withdraw` joins `MAILBOX`; `withdrawn` added to `CLOSED_REASONS`; `_owing_question`'s refusal reads *closed as withdrawn: nothing is owed*; the Inbox's FYI row reads *withdrawn by <sender>* and the Sent card *withdrawn*; `ao --skill`'s Mail section names the verb.

**Done when:** tests that a sender's withdraw closes its own open `ask` and its own paused `steer` as `withdrawn` (on the person's copy and its outbox, `paused_at` cleared, no `system` note written), that it is refused on another sender's question, an orphaned one, a `note`, a closed one and an entry not in the person inbox, that a person's Reply on a withdrawn entry is refused naming the withdraw while its Dismiss removes it, that withdrawing a passed-up question closes the seat's copy too, that a withdrawn look's handed entry still owes, and that from a node the withdraw is forwarded while the link is up and refused in words while it is down; `ao --skill` prints the verb; and the doc-bound tests pass.
