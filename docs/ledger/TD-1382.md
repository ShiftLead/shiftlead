## TD-1382: A sender withdraws its own open question to the person with ao msg --withdraw

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04 (the designer, from TD-1379's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1379
**Location:** design §4.10 *Withdrawing a question*, *One way of being closed*, *Outcomes*; §4.7 `ao msg`; §4.5 screen 6 FYI; §4.5a *Focus side panel: Sent*, *Inbox row: `note` and the rest of FYI`; the code: `rpc_msg` and the person-inbox RPCs (`inbox_delete`, `inbox_pause`, `inbox_snooze`, Go with it) in `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py`, `_close_entry` there, `CLOSED_REASONS` in `src/sessionorc/models.py`, `ao msg`'s parser in the CLI, the FYI and Sent words in `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js` (`closed_reason` → word), `src/shiftlead/skill.md` (`ao --skill`).

**Why:** a sender that put a question to the person by mistake has no way to take it back — Delete (`declined`) is the person's alone, and an `--outcome dropped --for` settles only a question the person answered — so on 2026-11-04 both copies of ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead's duplicate question stayed on the Queue until Paul answered both (TD-1379).

**Fix:** as §4.10 *Withdrawing a question* says: a `msg_withdraw` RPC and `ao msg --withdraw <id>` (the id its only word, as `--pass-up` takes one); the home checks the entry is an `ask` or `steer` in the person inbox, open, and the caller's own along its `superseded_by` chain, then closes it `withdrawn` on every copy and clears `paused_at`; no `system` note, no notification; the four refusals in the section's words; Reply, Go with it, Pause, Resume, Snooze and Delete on a withdrawn entry refused with *m-… was withdrawn by its sender at <time>: nothing waits on an answer*; `withdrawn` added to `CLOSED_REASONS`; `_owing_question`'s refusal reads *closed as withdrawn: nothing is owed*; the Inbox's FYI row reads *withdrawn by <sender>* and the Sent card *withdrawn*; `ao --skill`'s Mail section names the verb.

**Done when:** tests that a sender's withdraw closes its own open `ask` and its own paused `steer` as `withdrawn` (on the person's copy and its outbox, `paused_at` cleared, no `system` note written), that it is refused on another sender's question, an orphaned one, a `note`, a closed one and an entry not in the person inbox, and that a person's Reply on a withdrawn entry is refused naming the withdraw; `ao --skill` prints the verb; and the doc-bound tests pass.
