## TD-2127: Tell the person when memory runs short or processes outlive their sessions

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** cc25636b92cd4dd7b40edf094c001277
**Part of:** TD-2080

**Why:** Part of TD-2080: swap reached 100% on 2026-11-23 and nothing told anyone, since TD-2083's warning covers temporary space alone. It was found by reading the machine: 128 orphaned processes, up to 36 days old, held about 3.6 GB of memory and 2.75 GB of swap. TD-2067 and TD-2102 close two causes, and nothing would notice the next one.

**Design PR:** #4462

**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py, src/sessionorc/diskbook.py, src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py, design §4.6

**Fix:** Design a reading of memory and swap, and of processes of this user that have lost their parent and look like ShiftLead's own (a deleted working directory, a test or walk command), with a warning once per episode that names the largest holders. Settle the thresholds and how a process is judged ours. This reports only: it kills nothing, since a session never kills a process it did not start without the person's yes.

**Done when:** With the readings stubbed, low memory or a held orphan raises one row and one message naming the largest holders, does not repeat within the episode, and clears when the reading recovers. No process is signalled. A person's detached job or a service that is legitimately parented by init is not named as ShiftLead's.
