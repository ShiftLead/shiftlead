**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-24: an orange Queue card should mean something needs him now. The flows card was orange for TD-2078's question (compare the flows after three weeks) while that entry was also blocked by TD-2077 and TD-2073, so his answer could start nothing for weeks. Design 4.4 (the passage on *waiting, then yours*) counts a blocker as open work only when it does not itself carry `decision (paul)`, and accepts that a member blocked only by such blockers is asked at once; both of TD-2078's blockers carried the gate, and TD-2073 also waits on seven open builds of its own, which the rule does not read. Beside it, TD-2164's question, whose build waits on TD-2053, was held back as *waiting, then yours*, so the two read as inconsistent to him. Also, TD-2078's question was a ledger `Ask` with no board item and no `Answers`, and the card offered only Lift the hold, with no yes to press; he pressed it and typed "go ahead". Paul, later the same day: an early question is fine, and may be orange, where its answer keeps work moving (TD-2164's would let a further review round run beside TD-2053); where the entry still waits on something, the orange card belongs in the Queue's Waiting group and not among the cards that need him now.

**Design PR:** #4571

**Location:** docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md (waiting, then yours; Where Lift the hold is drawn)

**Fix:** Design first, in 4.4 (*waiting, then yours* and *Where Lift the hold is drawn*) and screen 12. Keep a question askable early, since an early answer can keep work moving, and decide where its card is drawn: a card whose only need of the person is a question on an entry that still waits on something (open work, or a blocker that carries the person's gate and itself waits) stays orange and sits in the Waiting group, apart from the cards that need the person now. Decide the same for a question held today as *waiting, then yours*, which is not offered at all: whether it too is offered early there. Say what counts as a wait for this, and how the header's count of what needs the person reads such a card. Decide what a ledger question with no board item and no answers of its own offers in the answer bar, so a yes or no question has a yes to press beside Lift the hold. File the builds.
