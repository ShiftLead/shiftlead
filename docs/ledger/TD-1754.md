**Why:** Design §4.4 (docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md, the gate-sweep paragraph near line 326), changed by #3648 (TD-1751), now narrates the old bug in the design: "one `lift/TD-n/until` for every `until` of an entry found an earlier day's lift, closed or merged, and its branch, which a close leaves on origin, so an entry re-gated to a later day was asked about and never lifted (TD-1751; …)". That is the dated record of how the rule came to be, in the past tense. CLAUDE.md says the design is written in the present tense and the dated fact goes to `docs/history/TD-<n>.md` under the same section; #3648 did add `docs/history/TD-1751.md` with the same account, so the design carries it twice. The sentence also breaks the paragraph's own line, "It keeps `gate_sweep.py`'s branch names and rules", and is hard to read: its colon runs into "no lift while a PR on its branch is open or merged…", which belongs to the rule, not the aside. The two touched lines are also unwrapped (lines 322 and 329 run past the section's ~110-column wrap).

**Location:** docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md (gate sweep, lift branch)

**Fix:** Reword the paragraph in present tense, with the rule only: the home keeps `gate_sweep.py`'s branch names and rules, but an `until` gate's lift branch carries the date (`lift/TD-n/until-<date>`), so a lift for one day's gate never reads another day's closed or merged PR; a by-hand lift on `…/until` still counts while open, by the rule on another open PR's lift. Drop "found an earlier day's lift…" and the TD-1751 parenthesis from the design (the history file already holds them), and rewrap the two long lines. No behaviour change.

**Done when:** Design §4.4's gate-sweep paragraph states the until-branch rule in the present tense with no account of the old bug; docs/history/TD-1751.md keeps the dated fact.
