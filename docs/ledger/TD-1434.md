## TD-1434: A team seat switched to interactive by a stray Take over sleeps through its mail, and nothing records or raises the switch

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06 (ao-led-tds-ledger, through the techlead seat; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** b55d5f3242ea4e2a8e608a26a0ace3ad
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent.py (`rpc_set_mode`); src/sessionorc/mail.py (`mail_wakes`); src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js and Focus's header (Take over, `data-act=mode`); design §4.5a *Focus: Take over*, §4.10, §4.4 *The card's status*

**Why:** Asked by the person's session `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-abff6dca44a2). A team seat was switched to interactive and nobody meant to do it, and after that no mail woke it. Seen 2026-11-06: `ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1`'s record went `unattended: False` some time after its last doorbell wake (05:39:56Z). Five messages then arrived (05:56Z from `ao-led-tds-ledger`, 05:57Z from `grinder-ao-2`, two asks from the person at 17:19Z and 17:3xZ, and a note), and none of them woke it, because `mail_wakes()` (`src/sessionorc/mail.py`) never rings an interactive session. The seat sat idle for about 11h, and only the card's unread chip showed it. Paul does not remember switching it. He had opened Focus on the seat from the Sessions page to watch it, and Focus's header carries **Take over** (`data-act=mode`), so a stray press is the likely cause. He pressed **Hand back** at about 17:40Z and the seat woke.

Three gaps:
1. `rpc_set_mode` (`src/sessionorc/agent.py`) logs nothing and keeps no record field, so a flip cannot be traced to a press, a time or a caller.
2. When a team member (a seat with a role) is left interactive with unread asks, nothing shows it beyond the chip.
3. **Take over** on a watched seat takes effect at once, with no confirm and no word that it stops the seat's doorbell.

**Fix:** (1), the trace, needs no design: `rpc_set_mode` writes a log line (the session, the new mode, the caller) and the record keeps when its mode last changed and who changed it, as `mode_at` and `mode_by` or the record's existing event log if it has one, shown on Focus's side panel.

(2) and (3) need the design first (§4.5a *Focus: Take over*, §4.10 *Mail* on what wakes an interactive session, §4.4 *The card's status*). Proposals from the ask:
- a needs-you on the seat's card, or a note in the person's inbox, once a seat with a role has been interactive with unread asks for more than N minutes, or when its teammates' asks to it go unanswered;
- **Take over** on a seat with a role either asks to confirm, or shows an undo toast that says it stops the seat's doorbell.

Settle these in a design round with Paul, then file the build entries.

**Done when:** A mode change has a log line and a record field naming when it happened and who made it. The design says what a seat left interactive with unread asks raises, and what **Take over** on a seat with a role shows. Build entries carry both, each with `Part of: TD-1434`.
