## TD-2261: The design does not say what the director shows when none of its logins can be used

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-26
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 6e457fa5ca494b6fa46f6f757f6e7132

**Why:** Audit of #4737 (TD-2068 slice 8). After the cut-over the director's fill picks from its record's list, and where none fits, `_builtin_login` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`) fills nothing and sets `self.unfillable[s.id]` to *no login usable* or *cannot run*; `tests/test_director_on_login.py` (`test_no_login_fit_for_it_fills_nothing_and_says_why`) asserts it, and #4737's description says "With none fit, nothing is filled, and the record is marked". The director's record carries no `builtin` (§4.9d *How each one runs*), and the design gives these two words to built-in seats alone:

- §4.9b *A seat that cannot answer is routed around* (`docs/design/4.9b-techlead.md:871`): "after the cut-over a built-in seat's *no login usable* or *cannot run*, §4.9d".
- §6's stuck reasons (`docs/design/6-policies.md:1662`): "after the cut-over a built-in seat's *no login usable* and *cannot run*, §4.9d *The login a seat starts on*".
- §4.9d *The login a seat starts on* reads the set's list, which the director does not use.
- §4.9e *The launch on a login*, **The director seat**, says it "is launched by the same pick" and passes over a login that leaves its prompts on, and nothing of what the seat reads when no login of its list is usable or offers its model.

The same section of §4.9b (`docs/design/4.9b-techlead.md:1299`) still describes the key as "its profile and brief" with no word that after the cut-over it is `model` and `logins` (§5, §4.9e).

**Location:** docs/design/4.9e-switch-over.md; docs/design/4.9b-techlead.md; docs/design/6-policies.md

**Fix:** In §4.9e **The director seat**, say that with no login of its list usable nothing is filled and the seat is unfillable as *no login usable*, and with none offering its model as *cannot run*; name the director beside the built-in seats in §4.9b's and §6's lists; say in §4.9b *The director seat* that after the cut-over the key holds `model` and `logins` in place of `profile`. Each with its line in `docs/history/` under the section.

**Done when:** §4.9e **The director seat** says what its fill reads when no login of its list fits, §4.9b's and §6's lists of refusal words name the director with the built-in seats, and §4.9b *The director seat* names `model` and `logins` for after the cut-over.
