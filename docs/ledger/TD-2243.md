**Why:** #4691 (TD-2184 slice 3) added `line_early` and `mail_early` in src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py, which decide whether a board line or a lifting question is drawn as asked early and which wait its line names. The tests give every early member the same line, put only gated entries on the board line, and give every ask a stamped `repo` and no `orphaned` stamp.

Each change below, made alone at cd2826f33, left `tests/test_queue*.py`, `test_go_now_*.py`, `test_ui_inbox*.py`, `test_ui_repo*.py` and `test_ui_queue*.py` green (837 passed); each probe passed on the unchanged code.

- `line_early`: the first member in key order replaced by `members[0]`, and separately `min` by `max`. A probe's line over members with different lines then named another entry's wait than the lowest key's. The design: docs/design/4.4-host-agent.md, "An open undecided `decide` line all of whose covered members are asked early is drawn early in the same way, in their place"; the function's docstring: the first such member's `early_line` in key order.
- `line_early`: `and ledger_mod.decided_by(…, PERSON)` dropped. A probe's line listing, in `Parts:`, an entry with no gate beside an early one then read as no early line, so it would be drawn as a need.
- `mail_early`: `or e.get("orphaned")` dropped. A probe's orphaned `ask --lift` was then marked early, which draws it grey on its card with no pill.
- `mail_early`: the `askers` fallback dropped. A probe's ask with no stamped `repo` (an entry sent before the stamp, or one whose sender resolved to none: `MailEntry.repo` in src/sessionorc/models.py) and a bare `TD-n` then lost the mark, so it would be drawn as a need.

With four of these (`members[0]`, `decided_by`, `orphaned`, `askers`) and TD-2242's change applied together, `pytest -n 8` over the whole suite passed at cd2826f33 (10212 passed, 24 skipped), and with the same five changes on e87543062 the `test_queue*`, `test_go_now_*`, `test_ui_inbox*`, `test_ui_repo*`, `test_ui_queue*` and `test_ui_answered_hold` modules passed (863 passed).

**Fix:** In tests/test_queue_asked_early_items.py: a covering line over two early members whose lines differ, the lower key named second, asserting the lower key's line; one whose `Parts:` lists an ungated entry, still early; and for `mail_early`, an orphaned ask (none) and an ask with no `repo` read through `askers` (its line).
