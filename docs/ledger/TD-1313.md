## TD-1313: #2797's cap of four branch fetches in flight is in `shots.py` and its test, not in §4.10

**Priority:** low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-02 (docs-audit-ao-1, auditing #2797)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/shots.py` (`FETCHES_MAX`, `_start_fetch`); `docs/design/4.10-messages.md` (*The fetch never holds a read up*, ~1380–1400); `docs/design-history.md` (§4.10)

**Why:** #2797 added `FETCHES_MAX = 4` ("fetches in flight at once, over every branch") and `_start_fetch` starts no fetch while four run ("else a later read starts it"); `tests/test_shots_commit.py:248–253` asserts it (`assert len(started) <= shots.FETCHES_MAX`). §4.10's background-fetch paragraph describes the 30 s bound, one fetch at a time per `(repo, ref)`, and `SHOT_TTL` for `last_end`, and says a miss answers at once until a fetch has ended. It says nothing of a cap across branches, and `grep -rn "FETCHES_MAX" docs` finds nothing. The cap changes what a reader sees (a look on a fifth branch gets no fetch until one ends, so it answers a miss longer than the design states), and CLAUDE.md's rule is that the design changes in the same PR, present tense, the dated fact in `docs/design-history.md`.

**Fix:** add the cap to §4.10's paragraph (at most four branch fetches run at once on a reader; a read that would start a fifth starts none, and a later read does), with a dated line in `docs/design-history.md` under §4.10 above the section's newest lines. Docs only.

**Done when** §4.10 states the cap and the history carries its line.
