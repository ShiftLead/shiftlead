**Why:** TD-1422's design (§6 rule 3 *The ceiling is a rate, not a stop*; §4.9b *The rule for the seat is the tick's*). On 2026-11-05 techlead-ao-1 was filled six times in an hour, once for each ask, closed after `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE` each time, tripped `FILL_CEILING` at 15:04:38Z and was never filled again: `_fill` returns at once on any `restart_ceiling` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`), so eight asks waited seven hours until the person pressed Resume. The design makes the tripping seat's mark lapse after `FILL_WINDOW`, holds a second trip running for a person, and keeps a seat whose trigger is `asks` open `SEAT_ASK_LINGER` idle so a trickle of asks is one fill.

**Fix:** All in the tick (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, `src/sessionorc/agent_common.py`, `src/sessionorc/models.py`) and the row's and card's words; nothing here touches the `restart` RPC (TD-1429).
1. **The field.** Add `fill_lapsed: dict | None` (`{at}`) to `Session` (`models.py`): in `HOME_OWNED` beside `restart_ceiling` (design §4.4a's list names it), and through `to_dict`/`from_dict`. `_replay` carries it to the record any replay creates (a fill, a flow's restart, a start, a lost brief's relaunch) where it sets `new.restarts = history` (`agent_tick.py`, its one site), whatever the replay's `why`. It is never an entry in `restarts`: every reader of `restarts` (`_counted`, `NOT_RESTARTS`, `cards.restarted_view`'s chip, `ending.restart_words`) stays as it is.
2. **The lapse.** In `_seat_pass`, after the `seat_due` save and the link check and before the state branch — on every pass that gets that far, cause due or not, so the row leaves on time; a suspended or sat-out seat, which the pass leaves earlier, keeps its mark — a seat whose `restart_ceiling` is `{why: fill}` without `held`, its `at` at least `FILL_WINDOW` old, has the mark cleared and `fill_lapsed = {at: now}` written, saved and pushed; the pass then goes on as for a seat with no mark (filled if ended and due, under the same count). A `why: lost-brief` mark, a crash ceiling and a `held` mark are left as they are.
3. **The held second trip.** Where `_fill` writes `restart_ceiling = {at, count, why: fill}`, it adds `held: true` when every counted fill (the `why: fill` entries inside `FILL_WINDOW` over the fellows) is newer than the newest `fill_lapsed.at` among those fellows and that lapse is no more than `2 × FILL_WINDOW` old. With no such lapse, or a counted fill older than it, the mark is plain.
4. **The linger.** Add `SEAT_ASK_LINGER = timedelta(minutes=15)` beside `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE` in `agent_common.py` (re-exported from `agent.py` as the others are). `_seat_has_run` uses it in place of `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE` for a seat whose `seat.trigger` is `asks` (the techlead and the fact-checker presets), counted from `idle_from` as now; every other seat keeps `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE`. An ask that lands on the lingering idle seat rings it as mail rings any idle session, and `asks_waiting` > 0 keeps it open as today. A ring refused (wake budget spent, profile gated) leaves the ask waiting as on any idle seat: nothing new is built for it.
5. **The words** (Restart joins the held hover in TD-1429, when the press works on a seat). `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`'s *fills exhausted* reason and the card's line (`src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`, §4.5 screen 10) read — in place of today's dash clause (*— the seats beside it were filled as often as the host agent will*), so the row reads exactly the card's line — *fills exhausted · <n> in 1 h · fills again after the hour* on a plain mark and *fills exhausted · <n> in 1 h · tripped twice running — yours* on a `held` one; the card's hover says, for a plain mark, that the host agent fills it again after the hour, and for a held one *it is yours now: Resume it, or Forget it*. The row's fixed caption (`inbox_row.html`, *the host agent will not restart it — yours now*) reads *fills again after the hour* on a plain fill mark and stays as it is on every other restart row, the held mark's included (§4.5a). The row leaves when the mark is cleared, as it does now.
6. **Telegram** (§4.10 *What is told*): in `src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py` `_restart_at`, a seat's plain fill mark returns `""` at once (not told, and not falling through to a `restart_blocked` on the same record) and a `held` one returns its `at`, so *told once per spell*, keyed on `at`, tells the held mark once.
7. Tests: a plain mark is cleared (and kept on a suspended seat), and `fill_lapsed` written, on the first pass after `FILL_WINDOW` though no cause is due; the seat is then filled when its cause is due; a trip whose counted fills all postdate the group's `fill_lapsed` writes `held` and never lapses; a trip counting an older fill, or after a lapse older than two hours, is plain; a `lost-brief` mark never lapses; `fill_lapsed` survives a fill; an `asks` seat idle 3 minutes with nothing waiting is not closed and is closed at 15; a `team` (manager on call) seat still closes at `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE`; the plain mark is not pushed to Telegram and the held one is.
