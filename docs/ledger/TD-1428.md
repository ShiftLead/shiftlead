## TD-1428: Build TD-1422 (1): a seat's fill ceiling lapses after the hour, a second trip holds, and an asks seat lingers fifteen minutes

**Priority:** high
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-05
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 39a310ab88cc48acbd7c3415812e2100
**Part of:** TD-1422
**Location:** src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py (_seat_pass, _fill, _seat_has_run); src/sessionorc/agent_common.py (SEAT_ASK_LINGER, _counted); src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py, src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py (the row's words); design §6 rule 3, §4.9b

**Why:** TD-1422's design (§6 rule 3 *The ceiling is a rate, not a stop*; §4.9b *The rule for the seat is the tick's*). On 2026-11-05 techlead-ao-1 was filled six times in an hour, once for each ask, closed after `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE` each time, tripped `FILL_CEILING` at 15:04:38Z and was never filled again: `_fill` returns at once on any `restart_ceiling` (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`), so eight asks waited seven hours until the person pressed Resume. The design makes the tripping seat's mark lapse after `FILL_WINDOW`, holds a second trip running for a person, and keeps a seat whose trigger is `asks` open `SEAT_ASK_LINGER` idle so a trickle of asks is one fill.

**Fix:** All in the tick (`src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, `src/sessionorc/agent_common.py`); nothing here touches the `restart` RPC or the UI (TD-1429).
1. **The lapse.** In `_seat_pass` (or at the top of `_fill`), a seat whose `restart_ceiling` is `{why: fill}` without `held`, its `at` at least `FILL_WINDOW` old, has the mark cleared and `{at, why: lapse}` appended to its `restarts`, saved and pushed; then the pass goes on as for a seat with no mark (filled if it is ended and due, under the same count over its fellows). A `why: lost-brief` mark, and any other ceiling, is left as it is.
2. **The held second trip.** Where `_fill` writes `restart_ceiling = {at, count, why: fill}`, it adds `held: true` when the seat's `restarts` carry a `why: lapse` entry inside `FILL_WINDOW` of now. A held mark never lapses (step 1 skips it). The `restarts` list is carried to the new record by `_replay` as it is today, so the lapse entry reaches the seat's next record.
3. **`lapse` counts toward nothing.** `agent_common._counted` leaves `why: lapse` out as it leaves `person`; the fill count in `_fill` already counts only `why: fill`.
4. **The linger.** Add `SEAT_ASK_LINGER = timedelta(minutes=15)` beside `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE` in `agent_common.py` (re-exported from `agent.py` as the others are). `_seat_has_run` uses it in place of `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE` for a seat whose `seat.trigger` is `asks` (the techlead and the fact-checker presets); every other seat keeps `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE`. Nothing else changes: an ask that lands on the lingering idle seat rings it as mail rings any idle session, and `asks_waiting` > 0 keeps it open as today.
5. **The row's words.** `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py`'s *fills exhausted* reason gains *· again after the hour* when the mark carries `held`, and the card's line (`src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`) the same; the row leaves when the mark is cleared, as it does now.
6. Tests: a seat at the ceiling is filled once the hour has rolled and a cause is due; a trip inside `FILL_WINDOW` of a lapse writes `held` and is not lapsed; a `lost-brief` mark never lapses; `_counted` ignores `lapse`; an `asks` seat idle 3 minutes with nothing waiting is not closed and is closed at 15; a `team` (manager on call) seat still closes at `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE`.

**Done when:** A seat whose fill tripped the ceiling is filled again, without a person, on the first pass after `FILL_WINDOW` that its cause is due; a second trip within `FILL_WINDOW` of that lapse writes `held: true` and stays for a person, its row reading *· again after the hour*; a seat whose trigger is `asks` closes idle after `SEAT_ASK_LINGER`, every other seat after `SEAT_IDLE_GRACE`; the tests in the Fix pass.
