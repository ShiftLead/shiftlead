**Why:** Build TD-1437 (4), from TD-1554's Hold revision (design §6 *Promote*, *The hold*, *A copy installed by hand*; §4.5a *Inbox row: promote*; §4.7 `ao promote`). Paul's Hold on TD-1437 (m-8e970f0e4961): *drop Dismiss. A failure blocks only the failed commit; auto retries once main moves past it, and Promote or a rollback clears it.
