**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held* (TD-1564), §6 rule 8 (`seats_held`, its Telegram and Dismiss), §4.10 *A team or a repo as the address* and its `read_when` row, §4.9b (a held seat stays able), §4.9a *Out of work does not mean out of reach*:
1. **The helper.** `src/sessionorc/work.py` `seats_held_by(records) -> {by, at} | None` over one team's records: None where any of its crew (`crew`) is live (not in `DEAD`; `scheduled` counts as live); else over its current crew records (not superseded, not sat out, not run-only), leaving out rule 3's own seat closes (`closer.why == "seat"`), the newest end (`record_end`) and those within `FINISHED_SETTLE` of it read by `_how_stopped`: `{by: person|until, at}` where they say so, else None. It does not read `team_wound_down`.
2. **The hold.** `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` `_seat_pass`: for each seat whose record carries a `team` (a seat with none is never held), every pass, due or not, read the helper over that team's records; while it returns a word, write `stop_held: {at, by, team}` (a new home-owned `Session` field in `src/sessionorc/models.py` — never `seat_held`, the anchor seat's retired mark, which `tests/test_anchor_retired.py`, `test_manager_on_call.py`, `test_ui.py` and `test_ui_org.py` pin absent or inert, and which old records on disk may still carry; `at` and `by` the helper's, rewritten when either changes; after the suspended and sat-out returns, which keep their own words; pushed for a node's seat as its other home-owned marks are) and clear it once it returns None. Test the hold **before** `_unfillable_word` and the fill ceiling: a held seat gets no `unfillable` word and `_fill` is not called for it on any cause; `seat_due` stands. Where td-max's extra fill and mail mover are built (TD-1529's builds), they fill no extra of a held seat. `create`'s in-place replacement at a Start writes a fresh record with no `stop_held` (unlike `restarts`). `stopped_by` shares the same filter (one helper both call: rule 3's seat closes left out), so a team's `stopped.by` and its seats' hold read the same word — a change to rule 8's reading: a Wind down left undeclared followed by a seat close reads `person`, not `other`.
3. **The address and the reply line.** `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` `_question_seat`/`_fillable` read no team, so a held seat already counts: pin it with tests (`ao msg --team`, `--repo`, `ao td add --repo` land there, not routed around). `sessionorc.mail.read_when` gains a keyword (`held`, `{by, team}` or None) and the row *waits in the seat's mailbox: its team <team> is stopped (by you | by its stop time), and the seat is filled at its next Start — `ao msg person` if it cannot wait*, with the bound's tail the other on-call rows carry, first among the seat-on-call rows; the send passes the record's `stop_held`, else its own `work.seats_held_by` over the graph's records of the seat's team.

4. **The team's mark.** `_work_mark` (rule 8) attaches `seats_held: {<seat id>: n}` — only seats with `n` ≥ 1, `n` the seat's `asks_waiting(home=…)` — on **every** return (lane news, a question's end and its two `questions` returns, an unreadable ledger's kept mark, news inside `WORK_SETTLE`), never under `held`, which is rule 8's start bound, read by `work_held()` and stripped by `_work_marks` unless a start is due (the strip leaves `seats_held`); where it would return None and a held seat has questions, it returns a held-only mark (`members` empty, `seats_held` alone, `repo` the seat record's, no settle, `at` the standing mark's else the first tick that read a question waiting), stamped by `_stamped` as any mark. Under `off`, nothing. A held-only mark never reaches `_work_start`, under any `on_work`, stopped or wound down — guard `_work_marks`' `on_work == "start" and not stopped` condition — and `rpc_work_start` (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`) on a mark with no lane ids answers `started: false` and starts nothing; `rpc_clear_work` on a held-only mark answers `cleared: false` and leaves it, and on a mixed mark clears the entries and keeps `seats_held`. **Telegram** (`src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py`): a mark with lane ids is told by `stopped.by` as now; a held-only mark, `stopped.by` where it carries `stopped`, else its held seat's `stop_held.by`: for `until`, told once per mark at its first `seats_held` key, whatever `on_work` (today's branch skips a non-stopped mark under `start`) — *<team> · stopped at its stop time · n questions wait on <seat name>*, `n` the total and the seats joined by *and*, a line of its own in `src/sessionorc/notify.py` beside `work_line` — never again for a rising count or a second seat; for `person`, never.
