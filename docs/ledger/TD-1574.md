**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held* (TD-1564), §6 rule 8 *A team that stopped* (the `held` count), §4.10 *A team or a repo as the address* and its `read_when` row, §4.9a *Out of work does not mean out of reach*:
1. **The hold.** In `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, before a seat is filled on any cause (`_fill`, the one fill site today, from `_seat_pass`; and td-max's extra fill and mail mover where TD-1529's builds have landed), read the seat's team's records as rule 8 does (the team's records through `work.crew`, `work.team_stopped`); where it reads stopped with `by` `person` or `until`, fill nothing, keep `seat_due`, and write `seat_held: {at, by, team}` on the record (a new home-owned `Session` field in `src/sessionorc/models.py`, carried as `restarts` is; `at` written once while it holds; pushed for a node's seat as its other home-owned marks are). Clear it at the fill and wherever the team no longer reads stopped by `person`/`until`; `create`'s in-place replacement at the Start writes a fresh record, which must not carry it (unlike `restarts`). A team that stopped `other`, a wound-down team, and a running team fill as now. The tagging seat's existing *live team* test is unchanged.
2. **The address resolves to a held seat.** `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` `_question_seat`/`_fillable` read no team, so a held seat already counts: pin it with tests (`--team`, `--repo` and `ao td add --repo` land there), and add no word to `unfillable` (`_unfillable_word`, `_seat_pass`) for the hold, so `seat_able` stays true and nothing routes it around (§4.9b).
3. **The reply line.** `sessionorc.mail.read_when` gains the row *waits in the seat's mailbox: its team <team> is stopped (by you | by its stop time), and the seat is filled at its next Start — `ao msg person` if it cannot wait*, first among the seat-on-call rows, read from the record's `seat_held`, any kind.
4. **The team-start mark.** `_work_mark` (rule 8) writes `seats_held: {<seat id>: n}` (never `held`, which is rule 8's start bound, read by `work_held()` and stripped by `_work_marks` unless a start is due — the strip leaves `seats_held`) — the open asks and steers waiting in each held seat's mailbox — on a stopped team's `work_waiting`, and writes the mark for a stopped team whose lanes gained nothing where a held seat has questions (`members` empty, `seats_held` alone — where its no-news branch returns None today); removed as the mark is (Start, a crew session live, `off`, Dismiss). The row's place is unchanged (*Later* for a person's Stop).
