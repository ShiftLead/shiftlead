**Why:** Paul could not stop `ao-grind` (TD-1564): after each Stop the tick filled the team's fact-check seat again for a question waiting on it (2026-11-07 18:03–18:08 MDT, six fills; 2026-11-09 16:12 and 16:14). Design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held* now says a seat whose team the person or its stop time stopped is held — filled for no cause until the team's Start — while TD-1128's *whatever its team* still holds for every other team.

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held* (TD-1564), §6 rule 8 *A team that stopped* (the `held` count), §4.10 *A team or a repo as the address* and its `read_when` row, §4.9a *Out of work does not mean out of reach*:
1. **The hold.** In `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py`, before a seat is filled on any cause (`_fill`, and the scaled seat's extra fill and mail mover), read the seat's team's records as rule 8 does (`work.team_crew`/the team's records, `work.team_stopped`); where it reads stopped with `by` `person` or `until`, fill nothing, keep `seat_due`, and write `seat_held: {at, by, team}` on the record (a new home-owned `Session` field in `src/sessionorc/models.py`, carried as `restarts` is; `at` written once while it holds; pushed for a node's seat as its other home-owned marks are). Clear it at the fill and wherever the team no longer reads stopped by `person`/`until`. A team that stopped `other`, a wound-down team, and a running team fill as now. The tagging seat's existing *live team* test is unchanged.
2. **The address resolves to a held seat.** `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` `_question_seat`/`_fillable`: a closed `asks` seat held only by its team's stop still counts (it is filled at the Start), so `--team`, `--repo` and `ao td add --repo` land there; `seat_able` does not read it unable, so nothing routes it around.
3. **The reply line.** `sessionorc.mail.read_when` gains the row *waits in the seat's mailbox: its team <team> is stopped (by you | by its stop time), and the seat is filled at its next Start — `ao msg person` if it cannot wait*, first among the seat-on-call rows, read from the record's `seat_held`, any kind.
4. **The team-start mark.** `_work_mark` (rule 8) writes `held: {<seat id>: n}` — the open asks and steers waiting in each held seat's mailbox — on a stopped team's `work_waiting`, and writes the mark for a stopped team whose lanes gained nothing where a held seat has questions; removed as the mark is (Start, a crew session live, `off`, Dismiss). The row's place is unchanged (*Later* for a person's Stop).

**Done when:** Tests: a team stopped by the person (a Stop, and a Wind down left undeclared) and one stopped by its stop time each fill no seat for an `asks`, `prs`, `every` or `swept` cause nor a scaled seat's extra, keep `seat_due`, and write `seat_held` once; an `ask` sent to such a team's seat with `ao msg --team` and `ao td add --repo` lands in the seat's mailbox, is not routed around, and its reply line is the held sentence; a team stopped `other` (a crash given up, a team of seats alone between questions) and a wound-down team fill as now; after `ao team start` the seat is filled on its standing cause on the next pass with the mail intact and `seat_held` cleared, for a team with members and for a team of seats alone; `work_waiting` carries `held` with the waiting count, written even with no lane news. Live: after a Stop of a team with a question seat, a question to it fills nothing and the journal logs no *the seat is due (asks) — filling it* until the Start.
