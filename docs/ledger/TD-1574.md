**Ask:** Should TD-1564's builds (TD-1574, TD-1575: a stopped team's seats are held until its Start) go ahead as written after four review rounds ended at the cap, get one more round, or is this the wrong design (Hold, with a note)? The board's TD-1564 decide item asks it; Go lifts this gate.

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held* (TD-1564), §6 rule 8 (`seats_held`, its Telegram and Dismiss), §4.10 *A team or a repo as the address* and its `read_when` row, §4.9b (a held seat stays able), §4.9a *Out of work does not mean out of reach*:
1. **The helper and the predicate.** `src/sessionorc/work.py` `held_for(r, records) -> bool` (a seat, supervised, unattended, neither suspended nor sat out, whose team's `seats_held_by` gives a word; called by the hold, `stop_held`, `seats_held`, `read_when`'s callers, the blank pick and `_move_handed`) over `seats_held_by(records) -> {by, at} | None` over one team's records: None where any of its crew (`crew`) is live (not in `DEAD`; `scheduled` counts as live); else over its current crew records (not superseded, not sat out, not run-only), leaving out a record whose end `record_end` reads is rule 3's own seat close (no `ended`, `closer.why == "seat"`), the newest end (`record_end`) and those within `FINISHED_SETTLE` of it read by `_how_stopped`: `{by: person|until, at}` where they say so, else None. It does not read `team_wound_down`. `stopped_by` shares the same filter for its `by` alone (one helper both call: rule 3's seat closes left out; its `FINISHED_SETTLE` window anchored on the newest end that remains, `other` where none does), while its `at` stays the newest end over all current crew records, seat closes included — so a team of seats alone keeps a non-blank `stopped.at`; so a team's `stopped.by` and its seats' hold read the same word — a change to rule 8's reading: a Wind down left undeclared followed by a seat close reads `person`, not `other`.
2. **The hold.** `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` `_seat_pass`: for each seat whose record carries a `team` (a seat with none is never held), every pass, due or not, read the helper over that team's records; while it returns a word, write `stop_held: {at, by, team}` (a new home-owned `Session` field in `src/sessionorc/models.py` — never `seat_held`, the anchor seat's retired mark, which `tests/test_anchor_retired.py`, `test_manager_on_call.py`, `test_ui.py` and `test_ui_org.py` pin absent or inert, and which old records on disk may still carry; `at` and `by` the helper's, rewritten when either changes; after the suspended and sat-out returns, which keep their own words; pushed for a node's seat as its other home-owned marks are) and clear it once it returns None; `_seat_pass`'s returns in code order: (a) not a supervised unattended seat, `superseded_by`, or suspended (one `return`), (b) sat out, each clearing any `stop_held` it finds; the write sits after them and before (c) the link-down return, which keeps it. Test the hold **before** `_unfillable_word` and the fill ceiling: a held seat gets no `unfillable` word and `_fill` is not called for it on any cause; `seat_due` stands. Where td-max's extra fill and mail mover are built (TD-1529's builds), they fill no extra of a held seat. `create`'s in-place replacement at a Start writes a fresh record with no `stop_held` (unlike `restarts`).
3. **The address and the reply line.** `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` `_question_seat`/`_fillable` read no team, so a held seat already counts: pin it with tests (`ao msg --team`, `--repo`, `ao td add --repo` land there, not routed around). A held seat is no target across teams: the handed entry's blank Team pick (`agent_inbox.py`, the `seated` choice through `_seat_id_able`) and the tick's `_move_handed` (`agent_mail.py`) take the first team whose techlead is able **and** not held (`work.held_for`) — and the tick also calls the move, every pass, for a blank-pick handed entry waiting on a held seat (hoisted above the `if why is None: continue` in `agent_mail.py`, since a held seat's `seat_unable` is None), its `why` *its team is stopped*, so it leaves once another team's techlead is able and unheld; with none, it lands on the first team's seat and waits, with the held sentence where that seat is held; a pick the person made still lands on a held seat. `sessionorc.mail.read_when` gains its own keyword `held: dict | None = None` (`{by, team, wound}`; not TD-1529's `extras`, which is not built) and the row *waits in the seat's mailbox: its team <team> is stopped (by you | at its stop time) — wound down where `wound` says so — and the seat is filled at its next Start — `ao msg person` if it cannot wait* (to the person sending, *— Start the team to have it read*), with the bound's tail the other on-call rows carry, first among the seat-on-call rows and below the `unreachable` row, which still wins, passed only for a record that is a seat (`r.seat`), supervised, unattended, not suspended and not sat out, whose team's `seats_held_by` gives a word — one shared predicate, `work.held_for(r, records)`, since `_fillable` tests no seat and `mail.read_when` cannot read the graph; `_read_when_kwargs(r, now)` gains the graph's records to call it. Every home caller that names a seat computes it from `work.seats_held_by` and `work.team_wound_down` over the graph's records of the seat's team, never from the stored `stop_held`: `_read_when_kwargs` (shared by the send, `agent_mail.py`, and the lapse stamp's tail; retyped from `dict[str, bool]` to carry the dict), `_entry_read_when` (`agent_inbox.py`, the reply line for `ao td add --repo` and the entry hand-off), and the home's record view's composer sentences (`agent_serve`; a node's view of its own seat reads the pushed `stop_held`, display only, saying *stopped*); the seat card's own composer sentence (`src/shiftlead/ui/cards.py`, which reads `read_when(None, k, now, seat=True)`) passes the view's `stop_held` with the team's wound-down reading, display only.
4. **The team's mark.** Lane news that comes to a mark holding no lane ids and no `questions` (held-only, or left by Dismiss) takes `now_iso()` as its `at`, not the old mark's, so the `work:<team>` row is aged and told from the news. `_work_mark` (rule 8) attaches `seats_held: {<seat id>: n}` — keyed by the seats `work.held_for` holds this pass (never the stored `stop_held`), only with `n` ≥ 1, `n` the seat's `asks_waiting(home=…)` — with `seats_held_by` (`person`|`until`, `seats_held_by`'s word) and `seats_held_at` (when its first key was written; dropped with its last; kept through Dismiss), on **every** return (lane news, a question's end and its two `questions` returns, an unreadable ledger's kept mark, news inside `WORK_SETTLE`), never under `held`, which is rule 8's start bound, read by `work_held()` and stripped by `_work_marks` unless a start is due (the strip leaves `seats_held`); where it would return None and a held seat has questions, it returns a held-only mark (`members` empty, `seats_held` alone, `repo` the seat record's, no settle, `at` the standing mark's else the first tick that read a question waiting), stamped by `_stamped` as any mark. Under `off`, nothing. A held-only mark never reaches `_work_start`, under any `on_work`, stopped or wound down — guard `_work_marks`' `on_work == "start" and not stopped` condition — and `rpc_work_start` (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`) on a mark with no lane ids answers `started: false` and starts nothing — that check before the one that refuses a mark carrying `stopped`, and before `work_waiting` is popped, so the mark stays; `rpc_clear_work` on a held-only mark answers `cleared: false` and leaves it, and on a mixed mark leaves `{at, repo, members: {}, seats_held, seats_held_by, seats_held_at}` (with `stopped` where it stood; `questions` dropped, so `_work_mark` does not redraw their clauses). **Telegram** (`src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py`): the lanes keep their row, `work:<team>`, told by `stopped.by` as now; the questions on a held seat get a notify row of their own, `held:<team>`, for any mark carrying `seats_held` whose `seats_held_by` is `until` — with lane ids or without, whatever `on_work` and whatever bound `held` names (today's branch skips a non-stopped mark under `start`) — its `began` the mark's `seats_held_at`, its `link` `work:<team>` and its `snooze` `work:<team>|work` (the team-start row's), told once: *<team> · stopped at its stop time · n questions wait on <seat name>* (*stopped* whatever the team reads), `n` the total and the seats joined by *and*, each name resolved from the graph (the id where none), a line of its own in `src/sessionorc/notify.py` beside `work_line`; never again for a rising count or a second seat; for `person`, never.
5. **The held reason.** `unanswered.read` (§4.9b *Why it did not answer*, `src/sessionorc/unanswered.py`) gains `held` in `STATES` after `suspended` and before `fills_exhausted`/`restart_ceiling`, for a seat carrying `stop_held`: the stamp `{state: "held", since, team, stopped_by}` (`since` the mark's `at`; the stamp's own `by` and its ceiling `held` flag untouched), its words *`<seat>` waits for its team's Start: <team> was stopped (by you | at its stop time)* (`words`' fields for `held`: `since`, `team`, `stopped_by`). Rule 13's stuck reading (`sessionorc.stuck`, not built yet: where TD-1496 has landed, else TD-1496 takes it from the design) counts a held seat as not acting, its reason `held`, and never as the fillable-seat *acting* branch. `ao team list` (`src/shiftlead/cli.py`) counts lane ids alone and shows no waiting questions: the cards and the row say them.
6. **The Start keeps a held seat's mail.** `keep_mail_for` (`src/shiftlead/teamrun.py`) asks `keep_mail` for a `supersede` holder whose state is `closed` alone; a seat the person killed is `exited` (`pane: False`) and held, and its waiting questions would be dropped at the Start. Ask `keep_mail` also for an `exited` holder carrying `stop_held` (the same grant rule: a person, or one of its controllers): the name verdicts carry only `holder_state`, so read `stop_held` from the `call("list")` rows by holder id, fetched before the early return.
7. **Rule 13's team words.** Where rule 13 reads a lane work order's team words from `work_waiting` (*<team> waits for your Start*), a held-only mark (`seats_held` and no lane ids) reads as no mark.
