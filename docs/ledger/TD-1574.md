**Why:** Paul could not stop `ao-grind` (TD-1564): after each Stop the tick filled the team's fact-check seat again for a question waiting on it (2026-11-07 18:03–18:08 MDT, six fills; 2026-11-09 16:12 and 16:14). Design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held* now says a seat whose team the person or its stop time stopped is held — filled for no cause until the team's Start — while TD-1128's *whatever its team* still holds for every other team.

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 3 *A stopped team's seats are held* (TD-1564), §6 rule 8 (`seats_held`, its Telegram and Dismiss), §4.10 *A team or a repo as the address* and its `read_when` row, §4.9b (a held seat stays able), §4.9a *Out of work does not mean out of reach*:
1. **The helper.** `src/sessionorc/work.py` `seats_held_by(records) -> {by, at} | None` over one team's records: None where any of its crew (`crew`) is live (not in `DEAD`; `scheduled` counts as live); else over its current crew records (not superseded, not sat out, not run-only), leaving out rule 3's own seat closes (`closer.why == "seat"`), the newest end (`record_end`) and those within `FINISHED_SETTLE` of it read by `_how_stopped`: `{by: person|until, at}` where they say so, else None. It does not read `team_wound_down`.
2. **The hold.** `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` `_seat_pass`: for each seat whose record carries a `team` (a seat with none is never held), every pass, due or not, read the helper over that team's records; while it returns a word, write `seat_held: {at, by, team}` (a new home-owned `Session` field in `src/sessionorc/models.py`; `at` written once; pushed for a node's seat as its other home-owned marks are) and clear it once it returns None. Test the hold **before** `_unfillable_word` and the fill ceiling: a held seat gets no `unfillable` word and `_fill` is not called for it on any cause; `seat_due` stands. Where td-max's extra fill and mail mover are built (TD-1529's builds), they fill no extra of a held seat. `create`'s in-place replacement at a Start writes a fresh record with no `seat_held` (unlike `restarts`).
3. **The address and the reply line.** `src/sessionorc/agent_mail.py` `_question_seat`/`_fillable` read no team, so a held seat already counts: pin it with tests (`ao msg --team`, `--repo`, `ao td add --repo` land there, not routed around). `sessionorc.mail.read_when` gains a keyword (`held`, `{by, team}` or None) and the row *waits in the seat's mailbox: its team <team> is stopped (by you | by its stop time), and the seat is filled at its next Start — `ao msg person` if it cannot wait*, with the bound's tail the other on-call rows carry, first among the seat-on-call rows; the send passes the record's `seat_held`, else its own `work.seats_held_by` over the graph's records of the seat's team.
4. **The team's mark.** `_work_mark` (rule 8) writes `seats_held: {<seat id>: n}`, `n` the seat's `asks_waiting(home=…)`, on the team's `work_waiting` (stopped or wound down) — never under `held`, which is rule 8's start bound, read by `work_held()` and stripped by `_work_marks` unless a start is due; the strip leaves `seats_held`. Under `ask` and `start`, where the lanes gained nothing and a held seat has questions, write the mark with `members` empty and `seats_held` alone (where the no-news branch returns None today), `at` the first tick that read a question waiting, `repo` the seat record's, no `WORK_SETTLE`; under `off`, nothing. **Dismiss** (`src/sessionorc/agent_inbox.py`, the `work_waiting` dismiss) clears the entries and keeps `seats_held` (the mark stands while a held seat has questions). **Telegram** (`src/sessionorc/agent_notify.py`, the work pass that tells `_work_ids(mark)`): for `stopped.by` (or the held word) `until`, tell a mark once when `seats_held` is first written — *<team> · stopped at its stop time · n questions wait on <seat name>* — never again for a rising count; for `person`, never.

**Done when:** Tests: `seats_held_by` reads `person` for a Stop, a Close or Kill of the last live record, and a Wind down left undeclared; `until` for the stop time; None for a crash given up, a kill by a session, members closed after finishing, and a team whose only ends are rule 3's seat closes; still `person` when rule 3 closes the seat twenty minutes after a Wind down; `person` when the person closes the seat of a team whose members all declared out of work (wound down). A held seat fills for no cause (`asks`, `prs`, `every`, `swept`), takes no `unfillable` word on a gated profile or at the ceiling, keeps `seat_due`, and carries `seat_held` from the first pass whether due or not; a seat with no `team` is never held; an `ask` sent by `ao msg --team` and `ao td add --repo` lands in its mailbox, is not routed around, and its reply line is the held sentence with the bound's tail — also for the first send after a Stop, before any pass; the ask lapses at its bound as on any closed seat. After `ao team start` (with and without a start time) the record `create` writes carries no `seat_held` and the seat is filled on its standing cause on the next pass with the mail intact, for a team with members and for a team of seats alone. `work_waiting` carries `seats_held` (`asks_waiting` per seat), written with no lane news under `ask` and `start` (`members` empty), survives the `held` strip, leaves `held` (the start bound) as it was, survives Dismiss, and is not written under `off`; Telegram tells an `until` mark once and a `person` one never. Live: after a Stop of a team with a question seat, a question to it fills nothing and the journal logs no *the seat is due (asks) — filling it* until the Start.
