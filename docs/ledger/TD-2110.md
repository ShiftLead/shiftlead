**Why:** When the home asks the person about an entry, its writer tests for a standing decision with a pattern that misses three forms that still hold an entry: the old `decision (bob) — words`, a retired role, and the retired word `hold`. On such an entry the question lands and adds a second person beside the one already holding it. ledgerboard's TD-161 designs `ask_gate()` to refuse all three, and its merge-gate verdict sends any such PR to review, so until this lands each one shows in the weekly audit as an unreviewed merge.
