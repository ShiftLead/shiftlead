**Why:** A decided board line is a work order in every `free-pick` lane, so the person's answer to a design's end-of-rounds item goes to the grinders and never to the designer whose round it is. In ledgerboard on 2026-11-10, a grinder claimed *One more round* on TD-058 and dropped it as the designer's work, while `lb-designer` sat idle waiting on that very answer until the grinder mailed it by hand (TD-1590). TD-1590's design (§6 rule 6 *save a design's answer*, §4.9a *A design's answer*) routes such a line to the `design-first` lanes, the design's own designer's alone (`for`) where one is, and to `free-pick` only where the repo has no designer to reach.

**Ask:** Will you let TD-1590's design (a decided line answering a design's end-of-rounds item goes to that design's designer instead of the grinders, and a member's own dropped work order stops ringing it after each none), whose four rounds ended at the cap with every build-changing fix read by a later review, go to its builds TD-1705 and TD-1706, or buy one more round, or hold it with a note?

**Fix:** Build design §6 rule 6 *save a design's answer* (TD-1590), with §4.4 *Board write-back*, §4.9a *A design's answer*, §4.5 screen 6 (*pickable by <team>*) and §4.5a *Repo page: decided lines*:

1. **The join, every tick.** The board's work orders stay as `_read_repo`/`_read_boards` store them. Each tick, on the event loop, a join reads the stored orders, the ledger reading and the repo's records (a node's included, as the lanes' peers are read) and writes four fields onto the stored order dicts (the `entry()` output), only when one changes (on a ledger reading carrying `error` it leaves the stored fields as they are): `lane`, `design`, `owner` (overwriting `entry()`'s empty one) and `for`. `_read_boards` and `_read_repo` are static and hold no records, so they hand the fresh orders back, and each site that stores them runs the join first, in the same synchronous step, before it stores them: the tick's refresh (`agent_tick.py` ~4168-4185, and before its `changed` test, which would otherwise see unjoined orders as changed every pass) and a press's re-read (`agent_inbox.py` `_reread_board` ~1239, which stores the orders unconditionally). No reader sees a routed line unjoined. The join routes a decided line with exactly one `Context:` gate `TD-n decision (<any person>)` (parsed from the line's `text`, since `refs` holds bare ids) whose TD-n is an open `Kind: design-first` entry of the reading; a line with two such gates is not routed. It routes only when the repo has a **designer to reach**: a supervised member, not a seat and not a scale extra, of a team servicing the repo, whose `lane` `lane_matches` the routed order (owner narrowing by TD-n's `Owner:` included), whose current record carries no TD-1706 `released` mark for the line whose `at_drop` equals its `Decided:`, not sat out and not run-only (rule 8's crew, `work.sat_out`), and that is live (suspended or wrap-up asked counts) or that rule 8 would start for work (its team wound down and not stopped by the person, or finished while its team runs on; under `on_work: ask` it waits on the team-start row, with no fallback). A record carrying `superseded_by` never counts. A routed order gets `lane: "design-first"`, `design: "TD-n"`, `owner` = TD-n's `Owner:`, and `for` (step 3) where one is: the record's address, `id@host` for a node's record, as `agent_mail._successor` addresses one. Any other order carries none of them and stays `free-pick`'s.
2. **`ledger.lane_matches`.** A work order with `lane: "design-first"` matches any lane holding `design-first`, whatever else the lane holds, and never matches by `free-pick`. It keeps the work-order branch's `pickable == "yes"` and `settling`/`waiting` tests, and is narrowed by `owner:<word>` against its `owner` as an entry is. One without `lane` matches `free-pick` as today and is never narrowed. `lane_matches` takes a new optional argument, the lane's record address. An order with `for` matches only that record; called with none, it matches no lane. Every caller passes the address of the record whose lane it reads: `work.reread`, `ledger.in_lanes` (its members, and its team-level `taken` and `waits`, whose `(lane, team)` pairs carry the record's address), `stuck.lane_records` (rule 13's actors; and `stuck.orders`, which today re-derives each order from `boards` through `workorders.entry`, reads the reading's stored `work_orders` instead, wrapping each stored entry as `{id, entry}` as today, `pr_orders` as before), `agent_tick` (`_lane_news` ~2239, ~2793), `queue.py` ~1720, `teamrun.py` ~699; `in_lanes` reads each record's `id`: the views its callers build (`teamrun.py` ~1601, `agent_scale.py` ~147, `agent_shape.py` ~119) carry the record's address beside it, checked by the build. `ui/inbox.py` `_order_teams` reads the stored order's `lane` and `for` in place of its synthetic `{id, work_order, pickable}` entry.
3. **The design's own designer.** Of the designers to reach, the one whose own latest `progress` word on TD-n is `claimed` or `done` (never `dropped`), the newest such word winning, is the order's `for`. Its words on TD-n are read along each record's line of successors (`superseded_by`), with the newest record of the line standing for it, since a restart carries only `claimed` words; the `released` test of step 1 reads the current record alone. Once an ended record is removed (`CLOSED_KEEP`) its words go and `for` falls back to every designer, accepted. When `for` changes (a record gone past rule 8's reach, its word on TD-n a drop, its `released` mark for the line written), `work.reread`'s pruning makes the line news to the lanes it now matches, as an entry that enters a lane is, with no new memory.
4. **The readers.** `in_lanes` keeps an order with a `lane` out of every team's `rest`. The team card, `ao repo`, rules 8 and 13, the Repo page's *decided lines* and the Inbox's row read the stored fields. The Repo page draws the line in the column whose stage's lane takes `design-first` (first in Design in the fixed columns), with *for <designer>* where it names a `for`. The Inbox shows *pickable by <team>*, naming the teams whose `design-first` lanes hold it, followed by *· for <designer>* where it names a `for`. Nothing re-derives these.
5. **The briefs.** In the shipped grinder template (`src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md`), *out of work* reads *no decided line your lane takes that nobody has claimed*, in place of *no decided line nobody has claimed*. Where a grinder takes a design's answer (the fallback, or a claim taken before the line was routed), it does a *Go* itself and carries *One more round* or *Hold* to a `Kind: design-first`, `Owner: designer` entry `Part of` the design, closing the line `--carried-to`, never dropping it. In the shipped designer template, a design's answer among its work orders is its own (§4.9a *A design's answer*). In this repo's `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md`, rewrite the whole bullet *The answered item is a decided board line* for the designer as the line's taker. On *Go* it drops the blocks and moves the fixes. On *One more round* or *Hold* it files its round entry and closes the line `--carried-to` in a ledger PR before the round, then runs the round. Keep the fallback's grinder in one clause. This is a held path: the techlead reads it.
6. **Tests.** TD-1590's case: a decided end-of-rounds line on a design-first entry whose designer reported `done` with its design PR is that designer's alone (`for`), in no grinder's lane, and rule 6 tells it, not the grinder. With no designer to reach (no record with `design-first`, or only a seat or an extra with it), it is in `free-pick` as before. A wound-down team's designer still counts (rule 8 starts it), and after that start the new record, not the superseded one, is its `for` and is told. A line whose Context names a build's gate stays `free-pick`'s. When `for` changes, the line is news to another designer. When the `for` designer drops the line, it goes to the other designers; when a repo's only designer drops it, it goes to `free-pick`; when the person answers again, that designer is to reach once more, stays so on the ticks after (its `at_drop` does not move) and is told once. A designer restarted under its own name is to reach whatever its predecessor dropped. No reader between `_read_boards`' refresh and the join sees a routed line as `free-pick`'s. TD-n owned `techlead` with only `[design-first, owner:designer]` lanes leaves it `free-pick`'s. A lane holding both `free-pick` and `design-first` takes it. It is in no team's `rest`. Rule 13's actors for a routed line are its `for` designer (`stuck.orders` reads the stored orders). `lane_matches` with no id matches no `for` order. The `for` designer's own silence after its drop is TD-1706's, landed first.

**Done when:** A decided line with exactly one `Context:` gate on an open design-first entry is, wherever the repo has a designer to reach (§6 rule 6), in the `design-first` lanes — the design's own designer's alone (`for`) where one is — and in no `free-pick` lane; with no designer to reach it is `free-pick`'s as before. `ao repo`, the Repo page, the Inbox and rules 6, 8 and 13 read it from the stored reading, the briefs say it, and tests pin TD-1590's ledgerboard case.
