## TD-1331: A held entry whose question the person has answered reads *answered — waiting on <asker>*, not as a question still open

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (the designer, from TD-1316's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1316
**Location:** `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_state` ~488, the need and its word; `reading` ~257, which takes the answered questions and the pull readings); `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (~3371, the `queue_mod.reading` call, which passes them from the person inbox and from the pull readings, which live on the `host` reading, not `repos`; ~3249–3256, the ledger re-read on its mtime, which must record the head it read); `src/sessionorc/promote.py` (`pull` ~1170, the reading's `at`, `outcome` and `to`); `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (the Repo page's For you, `ledger_for_you` ~1956, and the queue reading its handler holds ~1882); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`put` ~254, the want, beside `with_you`; the card's `items_start` ~602); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the ledger row's line ~1188 and foot ~1210); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html` (~93); the Repo page's For you rows; `tests/test_queue.py`, `tests/test_ui_queue.py`

**Why:** TD-1316's second case (Paul, 2026-11-02: *TD1310 seems to be in a bad state — the same question seems to be popping up in the queue*): after the person answers a designer's `ask` about a held entry, the entry's `decision (paul)` row keeps drawing **Decide**, its **Answer…** and **Lift the hold**, until the designer's PR drops the hold and the checkout pulls it — up to 35 minutes, then about 100 s more on TD-1310.

**Fix:** as designed in §4.4 *The card's status* (*A `decision (paul)` member whose question the person has answered is no need while its asker acts on the answer*), *A row's state* (the word's place in the order) and §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* and *For you*:
- The reading's `_state` gives a `decision (paul)` member the word *answered — waiting on <asker>* and no need when an `ask` or `steer` to the person that `mail_card` puts on the member's card and names the member was closed by the person's answer (`replied`, or `go_with_it`) and still owes its outcome, or has a `done` outcome and no ledger reading since came from a checkout a `pulled` pull (`at` later than the outcome) moved — the reading's head at or past that pull's `to`; a `current` pull never counts. The newest such question names the asker. The id lists still name the member the person's, as for *with you in <name>*.
- It needs the person again on a `blocked` or `dropped` outcome, when that reading still holds the hold, when the asker exits without reporting, when the person dismisses the owing question, and when it is asked again (`asked_again`).
- The Queue card draws the item in the grey section beside *with you in <name>*: its line, the foot from the question's own `answer_given`/`answer_how` (*you answered "<answer>"*, *you answered* alone for a typed reply, *you let it go with its default*) then *in <asker>'s <ask|steer>, <owed age> ago — the hold goes when its PR merges*, **Open** and its ⋯ with **Lift the hold** in it, and no answers, **Answer…** or Start session (it does not count toward `items_start`); counted on no pill, no colour, not in **Needs you (n)**. The Repo page's For you row reads the word from the queue reading its handler holds and draws the same.

**Done when** a test builds a held entry with an answered ask about it owing its outcome and finds the word, no need on the card and no Answer… or Lift the hold on its item; a second gives the ask a `done` outcome and finds the word through a `current` pull and until a reading after a `pulled` pull, then the need again with the hold still standing; a third gives it `blocked` and finds the need; a fourth draws the foot for a typed reply as *you answered* with no words, and finds **Lift the hold** in the item's ⋯; and `pdm run test` passes.
