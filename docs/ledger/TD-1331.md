## TD-1331: A held entry whose question the person has answered reads *answered — waiting on <asker>*, not as a question still open

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (the designer, from TD-1316's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Part of:** TD-1316
**Location:** the repo-facts build that calls `src/sessionorc/ledger.py` `entries` (~483, which takes text and stays unchanged): the caller adds each entry's `written`, its file's mtime (the one-file ledger's own mtime), §4.4 *Repo facts*; `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_state` ~488, the need and its word, after the `own` early return — *with you in <name>* wins; `reading` ~257, which takes the person inbox's answered questions about the card's members); `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (~3371, the `queue_mod.reading` call, whose signature gains those questions from `self.person_inbox`, and the unfiltered `records` — `reading`'s `recs` ~291 drops exited and closed ones — where an absent asker reads as gone); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`ledger_for_you` ~2434, which draws the Repo page's For you and the Inbox's *Later* ledger rows off the repo facts and applies the same rule from the person inbox; `_owing` ~1906 for `answer_given`/`answer_how`); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`put` ~256, the want, beside `with_you`; the card's `items_start` ~603); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the ledger row's line ~1188 and foot ~1210); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html` (~93, the item's ⋯); `tests/test_queue.py`, `tests/test_ui_queue.py`, `tests/test_ui_inbox_ledger.py`

**Why:** TD-1316's second case (Paul, 2026-11-02: *TD1310 seems to be in a bad state — the same question seems to be popping up in the queue*): after the person answers a designer's `ask` about a held entry, the entry's `decision (paul)` row keeps drawing **Decide**, its **Answer…** and **Lift the hold**, until the designer's PR drops the hold and the checkout pulls it — up to 35 minutes, then about 100 s more on TD-1310.

**Fix:** as designed in §4.4 *The card's status* (*A `decision (paul)` member whose question the person has answered is no need while its asker acts on the answer*), *A row's state* (the word's place in the order) and §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* and *For you*:
- **The match:** `reading` finds each answered question's first ref (`queue_mod.refs(about)`, else `refs(subject + text)`, the first only; a bare `TD-n` only where exactly one open member across the reading's repos carries it, else no link) — `mail_card`'s match (queue.py ~102), shared rather than restated, but without `card_index`, which does not exist yet while `_state` runs inside `row()`.
- **The row:** a member it applies to carries `answered: {mid, asker, kind, closed_at, outcome}` (the newest by `closed_at`); `_state` returns the word *answered — waiting on <asker>* with `need` still set, as for *with you in <name>* (the id lists read it), and the card's status tests skip such a member. `ui/queue.py` gains the want `answered` (beside `with_you`, outside `WANTS`): the Queue's ledger items arrive as `ledger_for_you`'s `later` rows, so `answered` rides on that row (set there by the same rule, below) and `put` reads it; the foot's words come from `_owing` on the question found by `mid` in the person inbox.
- The reading's `_state` gives a `decision (paul)` member the word *answered — waiting on <asker>* and no need when an `ask` or `steer` to the person that `mail_card` puts on the member's card and names the member was closed by the person's answer (`replied`, or `go_with_it`) and still owes its outcome, or has a `done` outcome while the entry's `written` is not later than the answer's `closed_at`, and for no more than a day after the outcome (both as aware UTC datetimes: `closed_at` parsed, the mtime from its epoch float; never compare strings). The newest such question names the asker. The id lists still name the member the person's, as for *with you in <name>*, which wins where the person's own session also holds it.
- It needs the person again on a `blocked`, `dropped` or `asker_gone` outcome, when, after a `done`, the entry file is written after the answer and still holds the hold, when the asker's record (by the question's `from`) is exited or closed with the debt unpaid, when the person dismisses the owing question (outcome `dismissed`: any outcome but `done` is the need again), and when it is asked again (`asked_again`).
- The Queue card draws the item in the grey section beside *with you in <name>*: its line, the foot from the question's own `answer_given`/`answer_how` (*you answered "<answer>"*, *you answered* alone for a typed reply, *you let it go with its default*) then *in <asker>'s <ask|steer>, <age> ago — the hold goes when its PR merges* (the answer's age, `closed_at`), **Open** and its ⋯ with **Lift the hold** in it, and no answers, **Answer…** or Start session (it does not count toward `items_start`); counted on no pill, no colour, not in **Needs you (n)**. `ledger_for_you` draws the same on the Repo page's For you and the Inbox's *Later*.

**Done when** a test builds a held entry with an answered ask about it owing its outcome and finds the word, no need on the card and no Answer… or Lift the hold on its item; a second gives the ask a `done` outcome and finds the word until the entry's `written` passes the answer, then the need again with the hold still standing (including a write before the `done` was sent), and the need again a day after the outcome with the file unwritten; one each for `asker_gone`, an exited asker, a dismissed question and `asked_again`, each finding the need; a third gives it `blocked` and finds the need; a fourth draws the foot for a typed reply as *you answered* with no words, and finds **Lift the hold** in the item's ⋯, whose press sends the ledger row's decided line and takes the item away; one finds *with you in <name>* winning over the answered word; one finds a second open question about the entry drawn as its own Decide row beside the word; one finds the facts carrying `written` (per file, and the one file's mtime for a one-file ledger, where a sibling's edit after the `done` ends the word); one finds `ledger_for_you` drawing the word and no Answer… on the Repo page's For you row; and `pdm run test` passes.
