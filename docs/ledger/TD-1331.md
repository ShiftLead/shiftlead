## TD-1331: A held entry whose question the person has answered reads *answered — waiting on <asker>*, not as a question still open

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (the designer, from TD-1316's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — slice 1 (the home's reading: `written`, `answered_hold`, the row's word and `answered`) in #2840; slice 2, the UI (`ledger_for_you`, `ledger_view`'s inbox, `ui/queue.py`'s want, the two templates), is left.
**Part of:** TD-1316
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/app.py` (the Repo page's gather ~1866 and its `ledger_for_you` call ~1956; `ledger_view` ~4603 and its callers); the repo-facts build that calls `src/sessionorc/ledger.py` `entries` (~483, which takes text and stays unchanged): the caller adds each entry's `written`, its file's mtime (the one-file ledger's own mtime), §4.4 *Repo facts*; `src/sessionorc/queue.py` (`_state` ~488, the need and its word, after the `own` early return — *with you in <name>* wins; `reading` ~257, which takes the person inbox's answered questions about the card's members); `src/sessionorc/agent_tick.py` (~3371, the `queue_mod.reading` call, whose signature gains those questions from `self.person_inbox`, and the unfiltered `records` — `reading`'s `recs` ~291 drops exited and closed ones — where an absent asker reads as gone); `src/shiftlead/ui/inbox.py` (`ledger_for_you` ~2434, which draws the Repo page's For you and the Inbox's *Later* ledger rows off the repo facts and applies the same rule from the person inbox; `_owing` ~1906 for `answer_given`/`answer_how`); `src/shiftlead/ui/queue.py` (`put` ~256, the want, beside `with_you`; the card's `items_start` ~603); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html` (the ledger row's line ~1188 and foot ~1210); `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_row.html` (~93, the item's ⋯); `tests/test_queue.py`, `tests/test_ui_queue.py`, `tests/test_ui_inbox_ledger.py`

**Why:** TD-1316's second case (Paul, 2026-11-02: *TD1310 seems to be in a bad state — the same question seems to be popping up in the queue*): after the person answers a designer's `ask` about a held entry, the entry's `decision (paul)` row keeps drawing **Decide**, its **Answer…** and **Lift the hold**, until the designer's PR drops the hold and the checkout pulls it — up to 35 minutes, then about 100 s more on TD-1310.

**Fix:** as designed in §4.4 *The card's status* (*A `decision (paul)` member whose question the person has answered is no need while its asker acts on the answer*), *A row's state* (the word's place in the order) and §4.5 screen 12 *Three wants* and *For you*:
- **One rule, one function:** a pure `answered_hold(member, questions, states, open_refs, now)` in `src/sessionorc/queue.py` returns the member's `answered: {mid, asker, kind, closed_at, outcome}` or None. `member` is the entry with its repo, `written` and its hold; `questions` the person inbox's answered `ask`s and `steer`s; `states` a map from record id to state, built by each caller from the **unfiltered** records (`reading` has `Session` objects, agent_tick.py ~3371 — take `records = list(records)` before `recs` consumes and filters it, queue.py ~287; `ledger_for_you` has the `list` RPC's view dicts), keyed by bare record id on both sides (a `Session`'s `id`, a view dict's `id`), an absent id gone; `open_refs` the open-member index below, built over **every** repo's facts (`h.repos()`) at each call site, so the Repo page (which passes one repo, app.py ~1956) and the Queue link alike; `now` aware UTC (`ledger_view` passes none today, app.py ~4613: `now or datetime.now(UTC)`). Both callers use it: `reading` (for `_state`) and `ledger_for_you` (the Repo page's For you, the Inbox's *Later*, and the Queue's ledger items, which arrive as its `later` rows). Neither has the person inbox today: the Repo page's `asyncio.gather` (app.py ~1866) adds `h.person_inbox`, and `ledger_view(fleet)` (~4603) takes `inbox` as a second argument, which three of its four callers already hold (~4698 holds only `fleet` and reads it) — both read `h.person_inbox(fleet, …)`, the UI's with `_owing` applied, so `mid` finds a row that already carries `answer_given`/`answer_how`, and the Repo page's gather calls the same, never a raw read — with its callers (~3950, ~4654, ~4698, ~4852).
- **The match:** a question's first ref — `refs(about)`, else `refs(subject + text)`, `named[0]` as `mail_card` takes it (queue.py ~102), extracted into a helper both share. A `<repo>#TD-n` ref names that repo's member; a bare `TD-n` links only where exactly one **open** member across the repos carries it (an index over open entries, archived rows out), else no link. `card_index` is not used: it does not exist yet while `_state` runs inside `row()`.
- **It applies** when the question was closed by the person's answer (`replied`, or `go_with_it`, the person's **Go with it**) and still owes its outcome, or has a `done` outcome while the entry's `written` is not later than the answer's `closed_at`, for no more than a day after the outcome's `outcome.at` (all three aware UTC datetimes: `closed_at` parsed, the mtime from its epoch float; never compare strings). The newest by `closed_at` names the asker.
- **It does not** on any outcome but `done` (`blocked`, `dropped`, `asker_gone`, `dismissed`, `asked_again`); when, after a `done`, the entry file is written after the answer and still holds the hold; or when the asker's record — the question's `from` split by `naming.split_address` (it is `id@host`, as agent_tick.py ~1338 splits an address), looked up in `states` — is exited, closed or absent while the debt is unpaid. (The Inbox's own asker lookup, app.py ~1343, reads `from` unsplit; the split here makes an `id@host` sender read alike on both.) `closed_at` is `now_iso()` with a `Z` (models.py ~201), which `datetime.fromisoformat` reads on 3.11+.
- **The row:** `_state` keeps the `own` early return first (*with you in <name>* wins), then returns the word *answered — waiting on <asker>* with the same `need` (`decision`), as *with you in <name>* does: the id lists read it, and the card's status tests skip such a member (queue.py ~676). `ui/queue.py` gains the want `answered` (beside `with_you`, outside `WANTS`), set by `put` from the `later` row's `answered`, after `with_of` is tried (ui/queue.py ~260 runs it only while the want is in `WANTS`, so `put` tests `with_of` first and `answered` only where it gives none); the foot's words come from `_owing` on the question found by `mid` in the person inbox — `mid` is all the UI takes from `answered_hold` for them, so no wording lives in `sessionorc`.
- The Queue card draws the item in the grey section beside *with you in <name>*: its line, the foot from the question's own `answer_given`/`answer_how` (*you answered "<answer>"*, *you answered* alone for a typed reply, *you let it go with its default*) then *in <asker>'s <ask|steer>, <age> ago — the hold goes when its PR merges* (the answer's age, `closed_at`), **Open** and its ⋯ with **Lift the hold** in it, and no answers, **Answer…** or Start session (it does not count toward `items_start`); counted on no pill, no colour, not in **Needs you (n)**. `ledger_for_you` draws the same on the Repo page's For you and the Inbox's *Later*.

**Done when** a test builds a held entry with an answered ask about it owing its outcome and finds the word, no need on the card, no answers or Answer… on its item, and Lift the hold only in its ⋯; a second gives the ask a `done` outcome and finds the word until the entry's `written` passes the answer, then the need again with the hold still standing (including a write before the `done` was sent), and the need again a day after the outcome with the file unwritten; one each for `asker_gone`, an exited asker, a dismissed question and `asked_again`, each finding the need; a third gives it `blocked` and finds the need; a fourth draws the foot for a typed reply as *you answered* with no words, and finds **Lift the hold** in the item's ⋯, whose press sends the ledger row's decided line and takes the item away; one finds a bare `TD-n` carried by one repo's open member linking, and one carried by open members of two repos linking to neither; one finds *with you in <name>* winning over the answered word; one finds a second open question about the entry drawn as its own Decide row beside the word; one finds the facts carrying `written` (per file, and the one file's mtime for a one-file ledger, where a sibling's edit after the `done` ends the word); one finds `ledger_for_you` drawing the word and no Answer… on the Repo page's For you row; and `pdm run test` passes.
