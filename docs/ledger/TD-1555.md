## TD-1555: Answer… on a board item sent its words as a mail reply to another item's ask: bind the box's target to its own item, across the Queue's re-reads

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-07 (Paul, through ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-shiftlead-techlead-ao-1)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 36ed63ba605847f0a480b06b6de549a9
**Location:** src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.js (the click handler's reply and board_reply branches, AO.compose, the Queue's reread, AO.openMenus/AO.reopenMenus); src/shiftlead/ui/templates/inbox_row.html (answer_row, board_answer); design §4.5 screen 12 Three verbs: Answer, §4.5a Queue card: answers, Answer…

**Why:** On 2026-11-07, Paul pressed **Answer…** on the TD-1437 end-of-rounds board item. That item is drawn on card shiftlead#TD-1369 as *on shiftlead#TD-1437 · decide · board line · design #3225*. He typed his Hold into it (*Hold: drop Dismiss. A failure blocks only the failed commit…*). The words never reached the board: line 17 of `docs/user_attention.md`, the TD-1437 decide, stayed open with no `Decided:`.

Instead, shiftlead-ui logged `POST /api/person/reply` at 12:34:07 MDT. The text was stored as reply m-8e970f0e4961 to m-998693d16e2a, the designer's td-max shape ask (TD-1529), which was open on another card, and that ask closed *replied*. The designer saw the mismatch, so td-max came to no harm, but Paul's decision on TD-1437 was lost without a word. TD-1554 (#3233) carried it by hand. The report came through `ao-led-tds-ledger` (m-dea2c477dcf1).

An answer that lands on the wrong item is worse than one that fails. It closes a question the person never answered, and it leaves the one they did answer open, with nothing on the page that says so.

**Fix:** First, find the road the press took. The board item's **Answer…** is `board_answer` (`data-act="board_reply"`, `inbox_row.html`). It posts `/api/person/board` with `action: reply`. That road appends the words to the line and leaves a `decide` line due. Design §4.5 screen 12 *Three verbs: Answer* and §4.5a *Queue card: answers, Answer…* say something else: a board line's answers, Answer… among them, take *a board line's decided line* (§4.4 *Board write-back*). `POST /api/person/reply` comes only from a mail row's `data-act="reply"` button. So the click that opened the box, or the button the page read once the box closed, was the TD-1529 ask's, not the TD-1437 line's. Rule out each road, and keep the test or the fix of whichever one it was:
- **(a)** The box was opened on the TD-1529 ask's **Answer…**, and the Queue's re-read (`reread`, every 30 s, `lists.innerHTML = …`) then moved the cards under it, so the page showed the TD-1437 item where the box had been opened.
- **(b)** `AO.reopenMenus` reopens a ⋯ the person had open after the swap by its home's key and the menu's **index** within that home (`{k: card, v, i}`). Where the home is a card and its items' order or number changed, a different item's ⋯ opens, with that item's **Answer…** under the cursor.
- **(c)** The click handler reads `b.dataset` (`msg`, `from`, `board`, `line`, `key`) **after** `await AO.compose(…)`, not when the button was pressed (app.js, the `message`/`reply` and `board_reply` branches). Whatever can change a live button's data during the compose would retarget the press.

Then make the target bound for good. **Answer…** reads its target — kind (mail reply, board reply, ledger reply), message id or board key and line, and repo — from the button **when it is pressed**, before the box opens. The box carries that target and says it, in its title (*Reply to …* names the item, not only the addressee). What it sends is that target, whatever the page re-renders meanwhile. A re-read while a compose box is open keeps the box's target, and the box closes with *the item changed — your words are kept* where its item has left the page. `reopenMenus` finds a menu by its item's own key (`data-msg`, `data-key`), never by an index within a card. A board item's Answer… never sends a mail reply. The decided-line gap — on a `decide` line, Answer… writing the person's words as the line's `Decided:` — is TD-1557's: §4.4 *Decide* and §4.5 screen 12 disagree on it, a design round's question (the techlead's answer, m-742329aa2fc1, 2026-11-07).

**Done when:** The road the 2026-11-07 press took is named in the PR, among (a), (b) and (c) or another. A test in the Queue's JS tests (or a Playwright run on a scratch home) opens **Answer…** on a board decide item, re-reads the Queue under the open box with the cards reordered, and sends: the board line gets its reply, and no `/api/person/reply` is posted. The same holds for a mail item's Answer… with a board item drawn where it was. A ⋯ open across a re-read that reorders a card's items reopens on the same item or not at all.
