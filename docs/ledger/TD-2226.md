## TD-2226: The designer briefs move a design's unread fixes into its builds when its question is raised, not at the Go

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 7aa7ac1c9a524d9fa5b57c4af701997d
**Part of:** TD-2222

**Why:** The designer briefs say that a *Go* on a design's end-of-rounds item is the designer's work order: drop the blocks and move the unread fixes into the builds. But the press is the decide lift and closes the line, so no session receives it, and a lifted build can be picked before its fixes reach it (TD-2222). Design §4.9a *A design's answer* now has the designer write the unread fixes into the builds when it raises the item, and leaves the Go to the press and the home's record pass.

**Location:** docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md; src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md; src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md

**Fix:** Bring the briefs into line with design §4.9a *A design's answer* (TD-2222):

1. `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` (a held path): in *The end-of-rounds item*, the PR that raises the item also writes each fix no review has read into the Fix of the build it concerns (`ledger.py edit TD-n --section Fix`), marked *fixed, not reviewed; the builder checks it*. The detail block still lists them by build. A later round that reads a fix takes its mark away in that round's PR. In *The answered item is yours*, *On `Go`* becomes: a Go pressed on the item comes to nobody (the press lifts and closes the line, and the home's record pass writes the record); only a Go on a line that names no gate comes as a work order, and its session drops the blocks, writes the record's `Go by:` and `Decided:`, and closes the line in one PR.
2. `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md` (*A design's answer*) and `src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md` (where a grinder takes a design's answer): the same two changes in each brief's own words, with the template tests (`tests/test_briefs*.py`, whichever hold these texts) updated.
3. `docs/briefs/techlead-context.md` is not touched unless it repeats the Go rule (grep it first).

**Done when:** All three briefs say that the unread fixes go into the builds' Fix when the end-of-rounds item is raised, and that a Go on a gated item comes to no session, while a Go on a line that names no gate is the session's. Their tests pass.
