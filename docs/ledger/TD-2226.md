## TD-2226: The designer briefs move a design's unread fixes into its builds when its question is raised, not at the Go

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 7aa7ac1c9a524d9fa5b57c4af701997d
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-2222

**Why:** The designer briefs say that a *Go* on a design's end-of-rounds item is the designer's work order: drop the blocks and move the unread fixes into the builds. But the press is the decide lift and closes the line, so no session receives it, and a lifted build can be picked before its fixes reach it (TD-2222). Design §4.9a *A design's answer* now has the designer write the unread fixes into the builds when it raises the item, and leaves the Go to the press and the home's record pass.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that writes your Go onto a design's review record once your answer lifts its gate?

**Location:** docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md; src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md; src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md

**Fix:** Bring the briefs into line with design §4.9a *A design's answer* and §4.4 *The same pass records a design's Go* (TD-2222):

1. `docs/briefs/designer-ao-1.md` (a held path):
   - In *The end-of-rounds item*, the PR that raises the item also writes each fix no review has read into the Fix of the build it concerns (`ledger.py edit TD-n --section Fix`), marked *fixed, not reviewed; the builder checks it*. The detail block still lists them by build.
   - A later round's PR (a bought round's or a `Hold` revision's) brings the builds into line with the design as it then stands: a fix its reviews read loses its mark, and one the round changes, rejects or drops is rewritten or taken out of the build's Fix.
   - A `Hold` revision writes `Decided: pending` and `Go by: pending` back on the record, so the home's record pass writes the next Go.
   - In *The answered item is yours*, *On `Go`* becomes: a Go comes to no designer. Pressed on the item it lifts and closes the line, and the home's record pass writes the record. On a line that names no gate it is `free-pick`'s (§6 rule 6), a grinder's (step 3).
   - Each changed rule carries a line in the brief's house form for a departure from the pinned cadence: *(departs from cadence §4.8 After the item until a pinned §4.8 says it, when this line goes; proposed to agent-cadence by the person, board item 2026-11-25-d8adcc14)*.
2. `src/shiftlead/briefs/designer.md` (*A design's answer*): the same changes in its own words.
3. `src/shiftlead/briefs/grinder.md` (where a grinder takes a design's answer, line 16's *do a Go yourself*; the same line's *a PR the gate sweep opens (a lift or a sweep PR…)* and *red, lift and sweep orders* name a record PR too, §4.4 *The same pass records a design's Go*): its Go clause narrows to a decided *Go* on a line that names no gate, which is `free-pick`'s. The grinder lifts nothing: it finds the design by the line's `Context:` (the design's `TD-n` alone), raises a `decide` item on the design's gate (`board_edit.py add --kind decide --context "TD-n decision (<person>)" --parts <every build gated on it> --answer "Go — the grinders build it" --answer "Not yet — nobody picks the builds until you say" --lift Go`, its detail naming the old line), and closes the old line (`board_edit.py done`) pointing at it, in one PR; `--parts` lists only the builds that still carry the gate. Where the design's gate is lifted already, it closes the old line with its why and raises nothing; where an item on that gate is open already (its `Context:` exactly `TD-n decision (<person>)`, *Go* among its lifts), it closes the old line pointing at that item and raises an item only for the gated builds that item's `Parts:` leave out. The person's press on the new item lifts the design and its builds, and the home's record pass writes the record. It writes no record, runs no `--override` (refused to a seat) and no `--by <person>` lift (agent-cadence's recorded-answer route lifts no build from a bare `TD-n` line).
4. No test holds these texts today (checked 2026-11-25). Add one in `tests/test_briefs_layout.py` that reads `designer.md` and `grinder.md` and asserts the new rule's phrases: the fixes written when the item is raised, a Go coming to no designer, and the grinder carrying a no-gate Go to a new `decide` item on the design's gate rather than lifting it.
5. `docs/briefs/techlead-context.md` does not repeat the Go rule (checked 2026-11-25). Touch it only if it has come to repeat it.

**Done when:** All three briefs say that the unread fixes go into the builds' Fix when the end-of-rounds item is raised, that a later round brings them into line, and that a `Hold` revision writes the record's Go lines back to pending. They say that a Go on a gated item comes to no session, while a Go on a line that names no gate is a grinder's. The designer briefs mark the departure from cadence §4.8. Their tests pass.
