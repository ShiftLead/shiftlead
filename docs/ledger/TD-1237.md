**Why:** The precheck of #2665 (docs-only: memory files and a §4.5a rewrap) at ccffe81 failed this one test, with 4627 others passing: `explain`'s reason read `rule gemini-usage-limit matched, applied as scraped; held for its screen` where the test expects `… but the usage poll's limited stands — its reading kept`.
