**Fix:** Design first, after agent-cadence's round-ending guidance (filed there separately; its id was not known when this entry was filed — once it is, it goes in `Blocked by:` as `agent-cadence#TD-n`):
- the techlead makes the end-of-rounds decision under agent-cadence's guidance: One more round while build-changing findings shrink but some are unread; Go once no build-changing fix is unread; Hold-and-split when findings are flat or growing, or a concern recurs; Hold-and-raise for a question only the person can settle;
- a setting `design_go`: `far-reaching-to-person` (default) | `techlead` | `person`. Under the default, a routine design's Go is the techlead's, recorded as made for the person (ledgerboard TD-132), and a far-reaching design's Go comes to the person;
- Hold-and-raise goes to the director where its delegation covers the question (TD-1910), otherwise to the person;
- ShiftLead's own `[far-reaching]` globs (agent-cadence's `docs/cadence.toml`) cover its briefs, roles, org schema, glossary and anything changing who acts for the person.

Related: TD-1910 (the director as chief of staff), ledgerboard TD-132, design §4.9b (the techlead seat), §4.9c (the end-of-rounds item).

**Done when:** The techlead judges a design's counted verdict, the default judge standing in where it cannot; ShiftLead's `[far-reaching]` table is written, so a far-reaching design's Go reaches the person and a routine design's judge's Go reaches no one; listing `"**"` there sends every design to the person.
