## TD-2072: ao switch-over move and undo: the window's nine steps, and going back

**Why:** The home moves from profiles and members to logins and flows in one run, with every team stopped, and can be put back until a team is started (design 4.9e, The window, Work in flight, Going back). Nothing does this today.

**Ask:** Should the builders build the switch-over's move and its undo?

**Fix:** Build ao switch-over move and undo. move runs the nine steps of design 4.9e The window in order, writing each finished step to the journal, which holds the answers' hash, and resuming from it; a changed answers file refuses a resume; a failed step leaves the host agents stopped and says so. --rehearse runs on a copy, stops or starts nothing and does not refuse on a blocker of the day; --host does one ssh node's steps with its own answers, journal and backup and starts its agent again. It backs up the four files, the session records and the home's record of its teams; writes each host's logins.yml before removing profiles.yml, a container node's from that node's profiles; moves no folder; rewrites each record's login, account and profile; writes every team's settings from the answers, a team's schedule.login among them, builtins.default, director and schedule, every team's stopped record with its time, and last the marker. It writes nothing for work in flight. After the marker the home reads built-in flows from flows3, refuses a flow with no format key, ignores a teams block in org.yml or a repo file and names it in ao org check. A fresh home with no profiles and no teams is moved with no answers. undo puts the backup's files back, of each session record only profile, login and account, removes the marker, on a finished window or a half-run one, then moves the journal and backup aside; it is refused once a team's record is not the one the move wrote, where the journal holds that step, or a session launched since is alive, after printing what is lost.

**Done when:** On a fixture home shaped like today's: move leaves every host switched, every team stopped with its picked flow, logins and counts, the marker written and the backup kept; killed after each step in turn and run again, it ends in the same state; a changed answers file refuses the resume; --rehearse leaves the real files untouched and runs with a team still started; undo restores the four files and the teams' record byte for byte and each session record's three fields, keeping what a live session wrote to its record since, and moves the journal aside; a second move then runs from its first step; undo is refused after a team's Start, and is not refused on a window killed before the teams' records were written. A fresh fixture home is moved with no answers.
