**Owner:** grinder

**Status:** Open

**Why:** The design cost model (docs/decisions/2026-11-12-design-cost-model.md, TD-1764) attributes a reviewer to a design by its first turn falling in the design's window of the designer session. One designer session works several designs' branches in turn, so TD-1543's window takes in the reviewers of TD-1550 (on `td1550-design`) and TD-1590 (on `td1590-design`), designs the cost model did not select: its `turns.csv` counts 360 rows under TD-1543 that are theirs, about $36 of TD-1543's $110.44 reviewer spend. Found by TD-1775's baseline capture (docs/decisions/2026-11-12-design-trial/baseline/README.md), which keeps each turn under one design by the branch it ran on.

**Location:** docs/decisions/2026-11-12-design-cost-model/

**Fix:** Re-run the cost model's `tables.py select` with a reviewer kept only where its own rows' branch is the design's (or a launch branch, as the baseline's `capture.py` `one_design_each` rules), rebuild `turns.csv`, and correct every figure the document prints from it (TD-1543's rows, the pooled shares, the 51% applying share if it moves). Say in the document what changed and why.

**Done when:** Done when the cost model's turns.csv holds no reviewer of another design under TD-1543 and its document's figures are re-derived from the corrected rows.
