## TD-2220: Tell a stage's, an audit's and a job's session that the repo's rules win over the person's, as a member's brief does

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** fb519b13ac1c429aa6cc9cf3192a0ea4
**Part of:** TD-2215

**Why:** TD-2215 (design 4.9c *The person's own rules under a flow*): a stage's, an audit's, a job's and a review stage's session already carry the person's layer under *Your own rules* before *This repo's rules* (TD-2049, TD-1016), but their templates (`stage.md`, `job.md`, `audit.md`, and `reviewer.md`, which a review stage composes from) lack the sentence every member's template carries before those two parts, so such a session is never told which wins when they disagree, that both win over its stage's brief, nor that neither may take from what it is told never to do.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/briefs/stage.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/job.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/audit.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/reviewer.md

**Fix:** Design 4.9c *The person's own rules under a flow*, §4.8. `src/shiftlead/briefs/stage.md`, `job.md`, `audit.md` and `reviewer.md` (a review stage's and a job's review compose from it) gain, as the first lines under *Your own rules*, the members' sentence of `grinder.md`'s line 7 with *this brief* read as everything the session is told: *What follows are the two parts of what you are told that did not ship with shiftlead: first the person's own rules, from their agent-cadence layer (`none` means they added nothing), then this repo's own part. Where these and the rest of what you are told disagree — your brief among it — the later wins — the repo's over the person's, and both over the rest — except that they may add to what you are told never to do, and never take from it. What the host agent enforces is not words, and no brief moves it.* Nothing else in the templates changes; *Your brief* stays last in the stage and job templates.

**Done when:** A test composes a stage's, an audit's, a job's and a review stage's prompt and finds the sentence once, under *Your own rules*, before the person's part and the repo's; the existing tests of those templates' order still pass.
