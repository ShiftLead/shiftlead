## TD-2220: Tell a stage's, an audit's and a job's session that the repo's rules win over the person's, as a member's brief does

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** fb519b13ac1c429aa6cc9cf3192a0ea4
**Part of:** TD-2215

**Why:** TD-2215 (design 4.9c *The person's own rules under a flow*): a stage's, an audit's and a job's session already carry the person's layer under *Your own rules* before *This repo's rules* (TD-2049, TD-1016), but their templates lack the sentence every member's template carries before those two parts, so such a session is never told which wins when they disagree, nor that neither may take from what it is told never to do.

**Location:** src/shiftlead/briefs/stage.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/job.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/audit.md

**Fix:** Design 4.9c *The person's own rules under a flow*, §4.8. `src/shiftlead/briefs/stage.md`, `job.md` and `audit.md` gain, as the first lines under *Your own rules*, the members' sentence in their words: the two parts that follow did not ship with ShiftLead, the person's own rules from their agent-cadence layer (*none* meaning they added nothing) and then the repo's own part; where these and the rest of the brief disagree the later wins, the person's over ShiftLead's and the repo's over the person's, except that they may add to what the brief says never to do and never take from it; what the host agent enforces is not words and no brief moves it. Its wording follows `grinder.md`'s line 7 with *this brief* kept. Nothing else in the templates changes; *Your brief* stays last.

**Done when:** A test composes a stage's, an audit's and a job's prompt and finds the sentence once, before the person's part and the repo's; the existing tests of those templates' order still pass.
