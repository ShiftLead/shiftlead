## TD-2220: Tell a stage's, an audit's and a job's session that the repo's rules win over the person's, as a member's brief does

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-25
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** fb519b13ac1c429aa6cc9cf3192a0ea4
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)
**Part of:** TD-2215

**Why:** TD-2215 (design 4.9c *The person's own rules under a flow*): a stage's, an audit's, a job's and a review stage's session already carry the person's layer under *Your own rules* before *This repo's rules* (TD-2049, TD-1016), but their templates (`stage.md`, `job.md`, `audit.md`, and `reviewer.md`, which a review stage composes from) lack the sentence the member templates (all but `reviewer.md`) carry before those two parts, so such a session is never told which wins when they disagree, that both win over its stage's brief, nor that neither may take from what it is told never to do.

**Ask:** Should the grinders build the design that keeps your own layer briefs reaching a flow's sessions, as its review rounds left it?

**Location:** src/shiftlead/briefs/stage.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/job.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/audit.md, src/shiftlead/briefs/reviewer.md

**Fix:** Design 4.9c *The person's own rules under a flow*, §4.8. `src/shiftlead/briefs/stage.md`, `job.md`, `audit.md` and `reviewer.md` (a review stage's and a job's review compose from it) gain, as the first lines under *Your own rules*, the members' sentence, with *this brief* read as everything the session is told: *What follows are the two parts of what you are told that did not ship with shiftlead: first the person's own rules, from their agent-cadence layer (`none` means they added nothing), then this repo's own part. Where these and the rest of what you are told disagree — your brief among it — the later wins — the repo's over the person's, and both over the rest — except that they may add to what you are told never to do, and never take from it. What the host agent enforces is not words, and no brief moves it.* A member in the reviewer role composes from `reviewer.md` too and gains the sentence with it. The other member templates (`grinder.md`, `techlead.md`, `auditor.md` and the rest) keep their own wording, which says the same for a member's brief. Nothing else in the templates changes; *Your brief* stays last in the stage, audit, job and reviewer templates.

**Done when:** A test composes a stage's, an audit's, a job's, a review stage's and a reviewer member's prompt and finds the sentence once, under *Your own rules*, before the person's part and the repo's; the existing tests of those templates' order still pass.
