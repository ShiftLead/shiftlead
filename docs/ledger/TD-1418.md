**Why:** TD-1412's design (§5 *The seams*, `{merge}`; §4.9c *A record written before the layout slots*). agent-cadence TD-233, in shiftlead's pin since #2983, makes a cadence merge step 5's line. That line hands the gate's reading to `forge.py squash-message`, which composes a squash ending in `Merged-by:`, and its merge stays a literal `gh pr merge`. The built-in briefs still teach a merge with no trailer. `techlead.stage.md` and `flows/td/review.md` say `gh pr merge <n> --squash`, and the rest say only *merge once the gate passes ({gate})*. After agent-cadence TD-234 the weekly read (`check_cadence.py --since`) lists every such merge as merged outside step 5.

**Fix:** As §5 *The seams* says of `{merge}`:
- `cadence.composes(root)` (`src/sessionorc/cadence.py`) is true when the tree the gate runs from holds a `scripts/forge.py` whose text names `squash-message`. That tree is the pinned tree in a pinned repo and the checkout's `scripts/` (or `files/scripts/` in agent-cadence's own source) elsewhere. Read it as `repoconfig.layout` reads its other files (`there()`, and `cfg.read` on a node). TD-1419 reuses it.
- `repoconfig.layout` gains `{merge}` in `LAYOUT_SLOTS` and its return. Pinned: the line with both scripts as `cadence.typed('run', …, venv=venv)` gives them (`check_cadence.py --pr {pr} --trailers "$m/gate"`, `forge.py squash-message {pr} --gate "$m/gate" --out "$m"`). Copy or source: `python3 <pre>scripts/check_cadence.py …` and `python3 <pre>scripts/forge.py …`. The tail is always `&& gh pr merge {pr} --squash --match-head-commit "$(cat "$m/sha")" --subject "$(cat "$m/subject")" --body-file "$m/body"`. Where `composes` is false it is `gh pr merge {pr} --squash`. `{pr}` is filled after it as after `{gate}` (`repoconfig.py` around line 648; `sessionorc/brief.py`'s slot list).
- §4.9c's replay check reads the nine names, `{merge}` among them.
- Every built-in brief and flow stage that has a session merge a PR names `{merge}`, never a bare `gh pr merge`. That is `techlead.stage.md` and `flows/td/review.md` (the unattended asker's merge, keeping *never `--delete-branch`*), and every *merge once the gate passes ({gate})* under `src/shiftlead/briefs/` and `src/shiftlead/flows/`, which becomes *merge once the gate passes ({gate}), through step 5's line: {merge}* or the like. `grep -rn merge` over both directories finds each one.
- Tests: the pinned, copy, node and no-`squash-message` forms of `{merge}`; `{pr}` filled in it; a record written before `{merge}` replays its stored prompt; no built-in brief names `gh pr merge` outside `{merge}`'s fill.

**Done when:** A team member started in a pinned repo is briefed with step 5's line, its `{pr}` filled. `grep -rn "gh pr merge" src/shiftlead/briefs src/shiftlead/flows` finds nothing. The tests above pass.
