## TD-1418: Briefs name cadence step 5's line: the {merge} layout slot, and every built-in brief's merge names it

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-05
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 2cb31d2dcee943d4a740be55fe9a3407
**Part of:** TD-1412
**Location:** src/shiftlead/repoconfig.py (LAYOUT_SLOTS, layout); src/sessionorc/cadence.py (composes); src/sessionorc/brief.py; src/shiftlead/briefs/; src/shiftlead/flows/; design §5 The seams, §4.9c

**Why:** TD-1412's design (§5 *The seams*, `{merge}`; §4.9c *A record written before the layout slots*). agent-cadence TD-233, in shiftlead's pin since #2983, makes a cadence merge step 5's line. That line hands the gate's reading to `forge.py squash-message`, which composes a squash ending in `Merged-by:`, and its merge stays a literal `gh pr merge`. The built-in briefs still teach a merge with no trailer. `techlead.stage.md` and `flows/td/review.md` say `gh pr merge <n> --squash`, and the rest say only *merge once the gate passes ({gate})*. After agent-cadence TD-234 the weekly read (`check_cadence.py --since`) lists every such merge as merged outside step 5.

**Fix:** As §5 *The seams* says of `{merge}`:
- **`cadence.composes(root)`** (`src/sessionorc/cadence.py`) answers yes, no or unreadable (§5). Yes means the tree the gate runs from holds a `scripts/forge.py` whose text names `squash-message`. No means the tree was read and its `forge.py` does not name it. Unreadable means the tree could not be resolved or read (`cadence.tree` gave `(None, why)`, `ensure_tree` raised `Refusal`, or a read across the link failed). It is raised as `OSError(why)`, never returned as no: `layout` lets it go up as its other failed reads do, and TD-1419 counts it as *no reading*. The tree depends on the checkout:
  - pinned: the pinned tree, `cadence.tree(root)`;
  - a copy: the checkout's `scripts/`;
  - agent-cadence's own source: `files/scripts/`.
  On a container node, read the pin's source and ref from the node's `docs/cadence.toml` through `cfg.read`. Where that pin is the registry checkout's for the repo, the tree is `cadence.tree(root)`. Otherwise it is the home's own copy, `agent_cadence.ensure_tree(source, ref)`, with its scripts under `files/scripts/`, cached per pin. That call runs under `TREE_TIMEOUT` and after the launcher's interface check, as `cadence.tree` runs it. A timeout is unreadable. A copy's `scripts/forge.py` there is read through `cfg.read`, as `repoconfig.layout` reads its other files.
- **`repoconfig.layout` gains `{merge}`** in `LAYOUT_SLOTS` (and `sessionorc/brief.py`'s list) and in its return.
  - Pinned: only the launcher's words go through `cadence.typed('run', 'check_cadence.py', venv=venv)` and `cadence.typed('run', 'forge.py', 'squash-message', venv=venv)`. Everything else is appended as text, as `{gate}` appends ` --pr {pr}` (`typed` is `shlex.join` and would quote `$m` and `{pr}`). The result is `m=$(mktemp -d); <typed check> --pr {pr} --trailers "$m/gate"; <typed squash-message> {pr} --gate "$m/gate" --out "$m" && gh pr merge {pr} --squash --match-head-commit "$(cat "$m/sha")" --subject "$(cat "$m/subject")" --body-file "$m/body"`.
  - Copy or source: `python3 <pre>scripts/check_cadence.py …` and `python3 <pre>scripts/forge.py squash-message …`, with the same tail.
  - Where `composes` is false: `gh pr merge {pr} --squash`.
  - `{pr}` is filled after it, as after `{gate}` (`repoconfig.py` around line 648).
- **§4.9c's replay check** reads the nine names, `{merge}` among them.
- **The briefs.** Every built-in brief and flow stage that has a session merge a PR names `{merge}`, never a bare `gh pr merge`, and says to type it whole as one command. That covers:
  - `techlead.stage.md` and `flows/td/review.md`: the unattended asker's merge, keeping *never `--delete-branch`*;
  - every *merge once the gate passes ({gate})* under `src/shiftlead/briefs/` and `src/shiftlead/flows/` (for example *merge once the gate passes ({gate}), through step 5's line, typed whole as one command: {merge}*);
  - the looser forms in `techlead.md`, `sweeper.md` and `entry.md`.
  `grep -rn merge` over both directories finds each one.
- **Tests:**
  - an unreadable tree raises `OSError` from `layout` and is never the bare form;
  - a node whose pin is the checkout's uses `cadence.tree`, and another pin's `ensure_tree` is bounded by `TREE_TIMEOUT` (a hung fetch shim gives unreadable);
  - the pinned, copy, source, node and no-`squash-message` forms of `{merge}`, each asserting the exact string (no quote around `$m` or `{pr}`);
  - `{pr}` filled in it;
  - a record written before `{merge}` replays its stored prompt;
  - no built-in brief names `gh pr merge` outside `{merge}`'s fill.

**Done when:** A team member started in a pinned repo is briefed with step 5's line, its `{pr}` filled. `grep -rn "gh pr merge" src/shiftlead/briefs src/shiftlead/flows` finds nothing. The tests above pass.
