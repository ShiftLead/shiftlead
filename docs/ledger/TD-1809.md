Keep every `data-` attribute and key the holds and patches read unchanged: only drawn words change. Update the tests that assert the old words (`grep -rn "Decide (\|overdue\|board line" tests`). Shots in light, dark and phone width by `scripts/look_home.py`, under `docs/mockups/reviews/`.

**Done when:** The Queue and the card's page draw block headings, kind marks, sources, due dates, dates, relation ids, titles, button labels and the phone title as *The Queue's words* says, no raw backtick, asterisk or `2026-11-19` date is left on either page, the press's decrement writes the new headings, the tests pass, and the PR names its light, dark and phone shots.
