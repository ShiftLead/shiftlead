**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-19, on shiftlead#TD-794's card: "This seems like a confusing way to show information to the user … are we saying that i actually need to lift the holds on the items, then they will just go by the blocked-by chain?" A design's end-of-rounds question is one board item (`Context: TD-1919 decision (paul)`, `Parts: TD-1947, TD-1948`), but the design and each held build carry a `decision (paul)` gate of their own, each with the same Ask, so the card draws it as up to three Decide rows, each with **Answer…**, **Not clear** and **Lift the hold**, and after an answer each row reads its own wait (*answered — waiting on …*, or, through TD-2020's bug, *needs you* again). **Lift the hold** on a held build is in effect a Go on the whole design from one build's row, and its name does not say so. The builds' wait on the design is already expressible as a dependency: `Blocked by: TD-1919`.

**Design PR:** #4269

**Location:** design §4.4 The card's status, Where Lift the hold is drawn; §4.5 screen 12; src/sessionorc/queue.py; the cadence's end-of-rounds item

**Fix:** Design first (§4.4 *The card's status*, *Where Lift the hold is drawn*, *Board write-back*; §4.5 screen 12; cadence §2.4 / procedures/cadence.md step 3 for what the design's end of rounds writes on its builds, a change in agent-cadence if it moves). The design says:
1. **One question, one row.** An end-of-rounds question is drawn once on its card, on the design's row, naming the builds it releases; a held build draws no Decide row of its own and reads *waits on TD-<design>'s question* (or, answered, the bought round's entry).
2. **How a build waits.** Weigh carrying the wait as `Blocked by: TD-<design>` (the design stays open until its question is answered Go, and archives on Go, which unblocks its builds by the ordinary chain) against today's per-build `decision (paul)` gate written and lifted with the design's; pick one, and say what the gate sweep, `review_stats.py --verdict`, ledgerboard's decide lift and `Parts:` become.
3. **The press's name.** On an answered or held question, **Lift the hold** is named for what it does (a Go on the design and its builds), or goes where **Answer…**'s Go says the same.
Agree the card's layout with Paul on mockups (docs/mockups/reviews/) before any model round. Builds under it as their own entries.
