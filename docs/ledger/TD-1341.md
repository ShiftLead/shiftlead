## TD-1341: A Waiting card with a long wait phrase is cut off at its right edge: the phrase does not wrap, so it pushes the header past the card, and the card clips it

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03 (Paul, through Add entry; drafted by ao-shiftlead-card-session)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** live-check #2860
**Status:** Built 2026-11-03 in #2860 (grinder-ao-2): `.qstatus.s-waits` wraps (`white-space: normal; overflow-wrap: anywhere`) and `.qright` shrinks to the card (`min-width: 0; max-width: 100%`); the design's *A card* sentence says the phrase wraps; a scratch home's UI check at 1100, 800 and 375 px showed the whole header, the old rules clipping the pill at 1100. Row state words and the holder's pill are bounded, checked and unchanged. **Live check:** once #2860 is live (`ao promote status`), read the live Queue headless (no press) at 1100 px and 375 px: a Waiting card with a long wait phrase (TD-1302's, or any) has its phrase wrapped and its pill inside the card.
**Location:** `src/shiftlead/ui/templates/queue_card.html` (~60, the header's `<span class="qstatus s-waits">{{ c.wait.phrase }}</span>`); `src/shiftlead/ui/static/app.css` (`.qstatus { white-space: nowrap; … }` ~1224; `.qright` ~1206, which wraps between its children but not inside one; `.qcards:not(.bcards) > .qcard { … overflow: hidden; }` ~1153, which clips; `.queuepage { max-width: 1100px }` ~1284); `docs/design/4.5-ui.md` (screen 12 *A card*, the header's status: *on a Waiting card its wait phrase*); `docs/design/4.5a-controls.md` (row *Queue page: card*, ~128)

**Why:** Paul, 2026-11-03, with a screenshot of TD-1302's card on the Queue: *It looks like this card is cut off on the right.* The card is Waiting, and its wait phrase is the `Waits:` line of TD-1305 (*waits on Paul's turning push on from a device (the board line *Once #2820 is promoted, turn on push from your iPhone*), after setting `notify.push.secrets` and **Make key***). Because every `.qstatus` is `white-space: nowrap`, the phrase is one unbreakable run about as wide as the page. `.qright` sends it to its own line, but that line is still wider than the card. The card's `overflow: hidden` then clips the phrase mid-word and cuts off the header's right edge with it: the amber *Decide 2 · Do 1* pill reads *Decide 2 ·*. The person loses the part of the header that says what the card wants of them. Any Waiting card whose wait comes from a long `Waits:` line clips the same way, so this is not a one-off.

**Fix:**
- **Let the wait phrase wrap** (e.g. `.qstatus.s-waits { white-space: normal; overflow-wrap: anywhere; }`), or clamp it to one line with an ellipsis and keep the whole phrase in its `title`. The builder picks one and writes it in the design's *A card* sentence. The short status words (*ready*, *steer*, *stalled*, the pill) stay `nowrap`.
- **Check the rest of the header at the page's width and at narrow widths**: the pill, the count and the meter still sit at the right edge, and nothing overflows the card at 1100 px, 800 px or 375 px.
- **Look for the same clip elsewhere**: any other unbounded text inside an `overflow: hidden` card (a row's state word with a long `Waits:` hover, a holder name) is fixed the same way, or named here as checked.
- **Test**: a render test of a Waiting card with a long wait phrase, asserting the phrase's span can wrap (or carries the ellipsis and its title). The UI check on a scratch home at 1100 px shows the card's whole header.

**Done when** TD-1302's card, or any Waiting card with a long wait phrase, shows its whole header on the live Queue at the page's width and on a phone, the design says how the phrase is drawn, and `pdm run test` passes.

**Related:** TD-1302, TD-1305 (the card and the `Waits:` line that showed it); TD-915 (the card's box and its `overflow`).
