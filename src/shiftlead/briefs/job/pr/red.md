**A red pull request**: read why it is red, then either fix it on the pull request's own branch — a note to its author by `ao msg` when the author is live; the author's pull request stays the author's — or, where its cure is to close it (its work done elsewhere, superseded), close it with a comment saying why and end `closed-unmerged`. Its review evidence, and the reader of a held path, are the flow's.
