The person asked for a new entry in the ledger of {repo}: {ledger}, with Type {type} (shiftlead's design §4.9 *Add an entry to the ledger*). Their first message is their words for it. Turn them into one entry, asking the person only for what the words and the repo do not settle: a priority, which of two readings is meant, what *done* is.

- **Read before you write**: the ledger's preamble and template (its first entry), and its three newest entries, for the shape and the header lines the file asks for — **Priority**, **Type**, **Added**, the preamble's own lines (an owner, a kind, where it declares them; what blocks it as `**Blocked by:**`, never a written *pickable*), **Status**, **Location**, **Why**, **Fix** — and the code and the design the words touch, so **Location** names files and sections and **Why** says the harm or the value, not only the symptom (cadence §2.3).
- **Type**: `**Type:** {type}` after **Priority** (cadence §2.11). A feature's entry is the short one: its design round does the fleshing out.
- **The number** is `ao td next`'s: the home hands out the next free id, over main, its archive and every open PR, and records it, so nobody else is given it (design §4.4 *A ledger entry's id is handed out by the home*). Where `ao` is not there or refuses, the old rule: the first free one over the ledger and its archive on `origin/<default>` **and** every open PR (`gh pr list --state open`, and each one's diff of the ledger), read again right before the merge, and changed when another PR took it. If you are an interactive session and the person is still deciding on the entry, take its id with `ao td next --held`: it lands held until they release it.
- **Team**: where the ledger's `Fields:` line names two or more teams in its `Team` vocabulary, write `**Team:** <team>` after **Kind** (after **Waits** where both are) — never where it names fewer, since an undeclared field fails `ledger.py --check --since` (design §4.4 *An entry names its team*). The team is your own unless the work is plainly another's; a designer's builds take the designed entry's; a handed entry the `entry.team` it carries; else the team the words and the location point to. **Filled `by: tag`** (your fill's line reads *you are filled for: tag — TD-801, TD-802*): tag every listed id in one ledger PR, landed as below; where two readings stand, open the PR with your choice and `steer` the person with it as `--default`.
- **Added** names both hands: `**Added:** <date> (<the person>, through Add entry; drafted by <your session>)`, the date from `date`, never guessed.
- **The Summary row** goes in the same change: one row, `| TD-NNN | <title> | <priority> | <status> |`, in number order.
- **How it lands**, as every ledger change does (cadence §4): a branch off `origin/<default>`, a PR, the docs fact-check against the repo, the gate, a squash merge. The person does not approve the entry before it is filed: an entry that reads wrong is a line's edit afterwards.
