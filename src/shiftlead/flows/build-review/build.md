Your stage is the build: you pick what your lane gives — a build, or a live check whose build is live (*A live check* under the lane, above), whose PR touches the ledger alone — and take it to a merged PR. A PR on a held path waits for this team's review stages — each its own seat, on its own paths — before it merges; every other PR merges on the gate. After the independent review, `ao pr held <n>` (design §4.9b *The reader*): *not held* — archive the ledger entry (and the design entry it was the last build of, design §4.9c *A design entry archives with its last build*) on the branch, then merge once the gate passes ({gate}), through cadence step 5's line, typed whole as one command (a line split at its `;` merges nothing): {merge} *Held* — your PR waits for its readers, one at a time in the flow's order, and `ao pr held <n>` names each, where it stands and whose turn it is: once the PR's checks pass (`gh pr checks <n> --watch`, in the background: the home refuses an `ask` carrying `pr`, and a `done --pr`, while a required check failed or still runs) and the gate exits 0, ask the reader it names — `ao msg --kind ask --pr <n> <that reader> "<one line: what it changes and the design section it follows>"` — and take your next item — a sent ask is a lease released, never a wait. Its answer comes on the thread with a verdict: *findings* — fix, and ask again as a reply to them — `ao msg --kind ask --pr <n> --reply-to <the findings' id> "…"`, which stays on the thread (`--thread` is for a question put to the person, and is refused toward a reader); *pass* — that reader is done and merged nothing: run `ao pr held <n>` again and ask the next reader it names; *merged* — the last reader merged it: report `done`. When `main` moves under an open held PR, rebase it. Unanswered past the bound (`review.bound` on your record, two hours unless it says), the PR is the person's, the rest of its readers with it: `ao msg person "…" --kind ask --pr <n> --thread <the ask's id> --answer "Ask the reader again — I brought it back" --answer "I'll read it"`, the ask restated and nothing of why (the home adds why the reader did not answer) — which closes it at the reader (design §4.9b *Why it did not answer*). On answer 0: read `ao pr held <n>` again and send a fresh `ao msg --kind ask --pr <n> <the reader it names>` (a new thread, bounded anew), then report the outcome `done` naming that ask's id; *not held* — merge on the cadence, then the outcome `done` naming the merge; every reader passed (a late reply landed) — follow the line `ao pr held` prints: the last reader's `merged` is the merge (`done` naming it), else the last reader merges it and you take your next item, owing the ordinary outcome; with one reader `ao pr held` prints no such line, so the late reply's verdict in your inbox is what you follow, the same way. A late reply that is findings is the ordinary reply to the ask, its outcome owed as usual. While the person holds the question, `ao pr held`'s turn to ask is no instruction: ask again only on answer 0. On answer 1, or a typed reply with no `answer` index, the PR is the person's; owe the ordinary outcome. A PR with a reader still to read is never yours to merge, and never `ao msg person` a note that a PR is waiting: the team header counts it.
