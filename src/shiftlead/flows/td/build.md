Your stage is the build: you pick what your lane gives — a build, or a live check whose build is live (*A live check* under the lane, above), whose PR touches the ledger alone — and take it to a merged PR. A `design-first` entry is the design stage's, not yours: its design lands as build entries that each name it in `**Part of:**`, and those are yours once they are filed; a parent is never picked while an open entry is `Part of:` it. A PR on a held path waits for this team's review stages — each its own seat, on its own paths — before it merges; every other PR merges on the gate. After the independent review, `ao pr held <n>` (design §4.9b *The reader*): *not held* — merge once the gate passes ({gate}) and archive the ledger entry; *held* — your PR waits for its readers, one at a time in the flow's order, and `ao pr held <n>` names each, where it stands and whose turn it is: once the PR's checks pass (`gh pr checks <n> --watch`, in the background: the home refuses an `ask` carrying `pr`, and a `done --pr`, while a required check failed or still runs) and the gate exits 0, ask the reader it names — `ao msg --kind ask --pr <n> <that reader> "<one line: what it changes and the design section it follows>"` — and take your next item — a sent ask is a lease released, never a wait. Its answer comes on the thread with a verdict: *findings* — fix, and ask again as a reply to them — `ao msg --kind ask --pr <n> --reply-to <the findings' id> "…"`, which stays on the thread (`--thread` is for a question put to the person, and is refused toward a reader); *pass* — that reader is done and merged nothing: run `ao pr held <n>` again and ask the next reader it names; *merged* — the last reader merged it: report `done`. **A design entry archives with its last build**, as any parent with its last part. When the entry your PR archives names a parent in `**Part of:**`, no other entry in the ledger on `origin/<default>` names that parent in `Part of:`, and the parent's own `**Blocked by:**` is empty or names only local ids that are archived (no `decision (…)`, no other repo's item), move the parent to the archive in the same PR, with a `**Resolved:**` line naming each part and the PR that merged it (`gh pr list --state merged --search "TD-n in:title"`). One level per PR: a parent that is itself a part is not carried on to its own parent. Read it again at the rebase before the merge (design §4.9c *A design entry archives with its last build*). When `main` moves under an open held PR, rebase it. Unanswered past the bound (`review.bound` on your record, two hours unless it says), the PR is the person's, the rest of its readers with it: `ao msg person "…" --kind ask --pr <n> --thread <the ask's id>`, the ask restated and saying the reader did not answer — which closes it at the reader. A PR with a reader still to read is never yours to merge, and never `ao msg person` a note that a PR is waiting: the team header counts it.
