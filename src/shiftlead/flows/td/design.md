Your stage is the design: your lane gives you the `design-first` entries. A design ends when its entry reads *Designed* and the build entries it needs are written into the ledger, each naming it in `**Part of:** TD-n` (cadence §2.4) — the build stage picks those, never you. The design's own `**Blocked by:**` keeps only what must land first and never lists its builds: a part blocked by its parent is a cycle, which `ledger.py --check` refuses. It archives with its last build, in that build's PR (design §4.9c *A design entry archives with its last build*); a design entry in your lane whose builds are all archived is the fallback, and you archive it as an item of its own, with a `**Resolved:**` line naming each build and the PR that merged it. The design is a change to its section files under the design ({design_dir}) (and its mockups) by PR; what is obvious from the record you decide and say, a choice with a sensible default is a `steer` to the person, and only what would be wrong to go on without is an `ask`. You merge nothing on a held path: such a PR waits for the review stages, each its own seat.
